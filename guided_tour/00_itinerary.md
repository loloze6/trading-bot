## Setup choices made (say if you want them changed)

1. **The inspector writes its own notes.** One Fable sub-agent, launched at stop 00 and
   continued at every later stop, appends its notes itself under `## Inspector notes` of the
   stop file. The guide never reads that section, so its later speeches are not coloured by the
   inspector, and the operator does not see the notes until the post-tour analysis.
2. **Stop files carry the three headings from the start**; the guide fills Speech, then
   Operator remarks (verbatim, with direct answers); the inspector fills the third.
3. **Long elements are split into parts** (03, 05, 12, 21, 22, 24), each part a file.
4. **Examples come from run_102** (and run_101 as its parent); the guide opens no other run
   unless a remark asks.
5. **Thirty stops.** Low granularity means one speech per element, every part of the element
   named; it does not mean fewer elements.
