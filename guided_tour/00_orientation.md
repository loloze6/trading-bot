## Speech (verbatim)

**Stop 00 of 29: Orientation: the two machines and the map.** Open on the side: `strategy-research/CLAUDE.md` (whole, 121 lines); `strategy-research/docs/USER_GUIDE.md` lines 150-325; `strategy-research/runs/run_102/pipeline_state.yaml` lines 1-40.

This stop is one speech in three parts: the research project's house rules (SR/CLAUDE.md), the official stage map (USER_GUIDE §2), and the real run that shows which stages ran (run_102's `pipeline_state.yaml`).

**The two machines.** I present you the repository's two halves, as the root `CLAUDE.md` names them. `trading-bot/` (TB) is the engine: given a strategy config and a period of market prices, it replays the market bar by bar, turns the strategy's forecast into a position, and charges fees, the way the live bot would trade. A replay like that is a *backtest*. `strategy-research/` (SR) is the factory that feeds it: it writes trading ideas, turns them into configs for the engine, runs the backtests, judges the results, records them and picks the next idea. The tour walks the factory in the order it runs, and goes into the engine at stops 12 to 14, where the factory first uses it.
