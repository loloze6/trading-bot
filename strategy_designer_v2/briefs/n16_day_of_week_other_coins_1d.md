# Day of week on other coins (night brief 16)
A weekday effect found on three large coins is a claim about those coins and that fold. This brief asks the same family of questions on three others, with freedom on the definition: which day or days, long or short, held how long, and whether the effect is in returns or only in volatility. Ideas that find the opposite are welcome. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
