# Round numbers on hourly bars (night brief 30)
Orders cluster at round prices. This brief asks whether the distance of the close to the nearest round level (in units of recent volatility), or a cross of such a level, predicts the next hours on BTC and ETH, as direction or as volatility. Ideas must define the level set from the price itself. Fees 0.15% per round trip. Both coins must pass.
