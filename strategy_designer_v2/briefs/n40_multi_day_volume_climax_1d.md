# Multi-day volume climax on daily bars (night brief 40)
One loud day is noise; three loud days are a crowd. This brief asks whether 2 to 5 consecutive days of volume well above the trailing month, with or against the price move, mark a reversal or a continuation over the next 5 to 20 days on three coins. Ideas must define the climax from the bars. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
