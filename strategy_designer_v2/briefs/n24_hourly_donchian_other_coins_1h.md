# The hourly channel breakout on other coins (night brief 24)
A rule found on two coins is a claim about those coins and that fold. This brief asks whether channel breakouts on hourly bars pay on three other coins, with freedom on the channel length (24 to 168 bars), the exit (opposite channel, fixed horizon, trailing) and any gate. Ideas that find the opposite are welcome. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
