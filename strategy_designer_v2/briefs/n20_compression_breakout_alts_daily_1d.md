# Compression and breakout on altcoins, daily (night brief 20)
Quiet ranges end. This brief asks whether the end of a compressed range on altcoin daily bars (range or volatility in the bottom part of its own history, then a close outside the range) predicts continuation, and over which horizon. Ideas must define compression and the break from bars and say the horizon. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
