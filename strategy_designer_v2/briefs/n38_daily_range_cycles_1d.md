# Range cycles on daily bars (night brief 38)
Narrow days cluster and so do wide days. This brief asks whether the narrowest range in 4 to 20 days, or the widest, predicts the size or the direction of the next 1 to 5 days on three coins with full history. Ideas must define the range cycle from high, low and close. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
