# Price and volume divergence on daily bars (night brief 33)
Volume tells whether a move is bought. This brief asks whether the sign of the recent price trend against the sign of the recent volume trend, over 5 to 20 days, predicts the next 5 to 20 days on three coins with full history. Ideas must define both trends from the bars. Fees 0.15% per round trip. All three coins must pass.
