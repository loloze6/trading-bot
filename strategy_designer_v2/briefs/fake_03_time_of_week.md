Crypto trades all week but the people do not. Weekends, the US open, the Asian session, the monthly options expiry: I suspect some of these leave a mark on returns or on volatility. Ideas should say which clock they use and whether the effect is in returns, in volatility, or in both. Multi-coin ideas are welcome if the pattern is the same across the three pairs.
