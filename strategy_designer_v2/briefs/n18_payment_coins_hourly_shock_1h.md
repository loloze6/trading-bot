# Shocks on payment coins, hourly (night brief 18)
XRP and TRX move on news and on large transfers. This brief asks what an hourly shock (a bar several times its usual size, with or without a volume surge) means for the next hours on these two coins: continuation, reversal, or only volatility. Ideas must define the shock from the bars and name the horizon. Fees 0.15% per round trip. Both coins must pass.
