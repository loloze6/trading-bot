What a brief's theme is, and when an idea stays on it (the operator, 2026-11-24; topic 50). Designed, not yet read by a
step: steps 7.a, 7.b, 7.c, 7.e, 7.g and step 1 will read it once topic 50 is built.

**A brief's theme is its theory in one sentence: a situation in a coin's bars, and the effect it claims follows.**
Example (brief 031, indecision, night 6): situation "after a run of several days, a wide day that closes near its
open"; effect "the coin moves back over the next days".

**Its elements:**
- **The theme's situation:** what happens in the bars before the effect ("after a run of several days, a wide day that
  closes near its open"). It is what the brief is about.
- **The theme's effect:** what follows, which way, and over what time ("the coin moves back over the next days"). When
  the brief is a question, each answer it gives a reason for is an effect.

**An idea on the theme** has the theme's situation, or a narrower case of it:
- a stronger case of the same situation ("a wider day", "a longer run");
- the same situation in one market profile (`market_profile.md`);
- its mirror, when the brief names both directions (a run up and a run down);
- the same situation on other coins, another bar size or another horizon.

It may change the effect's side when the brief is a question, the measure, the horizon, the coins and the profile.

**An idea off the theme** has another situation. Example (night 6): from the indecision brief, "a wide day that closes
near its low rebounds" is another situation (a low close after a fall, not a flat close after a run): off the theme.

**A theme found real:** at least one idea on the theme is confirmed by step 5 (its direction check included).

**A theme found not real:** the brief's first ideas and the follow-ups on the theme are not confirmed, and the lines
that read them find no other idea on the theme worth testing: they answer "no ideas: the theme's effect is not in the
data". The brief is closed with that reading; it is a result, not a failure (most traders' theories are not in the bars:
topic 47, 1d-12).

**A discovery:** a pattern off the theme that a line finds in a backtest (line 7.g reads patterns; line 7.e reads what a
book really earns from). It is not dropped: it becomes a theme of its own, a new brief, so it is tested as a first idea
of its own theory and is not counted as the old brief's result.

**Why it matters:** on night 6 the four themes were not confirmed (0 of 16 first ideas), and every confirmed book came
from follow-ups off the theme, 8 of 10 families re-finding what the portfolio already holds (topic 47, 1d-13); the
themes' answer, "not real here", was never written.
