Idea: Over the 2 hours from 23:00 UTC, a coin moves in the direction of its own 3-day return, and the larger that 3-day return, the larger the move with it.
Consequence: Add with the 3-day trend late in the UTC evening, where the continuation is as strong as at the 16:00 settlement.
Why:
- F5: Late in the UTC evening the 3-day trend continues: for the bar stamped 22:00 the slope is +5.61 bp (t 5.5, 16,778 cases), with 21:00 at t 3.1 and 23:00 at t 3.0; it is positive in all six coins (t from 1.6 to 2.9) and in seven of eight years.
- F6: The bars stamped 21:00 and 22:00 UTC rise on average by +5.5 bp (t 6.9) and +4.4 bp (t 5.8), positive in all six coins (t from 3.0 to 4.6) and in seven of eight years (2022: -2.2 bp, t -1.5).
