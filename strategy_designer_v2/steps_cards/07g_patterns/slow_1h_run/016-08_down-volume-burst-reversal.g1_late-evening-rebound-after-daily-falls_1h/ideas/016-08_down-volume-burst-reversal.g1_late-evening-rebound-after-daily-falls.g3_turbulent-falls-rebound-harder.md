Idea: After a 24-hour fall, the higher the coin's 24-hour volatility relative to its 30-day volatility, the more the coin rises over the next 24 hours.
Consequence: A trader would favour buying falls that came with a burst of turbulence, and refuse to short them, for a one-day rebound.
Why:
- F4: Among 24-hour falls, how the fall was made matters beyond its size, but not the way the brief expects.
