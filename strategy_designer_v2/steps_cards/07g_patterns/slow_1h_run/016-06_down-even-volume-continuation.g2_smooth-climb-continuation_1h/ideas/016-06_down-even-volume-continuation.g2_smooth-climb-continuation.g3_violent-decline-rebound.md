Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days, the higher its hourly volatility over those 3 days, the more it rises over the following 3 days.
Consequence: A violent decline is a reason to lean long for the next 3 days, and a quiet decline gives no such reason.
Why:
- F5: Also after 3-day falls, higher hourly volatility over the past 3 days is followed by higher 3-day forward excess return (t 2.75, n 2674). Among climbs the same effect is absent (t 0.60).
