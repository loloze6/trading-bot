Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days, the larger the share of those hours that closed up, the more the coin rises over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A 3-day rise made of many small up hours is a reason to go long for the following days, not to fade it.
Why:
- F4: Three-day rises built as a grind keep going.
- F5: The book's confirmed slope comes from grinds rising, not from jumps falling, so the short loses everywhere.
