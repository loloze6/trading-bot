Idea: The more a coin has risen over its last 3 days, the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A strong 3-day rise is a reason to hold a long position for about a week, where the move per case is several times a round trip.
Why:
- F4: Over 5 days the sign turns: a coin's 3-day return is followed by more of the same. The slope of the 5-day excess return on the standardised 3-day return has t 3.0 (3106 cases), and t 3.4 on the grid shifted by 60 hours (3184 cases). The highest fifth of 3-day returns is followed by +72 bps of unusual move (total +129 bps) on the first grid and +185 bps (t 3.7; total +246 bps) on the shifted one. The coin's usual 5-day move is about 1054 bps. Across coins the t is positive on 4 of 6: XRP 2.7, ZEC 2.0, BTC 1.5, ETH 1.2.
