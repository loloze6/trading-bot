Idea: The higher a coin's volume over the past 1 day relative to its own 20-day average volume, the higher its return over the next 3 days, whatever the sign of its 30-day return.
Consequence: Unusually heavy volume is a reason to lean long for the next few days even in a falling month, and unusually thin volume is a reason to refuse longs.
Why:
- F4: Heavy relative volume predicts the 3-day return about as well outside the rising-month regime as inside it.
