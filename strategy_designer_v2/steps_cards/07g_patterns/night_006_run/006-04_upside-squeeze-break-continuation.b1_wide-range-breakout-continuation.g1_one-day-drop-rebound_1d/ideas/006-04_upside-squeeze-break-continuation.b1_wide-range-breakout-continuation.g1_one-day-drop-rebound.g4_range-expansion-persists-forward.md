Idea: The wider a coin's 5-day average daily range relative to its 60-day average range, the larger its absolute move over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader would size option-like or breakout exposure up after range expansion, not after compression, and expect quiet coins to stay quiet.
Why:
- F5: Range compression does not precede larger moves; the reverse holds.
