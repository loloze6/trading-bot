Idea: For a coin, the narrower its 5-day high-low range against its 30-day range, the smaller its move over the next 3 days relative to its 20-day volatility.
Consequence: A trader would avoid paying for breakout or volatility positions just because a coin's range has compressed.
Why:
- F6: Range compression does not precede larger moves, contrary to the brief. The 3-day move scaled by 20-day volatility is 0.76 and 0.75 in the two most compressed fifths (5-day range over 30-day range), against 0.87 to 0.88 in the two widest.
