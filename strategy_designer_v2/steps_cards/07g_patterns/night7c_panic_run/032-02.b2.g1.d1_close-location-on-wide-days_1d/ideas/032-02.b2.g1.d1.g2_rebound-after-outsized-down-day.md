Idea: The larger a coin's one-day fall, measured against its usual daily move over the past 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 1 day.
Consequence: A trader would take a one-day long after an unusually deep down day, sized by how unusual the fall was, and would not fade ordinary down days or up days.
Why:
- F3: A large one-day fall is followed by a rebound: the bottom fifth of one-day returns gains +0.71 % in excess the next day (t 4.56, 1722 cases). The other four fifths run from -0.05 % to -0.31 % (t +4.56 is the only positive t above 1), so the reversal sits in the down tail only.
