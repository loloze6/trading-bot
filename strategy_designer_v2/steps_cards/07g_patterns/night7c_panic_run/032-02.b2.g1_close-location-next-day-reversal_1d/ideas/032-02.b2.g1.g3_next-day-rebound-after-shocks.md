Idea: The larger a coin's daily fall is against its standard deviation of daily returns over the past 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 1 day.
Consequence: A trader would buy for one day after an unusually large down day, sized by how many usual moves the fall spans.
Why:
- F5: A large down day against the coin's 20-day volatility is followed by a rebound the next day.
