Idea: The lower a coin's daily close sits within its high-low range of the past 5 days, the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader would refuse multi-day longs after a close near the 5-day low and lean with the move for the following five days.
Why:
- F6: Over 5 days the close's place in the 5-day range carries on rather than reverses (non-overlapping 5-day grid).
