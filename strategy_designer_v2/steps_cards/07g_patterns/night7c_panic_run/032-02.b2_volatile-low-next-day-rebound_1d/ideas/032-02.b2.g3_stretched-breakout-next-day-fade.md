Idea: When a coin closes above its highest high of the prior 30 days, the further the close sits above that high, the more the coin falls over the next 1 day.
Consequence: A trader would refuse to chase a stretched breakout above the monthly high for the next day and would favour fading the most extended ones.
Why:
- F4: Among the 320 closes above the prior 30-day high, the further above the high the close sits, the lower the next day's excess return (Spearman −0.205, p 0.0002).
