Idea: The nearer a coin's daily close sits to the day's high within that day's range, the lower the coin's return over the next 1 day, and the nearer the close sits to the low, the higher.
Consequence: A trader would refuse to buy a close pinned at the day's high and would favour buying a close pinned at the day's low for the next day.
Why:
- F4: Over all bars, where the close sat in the day's range predicted the next day the other way. Closes in the bottom fifth of the range were followed by +0.57 % (t 4.21, n 1740), and closes in the top fifth by -0.31 % (t -2.32). The day-clustered slope t was -2.91 over 1448 days, and -2.15 over 1401 days outside the falling, agitated regime.
