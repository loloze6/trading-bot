Idea: The larger a coin's 5-day return relative to its recent daily volatility, the more its return over the next 5 days exceeds its normal drift.
Consequence: A strong week, scaled by the coin's own volatility, is a reason to lean long on that coin for the next week.
Why:
- F4: The prior 5-day return, scaled by 20-day volatility, points the same way. Its top fifth is followed by +1.75 % over the drift over 5 days (t 2.38, 276 cases). Its bottom fifth is followed by -1.39 % (t -1.88).
- F1: How close a coin closes to its highest high of the prior 30 days sorts the next 5 days, on a non-overlapping 5-day grid across folds B and C. The top decile, closing about 0.3 % above that high, then beats the coin's drift by +3.89 % (t 2.50 with same-day cases taken together, 126 cases on 50 days). The next decile, about 5 % below the high, beats it by +3.29 % (t 2.50, 121 cases on 67 days). The four deciles 10 % to 35 % below the high all trail the drift, by -1.9 % to -3.4 % (decile 3: -3.39 %, t -2.69, 67 days).
