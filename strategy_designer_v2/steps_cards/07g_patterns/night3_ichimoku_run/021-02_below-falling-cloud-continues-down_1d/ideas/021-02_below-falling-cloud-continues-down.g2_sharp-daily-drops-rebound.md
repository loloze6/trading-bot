Idea: The larger a coin's one-day fall, measured against its own 30-day daily volatility, the higher its return over the next day.
Consequence: A sharp daily drop is a reason to buy for a one-day rebound rather than to sell into it.
Why:
- F4: A daily fall of at least 2 times the coin's 30-day volatility is followed by a next-day return about 100 bps above average.
