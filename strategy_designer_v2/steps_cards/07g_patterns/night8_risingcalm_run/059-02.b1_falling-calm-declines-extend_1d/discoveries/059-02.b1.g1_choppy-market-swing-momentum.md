A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's 14-day return measured in units of its own 30-day daily swing, the further the coin moves the same way over the next 5 days, up after a rise and down after a fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a sideways coin would lean with its two-week swing-scaled move for the next five days, long after strong rises and short after strong falls, rather than fading it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
