Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further it keeps rising over the next 5 days, beginning only from rises of more than one usual daily swing.
Consequence: A trader buys only rises well beyond a usual day's swing in calm markets and leaves ordinary up days alone, because those are followed by falls rather than gains.
Why:
- F5: The tested idea's effect sits only at the top of the forecast, not across it. In choppy calm, rise days with z from 0 to 1 are followed by -1.52 % (t -1.44, 67 cases), z from 1 to 2 by +7.96 % (t 1.85, 14) and z above 2 by +3.51 % (t 1.62, 9). By forecast quartile, only the top quartile pays (+6.75 %, t 2.44, 24 cases); the other three are -0.64 %, -3.21 % and -0.77 %.
