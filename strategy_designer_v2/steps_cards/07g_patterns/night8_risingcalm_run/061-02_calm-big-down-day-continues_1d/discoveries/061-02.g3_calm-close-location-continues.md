A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the day's low within its high-low range, the lower its return over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In calm markets a close near the day's low is a reason to sell or stay out, and a close near the high a reason to hold, which is the opposite of the low-close rebound held in agitated markets.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-02_calm-big-down-day-continues_1d (idea 061-02_calm-big-down-day-continues, brief 061_calm-market-big-day-continues).
What it rests on:
- F6: In calm markets, where a day closes inside its own range predicts the next 5 days, graded. By quartile of close location (0 = at the low, 1 = at the high) the 5-day excess return is -2.73 % (t -2.72), +0.07 %, +0.66 % and +1.47 %. In a joint fit it adds +1.58 % per unit of close location on top of the swing-scaled move, which adds +1.44 % per unit.
- F7: The close-location effect turns over in agitated markets. There, low-close days are followed by +0.91 % over 5 days (t 0.85) and high-close days by -1.45 % (t -1.28), the reverse of the calm reading in F6.
