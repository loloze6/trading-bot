Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day return against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its return in the same direction over the 5 days that start one day after it.
Consequence: A trader who misses the first day of a calm-market breakout can still join it the next day in the same direction, and the larger the day, the more is left to gain.
Why:
- F2: In calm markets the follow-through grows with the day's size. By quartile of |day's return / 30-day std| it runs from +0.80 % (t 1.12) in the lowest to +2.66 % (t 2.57, 86 days) in the highest. Measured from the next day's close, the highest quartile gains +4.08 % over 5 days (t 3.47) against +0.87 % in the lowest.
- F5: The effect needs days to show. In calm markets it is +0.25 % at 3 days (t 0.72), +1.52 % at 5 days (t 2.75) and +1.54 % at 10 days (t 1.36), where the slope per swing unit is 2.03 % (t 2.87).
