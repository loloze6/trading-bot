A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the further a coin's 10-day return has fallen below zero, measured against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A quiet downtrend whose recent decline is deep against its usual swing tends to extend that decline, so it is a reason to hold a short and to refuse a long.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01_rising-calm-dip-rebound_1d (idea 058-01_rising-calm-dip-rebound, brief 058_rising-calm-pullbacks-recover).
What it rests on:
- F4: In falling calm, the two lowest quarters of the 10-day return scaled by swing (z10 below -0.42) were followed by further 5-day excess falls of -5.40 % (t -2.80, 37 cases) and -3.63 % (t -3.15, 37 cases). The top quarter was followed by +1.75 % (t 1.38). This is the short side of the calm continuation, a cell the map holds with only one strategy (035-01.b1).
