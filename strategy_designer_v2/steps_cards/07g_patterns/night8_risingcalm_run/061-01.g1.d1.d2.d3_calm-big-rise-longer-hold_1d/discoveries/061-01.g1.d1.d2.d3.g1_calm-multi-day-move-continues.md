A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the larger a coin's 5-day return against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further it keeps moving the same way over the next 10 days, falls continuing down as rises continue up.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a quiet market follows a strong 5-day move in its own direction for ten days, including shorting sharp quiet-market declines rather than buying them.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.g1.d1.d2.d3_calm-big-rise-longer-hold_1d (idea 061-01.g1.d1.d2.d3_calm-big-rise-longer-hold, brief 061_calm-market-big-day-continues).
What it rests on:
- F2: In calm markets a coin's 5-day return keeps going the same way over the next 10 days, and more so the larger it was in swing units. The continuation is -1.41 % for 0 to 1 swings, +0.85 % for 1 to 2, +1.93 % for 2 to 3 and +7.95 % above 3 (t 2.48, 47 cases on 23 days). Calm here is a 30-day swing below the coin's pooled median, an approximation of the profile's past-year percentile.
- F3: In agitated markets the same 5-day measure is not graded. It gives +2.53 % for 0 to 1 swings, -4.93 % for 1 to 2 (t -2.04), +0.24 % for 2 to 3 and +6.65 % above 3 (t 2.28, 27 days). This is consistent with continuation being an orderly-market effect.

- F5: Pooled 5-day falls of 3 swings or more are followed by -13.6 % over 10 days (t -6.06 ordinary; -2.28 and -3.46 day-clustered in the two deepest bins). The result is negative on all six coins, but it sits on only 11 days in windows w7, w14 and w16. It is a few crash episodes, not a broad pattern.
