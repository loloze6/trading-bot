Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day move against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further it keeps moving in that day's direction over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader follows large days in the day's direction when the coin has been swinging more than usual, sized by the day's size in swing units.
Why:
- F3: The follow-through is stronger in agitated markets than in calm ones, against the brief's claim. Agitated here means the 30-day swing is above its own 120-day median, a proxy for the profile's swing measure. Over 5 days, the graded slope was +0.0548 (t 3.00, 138 cases) after agitated down days and +0.0356 (t 1.99, 122 cases) after agitated up days. In calm markets the slopes were -0.0100 (t -1.36, 616 cases) after down days and +0.0109 (t 1.35, 488 cases) after up days.
