A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, calm market, the larger a coin's 5-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its further fall over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In calm markets with no 60-day trend, a trader can short after a large swing-scaled 5-day fall, a side and profile the map leaves almost empty.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01_calm-big-up-day-continues_1d (idea 061-01_calm-big-up-day-continues, brief 061_calm-market-big-day-continues).
What it rests on:
- F3: In calm markets the scaled prior 5-day return sorts the next 5 days at both ends. The lowest quintile has a mean excess return of -3.20 % (t -3.90, 130 cases) and the highest +2.90 % (t 2.89, 154 cases). In agitated markets the same quintiles give -0.52 % and +1.22 % (t -0.36 and 0.97).
- F4: Inside calm markets the scaled 5-day slope is strongest in a choppy 60-day trend (slope 0.048, t 3.09, 101 cases, 55 days). It is weaker in a falling trend (t 2.21, 219 cases) and in a rising trend (t 1.47, 178 cases).

- F6: The book's gain comes from its longs. Long bars sum to +6.63 (mean +0.27 % per bar, 2466 bars) and short bars to -0.96 (mean -0.04 %, 2459 bars). The 1259 short trades average -2.53 % net against +4.41 % for the 906 long trades.
