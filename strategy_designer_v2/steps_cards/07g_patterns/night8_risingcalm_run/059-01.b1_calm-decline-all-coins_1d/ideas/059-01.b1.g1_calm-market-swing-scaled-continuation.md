Idea: In a calm market, the further a coin's 14-day return lies from zero in units of its own 30-day daily swing, the larger its move in the same direction over the next 5 days, up after a rise and down after a fall.
Consequence: In calm markets a trader would follow a coin's steady two-week drift both ways, more strongly the larger the drift is against the coin's usual swing, instead of only shorting declines.
Why:
- F1: In calm markets the 5-day move follows the sign and size of the swing-scaled 14-day return on both sides, not only the falling side. The bottom quintile of the swing-scaled 14-day return is followed by a drift-removed 5-day return of -2.93 % (t -3.93, 188 cases) and the top quintile by +2.86 % (t 3.37, 200 cases). The signed product (scaled return clipped at ±3, times excess return) averages +2.13 % with a day-clustered t of 3.10 (904 cases, 200 days). It is positive in all 12 visible windows and all 4 years (2018 +2.41 %, 2019 +0.62 %, 2020 +3.96 %, 2021 +1.78 %).
- F2: The same continuation is absent in agitated markets. There the signed product averages +0.11 % (t 0.12, 718 cases, 186 days), and the deepest-decline quintile is followed by a rebound of +1.62 % (t 1.24, 137 cases). The calm condition carries the effect.
- F4: In calm markets the rising side is as strong as the falling side. Calm rising-trend coins with a positive 14-day return gain +3.12 % in excess over 5 days (t 2.13, 160 cases, 50 days). Calm choppy-trend coins show +2.96 % on the rising side (71 cases) and +3.12 % on the falling side (55 cases). A short-only book leaves half of the effect unused.
- F5: The short book is mostly the market's own move inverted. On its worst 2 % of days the coins rose +7.02 % on average, and on its best 2 % they fell -8.29 % (29 days each). Its window means are positive mainly where the coins fell (w14 book +0.13 %/day, coins -0.43 %; w7 book +0.10 %, coins -0.44 %). The per-case unusual move is +0.99 % (t 2.66) against a cost of 0.22 %, but the normal drift in the short direction is -0.68 %, which leaves only +0.31 % in total.
