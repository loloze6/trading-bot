Idea: After a coin rises over one day by less than its usual daily move in units of its 20-day volatility, it falls over the next 3 days, and the closer the rise is to an ordinary small up day, the larger that fall.
Consequence: A modest up day is a reason to refuse a long, or to lean short, for the following few days.
Why:
- F5: Small up days of 0.3 to 1 unit are followed by weakness: -0.22% the next day (t -2.50, n 3366) and -0.44% on the 3-day grid (t -2.03, n 1187). The book holds a mean position of 0.37 after these days.
- F6: The book carries its gain only on its largest forecasts. Bars with a forecast of 10 or more gain +0.145% a day (n 3463, mean position 0.95), while bars with a forecast between 5 and 10 lose -0.046% a day (n 1211). The book holds only 19% to 30% of the book on average, against coin mean daily returns of +0.07% to +0.16%, which leaves it behind holding the coins in every coin.
