Idea: Among one-day falls, the heavier the day's volume relative to its 20-day average volume, the larger the coin's rebound over the next day.
Consequence: A heavy-volume sell-off is the stronger reason to buy for a one-day rebound, and a thin-volume one the weaker reason.
Why:
- F2: This next-day rebound after falls larger than one usual move comes mostly on heavy volume, the opposite of what the brief expects: heavy-volume falls (top tercile of volume over its 20-day mean) are followed by +0.73% (t 4.31, n 1225), against +0.38% (t 1.13, n 195) for thin-volume falls and +0.28% (t 1.25, n 516) for mid-volume falls.
- F1: After a down day, the next day's excess return grows with the size of the fall in 20-day-volatility units: falls beyond 2 units are followed by +0.74% (t 2.32, n 425) and falls of 1 to 2 units by +0.55% (t 4.06, n 1511), against -0.13% (t -1.97, n 4487) after flat days. The slope of the next-day excess return on the downside move is negative in all six coins (t from -1.55 for BTC to -5.82 for XMR, about 2535 bars each), and it holds in both halves of the windows (w1-12: +0.50%, t 3.14, n 963; w13-24: +0.64%, t 3.28, n 973).
