Idea: After a coin rises over one day by more than usual in units of its 20-day volatility, it keeps rising over the period from one to five days after that day. The larger the rise, the larger that later rise.
Consequence: Lean long after a sharp up day starting a day later, not at that day's close, with conviction growing with the size of the rise.
Why:
- F4: After a sharp up day, the continuation the idea predicts does not show on the first day; it shows from the second day on. The next-day excess is -0.11% after rises of 1 to 2 units (n 1596) and -0.04% after rises beyond 2 units (n 501), while day 2 gives +0.44% and +0.74% and days 3 to 5 stay positive (+0.05% to +0.36%). On the 5-day grid this sums to +1.58% (t 2.51, n 295) and +2.69% (t 1.88, n 96).
- F6: The book carries its gain only on its largest forecasts. Bars with a forecast of 10 or more gain +0.145% a day (n 3463, mean position 0.95), while bars with a forecast between 5 and 10 lose -0.046% a day (n 1211). The book holds only 19% to 30% of the book on average, against coin mean daily returns of +0.07% to +0.16%, which leaves it behind holding the coins in every coin.
