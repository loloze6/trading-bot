Idea: The larger a coin's 20-day return, the larger its return over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A trader would favour long trades in coins with strong one-month gains and refuse them in coins with weak one-month returns, over a ten-day horizon.
Why:
- F4: The 20-day return predicts the next 10-day excess return. The top quintile averages +2.13% and the bottom -0.58% (slope t 2.21, n 1375). This also appears only in w13–w24 (t 2.65; w1–w12 t -0.15), and it drops to t 1.49 once the volume ratio is included.
