Idea: The higher a coin's volume over the last 2 days is relative to its volume over the previous 5 days, in the middle and end of the week, the more its 2-day return continues in the same direction over the next 2 days.
Consequence: A trader follows heavy-volume midweek moves rather than fading them, and gives them more weight the heavier the volume.
Why:
- F6: Midweek moves made on heavy volume tend to continue rather than reverse. On the 23:00 bars of Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, the 48-hour slopes in the thickest volume third are +0.079 (t 2.6), +0.081 (t 3.0) and +0.053 (t 2.0), about 615 cases each. The thin thirds on the same days give -0.03 to -0.05 (t > -1.4). This is weaker evidence given the 21 cells examined.
