Idea: While a coin's 30-day hourly volatility is low against its last 6 months, a fall from Saturday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC is followed by a further fall over Monday and Tuesday: the larger the weekend fall, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A trader would not buy a weekend dip in calm weeks and would treat it as a reason to stay short over the next 2 days.
Why:
- F4: The weekend reversal holds only in the high-volatility regime: the slope of the Monday–Tuesday move on the weekend move is −0.125 (t −4.3, 1 666 cases), and the top quintile of weekend rises is followed by −1.38 % unusual. In calm regimes the slope is +0.066 (t 2.25, 2 499 cases), and a weekend fall is followed by a further unusual −0.68 % (t −3.43).
