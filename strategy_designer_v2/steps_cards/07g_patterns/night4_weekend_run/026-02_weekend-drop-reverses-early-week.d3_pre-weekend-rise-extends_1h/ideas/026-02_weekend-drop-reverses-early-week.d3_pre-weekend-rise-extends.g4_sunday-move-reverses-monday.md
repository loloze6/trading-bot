Idea: A coin's move over Sunday (its 24-hour return to Monday 00:00 UTC) is reversed over Monday, and the larger Sunday's move, the larger Monday's move against it.
Consequence: A trader can take the side opposite to Sunday's move at the start of Monday and hold it through the day.
Why:
- F4: A coin's 24-hour return to 00:00 UTC is partly reversed over the next 24 hours on most days of the week, in non-overlapping daily cases (about 2100 to 2240 per weekday).
