Idea: A coin's return over the two hours after the 21:00 UTC close is upward, and it is larger the higher the coin's average absolute hourly return over the last 30 days.
Consequence: A trader would time a planned purchase to occur before 21:00 UTC instead of after, especially when the coin is volatile.
Why:
- F7: Coins rise in the hours from 21:00 to 23:00 UTC, more so when the coin is volatile. The hours closing at 21:00 and 22:00 earn +5.45 bp (t 6.75) and +4.36 bp (t 5.66) over the next hour, out of 24 hourly slots. The two hours after the 21:00 close are positive in 7 of 8 years and rise with the coin's hourly-volatility tercile: +4.3 bp (t 3.13), +7.6 bp (t 4.05) and +15.1 bp (t 6.00). Even the top tercile's move stays below the 0.19 % trade cost.
