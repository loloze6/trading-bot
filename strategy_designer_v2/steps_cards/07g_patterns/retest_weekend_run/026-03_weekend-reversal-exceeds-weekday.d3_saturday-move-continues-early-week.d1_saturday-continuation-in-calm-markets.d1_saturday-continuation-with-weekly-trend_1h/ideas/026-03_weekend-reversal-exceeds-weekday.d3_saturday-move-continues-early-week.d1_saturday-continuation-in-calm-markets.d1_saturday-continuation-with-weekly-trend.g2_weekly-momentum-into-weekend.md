Idea: A coin's 7-day return, read at the start of Friday, is followed over Friday and Saturday by a move in the same direction that grows with the size of the 7-day return scaled by its 30-day volatility of hourly returns, the more so the lower that volatility is against its own past year.
Consequence: A trader would ride the week's direction through Friday and Saturday, most of all in calm markets, ahead of the Saturday-continuation trade.
Why:
- F3: The 7-day return at Friday 00:00 UTC predicts the volatility-scaled return over Friday and Saturday in the same direction.
- F4: Saturday's own return is positive on average, in both halves and for every coin.
