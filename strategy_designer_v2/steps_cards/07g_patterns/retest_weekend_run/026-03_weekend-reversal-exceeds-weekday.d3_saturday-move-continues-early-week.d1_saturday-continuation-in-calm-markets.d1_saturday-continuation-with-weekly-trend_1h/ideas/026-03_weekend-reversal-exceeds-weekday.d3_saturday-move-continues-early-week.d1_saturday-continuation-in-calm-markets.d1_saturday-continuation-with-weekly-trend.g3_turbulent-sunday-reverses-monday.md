Idea: A coin's 24-hour return over Sunday is followed on Monday by a move in the opposite direction that grows with the size of Sunday's move scaled by its 30-day volatility of hourly returns, while that volatility is high against its own past year.
Consequence: A trader would lean against Sunday's move during Monday in turbulent markets, and refuse to follow Sunday's move into the week.
Why:
- F5: Sunday's volatility-scaled return is partly reversed on Monday, but only in turbulent markets and mostly in the earlier years, so this is the weakest finding.
