Idea: A coin's large move over the past 24 hours is reversed more over the next 24 hours the higher the past day's hourly volatility is against its own last 30 days.
Consequence: Lean against a large daily move more firmly when it came in a burst of volatility, and less when the coin was already this agitated.

- F3: Among these large moves, the reversal is about twice as large when the past day's hourly volatility is high against its 30-day level. In the highest tercile of that ratio (mean 2.15) it is +0.329 usual moves. In the lower two terciles it is +0.163 and +0.168. Each tercile has about 522 cases.
