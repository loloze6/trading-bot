Idea: A coin's move over the past 24 hours is partly reversed over the next 24 hours. The reversal grows steeply with the size of that move against the coin's usual daily move, and is largest after the rarest moves.
Consequence: After an unusually large daily move in a coin, lean against it for the next day, and lean harder the more extreme the move, since only the largest moves reverse by several times the cost of a trade.
