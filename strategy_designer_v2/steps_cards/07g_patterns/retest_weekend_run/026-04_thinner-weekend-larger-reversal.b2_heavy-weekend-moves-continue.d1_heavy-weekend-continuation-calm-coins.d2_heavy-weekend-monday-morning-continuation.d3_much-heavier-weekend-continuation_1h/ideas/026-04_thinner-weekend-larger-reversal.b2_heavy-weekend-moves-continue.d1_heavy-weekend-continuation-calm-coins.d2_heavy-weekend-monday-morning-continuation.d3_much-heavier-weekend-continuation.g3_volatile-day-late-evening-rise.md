Idea: A coin rises over the two hours from 21:00 to 23:00 UTC. The rise grows with how high the coin's past-day hourly volatility is against its last 30 days.
Consequence: Take the evening-hours long only in days of heightened volatility, because on ordinary days the rise is smaller than the cost of a trade.
Why:
- F4: Every coin tends to rise over the two hours 21:00–23:00 UTC.
- F5: The 21:00–23:00 rise is concentrated where the past day's volatility is high against its 30-day level.
