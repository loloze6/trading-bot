Idea: A coin's Saturday–Sunday move continues over the first 12 hours of Monday more strongly the heavier the weekend's hourly volume is against the five weekdays before it, whatever the size of the move.
Consequence: Size the Monday-morning continuation by the weekend volume multiple alone, not by the move's size, which adds nothing to the effect.
Why:
- F6: The tested signal's Monday-morning continuation comes from the weekend's volume and not from the size of the weekend's move.
- F7: The book's gain rests on a few days.
