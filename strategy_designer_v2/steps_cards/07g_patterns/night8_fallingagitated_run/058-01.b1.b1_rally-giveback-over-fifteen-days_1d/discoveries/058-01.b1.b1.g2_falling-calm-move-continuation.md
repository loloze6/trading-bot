A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's 3-day move is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further its price continues in that same direction over the next 15 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would follow a large scaled few-day move in a falling, calm market for two weeks rather than fade it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
