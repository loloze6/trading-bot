A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin closes up after falling over the four days before, it gives part of that up day back over the next day. The deeper the four-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger the next-day fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would short a one-day bounce that follows a multi-day slide in a falling, agitated market for one day, sizing up with how deep the slide was against the coin's usual swing; this fills the empty falling-agitated short cell.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.g2.b1.d2.b1.d2.d3_multi-day-rally-fades-next-day_1d (idea 058-01.g2.b1.d2.b1.d2.d3_multi-day-rally-fades-next-day, brief 058_falling-agitated-multiday-rallies-fail).
What it rests on:
- F2: Among in-profile up days that come after a fall over the four days before, the larger that prior fall against the 30-day swing, the larger the next-day fall (rho 0.233, p < 0.0001, n 380; three days ahead rho 0.215, p 0.012, n 135).
- F3: The bounce-fade of F2 holds in every year seen: the deeper-fall half gives +1.80 %, +1.19 %, +2.82 % and +2.24 % next day in 2018 to 2021. Over all prior-fall depths it is positive on 9 of 10 coins; DOGE is the exception, at -1.31 % with 10 cases.
- F4: Outside the profile the same bounce-fade is present but weaker (rho 0.076, p 0.0014, n 1,787). Its deepest-fall quartile gives +1.00 % next day (t 2.32) and +2.57 % over three days (t 2.30), so in profile it is about twice as large per case.
- F1: Inside the falling-agitated profile, an up day is followed by a fall the next day whatever its size. Over all up days the mean unusual move in the short direction is +0.96 % (t 2.48, 718 cases). But the up day's size against the 30-day swing does not grade it (Spearman rho 0.000, p 0.99, n 639; the largest quartile, at 1.43 swings, gives only -0.11 % next day, t -0.14).
