A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's fall over the past 5 days against its usual 5-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, scaled to 5 days), the larger its further fall over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would short coins after multi-day falls in falling calm markets and hold for about 5 days, sizing up with the fall's size in usual swings, instead of buying the dip as in agitated falls.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.g2.b1.d2.b1.d2.g1_multi-day-rally-give-back_1d (idea 058-01.g2.b1.d2.b1.d2.g1_multi-day-rally-give-back, brief 058_falling-agitated-multiday-rallies-fail).
What it rests on:
- F1: In falling calm markets, falls continue and the effect is graded. On the 5-day grid, the next-5-day unusual move rises steadily across quintiles of the 5-day return in usual swings: -4.38 % (t -1.65, 23 days), -3.21 % (t -1.44), -0.33 %, +0.95 %, +2.38 % (53 cases each). On the 3-day grid, the second-lowest quintile alone gives -2.74 % (t -2.64, 52 days).
- F2: The falling-calm continuation after a 3-day fall holds across most of the shown data, though the grading inside the falls is weak. After a 3-day fall, the unusual next-3-day move was negative in 2018 (-2.93 %, t -1.76), 2019 (-2.50 %, t -1.77) and 2021 (-0.70 %). It was negative in 7 of the 9 windows with more than 3 cases, for example w13 -3.58 %, w14 -5.38 % and w15 -4.74 %. Across those falls, the correlation between the size of the fall and the move that follows is only 0.025.
