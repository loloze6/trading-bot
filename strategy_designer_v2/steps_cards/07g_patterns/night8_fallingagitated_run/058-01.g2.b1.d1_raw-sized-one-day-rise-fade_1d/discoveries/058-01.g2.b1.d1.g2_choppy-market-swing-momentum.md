A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's 10-day return measured in standard deviations of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further its price moves the same way over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a market going nowhere over 60 days, a trader would lean with a strong 10-day move in proportion to its size in usual swings, on either side.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.g2.b1.d1_raw-sized-one-day-rise-fade_1d (idea 058-01.g2.b1.d1_raw-sized-one-day-rise-fade, brief 058_falling-agitated-multiday-rallies-fail).
What it rests on:
- F4: Outside the profile, the 10-day return in 30-day swings continues over the next 5 days, the opposite of the reversal inside the profile (slope -0.031, t -2.06, 263 cases).
- F5: This out-of-profile 10-day continuation is graded and broad, though it is only marginally past chance given the many angles looked at.
