A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's 3-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further its price falls over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would short a coin whose fall is large for its usual swing in a calm decline, expecting the fall to continue for about a week instead of rebounding.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.b1.b1.b2_large-rallies-fail-within-week_1d (idea 058-01.b1.b1.b2_large-rallies-fail-within-week, brief 058_falling-agitated-multiday-rallies-fail).
What it rests on:

There, the slope of the 5-day excess return on the scaled 3-day return is +0.0265 (clustered t 2.52, 265 cases on 81 days), and on the scaled 10-day return +0.0235 (t 2.43). After 3-day falls the mean is -2.78 % (t -1.75) and the slope +0.0364 (t 1.95). After 10-day falls the mean is -2.90 % (t -2.03, 57 days).
- F5: Outside the profile, scaled 3-day moves continue over 5 days. By quintile of the scaled 3-day return, the 5-day excess returns are -1.50 % (t -2.18), -1.96 % (t -2.90), +0.73 %, +0.34 % and +2.45 % (t 3.15), with 272 cases each.
