Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the further a coin's close sits below its close of 30 days earlier, as a plain percent of that earlier close, the more it falls over the next 7 days, with the deepest falls carrying most of the decline.
Consequence: A trader shorts a coin's month-long fall in a trendless but agitated market, in proportion to the fall's depth, and leaves the same fall alone when the market is calm.
Why:
- F3: The falls that continue sit mostly in choppy agitated markets, not choppy calm ones. The lowest third of 30-day returns is followed by -11.2 % unusual over 7 days in choppy agitated (clustered t -1.74, about 20 cases) against -3.5 % in choppy calm (t -0.93). The round-1 split, with agitation read as 30-day against 120-day volatility, agrees: the lowest fifth gives -11.8 % in agitated chop (t -3.5, 18 cases, not clustered) and -8.5 % in calm chop (9 cases). The map's choppy agitated short cell is empty (0 strategies).
- F2: In choppy markets the month's move continues, as the brief claims. Rho is +0.33 in choppy calm (p 0.004, 76 cases on 33 days) and +0.24 in choppy agitated (p 0.07, 59 cases on 35 days). The continuation return, signed by the month's direction, is +3.8 % (clustered t 1.49) in choppy calm and +5.5 % (t 1.91) in choppy agitated. Neither t reaches 2 once the cases of one day are taken together.
