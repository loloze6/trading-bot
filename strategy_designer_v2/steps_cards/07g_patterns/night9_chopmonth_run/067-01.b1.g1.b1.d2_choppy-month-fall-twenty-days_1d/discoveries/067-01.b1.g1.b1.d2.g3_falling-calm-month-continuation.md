A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's 30-day move measured against its daily swing over the past 30 days, the further it moves the same way over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a quiet downtrend, a trader should follow the month's direction for about ten days instead of buying the dip.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
