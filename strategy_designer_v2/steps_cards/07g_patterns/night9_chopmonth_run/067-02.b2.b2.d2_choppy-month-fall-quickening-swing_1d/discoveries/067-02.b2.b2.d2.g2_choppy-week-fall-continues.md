A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the deeper a coin's 5-day fall against its usual daily swing, the more it keeps falling over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A sharp week of losses in a trendless market is a reason to short the coin for about two weeks, not to buy the dip.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-02.b2.b2.d2_choppy-month-fall-quickening-swing_1d (idea 067-02.b2.b2.d2_choppy-month-fall-quickening-swing, brief 067_choppy-market-monthly-move-continues).
What it rests on:
- F3: The effect is graded across choppy 5-day-move quintiles. The excess 10-day return runs -5.91 % (t -3.30), -4.13 % (t -2.52), +1.60 %, -2.45 %, +8.94 % (t 2.17), about 80 cases each.
- F4: The fall side is the surer one. After a 5-day fall in a choppy market the excess 10-day return is -4.65 % (t -3.99, 177 cases); after a 5-day rise it is +2.98 % (t 1.64, 225 cases).
- F7: The tested idea's own situation shows here. In choppy bars after a 30-day fall with the 10-day swing above the 30-day swing, the excess 10-day return is -6.02 % (t -3.36, 71 cases), against -1.95 % (t -1.77, 122 cases) without the quickening swing. The book still earned only +0.6 % a year, and its mean daily return on days in a position was 0.0002 against a cost of 0.22 % per trade.
