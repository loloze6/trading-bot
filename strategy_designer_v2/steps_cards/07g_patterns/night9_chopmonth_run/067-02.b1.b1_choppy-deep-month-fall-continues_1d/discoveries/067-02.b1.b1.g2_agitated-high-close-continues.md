A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market, the higher a coin's daily close sits within that day's high-to-low range, the more the coin rises over the next 7 days. Closes at the very top are followed by the largest gains and closes at the bottom by further falls.
What it would mean for a trader: When a coin swings more than usual, a trader can lean long for about a week after days that close near their high, and refuse longs after days that close near their low.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-02.b1.b1_choppy-deep-month-fall-continues_1d (idea 067-02.b1.b1_choppy-deep-month-fall-continues, brief 067_choppy-market-monthly-move-continues).
What it rests on:
- F5: In trending agitated markets (rising or falling), a day that closes in the top fifth of its range is followed by a 7-day excess return of +7.74 %. A day closing in the bottom fifth is followed by -2.02 % (Welch t 4.49, 546 cases). The top-minus-bottom gap over all bars is positive in every year: 2018 +3.5 %, 2019 +5.1 %, 2020 +5.5 %, 2021 +9.1 % (t 1.17 to 3.66).
- F6: Where the close sits in its 7-day range sorts the next week in order. By quintile the next 7 days run -2.33 %, -0.94 %, +0.22 %, +0.15 % and +1.76 %; the bottom quintile has t -2.60 on 89 days and the top t 1.76 on 96 days.
