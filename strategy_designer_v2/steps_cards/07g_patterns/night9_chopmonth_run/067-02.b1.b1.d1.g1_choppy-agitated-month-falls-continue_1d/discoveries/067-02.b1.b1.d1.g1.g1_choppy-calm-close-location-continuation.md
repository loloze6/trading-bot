A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, calm market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the day's high, as a share of the day's range, the more the coin rises over the next 7 days. The nearer it sits to the day's low, the more it falls.
What it would mean for a trader: In a trendless, quiet market, a trader leans with the day's closing location, in proportion to how near the high or the low the day closed, and holds about a week.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-02.b1.b1.d1.g1_choppy-agitated-month-falls-continue_1d (idea 067-02.b1.b1.d1.g1_choppy-agitated-month-falls-continue, brief 067_choppy-market-monthly-move-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: Where a day closes in its own range points the next week's move. On the non-overlapping 7-day grid, the days closing in the top fifth of their range (mean close position 0.91) are followed by +4.22 % beyond the coin's drift (t 2.43 with one day's cases taken together; 277 cases on 95 days), and the Spearman correlation of close position with the 7-day unusual move is +0.081 (p 0.0025, 1391 cases). The correlation is positive on all 10 coins (0.02 to 0.29), in 3 of 4 years (2019 0.18, 2020 0.05, 2021 0.09, 2018 -0.01) and in 7 of 12 windows (w18 0.47, w8 0.41).
- F2: This close-position effect is not cleanly graded and shows only over a week. The middle fifth (close position 0.56) is followed by -2.66 % (t -1.92), below the bottom fifth's -1.53 % (t -0.86). Over 1 day the top fifth reverses slightly (-0.37 %, t -1.75), and over 3 days every fifth is flat (|t| < 1). By the multiple-angle standard it is a candidate, not a strong result.
- F3: The close-position effect is strongest in choppy calm bars. There the rank correlation is +0.41 and the top-minus-bottom gap is +12.1 % over 7 days (60 cases). In rising agitated bars it is +0.25 and +13.3 % (114 cases). In choppy agitated (49 cases), falling agitated (119) and falling calm (166) it is near 0 (+0.03, +0.06, +0.07).
