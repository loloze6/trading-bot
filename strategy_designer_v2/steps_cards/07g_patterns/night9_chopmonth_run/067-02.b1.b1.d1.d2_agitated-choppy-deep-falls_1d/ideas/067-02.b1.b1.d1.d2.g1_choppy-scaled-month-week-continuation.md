Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's 30-day return sits from zero, measured against its own swing over those 30 days, the further it moves the same way over the next 7 days: up after a strong month, down after a weak one, more for a larger month.
Consequence: A trader in a trendless coin trades with its month's move over the following week, long after strong months and short after weak ones, and does not hold the bet for three weeks.
Why:
- F1: In a choppy market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %), the coin's 30-day return points the way of its next 7 days in a graded way on both sides: from the lowest to the highest quintile of the 30-day return, the 7-day move beyond the coin's normal drift is -12.9 % (t -1.85, 15 cases, 7 days), -5.6 % (t -1.99, 17 cases), +0.4 % (45 cases), +5.1 % (t 1.23, 24 cases) and +10.8 % (t 1.81, 8 cases). Outside chop the quintiles show no order (+0.7 %, -3.9 %, -0.9 %, +4.0 %, +0.3 %).
- F2: Scaled by the coin's own 30-day swing, the same order holds in chop over 7 days: the lowest tercile is -8.4 % (t -1.73, 29 cases, 15 days), the middle -1.4 % (55 cases) and the highest +10.2 % (t 2.44, 25 cases, 9 days). In falling markets the terciles show no order (-1.2 %, -0.8 %, +5.1 %, all t below 1.2), and in rising markets only the lowest tercile stands out (-14.6 %, 9 cases).
- F3: Over the tested 21-day horizon, the order breaks down in chop: the scaled terciles give -12.4 % (7 cases), +28.2 % (10 cases) and +10.0 % (11 cases), none with t above 1.3. Over the whole book the 30-day return and the next 21 days correlate at -0.06. The week, not three weeks, carries the continuation.
- F4: The tested short-only book could not pay. The coins drifted upward by 3.70 % per 21 days against the short, which left a total move of +0.14 % per case against a trade cost of 0.22 %. The book was in the profile on only 4.6 % of bars. On its position days, the deepest-fall tercile earned +0.04 % a day, below the middle tercile's +0.37 %, so convex depth did not help.
