A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the deeper a coin's fall over the past 30 days, the more it keeps falling over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader shorts a coin sliding quietly in a falling market in proportion to the month's fall, rather than buying it as an agitated fall would invite.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-01.b1.g1.b1.d1.d2_proportional-month-slide-continues_1d (idea 067-01.b1.g1.b1.d1.d2_proportional-month-slide-continues, brief 067_choppy-market-monthly-move-continues).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a falling, calm market the 30-day move also continues over the next 10 days (Spearman 0.21, p 0.03, 107 cases). Cases after a month fall ended −3.7 % over drift (81) and cases after a rise ended +2.4 % (26), with a signed t of 2.15 over 30 days. In a falling, agitated market the month move instead reverses (Spearman −0.21, p 0.01, 146 cases).
