Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the larger a coin's 30-day return relative to its own daily swing over the past 30 days, the further it moves the same way over the next 7 days, up after a strong month and down after a weak one.
Consequence: In a trendless, quiet coin, a trader leans with the month's move for a week, sized by how large that move is against the coin's usual swing, instead of fading it as range trading would.
Why:
- F1: Inside the choppy profile, a coin's 30-day return scaled by its 30-day daily swing ranks its next 7-day unusual move with a Spearman of 0.276 (215 non-overlapping cases), against 0.028 outside the choppy profile (1004 cases). In choppy, the quintiles of the scaled 30-day return run from -8.9 % (lowest, 18 days) and -4.5 % (second, t -3.57, 35 days) through -0.4 % to +2.5 % and +1.6 % (top two).
- F3: Within choppy, the graded continuation (scaled 30-day return × unusual 7-day move) sits in calm markets: 0.030 with t 2.25 and Spearman 0.28 (159 cases, 61 days). Agitated choppy markets give t 1.10 and Spearman 0.16 (56 cases).
- F5: The same continuation holds with a 14-day lookback: graded t 2.05, Spearman 0.24, 215 cases. It is weaker over a 3-day horizon: graded t 1.55, Spearman 0.10, 520 cases on 214 days. The move builds over the week rather than the next days.
