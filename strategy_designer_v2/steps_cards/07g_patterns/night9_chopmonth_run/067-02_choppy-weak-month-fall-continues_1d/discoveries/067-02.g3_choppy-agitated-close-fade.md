A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, agitated market, the higher a coin closes within its daily range, the more it falls over the next day, and the lower it closes, the more it rises.
What it would mean for a trader: In a trendless but swinging coin, a trader fades the day's close location for one day, shorting high closes, which no held strategy does in this market.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-02_choppy-weak-month-fall-continues_1d (idea 067-02_choppy-weak-month-fall-continues, brief 067_choppy-market-monthly-move-continues).
What it rests on:
- F7: Next-day reversal of a day's close location within its range is clearest in choppy agitated markets: graded (0.5 - close location) × next-day unusual move is 27.2 bp, t 2.81, 435 bars. The other profile pairs give 2.8 to 8.5 bp with t at most 1.64. Across all bars, the next-day unusual move falls from +0.54 % in the lowest-close quintile to -0.39 % in the highest (t 1.62 / -1.74).
