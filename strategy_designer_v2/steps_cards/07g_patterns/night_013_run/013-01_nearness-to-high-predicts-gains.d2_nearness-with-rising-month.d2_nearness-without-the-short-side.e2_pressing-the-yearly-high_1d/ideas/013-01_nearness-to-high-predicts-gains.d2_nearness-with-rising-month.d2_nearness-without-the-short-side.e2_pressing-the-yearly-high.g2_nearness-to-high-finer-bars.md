Idea: The nearer a coin's daily close sits to its highest close of the past 52 weeks, the higher its return over the next 7 days, with the effect concentrated in the closes closest to that high.
Consequence: Being long is worth it mainly when the coin is pressing its 52-week high, not merely when it sits in the upper part of its range.
Why:
- F4: The tested forecast does not sort the 7-day return in a graded way. Its forecast quintiles give excess returns of +0.43%, −2.07% (t −3.43), +0.50%, +0.25% and +0.89% (t 1.16, n 477) for the top. The raw distance-from-high quintiles are flat until the top one, which reaches +1.49% (t 1.87).
