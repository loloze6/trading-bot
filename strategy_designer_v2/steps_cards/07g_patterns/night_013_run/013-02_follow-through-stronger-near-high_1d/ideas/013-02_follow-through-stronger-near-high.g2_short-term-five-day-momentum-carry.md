Idea: A coin's 5-day return carries on in the same direction over the next 10 days, and the larger the 5-day move, the larger the follow-on move.
Consequence: A strong recent week is a reason to hold a position in its direction for the following ten days.
Why:
- F3: A coin's 5-day return predicts its excess return over the next 10 days with slope 0.19, t 4.6, over 1,226 independent cases. This holds jointly with the volume ratio, and its slope against the 30-day return is t 3.6 on 386 cases.
