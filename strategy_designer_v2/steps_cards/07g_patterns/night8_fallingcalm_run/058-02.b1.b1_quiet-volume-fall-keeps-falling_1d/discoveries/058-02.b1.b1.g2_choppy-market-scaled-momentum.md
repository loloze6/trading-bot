A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's 30-day return measured in its own usual daily swings, the more it moves the same way over the next 5 days, up after a rise and down after a fall.
What it would mean for a trader: In a sideways market, a coin's recent 30-day direction is a reason to trade with it, not against it, for the next week.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-02.b1.b1_quiet-volume-fall-keeps-falling_1d (idea 058-02.b1.b1_quiet-volume-fall-keeps-falling, brief 058_quiet-volume-calm-decline-continues).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a choppy market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %), the 30-day return scaled by its swing predicts the next 5 days in the same direction.
- F5: The same scaled-momentum slope is absent in the other trends: falling agitated t -0.27, rising agitated t 0.01, rising calm t 1.51, falling calm t 1.70.
