A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, agitated market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a rising, agitated market, a trader leans long when volume has dried up and stands lighter when volume has grown, the opposite reading of volume from a falling market.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-02.d1_volume-growth-fall-still-running_1d (idea 060-02.d1_volume-growth-fall-still-running, brief 060_volume-dry-up-extends-downtrends).
What it rests on:
- F3: In a rising, agitated market, the 30-day volume growth against the 30 days before predicts a lower 5-day return (slope -0.059, t -2.84, 139 cases), the reverse of the falling market's sign. Outside the falling market, the quintile with the most shrunk volume (log ratio -0.59) is followed by +3.99 % excess, against -0.9 % to -0.1 % for the other four. The effect is carried mostly by 2021 (t -4.34); 2019 is weaker (t -1.55) and 2018 and 2020 are near 0.
