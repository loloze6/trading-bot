A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, a coin's return on its last day, measured against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, continues over the next 5 days: the larger that day's rise, the larger the rise that follows, and the larger that day's fall, the larger the fall that follows.
What it would mean for a trader: In a calm market, a trader leans in the direction of the last day's move, in proportion to how unusual it was, and holds about 5 days, short after sharp down days as well as long after sharp up days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-02.d1_volume-growth-fall-still-running_1d (idea 060-02.d1_volume-growth-fall-still-running, brief 060_volume-dry-up-extends-downtrends).
What it rests on:
- F1: Outside the falling market (the "chop" regime, 60-day return at or above -10 %), the last day's return continues over the next 5 days. The 1-day return has a slope of 0.28 on the 5-day excess return (t 3.75, 991 non-overlapping cases). Scaled by the 30-day swing, the lowest quintile of the move is followed by -2.45 % excess and the highest by +3.43 %, with the quintiles between nearly monotone (-0.13 %, +0.17 %, +0.28 %). Taking the sign of the move, the unusual move is +1.22 % per case, a strength of 0.090 (739 cases).
- F2: This continuation of the scaled day move holds in calm markets (slope t 4.10, 416 cases) more than in agitated ones (t 2.00, 323 cases). It holds in choppy (t 2.51, 127 cases) and rising (t 2.69, 318 cases) markets but is weaker in falling ones (t 1.79, 401 cases). It is positive in 2019, 2020 and 2021 (t 2.77, 2.00, 2.69) but not in 2018 (t 0.15), and on all six coins, though clearly only on XMR, XRP and ZEC (t 2.3 to 3.2).
