Idea: In a rising market, the deeper the coin's fall over the last 20 days, the further it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader in a rising market does not buy a 20-day pullback as a dip; he leans short on it, and harder the deeper it goes.
Why:
- F1: Across all trends, a 20-day fall of 10 to 25 % is followed by a 5-day excess return of -2.57 % (t -3.72, 281 non-overlapping cases), while falls deeper than 25 % are followed by none (-0.03 %, t -0.03, 186 cases). Weighting falls of 0 to 25 % by their depth, continuation shows with t 2.06 over 178 independent days, and it points the same way in 2018 (t 1.80), 2019 (t 1.60) and 2020 (t 0.75) but not in 2021 (t -0.05).
- F2: The continuation after a 20-day fall is largest outside falling markets and is graded by depth there. In a rising market, a 20-day return of 0 to -10 % is followed by +1.02 %, -10 to -25 % by -6.02 %, and below -25 % by -9.96 %. In a choppy market the same bands give -2.55 %, -7.32 % and -11.23 %. In a falling market they give only -2.45 %, -1.79 % and +0.54 %.
