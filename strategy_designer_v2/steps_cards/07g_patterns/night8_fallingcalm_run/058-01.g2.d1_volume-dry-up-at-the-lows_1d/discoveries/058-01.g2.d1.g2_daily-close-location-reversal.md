A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: The closer a coin's close sits to the top of that day's high-low range, the lower its return over the next day, and the closer to the bottom, the higher.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader reads where each day closed in its range as a one-day reversal signal: leaning short after closes at the high and long after closes at the low.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.g2.d1_volume-dry-up-at-the-lows_1d (idea 058-01.g2.d1_volume-dry-up-at-the-lows, brief 058_quiet-volume-calm-decline-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: Across all coins and windows, the next day's excess return falls steadily with where the day closed in its range. Closes in the bottom fifth of the range were followed by +0.515 % (1,666 cases, day-clustered t 2.10) and closes in the top fifth by −0.371 % (t −1.83). The top-minus-bottom gap was negative in 10 of 12 windows and in every year (2018 −1.04 %, 2019 −0.90 %, 2020 −1.92 %, 2021 −0.37 %).
