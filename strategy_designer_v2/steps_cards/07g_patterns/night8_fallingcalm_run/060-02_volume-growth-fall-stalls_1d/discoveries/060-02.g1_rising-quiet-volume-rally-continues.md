A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A rally that keeps going on thinning volume is a reason to stay long, while a rally on swelling volume is a reason to stop adding, because it tends to stall.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-02_volume-growth-fall-stalls_1d (idea 060-02_volume-growth-fall-stalls, brief 060_volume-dry-up-extends-downtrends).
What it rests on:
- F3: In rising markets, outside the tested profile, the 30-day over prior-30-day volume ratio works the opposite way to the tested idea. The 5-day excess return falls with the log ratio (slope -0.0405, t -2.92, 319 grid cases). By volume-ratio tercile the lowest tercile gains +3.52 % (t 2.38, 106 cases), the middle +1.00 % (t 1.23) and the highest -0.47 % (t -0.30). By year the slope is negative in 2019 (t -2.00, 72 cases), 2020 (t -1.01, 120) and 2021 (t -3.92, 119).
