A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger the coin's fall over the past 5 days, the further it falls over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a sideways market treats a sharp 5-day drop as the start of a further decline to short, not as a dip to buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-02.d2.d1_volume-dry-up-short-only_1d (idea 060-02.d2.d1_volume-dry-up-short-only, brief 060_volume-dry-up-extends-downtrends).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a choppy market (60-day return between −10 % and +10 %), the quintile with the largest 5-day fall is followed by a further unusual 5-day move of −10.54 % (t −4.11, 26 cases), while the other four quintiles lie between −0.26 % and +1.86 % (|t| ≤ 1.11). Next day, a choppy calm close in the lowest tenth of the range is followed by −2.87 % (t −3.06, 19 cases, 16 days). The choppy short cells are empty or thin on the map (choppy agitated short: 0; choppy calm short: 1), but the cases are few.
