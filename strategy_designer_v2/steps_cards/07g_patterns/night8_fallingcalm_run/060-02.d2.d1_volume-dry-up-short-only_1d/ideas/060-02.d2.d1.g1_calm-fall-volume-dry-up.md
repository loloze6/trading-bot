Idea: In a falling, calm market, while the coin's 10-day return is still negative, the more its trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader keeps the volume dry-up short to falling markets that are still calm, where thin selling drifts on, and stays out of it once the fall turns agitated and starts to rebound.
Why:
- F1: In a falling market (60-day return below −10 %) on the 5-day grid of folds B and C, the tested situation stands out clearly: a negative 10-day return with shrinking 30-day volume is followed by an unusual 5-day move of −3.71 % (t −4.25, 147 cases), and the most-shrunk volume quintile by −3.59 % (t −2.76, 81 cases), while the growing-volume quintiles are +1.81 % (t 1.73) and +0.49 % (t 0.32); this is the idea itself, not a new pattern.
- F2: The continuation is stronger in calm falling markets than in agitated ones: with shrinking volume and a negative 10-day return, calm gives −4.07 % over 5 days (t −2.03 with cases of one day together, 79 cases, 34 days) against −3.28 % for agitated (t −1.39, 68 cases); by the coin's own 30-day swing, the falling market's calmest tercile drifts −2.45 % over 5 days (t −2.62, 101 cases), the middle −1.02 % (t −1.10) and the most agitated +0.95 % (t 1.02).
- F3: Day by day, a calm falling market also continues down after middling closes: the next day's unusual return is −0.83 % after a close at 20–40 % of the day's range (t −2.07, 199 cases, 120 days) and −1.05 % after 40–60 % (t −2.51, 162 cases); over 2 days, closes above 40 % of the range are followed by −1.13 % (t −1.97, 232 cases). An agitated falling market instead rebounds after a low close, +1.99 % (t 2.62, 146 cases) and grows with range/swing to +3.46 % (t 2.29), and after a fall of more than one daily swing, +2.03 % (t 2.22). That rebound is already held by 039-02, 044-01, 045-01 and 047-01.
