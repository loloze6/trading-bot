Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the deeper a coin's 30-day fall measured against its usual 30-day move, the more it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: After a sharp month-long drop that has erased a prior rise while the coin swings more than usual, a trader has reason to short, and to refuse a dip-buy, more firmly the deeper the drop.
Why:
- F1: In the choppy, agitated market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %, 30-day swing above its median), the deeper a coin's 30-day fall measured in its usual 30-day moves, the lower its drift-removed 5-day return. The Spearman correlation is 0.55 (p < 0.001, 46 non-overlapping cases). By tercile, the deepest falls averaged -14.1 % (t -2.32 with cases of one day together, 10 days), the middle -4.2 % (t -2.04, 15 days) and the shallowest +1.0 % (t 1.00). Of the 48 profile-by-predictor correlations examined, this is the only one under p 0.001, and the profile map has no short strategy in this profile.
- F2: Overall, the choppy, agitated grid cases fell 5.6 % beyond the coins' drift over 5 days (t -3.36, 46 cases), compared with +0.8 % in choppy calm and -1.3 % in falling calm. Five of six coins were negative (XMR -6.9 %, t -2.24; XRP -7.3 %; BTC -5.6 %), as was every year from 2018 to 2021.
