A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, the more a coin's volume over its last 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the further it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In any downtrend, calm or agitated, volume drying up is a graded reason to stay short or refuse a long, and growing volume is a warning the fall may stall.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-01.g2_volume-drying-decline-continues_1d (idea 058-01.g2_volume-drying-decline-continues, brief 058_quiet-volume-calm-decline-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: In a falling market (60-day return below -10 %, calm or agitated), a shrinking 30-day volume against the 30 days before is followed by the coin carrying on its 30-day direction. The 5-day unusual move, signed by that direction, averages +2.96 % (t 2.57 with each day's cases taken together; 194 cases on 66 days). When volume grew instead, it averages -1.74 % (t -1.01; 175 cases on 61 days).
- F2: Split by thirds of the volume ratio, falling-market grid cells in the most-shrunk third have a 5-day unusual return of -2.31 % (t -2.44, 134 cases). The middle third shows +0.19 % (t 0.25) and the growing third +0.59 % (t 0.51). The continuation sits in the dry-up, not in the fall alone.
- F3: Shrinking volume also comes with continuation outside falling markets. In rising markets the signed move is +3.14 % (t 2.02, 61 days) and in choppy ones +4.41 % (t 2.03, 40 days), against +1.80 % (t 1.28) and +2.18 % (t 1.72) when volume grew. This goes against the brief's claim that the effect lives only in the calm downtrend.
