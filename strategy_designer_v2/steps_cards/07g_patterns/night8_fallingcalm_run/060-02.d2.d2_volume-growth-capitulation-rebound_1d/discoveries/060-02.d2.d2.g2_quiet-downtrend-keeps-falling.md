A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, the calmer a coin's daily swings over the past 30 days have been against its past year, the larger its further fall over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader keeps or adds to shorts in quiet downtrends and avoids treating a calm fall as a bottom, a side the profile map leaves almost empty.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-02.d2.d2_volume-growth-capitulation-rebound_1d (idea 060-02.d2.d2_volume-growth-capitulation-rebound, brief 060_volume-dry-up-extends-downtrends).
What it rests on:
- F3: Calmness alone also grades the falling-market drift. In the coin's low, middle and high 30-day-swing terciles, the 5-day unusual move is -2.51 % (t -2.74, 104 cases), -0.96 % and +1.07 %.
- F2: The dry-up continuation is concentrated in the coin's calmer stretches. In falling markets, the two driest volume quintiles in the lowest 30-day-swing tercile give -3.93 % (25 cases, 14 days, t -1.24) and -4.33 % (30 cases, 20 days, t -1.86). The same quintiles in the highest tercile give -3.24 % and +1.32 %.
