A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the higher a coin's return over the last 20 days, the higher its return over the next 5 days, and the lower that 20-day return, the lower the next 5 days' return.
What it would mean for a trader: In a coin going nowhere over two months, its last-month direction is a reason to trade the next week the same way, on either side.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 064-01_rising-agitated-volume-shrink-rises_1d (idea 064-01_rising-agitated-volume-shrink-rises, brief 064_rising-agitated-volume-dry-up-rises).
What it rests on:
- F1: Among bars in the choppy 60-day trend (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %), the coin's 20-day return predicts its excess 5-day return. The slope is +4.5 % per standard deviation, with t 4.65 on 125 non-overlapping cases, or t 2.51 with the cases of one day taken together (65 days). It stands out among the roughly 90 regressions examined.
- F2: Among bars whose 20-day return lies within ±10 %, the excess 5-day return rises across the 20-day-return quintiles: -1.85 %, -0.37 %, +0.39 %, +0.87 %, +4.60 % (about 103 cases each). The slope is positive in every year: t_day 0.96 in 2018, 1.36 in 2019, 2.11 in 2020 and 1.73 in 2021. It holds whether the coin swings more than usual (t_day 2.35, 92 cases) or less (t_day 2.20, 210 cases).
