A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose 5-day fall is large against its 20-day daily swing keeps falling over the next 3 days, and the larger the fall in those swing units, the larger the further fall.
What it would mean for a trader: In a calm decline, a trader should side with a multi-day slide that is large for the coin's usual swing, sized by that slide, for about three days, rather than buy it expecting a rebound.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 064-01.g1.b1_calm-decline-big-rise-next-day-fade_1d (idea 064-01.g1.b1_calm-decline-big-rise-next-day-fade, brief 064_falling-calm-scaled-rallies-fade).
What it rests on:
- F1: In the falling calm profile, the coin's 5-day return measured in units of its 20-day daily swing tends to continue over the next 3 days. On the 3-day grid the signed unusual move is +1.51 % with a day-grouped t of 3.02 (537 cases, 194 days). Outside the profile it is +0.32 % with t 0.89 (1724 cases, 385 days). The effect is positive on all six coins (BTC +0.58 %, ETH +2.55 %, LTC +1.36 %, XMR +1.45 %, XRP +1.07 %, ZEC +1.86 %) and in 3 of 4 years (2018 +2.42 % t 2.73, 2019 +0.59 %, 2021 +2.78 % t 2.79). The exception is 2020, at −1.87 % with t −1.78 on only 43 cases.
- F2: The continuation in F1 comes mainly from falls, and it is graded. After a negative scaled 5-day return in falling calm, the coin's 3-day unusual move is −2.01 % with t −2.84 (322 cases, 139 days). After a positive one it is +0.75 % with t 0.99. By size of the 5-day fall:
- F3: Large down days do not rebound inside falling calm, unlike elsewhere. In profile, a next day after a fall beyond 2 swings shows −0.76 % (t −1.19, 79 cases). Out of profile the same case shows +2.99 % (t 4.55, 104 cases), and −2 to −1 swings shows +0.87 % (t 3.20, 502 cases), positive in all four years (+0.36 % to +1.52 %). That out-of-profile rebound is the agitated fall rebound the map already holds heavily (16 long strategies in falling agitated), so it is no discovery.
