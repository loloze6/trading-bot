## Findings
- **F1:** In the falling calm profile, the next-day excess return after an up close falls steadily with the depth of the down run before it, counted in usual daily moves: −0.42 % at 1–2 moves (t −0.95, 89 days), −0.89 % at 2–3 (t −1.47, 59 days), −1.42 % at 3–5 (t −1.89, 47 days) and −3.62 % at 5 or more (t −3.53, 25 days); after up closes with no down run before them it is +0.13 % (t 0.36, 184 days).
- **F2:** On falling calm up days, a regression of the next-day excess return on the run depth and on the up day's own size gives a depth slope of −0.48 % per usual move (t −5.81) and a size slope of −0.31 % (t −1.95); the depth carries the fade, not how large the relief day is.
- **F3:** The fade after deep runs (3 or more usual moves, falling calm) lasts past the next day: over the next two days the excess return is −2.52 % (t −3.01, 106 cases, 61 days), against about −1.4 % to −3.6 % over one day.
- **F4:** The deep-run fade is uneven across years: 2018 −3.75 % (t −4.05, 33 days), 2019 −0.46 % (t −0.31), 2020 −0.70 % (t −0.54, 4 days), 2021 −1.36 % (t −1.56, 9 days); the book's trades show the same split (2018 52 trades at +3.28 % mean, 2019 36 at −0.01 %).
- **F5:** Outside falling calm, the same deep relief day fades in a falling trend (−5.44 %, t −2.79, 15 days, which is falling agitated), less in a choppy trend (−1.87 %, t −1.08) and not in a rising one (+1.03 %, t 0.96). The falling cell is already held (074-01.b1.d1.d1 and 074-01.b1.g1, falling, short), so it is not a discovery.
- **F6:** Mirror pattern outside falling calm: a down day that breaks a run of up days is followed by a next-day excess rise that grows with the run's gain in usual moves (slope +0.18 % per move, t 2.67, 1728 cases). After runs of 2 or more moves the rise is +0.81 % to +1.73 % in every year 2018–2021 (2020 t 2.56), and +2.94 % in a falling trend (t 2.40, 30 days).
- **F7:** Falling calm pulls the other way for short up runs: a down day after an up run of under 1 usual move is followed by −0.88 % (t −2.44, 134 days). After up runs of 3 to 5 moves it is +0.26 % (t 0.44), and after 5 or more +1.23 % (t 1.44, 19 days), so the rebound after long up runs points the same way there but cannot be told from 0.
- **F8:** Weekday and the day's own move show nothing that stands out once the many looks are counted. Falling calm by weekday ranges from −1.17 % on Monday (t −1.92) to +0.47 % on Saturday (t 1.59). Large one-day falls outside the profile rebound (+3.15 %, t 2.17), which the 16 held falling agitated long books already cover.

## Ideas
- `070-01.b1.b1.b1.g1_deep-calm-relief-two-day-fade` (from F1, F2, F3): In a falling, calm market, a day that closes up after an unbroken run of down days is followed by a fall over the next two days. The more usual daily moves that run of down days took off the price, the larger that two-day fall.
