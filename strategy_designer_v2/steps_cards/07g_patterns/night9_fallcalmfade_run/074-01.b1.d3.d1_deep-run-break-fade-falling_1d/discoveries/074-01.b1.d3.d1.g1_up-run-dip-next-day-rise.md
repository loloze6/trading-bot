A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: The first down day after a run of consecutive up days is followed by a rise in the coin's close over the next day. The higher the run's gain (its return over the up days against its usual daily swing over the last 30 days), the larger that rise; after shallow runs it is about nothing.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader buys the first down close that interrupts a strong up run, sized by how many usual daily swings the run gained, and holds for one day, instead of reading the dip as the end of the run.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
