A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, an up day that follows a down day is followed by a fall over the next day, and that fall is larger the larger the up day's rise against the coin's usual daily swing over the last 30 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a falling coin should fade the first up day after a down day more heavily the stronger that bounce is, and not fade up days that follow other up days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 070-01.g1.d2.d2_falling-market-depth-run-break-fade_1d (idea 070-01.g1.d2.d2_falling-market-depth-run-break-fade, brief 070_falling-calm-run-break-fade).

- F2: In a falling trend, the prior day's direction is what matters.
