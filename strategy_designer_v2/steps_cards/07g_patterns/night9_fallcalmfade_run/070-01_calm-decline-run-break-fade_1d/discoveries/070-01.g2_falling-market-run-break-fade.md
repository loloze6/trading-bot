A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, an up day that ends a run of down days over the past days is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the longer that run of down days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader can fade the first up day after several down days whenever the coin's 60-day trend is down, whether the market is calm or agitated, a short side the map holds only thinly.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 070-01_calm-decline-run-break-fade_1d (idea 070-01_calm-decline-run-break-fade, brief 070_falling-calm-run-break-fade).
What it rests on:
- F3: The fall after a run of 2 or more down days is not confined to falling calm. In falling agitated markets it is -1.15 % (t -1.65, 145 cases), against -1.58 % (t -2.65, 108 cases) in falling calm. Outside the book's profile, 2-day runs give -1.06 % (t -4.43, 426 cases). In rising markets it vanishes: +0.89 % in rising calm (87 cases) and -0.09 % in rising agitated (89 cases).
- F1: Across all coins and profiles, an up day that ends a run of down days is followed by a next-day unusual fall that depends on the run's length. After a 1-day run the fall is only -0.20 % (t -0.85, 1236 cases, 463 days). After a 2-day run it is -1.03 % (t -3.07, 551 cases, 263 days), and after runs of 5 days or more it is -2.37 % (t -3.53, 76 cases, 56 days); these t take the cases of one day together.
- F4: The fall after a run of 2 or more down days holds on every coin, between -0.55 % and -1.23 %. It reaches t -2.4 to -3.1 on five coins (LTC: t -1.19). It is negative in all four years, from -2.02 % in 2018 (t -3.76) to -0.47 % in 2020 (t -0.96), and in 11 of 12 windows (the exception is w11, +0.90 %).
- F5: This move is about 5 to 8 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade. The run-2+ cases move about -1.0 % per case unusual, against +0.20 % for the tested idea's case mix. The tested book earned +1.3 % a year, and almost all of its gain came from two windows, w7 (+0.10 %/day) and w14 (+0.07 %/day); the other windows were near 0 or negative.
