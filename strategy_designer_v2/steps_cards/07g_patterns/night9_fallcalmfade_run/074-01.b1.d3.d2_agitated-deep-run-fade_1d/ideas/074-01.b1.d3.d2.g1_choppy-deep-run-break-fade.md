Idea: In a choppy market, the first up day after a run of consecutive down days is followed by a fall in the coin's close over the next day. That fall grows faster than the run's depth (the run's loss over the down days against the coin's usual daily move over the last 30 days), concentrated after the deepest runs.
Consequence: A trader can extend the convex run-break short from downtrends to sideways coins, a box where no strategy is short today, and size it on the run's depth.
Why:
- F1: The fade after a run-ending up day grows with the run's depth (the run's loss over the mean absolute daily move of 30 days), and the growth is convex in every market, not only in the tested profile. Over all run breaks of 2 or more down days, the next day's unusual move by depth tercile is -0.19 % (t -0.77), -0.52 % (t -1.75) and -1.75 % (t -5.61), with 288 cases per tercile. Outside the falling agitated profile alone, the terciles read -0.06 % (t -0.23), -0.21 % (t -0.70) and -1.61 % (t -4.72), with 243 cases each.
