Idea: Among coins that have risen over the past 28 days, the closer the day's close is to the highest close of those 28 days, the larger the further rise over the next 2 days.
Consequence: A trader would buy uptrending coins at their 28-day high for a short 2-day hold, more so the nearer the high, and refuse quick longs on coins pulled back from it.
Why:
- F4: Among 28-day rises, the closing-high proximity predicts the 2-day excess return more surely than the tested 5-day one: slope +0.133, t 3.47 over 638 cases, against step 5's t 1.65. Over all regimes, the top tenth of proximity (mean 0.9996) gained +4.23 % over 5 days (t 3.06), while tenths 2 to 4 (proximity 0.76 to 0.86) lost 1.9 % to 3.9 % (t -1.90 to -3.96).
