Idea: The lower a coin's mean volume over the past 5 days relative to its mean volume over the past 28 days, the lower its return over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader should refuse to go long a coin whose trading is drying up, more firmly the more it has dried up.
Why:
- F4: When a coin's 5-day mean volume is low against its 28-day mean, its next 5 days are weak. The lowest fifth of that ratio (0.19 to 0.64) is followed by an excess return of -2.52 % (t -3.79, 295 cases), against +1.17 % (t 0.76) in the highest fifth. The slope of excess return on the ratio has a t of 2.62 (1475 cases).
