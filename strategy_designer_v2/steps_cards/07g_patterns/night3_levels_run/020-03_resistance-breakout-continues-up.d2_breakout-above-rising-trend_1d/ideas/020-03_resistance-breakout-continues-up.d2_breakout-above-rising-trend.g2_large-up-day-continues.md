Idea: After a day on which a coin rises by much more than its 20-day volatility, it tends to keep rising over the next 5 days, and the larger that day's rise relative to its volatility, the larger the 5-day rise.
Consequence: A trader would lean long after a coin's unusually large up days and refuse to fade them over the following week.
Why:
- F4: After a day whose rise is large against the coin's 20-day volatility (top fifth), the next 5 days beat the coin's normal drift by +1.63 % (t 3.24, 570 cases). The other four fifths range from -0.65 % to +0.02 %, and the overall Spearman is only 0.022 (2843 cases), so the effect sits in the largest days, not along a slope.
