Idea: In the two hours that follow 20:00 UTC each day, the coin rises more than its normal drift. The rise is larger the larger the coin's typical hourly move over the past 30 days.
Consequence: A trader would avoid opening shorts just before 20:00 UTC and favour holding or adding longs through the next two hours, more so in volatile months.
Why:
- F1: Across all bars, the hours opening at 20:00 and 21:00 UTC end above the coin's normal drift: +3.7 bp an hour on BTC (t 3.81, 5837 hours) and +4.8 bp on ETH (t 3.92, 5837 hours). The effect is positive in 7 of 8 years and larger in the high-volatility years (2020 +7.2 bp, t 2.93; 2021 +7.7 bp, t 3.02) than in the calmer 2023 (+2.8 bp, t 1.93). It is positive on all seven weekdays (+3.4 to +5.3 bp). Over its two hours it is about 9 bp, still below the 0.11 % a trade costs.
