Idea: A coin's price tends to rise over the two hours starting 20:00 UTC, more strongly the larger the coin's usual hourly move over the past 30 days.
Consequence: A trader would time long entries before 20:00 UTC rather than after, and refuse to open shorts going into those hours.
Why:
- F3: The hours from 20:00 to 22:00 UTC carry an unusual 1-hour rise of +3.7 bp for BTC (t 3.81, 5837 hours) and +4.8 bp for ETH (t 3.92). The hours from 00:00 to 04:00 UTC carry an unusual fall of -2.0 bp (t -3.24) and -2.4 bp (t -3.02). Both stand out among 48 hour-by-coin readings.
- F4: The 20:00 to 22:00 UTC rise appears in 7 of 8 years (2022 is the exception, at -3.1 bp, t -1.45). It strengthens in later window groups: +0.5 / -0.9 bp in w1–w6, and +4.4 / +6.6 bp (t 3.31 / 3.45) in w19–w24. Its size of about 8 to 10 bp over the two hours is below the 0.11 % cost of a trade.
