Idea: When a coin's 30-day hourly volatility is low against its own history, its next 6 hours continue its past 6-hour return, and the larger that return in usual 6-hour moves, the larger the continuation.
Consequence: In calm markets a trader can lean with the last six hours' move, sized by how unusual that move was.
Why:
- F3: How the next 6 hours follow the past 6-hour return depends on the coin's 30-day hourly volatility.
