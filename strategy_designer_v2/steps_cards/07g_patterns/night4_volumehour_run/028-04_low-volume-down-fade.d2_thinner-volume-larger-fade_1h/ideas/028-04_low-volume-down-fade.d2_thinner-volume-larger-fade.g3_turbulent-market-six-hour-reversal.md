Idea: When a coin's 30-day hourly volatility is high against its own history, its next 6 hours move against its past 6-hour return, and the larger that return in usual 6-hour moves, the larger the reversal.
Consequence: In turbulent markets a trader should fade, not chase, an unusually large six-hour move.
Why:
- F3: How the next 6 hours follow the past 6-hour return depends on the coin's 30-day hourly volatility.
