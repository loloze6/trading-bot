Idea: A coin's 24-hour return, measured in multiples of its typical 30-day move, is followed by a move in the opposite direction over the next 24 hours, larger the more extreme the past 24-hour return.
Consequence: A day of unusually large gains is a reason to refuse a long for the next day, and a day of unusually large losses a reason to refuse a short.
Why:
- F5: A coin's past 24-hour return, scaled by its typical move, is followed by the opposite unusual move over the next 24 hours. The pooled slope is -0.0016 (t -4.30, n 5312 non-overlapping days) and is negative in all four coin × half splits (t from -0.79 to -2.81). The lowest fifth of past returns gains +0.38 % (t 2.48) and the top two fifths lose 0.16-0.22 %.
