Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical 30-day hourly move, the price rebounds over the next four hours, and the heavier that hour's volume relative to its 30-day average, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A heavy-volume hourly sell-off is a reason to refuse a short, or to buy, for the next few hours.
Why:
- F4: Large hourly falls are followed by rebounds whose size grows with the hour's volume ratio to its 30-day mean, which runs against the brief's continuation. The unusual 4-hour return is -0.08 % at a ratio up to 1, -0.03 % at 1-2, +0.14 % at 2-4, +0.42 % at 4-8 (t 2.14, n 308) and +0.49 % above 8 (n 96).
