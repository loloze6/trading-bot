A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day move measured in units of its daily swing over the previous 20 days, the further it moves the same way over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader takes a sharp single day that stands out against a quiet coin's own noise as the start of a move to follow for about a week.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01_swing-scaled-rise-continues_1d (idea 059-01_swing-scaled-rise-continues, brief 059_swing-scaled-weeks-move-continues-anywhere).
What it rests on:
- F6: A single day's move measured against the coin's 20-day swing also continues over the next 5 days.
