A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: A coin that rises on a day while its close still sits low in its 20-day high-low range falls over the next day, and the lower that close sits in the 20-day range, the larger the fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader fades one-day rises that have not lifted the coin off the bottom of its recent range, a short side the profile map barely holds outside falling agitated.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-01.g1.d2.d1_own-range-close-height-fade_1d (idea 060-01.g1.d2.d1_own-range-close-height-fade, brief 060_falling-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F5: Outside the falling agitated profile, an up day that leaves the coin low in its 20-day high-low range falls the next day, graded from low to high. The lowest tercile of 20-day position gives -0.76 % (t -2.92, 414 days, 1001 cases), the middle -0.10 % (t -0.54) and the highest +0.32 % (t 1.21).
