A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, agitated market, the higher a coin closes within its own day's high-low range, the larger its fall over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader takes a graded short after high closes when the coin has gone nowhere over 60 days but has swung more than usual, a box no strategy we hold trades short.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 069-02.d3_below-high-up-day-two-day-fade_1d (idea 069-02.d3_below-high-up-day-two-day-fade, brief 069_low-in-range-rise-fades).
