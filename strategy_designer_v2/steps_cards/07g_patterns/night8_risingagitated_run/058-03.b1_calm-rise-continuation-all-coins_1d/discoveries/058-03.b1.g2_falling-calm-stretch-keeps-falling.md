A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the further a coin's close stands below its 20-day average price, measured in the coin's daily swings over the last 30 days, the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a quiet decline, a coin stretched below its average is a short to hold, not a rebound to buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
