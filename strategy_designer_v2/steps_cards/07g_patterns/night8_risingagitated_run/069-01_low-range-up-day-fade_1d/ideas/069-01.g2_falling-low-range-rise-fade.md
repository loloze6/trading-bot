Idea: In a falling market, when a coin closes up on the day, the lower that close sits within its high-low range of the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: In a downtrend, a rebound day that still closes near the bottom of the recent range is sold the next day, and should be faded more the deeper it closes.
Why:
- F1: Among up days in folds B–C, the next-day excess return falls steadily as the close sits lower in its 20-day high-low range: −0.91 % (t −2.25, 738 cases, 303 days) in the lowest fifth, −0.54 % (t −2.05) in the second, −0.17 % and −0.12 % in the middle fifths, and +0.25 % (t 0.75) in the top fifth; without the up-day condition the low fifths show only −0.12 % and −0.34 % (t −0.34, −1.61), so the up day carries the effect.
- F2: The step 5 reject conflicts with its own size line: the size says the short case gained +0.54 % unusual move (t 2.93, 911 days), and the book's short bars gained +0.21 % (choppy) and +0.39 % (falling) per bar; the main measure (excess × −forecast/10 = −0.39 %, t −2.71) rewards a rise on a short case and looks inverted, so the theme's effect appears to be in the data.
- F3: The effect lives outside rising markets: low-range up days (lowest two fifths) give next-day excess −0.75 % (t −2.10, 725 cases, 309 days) in choppy markets and −0.93 % (t −1.96, 563 cases, 193 days) in falling ones, but +0.01 % (t 0.01, 188 cases) in rising ones, where the book's short bars lost −0.08 % per bar.
- F4: Outside rising markets the grading holds across all five fifths of range position: −0.99 % (t −2.22), −0.67 % (t −2.27), −0.40 % (t −1.82), −0.18 % (t −0.64), −0.10 % (t −0.24); in rising markets there is no order (−0.29 %, +0.25 %, +0.58 %, −0.01 %, +0.78 %).
- F5: Outside rising markets the fade is broad: negative in 10 of 12 windows and on all six coins (BTC −1.03 % t −2.79, ZEC −1.22 % t −3.28, XMR −0.94 % t −2.57, LTC −0.82 % t −2.30, ETH −0.68 % t −1.84, XRP −0.37 % t −0.91), and it does not reverse on the second day (choppy −0.88 % t −1.76, falling −0.94 % t −1.49 over two days).
