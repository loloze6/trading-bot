A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's rise on the day, measured in units of its 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader sells into sharp up days of a coin that is falling and swinging more than usual, and sizes the short with the size of the rise.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 069-02.d1_falling-market-below-high-up-day-fade_1d (idea 069-02.d1_falling-market-below-high-up-day-fade, brief 069_low-in-range-rise-fades).
What it rests on:
- F2: Inside the falling market this reversal lives in the agitated half. Agitated up days are followed by -0.76 % excess (n 776, t -3.74, t with same-day cases summed -1.95, 274 days) and agitated down days by +0.81 % (n 832, t 4.13, t_day 2.16). Calm up days are followed by -0.32 % (t_day -1.13) and calm down days by -0.25 % (t_day -0.93), so there is no reversal there.
- F3: Graded, in the falling agitated market the size of the day's move in units of its 20-day swing ranks against the next day's excess with Spearman -0.170 (n 1608, p 7e-12). Every other profile is weaker: falling calm -0.044 (p 0.13), rising agitated -0.019 (p 0.61). On agitated falling up days the larger two terciles of the rise give -0.96 % and -0.89 % against -0.43 % for the smallest (n about 259 each).
- F1: In a falling market (60-day return below -10 %), the next day runs against the day's own move whether or not the close sits deep below the high: up days are followed by a short-direction excess of +0.50 % (n 1578, t 3.75) and down days by a long excess of +0.41 % (n 1852, t 3.31); outside the falling market the same split is +0.15 % (t 1.37) and +0.18 % (t 1.50).
