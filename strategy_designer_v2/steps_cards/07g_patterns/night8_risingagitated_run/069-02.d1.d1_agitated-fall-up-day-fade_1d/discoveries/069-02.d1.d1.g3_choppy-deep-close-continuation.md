A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close sits below its 20-day high, measured in units of its 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next day, whether the day itself closed up or down.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a sideways market shorts coins pressed deep below their recent high, which is a breakdown continuation in the empty choppy short boxes, not a rebound.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 069-02.d1.d1_agitated-fall-up-day-fade_1d (idea 069-02.d1.d1_agitated-fall-up-day-fade, brief 069_low-in-range-rise-fades).
What it rests on:
- F5: In choppy markets a close deep below the 20-day high is followed by a further fall, whichever way the day closed. The deepest quintile gives -1.75 % after up days (58 cases) and -1.99 % after down days (54 cases). For down days the quintiles climb steadily to +0.86 % at the shallowest. The cases are few, with slope t 1.19 for up days and 1.13 for down days.
