A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, a day that closes down with a high-low range wide against the coin's 30-day daily-return swing is followed by a rise over the next 3 days, and the rise is larger the wider the day's range against that swing.
What it would mean for a trader: A violent down day inside an uptrend is a shakeout to buy rather than the start of a fall, more so the wider it is.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 043-01_falling-market-narrow-day-rise_1d (idea 043-01_falling-market-narrow-day-rise, brief 043_quiet-days-mark-downtrend-exhaustion).
What it rests on:
- F5: Outside the falling profile, the widest fifth of days is followed by +2.01 % excess over 3 days (278 cases on 132 days, day-clustered t 2.21). The whole gain comes from wide days that closed down: +4.27 % on 126 cases, against +0.14 % on 152 wide up days.
- F6: The wide-day rebound outside falling markets is strongest in rising markets: +3.42 % in the widest fifth when the 60-day return is above +10 %, against +0.55 % otherwise. It is positive on all six coins (+0.9 % to +4.7 %) and in all four years (+0.4 % to +3.0 %). Inside the falling profile, wide days are followed by −0.89 %.
