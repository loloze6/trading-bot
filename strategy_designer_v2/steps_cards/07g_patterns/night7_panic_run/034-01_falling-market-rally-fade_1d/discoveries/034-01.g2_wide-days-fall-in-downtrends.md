A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, a coin's wide day is followed by a decline over the next 3 days, and the wider that day's high-low range in units of the coin's 30-day daily swing, the larger the decline.
What it would mean for a trader: In a downtrend, an unusually wide day, whichever way it closes, is a reason to be short for the next few days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 034-01_falling-market-rally-fade_1d (idea 034-01_falling-market-rally-fade, brief 034_squeezed-rallies-fade-in-falling-markets).
What it rests on:
- F4: The day's high-low range in swing units predicts the next 3 days with opposite signs by trend. In falling markets the slope is -1.58 % per unit (t -2.19, 673 cases, 160 days). In rising markets it is +1.52 % per unit (t 2.41, 531 cases, 137 days).
