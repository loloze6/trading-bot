A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: A coin's rise over its last 3 days, measured in units of its 30-day daily swing, is followed by a further rise over the next 5 days, and the larger that scaled rise the larger the gain that follows.
What it would mean for a trader: A sharp multi-day rally is a reason to be long the coin for the following week, not to fade it, whatever its trend.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 034-01_falling-market-rally-fade_1d (idea 034-01_falling-market-rally-fade, brief 034_squeezed-rallies-fade-in-falling-markets).
What it rests on:
- F2: Large up days are followed by further rises, not by a fade. Over the next 5 days, the slope of the return on the day's scaled rise is +1.13 % per unit (t 2.06, 1422 cases, 256 days). Days above 2 swings are followed by +6.41 % above the coin's normal drift (t 2.05, 38 cases on 26 days), which is about 34 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
- F3: The 3-day rise in swing units also predicts a 5-day rise of +1.32 % per unit (t 2.26, 1422 cases, 256 days). The slope points the same way in every trend: rising +2.26 % (t 2.38), choppy +3.75 % (t 2.01), falling +1.61 % (t 1.46). At the 3-day horizon it is also positive in all three trends (t 0.46 to 1.15).
- F1: The tested effect is not in folds B and C. In a falling market (60-day return below -10 %), the slope of the next 3-day return on the day's rise in 30-day swing units is -0.80 % per unit with a day-clustered t of -1.14 (673 cases, 160 days). The tested book's 1434 shorts lost a mean of 2.11 % each after fees, and 9 of the 12 windows had a negative mean daily return.
