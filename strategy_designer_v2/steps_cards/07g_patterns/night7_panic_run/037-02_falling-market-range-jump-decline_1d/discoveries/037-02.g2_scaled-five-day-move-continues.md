A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: A coin's 5-day return, measured in units of its 30-day daily swing, is followed over the next 3 days by a move the same way. The larger that scaled 5-day move, the larger the move that follows.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader sides with a coin's recent volatility-scaled 5-day move for the next 3 days, rather than fading it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 037-02_falling-market-range-jump-decline_1d (idea 037-02_falling-market-range-jump-decline, brief 037_wide-days-precede-decline-in-falling).
What it rests on:
- F4: A coin's 5-day return in units of its 30-day daily swing continues over the next 3 days, a pattern the tested idea does not explain. The slope of the 3-day excess on it is positive in all 4 years (2018 t 2.31, 2019 t 0.11, 2020 t 1.51, 2021 t 0.52) and all 6 coins (t from 0.43 for ZEC to 2.06 for XRP). It is strongest in a choppy market, slope 0.269 with t 3.92 on 218 cases (OLS, not day-clustered), where the bottom quintile is followed by -6.8 % with t -2.41 on 27 days.
- F5: Across windows the 5-day continuation is unstable: slopes are positive in 7 of 12 windows, and only w14 (t 1.96) and w16 (t 1.95) come near t 2.
