A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, after a down day, the coin rises over the next 3 days, and the larger that day's fall measured in its 30-day daily swing, the larger the rise.
What it would mean for a trader: In a coin that has climbed for two months, a sharp down day is a dip to buy for a few days rather than a reason to sell.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 032-01_monthly-low-break-cascade_1d (idea 032-01_monthly-low-break-cascade, brief 032_monthly-low-in-falling-agitated).
What it rests on:
- F5: In a rising market (60-day return above +10 %), a down day of 1 to 2 daily swings is followed by an unusual 3-day move of +7.18 % (t 2.97, 49 cases on 21 days) and an unusual 2-day move of +3.39 % (t 2.66). A down day beyond 2 swings gives +2.40 % (t 2.49, 7 cases on 6 days). Down days in falling or choppy markets show no such rebound (-1.48 %, t -0.71).
