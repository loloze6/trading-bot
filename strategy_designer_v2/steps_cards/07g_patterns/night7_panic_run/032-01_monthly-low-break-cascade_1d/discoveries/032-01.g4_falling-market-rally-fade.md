A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, after an up day, the coin falls over the next 3 days, and the larger that day's rise measured in its 30-day daily swing, the larger the fall.
What it would mean for a trader: In a coin that has dropped for two months, a sharp up day is a rally to fade for a few days, not a turn to chase.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 032-01_monthly-low-break-cascade_1d (idea 032-01_monthly-low-break-cascade, brief 032_monthly-low-in-falling-agitated).
What it rests on:
- F6: In a falling market, an up day larger than one daily swing is followed by an unusual 3-day move of -3.74 % (t -2.11, 64 cases on 26 days). In a choppy market the same up day gives -2.10 % (t -2.36, 32 cases on 22 days). In a rising market it gives +1.31 % (t 0.93).
