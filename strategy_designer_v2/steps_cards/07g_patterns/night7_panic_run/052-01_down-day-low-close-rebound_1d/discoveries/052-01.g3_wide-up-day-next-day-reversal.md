A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: After an up day, a coin falls over the next day, more so the wider that day's high-low range was as a share of its close.
What it would mean for a trader: A wide up day is a reason to cut or fade a long over the next day rather than to chase it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 052-01_down-day-low-close-rebound_1d (idea 052-01_down-day-low-close-rebound, brief 052_down-day-low-close-rebound).
