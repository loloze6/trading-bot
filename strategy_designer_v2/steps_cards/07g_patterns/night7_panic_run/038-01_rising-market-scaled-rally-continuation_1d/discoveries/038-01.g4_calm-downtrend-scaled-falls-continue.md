A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, a coin keeps falling over the next 3 days, more so the larger its 3-day fall measured in units of the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader refuses to buy a scaled 3-day drop in a calm downtrend and treats it as a reason to stay out or go short.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 038-01_rising-market-scaled-rally-continuation_1d (idea 038-01_rising-market-scaled-rally-continuation, brief 038_scaled-rallies-continue-in-rising-markets).
What it rests on:
- F4: Outside rising markets, in the coin's lower two thirds of 30-day swing, a 3-day fall is followed by a further fall below the coin's drift. The 3-day strength is -0.159 (t -3.55, 279 cases) in the lowest volatility tercile and -0.203 (t -3.61, 258 cases) in the middle one. After a 3-day rise in the same terciles it is +0.038 and +0.053. Among falls outside rising markets, the strength rises with z3 (slope +0.115, t 2.01, 788 cases), so deeper falls continue more.
