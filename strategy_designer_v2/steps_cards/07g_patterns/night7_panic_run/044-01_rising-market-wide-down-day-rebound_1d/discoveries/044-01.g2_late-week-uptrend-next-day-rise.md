A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, a coin's return on the day after a Thursday or Friday close is larger the stronger its 60-day rise.
What it would mean for a trader: In an uptrend, a trader holding into the late-week next day expects a gain above the coin's usual day, and more so the steeper the trend.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 044-01_rising-market-wide-down-day-rebound_1d (idea 044-01_rising-market-wide-down-day-rebound, brief 044_rising-market-wide-down-day-shakeout).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a rising market, the day after a Thursday or Friday close rises +0.94 % more than the coin's mean day (t 3.10, 1072 cases, 277 days), against -0.25 % after other weekdays (t -1.09). Outside rising markets the same days give +0.04 % (t 0.16). Over all markets the Thursday/Friday effect is positive on all six coins (+0.24 % to +0.75 %, t 1.26 to 2.87) and in all four years (+0.17 % to +0.68 %). It stands out among the roughly 60 groupings examined.
