A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, after a day whose move is large against the coin's swing of the past 30 days, the coin moves back the other way over the next day, after a rise as after a fall, and the move back is larger the larger that day's move.
What it would mean for a trader: In a downtrend, a trader would fade the previous day's outsized move for one day, with a larger position the larger the move, and would not fade small moves or moves in other markets.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 045-01_rising-market-late-week-gain_1d (idea 045-01_rising-market-late-week-gain, brief 045_late-week-gains-in-uptrends).
What it rests on:
- F3: In falling markets, betting against the previous day's move pays more the larger that move is against the coin's 30-day swing: +0.091 %, +0.211 %, +0.448 % and +0.931 % by quartile of the move's size, with t 0.33, 0.65, 1.58 and 1.93 on 242, 264, 260 and 177 days, cases of a day together.
- F4: This falling-market reversal does not appear elsewhere: in rising markets the same quartiles gave +0.302 %, −0.019 %, −0.382 % and −0.006 %, and in choppy markets they gave +0.261 %, −0.203 %, +0.426 % and +0.118 %, with no gradient.
- F5: Within the two largest quartiles of the falling-market reversal, the effect is the same after up days (+0.671 %, t 1.55, 172 days) and after down days (+0.682 %, t 1.61, 172 days), it is positive on all six coins (+0.262 % on XRP to +0.938 % on ZEC), and its size varies by year (2018 +0.558 %, 2019 +0.275 %, 2020 +2.975 %, 2021 −0.152 %).
