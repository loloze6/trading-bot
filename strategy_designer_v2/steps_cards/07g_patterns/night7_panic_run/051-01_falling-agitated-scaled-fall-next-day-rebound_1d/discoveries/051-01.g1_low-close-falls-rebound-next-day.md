A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: After a down day, a coin rises over the next day, and the rise is larger the nearer that day's close sits to the day's low, as a share of the day's high-low range.
What it would mean for a trader: A down day that closes at its low is a reason to buy for the next day, and a down day that recovers into its high is a reason to refuse that buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 051-01_falling-agitated-scaled-fall-next-day-rebound_1d (idea 051-01_falling-agitated-scaled-fall-next-day-rebound, brief 051_agitated-downtrend-falls-rebound-next-day).
