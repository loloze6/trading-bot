A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: A coin rises over the next day, more so the larger that day's fall is against the coin's usual one-day move.
What it would mean for a trader: A large down day is a reason to be long for the next day, and the close location adds nothing to it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 042-01_low-close-next-day-rebound_1d (idea 042-01_low-close-next-day-rebound, brief 042_close-location-reverses-next-day).
What it rests on:
- F3: Close location adds nothing beyond the day's own return.
