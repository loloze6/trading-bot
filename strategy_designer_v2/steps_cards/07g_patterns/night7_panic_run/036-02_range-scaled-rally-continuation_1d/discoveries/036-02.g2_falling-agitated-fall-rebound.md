A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market that is falling, a coin whose one-day fall is large against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days rises over the next day, more the larger the swing-scaled fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader can concentrate next-day rebound buying in falling, agitated markets, where the swing-scaled fall predicts the bounce most strongly.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling agitated market.
