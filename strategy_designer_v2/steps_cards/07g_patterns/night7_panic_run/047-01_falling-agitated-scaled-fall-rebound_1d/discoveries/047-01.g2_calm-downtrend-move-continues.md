A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's 5-day return in units of its 30-day daily swing, the further the coin moves the same way over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a calm downtrend, a 5-day fall that is unusual for the coin is a reason to stay short or out, and an unusual 5-day rise a reason to hold long, for the following days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 047-01_falling-agitated-scaled-fall-rebound_1d (idea 047-01_falling-agitated-scaled-fall-rebound, brief 047_agitated-downtrend-scaled-days-reverse).
What it rests on:
- F1: Outside the tested profile, in coins not in a rising trend, the 5-day return scaled by the 30-day daily swing carries on over the following days. The slope of the unusual move on it is +0.0179 over 3 days (t 2.46, 591 cases on 186 days) and +0.0344 over 5 days (t 3.04, 344 cases on 110 days), with day-clustered t's.
- F3: The carry-on is weaker when the coin is rising: the slope is +0.0042 over 3 days (t 0.49) and +0.0196 over 5 days (t 1.76).
- F4: Inside the falling-agitated profile, the 5-day scaled move has a negative slope (−0.0071, t −0.57), the opposite sign to outside it.
