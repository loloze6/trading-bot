A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, the narrower a day's high-low range against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, the larger the coin's rise over the next 3 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a downtrend an unusually quiet day marks selling exhaustion and a short-lived bounce, a reason to stop pressing shorts.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 037-01_falling-market-wide-day-decline_1d (idea 037-01_falling-market-wide-day-decline, brief 037_wide-days-precede-decline-in-falling).
What it rests on:
- F4: In falling markets the narrowest range quintile (mean range 0.65 swing, mean close-to-close move 0.01 swing) is followed by an unusual 3-day rise of +3.29 % (t 2.51, 138 cases on 60 days), positive in 9 of 11 windows. The other four quintiles are all at or below -0.7 %, and the range-to-move correlation is -0.13.
- F3: In falling markets wide days are followed by no reliable decline. Range of at least 2 swings gives -0.81 % (t -0.99, 163 cases), and the widest range quintile gives -0.83 % (t -0.54, 61 days). This matches the verdict's inconclusive -0.52 % (t -1.12).
