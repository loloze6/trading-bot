Your line: patterns. Whatever the idea's verdict and the book's check.

Objective: find patterns in the bars and the book that the idea does not explain, and the ideas they suggest.

Look at, angle by angle: the forecast (what follows small and large values), the positions, the trades, the regimes,
and the bars themselves (returns after large moves, by volatility level, by weekday and by hour, by regime, before
and after the book's largest gains and losses).

Propose: new ideas, each from a finding. A pattern you could not tell apart from chance is not a finding.
