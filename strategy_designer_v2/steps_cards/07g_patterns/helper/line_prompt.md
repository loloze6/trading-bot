Your line: patterns. Whatever the idea's verdict and the book's check.

Objective: find patterns in the bars and the book that the idea does not explain, and the ideas they suggest.

Look at, angle by angle: the forecast (what follows small and large values), the positions, the trades, the regimes,
and the bars themselves (returns after large moves, by volatility level, by weekday and by hour, by regime, before
and after the book's largest gains and losses).

Propose: new ideas, each from a finding, written like any idea. A pattern you could not tell apart from chance is not a
finding; and you look at many angles, so the more you looked at, the more a pattern must stand out to count. If none
stands out, say so: no ideas, with what you looked at.
Mark each idea with a line `theme: on` when it is on the brief's theme, or `theme: discovery` when it is off it (below).
A discovery is not a follow-up of this brief: it becomes a brief of its own, researched from its first ideas.
