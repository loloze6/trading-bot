Idea: After a coin's 5-day run, the flatter-bodied the day's close is against its open, the further the coin continues in the run's direction over the next day.
Consequence: A trader would read a flat day inside a run as a pause to keep riding, not as the run's end.
Why:
- F6: The brief's own pattern runs the other way. Flat-bodied days after a 5-day run are followed by a next-day move with the run, not against it, in every range quartile: -0.23 %, -0.56 %, -0.88 % and -0.59 % against the run (t -0.57, -1.93, -2.05, -1.28). The move does not grow with range.
