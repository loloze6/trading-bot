A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, a day that closes near its low is followed by a rise over the next day, and the rise is larger the wider the day's range against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a rising coin would buy a wide day that closes near its low for one day, treating it as a dip that is bought back, not as a turn.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-03.g1.b1_choppy-agitated-wide-high-close-fall_1d (idea 077-03.g1.b1_choppy-agitated-wide-high-close-fall, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).

In choppy markets they give only +0.10 % (t 0.12, 61 cases). In rising markets the rebound holds with both swing levels: +2.10 % (t 2.32, 24 days) with a high swing and +1.53 % (t 1.60, 29 days) with a low swing.
