A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, calm market, a day whose range is wide against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns and that closes near its low is followed by a rise over the next day, larger the wider the day.
What it would mean for a trader: In a rising, calm coin, a wide day that ends at its low is a next-day buy, stronger the wider the day.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 060-01.b1_falling-calm-high-close-fade_1d (idea 060-01.b1_falling-calm-high-close-fade, brief 060_falling-calm-wide-high-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F4: Wide days closing in the bottom quarter of their range are followed by a next-day rebound that grows with width: range 1–1.5 swings −0.30 %, 1.5–2 swings +0.68 % (t 1.42), 2–3 swings +1.02 % (332 cases, 159 days, t 2.21), above 3 swings +2.21 % (165 cases, 79 days, t 2.21). The rebound is positive in every year: 2018 +0.77 %, 2019 +0.88 %, 2020 +2.61 %, 2021 +1.56 % (t 2.01). It is strongest in a rising 60-day trend: +1.91 % (105 cases, 53 days, t 2.68), against +1.54 % in falling (t 1.52) and +1.15 % in choppy (t 1.21). The map already holds this pattern in the agitated profiles (039-02.d1, 044-01, 045-01), but not in rising calm.
