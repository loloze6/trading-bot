A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market, a day that closes near its high on a range wide against the coin's 30-day swing is followed by a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day and the nearer the close sits to its high.
What it would mean for a trader: In agitated markets a trader treats a wide day closing at its high as a short-term top to fade, not a breakout to chase.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-03.d1.b1_widest-low-close-next-day-rebound_1d (idea 077-03.d1.b1_widest-low-close-next-day-rebound, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
