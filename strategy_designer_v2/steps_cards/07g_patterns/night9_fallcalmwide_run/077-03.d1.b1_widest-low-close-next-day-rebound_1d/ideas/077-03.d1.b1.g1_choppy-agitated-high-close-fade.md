Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, a day that closes near its high on a range wide against the coin's 30-day swing is followed by a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day against that swing and the nearer the close sits to the high.
Consequence: A trader in a choppy, agitated market sells or refuses to buy after a wide day closing at its high, where the next day tends to give the move back.
Why:
- F5: On the brief's own short side, in choppy, high-swing bars, wide days closing in the top fifth of their range fell -4.16 % the next day (t over days -2.27, 11 days). Narrow ones rose +0.61 % (6 days). Inside the recorded profile, the widest quarter of high closes fell -3.59 % (t -2.19, 13 cases). Choppy agitated short is empty on the profile map, but these case counts are very small.
