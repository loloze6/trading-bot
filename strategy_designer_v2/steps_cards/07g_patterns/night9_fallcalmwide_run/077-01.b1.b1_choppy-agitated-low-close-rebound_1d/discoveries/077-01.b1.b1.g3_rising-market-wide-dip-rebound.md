A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, a day that closes near its low rises over the next three days, by more the wider that day's range is against the coin's range over the past 20 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a rising market, a trader can buy the close of an unusually wide day that ends near its low as a dip within the uptrend, sized by its width.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-01.b1.b1_choppy-agitated-low-close-rebound_1d (idea 077-01.b1.b1_choppy-agitated-low-close-rebound, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F5: Wide low-close days (≥1.5x range, bottom quarter) rebound most in a rising market: +2.40 % next day (t 3.41, 65 cases, 39 days). In a falling market the rebound is +1.90 % (t 0.93, 72 cases) and in a choppy market +1.40 % (t 1.27, 36 cases).
- F2: The rebound after very wide low-close days lasts beyond the next day. For days of at least 1.5x the mean range closing in the bottom quarter (278 cases, 130 days), the excess return is +1.67 % over 2 days (t 2.62) and +2.40 % over 3 days (t 2.86). The sign is positive in every year shown (2018 +0.5 %, 2019 +1.2 %, 2020 +3.5 %, 2021 +1.2 %) and on every coin with enough cases (BTC +1.1 %, ETH +1.7 %, XRP +4.0 %, XMR +1.4 %).
