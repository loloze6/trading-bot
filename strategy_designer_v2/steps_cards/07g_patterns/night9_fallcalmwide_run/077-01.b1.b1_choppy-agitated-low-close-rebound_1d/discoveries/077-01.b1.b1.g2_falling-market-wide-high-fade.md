A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, a day that closes near its high falls over the next day, by more the wider that day's range is against the coin's range over the past 20 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a falling market, a trader can short the close of an unusually wide day that ends near its high, and avoid buying that strength.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-01.b1.b1_choppy-agitated-low-close-rebound_1d (idea 077-01.b1.b1_choppy-agitated-low-close-rebound, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a falling market (60-day return below −10 %), days of at least 1.25x the mean range closing in the top quarter fall the next day by −1.89 % excess (t −2.59, 116 cases, 54 days). The sign is negative in every year (2018 −1.8 %, 2019 −2.2 %, 2020 −3.8 %, 2021 −0.3 %). The same days in a rising market rise +0.68 % (t 1.10, 225 cases), and in a choppy market they fall −0.91 % (t −1.20, 59 cases).
