A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, calm market, a day closing near its low is followed by a rise over the next day, larger the closer the close sits to the low and the wider the day's range against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a calm uptrend would buy a wide day that closes near its low for one day, the most after the widest days closing at their lows, instead of reading it as the trend breaking.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-03.g1_choppy-agitated-high-close-fall_1d (idea 077-03.g1_choppy-agitated-high-close-fall, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F2: In a rising, calm market (60-day return above +10 %, 30-day swing at or below the coin's median), down days rebounded the next day by two separate measures. Wide low-quarter closes gave +1.84 % (t 2.55, n 72) and bottom-quintile one-day returns gave +1.11 % (t 2.31, n 198). In falling calm the same cases gave +0.22 % (t 0.26, n 113) and −0.24 % (t −0.45, n 237), and in choppy calm −0.13 % (t −0.16, n 53) and −0.59 % (t −0.79, n 114).
- F1: Across all coins and profiles, a wide day closing near its low rose the next day by more as its volume grew: wide-range (top tercile) low-quarter closes gave +0.80 % (t 0.73, n 27), +0.59 % (t 1.25, n 218) and +1.50 % (t 2.98, n 454) by volume tercile, and +1.19 % (t 2.84, n 699) together. The extra move did not grow on the second day (+1.24 %, t 2.58), so it is spent in one day.
