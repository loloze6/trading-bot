A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, calm market, a day that falls by more than usual against the coin's 30-day swing is followed by a rise over the next three days, larger the larger that day's fall in units of the swing.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader buys pullback days in a calmly rising coin rather than reading them as the start of a reversal, sizing up the sharper the pullback.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising calm market.
