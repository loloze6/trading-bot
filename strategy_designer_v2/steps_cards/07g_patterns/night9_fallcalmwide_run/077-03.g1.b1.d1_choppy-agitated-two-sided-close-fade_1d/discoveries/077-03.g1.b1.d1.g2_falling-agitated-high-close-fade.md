A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, a wide day closing near its high falls over the next day, more so the wider the day against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns and the closer its close to the day's high.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would short wide high-close days for one day in falling, swinging coins and refuse the same short when the coin has been rising.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-03.g1.b1.d1_choppy-agitated-two-sided-close-fade_1d (idea 077-03.g1.b1.d1_choppy-agitated-two-sided-close-fade, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F1: Across every trend, fading where a day closes in its range only pays when the coin is swinging more than its own usual. In the middle tercile it is +0.20 % long (t 0.67) and +0.44 % short (t 1.86). In the lowest tercile it is +0.09 % long (t 0.24) and −0.05 % short (t −0.20).
