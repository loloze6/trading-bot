Idea: On a day a coin rises, the lighter its volume against its own average daily volume over the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: A trader refuses to buy, or leans short, the day after a coin rises on thin volume. The short side is thinly held among strategies acting in every profile.
Why:
- F5: Up days on light-to-ordinary volume drift lower the next day. The bottom three volume fifths give −0.39 %, −0.58 % and −0.61 % (t −2.64, −3.71, −3.85; about 1130 cases each). The top fifth gives 0.00 %. The fall is not graded at the light end, and these t's are not clustered by day.
