A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, calm market, a day that falls against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns is followed by a rise over the next three days. The larger the day's fall measured in those swings, the larger the rise.
What it would mean for a trader: In an orderly uptrend, a one-day fall that is large for the coin's own swing is a dip to buy for a few days, more so the larger the fall.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 074-01_rising-calm-wide-low-close-rebound_1d (idea 074-01_rising-calm-wide-low-close-rebound, brief 074_rising-calm-wide-low-close-rebound).
What it rests on:
- F4: In the rising calm profile, a down day measured in the coin's own 30-day swings is followed by a rise that grows with the size of the fall. This holds whether or not the day is wide.
- F1: In the rising calm profile, wide days (range at least 2 swings) that close in the lower third rise more over the next three days than over one.
