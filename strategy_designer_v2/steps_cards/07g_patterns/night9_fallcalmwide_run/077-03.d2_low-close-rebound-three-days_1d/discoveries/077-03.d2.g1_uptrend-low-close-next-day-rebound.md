A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: After a coin has risen over its last 20 days, a day closing below the middle of its range is followed by a rise over the next day. The rise is larger the stronger the 20-day rise and the nearer the close sits to the day's low.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader buys the weak close inside a 20-day uptrend for one day, rather than reading it as the start of a reversal.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-03.d2_low-close-rebound-three-days_1d (idea 077-03.d2_low-close-rebound-three-days, brief 077_choppy-agitated-wide-close-fade).
What it rests on:
- F1: Across all bars of folds B and C, a day closing below the middle of its range is followed by a rise the next day only when the coin has risen over its last 20 days: +0.63 % over the coin's normal one-day drift (day-clustered t 2.81, 1,675 cases on 552 days). The rise is +0.99 % over two days (t 2.15) and +1.11 % over three days (t 1.55), so most of it comes on the first day.
- F2: This up-trend rebound grows with the size of the 20-day rise: +0.22 % (t 0.87), +0.33 % (t 1.29) and +1.35 % (t 3.39) across the terciles of the 20-day return. It holds in calm markets (+0.52 %, t 2.04) and in agitated ones (+0.76 %, t 2.52), and on 9 of 10 coins. By year it is +1.23 % in 2020 and +0.76 % in 2021, but −0.49 % in 2018.
- F5: Without the trend condition, the close's place in the range barely predicts the next day: Spearman correlation −0.08 at one day and −0.02 at three days. Over three days, the quintiles of the close's place in the range are all within ±0.3 % of drift (|t| ≤ 0.76).
- F8: The pullback moves in F1 and F3 are 0.6 % per case in one day, about three times the 0.22 % cost of a trade. A next-day hold could therefore pay, unlike the tested idea, whose total move was 0.1 times the cost.
