A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, a coin keeps moving over the next 5 days in the direction of its last 5-day return, and the further that 5-day return is from zero, measured against the coin's usual 5-day move, the larger the move that follows.
What it would mean for a trader: When a coin's swings are quieter than usual, a trader can follow its last week's move for the next week, sized by how unusual that move was, and should not do so in agitated markets.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 054-02.b1_reclaim-rebound-all-coins_1d (idea 054-02.b1_reclaim-rebound-all-coins, brief 054_reclaimed-monthly-low-in-falling-agitated).
What it rests on:
- F4: In calm markets (30-day swing at or below its 90-day level), a coin's 5-day return, scaled by its usual 5-day move, predicts the next 5 days in the same direction. The day-clustered slope is +0.375 usual moves per unit (t 3.23, 623 cases, 139 days). By quintile of the past 5-day move, the excess 5-day return rises steadily: −3.0 %, −1.9 %, +1.4 %, +1.5 %, +4.6 %.
- F5: In agitated markets the same past-5-day continuation is absent (slope +0.108, t 0.82, 100 days). It is strongest in choppy calm markets (slope +0.83, t 2.97, 50 days) and still present in falling calm ones (+0.32, t 2.22, 88 days). In calm bars, the 5-day excess taken in the direction of the past 5-day move is positive in 11 of 12 windows (w8 −0.1 %) and in all four years (+0.9 % to +3.4 %).
