A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: When a coin closes below its lowest price of the past 30 days, it keeps falling over the next 10 days, and the fall is larger the higher the coin's 60-day return before that day.
What it would mean for a trader: A first monthly-low break after a rise marks the start of a reversal rather than a dip to buy, so a trader should short it harder, or refuse a long, the more the coin had risen beforehand.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 054-04.b1_reclaimed-low-after-rise_1d (idea 054-04.b1_reclaimed-low-after-rise, brief 054_reclaimed-monthly-low-in-falling-agitated).
