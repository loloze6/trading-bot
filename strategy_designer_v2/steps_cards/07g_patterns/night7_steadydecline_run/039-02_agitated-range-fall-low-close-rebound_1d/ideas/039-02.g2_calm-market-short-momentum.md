Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's 5-day rise, the larger its rise over the next 3 days.
Consequence: In calm markets a trader would follow the last week's direction for a few days instead of fading it.
Why:
- F4: In calm bars the coin's 5-day return is followed in the same direction over the next 3 days, graded across quintiles: lowest -1.44 % (652 cases, t -2.63), then -0.57 %, +0.19 %, +0.22 %, highest +1.32 % (772 cases, t 2.70); in agitated bars there is no such order (lowest -0.70 %, t -0.91; highest +0.22 %, t 0.40). These 3-day returns were taken from every bar and so overlap, which makes the t's larger than they would be on separate cases.
