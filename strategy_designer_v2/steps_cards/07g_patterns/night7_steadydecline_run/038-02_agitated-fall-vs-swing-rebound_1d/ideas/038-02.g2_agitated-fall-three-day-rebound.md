Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days, the larger its rise over the next three days.
Consequence: The rebound after a forced sweep in an agitated decline keeps building for several days, so a trader can hold it longer than one day and pay the trade's cost once for a larger move.
Why:
- F3: In the falling agitated profile, the bounce does not fade after the first day. After falls beyond one standard deviation (129 cases), the mean return is +2.91 % over the next day (t 4.46), +2.51 % over two days (t 3.26) and +3.57 % over three days (t 3.84). These are overlapping returns from distinct signal bars, not a non-overlapping grid.
