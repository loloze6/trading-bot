Idea: In a rising market, a coin's rise over the next 5 days is larger the fewer of its own daily swings over the last 20 days its daily close stands below its prior 20-day high, with the largest rise once the close reaches the high.
Consequence: In a rising market, a trader scales up longs as a coin closes in on its recent high, instead of waiting for it to stand far above.
Why:
- F4: In rising markets, the gain comes from being at or above the high, not from how far above it the close stands:
- F5: The book's trades still rank by entry forecast. Median net gain rises from +0.66 % in the lowest entry-forecast quarter to +5.80 % in the highest (about 170 trades each).
