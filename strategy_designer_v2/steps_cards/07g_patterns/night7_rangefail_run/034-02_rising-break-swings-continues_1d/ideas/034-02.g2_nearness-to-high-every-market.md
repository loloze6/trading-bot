Idea: A coin's move over the next 5 days is larger the nearer its daily close stands to its prior 20-day high, measured in its own daily swings over the last 20 days: higher near or above the high, lower deep below it.
Consequence: A trader leans long on coins closing near their recent high and away from coins far below it, whatever the market's trend.
