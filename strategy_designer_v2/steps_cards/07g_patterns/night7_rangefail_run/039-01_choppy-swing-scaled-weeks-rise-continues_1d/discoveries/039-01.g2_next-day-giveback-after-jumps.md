A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: The larger a coin's one-day return in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the more it moves back the other way over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader entering after a large one-day move should expect it to give back part of that move the next day.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 039-01_choppy-swing-scaled-weeks-rise-continues_1d (idea 039-01_choppy-swing-scaled-weeks-rise-continues, brief 039_choppy-market-swing-scaled-moves-continue).
What it rests on:
- F6: Over the next single day, a large swing-scaled one-day move partly reverses in every profile. One-day slope is -0.0045 (t -2.52, 714 days) in the choppy profile and -0.0034 (t -2.52, 1197 days) outside it.
