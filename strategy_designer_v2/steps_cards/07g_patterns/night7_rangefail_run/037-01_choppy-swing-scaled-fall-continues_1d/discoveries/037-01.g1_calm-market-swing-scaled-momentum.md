A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the more of its own 20-day daily swings a coin's 5-day move spans, the further the coin keeps moving the same way over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: When a coin is swinging less than usual, a 5-day move that is large for the coin is a reason to trade with it rather than against it, and a reason to refuse fading it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 037-01_choppy-swing-scaled-fall-continues_1d (idea 037-01_choppy-swing-scaled-fall-continues, brief 037_choppy-market-multiday-falls-continue).
What it rests on:
- F1: In calm markets (the coin's 30-day swing at or below its own median), the next 5 days follow the 5-day move measured in 20-day swings, in steps across the quintiles: −3.71 % (t −2.77, 157 cases, 63 days) after the largest falls, −1.19 %, −0.08 %, +0.98 %, and +4.40 % (t 1.87, 207 cases, 69 days) after the largest rises. In agitated markets every quintile is flat (−0.94 % to +0.48 %, all |t| < 0.5).
- F2: The calm-market continuation points the same way in every year shown: the mean of z5 × unusual move is positive in 2018 (t 1.51), 2019 (t 0.36), 2020 (t 1.84) and 2021 (t 2.12). The same 20-day measure in calm markets also steps from −3.33 % (t −2.25) to +4.92 % (t 1.88).
