A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin keeps moving over the next 5 days in the direction of its 20-day return, and moves further the more of its own 20-day daily swings that return spans.
What it would mean for a trader: In a sideways market a trader would follow a coin's 20-day move on either side, larger the more swings it spans, instead of fading it as a range trader would.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 037-01.b1_choppy-extended-fall-continues_1d (idea 037-01.b1_choppy-extended-fall-continues, brief 037_choppy-market-multiday-falls-continue).
What it rests on:
- F1: In the choppy profile (60-day return within ±10 %), the coin's 20-day return in units of its 20-day daily swing predicts the next 5-day unusual move in the same direction: the slope is 0.065 per swing unit (t 2.65, 189 non-overlapping cases), and over 10 days it is 0.088 (t 2.55, 96 cases). Measured on the backtest's own profile regimes, the 5-day slope is 0.040 (t 2.17, 346 cases).
- F4: The same choppy grading on the 20-day move is in the 2020 and 2021 cases but not the earlier ones. The yearly 5-day slopes are -0.030 (2018, 20 cases), -0.003 (2019), 0.072 (2020, t 1.21) and 0.088 (2021, t 2.57). The 10-day slopes are positive in 2019, 2020 and 2021 (0.033, 0.104, 0.108).
