A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, the more of its own 20-day daily swings a coin's 20-day rise spans, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In an uptrend a trader would add to coins whose recent rise is large for their own swing, holding for about two weeks.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising market.

What it rests on:
- F5: In the rising profile, the 10-day move continues a 5-day or 20-day move in swing units: slope 0.041 on the 5-day (t 2.21) and 0.040 on the 20-day (t 2.34), 239 cases. The 5-day slope is positive in each year with enough cases: 0.045 (2019, t 2.62), 0.044 (2020, t 1.44), 0.038 (2021, t 1.27). At 5 days it is weaker (t 1.30). In the falling profile there is no grading (5-day slope t 1.01, 10-day t -0.28).
