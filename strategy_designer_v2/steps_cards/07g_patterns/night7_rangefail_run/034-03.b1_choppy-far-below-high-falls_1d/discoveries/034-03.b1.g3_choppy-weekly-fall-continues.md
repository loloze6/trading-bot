A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin whose 5-day return is negative falls further over the next 5 days, more the more of its own daily swings (20-day) the 5-day fall spans.
What it would mean for a trader: In a sideways market, a coin that has just fallen several swings in a week is a short candidate rather than a rebound buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
