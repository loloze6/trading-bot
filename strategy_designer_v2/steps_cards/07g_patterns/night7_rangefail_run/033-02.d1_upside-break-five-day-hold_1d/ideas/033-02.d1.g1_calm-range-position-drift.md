Idea: In a calm market, the higher a coin's daily close sits within its prior 20-day high-to-low range, the more it rises over the next 5 days, and the lower it sits, the more it falls, with closes above the 20-day high rising most.
Consequence: In calm markets a trader leans long in proportion to how high the close sits in its 20-day range and short in proportion to how low it sits, and stays out of this bet when the coin is swinging more than usual.
Why:
- F1: The tested effect lives in calm markets. On 5-day non-overlapping cases, the slope of the unusual 5-day move on the close's position in its prior 20-day high-low range is 6.93 % per unit of range in calm markets (30-day swing at or below its median; 713 cases, correlation 0.217, t 5.92). In agitated markets it is only 2.16 % (709 cases, correlation 0.050, t 1.34).
