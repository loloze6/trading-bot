A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's 20-day rise, the larger its rise over the next 10 days, and the larger its 20-day fall, the larger its fall over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a coin stuck in a two-month sideways market, a trader should follow its last twenty days' direction for the next ten, in proportion to the size of that move.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-02.b1.b2.b1.b2.b1_calm-rising-range-position_1d (idea 058-02.b1.b2.b1.b2.b1_calm-rising-range-position, brief 058_choppy-calm-range-edge-reversion).
What it rests on:
- F4: The coin's 20-day direction carries the next 10 days most strongly in choppy markets. The sign of the 20-day return times the excess 10-day return averages +9.0 % (t 4.91, 77 cases) in choppy markets, +4.1 % (t 2.88, 188) in rising markets and +1.6 % (t 1.51, 209) in falling markets.
- F5: In all markets together, the excess 10-day return rises with the 20-day return quintile. It runs from -3.5 % (t -3.02) in the second-lowest quintile to +2.2 % (t 1.76) and +4.0 % (t 2.15) in the two highest, about 160 cases each.
