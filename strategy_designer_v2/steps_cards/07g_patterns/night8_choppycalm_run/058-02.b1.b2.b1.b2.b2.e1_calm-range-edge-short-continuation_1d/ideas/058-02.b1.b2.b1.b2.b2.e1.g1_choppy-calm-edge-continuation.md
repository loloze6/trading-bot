Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the nearer a coin's close sits to the top of its 20-day high-low range, the larger its rise over the next 10 days, and the nearer to the bottom, the larger its fall.
Consequence: A trader in a calm coin without a 60-day trend can lean with a close near its 20-day edge and hold for about two weeks, where the move per case is several times a trade's cost instead of twice it.
Why:
- F1: In calm markets the range-edge effect grows with the horizon. On a 5-day non-overlapping grid, closes in the top fifth of the 20-day range are followed by +4.40 % above the coin's drift (t 3.10, 177 cases, 69 days), and closes in the two bottom fifths by -2.41 % (t -1.91) and -2.38 % (t -2.38). At 2 days the top fifth gives only +1.20 % (t 2.21) and the bottom fifth -0.67 % (t -1.23).
