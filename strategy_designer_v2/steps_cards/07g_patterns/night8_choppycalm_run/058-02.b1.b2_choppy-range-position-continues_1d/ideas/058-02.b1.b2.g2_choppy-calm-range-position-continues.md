Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the higher a coin's close sits within its 20-day high-low range, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: When a coin has gone sideways and swung less than usual, a trader should lean with the side of the range its close sits on instead of betting on a return to the middle.
Why:
- F4: In choppy calm bars, range position is followed by continuation, not by the brief's pull back to the middle.
- F5: The tested idea, pooled over the whole choppy profile, is too small to tell from zero and too small to pay.
