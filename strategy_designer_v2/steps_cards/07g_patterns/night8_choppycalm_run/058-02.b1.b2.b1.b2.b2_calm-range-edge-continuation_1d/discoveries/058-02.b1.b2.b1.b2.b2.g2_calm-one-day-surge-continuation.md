A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a calm coin, a trader should treat an unusually large up day as a reason to buy for the next week, not to sell.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a calm market.
