## Findings
- **F1:** Inside the falling, agitated profile, the next-day excess return depends on the last day's fall and not on the slide before it. With the last day's fall, its rise and the prior four days' fall in one regression (day-clustered, 1461 cases), the last day's fall has −1.91 % per standard deviation (t −4.76). The prior four-day fall has −0.11 % (t −0.25) and the last day's rise +0.12 % (t 0.41).
- **F2:** The five-day depth that the forecast uses is not graded inside the profile. Over the five quintiles of a negative five-day return, the next-day excess return is +2.00 % (t 1.56, 66 days), −0.06 %, −0.00 %, −0.69 % and −0.23 %. With the last day included in the regression, the five-day return falls to −0.84 % per standard deviation (t −1.36) while the last day keeps −1.91 % (t −3.25).
- **F3:** The rebound follows the worst last day whatever the slide before it. In the bottom last-day tercile, the next-day excess return is +2.34 % (t 3.00, 114 days) after the deepest five-day tercile and +1.63 % (t 1.97, 46 days) after the shallowest. A middle last day after the deepest five-day tercile gives −0.45 % (t −0.52), which runs against the brief's claim that a small down day ending a deep slide still rebounds.
- **F4:** After a deep prior four-day slide inside the profile, the next-day excess return falls steadily across the last day's quintiles: +3.44 % (t 2.80, 65 days), +1.14 %, −0.72 %, −1.49 % and −0.47 %. This is the same one-day-fall rebound the held books 047-01 and 051-01 ("falling agitated daily fall rebound") already earn.
- **F5:** The rebound does not appear outside the recorded profile.
- **F6:** After a deep prior slide in the profile, an up day is followed by a next-day excess return of −1.01 % (t −1.62, 363 cases), negative in every year (2019: −1.60 %, t −3.15). That return is not graded by the size of the up day (+0.41 % per standard deviation, t 0.68), so it is neither sure enough nor graded given the many angles examined.
- **F7:** The book earned +4.5 % a year with Sharpe 0.42, 0.13 below holding, and gained little in any year. Its mean daily return is −0.011 % in 2018 and +0.002 %, +0.043 % and +0.022 % in 2019 to 2021. The five-day forecast spreads its positions over slides that do not rebound (F2, F3).

## Ideas
- No ideas: The brief's claim that the depth of the multi-day slide sets the rebound is not in the data, since the prior days add nothing once the last day's fall is known (F1, F2, F3). What does pay is the one-day fall rebound in a falling, agitated market (F4, F5), which the profile map already holds (047-01, 051-01, 038-01). The only other pattern, a fall after an up day that ends a slide (F6), is not graded and not sure enough to count.
