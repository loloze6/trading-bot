A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's one-day rise compared with its usual daily swing over the past 20 days, the larger its further rise over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a coin that has gone nowhere for two months, a day that jumps well beyond its usual swing is a reason to buy for the following week rather than to fade the jump back into the range.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-02.b1.b2.b1.b2.b2.e2_choppy-calm-edge-continuation_1d (idea 058-02.b1.b2.b1.b2.b2.e2_choppy-calm-edge-continuation, brief 058_choppy-calm-range-edge-reversion).
What it rests on:
- F4: A large up day relative to the coin's usual daily swing is followed by more rise over the next 5 days, and a large down day is not followed by more fall. Days above +2 of the prior 20-day swing are followed by an unusual +6.18 % (t 2.61, 52 cases) and days from +1 to +2 by +2.22 % (t 1.87, 126 cases), against -0.35 % for ordinary days (1153 cases). The up-day part has slope 0.0186 (t 2.39, 1540 cases, 243 days) and the down-day part 0.0052 (t 0.59).
- F5: The up-day continuation is strongest in a choppy market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %): slope 0.0545 (t 2.76, 152 cases, 68 days), against 0.0210 (t 1.42, 377 cases) in rising markets and 0.0079 (t 0.63, 437 cases) in falling ones. Its slope is positive in every year from 2018 to 2021 (0.0025 to 0.0117), but no single year exceeds t 1.25.
