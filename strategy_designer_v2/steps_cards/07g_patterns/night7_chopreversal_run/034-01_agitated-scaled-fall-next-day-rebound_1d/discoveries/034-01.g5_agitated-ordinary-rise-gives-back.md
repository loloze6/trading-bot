A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market, a coin's ordinary one-day rise is partly given back the next day, and the larger the rise within its usual daily swing, the larger the give-back.
What it would mean for a trader: In agitated markets a trader should not chase routine up days; they tend to reverse the next day.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 034-01_agitated-scaled-fall-next-day-rebound_1d (idea 034-01_agitated-scaled-fall-next-day-rebound, brief 034_agitated-market-fall-next-day-rebound).
What it rests on:
- F5: In agitated markets, ordinary days within one usual swing are partly reversed the next day, and in calm markets they are not.
