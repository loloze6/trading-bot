A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, the larger a coin's 5-day return against its 30-day daily swing, the further the coin moves in the same direction over the next 5 days, up after rises and down after falls.
What it would mean for a trader: In a calm market a trader follows a large scaled 5-day move, long after rises and short after falls, for the next 5 days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 036-02_agitated-scaled-fall-delayed-rebound_1d (idea 036-02_agitated-scaled-fall-delayed-rebound, brief 036_agitated-fall-three-day-recovery).
What it rests on:
- F5: In calm markets a coin's 5-day return, scaled by its 30-day daily swing times √5, predicts the same-signed excess return over the next 5 days. The slope is +1.991 % per unit (t 5.60, 773 non-overlapping cases), and the sign-aligned mean is +1.17 % (t 2.35 with the cases of one day together, 196 days).
- F6: This calm 5-day momentum holds on both sides and is graded. The slope is +2.11 % (t 3.04) after rises and +2.97 % (t 3.69) after falls. Excess returns go from −4.84 % (t −2.89, 40 cases) below −1.5 units to +3.53 % (t 2.09, 66) above +1.5 units. It is positive on all 6 coins (t 1.16 to 3.84) and in 3 of 4 years (2019: −0.28 %, t −0.41). The sign-aligned mean of 1.17 % is about 6 times the 0.19 % cost.
