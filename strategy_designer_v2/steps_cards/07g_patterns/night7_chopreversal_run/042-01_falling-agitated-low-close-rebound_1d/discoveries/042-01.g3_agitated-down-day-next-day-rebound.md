A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's fall from that day's open to its close, the larger its rise over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: In a fallen, swinging coin, an open-to-close sell-off is a one-day long bet sized by the size of the drop.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 042-01_falling-agitated-low-close-rebound_1d (idea 042-01_falling-agitated-low-close-rebound, brief 042_falling-agitated-low-close-rebound).
What it rests on:
- F6: In profile, the day's own open-to-close return predicts the next day against itself, with a slope of -0.22 (t -3.53, 1298 cases, 467 days), against -0.0009 out of profile.
- F5: The rebound is a one-day move. In profile, the next-three-day slope has a t of 0.59, against 2.95 over one day, and bottom-fifth closes are only +0.93 % up after two days (t 1.11) against +2.17 % after one.
