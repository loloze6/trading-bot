A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market, the higher a coin closes within its day's high-to-low range, the lower its return over the next day, and the more so the wider that day's range against its average daily range over the past 30 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In agitated markets a trader fades strong closes on wide days and buys weak ones, rather than following the day's direction.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 044-02_agitated-down-day-range-rebound_1d (idea 044-02_agitated-down-day-range-rebound, brief 044_agitated-wide-range-fall-rebound).
What it rests on:
- F1: In agitated bars, an up close is followed by a next-day fall of -0.54 % beyond drift (t -2.23, 1858 cases, 682 days), against -0.04 % in calm bars (t -0.25, 2327 cases); the first up close after a down day carries most of it, at -0.89 % (t -2.70, 1029 cases, 416 days).
- F2: Across all agitated days, the next-day excess return falls the higher the close sits in the day's range (coefficient -0.0249 per unit of close location, t -3.58, 3372 cases), and this grows on wider days (close location x log range multiple: -0.0293, t -2.30); a low close on a wide day gives +1.41 % (t 2.24, 302 cases) and a high close on a wide day gives -0.77 % (t -1.59, 297 cases).
