A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, after a day the coin closes higher, the next day's fall grows with the product of the day's rise against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns and the day's high-to-low range against its average daily range over the past 30 days; the largest rises on the widest days are followed by the largest falls over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A sharp, wide up day inside a falling, agitated market is a short-lived squeeze to sell into for one day, not a turn to buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 044-01_agitated-wide-range-fall-rebound_1d (idea 044-01_agitated-wide-range-fall-rebound, brief 044_agitated-wide-range-fall-rebound).
