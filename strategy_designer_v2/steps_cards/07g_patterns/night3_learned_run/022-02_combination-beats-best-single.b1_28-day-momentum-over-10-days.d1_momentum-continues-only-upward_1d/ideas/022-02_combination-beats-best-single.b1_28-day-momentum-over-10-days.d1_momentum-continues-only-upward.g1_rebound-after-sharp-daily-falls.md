Idea: The larger a coin's fall over its last day, the higher its return over the next 3 days, relative to its normal drift.
Consequence: A trader would lean long after a coin's sharp one-day drop, more the deeper the drop, rather than selling into it.
Why:
- F3: Large one-day falls are followed by rebounds; large one-day rises are not followed by declines. On a non-overlapping 3-day grid, the 288 days whose return was below -1.5 standard deviations were followed by a 3-day unusual move of +0.169 of the coin's usual 3-day move. The 286 days above +1.5 were followed by -0.018. The slope on the size of these large days has t -1.96, and over all 5435 cases the one-day return predicts the next 3 days with t -2.44.
