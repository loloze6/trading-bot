Idea: A day whose volume is high relative to the coin's 20-day mean volume is followed by a higher return over the next 5 days, and the higher that volume ratio, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader would read heavy-volume days as a reason to hold or add long, and quiet days in falling coins as a reason to stay out.
Why:
- F3: A day's volume against its 20-day mean predicts a higher 5-day unusual move. The slope on log volume ratio is +0.0159 (t 3.49) in rising coins, where the top quintile earns +2.30 % (t 2.62), and +0.0057 (t 2.01) in falling coins, where the quietest quintile is followed by -1.67 % (t -4.73, 595 cases).
