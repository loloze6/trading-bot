Idea: In a coin whose 90-day and 30-day returns are both negative, the fall over the next 5 days grows steeply with the size of its one-day rise in usual daily moves, being several times larger after the rarest rises than after ordinary ones.
Consequence: A trader would short only the most extreme bounces inside a fall and hold for about 5 days rather than 2, refusing the more common, milder bounces whose fade does not cover a trade.

- F3: Inside the falling regime (90-day and 30-day returns both negative), the 5-day fall after a one-day rise grows steeply with the size of the rise, beyond where the tested forecast saturates (about 1.6 moves):
