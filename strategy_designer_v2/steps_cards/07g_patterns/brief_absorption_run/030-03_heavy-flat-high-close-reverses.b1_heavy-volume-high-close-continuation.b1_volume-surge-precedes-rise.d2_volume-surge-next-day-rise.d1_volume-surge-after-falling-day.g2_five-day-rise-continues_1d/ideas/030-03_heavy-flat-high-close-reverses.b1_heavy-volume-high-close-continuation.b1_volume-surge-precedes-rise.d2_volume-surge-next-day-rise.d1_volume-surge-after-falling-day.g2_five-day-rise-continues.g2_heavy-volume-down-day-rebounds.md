Idea: The heavier a coin's volume on a day it closes lower, measured against its average volume over the past 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A trader buys a coin after a heavy-volume down day, larger the heavier the volume, as a sign that the selling has been absorbed and exhausted.
Why:
- F2: On a coin's heaviest-volume days (top fifth of volume against its 20-day average), the next 3 days' excess return is +1.59 % (t 3.51, n 476) when the day closed down, and +0.00 % (t 0.01, n 525) when it closed up.
