Idea: A coin's Monday move reverses over the next 2 days: the larger Monday's fall, measured in the coin's usual one-day moves over the past 60 days, the more the coin rises from Monday's close to Wednesday's close.
Consequence: A sharp Monday drop is a reason to buy at Monday's close, and to refuse a short carried into Tuesday.
Why:
- F4: Monday's move reverses over the next 2 days in a mostly graded way. By Monday-move quintile, from largest fall to largest rise, the 2-day return minus drift is +1.25 % (t 4.83), +0.04 %, −0.43 %, −0.73 % (t −3.17), +0.35 %, about 853 cases each.
