Idea: A coin's Friday close-to-close move continues in the same direction over the next 3 days (Saturday to Monday), more so the larger the Friday move and the higher the coin's recent volatility relative to its usual level.
Consequence: A trader would follow a large Friday move into the weekend in a turbulent market, rather than fading it.
Why:
- F6: Friday moves continue over the weekend. Over Saturday to Monday they continue by 0.37 % (t -3.00, 4263 cases; size-weighted t -3.58), mostly in the wild-volatility third (size-weighted t -3.42). The sign is not stable by year: Friday moves reversed in 2018–2020 (t about +2.2 to +2.3 each year) and continued in 2022–2024 (t down to -3.08).
