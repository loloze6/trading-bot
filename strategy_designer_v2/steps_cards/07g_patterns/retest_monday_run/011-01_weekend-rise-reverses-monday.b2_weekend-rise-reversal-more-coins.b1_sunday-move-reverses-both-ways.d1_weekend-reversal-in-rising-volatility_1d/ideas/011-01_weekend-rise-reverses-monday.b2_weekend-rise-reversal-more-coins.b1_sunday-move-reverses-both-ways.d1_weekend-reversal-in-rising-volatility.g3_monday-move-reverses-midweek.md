Idea: While a coin's 20-day daily volatility is at least its 60-day volatility, its Monday move (its Sunday-to-Monday close-to-close return) reverses over the next 2 days, and the larger the Monday move, the larger the move back.
Consequence: A trader would fade a coin's Monday move over Tuesday and Wednesday when volatility is rising.
Why:
- F7: Monday's move also reverses over Tuesday and Wednesday while volatility is rising: the mean reversal is +0.87 % (t 3.63, 1146 cases), +1.08 % (t 2.80, 509 cases) for moves above one standard deviation, and it is positive in all 8 years but reaches t ≥ 2 only in 2023 (t 2.72) and 2025 (t 4.36).
