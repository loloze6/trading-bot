Idea: After a Sunday fall, the rise over the next 3 days (Monday to Wednesday) against the coin's normal drift is larger the further the coin had fallen over the previous 20 days, measured in its usual daily moves.
Consequence: Buy Sunday falls in coins already in a 20-day decline, and refuse them in coins that have been rising.
Why:
- F6: The rebound after Sunday falls (z < -1) sits in coins already down over 20 days, at +0.34 usual 3-day moves (t 2.83, n 127), against -0.004 (t -0.04, n 127) in coins up over 20 days. It is also stronger in calm than in turbulent volatility (+0.21 t 2.17 vs +0.02 t 0.14). Both are tertile splits among many angles, so they are suggestive only.
- F2: The Sunday reversal is graded and works on both sides of zero, with moderate moves. The next 3-day excess return, in usual 3-day moves, is +0.27 after Sunday z in (-1.5,-1] (n 232), +0.16 in (-1,-0.5] (n 691), +0.06 in (-0.5,0] (n 1066), -0.10 in (0,0.5] (n 1040), -0.10 in (0.5,1] (n 637) and -0.16 in (1,1.5] (n 284). So Sunday rises are faded as much as Sunday falls rebound, and the tested idea is long-only and leaves that side out.
