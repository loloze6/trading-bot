Idea: Over the 4 hours from 20:00 to 24:00 UTC, the coin returns more than its usual 4-hour return. The larger the coin's 24-hour move up to 20:00 UTC, in either direction, the larger that excess.
Consequence: A trader who is already deciding to buy should time the purchase before 20:00 UTC, more so after a large 24-hour move, and avoid selling into that hour.
Why:
- F5: The 4h bar from 20:00 to 24:00 UTC returns more than the coin's mean 4h return, consistently across coins and years.
