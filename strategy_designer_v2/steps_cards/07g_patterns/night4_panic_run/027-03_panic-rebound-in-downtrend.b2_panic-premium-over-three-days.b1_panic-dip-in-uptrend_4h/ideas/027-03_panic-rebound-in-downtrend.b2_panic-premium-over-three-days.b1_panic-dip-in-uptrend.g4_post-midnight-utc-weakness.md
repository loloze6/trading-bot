Idea: Over the 4 hours from 00:00 to 04:00 UTC, the coin returns less than its usual 4-hour return. The larger the coin's absolute 24-hour return up to midnight UTC, the larger that shortfall.
Consequence: A trader who is already deciding to buy should prefer to wait until after 04:00 UTC rather than buy at midnight UTC, more so after a large 24-hour move.
Why:
- F6: The 4h bar from 00:00 to 04:00 UTC returns less than the coin's mean.
