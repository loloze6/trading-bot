Idea: Each coin tends to rise during the 4 hours from 16:00 to 20:00 UTC, by an amount that grows with the size of its usual 4-hour move (the more volatile the coin over its past 30 days, the larger that afternoon rise).
Consequence: A trader who means to buy a coin does better buying before 16:00 UTC than after 20:00 UTC, and is wary of shorting into that block.
Why:
- F1: The 4-hour bar opening at 16:00 UTC rises more than any other block of the day: +0.099 % on average over 17506 bars pooled (t 6.64). It is positive in all six coins (t 1.94 to 3.59; mean +0.069 % in BTC up to +0.154 % in ZEC, against about +0.01 % in the other blocks) and in all eight years (t 0.62 to 4.00).
