Idea: Each coin tends to rise over the UTC Saturday, by an amount that grows with the size of its usual daily move over its past 30 days.
Consequence: A trader times new long entries for Friday's close and avoids opening shorts into the weekend.
Why:
- F2: Saturday is the strongest UTC day: the coins' mean daily return is +0.454 % over 2484 coin-days (t 5.53). It is positive in all six coins (t 2.15 to 2.92) and has t ≥ 1.93 in six of eight years, with 2022 (t 0.42) and 2023 (t −0.01) the exceptions.
