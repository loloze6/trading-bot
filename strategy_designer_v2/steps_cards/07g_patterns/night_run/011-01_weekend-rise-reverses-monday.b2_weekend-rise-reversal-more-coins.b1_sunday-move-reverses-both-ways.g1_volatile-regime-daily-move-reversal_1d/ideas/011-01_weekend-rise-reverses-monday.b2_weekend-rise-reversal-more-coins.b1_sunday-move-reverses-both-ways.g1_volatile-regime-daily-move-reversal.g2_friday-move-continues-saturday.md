Idea: A coin's Friday move continues in the same direction over the next day, and the larger the Friday move, the larger the continuation.
Consequence: A trader would follow rather than fade a coin's Friday move into Saturday, sizing with the move.
Why:
- F2: Friday moves continue rather than reverse: the fade gain after a Friday move is −0.00189 (t −2.95, n 4301), the only weekday with a significantly negative value.
