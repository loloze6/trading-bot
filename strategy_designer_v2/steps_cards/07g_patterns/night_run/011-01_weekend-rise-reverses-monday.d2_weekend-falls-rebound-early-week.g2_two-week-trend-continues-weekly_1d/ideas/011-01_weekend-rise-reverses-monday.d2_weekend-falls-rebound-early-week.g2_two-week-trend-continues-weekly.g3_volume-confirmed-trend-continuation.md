Idea: A coin's 14-day return scaled by its 20-day volatility is followed by a move in the same direction over the next 7 days, larger the larger the scaled return times the day's volume relative to its 30-day average volume.
Consequence: Ride the two-week trend mainly when today's volume is heavy, and refuse it on quiet days.
Why:
- F5: The 14-day trend's continuation is concentrated on bars with high volume relative to the coin's 30-day average.
