Idea: A coin's Friday one-day fall, scaled by its 20-day volatility, continues over the next day, and the continuation is larger the larger the fall and the higher Friday's volume relative to its 20-day usual.
Consequence: Avoid buying a heavy-volume Friday sell-off before the weekend, and treat it as a reason to stay short into Saturday.
Why:
- F5: Friday's move continues into Saturday rather than reversing. The slope of the next-day excess return on z is +0.0033 (t 5.40, n 4401), the fade is -0.0038 (t -4.33), and the two-day continuation is 0.0037 (t 3.39). The date-clustered t is only 0.87, and the sign flips by year: 2018 is -0.0132 (t -4.67), while 2024 is +0.0117 (t 4.79) and 2025 is +0.0053 (t 4.10).
- F6: Friday's continuation sits in high-relative-volume Fridays, at 0.0094 (t 4.00) against 0.0004 (t 0.61) on low-volume Fridays. It is also one-sided toward falls: Friday falls beyond -2 z are followed by -1.75% (t -3.88, n 146) and falls of -1 to -2 z by -0.59% (t -3.06), while rises beyond 2 z are followed by only +0.40% (t 0.67).
