Idea: When a coin's 20-day volatility is low relative to its own history, its 1-day return, scaled by that volatility, is followed by a move in the same direction over the next 2 days, and the larger the move, the larger the follow-through.
Consequence: In calm periods a trader would follow large daily moves rather than fade them.
Why:
- F6: Across all weekdays (non-overlapping 2-day grid), the 1-day scaled move reverses over 2 days in the coin's highest volatility tercile (slope -0.0058, t -3.61, n 5319). It continues in the lowest tercile (slope +0.0026, t 3.36, n 5321). The high-volatility reversal is negative in 7 of 8 years, with t -2.2 to -3.2 in 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2025.
