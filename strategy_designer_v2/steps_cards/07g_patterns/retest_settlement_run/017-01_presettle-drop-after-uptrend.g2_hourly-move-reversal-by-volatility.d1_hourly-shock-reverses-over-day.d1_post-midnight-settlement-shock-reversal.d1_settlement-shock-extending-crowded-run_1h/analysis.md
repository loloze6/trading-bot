## Findings
- **F1:** Across all hours except 00:00, fading the coin's 24-hour return (in units of its 24-hour hourly volatility) over the next 24 hours depends on the volatility regime. When the 24-hour volatility is in the top third relative to its 30-day level, the fade pays: slope +25.4 bp per unit, t 14.3, sign mean +0.26 %, t 15.1, on 98,930 overlapping bar cases. When it is in the bottom third, the sign flips: slope −16.4 bp, t −10.5, sign mean −0.10 %, t −7.7. In calm regimes the 24-hour move continues rather than reverses.
- **F2:** On the 00:00 UTC bar, the tested idea's gain sits almost entirely in high relative volatility. In the aligned cases, the top third of volatility gives slope 55.2 bp, t 5.5, sign mean +0.77 %, t 6.3, on 2,012 cases. The bottom third gives slope 11.8 bp, t 1.4, sign mean +0.16 %. Fading the full 24-hour return at 00:00 shows the same split: top third t 5.0, sign mean +0.39 %; bottom third t 0.4, sign mean −0.01 %.
- **F3:** In the hours just after the 00:00 UTC settlement, an hour that moves against the coin's previous 23-hour return is followed by a 24-hour return in that hour's own direction, not a fade. Fading it gives sign means of −0.21 %, −0.25 % and −0.29 % at 01:00, 02:00 and 03:00, with t −4.2, −4.8 and −5.7, on about 8,900 cases each. The same opposed hours at 11:00–14:00 give positive fades (t +2.2 to +3.5). Counter-trend 3-hour moves ending at 03:00 also continue: slope +21 bp, t 2.7, on 5,815 cases, while those ending at 09:00, 15:00 and 21:00 show nothing (t 0.08, 0.16, −0.83).
- **F4:** Fading the coin's 24-hour return depends on the hour it is read at. Read at 23:00–04:00 UTC, the fade pays, with slopes +12 to +23 bp and t 2.7 to 5.0 (01:00: t 5.0, sign mean +0.17 %). Read at 12:00–15:00 UTC, it is negative, with slopes −8 to −17 bp; at 14:00 the t is −3.8 and the sign mean is −0.09 %. Volume follows the same clock: relative log volume is +0.15 at 00:00 and −0.39 at 05:00, and peaks at +0.45 at 16:00.
- **F5:** The tested aligned post-settlement fade holds in every year, with t from 1.6 to 3.7 from 2018 to 2025, and in every coin, with t from 1.5 for BTC to 3.7 for LTC and XMR. The weakest coin is BTC: slope 11.8 bp, sign mean +0.24 %. The broader unconditioned 00:00 fade of the 24-hour return has faded away: t 4.6 in 2018, −0.4 in 2024 and −1.5 in 2025.
- **F6:** The book's worst days come with the coin's largest moves. The bottom decile of book days averages −2.99 % on days when the coin moved 6.3 % in absolute terms. The middle deciles see coin moves of 1.9–3.4 %. Losses concentrate on days of outsized moves, not at random.
