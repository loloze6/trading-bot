Idea: The larger a coin's 72-hour return against its usual 72-hour move, the more it rises over the next 72 hours.
Consequence: A strong three-day run is a reason to hold or add to a long for the next three days rather than fade it.
Why:
- F4: A strong 72-hour rise keeps going over the next 72 hours.
