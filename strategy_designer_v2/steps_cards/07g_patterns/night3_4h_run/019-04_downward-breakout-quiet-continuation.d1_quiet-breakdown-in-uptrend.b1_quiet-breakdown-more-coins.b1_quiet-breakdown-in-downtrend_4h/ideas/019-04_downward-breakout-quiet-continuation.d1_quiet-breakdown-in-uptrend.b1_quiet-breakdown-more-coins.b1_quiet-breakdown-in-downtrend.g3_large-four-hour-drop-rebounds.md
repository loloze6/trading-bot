Idea: After a four-hour bar whose return is a fall, the coin rises over the next day. The larger that fall is against the coin's usual four-hour move, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Buy after a large single four-hour drop, sized by how unusual the drop was, and refuse to short into it.
Why:
- F6: After a single 4h fall, the coin rebounds over the next day, and the larger the fall against the coin's usual 4h move, the larger the rebound.
