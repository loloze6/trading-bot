Idea: When a four-hour bar closes beyond the preceding two-day range against the sign of the coin's 30-day return, the coin moves back the other way over the next three days. The wider that two-day range is compared with the coin's 30-day average four-hour range, the larger the move back.
Consequence: Fade wide-range breaks that go against the month's trend, more so the wider the range, and refuse to follow them.
Why:
- F5: A 4h close beyond the two-day range against the sign of the 30-day return reverses.
