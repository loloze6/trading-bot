Idea: When a coin's past 30 days have moved steadily in one direction compared with the sum of its daily moves, its 7-day fall counted in 30-day daily swings tends to be followed by a rise over the next 7 days. The larger that scaled fall, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Outside chop, a trader should refuse to follow a sharp weekly fall and expect part of it to be given back.
Why:
- F5: The tested 7-day continuation reverses outside chop. Signed by the 7-day move, the next 7 days are -1.47 % at an efficiency ratio of 0.3 to 0.45 (t -1.13, n 179) and -3.70 % at |scaled move| ≤ 0.5 with a ratio above 0.3 (t -2.62, n 82). Outside chop, down weeks are followed by +3.12 % (t 2.26, n 115).
