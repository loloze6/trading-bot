Idea: When a coin's past 30 days have gone back and forth with little net progress compared with the sum of its daily moves, its 30-day move counted in 30-day daily swings tends to be followed by a move the same way over the next 7 days. The larger that scaled 30-day move, the larger the following move.
Consequence: In moderately choppy conditions, a trader should lean with the coin's month-long drift over the following week, not against it.
Why:
- F4: When the 30-day efficiency ratio is between 0.15 and 0.3, the next 7 days follow the sign of the 30-day move by +3.36 % demeaned (t 3.89, n 289 non-overlapping cases). This is larger than at a ratio of 0.15 or below (+0.86 %, t 1.10, n 323) and above 0.3 (+0.41 %, t 0.32, n 179). It is positive on all 6 coins and in 9 of 12 windows.
