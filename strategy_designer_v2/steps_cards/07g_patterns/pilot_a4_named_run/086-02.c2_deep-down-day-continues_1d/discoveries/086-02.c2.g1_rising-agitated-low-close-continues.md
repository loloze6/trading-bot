A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, agitated market, the closer a coin's day closes to its low within the day's range, the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In agitated uptrends, a day that closes at its low is a reason to short for the following week, scaled by how near the low it closed, rather than to buy it as a rebound.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a rising agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 086-02.c2_deep-down-day-continues_1d (idea 086-02.c2_deep-down-day-continues, brief 086_rising-agitated-first-dip-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: In the rising agitated profile, sampled every 5 days on its own grid, the days with the lowest closes within their range (bottom quartile of close location, 32 cases) were followed by a short-side excess move of +4.98 % over 5 days (t 2.98); the other three quartiles were near zero (-2.17 %, -0.48 %, -0.71 %, all |t| < 0.6). Low closes inside agitated uptrends kept falling. The held agitated long books (039-02, 045-01) buy these same days as rebounds, so this is the opposite side.
- F3: The tested 10-day short had no edge. Its unusual move was -0.16 % per case (t -0.10, 218 days). The total move was -1.32 %, against a cost of 0.37 % a trade. The book's Sharpe was -0.08.
