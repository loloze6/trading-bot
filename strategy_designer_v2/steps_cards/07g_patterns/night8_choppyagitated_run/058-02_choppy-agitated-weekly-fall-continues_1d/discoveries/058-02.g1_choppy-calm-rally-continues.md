A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, calm market, a coin whose 7-day return is a rise keeps rising over the next 7 days, and the larger that 7-day rise is against the coin's usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger the further rise.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a trendless, quiet coin would ride a strong week-long rally for another week rather than fade it as range-bound.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 058-02_choppy-agitated-weekly-fall-continues_1d (idea 058-02_choppy-agitated-weekly-fall-continues, brief 058_choppy-agitated-weekly-continuation).
What it rests on:
- F3: In choppy markets outside the agitated profile (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %, calm swing), a week with a large rise against the usual swing kept rising over the next 7 days. The top quartile of the 7-day scaled return was followed by an unusual move of +11.5 % (18 cases, 9 days, clustered t 3.34). The three lower quartiles gave +0.0 %, -1.5 % and -4.0 %, all with |t| ≤ 1.3.
- F4: The same lean shows weakly across all bars outside the profile, but the grading is faint. The top two quintiles of the 7-day scaled return were followed by +2.4 % unusual 7-day moves (t 1.98 and 1.70, about 160 cases each). The bottom two gave -0.6 % (t -0.5). The rank correlation is only 0.035 over 795 grid cases.
