A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, a coin's move over the past 7 days, measured in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days, continues in the same direction over the next 7 days, and the larger that scaled move, the larger the further move.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a quiet coin that has been sliding for two months should expect a week's sharp fall to keep falling and a week's sharp rise to keep rising, rather than reverting, and should size by how large the week was against the coin's usual swing.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2_choppy-calm-rise-one-week_1d (idea 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2_choppy-calm-rise-one-week, brief 059_choppy-calm-weekly-rise-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: In a falling, calm market (60-day return below -10 %, 30-day swing at or below the coin's median), the coin's next-7-day excess return grows with its past 7-day return scaled by its usual 7-day swing: slope +3.88 % per unit of scaled move, day-clustered t 2.04, on 117 grid cases over 40 days. In a falling, agitated market the same slope is -0.62 % (t -0.47, 168 cases, 50 days).
- F2: In the falling, calm market the past week's move carries on in its own direction. After a 7-day fall, the next 7 days averaged -6.38 % against the coin's drift (72 cases). After a 7-day rise they averaged +4.61 % (45 cases). Taken in the direction of the week's move, the excess is +5.70 % per case, t 2.61 with the cases of one day summed (40 days).
- F3: This continuation in falling calm markets is not steady across years. The slope is +6.49 % (t 2.66) in 2018 and +5.32 % (t 4.13) in 2021, but -1.38 % (t -0.95) in 2019. Across coins the slope is positive on all six with enough cases (ADA t 2.34, LTC 1.99, XRP 1.72, ZEC 1.15, XMR 0.64, ETH 0.65).
