Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a down day on heavy volume against the coin's own 20-day average is followed by a next-day rise that is larger the deeper the coin's 5-day return was at that day's close.
Consequence: A heavy-volume fall that ends a deep 5-day slide in a falling, agitated market is the forced-selling flush most worth buying for the next day, while one after a shallow slide barely repays.
Why:
- F1: Down days rebound more after a deeper 5-day fall. Among the book's 227 cases (down days in the falling agitated profile, volume above the 20-day average), the third with the deepest 5-day return gained +5.66 % above drift the next day (t 2.99, 29 days). The middle third gained +1.34 % (t 1.57, 51 days) and the shallowest third +0.44 % (t 0.46, 44 days). The deep-minus-shallow gap is about +5.2 %, with a rough t near 2.5.
