A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, calm market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its rebound over the next 3 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a quiet uptrend should treat an unusually large down day as a buying opportunity for a few days. The same buy is not worth making in quiet choppy or falling markets.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01.b1.b1_choppy-calm-rise-two-weeks_1d (idea 059-01.b1.b1_choppy-calm-rise-two-weeks, brief 059_choppy-calm-weekly-rise-continues).
What it rests on:
- F2: After a large one-day fall scaled by the coin's 30-day daily swing, the coin rebounds in rising calm markets. On the 100 days with a scaled fall below -1.09 (54 distinct days), the next day's excess return is +1.85 % (t 2.88, days clustered) and the next 3 days' is +3.35 % (t 2.78).
- F3: The same rebound is absent in the other calm profiles. In choppy calm the next-day excess is -0.70 % (t -0.75, 44 cases). In falling calm it is -0.01 % next day (t -0.02) and -1.83 % over 3 days (t -1.73, 151 cases).
- F4: Over all profiles, the next-day excess after a scaled fall grows with the fall's size: slope 0.0084 per unit (t 2.35, 4,035 falling days). The largest-fall decile gains +1.11 % next day (t 2.51 clustered, 822 cases on 238 days). The yearly means are +0.84 %, -0.02 %, +1.57 % and +1.64 % for 2018 to 2021.
