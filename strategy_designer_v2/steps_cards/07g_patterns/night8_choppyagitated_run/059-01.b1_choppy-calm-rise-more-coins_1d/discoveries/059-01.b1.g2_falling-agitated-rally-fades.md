A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's rise on a day against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the more it falls back over the next 1 to 3 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader can sell or short a sharp up day in a falling, swinging coin, expecting the bounce to be given back within days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01.b1_choppy-calm-rise-more-coins_1d (idea 059-01.b1_choppy-calm-rise-more-coins, brief 059_choppy-calm-weekly-rise-continues).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a falling, agitated market, the next day's excess return falls steadily as the day's scaled move rises: by quintile of the 1-day move it is +1.2 %, +1.3 %, −1.1 %, −1.4 % and −1.4 % (day-clustered t 1.49, 1.80, −1.26, −2.34, −2.03). After the largest rises, the excess return over the next 3 days is −3.6 % (t −1.74). The choppy agitated cell shows the same next-day giveback after the largest rises (−1.4 %, t −2.29), while rising agitated does not (+0.6 %, t 0.96).
