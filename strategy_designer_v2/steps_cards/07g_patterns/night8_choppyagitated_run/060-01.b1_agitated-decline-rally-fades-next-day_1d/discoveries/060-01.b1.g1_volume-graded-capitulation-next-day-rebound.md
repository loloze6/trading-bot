A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, a coin's down day traded on volume that is larger against its average daily volume of the past 30 days is followed by a larger rise over the next 1 day.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in an agitated decline should treat a heavy-volume fall as forced selling likely to be partly repaid by the next day's close, and a light-volume fall as a weaker reason to buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
