A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, the nearer a coin closes to the lowest close of its last 7 days, the larger its further fall over the next 7 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a coin that has been falling for two months should short, or refuse to buy, a close pinned at its weekly low, rather than expect a rebound.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2.d1_choppy-calm-rise-held-high_1d (idea 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2.d1_choppy-calm-rise-held-high, brief 059_choppy-calm-weekly-rise-continues).
What it rests on:
- F1: Outside the tested profile, where a coin closes in its own 7-day closing range predicts the next 7 days. On the grid at offset 0 (1,143 cases), closes in the lowest quarter of the range trail the coin's normal 7-day drift (+1.43 %) by 2.7 %, and closes in the top quarter beat it by 1.2 % (Welch t 2.76). The top-minus-bottom difference is positive on 5 of the 7 grid offsets, with Welch t 2.76, 1.43, 3.49, 1.00 and 2.11, and slightly negative on the other two (-0.39 and -0.13).
- F2: The shortfall after a close in the bottom quarter of the 7-day range holds in every year shown: -2.6 % in 2018 (93 cases), -1.8 % in 2019 (60), -3.1 % in 2020 (72) and -2.2 % in 2021 (103).
- F3: In a falling market (60-day return below -10 %), closes in the lower half of the 7-day range trail the drift by 1.8 % (bottom quarter, 90 cases) and 4.1 % (second quarter, 63 cases). Closes in the upper half beat it by 1.7 % in each quarter (70 and 62 cases). So a fall in a falling market continues; this is the falling-agitated short box the map shows empty, and the falling-calm short box held only by 035-01.b1.
