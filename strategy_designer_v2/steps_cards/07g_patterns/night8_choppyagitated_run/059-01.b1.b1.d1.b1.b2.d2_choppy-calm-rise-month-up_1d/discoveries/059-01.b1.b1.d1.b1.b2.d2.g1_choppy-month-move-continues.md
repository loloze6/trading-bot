A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin's return over the next 7 days follows the direction of its return over the past 30 days, and the larger that 30-day move, the larger the move that follows the same way, both after rises and after falls.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a trendless market should lean with the coin's last month, long after a strong month and short after a weak one, more for a bigger month, rather than fade it as range trading would.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2.d2_choppy-calm-rise-month-up_1d (idea 059-01.b1.b1.d1.b1.b2.d2_choppy-calm-rise-month-up, brief 059_choppy-calm-weekly-rise-continues).
