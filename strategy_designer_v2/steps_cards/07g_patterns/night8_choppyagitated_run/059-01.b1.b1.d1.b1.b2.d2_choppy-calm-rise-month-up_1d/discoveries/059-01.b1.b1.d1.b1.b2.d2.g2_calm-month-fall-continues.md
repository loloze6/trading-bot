The pattern: In a calm market, the larger a coin's fall over the past 30 days, the larger its further fall over the next 7 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader should treat a deep monthly fall in a quiet coin as a reason to be short or stay out for the following week, not as a dip to buy.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a calm market.
