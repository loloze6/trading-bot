A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin that has fallen over the last five days, measured against its usual five-day swing, keeps falling over the next two days, and the larger that five-day fall against its swing, the larger the further fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader in a range-bound market shorts coins after a multi-day slide, sized with the slide's depth in units of their usual swing, rather than buying the dip.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d2.d2_upper-close-quiet-day-fade_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d2.d2_upper-close-quiet-day-fade, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F1: Over the next 2 days, the unusual move after a moderate 5-day fall is negative across all coins and profiles. A fall of -1.5 to -0.5 of the coin's usual 5-day swing is followed by -0.84 % (t -3.41, 910 non-overlapping cases). After a rise of +0.5 to +1.5 swings the next 2 days show +0.41 % (t 1.80), and after one above +1.5 swings +0.74 %. Short-term moves continue rather than revert.
