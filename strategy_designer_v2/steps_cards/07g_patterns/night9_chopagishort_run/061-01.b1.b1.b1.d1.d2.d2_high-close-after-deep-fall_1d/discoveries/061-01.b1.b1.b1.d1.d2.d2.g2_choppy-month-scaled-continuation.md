A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin's 20-day return measured against its daily swing continues over the next five days, and the larger that scaled return, the larger the move in its direction.
What it would mean for a trader: In coins that have gone nowhere for two months, a trader leans with the last month's move, sized by its strength against the coin's swing, rather than fading it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2_high-close-after-deep-fall_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2_high-close-after-deep-fall, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F6: Across all bars in a choppy market, the 20-day return scaled by the 20-day swing also leans toward continuation: slope 0.065 (t 2.65, 189 cases, 76 days). Its terciles go -5.9 % (t -1.87), -2.0 %, +8.7 % (t 1.33). The slope is 0.005 in falling markets (t 0.44) and 0.013 in rising ones (t 1.13). It is not stable by year: -0.030 in 2018, +0.088 in 2021.
- F4: Among all high-close bars, the next 5 days follow the coin's 20-day return, but only in a choppy market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %). There the slope of the unusual 5-day move on the 20-day return is 0.309 (t 5.50 with cases of one day pooled, 44 cases on 29 days). In falling markets the slope is 0.074 (t 0.82, 86 cases) and in rising markets 0.042 (t 0.65, 118 cases). On bars without a high close it is -0.0005 (t -0.03, 1608 cases).
