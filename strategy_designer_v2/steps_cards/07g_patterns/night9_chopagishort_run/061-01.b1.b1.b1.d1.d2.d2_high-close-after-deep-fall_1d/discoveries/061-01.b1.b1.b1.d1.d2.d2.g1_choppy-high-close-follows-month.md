A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, when a coin's daily close sits near the top of that day's high-low range, its price moves over the next five days in the direction of its 20-day return, and the larger that 20-day return in either direction, the larger the move.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader reads a high close in a range-bound coin through its last 20 days: he fades it after a 20-day fall, follows it after a 20-day rise, and sizes the trade by the size of that return.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2_high-close-after-deep-fall_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2_high-close-after-deep-fall, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F4: Among all high-close bars, the next 5 days follow the coin's 20-day return, but only in a choppy market (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %). There the slope of the unusual 5-day move on the 20-day return is 0.309 (t 5.50 with cases of one day pooled, 44 cases on 29 days). In falling markets the slope is 0.074 (t 0.82, 86 cases) and in rising markets 0.042 (t 0.65, 118 cases). On bars without a high close it is -0.0005 (t -0.03, 1608 cases).
- F5: Among choppy high-close bars, those in the lowest third of 20-day return had an unusual 5-day move of -9.7 % (t -2.42, 11 cases on 11 days). The high-close cases in the lowest third of 20-day return across all trends fell 3.9 % (t -2.02, 136 cases, 44 days) and over 2 days fell 3.4 % (t -1.99).
- F2: The tested situation shows clearly on the non-overlapping 5-day grid.
