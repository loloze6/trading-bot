A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a calm market, a coin whose 20-day return is a deep fall keeps falling over the next five days, and the deeper that 20-day fall, the larger the further fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader shorts coins that have dropped hard over 20 days while their swing is no larger than usual, and stands aside from the same short when the market is agitated, where such falls rebound instead.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a calm market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d1.d1.d1_choppy-deep-month-fall-continues_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d1.d1.d1_choppy-deep-month-fall-continues, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F2: Deep 20-day falls (−10 % or worse) kept falling in calm markets in all three trends. Falling calm: −3.5 % over 5 days (112 cases, 36 days, t −1.98). Choppy calm: −6.9 % (28 cases, t −1.99). Rising calm: −8.8 % (17 cases, t −1.96).
- F3: In falling agitated markets the same deep falls did not continue: +1.1 % (185 cases, 48 days, t +0.48). Falls deeper than 30 % in falling markets gave −0.05 % (88 cases, t −0.02). The continuation is a calm-market effect, and the agitated side is the rebound family the profile map already holds (16 falling-agitated long books).
- F1: In choppy markets on folds B and C, the deeper the 20-day fall, the more the coin lags its 5-day drift. Falls of 10–15 % were followed by −3.3 % (15 cases, t −2.39), falls of 15–20 % by −8.6 % (16 cases, t −2.18) and falls of 20–30 % by −11.6 % (10 cases, t −1.62). Shallow falls of 0–10 % were followed by only −1.0 % to −3.0 % (t −0.33 and −1.94). This is the tested idea's grading, but on few cases.
