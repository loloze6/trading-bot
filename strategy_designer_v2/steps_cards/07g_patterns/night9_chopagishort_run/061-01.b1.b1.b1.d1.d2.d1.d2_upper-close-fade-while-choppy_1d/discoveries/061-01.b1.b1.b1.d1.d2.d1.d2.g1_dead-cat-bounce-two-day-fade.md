A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: When a coin closes in the upper half of its daily range after its 5-day return has been negative, its price falls over the next two days, the fall growing with the depth of that 5-day fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A bounce close inside a falling week is a short for two days, larger the deeper the week's fall, and a reason not to buy that bounce.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d1.d2_upper-close-fade-while-choppy_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d1.d2_upper-close-fade-while-choppy, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
