A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, a coin's 20-day return, measured against its usual 30-day swing, continues over the next five days, and the larger that scaled 20-day move, the larger the move in the same direction that follows.
What it would mean for a trader: In a market going nowhere over two months, a trader follows the coin's 20-day drift for the next five days (short after a scaled 20-day fall, long after a rise), sized by how large that drift is against the coin's usual swing.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2.d1_high-close-fall-falling-market_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1.d2.d2.d1_high-close-fall-falling-market, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
What it rests on:
- F2: In a choppy market (60-day return between −10 % and +10 %), the coin's 20-day return continues over the next 5 days. The unusual 5-day move rises 7.07 % per unit of the 20-day return scaled by its 30-day swing (t 2.57, 189 cases, 76 days). The same slope is 0.82 % in falling markets (t 0.71, 527 cases) and 1.05 % in rising ones (t 0.99, 484 cases).
- F3: The continuation in choppy markets also shows on the short side. After a negative 20-day return in a choppy market, the next 5 days' unusual move averaged −5.05 % (t −2.31, 97 cases, 50 days). In choppy agitated markets it averaged −8.82 % (t −2.10, 36 cases, 24 days), against +0.59 % in falling agitated markets (t 0.27, 149 cases).
- F4: The book's 5-day 20-day-return slope is not driven by one period: it is positive in both halves of the shown windows (w7–w12: 0.93 %, t 1.01; w13–w18: 1.33 %, t 1.44). It reaches a t above 2 only inside the choppy profile (choppy calm 6.74 %, t 2.30, 125 cases).
