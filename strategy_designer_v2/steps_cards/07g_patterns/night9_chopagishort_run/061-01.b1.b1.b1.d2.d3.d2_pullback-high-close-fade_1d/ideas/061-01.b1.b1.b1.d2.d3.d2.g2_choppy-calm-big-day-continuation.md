Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a coin's up day that is large against its usual daily swing over the last 20 days is followed by a further rise over the next five days, and the larger the day's gain against that swing, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader buys a large up day in a calm market that has gone nowhere for two months, instead of fading it as in agitated chop.
Why:
- F5: Outside the profile, in choppy markets (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %, mostly the calm side), an up day above one usual daily move is followed by a 5-day excess of +8.25 % (t 2.04, 28 cases on 19 days). In rising markets the same days give +2.59 % (t 1.18) and in falling markets +0.73 % (t 0.44). This is the opposite of the fade seen in the agitated profile.
