A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: When a coin's daily close sits near the top of that day's high-low range after its 5-day return has been negative, its price falls over the next two days. The deeper that 5-day fall is against the coin's usual daily swing, and the nearer the close is to the day's high, the larger the fall.
What it would mean for a trader: In any market, a trader shorts a day that closes near its high at the end of a 5-day fall, and expects the bounce to be given back within two days, more so the deeper the fall was.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1_high-close-after-fall-fades_1d (idea 061-01.b1.b1.b1.d1_high-close-after-fall-fades, brief 061_choppy-agitated-high-close-fades).
