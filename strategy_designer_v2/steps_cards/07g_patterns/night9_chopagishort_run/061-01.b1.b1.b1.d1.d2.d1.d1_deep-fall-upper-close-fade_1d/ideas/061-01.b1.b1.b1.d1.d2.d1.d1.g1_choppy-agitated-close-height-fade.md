Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the higher a coin's daily close sits in that day's high-low range, the more its price falls over the next five days, with closes pinned near the high followed by the largest falls.
Consequence: A trader shorts upper-range closes in choppy agitated markets and sizes the short by how near the close is to the high, rather than by the depth of the prior 5-day fall.
Why:
- F1: In the choppy agitated profile, with no condition on the 5-day return, closes in the top fifth of the day's range (location above 0.8) were followed by a 5-day unusual move of -7.18 %, t -2.80, over 21 cases on 16 days. Closes at 0.5 to 0.8 were followed by -6.87 % (t -1.29, 26 cases on 17 days). Closes at or below 0.5 were followed by +1.33 % (t 0.67, 39 cases on 34 days). The fall grows with close location and appears only in the upper half.
- F2: Outside the profile, the same 5-day grading points the other way. Closes above 0.8 were followed by +0.76 % (t 1.03, 389 cases), and closes at 0.6 to 0.8 by +0.98 % (t 1.49, 391 cases). The fade after a high close therefore lives in the choppy agitated profile, and it was negative in every year with cases: -4.0 % in 2019, -12.8 % in 2020 and -1.8 % in 2021 (upper-half closes).
- F3: In the profile, the tested grading by the depth of the 5-day fall is not monotonic. Over 5 days, the z5 quintiles gave -9.1 %, -5.2 %, -1.3 %, -1.7 % and -6.4 % (each with 5 to 22 cases). This matches step 5's failed check (top third minus bottom third of |forecast| -1.67 %). Close location carries the effect better than fall depth.
- F6: Effects from the other angles cannot be told from chance once the cases of one day are pooled. The 2-day move after Sundays was -1.15 % (t -1.42, 111 days). The 5-day move after the top quintile of one-day moves was +2.06 % (t 1.81, 107 days). The idea's size, +3.17 % per case, is 14 times the 0.22 % cost of a trade, so cost is not what limits the book.
