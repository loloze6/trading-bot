Idea: When a coin has fallen for four or more four-hour periods in a row with only a small total loss against its typical four-hour move over the past 30 days, it keeps falling over the next three days. The smaller the run's loss for its length, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A long but shallow down-run is a reason to refuse a bounce trade and lean short for three days.
Why:
- F5: Falls did not mirror rises. Down-runs of 4 or more bars were followed by −0.16 % (fading volume, t −0.76) and −0.37 % (rising volume, t −1.89). The smallest-gain tercile of down-runs continued down by −1.02 % (t −3.87, n 711), while the largest tercile turned up by +0.75 % (t 1.84, n 711).
