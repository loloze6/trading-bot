Idea: When a coin's 4-hour moves over the past day are large against its 4-hour moves over the past 30 days, the coin rises more than usual over the next three days. The larger that ratio, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader would lean long after a coin's volatility expands sharply and refuse shorts taken into such expansions.
Why:
- F4: A recent volatility expansion is followed by rises. The ratio of the 1-day to the 30-day standard deviation of 4h returns, by quintile, is followed by unusual 18-bar moves of -0.73 % (t -2.99), -0.15 %, -0.11 %, +0.39 % and +0.49 % (t 1.71), about 1030 cases each. Top minus bottom is +1.1 % to +2.3 % in 5 of 6 coins; XMR is the exception at -0.10 %.
