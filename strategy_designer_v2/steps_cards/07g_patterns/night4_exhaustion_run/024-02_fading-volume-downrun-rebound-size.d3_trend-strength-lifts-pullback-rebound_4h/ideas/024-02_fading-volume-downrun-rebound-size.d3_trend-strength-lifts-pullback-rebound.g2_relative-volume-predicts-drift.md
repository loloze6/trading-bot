Idea: A coin's return over the next three days rises with its past day's volume relative to its 30-day average: unusually heavy days are followed by gains above its normal drift, and unusually quiet days by gains below it.
Consequence: A trader leans toward holding a coin after heavy-volume days and away from it after quiet ones, in proportion to how unusual the volume is.
Why:
- F2: The excess 3-day move rises with the past day's volume against its 30-day average. The top fifth gave +1.13 % (t 3.64) and the bottom fifth −0.73 % (t −3.01), on about 1030 cases each. This survives on the two other non-overlapping grids: top fifth +0.54 % (t 1.74) and +0.94 % (t 3.15); bottom fifth −0.27 % (t −1.15) and −0.56 % (t −2.42).
- F3: The top-volume effect is positive in 5 of 6 coins (XRP +3.12 %, t 3.08; LTC −0.16 %) and in 7 of 8 years (2018 −0.32 %; 2020, 2021 and 2024 each above +1.6 % with t above 2), so no single coin or year carries it.
