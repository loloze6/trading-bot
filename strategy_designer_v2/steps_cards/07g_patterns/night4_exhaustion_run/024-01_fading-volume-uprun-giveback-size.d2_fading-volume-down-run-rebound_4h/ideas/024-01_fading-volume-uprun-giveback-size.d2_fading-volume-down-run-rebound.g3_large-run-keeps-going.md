Idea: The larger a coin's 3-day rise measured in its usual 4-hour moves over the past 30 days, the more it keeps rising over the next 3 days.
Consequence: An unusually large 3-day rise is a reason to stay long or buy for 3 more days, not to fade it.
Why:
- F6: A large rise keeps going rather than giving back. The top fifth of the 3-day return in usual moves is followed by +0.98 % (t 3.34, 1059 cases), and by t 2.33 when volume is also in its top fifth. This contradicts the brief's give-back for rises.
