Idea: The larger a coin's 7-day return, measured in units of its own 30-day volatility, the more it rises over the next three days beyond its normal drift.
Consequence: A trader sizes a long by the coin's volatility-scaled one-week rise rather than by its raw 30-day return.
Why:
- F3: The coin's 7-day return, divided by its 30-day bar volatility, carries the tested idea's effect.
- F4: The 30-day return alone is weak and unstable.
