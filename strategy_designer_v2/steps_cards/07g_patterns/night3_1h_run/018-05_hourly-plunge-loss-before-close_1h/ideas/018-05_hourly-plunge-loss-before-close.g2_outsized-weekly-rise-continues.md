Idea: When a coin's 7-day return, read at an hourly close, rises to a large multiple of its 30-day average absolute 7-day return, the price keeps rising over the next 7 days, and the larger the rise in those multiples, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: An outsized weekly rise is a reason to hold or add a long for the following week rather than to fade it.
