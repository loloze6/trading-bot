Idea: While a coin's last 20 days have been volatile relative to its own history, the larger its one-day fall in usual daily moves, the more it rises over the next day.
Consequence: A trader would buy a coin after a sharp down day in a high-volatility stretch, scaled to the size of the fall, and would refuse to short it into the next day.
