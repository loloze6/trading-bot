Idea: The larger a coin's return from Saturday's close to Tuesday's close (UTC), measured in its usual daily moves, the more the coin moves the other way over Wednesday. A weekend-to-Tuesday rise is followed by a Wednesday fall, and a fall by a Wednesday rise, each larger the larger the 3-day move.
Consequence: A trader would lean against a coin's weekend-to-Tuesday move over Wednesday, in proportion to the size of that move, and would refuse Wednesday trades in the direction of that move.
Why:
- F1: The Wednesday (UTC) move reverses the move from Saturday's close to Tuesday's close, and the reversal is graded. On Tuesday bars, the Spearman correlation between the 3-day vol-scaled return and Wednesday's excess return is -0.137 (p 6e-10, 2033 cases). This is the strongest of the 7 weekday tests, which otherwise range from -0.08 to +0.03. With the cases of one day taken together, -z3 × Wednesday excess has a t of 2.11 over 346 days.
