Idea: In a coin whose 30-day rise covers a large share of its 30-day high-low range, the larger its 5-day return, the further it rises beyond its normal drift over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A trader would favour clean 30-day risers that have just pushed hard over the last 5 days, holding them for the next 10 days, over those that have stalled.
Why:
- F3: Inside clean 30-day rises (ratio ≥ 0.5, 574 cases), the cases in the top tercile of 5-day return gained +6.5 % over 10 days beyond drift (t 2.89, 192 cases). The middle tercile gained −0.2 % and the bottom tercile +1.8 %. That top-tercile excess:
- F5: The book earns by avoiding the coins' bad days more than by catching their full gains.
