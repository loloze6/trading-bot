Idea: The larger a coin's 1-day rise relative to its usual daily move over the past 20 days, the further it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader would follow a coin's unusually large up day for the following 5 days instead of fading it.
Why:
- F3: A large 1-day rise was followed by further rise over the next 5 days, and large falls were not followed by further falls.
