A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: A coin whose 20-day return is negative keeps falling over the next 3 days, and the deeper that 20-day fall for the coin's own daily swing, the larger the further fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A coin down over the past 20 days for its usual swing is a reason to hold a short over the next few days, sized by the depth of the fall, rather than to buy the dip.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-01.b1.b1.b1.b1_choppy-two-sided-stretch-carries_1d (idea 067-01.b1.b1.b1.b1_choppy-two-sided-stretch-carries, brief 067_choppy-average-stretch-carries-forward).
What it rests on:
- F3: Momentum over the coin's 20-day return shows on both sides at 3 days. After a falling 20 days the unusual 3-day move is negative at every swing level: -1.13 % (t -2.45, 291 cases) at low swing, -0.55 % (t -1.08) at middle swing and -1.47 % (t -2.17, 246 cases) at high swing. After a rising 20 days it is +1.57 % (t 3.04, 273 cases) at middle swing.
- F1: On folds B and C, across every market (all trends together), the coin's 10-day unusual move rises steadily with the close's stretch from its 20-day average in 20-day daily swings, from -2.68 % (t -2.04, 141 cases) in the lowest fifth through -1.97 %, -0.24 % and +2.02 % to +3.74 % (t 2.69, 141 cases) in the highest. Both sides carry, and at that size they are about 14 to 20 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
