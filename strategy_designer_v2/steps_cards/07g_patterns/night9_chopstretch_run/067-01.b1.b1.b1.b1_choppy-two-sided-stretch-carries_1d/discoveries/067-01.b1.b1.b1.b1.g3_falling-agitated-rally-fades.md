A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, a coin's one-day rise, measured in its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns, is followed by a fall over the next 3 days, and the larger the rise for the coin's swing, the larger the fall.
What it would mean for a trader: In a falling, agitated coin, a sharp up day is a reason to short for about three days instead of chasing it.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-01.b1.b1.b1.b1_choppy-two-sided-stretch-carries_1d (idea 067-01.b1.b1.b1.b1_choppy-two-sided-stretch-carries, brief 067_choppy-average-stretch-carries-forward).
What it rests on:
- F4: In falling markets, the largest one-day rises for the coin's swing (top fifth) are followed by a 3-day unusual move of -2.71 % (t -3.42, 109 cases), -2.57 % (t -2.87, 91 cases) when the market is agitated. In agitated falling markets the move falls with the rise's size from the middle fifth, from +2.11 % to -0.28 % to -2.57 %.
- F5: The fall in F4 holds on every coin, from -0.95 % to -2.44 %, none significant alone. By year it holds in 2018 to 2020 (t -2.10, -3.30, -3.79) but reverses in 2021 (+4.87 %, t 3.54, 22 cases), so it is not stable through time. It may also be the profile map's falling-agitated "next day reversal" (058-02.b1.g1.b1.b1) seen at a longer horizon.
