A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, calm market, the further a coin's close sits above its 20-day average, measured in units of the standard deviation of its daily returns over the same 20 days, the more it keeps rising over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader would buy coins that push well above their average while the past two months have been falling and calm, and would buy more the larger the push, instead of fading it as a bounce in a downtrend.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling calm market.
