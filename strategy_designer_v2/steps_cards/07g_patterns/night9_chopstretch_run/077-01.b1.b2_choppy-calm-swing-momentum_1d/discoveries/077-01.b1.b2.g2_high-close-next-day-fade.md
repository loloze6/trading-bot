A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: The nearer a coin's daily close lies to the top of that day's high-low range, the lower its return over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A close at the day's high is a reason to short the coin for a day, or to refuse a new long, on the side the profile map leaves thinnest.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 077-01.b1.b2_choppy-calm-swing-momentum_1d (idea 077-01.b1.b2_choppy-calm-swing-momentum, brief 077_choppy-calm-swing-scaled-moves-carry).
What it rests on:
- F3: A day's close position in its own range grades the next day's excess return. By quintile of close position, from lowest to highest close, the next day's excess return is +0.47 % (t 3.56), +0.33 %, -0.14 %, -0.19 % and -0.35 % (t -2.66), about 1,650 days each. The top quintile, a close near the high, is a next-day fall of about 1.6 times the 0.22 % cost of a trade.
