A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: The higher a coin's daily volume against its own 30-day average volume, the higher its return over the next day; a day much quieter than usual is followed by a lower next day.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader can lean long for a day after unusually heavy volume and lean short after unusually light volume.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO.
