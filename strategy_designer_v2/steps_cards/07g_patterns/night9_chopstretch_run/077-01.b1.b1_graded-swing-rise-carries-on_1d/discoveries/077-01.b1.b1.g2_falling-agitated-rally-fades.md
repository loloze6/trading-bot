The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, a coin whose 14-day return is positive against its own daily swing over the past 30 days falls back over the next 7 days, and the larger that swing-scaled rise, the larger the fall back.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader can fade swing-scaled rallies in falling agitated markets, a short box the map holds only twice.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
