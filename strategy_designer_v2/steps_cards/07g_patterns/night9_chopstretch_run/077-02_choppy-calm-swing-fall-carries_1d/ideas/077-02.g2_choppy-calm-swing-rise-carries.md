Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a coin whose 14-day return is large and positive against its own daily swing over the past 30 days keeps rising over the next 7 days, and the larger that swing-scaled rise, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: Volatility-sized holders keep buying for days after a rise that is large against a small swing, so a trader can hold the long side of that rise.
Why:
- F2: Over the full range of that measure inside choppy calm, the 7-day unusual move rises with the swing-scaled 14-day return. The slope is +0.0435 per unit, with a day-clustered t of 3.17 over 117 cases on 60 days. Outside the profile the slope is +0.0122 (t 2.45, 708 cases). This is graded continuation, about 3.5 times stronger in the brief's profile.
- F4: The in-profile continuation leans to the upside. Rises give a slope of +0.050 (t 2.03, 65 cases, 35 days), while falls give +0.027 (t 0.55, 52 cases, 36 days). The swing-scaled momentum signed by the move's direction is positive on all 6 coins, from +0.1 % (XMR) to +8.1 % (ZEC) per 7 days.
