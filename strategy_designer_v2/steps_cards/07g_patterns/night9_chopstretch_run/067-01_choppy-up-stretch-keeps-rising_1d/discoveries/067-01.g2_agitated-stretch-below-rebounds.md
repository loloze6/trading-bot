A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In an agitated market, the further a coin's close stands below its 20-day average, measured in units of its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns, the more the coin rebounds over the next 10 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In a swinging market, a deep stretch below the average is a reason to refuse a short, since the calm-market carry turns into a rebound there.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 067-01_choppy-up-stretch-keeps-rising_1d (idea 067-01_choppy-up-stretch-keeps-rising, brief 067_choppy-average-stretch-carries-forward).
