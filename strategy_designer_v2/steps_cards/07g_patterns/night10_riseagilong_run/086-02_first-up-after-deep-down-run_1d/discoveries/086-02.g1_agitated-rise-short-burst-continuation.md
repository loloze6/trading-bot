A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising, agitated market, a coin's rise over the last 3 days continues over the next 3 days. The larger that 3-day return, measured against the standard deviation of the coin's daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger the further rise.
What it would mean for a trader: In a rising agitated market, a trader buys after a strong 3-day rise rather than waiting for a pullback to end, and buys more the larger the rise in usual swings, since dips there do not recover more than usual.
Seen on: 1d bars of LTC, ETH, XMR, INJ, GRT, DOGE, XRP, TRX, in a rising agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 086-02_first-up-after-deep-down-run_1d (idea 086-02_first-up-after-deep-down-run, brief 086_rising-agitated-run-break-continues).
What it rests on:
- F2: Inside the rising agitated profile, a coin's 3-day return scaled by its 30-day swing predicts its next days with the same sign.
- F3: The 3-day continuation holds on the rising side and is graded there.
- F4: The 3-day continuation is not carried by one period or one coin, but ETH does not share it.
- F1: The tested idea's effect runs the other way in this data. Its 5-day unusual move after the first up day is -0.11 % (t -0.13, 261 days, 389 cases), and its depth slope is negative (t -2.16): deeper down runs, relative to the coin's swing, are followed by weaker, not stronger, 5-day returns in rising agitated markets.
