A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, a down day whose high-to-low range is wide compared with the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days is followed by a rise over the next two days, and the wider that range is against the 20-day average, the larger the rise.
What it would mean for a trader: A violent down day inside an uptrend is a flush to buy rather than the start of a turn, most of all when the day's range is far beyond the usual.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising market.
