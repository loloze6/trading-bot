A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the nearer a day's close sits to that day's high, as a share of the day's high-to-low range, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: In a sideways market, a close pinned at the day's high is a reason to short the next day, more so the tighter the close is to that high.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 072-03_choppy-deep-down-day-continuation_1d (idea 072-03_choppy-deep-down-day-continuation, brief 072_choppy-deep-below-high-falls).
What it rests on:
- F4: Where the close sits in the day's range predicts the next day in reverse. Per unit of close position the next-day slope is −1.21 % (t −2.42, n 1,250) in choppy markets and −1.13 % (t −4.52, n 5,999) elsewhere. In choppy markets, up days closing in the top third of their range fall −0.62 % (t −2.11, 384 cases on 263 days). The per-coin rho is negative on 5 of 6 coins (−0.07 to −0.17; ETH 0.003), and the top tercile is negative in 2018–2020 and +0.09 % in 2021.
