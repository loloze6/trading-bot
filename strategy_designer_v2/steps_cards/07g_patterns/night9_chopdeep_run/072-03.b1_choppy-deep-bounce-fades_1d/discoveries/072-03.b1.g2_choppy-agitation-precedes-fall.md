A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy market, the more a coin's 30-day swing exceeds its usual swing over the past year, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
What it would mean for a trader: A sideways market that turns unusually agitated is a reason to go short, with a larger short the more agitated it gets, in a profile and side where no strategy acts on purpose.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 072-03.b1_choppy-deep-bounce-fades_1d (idea 072-03.b1_choppy-deep-bounce-fades, brief 072_choppy-deep-below-high-falls).
What it rests on:
- F4: In a choppy market, a coin swinging more than usual falls, and the fall is large against the cost of a trade. In choppy agitated bars the 5-day unusual move is -5.55 % (t -2.99, 60 cases on 48 days), about 29 times the 0.19 % cost. Across all choppy bars, the 5-day move falls with the coin's 30-day swing rank against its own year, by -2.23 % per sd of rank (t -3.17, 220 cases on 113 days). The fall sits mostly in the top swing-rank quartile (-5.67 %, t -2.74); the other three quartiles are close to 0 (-0.04 %, -0.46 %, -0.67 %).
- F5: The choppy swing-rank slope over 5 days is negative in every year and every coin. By year it is -1.44 % (t -1.08), -1.76 % (t -2.00), -6.02 % (t -3.28) and -2.89 % (t -1.57) for 2018 to 2021. By coin, all six slopes are negative, with t from -0.86 (XRP) to -2.40 (BTC). In the profile map, the choppy agitated short cell is empty.
- F1: The book lost because the coins rose while it was short: over 383 trades lasting 13 to 16 days, the coin rose in 66.8 % of them (mean coin return +6.18 %, median +3.25 %); the mean net trade loss did not grow with the size of the short, at -5.5 %, -8.0 %, -4.4 % and -7.3 % across the |entry forecast| quartiles; and the total move per case, +0.02 %, is 0.1 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
