A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling market, an up day is followed by a fall over the next day, the smaller the coin's 60-day fall, the larger the next day's fall.
What it would mean for a trader: A trader should fade one-day rallies in coins only just entering a downtrend, and leave rallies in coins already deeply fallen alone.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 074-01.b1.b1.b1_falling-widest-up-day-fade_1d (idea 074-01.b1.b1.b1_falling-widest-up-day-fade, brief 074_falling-market-wide-up-day-fade).

- F5: The shallow-fall fade of F4 (60-day return from −25 % to −10 %) has the same sign in both halves: −1.14 % in w7–w12 (t −2.84) and −0.51 % in w13–w18 (t −1.10). Up days in rising coins (60-day return above +10 %) do not fade: +0.57 %, +0.19 % and +0.06 % next day.
