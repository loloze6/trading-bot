A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a choppy, agitated market, a coin falls over the next five days, and it falls more the weaker its 10-day return in units of its 20-day daily-return standard deviation.
What it would mean for a trader: A sideways market that has started swinging more than usual is a place to be short for about a week, with a larger short after a weaker last ten days.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, in a choppy agitated market.
Found by: line 7.g patterns, in backtest 072-04.b2_choppy-deep-close-five-day-fall_1d (idea 072-04.b2_choppy-deep-close-five-day-fall, brief 072_choppy-deep-below-high-falls).
What it rests on:
- F5: Choppy agitated markets (4 % of grid bars) are followed by falls in every year. The drift-adjusted 5-day return was -7.0 % (t -2.32) in 2018, -2.9 % (t -2.11) in 2019, -14.1 % (t -2.20) in 2020 and -3.0 % (t -1.52) in 2021, with 58 cases. The fall is negative on all six coins and somewhat larger after a weaker 10-day return: -8.3 %, -4.4 % and -3.9 % by tercile of the 10-day return, from the weakest to the strongest. That gradient's slope t is only 0.20. The choppy agitated short cell of the profile map is empty.
