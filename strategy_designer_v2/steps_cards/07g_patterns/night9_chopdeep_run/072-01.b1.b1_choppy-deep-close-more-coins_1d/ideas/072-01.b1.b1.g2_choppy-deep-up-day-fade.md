Idea: In a choppy market, the further an up day closes below its 20-day high, measured in units of its 20-day daily-return standard deviation, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: In a sideways market, a bounce day that still closes deep below the range top is a reason to short for one day, since holders use the bounce to sell.
Why:
- F4: Over one day, choppy up days in the deepest fifth of depth (mean -5.9 vol units) were followed by an excess return of -1.07 % (t -2.25, 178 cases on 121 days). The other up-day fifths ranged from -0.09 % to -0.20 % (|t| ≤ 0.5). Choppy down days in the deepest fifth gave -0.53 % (t -0.83). The one-day fall after a deep close shows only after up days.
