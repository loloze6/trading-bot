Idea: In a choppy market, the more of its 20-day usual daily moves a coin's close sits below its 20-day closing high, the larger its fall over the next ten days, the fall growing with that count of usual moves itself, not with how deep the coin has lately tended to sit.
Consequence: In a sideways market, a close many usual moves below the range's top is a reason to hold a short sized by that depth for about ten days.
Why:
- F1: In choppy markets, the raw depth below the 20-day high in 20-day daily-return standard deviations predicts the drift-removed return in the idea's direction: over 10 days the slope is +0.0202 (t 2.64, 126 grid cases, 63 days) and over 5 days it is +0.0142 (t 2.12, 249 cases, 128 days); the book's forecast, the same depth standardised over its past 60 values, loses it (10 days slope +0.0020, t 0.76; 5 days +0.0014, t 0.86), even though the two correlate at 0.88.
- F2: In choppy markets the deepest fifth of closes (depth from -11.7 to -4.4 usual moves) is followed by a 10-day drift-removed return of -7.56 % (t -3.41, 26 cases) and the next fifth by -6.05 % (t -1.94, 27 cases), against +2.66 % for the fifth nearest the high (22 cases); the deep third is below the near third on all six coins (BTC -7.15 % vs -0.87 %, LTC -8.36 % vs -4.04 %, XRP -13.57 % vs +8.42 %, ZEC -6.98 % vs +4.02 %, XMR -4.29 % vs -3.33 %, ETH -1.07 % vs -0.51 %).
- F5: At one day the effect is weak: the deepest choppy fifth is followed by -1.12 % the next day (t -1.96, 185 cases), -1.73 % after up days (t -1.92, 58 cases) and -0.84 % after down days (t -1.16, 127 cases), so the brief's "whether up or down" holds in sign only.
