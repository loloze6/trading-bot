Idea: A second consecutive down day, each larger than half the coin's typical move, is followed by a next-day return above the coin's average, in and out of uptrends.
Consequence: A trader could treat a second consecutive meaningful down day as a long entry rather than a reason to cut.
Why:
- F5: Two consecutive down days of more than half the typical move were followed by a next-day excess return of +0.70% outside the uptrend (t 3.91, n 993) and +0.88% inside it (t 2.62, n 242); all other bars gave -0.20% outside the uptrend (t -4.14, n 8671).
