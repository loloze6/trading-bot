Idea: A large down day made when the coin already trades far below its 60-day high is followed by a next-day rise above the coin's average return.
Consequence: A trader could use the depth of the drawdown from the 60-day high to choose which large drops to buy outside uptrends.
Why:
- F3: Outside the uptrend, large drops (z < -1) made when price was more than 40% below its 60-day high were followed by +1.69% the next day (t 4.59, n 381), and by +0.55% at 25–40% below (t 2.22, n 541); drops within 10% of the 60-day high were followed by -0.12% (t -0.52, n 213).
