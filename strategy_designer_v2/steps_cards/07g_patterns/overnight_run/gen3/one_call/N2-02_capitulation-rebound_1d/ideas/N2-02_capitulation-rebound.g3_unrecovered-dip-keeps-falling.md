Idea: When a dip-buying long position still has not closed after a month while the coin stays below its 200-day average, the coin keeps falling over the following weeks.
Consequence: A trader can cut a capitulation long that has not recovered within a few weeks instead of holding it.
Why:
- F7: Trades held longer than 30 days lose: mean -0.6%, median -6.0%, n=60. Trades held 30 days or less gain +4.4% to +5.1% on average (n=528). The long holds lost most in 2018 (-26%, n=13) and 2022 (-14%, n=25).
