Idea: In a coin's daily bars, the bar that follows a Friday bar tends to close above its open-day level more than other days do.
Consequence: A trader would favour holding long exposure into the bar after Friday and be wary of the bar after Monday.
Why:
- F8: The bar after a Friday bar returns +0.24% in excess (t=2.66, n=2070), with t=1.78 in 2018–2021 and t=2.10 in 2022–2025. The bar after a Monday bar returns −0.26% (t=−2.57, n=2076), with t=−1.91 and t=−1.72 in the same two halves.
