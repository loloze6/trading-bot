Idea: A coin's close on the day after a Friday close (UTC) is higher than the Friday close more often than on other days of the week.
Consequence: Hold long exposure from Friday's close to Saturday's close and avoid adding longs on Wednesday's close.
Why:
- F5: The day after a Friday close is the strongest weekday.
