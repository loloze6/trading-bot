Idea: The larger a coin's fall over the past 3 days, the larger its rebound over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A trader would lean long after deep 3-day falls rather than stay flat, as the book does now.
Why:
- F8: Large falls were also followed by gains: the bottom past-return quintile (mean -0.103) gained +0.0059 over the next 72 bars, against -0.0040 for the middle quintile, so forward returns are U-shaped in the past 3-day move.
- F1: Of the book's mean daily return of 0.000706, 0.000387 (55%) is the market's own rise (the position held x the coin's mean daily return), leaving 0.000319 for timing; the book also trails holding in every rising year (for example 2021: 0.000895 against 0.004090), and only beats it in falling years (2018: 0.000185 against -0.003487).
