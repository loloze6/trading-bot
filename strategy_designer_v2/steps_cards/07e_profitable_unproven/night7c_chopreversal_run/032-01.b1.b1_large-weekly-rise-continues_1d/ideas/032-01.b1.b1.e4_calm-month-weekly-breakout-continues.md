Idea: When a coin's daily swings over the past 30 days are small compared with its usual swings, a 7-day rise that is large counted in those swings tends to be followed by a further rise over the next 7 days. The calmer the past 30 days, the larger that further rise.
Consequence: A trader should weight a scaled weekly breakout more when it comes out of a calm month than out of an agitated one.
Why:
- F7: After a z ≥ 1 rise, the next 7-day unusual move is larger when the coin's 30-day daily swing is in its calmest tercile: +4.79 % (t 1.90, n 60), against +2.22 % (n 46) in the middle tercile and +1.72 % (n 52) in the most agitated.
