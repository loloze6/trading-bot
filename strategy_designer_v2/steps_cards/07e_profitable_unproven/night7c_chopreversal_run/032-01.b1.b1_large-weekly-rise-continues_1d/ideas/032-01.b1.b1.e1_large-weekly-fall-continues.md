Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 7 days by a large amount compared with its typical daily swing over the past 30 days, it tends to keep falling over the next 7 days. The larger that 7-day fall counted in 30-day daily swings, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A trader should not buy a large weekly drop counted in the coin's own daily swings, and can lean short with it over the following week.
Why:
