Idea: When a coin's past 30 days have gone back and forth with little net progress compared with the sum of its daily moves, its 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings tends to be followed by a move the same way over the next 7 days. The larger that scaled 7-day move, the larger the following move.
Consequence: In choppy markets a trader should follow, not fade, a large weekly move measured in the coin's own swings.
