Idea: In a rising, calm market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average price, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days; the further it stands below, the less it rises.
Consequence: In a calm rising market, a trader holds a stretch above the average longer than a week, and a close sitting below the average is a reason to stay out.
Why:
- F3: What carries the book is being long in a rising calm market while the close is above its 20-day average at all. In rising-calm bars, the 5-day unusual move is +2.94 % when the close is above the average (142 cases) and -1.19 % when it is at or below it (36 cases).
- F5: The graded continuation shows better at a 10-day horizon than at the plan's 5 days.
- F2: The gain does not come from the idea's grading. Within rising-calm bars with the close above its 20-day average, the 5-day slope of the unusual return on the distance has t 0.21 (142 cases, 51 days). Trades entered on the lowest third of forecasts earn a mean of +7.45 % (206 trades), against +2.03 % for the highest third (202 trades).
