Idea: When a coin with a positive 30-day return falls in a streak of four-hour periods on rising volume, it rises over the next three days, and the more the volume rose across the streak, the larger that rise.
Consequence: A trader buys volume-backed pullbacks inside an uptrend for a few days, not for a week.
Why:
- F5: The tested signal works at 3 days rather than at the plan's 7: its cases give +0.42 % unusual over 3 days (t 2.42, 2387 cases) against +0.52 % over 7 days (t 1.38, 1365 cases); inside the uptrend, 3-day stretches that open on a forecast of at least 2 move +1.60 % (251 cases) against +0.23 % for the others (2588 cases), Welch t 2.35, while at 7 days the gap is 1.19 % against 0.41 %, t 0.52.
- F3: On non-overlapping 3-day stretches, the coin's unusual move is +0.35 % (t 2.08, 2839 cases) when the 30-day return is positive and −0.35 % (t −2.37, 2885 cases) when it is not; on 7-day stretches it is +0.49 % (t 1.15) against −0.48 % (t −1.39).
