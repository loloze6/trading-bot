Idea: When a coin with a positive 30-day return falls in a streak of four-hour periods on rising volume, it rises over the next seven days, and the more the volume rose across the streak, the larger that rise.
Consequence: A trader buys pullbacks within an uptrend that come on growing volume, sized by how much the volume grew, and holds them for about a week.
Why:
- F6: Fading volume adds nothing. In rising coins, a down streak of two or more bars on fading volume is followed by +0.90 % over 72 hours (227 cases, t 1.55); a streak on rising volume is followed by +1.08 % (179 cases, t 1.57). Over 168 hours the figures are +0.42 % (112, t 0.40) and +3.34 % (88, t 2.13).
- F3: What the book holds is the coin's 30-day trend. Over the next 72 hours, bars with a positive 30-day return show an unusual move of +0.35 % against −0.29 % after a negative one (Welch t 2.49, 2183 and 2101 non-overlapping cases), and the t of this gap is near or above 2 at most horizons from 4 to 240 hours (2.58, 2.70, 2.45, 2.49, 2.06).
