Idea: In a rising market, the more of its 20-day daily swings a coin's 20-day rise spans, the more the coin falls back below its usual drift over the next 14 days.
Consequence: In an uptrend, a rise that is very large for the coin's own swings is a reason to take profit or stay out rather than chase, because it tends to be given back within two weeks.
