A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a rising market, the more of its 20-day daily swings a coin's 20-day fall spans, the more the coin keeps falling over the next 14 days.
What it would mean for a trader: In an uptrend, a coin whose last weeks fell hard for its own swings is not a dip to buy, because it keeps lagging its usual drift.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a rising market.
Found by: line 7.e profitable but not proven, in backtest 040-01_rising-swing-scaled-weeks-rise-continues_1d (idea 040-01_rising-swing-scaled-weeks-rise-continues, brief 040_rising-market-swing-scaled-rise-continues).
What it rests on:
- F3: What the book earns from inside the rising profile is the sign of the 20-day move, not its size. Coin-days held with a positive forecast have a mean daily excess return of +0.25 % (t 2.23, n 4,034), against -0.23 % on in-profile days with no position (n 1,735). On the 14-day grid, cases with a positive swing-scaled rise make +1.19 % excess, and those with a zero or negative one make -3.90 %.
