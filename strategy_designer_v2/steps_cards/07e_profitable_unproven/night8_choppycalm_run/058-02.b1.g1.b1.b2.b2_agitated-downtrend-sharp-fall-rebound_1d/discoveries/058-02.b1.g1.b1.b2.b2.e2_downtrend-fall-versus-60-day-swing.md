A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against its usual daily swing over the last 60 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: In an agitated downtrend, a trader has a reason to buy a sharp down day for one day, measuring the fall against the coin's swing over two months rather than the last few weeks.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
