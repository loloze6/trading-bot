A pattern found in a backtest, off the theme of the brief that backtest tested: a theory of its own, to be researched from its first ideas.

The pattern: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's fall over the last 5 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
What it would mean for a trader: In an agitated downtrend, a trader has a reason to buy after a multi-day slide for one day, sized by how deep the 5-day fall is, rather than by a single day's move.
Seen on: 1d bars of BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR, XRP, ZEC, ADA, DOGE, LINK, TRX, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, SOL, INJ, NEAR, SUI, TAO, ONDO, in a falling agitated market.
Found by: line 7.e profitable but not proven, in backtest 058-02.b1.g1.b1.b2.b2_agitated-downtrend-sharp-fall-rebound_1d (idea 058-02.b1.g1.b1.b2.b2_agitated-downtrend-sharp-fall-rebound, brief 058_choppy-calm-range-edge-reversion).
What it rests on:
- F1: The book's gain is almost all timing, not the market's rise: of the summed position gain of 4.02 over 729 held coin-days (100 % long, all in the falling agitated regime), the market's own rise accounts for 0.38 and timing for 3.64, about 91 %.
- F3: Outside the profile the down-day rebound is absent. The slope of next-day excess return on the scaled fall has t 0.12 in falling markets (734 days), 0.93 in rising markets (885) and 1.24 in choppy markets (369), so the gain lives in falling agitated markets only.
- F6: The rebound is concentrated on falls that end a multi-day drop. Among big down days (below −1 swing), those in the deepest third of 5-day returns rebound +3.66 % the next day (102 cases), against +0.07 % (34) and +0.74 % (26) in the other thirds. Small down days after a deep 5-day fall still rebound +2.01 % (43), against −0.11 % (114) for the middle third.
- F7: The book's profit is concentrated in a few periods and trades. Windows w16 and w14 give 0.196 and 0.131 of the 0.52 summed daily book return (63 %), with down-day rebounds of +3.39 % and +0.93 %. The top 20 of 693 trades give 439 of the 803 summed net percent (55 %). By year, 2020 alone shows +6.6 % per big fall (29 cases).
