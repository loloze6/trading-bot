14. **A weekday measure component** (`WeekdayComponent`, the coming bar's weekday, for a regime rule) let step 3 write a faithful plan for the night's strongest recurring effect, the bar after Friday (t 4.3 in step 7, every coin).     **Tested (`--target-vol 0.2`, every book of the night, 24 books):** no verdict changes (all fail). N1-04 now passes Calmar (0.84) and fails the daily-return bar instead (0.043 % for 0.05 %).
