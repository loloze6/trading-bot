Idea: The size of the up day that makes the new 20-day closing high adds nothing. A new high made on an ordinary or small up day is followed by as large a rise over the next ten days as one made on an outsized day.
Consequence: A trader should weigh every new 20-day closing high the same, and not add size because the breakout day was large.
