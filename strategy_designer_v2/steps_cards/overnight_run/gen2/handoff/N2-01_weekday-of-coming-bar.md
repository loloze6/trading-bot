bar: 1d
period: folds A, B, C, D

Idea: The daily bar that follows a Friday-dated bar (the weekend Saturday session) returns more than the coin's average day, reflecting thin weekend liquidity dominated by retail buyers.
Consequence: A trader can favour holding or adding longs into the Saturday session and delay planned sells until after it.
