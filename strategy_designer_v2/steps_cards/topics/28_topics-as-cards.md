## Question
How do we record an improvement and the reasons of its decisions?

## Seen
- The parked list (`POTENTIAL_ADJUSTMENTS.md`) and the operator's topics table were two lists; their details were free
  text in different shapes; decisions' reasons were in conversations.

## Why it matters
A decision whose reason is lost gets asked again, or undone.

## Options
| Option | Gain | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| One folder of topic cards in one shape, with a decision table, and one index | one list; the why kept | upkeep |
| Keep the parked list | - | two lists, no reasons |

Recommended: topic cards.
