# Topic 08: An idea proven and profitable: what next

Status: open (larger topic)  
Where: research and live  
Opened: 2026-11-15 (operator)

## Question
Once an idea is real and its book pays, what do we observe, what do we advise, and how does it reach live trading?

## Seen
- 2026-11-14: 7.e's two confirmed and profitable ideas (002-03's e1, N2-04's e1) were the first inputs.
- 2026-11-18: the unseen test (`runs/UNSEEN_RUN.md`, outcome 1: 10 of 12 hold on 2026, 11 of 12 on 2016-17); 9 of the
  10 are one family ("in when the trend is up").
- 2026-11-19, the one-sided ideas' investigation (153 backtests with a verdict, 12 runs; topic 26). The 13 profitable
  books, per coin and window:

  | Profitable books | Windows where the market | Market | Book | Windows the book gained |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | long only | rose | +92 % | +24.7 % | 73 % |
  | long only | fell | -30 % | -3.4 % | 19 % |
  | both sides | rose | +63 % | +19.7 % | 79 % |
  | both sides | fell | -26 % | +4.0 % | 44 % |

  They earn in rises and stand aside in falls: they need rises somewhere, not a bull market (2026, a falling year:
  Sharpe 0.4 to 1.8, holding 0.1).

## Why it matters
Being real and profitable is the end of the research, where a decision about money starts.

## Options
| Option | Gain | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| A stage after steps 5 and 6 that observes and advises (gating nothing), then paper trading, then combining books | the decision is informed; nothing is filtered by a new gate | a stage to design |
| Diagnose with a step 7 line (7.f) | no new stage | being real and profitable is not a result to diagnose |

Recommended: the observe-and-advise stage, then paper trading via the live session.
