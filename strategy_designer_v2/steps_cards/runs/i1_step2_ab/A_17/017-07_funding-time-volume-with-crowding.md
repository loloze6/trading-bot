Idea: The stronger a coin's move in either direction over the past 7 days, the higher its spot volume in the 2 hours on each side of a funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 or 16:00 UTC), compared with its usual volume in those same hours.
Consequence: Crowded-side holders and basis traders close and reopen around settlement, raising spot volume at those clock times, and more so when crowding is heavier.
