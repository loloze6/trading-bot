Idea: The stronger a coin's fall over the past 7 days, the more its spot price falls in the 2 hours just after each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC).
Consequence: Once settlement has passed, crowded shorts reopen and the timing-driven pre-settlement pop is given back.
