Idea: The stronger a coin's rise over the past 7 days, the more its spot price falls in the 2 hours just before each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC).
Consequence: Before settlement, spot leans against the crowded long side as longs and basis traders close ahead of the funding payment.
