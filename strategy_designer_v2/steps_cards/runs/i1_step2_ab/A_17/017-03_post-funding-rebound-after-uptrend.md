Idea: The stronger a coin's rise over the past 7 days, the more its spot price rises in the 2 hours just after each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC).
Consequence: Once settlement has passed, crowded longs reopen and the timing-driven pre-settlement dip is recovered.
