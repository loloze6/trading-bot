Idea: The larger a coin's spot rise in the 2 hours before a funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 or 16:00 UTC), the more it falls in the 2 hours after that settlement.
Consequence: The pre-settlement move is flow driven by timing, not information, so it reverses once the payment has passed.
