Idea: After a coin's 3-day return has been strongly negative, its spot return over the 4 hours starting at each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is negative, and the stronger that 3-day run down, the larger the fall after the settlement.
Consequence: Once the payment has passed, shorts reopen and the timing-driven pre-settlement rise reverses.
