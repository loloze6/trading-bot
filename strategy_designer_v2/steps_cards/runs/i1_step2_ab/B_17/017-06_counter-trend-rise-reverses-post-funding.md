Idea: When a coin's spot price has risen against a negative 3-day trend over the 4 hours ending at a funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 or 16:00 UTC), it falls over the 4 hours after the settlement, and the larger that counter-trend rise into the settlement, the larger the fall.
Consequence: The counter-trend move before settlement is timing flow, not information, so it is given back after the payment.
