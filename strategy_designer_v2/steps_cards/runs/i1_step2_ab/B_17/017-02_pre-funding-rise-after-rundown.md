Idea: After a coin's 3-day return has been strongly negative, its spot return over the 4 hours ending at each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is positive, and the stronger that 3-day run down, the larger the rise into the settlement.
Consequence: Before settlement, spot leans against crowded shorts who close to avoid paying funding.
