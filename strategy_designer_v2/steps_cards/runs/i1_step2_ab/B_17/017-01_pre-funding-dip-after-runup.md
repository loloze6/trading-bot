Idea: After a coin's 3-day return has been strongly positive, its spot return over the 4 hours ending at each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is negative, and the stronger that 3-day run up, the larger the drop into the settlement.
Consequence: Before settlement, spot leans against crowded longs who close to avoid paying funding.
