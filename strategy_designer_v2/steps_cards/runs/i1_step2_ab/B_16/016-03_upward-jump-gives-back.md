Idea: When a coin's 3-day rise came mostly from one or a few violent up hours, it gives back part of the rise over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the rise made in its biggest few hours, the larger the giveback.
Consequence: An upward jump is forced flow, such as short liquidations, stop runs or a headline crowd, that overshoots. Once that flow is spent, liquidity providers lean against the move.
