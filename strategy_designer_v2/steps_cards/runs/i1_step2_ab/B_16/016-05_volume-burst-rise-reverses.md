Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days and its volume was packed into a few hours instead of spread evenly, it gives back part of the rise over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the 3-day volume traded in its busiest few hours, the larger the giveback.
Consequence: A volume burst marks forced buying behind the rise. Forced buying is spent once it is done and is then absorbed against.
