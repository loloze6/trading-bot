Idea: When a coin's 3-day fall came mostly from one or a few violent down hours, it recovers part of the fall over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the fall made in its biggest few hours, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A downward jump is forced selling, such as long liquidations, stop runs or headline panic, that pushes price below fair value. Once that selling is spent, the overshoot partly reverses.
