Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days and its volume was packed into a few hours instead of spread evenly, it recovers part of the fall over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the 3-day volume traded in its busiest few hours, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A volume burst marks forced selling behind the fall. Forced selling overshoots and partly reverses once it is spent.
