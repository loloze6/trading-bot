Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days through many small hourly gains in a row, so that its 3-day net rise is a large share of the total up-and-down hourly movement, it keeps rising over the next 3 days. The steadier the climb, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: An upward grind left by a patient buyer splitting a large order is not yet finished, so the buying pressure continues.
