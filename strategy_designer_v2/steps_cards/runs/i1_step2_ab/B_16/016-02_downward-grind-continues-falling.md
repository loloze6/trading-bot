Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days through many small hourly losses in a row, so that its 3-day net fall is a large share of the total up-and-down hourly movement, it keeps falling over the next 3 days. The steadier the decline, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A downward grind left by a patient seller splitting a large order is not yet finished, so the selling pressure continues.
