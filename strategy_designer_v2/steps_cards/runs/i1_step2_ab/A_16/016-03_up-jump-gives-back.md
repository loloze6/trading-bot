Idea: When most of a coin's 3-day rise came from one or a few hours, it gives back part of that rise over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the rise made by its biggest few hours, the larger the drop that follows.
Consequence: An upward jump is forced flow such as short squeezes or headline buying that overshoots, and it partly reverses once the flow is spent.
