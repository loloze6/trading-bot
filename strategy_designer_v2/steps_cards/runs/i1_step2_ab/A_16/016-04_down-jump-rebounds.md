Idea: When most of a coin's 3-day fall came from one or a few hours, it recovers part of that fall over the next 3 days. The larger the share of the fall made by its biggest few hours, the larger the rebound that follows.
Consequence: A downward jump is forced flow such as stop runs or long liquidations that overshoots, and it partly reverses once the flow is spent.
