Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days with its volume spread evenly across the hours, and no hour standing out, it keeps falling over the next 3 days. The more evenly the volume is spread, the further it falls.
Consequence: Modest, even volume during a fall marks a patient seller working a large order, so the selling continues.
