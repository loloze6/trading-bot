Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days through many small hourly losses, with most hours stepping downward and no single hour carrying much of the fall, it keeps falling over the next 3 days. The larger the share of down hours and the more evenly the fall is spread across hours, the further it falls.
Consequence: A downward grind left by a patient seller splitting a large order is unfinished, so the selling pressure keeps pushing price down.
