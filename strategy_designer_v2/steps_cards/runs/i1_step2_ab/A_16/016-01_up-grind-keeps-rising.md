Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days through many small hourly gains, with most hours stepping upward and no single hour carrying much of the rise, it keeps rising over the next 3 days. The larger the share of up hours and the more evenly the rise is spread across hours, the further it rises.
Consequence: An upward grind left by a patient buyer splitting a large order is unfinished, so the buying pressure keeps pushing price up.
