Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days with its volume spread evenly across the hours, and no hour standing out, it keeps rising over the next 3 days. The more evenly the volume is spread, the further it rises.
Consequence: Modest, even volume during a rise marks a patient buyer working a large order, so the buying continues.
