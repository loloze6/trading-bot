Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days and a large share of that period's volume traded in one or a few hours, it gives back part of the rise over the next 3 days. The larger the share of volume in those burst hours, the larger the drop.
Consequence: A volume burst during a rise marks forced buying that pushed price past fair value, and liquidity providers lean against it once the burst is spent.
