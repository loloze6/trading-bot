Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days and a large share of that period's volume traded in one or a few hours, it recovers part of the fall over the next 3 days. The larger the share of volume in those burst hours, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A volume burst during a fall marks forced selling that pushed price below fair value, and liquidity providers lean against it once the burst is spent.
