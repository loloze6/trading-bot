=== 048-01_falling-agitated-wide-rise-giveback  (night7_chopreversal_run; falling agitated)
Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a day's rise against the coin's 30-day daily swing, multiplied by how wide that day's high-to-low range is against its 30-day usual range, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: Up days in a falling, agitated market are short-lived squeezes, and the share of the gain later given back grows with the rise's size and the day's range together.

=== 077-01.b1.b1.g1_choppy-calm-swing-fall-carries  (night9_chopstretch_run; choppy calm)

Consequence: In a choppy calm market a trader can lean short in proportion to a swing-scaled 14-day fall, the mirror of the long carry.

=== 038-01.g2_scaled-rally-continues-calm-uptrend  (night7_panic_run; rising calm)

=== 058-02.b1.b1.g2_calm-uptrend-dip-three-day-rebound  (night8_risingcalm_run; rising calm)

=== 047-01.g1_agitated-wide-up-day-next-day-fade  (night7_chopreversal_run; falling agitated)

Consequence: In a falling, agitated market, a wide-ranging up day is a relief rally to sell into for one day, and the wider its range the stronger that case, while a narrow up day gives no such reason.

=== 067-02.b1.b1.d1.d2.b2_agitated-chop-falls-ten-days  (night9_chopmonth_run; choppy agitated)

=== 065-01.b1_slide-ending-sharp-day-rebound  (night8_choppycalm_run; falling agitated)

Consequence: In an agitated downtrend, a trader buys the rebound after a slide that ends in a sharp down day, and skips slides that end in a quiet day.

=== 067-01.b1.g1.b1.d1.d2.g1_choppy-calm-month-continuation  (night9_chopmonth_run; choppy calm)
Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the larger a coin's 30-day return is in either direction, the further it moves the same way over the next 10 days, so that a stronger month rise is followed by a larger rise and a deeper month fall by a larger fall.
Consequence: A trader in a flat, quiet two-month market follows the coin's month move both ways, scaling the position with the month's size, and takes the long side, where the coin's drift helps, as well as the short.

=== 061-01.d1.d1_rising-calm-big-day-follow-through  (night8_risingcalm_run; rising calm)

Consequence: A trader shorts the one-day bounce inside an agitated decline only for a day and sizes it by how deep the 3-day fall was, which keeps the book out of the shallow-dip bounces that made its deepest fall.

=== 033-02.d1_high-close-fades-in-selloffs  (night7_steadydecline_run; falling agitated)

Consequence: The bounce rests on flow that will not return and on supply from holders trapped through the slide, so a deeper recent fall gives a larger fade the day after the bounce.

Consequence: A wide up day in a trendless, swollen-swing market is forced buying that range sellers unwind the next day, with a giveback that grows with the overshoot.
