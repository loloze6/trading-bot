Idea: After a daily close that crosses up from below a coin's Ichimoku cloud to above it, the coin rises over the next 26 days, and the more the coin fell over the past 26 days before the cross, the larger the rise.
Consequence: An upward cloud cross that follows a fall is a reason to go long for the next few weeks, while a cross that comes after a run-up is a reason to refuse that trade.
Why:
- F1: The tested data gave 11 independent cases against the plan's minimum of 20. Over 6 coins and 143 coin-windows, about 122 daily bars each, there were 214 upward cloud crosses, about 1.5 per coin-window or 1.2 % of 17,505 bars. Of these, 20 crosses became bets (forecast of 2 or more) and only 11 counted as cases. That is 0.077 cases per coin-window: BTC 0, ETH 1, LTC 4, XMR 2, XRP 2, ZEC 2.
- F2: Most upward crosses carry no bet. On 180 of the 214 crosses (84 %) the forecast is 0, because the 26 days before the cross rose. Another 14 have a forecast between 0 and 2. Only 9.3 % of crosses reach a forecast of 2 or more. A cross from below the cloud after a 26-day fall is a rare situation, since price usually climbs through the cloud.
- F3: The 26-day horizon is long for windows of about 122 days, so each window holds at most 4 usable grid cells. 33 of the 214 crosses fell too late in their window to have 26 days left. Of the 20 bets, 9 were lost to late placement or a shared grid cell. 15 of 24 window numbers gave no case on any coin.
- F5: The idea's size is well above the cost of a trade. The total move per case is +9.77 % and the unusual move +5.83 % (strength 0.327), against 0.19 % for a buy and a sell, so 50 times and 30 times the cost. Cost is not what kept the idea from being observed; the count of cases is.
- F6: Adding the other 13 coins on daily bars would add about 158 coin-windows (98 in folds A and D, 26 in the partial B and C, about 24 for the fold-D-only coins). That makes about 300 coin-windows. At the measured 0.077 cases per coin-window this gives about 23 expected cases, just above the plan's minimum of 20.
