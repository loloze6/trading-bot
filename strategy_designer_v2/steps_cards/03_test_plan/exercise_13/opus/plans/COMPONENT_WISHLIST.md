# Component wish list

One block per idea that needs components the catalogue does not have.

## 005-01_bearish-rsi-divergence-reversal_1d
- Component: prior-high-RSI gap: on each bar, find the highest close of the last N bars excluding the most recent few bars (the previous swing high) and the RSI(14) on that bar, computed from closes; output RSI_at_prior_high minus RSI_now when the current close is at or above that prior high, and 0 otherwise, so it grows as the RSI falls further short of its earlier peak | measures: comparison
- Component: graded new-high excess: from closes, (close - previous swing high close) / previous swing high x 100 when the close is at or above that prior high, and 0 otherwise, growing with how far the new high exceeds the old one; multiplied by the RSI gap above, it gives a graded bearish-divergence strength (with a mirror version at lows for the bullish case in the brief) | measures: situation

## 005-02_bullish-rsi-divergence-rebound_1d
- Component: RSIBullishDivergence: on each bar, from closes only, find the lowest close of the prior `lookback` bars (for example 20 to 60, swing low separated by at least a few bars). If the current close is at or below that low, output RSI(`period`, default 14) now minus RSI on that prior low's bar, clipped at 0; otherwise output 0. The value grows with how much higher the RSI low is than at the earlier price low; it can optionally be multiplied by the depth of the new low below the old one, in percent. A bearish mirror (new high, lower RSI high) gives the negative side. | measures: situation
- Component: RSIAtPriorPriceLow: from closes, the RSI(`period`) value recorded on the bar of the lowest close in the prior `lookback` bars, carried forward each bar. Paired with the current RSI and a new-low condition, it supplies the RSI higher-low comparison. Its value is an RSI level, 0 to 100. | measures: comparison

## 005-03_divergent-high-weaker-than-confirmed_1d
- Component: RSIRangePosition: Wilder RSI(`period`, default 14) computed from closes, then (2 x pos - 1) x sf, where pos = (RSI - lowest RSI) / (highest RSI - lowest RSI) over the last `lookback` bars including the current one (Donchian-style, 0.5 when flat); graded and signed, it grows toward +sf as RSI nears its own recent high and falls toward -sf near its own recent low | measures: situation
- Component: PriceRsiHighDivergence: from closes, pos_price = the close's position in its `lookback`-bar close range (as in `DonchianBreakoutComponent`) and pos_rsi = RSI(`period`)'s position in its own `lookback`-bar range; outputs -max(0, pos_price - pos_rsi) x pos_price x sf for highs (and the mirror for lows); it is 0 when RSI confirms the price extreme or price is not near its high, and grows more negative as price sits at its high while RSI lags further below its own high | measures: situation

## 005-04_divergent-low-stronger-than-confirmed_1d
- Component: RsiLowDivergence: computes the RSI (Wilder, period 14) from the closes and finds the bar of the previous lowest close in the last N closes (N 20), excluding the current bar. It outputs the current RSI minus the RSI on that bar, in RSI points. The value is positive and grows when the current low comes on weaker downside momentum (a higher RSI low). It is negative and grows in size when RSI also makes a new low (confirming momentum). | measures: situation
- Component: RsiRangePosition: computes the RSI (Wilder, period 14) from the closes and outputs where the current RSI sits within its own range over the last N bars (N 20), (2 x (RSI - min RSI) / (max RSI - min RSI) - 1) x sf, from -sf to +sf. Paired with the price's Donchian position, it shows when price is at the bottom of its range but RSI is not. The value grows with how far RSI sits above its own recent low. | measures: comparison

## 005-05_bearish-divergence-uptrend-continues_1d
- Component: PriceRsiDivergence: from closes, computes the Wilder RSI(period, 14 by default) and, over the last N bars (default 14), the close's position in its close range (0 at the low, 1 at the high) minus RSI's position in its own range over the same bars. The output is signed and continuous. It is positive and grows as price sits nearer its range high while RSI sits further below its own high (bearish divergence). It is negative when price sits near its low while RSI sits above its own low (bullish divergence). It is near 0 when price and RSI agree. | measures: situation
- Component: RsiRangePosition: from closes, gives where the current Wilder RSI(14) sits between its lowest and highest value over the last N bars, scaled to -1 at the bottom and +1 at the top. It grows as RSI nears its recent peak. Combined with a negative weight against `DonchianBreakoutComponent` over the same N, it would give the price-minus-RSI divergence. | measures: comparison

## 005-06_bullish-divergence-downtrend-continues_1d
- Component: RsiRangePositionComponent: from closes, computes the Wilder RSI(period, default 14) on each bar, then gives (2 x pos - 1) x sf, where pos = (RSI now - lowest RSI over the last N bars) / (highest RSI - lowest RSI over the last N bars), with N matching the Donchian period. It is continuous and signed: -sf when RSI is at its N-bar low, +sf at its N-bar high. Combined with DonchianBreakoutComponent at the same N, with weights so the forecast is -(RSI range position - price range position) clipped at most 0, the short grows as price sits lower in its range while RSI sits higher in its own (a deeper bullish divergence). | measures: situation
