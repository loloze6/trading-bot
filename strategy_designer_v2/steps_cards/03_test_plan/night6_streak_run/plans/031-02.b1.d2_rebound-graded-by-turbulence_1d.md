Fidelity:
- Situation: the latest day's fall, against the size of the past 60 days' daily moves -> kept: the one-day fall divided by the 60-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Comparison: the past 10 days' daily moves against the past 60 days' -> kept: the 10-day standard deviation over the 60-day standard deviation, multiplied into the fall.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 2 days, more for a larger product -> kept: a long position graded by the product and held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the component computes the idea's exact product, so a sized long rebound either earns more with size or does not.
Forecast: One component, VolatilityRatioWeightedScaledFallComponent with vol_period 10 and base_vol_period 60. It computes the day's fall, max(0, -return), divided by the 60-day standard deviation of daily returns, then multiplies that by the 10-day over the 60-day standard deviation. The result is standardised over its past 61 values (min_periods 20), so a typical reading is about 10, capped at 20. The forecast is 0 on up and flat days and positive after a down day. It grows with the depth of the fall in usual moves and with how turbulent the last 10 days were against the last 60. A drop after a calm stretch gives a small position. The forecast is never short. There is no regime gate: the idea is a steady graded relation.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: Use a non-overlapping 2-bar grid in each window. On each grid bar, take the coin's 2-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 2-bar return over all windows (judged against holding the coin, before costs). Regress that excess return on the forecast on the grid bars with forecast > 0. The result is the slope and its t, with cases on the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: On grid bars with forecast > 0, the mean excess 2-bar return is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more AND the mean excess 2-bar return on forecast > 0 bars is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger turbulence-weighted falls are followed by smaller 2-day returns.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a mean excess return at or below 0. At least 300 grid bars with forecast > 0 are required, across at least 150 distinct days; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
