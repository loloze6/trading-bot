Fidelity:
- Situation: an up-close streak, the longer the more, only while the 30-day return is positive -> kept: streak rank as the graded size, 30-day return > 0 as the regime condition.
- Comparison: the streak length against its own completed up streaks over the past 365 days -> kept: `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` rank, `base_window` 365.
- Outcome: the price keeps rising over the next 20 days -> kept: long bets held 20 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the streak's rank among the coin's own past-year streaks, long only while the month is up, and it is judged on the 20-day move.
Forecast: Two parts set the forecast. - When it is active: only on bars whose 30-day close return is above 0 (`PriceEvolutionOnPeriodComponent`, `comparison_period` 30). Every other bar is flat. - How large it is: the rank of today's up-close streak among the coin's completed up streaks of the past 365 days (`UpCloseStreakLengthComponent`, `side` "up", `base` "rank", `base_window` 365). The rank is the share of those streaks no longer than the current one. It is 0 on any day that did not close higher. - Scale: the rank is standardised against its own past 366 values (`min_periods` 92), so a typical value is about +10. - Shape: `signed_power` with exponent 2 damps ordinary streaks toward 0 and amplifies unusually long ones. - Sign: the forecast is never negative. It is 0 without an up streak and grows with how unusual the streak is for that coin.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a forecast rise of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 20-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 20-bar return minus the coin's normal 20-bar drift (holding the coin, over all windows). The measure is the mean of these moves, with its t computed with cases summed per day, before costs.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases in the top third of forecast size is above that of the bottom third (top minus bottom > 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t >= 2 when the top-third case mean is not above the bottom-third one. At least 30 distinct case days are needed; fewer is inconclusive.
