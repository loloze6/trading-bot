Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 10-day return, in units of its 60-day daily volatility, is an extreme rise -> adapted: a regime is active only while the volatility-scaled 10-day return is at least 2, the convention for "extreme"; inside it the position is graded by that return.
- Comparison: extreme rises against modest rises and the coin's normal drift -> kept: modest rises hold nothing, and cases are judged against the coin's drift.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 5 days beyond its normal drift -> kept: long, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's extreme-rise situation, its direction and its 5-day horizon as the idea states them.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 10 and vol_period 60. Its value is (close[t]/close[t-10]-1) divided by (the std of the 60 daily returns x sqrt(10)). The regime detector reads this same measure and selects `trending` only while it is 2 or more; every other bar is `unknown`, which holds nothing (forecast 0). Inside `trending`, the same measure is standardised against its own past 61 values, with min_periods 20. It is then clipped at 0, so it is long only. The forecast is positive, and it grows with the size of the rise in volatility units, up to the cap of 20. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, in the long direction. A case is a bet, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-day drift, taken over all windows. The mean is tested with a t, the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 distinct case days.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2, over at least 30 distinct case days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 distinct case days.
