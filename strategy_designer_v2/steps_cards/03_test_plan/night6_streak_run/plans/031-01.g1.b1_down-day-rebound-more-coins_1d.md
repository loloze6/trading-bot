Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day fall, measured against its own 60-day daily volatility -> kept
- Comparison: the coin's normal drift -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next 3 days, the larger the scaled fall -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the scaled one-day fall, long and zero on up days, held for 3 days, and is judged against the coin's drift, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast starts from `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 1, `vol_period` 60, `lag` 0 and `vol_lookback` 1. Its raw value is today's one-day return divided by the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns. `standardise` (window 61, min_periods 20) puts it on the forecast scale, about 10 for a typical move. `negate` turns falls positive, and `clip` with `min` 0 drops up days. So the forecast is 0 on up and flat days and positive on down days. On down days it grows in proportion to the fall measured in usual daily moves: a one-usual-move fall gives about 12, capped at 20. It is long only and never negative. No regime is used, because the size starts from 0 and is graded by the fall itself.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 3-day return minus its normal drift on the forecast. The 3-day return runs from close[t] to close[t+3], and the normal drift is the coin's mean 3-day return over all its windows. The sample is the bars of the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window where the forecast is above 0, pooled over coins. The t of the slope takes the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases (the holding period's bets) is above 0, before costs.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when the slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes (mean unusual move above 0).
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirm with a mean unusual move at or below 0 is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 100 grid cases on at least 50 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
