Fidelity:
- Situation: the number of consecutive higher daily closes up to today -> kept: up-close streak length n, from `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` `side` "up"
- Comparison: against the lengths of its own up-close streaks over the past 365 days -> kept: n divided by the mean length of completed up streaks in the last 365 bars (`base` "mean_streak")
- Outcome: the more unusual the streak, the more the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days -> kept: a long position sized by that ratio, held 5 bars
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own ratio, and the test checks whether 5-day excess returns grow with it.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` with `side` "up", `base` "mean_streak", `base_window` 365 and `max_bars` 30. Its value is the current run of higher closes, in multiples of the coin's mean completed up-streak length over the past 365 days. It is standardised against its own past 365 values: an average reading gives about +10, capped at 20. The forecast is 0 on any day that did not close higher. It is positive during an up streak and grows with each extra higher close and with how long the streak is against the coin's usual streak. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Take bars 0, 5, 10, … of each window as a non-overlapping grid. On that grid, regress each coin's 5-bar forward return, minus that coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows, on the forecast. The result is the slope and its t, with cases on the same day pooled into one.
- Supporting check: Over the cases (bets on the 5-bar grid), the mean unusual move, meaning the return minus normal drift in the long direction, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope has t ≥ +2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope has t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and +2 is inconclusive. A slope t ≥ +2 with a mean unusual move at or below 0 is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 200 grid bars with forecast > 0, on at least 100 distinct days, or else it is inconclusive.
