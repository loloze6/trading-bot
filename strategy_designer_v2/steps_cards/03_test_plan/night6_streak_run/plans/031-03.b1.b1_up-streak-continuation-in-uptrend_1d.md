Fidelity:
- Situation: an up-close streak, graded by its length against the coin's own up-streaks of the past 365 days, only while the 30-day return is positive -> kept: the streak length divided by the mean completed up-streak length over 365 days; a positive 30-day return is a regime, and the book is flat outside it
- Comparison: the coin's own up-close streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days, more after longer streaks -> kept: long, held 5 daily bars
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's graded streak inside its 30-day-up condition, held for its 5-day horizon, so a confirm or reject on its bets is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the 30-day percent return and classifies a bar as "trending" when that return is above 0; every other bar is flat. In "trending", the forecast is the current up-close streak length divided by the mean length of the coin's completed up-streaks over the past 365 days (`UpCloseStreakLengthComponent`, `base` "mean_streak", `base_window` 365, `max_bars` 30). This is standardised over its past 365 values, so a streak of the usual length gives about +10, capped at +20. The forecast is 0 when the bar did not close up, and it grows with each further up close relative to the coin's usual streak. It is never negative (long only).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case: the coin's 5-day return after each bet, minus its normal 5-day drift over all windows. Cases are rises of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. The measure is the mean of these unusual moves and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: The size relation, a one-variable least-squares slope of the per-case unusual move on the case's forecast. It passes if the slope is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the slope on forecast size is positive.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t >= 2 with a slope at or below 0. A decision also needs at least 100 cases falling on at least 50 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
