Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's percentage fall on its latest day -> kept: the one-bar return, with up days read as 0.
- Comparison: the fall against the std of daily returns over the past 60 days -> kept: the return divided by the 60-bar std of one-bar returns.
- Outcome: the larger that ratio, the more the coin rises over the next 2 days -> adapted: the long position is graded by the ratio and held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the volatility-scaled fall, so a rise that grows with it confirms the idea and a fall that grows with it rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 1 and `vol_period` 60. It reads the latest day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns. It is standardised against its own past 61 values, negated so that a fall reads positive, and clipped at 0. On a down day the forecast is positive and grows linearly with how many usual moves the fall spans. On a flat or up day it is 0. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 2-bar grid of each window, all coins pooled, take the slope of the coin's 2-day return minus its normal drift (its mean 2-day return over all windows) on the forecast at the grid bar. The measure is that slope's t, with cases on the same day summed per day.
- Supporting check: On grid bars in the top fifth of positive forecasts, the coin's mean 2-day return minus its normal drift is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger falls are followed by larger further falls than usual.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more when the check fails. The measure needs at least 200 grid bars with a positive forecast, spread over at least 100 distinct days; below that the result is inconclusive.
