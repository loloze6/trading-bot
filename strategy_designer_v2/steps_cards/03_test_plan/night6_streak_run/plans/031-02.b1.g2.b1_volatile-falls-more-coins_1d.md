Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's percentage fall on its latest day, larger the bigger the fall -> kept: `VolatilityWeightedFallComponent` measures the one-day fall in percent and is 0 on up days.
- Comparison: the rebound is larger the higher the coin's past 60-day volatility -> kept: the fall is multiplied by the 60-day standard deviation of daily returns (`vol_period` 60).
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 2 days -> kept: long, held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: both the fall and its volatility weighting are measured as stated, and the outcome is read over the stated 2 days.
Forecast: The forecast is the latest day's fall in percent times the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns, standardised against its own past 61 values. It is zero on up days and flat days, and positive after a down day. It grows with the depth of the fall and with the 60-day volatility. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: Take the cases with a positive forecast on each window's non-overlapping 2-bar grid. Regress each case's 2-day return, minus the coin's mean 2-day return over all windows, on its forecast (`post_pipeline_value`). The measure is the slope's t, with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of their 60-day volatility, recomputed from closes. The mean excess 2-day return after high-volatility falls minus that after low-volatility falls must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a failed check. Fewer than 100 independent case-days is inconclusive whatever the t.
