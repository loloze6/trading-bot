- Situation: the up-close streak's length set against the coin's own up-streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept: `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` (side up, base mean_streak, base_window 365) gives the current streak in multiples of the mean completed streak.
- Comparison: the coin's own streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept: the mean_streak base, which keeps growing with streak length among the longest streaks, unlike a rank.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 10 days, more so the longer the streak -> kept: a long position graded by the streak, held for 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded streak measure, so a rising 10-day return with forecast size confirms it, and a falling one rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is one component: the current run of higher daily closes divided by the coin's mean completed up-streak length over the past 365 days. It is standardised against its own past 366 values, with target 3 so that it is not capped on ordinary streaks. It is 0 on any day that did not close higher. Otherwise it is positive and grows with the streak: about 3 for a 1-day streak, about 6 for 2 days, and so on, reaching the cap of 20 at about 7 days. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The 10-day forward return of each coin minus its normal 10-day drift (the coin's mean over all windows), regressed on the forecast. Cases are the non-overlapping 10-bar grid cells of each window, all coins pooled, including zero-forecast bars. The measure is the slope's t.
- Supporting check: Among grid cases with forecast above 0, the mean excess return of the top fifth by forecast minus that of the remaining positive-forecast cases is above 0. This checks that the rise still grows among the longest streaks.
- Confirm: Confirmed when the slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: Rejected when the slope's t is -2 or less: longer streaks are followed by lower returns than the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with the top-fifth difference at or below 0 is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 300 grid cases with forecast above 0, and at least 30 in the top fifth.
