Fidelity:
- Situation: an up-close streak that is long against the coin's own past-365-day streaks, only while 30-day and 60-day returns are positive -> adapted: the 30d>0 and 60d>0 condition is a regime, and inside it the streak length divided by the mean completed streak length over 365 bars is graded.
- Comparison: the coin's own up-streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 20 days, more the longer the streak -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the stated streak against the coin's own past streaks, only inside the stated two-trend condition, over the stated 20-day horizon.
Forecast: The regime is active only when the 30-bar return and the 60-bar return are both above 0. Everywhere else the forecast is flat. Inside the regime, the forecast is the current up-close streak length divided by the mean length of the completed up streaks of the past 365 bars (UpCloseStreakLengthComponent, base "mean_streak"). It is standardised over 365 bars, so a usual streak gives about +10. It is 0 on a bar that did not close up, it is never negative, and it grows with how long the streak is against that coin's usual streak, up to a cap of +20.
Config: 031-03.b1.b1.b2_up-streak-continuation-in-two-month-uptrend_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: Use the bars of the trending regime on each window's non-overlapping 20-bar grid. Fit the slope of the coin's forward 20-bar return, minus its mean 20-bar return over all windows, on the forecast. Take its t with the cases of one day grouped together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual 20-bar move of the cases (forecast rises of at least 2, one per grid cell) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope t is 2 or more and the cases' mean unusual move is above 0.
- Reject: The slope t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a slope t of 2 or more when the mean unusual move is at or below 0. A verdict needs at least 100 grid cases in the regime with a forecast above 0, on at least 50 distinct days.
