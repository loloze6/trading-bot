Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's positive 10-day return, large relative to its 60-day daily volatility -> kept
- Comparison: beyond the coin's normal drift; a large rise against a modest one -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days, more as the scaled rise grows -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own volatility-scaled 10-day rise, held long for its 5-day horizon and judged against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast is the 10-day return divided by the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns. This is VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 10 and vol_period 60; its sqrt(10) factor is a constant that standardise removes. Standardise scales it against its own past 61 values, so an average-sized move gives about 10, capped at 20. A clip at 0 then sets the forecast to 0 when the 10-day return is not positive. The forecast is positive only after a rise. It grows linearly with the rise measured in the coin's own volatility, and it is small when the rise is modest. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use bars on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window where the forecast is above 0. For each such bar, take the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its normal drift, which is its mean 5-bar return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the forecast (post_pipeline_value), pooled over coins, with errors clustered by day. The measure is the slope's t.
- Supporting check: Take the cases, meaning rises of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Their mean unusual move over 5 bars, in the long direction, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, on at least 200 grid bars with a positive forecast, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, on at least 200 grid bars with a positive forecast. A larger scaled rise is then followed by a smaller excess return.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more while the cases' mean unusual move is not above 0. Any result with fewer than 200 grid bars with a positive forecast is also inconclusive.
