Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day fall, measured against its own daily volatility over the past 60 days -> kept
- Comparison: the coin's normal drift over the next 3 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises above its normal drift over the next 3 days, more for a deeper fall -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall-in-usual-moves, graded, held for its 3-day horizon and judged against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast is built from VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 60. Its raw value is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), negated so that a fall reads positive, and clipped at 0. The forecast is 0 on up and flat days. On down days it is positive and grows in proportion to the depth of the fall in usual daily moves, about +10 for a fall of the usual size, capped at +20. It is never negative.
Config: 031-01.g1_volatility-scaled-down-day-rebound_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: This is a pooled least-squares slope across all coins and windows. It uses non-overlapping 3-bar grid bars in each window. The dependent value is the coin's 3-day return minus its normal 3-day drift (its mean over all windows), divided by its usual 3-day move. The explanatory value is the forecast at the grid bar. The t of the slope is computed with the grid bars of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Take the cases (forecast rises of at least 2 points). Their mean unusual move over 3 days, in the rise direction, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: deeper falls are followed by weaker returns than the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more when the cases' mean unusual move is not above 0. At least 100 grid bars with a forecast above 0 are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
