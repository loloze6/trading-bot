- Situation: a coin's 10-day rise reaches several of its 60-day daily volatilities -> adapted: the regime is on while the 10-day return over (60-day daily volatility x sqrt 10) is at least 0.9487, which is 3 daily volatilities, the usual reading of "several"; nothing is held outside it.
- Comparison: the raw ratio to its own volatility, not to the measure's own recent history -> kept: the regime rule reads the raw value; inside the regime, standardise only rescales the size.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 10 days beyond its normal drift, more for larger and sharply more for extreme rises -> adapted: a long that grows with the ratio and is made convex with signed_power exponent 2, held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test is long only in the stated situation, sized by the stated measure and judged against the coin's drift over 10 days, so either result carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 10 and vol_period 60. Its value is the 10-day return divided by the 60-day daily standard deviation times sqrt 10. The forecast is zero unless this raw value is at least 0.9487, which means a 10-day rise of at least 3 daily volatilities. Inside that regime the value is standardised over 70 bars (min_periods 20), passed through signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10, then clipped at 0 so it stays long. It is positive only in the situation. It grows with the square of the scaled rise, so the most extreme rises get much larger longs.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, measured on the 10-bar non-overlapping grid. A case is a long bet, meaning a forecast rise of at least 2 points. Its unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return from the signal's close minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows. The measure is the mean of these moves, with its t computed over days (cases on the same day pooled).
- Supporting check: Among the cases, the mean unusual move of the top third by forecast minus that of the bottom third must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 distinct case days.
