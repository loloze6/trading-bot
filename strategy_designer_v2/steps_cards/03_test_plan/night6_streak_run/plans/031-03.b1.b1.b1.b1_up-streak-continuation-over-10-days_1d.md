Fidelity:
- Situation: an up-close streak, graded by its length against the coin's own completed up streaks over 365 days -> kept
- Comparison: the coin's own up-streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept
- Outcome: the price keeps rising over the next 10 days, only while the 30-day return is positive -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the streak length over its 365-day usual length, active only in a positive 30-day return and held for 10 days, so a gain or a loss here is the idea's own.
Forecast: The forecast uses `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` (side "up", base "mean_streak", base_window 365, max_bars 30). Its value is the current run of higher closes divided by the mean length of the coin's completed up streaks over the past 365 days. It is standardised over its past 366 values, so a usual streak reads about +10, a longer one reads more (capped at 20), and a day that did not close higher reads 0. The detector allows a position only while the 30-day return (`PriceEvolutionOnPeriodComponent`, 30) is above 0. On all other bars the forecast is 0. The forecast is never negative: it is long only, and the position grows with how unusually long the streak is.
Config: 031-03.b1.b1.b1.b1_up-streak-continuation-over-10-days_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases over 10 bars. A case is a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The move is the coin's 10-day return from the signal bar minus its normal 10-day drift over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean unusual move of the cases whose forecast is in the top third is larger than that of the bottom third (top minus bottom above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the top-third case mean does not exceed the bottom-third mean. The verdict needs at least 100 cases falling on at least 50 distinct days; fewer than that is inconclusive.
