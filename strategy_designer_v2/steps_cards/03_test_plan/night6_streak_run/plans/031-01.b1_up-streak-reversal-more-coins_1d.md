Fidelity:
- Situation: the number of days in a row the coin has closed higher -> kept: the length of the current up-close streak (0 on a day that did not close up)
- Comparison: against the lengths of its own up-close streaks over the past 365 days -> kept: streak length divided by the mean length of the completed up streaks in the last 365 daily bars
- Outcome: the more unusual the streak, the more the price falls over the next 5 days -> adapted: a short graded by that ratio, held for the 5-day horizon
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own relative streak length as a short, so the 5-day forward moves it predicts are exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: One component, `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` (side "up", base "mean_streak", base_window 365). It gives the current up-close streak length in multiples of the coin's mean completed up-streak length over the past 365 days. It is standardised against its own past 365 values and then negated. The forecast is negative (short) on every bar that closes up, and its size grows with how long the streak is against the coin's usual streak, capped at -20. It is 0 on any bar that did not close up, where no streak exists. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, measured in the signal's (short) direction. A case is a short bet (a rise of the forecast toward short of at least 2 points), read once per 5-bar grid cell. The measure is the coin's 5-bar return after the case minus its normal 5-bar drift. Its t has the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast| at the case. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: Main-measure t >= 2, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: Main-measure t <= -2, meaning the price rose more than its drift after up streaks.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t >= 2 with the supporting check not positive. At least 100 distinct case days are required; fewer is inconclusive.
