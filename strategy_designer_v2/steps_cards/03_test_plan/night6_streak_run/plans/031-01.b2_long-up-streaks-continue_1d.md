Fidelity:
- Situation: days in a row a coin has closed higher -> kept: the current up-close streak length, 0 when the day did not close up
- Comparison: against its own up-close streak lengths over the past 365 days -> kept: streak length divided by the mean length of its completed up streaks over the last 365 bars
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next 3 days, the longer that relative streak -> kept: 3-bar forward return, judged by whether it grows with the position, against holding the coin
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own relative streak length, so the test's result on it is the idea's result
Forecast: UpCloseStreakLengthComponent (side "up", base "mean_streak", base_window 365, max_bars 30) gives the current up-close streak length divided by the mean length of the coin's completed up streaks over the last 365 days. Standardise scales it against its own past 365 values (min_periods 91). The forecast is 0 on any day that did not close up and positive otherwise. It grows with how long the streak is relative to the coin's usual streak, and it is capped at 20. It is long-only by construction and never negative. No regime is used, because the idea states a graded relation, not a condition.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 3-bar forward return, minus its normal 3-bar drift (mean over all windows), on the bar's forecast. It is measured on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window, all coins pooled, with its t.
- Supporting check: On grid bars with forecast ≥ 10, the mean 3-bar return minus the normal drift is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2, or a positive slope with t ≥ 2 while the check fails. At least 300 grid bars with forecast > 0 are required, else the result is inconclusive.
