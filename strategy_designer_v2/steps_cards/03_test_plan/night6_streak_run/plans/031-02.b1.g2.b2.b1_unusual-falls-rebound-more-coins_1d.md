Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's percentage fall on its latest day, measured against the standard deviation of its past 60 daily returns -> kept
- Comparison: the larger the fall in those units, the larger the rise -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 2 days, more after larger falls -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the day's fall in 60-day standard deviations, zero on up days, so a rise that grows with it confirms the idea and a fall that grows with it rejects it.
Forecast: One component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 60, at lag 0 and vol_lookback 1. It computes the latest day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 60 daily returns. That value is standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a one-standard-deviation fall reads about +12. It is then negated, so falls are positive, and clipped at 0, so up days and flat days are 0. The forecast is long only and positive only after a down day. It grows linearly with how many usual daily moves the fall spans, up to the cap of 20. It is never negative. It is ungated, because the fall itself is the graded situation.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: On each window's non-overlapping 2-bar grid, take the bars with forecast > 0. On those bars, regress the coin's 2-day forward return minus its normal 2-day drift (its mean over all windows) on the forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with cases on the same day taken together (day-clustered).
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with a day-clustered t of 2 or more, on at least 100 cases spread over at least 50 days.
- Reject: The slope is negative with a day-clustered t of -2 or less, on at least 100 cases spread over at least 50 days.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. Fewer than 100 cases with forecast > 0, or fewer than 50 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
