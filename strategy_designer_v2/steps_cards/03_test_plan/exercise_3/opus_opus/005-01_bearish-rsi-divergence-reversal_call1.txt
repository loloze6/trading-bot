forecast: Use a 14-bar RSI (Wilder) on daily closes, computed separately for each coin.
- At each bar's close, take the recent high H1, the highest close of the last 10 bars including today, and note its bar.
- Take the prior high H0, the highest close from 60 bars ago to 11 bars ago, and note its bar.
- A bearish divergence is active when H1 is above H0 and the RSI at H1's bar is below the RSI at H0's bar.
- When the divergence is active, the forecast is minus min((RSI at H0 − RSI at H1) / 20, 1). The position is therefore short, and it grows with how far momentum has faded behind the new high. It reaches a full short at a 20-point RSI shortfall.
- Otherwise the forecast is zero. This covers bars with no new high above H0, and new highs where RSI also makes a higher high.
- The forecast is never positive, because the idea speaks only about new highs.
- Since H1 is drawn from a rolling 10-bar window, the short stays on, recomputed each bar, for up to 10 bars after a divergent high. It then returns to zero unless a newer divergent high appears.
- All settings are conventional (RSI 14, 20/60-bar swing windows, a 20-point RSI gap for full size) and are fixed in advance.

confirm: The horizon is 10 bars. Take every bar where the forecast is nonzero, pooled across BTC, ETH and BNB. Compute the coin's 10-bar forward log return after each one. As a control, compute the same return after every bar where H1 is above H0 but RSI did not make a lower high, meaning a new high without divergence. The idea is confirmed if all of the following hold:
- The mean forward return after divergence bars is negative.
- It is below the control mean, with a one-sided block-bootstrap p-value under 0.05 (resampling blocks of 10 bars).
- The difference has the same sign in at least 2 of the 3 coins.
- Before costs, the strategy's 10-bar returns on active bars beat simply holding the coin over the same bars.

reject: The idea is rejected if either of these holds:
- The pooled mean 10-bar forward return after divergence bars is equal to or higher than after non-divergent new highs. This is the brief's own falsification line.
- The strategy's returns on active bars are not better than holding the coin over the same bars.

inconclusive: Any of the following counts as inconclusive:
- Divergence returns come out lower than the control but the bootstrap p-value is 0.05 or higher.
- The effect appears in only one of the three coins.
- Fewer than 30 independent divergence episodes are found, pooled. Overlapping bars from the same divergent high count as one episode.

deviation:
- Highs are measured on closes, and the "previous high" is taken from fixed windows (60 to 11 bars back versus the last 10 bars) rather than from discretionary swing pivots.
- The retracement "of part of the run" is tested as a negative 10-bar forward return compared with non-divergent new highs. It is not tested as a measured fraction of the prior advance.
- The position size following the RSI shortfall is a modelling choice the idea does not state.
- The mirror case (bullish divergence at new lows) from the brief is not tested, because this idea covers only highs.
