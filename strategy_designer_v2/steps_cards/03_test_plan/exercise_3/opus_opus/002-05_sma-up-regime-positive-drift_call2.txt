differs: The config uses the same gap, (SMA50 − SMA200) / SMA200. It is zero when the gap is zero or negative and is capped at allocation 1 (forecast 10). The divisor is different. The plan divides the gap by 10 × the standard deviation of daily close-to-close returns over the last 100 bars. The config divides it by the mean absolute value of the gap over the previous 200 bars, as required by `standardise`, so there is no return-volatility scale. As a result, a gap at its own recent average size gives forecast 10 (allocation 1). The cap is therefore reached whenever the gap is above its recent average absolute size, which is much sooner than under the plan's 10σ scale.
