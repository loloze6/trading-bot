Forecast: One indicator, `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent` with `period` 1, `base_period` 30, `side` "below", `high_current` 1 and `base_exclude_current` 1. It computes (high − close) / (the mean daily high-low range of the prior 30 days). This equals the close's place from the top of the day's range (0 at the high, 1 at the low) times the day's range in usual daily ranges. It is 0 when the day closes at its high and grows as the close sits lower and the range is wider. It is never negative, so the forecast is long only. It is standardised over 60 values (min_periods 20), so an average reading gives about +10. `signed_power` with exponent 2 then damps mid-range and high-close days toward 0 and enlarges low closes on wide days. Outside the agitated profile the book is flat; the profile is added by code.
