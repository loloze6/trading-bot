Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling, agitated market (the market profile), with the coin's close low within its day's high-low range -> kept: the profile is added by code, and the close location is measured directly, graded by how low it sits.
- Comparison: none -> kept
- Outcome: the lower the close in the range, the larger the coin's rise over the next day -> kept: the next-day return is graded by the close location, over 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same close location, in the same profile, against the same next-day return.
Forecast: The forecast is the day's close location measured from the high, (high − close) / (high − low): 0 at the high and 1 at the low. It is standardised over the 60 days before the bar, so a typical day reads about 10. It is averaged with equal weight against the constant −10. The forecast is therefore about 10 × location − 5. It runs from about −5 (close at the day's high) to about +5 (close at the day's low). It is positive when the close sits lower in the range than usual, negative when higher, and near zero in mid-range. It grows linearly as the close moves toward the low. It is ungated here; the code keeps it flat outside the falling agitated profile.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On every in-profile bar with a non-zero forecast, compute forecast × (the coin's next-day return − the coin's mean daily return over all windows). Sum these per day across coins, then take the t of the mean of the daily sums. The drift is removed, so the market's own move is not credited to the idea.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The mean is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 100 days of cases.
- Reject: The mean is negative with t ≤ −2, over at least 100 days of cases. That would mean low closes are followed by weaker next days, so the pressure continues.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is any result on fewer than 100 days with in-profile cases.
