Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling, calm market, and a coin that has fallen over the past 30 days -> kept: the profile is added by code; inside it, the 30-day fall is the forecast, and the forecast is zero when the coin has not fallen.
- Comparison: larger past 30-day falls against smaller ones -> adapted: graded, so the short position grows with the size of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next day, and falls more after a larger fall -> kept: the horizon is 1 bar, the book is short, and the size follows the fall.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the graded short inside the falling calm profile is the idea as stated, so a confirm confirms it and a reject rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is built from one indicator, the coin's 30-day percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30, sf 1). - It is standardised against its own past 60 values, then clipped at a maximum of 0. - It is negative (short) when the coin is down over 30 days, and grows in size as the fall deepens, up to the cap of -20. - It is 0 when the coin is up or flat over 30 days. - It never goes long. - Step 3's code keeps the book flat outside the falling calm profile.

Profile: falling calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The slope of next-day excess return on the size of the forecast. - Cases: every bar with a forecast below 0. On the 1-bar grid, every bar is its own case. - Next-day excess return: close[t+1]/close[t]-1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. - Size of the forecast: |forecast|. - The slope's t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean of the next-day excess return, taken in the short direction (the negated excess return), over all cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, and the mean short-direction excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is positive with t ≥ +2: larger past falls are followed by higher next-day returns, a rebound.
- Inconclusive: - Any slope with |t| < 2. - A slope with t ≤ -2 but a mean short-direction excess return at or below 0. - Fewer than 300 cases falling on at least 100 distinct days.
