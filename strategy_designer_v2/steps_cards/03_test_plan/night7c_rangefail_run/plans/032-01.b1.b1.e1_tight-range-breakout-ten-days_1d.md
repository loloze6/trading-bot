Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close above the prior 60-day high, in a coin that has gone nowhere over 60 days -> kept: both are conditions in a regime rule, a close above the prior 60-day high and a 60-day efficiency ratio of 0.3 or less, the usual cut for a trendless market.
- Comparison: narrowness of the prior 60-day high-low range as a share of the price -> kept: graded by nearness of the close to the prior 60-day low, in percent of that low, which spans the range up to the breaking close.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 10 days, larger for a tighter range -> kept: the 10-day return after the breakout day, held for 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on trendless 60-day breakouts, sized by how tight the range is, so its 10-day results confirm or reject the idea directly.
Forecast: The regime rule fires on a bar when two conditions hold. The close is above the highest high of the 60 bars before it (DistanceFromHigh, period 60, source high, exclude_current 1, percent form, above 0). The 60-bar efficiency ratio is 0.3 or less (smooth_period 5). On those bars the forecast is the proximity of the close to the prior 60-bar low, 1/(1+D), where D is the close's distance above that low in percent. It is standardised over 60 bars with min_periods 20, so a tighter prior range gives a larger long. The forecast is always positive in the regime and 0 on every other bar. It never goes short, and the bet is held for 10 bars.

- Main measure: A case is a bar where the forecast rises by 2 or more from 0, read once per 10-bar grid cell of each window. The measure is the mean of these, with its t, counting cases on the same day as one.
- Supporting check: The slope of the case's excess 10-day return on the case's `tight` post_pipeline_value is positive.
- Confirm: The mean excess 10-day return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean excess 10-day return has t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 with a slope of 0 or below. So is any result from fewer than 30 independent cases.
