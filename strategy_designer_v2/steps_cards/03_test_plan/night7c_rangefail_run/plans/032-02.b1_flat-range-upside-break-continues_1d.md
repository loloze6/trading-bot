Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin that has gone nowhere over the past 60 days closes above its prior 60-day high -> adapted: "gone nowhere" is a regime (60-day efficiency ratio at or below 0.3, the usual choppy level), and inside it the break above the prior 60-day high is graded, 0 at or below the high.
- Comparison: the further the close sits above the old high, the larger the rise -> kept: the forecast grows with the close's distance above the prior 60-day high, counted in mean daily ranges.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 5 days -> kept: long only, each bet held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds positions only on trendless-coin upside breaks, sized by the break, so a 5-day gain confirms the idea and a 5-day loss rejects it.
Forecast: The regime detector computes Kaufman's efficiency ratio over 60 daily closes, unsmoothed (smooth_period 1). When it is at or below 0.3, the coin has gone nowhere and the bar is `chop`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. In `chop`, the forecast is BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent with period 60, base_period 60 and side "above": the close's distance above the highest high of the prior 60 bars, in mean daily ranges of the last 60 bars. It is 0 when the close is at or below that high. The value is standardised against its own past 500 values (min_periods 120). The long window is needed because the series is 0 on most bars, and a 60-bar window would often have a past mean of 0. A clip at min 0 keeps it long only. The forecast is 0 outside a trendless break, positive during one, and larger the further the close sits above the old high (capped at 20).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bets: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar cell of each window's grid. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all its windows (non-overlapping). The measure is the mean of that unusual move and its t, with cases on the same day summed into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the slope of the unusual 5-day move on the entry forecast is positive (above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual 5-day move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the slope on the entry forecast is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual 5-day move is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a slope of 0 or below is also inconclusive. So is a result with fewer than 30 cases, or cases falling on fewer than 30 distinct days.
