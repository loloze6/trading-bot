Fidelity:
- Situation: a close first moves above the highest close of the prior 60 days -> kept: a regime gate on the close above the prior 60 closes; a close above a high it has just raised counts as a fresh break.
- Comparison: the margin of the close above that prior high, smaller is better -> kept: 1 / (1 + margin in percent), above 0 only on break bars.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 3 days for a smaller margin, long side -> kept: the book holds 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test sizes each break's long by its closeness to the prior high and judges the next 3 days against that closeness.
Forecast: A regime rule selects only the bars whose close is above the highest close of the prior 60 bars. The rule reads `DistanceFromHighComponent` with period 60, the close, the current bar excluded and the percent form; the bar is a break when that value is above 0. Every other bar is flat. On a break bar the forecast is `BreakoutMarginProximityComponent` (period 60), equal to 1 / (1 + margin above the prior high in percent), standardised over 60 bars. It is positive (long) only, largest when the close just clears the high, and shrinks smoothly as the overshoot grows. It is 0 on every bar without a break.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are break bars (regime "trending") in each window, kept only when no earlier kept case lies within the prior 3 bars. For each case, take the coin's 3-bar return minus its mean 3-bar return over all windows. Regress this excess return on the raw proximity (`margin_prox` last_history_value). The measure is the slope's t, with cases on the same day summed into one deviation.
- Supporting check: The proximity-weighted mean of the cases' excess 3-bar return is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes, over at least 100 cases.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less over at least 100 cases. That means larger overshoots are followed by larger rises.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more with the weighted mean excess return at or below 0. Fewer than 100 cases is also inconclusive.
