Fidelity:
- Situation: a close above the highest close of the prior 60 days -> kept: the component's gate is close[t] > max(close[t-60..t-1]), and the forecast is 0 on every other bar.
- Comparison: the smaller the 60-day return before that close -> adapted: graded as 1 minus the 60-day return into the prior bar, counted in usual 60-day moves and clipped at 0.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 20 days -> kept: each breakout bet is held 20 daily bars, long.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test compares 20-day rises across breakouts by their prior run-up, so it confirms or rejects the idea itself.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, `BreakoutRunUpDiscountComponent`, with period 60, rise_period 60, rise_lag 1 and window 120. It reads 0 on every bar that does not close above the prior 60 daily closes. On a breakout bar it reads max(0, 1 − R/m). R is the 60-day return ending the day before the breakout. m is the mean absolute 60-day return over the last 120 days. The reading is 1 after a flat climb and above 1 after a prior fall. It falls linearly as the run-up grows and reaches 0 at a run-up of one usual 60-day move or more. Standardise rescales it against its own past 180 values with target 1, not the usual 10. Breakouts fill only a small share of the bars, so a target of 10 would put almost every breakout at the cap of 20. The forecast is never negative: it is long only, and larger the more modest the climb.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The cases are breakout bars, recomputed from the closes, with one case per coin-window 20-bar grid cell (the first breakout in the cell). For each case, take the 20-bar forward return from the breakout close minus the coin's mean 20-bar return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the case's raw weight (`last_history_value` of brk_runup). Steep breakouts have weight 0 and are included. The measure is the t of the slope, with cases on the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Take the cases with weight above 0. Their mean 20-bar return, minus the coin's mean 20-bar return, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with a non-positive supporting check is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 40 breakout cases in total, at least 20 of them with weight above 0; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
