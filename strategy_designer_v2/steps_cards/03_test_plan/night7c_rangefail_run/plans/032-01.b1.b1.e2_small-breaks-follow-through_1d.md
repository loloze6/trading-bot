- Situation: a coin that went nowhere over 60 days closes above its prior 60-day high -> adapted: a regime of Efficiency Ratio(60) below 0.3 and DistanceFromHigh(60, close, prior bars) above 0.
- Comparison: the smaller the close's distance above the prior high, the larger the rise -> replaced: no component falls with the break's size, and the only workaround is a constant offset, which is barred inside a regime.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 20 days, long -> kept.
- Verdict transfer: no: without the inverse grading by break distance, the test cannot confirm or reject "the smaller the break, the larger the rise".
Reason: The forecast must shrink as the close's distance above the prior 60-day high grows. No catalogue component does this, alone or in a weighted sum. DistanceFromHigh's "proximity" form measures only distance below the high, and centring with BuyAndHold is a constant inside a regime, which the rules forbid.
