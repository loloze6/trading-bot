Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 60-day range of closes, narrow against its average absolute daily move -> kept: the 60-day closing range counted in mean absolute one-bar moves, graded so that a tighter range gives a larger value.
- Comparison: the range judged against the coin's own average absolute daily move -> kept: the range is divided by the mean absolute daily return over 60 bars.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 20 days, the tighter the range -> kept: horizon 20 bars, long, larger with compression.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own compression measure, so a monotone relation with the 20-day rise confirms it, and a negative one rejects it.
Forecast: `ClosingRangeInUsualMovesComponent` (period 60, window 60, form "compression") gives 1 / (1 + W). W is the 60-day range of closes before today, divided by today's close and by the mean absolute daily return over 60 days. The value is always positive and grows as the range narrows. It is standardised against its own past 61 values, so it sits near +10 at its usual compression and runs up to 20. An equal-weight constant offset of -10 is added. The forecast is (x - 10) / 2: positive and growing when the range is tighter than usual, negative when it is wider, and 0 at the usual compression. It is graded on every bar with no regime, because the idea is a steady relation and not a condition.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the bars at positions 0, 20, 40, ... of each window, per coin. At each, take the coin's 20-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 20-bar return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the bar's `forecast`, pooled over coins, before costs. Standard errors are clustered by date, so that cases on the same day count once.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 20-bar return of grid cases in the top fifth of `forecast` minus that of the bottom fifth is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2 and the top-minus-bottom difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive. This includes a slope with |t| < 2, or a positive slope with t >= 2 whose top-minus-bottom difference is not above 0. The test needs at least 300 grid cases on at least 100 distinct dates; fewer is inconclusive.
