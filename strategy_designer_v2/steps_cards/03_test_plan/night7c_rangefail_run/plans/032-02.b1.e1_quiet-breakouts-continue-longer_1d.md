Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin that has gone nowhere over the past 60 days closes above its prior 60-day high -> adapted: "gone nowhere" is a regime rule, 60-day efficiency ratio below 0.3 (the common Kaufman choppy convention); the close above the prior 60-day closing high is a second rule; the position exists only on those bars.
- Comparison: the smaller that day's own 1-day gain, the larger the rise -> kept: inside the regime the forecast is graded by the nearness of the day's gain to zero, 1 / (1 + 1-day gain in percent).
- Outcome: a rise over the next 10 days -> kept: a long held for 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the 10-day rise after these quiet-range breakouts and whether smaller breaking-day gains carry larger rises.
Forecast: The regime detector selects `trending` only on bars where the 60-bar efficiency ratio (smoothed over 5 bars) is below 0.3 and the close is above the highest close of the prior 60 bars. Every other bar is flat. On a selected bar the forecast is long. It is `DistanceFromHighComponent` (`extreme` "low", `period` 2, `form` "proximity", `power` 1), which reads 1 / (1 + the day's percent gain). It is standardised over its past 60 values, so a breakout on a near-zero gain gives about 12 to 13. A gain of a usual daily size gives about 6 to 8, and a 10% jump gives about 1. The forecast is always positive and shrinks smoothly as the breaking day's gain grows.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over 10 bars per case: the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close minus its normal 10-bar drift (holding the coin, all windows together). A case is a forecast rise of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The measure is the mean over cases, with its t taken with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: The cases are split at the median entry forecast. The check is the mean unusual move of the larger-forecast half minus that of the smaller-forecast half (Welch t).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting difference is above 0 with t ≥ 2.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2, or the supporting difference is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Anything else is inconclusive, including a main t between -2 and 2, or a main confirm with a check t between -2 and 2. At least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days are required; fewer is inconclusive.
