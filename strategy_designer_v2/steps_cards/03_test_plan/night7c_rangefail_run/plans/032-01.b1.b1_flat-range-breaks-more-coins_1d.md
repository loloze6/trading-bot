Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin that has gone nowhere over 60 days closes above its prior 60-day high -> adapted: a regime holds the position while the 60-day efficiency ratio is at or below 0.3 (the usual choppy convention) and the close sits above the prior 60-day high; "after the break" becomes "while above it".
- Comparison: the narrower and flatter the 60-day range, the larger the rise -> adapted: inside the regime the position is graded by the 60-day flatness and by how narrow the prior 60-day range is against the 180-day range.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 20 days -> kept: long bets are held for 20 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test is long only on breaks out of flat 60-day ranges, sized by flatness and narrowness, and it is judged on the 20-day move, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless two things hold at the bar's close. The 60-bar efficiency ratio must be at or below 0.3, meaning the coin went nowhere. The close must sit above the highest high of the 60 prior bars. On those bars the forecast is long and is the equal-weight mean of two standardised measures. The first is the 60-bar flatness-weighted range position (high/low range). It is near the flatness at a breakout and grows as the 60-day net move gets smaller against the range. The second is the compression of the prior 60-bar range against the 180-bar range. It grows as the prior range gets narrower. Both are clipped at 0, so the forecast is never negative and it grows with flatness and narrowness.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual 20-bar move after each case, in the long direction. A case is a long bet of the forecast, read once per 20-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 20-bar return from the signal's close minus its normal 20-bar drift over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases with an above-median forecast, minus that of cases with a below-median forecast, is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected in three cases: t between -2 and 2; t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails; or fewer than 30 cases (or fewer than 20 distinct days).
