- Situation: a daily close above the highest close of the prior 60 days -> adapted: "when" becomes "while", a regime active on each bar whose close is above the prior 60-day closing high.
- Comparison: the 60-day range of closes against the average absolute daily move, narrower meaning stronger -> kept: ClosingRangeInUsualMovesComponent (60, 60) computes this ratio, and its "compression" form, 1/(1+W), grows as the range narrows.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 5 days the tighter the range -> kept: each bet is held 5 bars, and the forecast is long and grows with tightness.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this rise on exactly these breakout bars, graded by exactly this ratio, so a confirm or a reject applies to the idea.
Forecast: On bars where the close is above the highest close of the prior 60 days, the regime is "trending". There the forecast is the standardised 60-day closing-range compression, 1/(1+W), where W is the prior 60-day range of closes in average absolute daily moves (window 60). The compression is standardised over its own past 61 values. The forecast is always positive (long only) and grows smoothly as the range was tighter against usual daily moves, capped at +20. It is near +10 for a typical tightness. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are breakout signals (forecast bets) on the 5-bar non-overlapping grid. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return after the signal bar minus the coin's normal 5-day drift over all its windows (the unusual move). Compare the cases in the upper half of the forecast with those in the lower half. The measure is the mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half, tested with Welch's t.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move over all cases is above 0.
- Confirm: Both conditions must hold: - the upper-minus-lower difference has Welch t ≥ 2; - the mean unusual move over all cases is above 0.
- Reject: The upper-minus-lower difference has Welch t ≤ -2: tighter ranges are followed by smaller rises.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t when the overall mean unusual move is not above 0. The test needs at least 30 cases in each half, 60 in all; fewer cases is also inconclusive.
