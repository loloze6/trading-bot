Fidelity:
- Situation: a close above the highest close of the prior 60 days -> kept: a regime is selected only on the bar with a new 60-day closing high (`BarsSinceHighComponent`, lookback 60).
- Comparison: the narrower the 60-day range between highest and lowest close -> kept: the forecast is 1/(1 + percent distance of the close above its prior 60-day lowest close), which is that range on a breakout bar.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 20 days -> kept: each bet is held 20 daily bars, and only long.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test grades breakout bars by range narrowness and measures the 20-day rise, as the idea states.
Forecast: On a bar whose close is above every one of its prior 60 closes, the forecast is long and graded by narrowness. The raw value is 1/(1+D), where D is the percent rise of the close above the lowest close of the prior 60 days. On a breakout bar, D is the 60-day closing range. This value is standardised against its own past 61 values. The forecast is therefore larger when the range is tighter and smaller when the range is wide, and it is never negative. On every other bar the regime is null and the forecast is 0. The 20-bar holding period keeps each breakout bet for 20 days.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: A case is a breakout bar (regime trending), taken once per 20-bar grid cell of each window. For each case, compute the 20-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 20-bar return over all windows. The measure is Welch's t of that excess return, narrower-half cases minus wider-half cases, with the halves split at the median of `narrow.last_history_value` per coin.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 20-bar return of the narrower-half cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when both conditions hold: - Welch t of narrow minus wide is 2 or more. - The narrower-half mean excess return is positive.
- Reject: Welch t of narrow minus wide is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more with a non-positive narrow-half mean. The test also needs at least 60 cases, with at least 20 in each half; fewer is inconclusive.
