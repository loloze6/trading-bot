Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin that has gone nowhere over the past 60 days closes above its prior 60-day high -> adapted: "after the close" made "while the close is above the prior 60-day high". Both parts are a regime: Kaufman efficiency ratio(60) at or below 0.3 (the usual choppy level), and the close above the prior 60-day high.
- Comparison: the narrower the 60-day range relative to the price, the larger the rise -> kept: graded by nearness to the prior 60-day low, 1 / (1 + (close − low) / low %). On a breakout bar this is the range in percent plus the bar's small overshoot.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 10 days -> kept: each breakout bet is held for 10 daily bars and long only.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on trendless 60-day breakouts, sized by how tight the range was, and held for 10 days, so its result after those bars is the idea's result.
Forecast: Two conditions must both hold. The 60-day efficiency ratio (smoothing 5) must be at or below 0.3, and the close must be above the highest high of the prior 60 bars. On those bars the forecast is a proximity standardised over its past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). The proximity is 1 / (1 + D), where D is the close's percent distance above the lowest low of the prior 60 bars. The forecast is always positive and grows as the two-month range gets tighter against the price. On every other bar the forecast is 0.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a long bet: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return from the case's close minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all its windows. The measure is its mean and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the rank correlation between the entry forecast (tightness) and the unusual move is above 0.
- Confirm: Both must hold: the mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting rank correlation is above 0.
- Reject: Either result rejects: the mean unusual move has t ≤ −2, or the rank correlation between tightness and unusual move has t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Every result between those, including a positive move with t < 2. A positive move with t ≥ 2 but a correlation at or below 0 is also inconclusive. At least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days are required, else inconclusive.
