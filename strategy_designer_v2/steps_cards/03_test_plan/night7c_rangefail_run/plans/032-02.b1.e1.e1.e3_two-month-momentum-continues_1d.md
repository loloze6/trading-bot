Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 60-day return, higher or lower -> kept
- Comparison: none (the relation is graded along the coin's own 60-day return) -> kept
- Outcome: the higher that return, the larger the rise over the next 20 days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: a graded signed 60-day momentum forecast held 20 days earns, against holding the coin, exactly when larger past returns are followed by larger rises.
Forecast: The forecast is the coin's 60-day close-to-close return from `PriceEvolutionComponent` (period 60), standardised against its own last 60 values (min_periods 20). It is positive after a 60-day rise and negative after a 60-day fall. Its size grows with the size of that return relative to its recent typical size, about ±10 on average and capped at ±20. It is zero only when the 60-day return is zero. It runs on every bar, with no regime gate.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 20-bar forward return on the forecast, with Welch-free OLS t. - The return is taken minus the coin's mean 20-bar return over all its windows. - Samples are non-overlapping, on the 20-bar grid of each window, all coins pooled.
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily return minus its gain from the market's own rise (the timing part) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the timing part of the book's mean daily return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope whose timing check fails. At least 300 non-overlapping grid cases with a nonzero forecast are needed, else inconclusive.
