Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close above the highest close of the prior 60 days -> adapted: "after the close" made "while the close is above it", a regime on the 60-day prior-high distance being above 0.
- Comparison: a smaller margin over the prior high means a larger rise -> kept: the position grows as the percent margin over the prior 60-day closing high shrinks.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 20 days -> kept: long, each bet held 20 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether 20-day returns after 60-day closing breakouts are larger for smaller margins, which is what the idea claims.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the percent distance of the close from the highest close of the prior 60 bars (bar t excluded). The book holds a position only on bars where that distance is above 0, which is a fresh 60-day closing breakout. On all other bars the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two parts. The first is a +10 offset. The second is the same percent margin, standardised against its own past 60 values, negated, and clipped at -10. Before the clip, that second part is minus the margin's size against the past mean absolute distance. The forecast is therefore about +5 for a close just over the old high and falls smoothly toward 0 as the margin widens to the usual size of the distance. It never goes below 0, so the forecast stays long. The smaller the margin, the larger the long.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The cases are breakout bets read on the non-overlapping 20-bar grid of each window. For each case, take the coin's 20-bar return from the signal bar minus the coin's normal 20-bar drift over all windows. Split the cases at the median of the margin, read from `margin.last_history_value`. The measure is Welch's t of that excess return for the small-margin half minus the large-margin half.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean excess 20-bar return over all cases is above 0.
- Confirm: Welch t ≥ 2, with the small-margin half rising more than the large-margin half, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: Welch t ≤ -2, with the large-margin half rising more than the small-margin half.
- Inconclusive: Welch t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a forecast-weighted mean excess return ≤ 0 is also inconclusive. Each half needs at least 20 cases, else the test is inconclusive.
