=== 005-06_bullish-divergence-downtrend-continues

The catalogue gives the price side (`DonchianBreakoutComponent`) but only a raw RSI level (`RSIPullbackComponent`), and no weighted sum of those reproduces "RSI above its own recent minimum". With an RSI range-position component, the forecast would be the standardised Donchian minus the standardised RSI position, clipped at max 0 to keep only the bullish-divergence case.
