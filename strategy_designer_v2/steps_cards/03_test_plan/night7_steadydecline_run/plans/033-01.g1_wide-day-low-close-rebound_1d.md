Fidelity:
- Situation: a day wide against its usual range that closes near its low (or near its high) -> kept: (high + low - 2 x close) / mean daily range, positive near the low, negative near the high, larger the wider the day.
- Comparison: the coin's usual daily range -> kept: the mean high-low range of the last 20 days (bar t included, the convention), the divisor of both parts.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next day after a near-low close, and a fall after a near-high close -> kept: the next 1-bar return, long or short by the forecast's sign.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded product and the outcome is its next-day return, so a confirm or reject of the graded relation is one of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is built from two components on the same base, the mean daily high-low range over the last 20 days (today included). The first is (high − close) / base: the day's width times how near the close sits to its low. The second is (high − low) / base: the day's width alone. Each is standardised on its own past 60 values, with the cap raised to 40 so that wide days are not cut off before they are combined. The second component is negated. Both carry weight 1. The forecast is their mean, about 5 x (high + low − 2 x close) / usual range. It is positive when the close sits in the lower half of the day's range and negative in the upper half. It is near 0 when the close is mid-range or the day is narrow, and it grows with both the width of the day and how far the close sits from the middle. It is clipped to ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's next-day return in excess of its mean daily return (over all windows) regressed on the bar's forecast, pooled over coins and windows. Every bar is one case (h = 1), and the t is taken with cases on the same day summed into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: The mean next-day excess return of bars in the top fifth of the forecast minus that of the bottom fifth must be above 0 (near-low closes beat near-high closes).
- Confirm: Slope t ≥ 2 and the top-minus-bottom-fifth difference is above 0.
- Reject: Slope t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between −2 and 2, or a t ≥ 2 while the fifth difference is not above 0. At least 500 bars with |forecast| ≥ 2 and 1,000 distinct days are needed; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
