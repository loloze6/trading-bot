Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close sits near the day's low, on a day whose range is wide against the past 20 days' usual swing -> kept: (high - close) / the mean high-low range of the prior 20 days, which equals the close's nearness to the low times the range multiple.
- Comparison: the day's range against the coin's usual daily swing over the past 20 days -> kept: the mean daily high-low range of the 20 prior days, with the current day excluded.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next day, more for a nearer close and a wider day -> kept: a long position over 1 bar, sized by the product.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded product, held for its one-day horizon.
Forecast: `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent` is used with `period` 1, `base_period` 20, `side` "below", `high_current` 1 and `base_exclude_current` 1. It gives (high[t] − close[t]) / mean(high − low over bars t-20..t-1), which is the close's distance below the day's high counted in usual daily ranges. It is standardised over 60 past values (min 20). The forecast is never negative. It is 0 when the close is at the high. It grows as the close nears the low, and grows further as the day's range widens against its 20-day mean. It is about +10 at its usual size and capped at +20. No regime is used, because the relation is graded on every bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Pool every bar of all coins and windows (each is its own non-overlapping 1-bar case). Take the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Measure the least-squares slope of that excess return on the bar's `post_pipeline_value`, with its t computed from the cases summed per day.
- Supporting check: The mean excess next-day return on bars in the top fifth of the forecast (per coin) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the top-fifth mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2. A close near the low after a wide day is then followed by below-usual next-day returns.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2, or a positive slope with t ≥ 2 while the top-fifth mean is ≤ 0. At least 1000 bars and 300 distinct days are needed, else inconclusive.
