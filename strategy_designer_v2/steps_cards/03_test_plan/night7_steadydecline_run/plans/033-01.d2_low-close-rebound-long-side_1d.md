Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closes in the lower part of its own high-low range -> kept: a regime, close location (close − low)/(high − low) below 0.5; flat otherwise.
- Comparison: nearer the close to the day's low, the larger the rise -> adapted: graded, the forecast grows from about 0 at mid-range to its largest at the low.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next day -> kept: long only, held 1 day (1 bar).
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether, on low-half closes, the next-day rise grows with the closeness of the close to the low.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "low") on each bar. Below 0.5 the bar is `mean_reversion`; at or above 0.5 it is `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime the forecast averages two parts with equal weights. The first part is `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "high"), the close's nearness to the low, standardised over 60 bars (min 20). It reads about 10 at mid-range and up to 20 at the low. The second part is the constant offset −10 (`BuyAndHoldStrategy`, standardised, then negated). The forecast is (x − 10)/2: about 0 at mid-range, rising smoothly to about +5 when the close sits on the low. It is long only in practice. A small negative appears only if the coin's usual close location drifts above 0.5, and it stays under 2 points, so it never trades from flat.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Use the bars in regime `mean_reversion`. Take the slope of the coin's next-day return, minus its mean daily return over all windows, on the forecast. Its t uses standard errors clustered by day, with each coin and window kept separate.
- Supporting check: The mean next-day return on regime bars with forecast ≥ 2, minus the coin's mean daily return, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ −2, or the excess next-day return on forecast ≥ 2 bars is negative with t ≤ −2 (further falls).
- Inconclusive: A slope t between −2 and 2, or a positive slope with a failing check. At least 300 regime bars on at least 200 distinct days are needed; fewer is inconclusive.
