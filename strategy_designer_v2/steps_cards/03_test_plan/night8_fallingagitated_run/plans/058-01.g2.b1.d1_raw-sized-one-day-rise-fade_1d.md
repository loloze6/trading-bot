- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a coin's one-day rise in standard deviations of its past-30-day daily returns -> kept: the 1-day return over the 30-day return std; the profile is added by code; down days give 0.
- Comparison: larger rises against smaller ones; small up days carry almost none -> adapted: graded by rise size, with a squared graded dead zone damping small rises.
- Outcome: price falls over the next 3 days, more after larger rises -> kept: a short held 3 bars, sized by the rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast shorts in proportion to the rise in usual swings, only inside the profile, over the idea's 3-day horizon.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 30. It reads the day's return divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns. The value is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20) and negated. It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so down days and flat days give exactly 0. Up days give a short. signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10 then damps small rises toward 0 and amplifies large ones. The forecast is 0 or negative, and it grows smoothly in size with the rise. Code keeps it flat outside the falling agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, as the workflow measures it. A case is a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points on the short side, read on the 3-bar grid. Each case's unusual move is the coin's 3-bar return after it, minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift, taken in the short direction. The t is computed with each day's cases taken together.
- Supporting check: Compare two groups of cases: those with forecast at or below -10 (large rises) and those between -2 and -10. The mean unusual move of the first group minus that of the second must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2, meaning the price rises further after the rises.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirm with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive, and so is any result on fewer than 30 cases or fewer than 20 distinct days.
