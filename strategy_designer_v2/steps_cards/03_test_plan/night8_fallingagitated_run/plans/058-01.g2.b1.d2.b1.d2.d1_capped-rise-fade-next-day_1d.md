Fidelity:
- Situation: a one-day rise, in a falling agitated market, no larger than about one usual swing -> kept: a regime holds bars with a rise above 0 and at most 1 thirty-day std; the code adds the profile.
- Comparison: the rise against the std of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the one-day return divided by the 30-day std of daily returns.
- Outcome: price falls over the next day, more after larger rises -> kept: a short graded by the scaled rise, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly these up days, sized by the scaled rise, for one day, inside the profile.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). The regime detector reads the same quantity. A bar is in the active regime only when that value is above 0 and at most 1, meaning an up day within one usual swing. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the value is standardised against its own past 60 values and negated. The forecast is therefore short, and its size grows linearly with the rise, from 0 for a flat day to its largest at one usual swing. Code adds the falling agitated profile, and the book is flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a bar with a short bet. For each case, take the coin's next-day return minus the coin's normal daily drift (its mean over all windows), in the short direction. The measure is the mean of this unusual move over the cases, with its t, cases on the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: Over the cases, regress the short-direction unusual move on the size of the forecast. The slope must be positive.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check's slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2: the coin rises more than its drift after these up days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming t with a slope at or below 0 is also inconclusive. Fewer than 30 distinct days with cases is inconclusive whatever the t.
