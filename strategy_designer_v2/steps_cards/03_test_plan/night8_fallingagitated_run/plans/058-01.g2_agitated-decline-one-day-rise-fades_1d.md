Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day rise, measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, in a falling, agitated market -> adapted: the one-day return in 30-day standard deviations (volatility includes the day itself), graded by size and zero on down days; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against its usual 30-day swing -> kept
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 3 days, more after a larger rise -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the short position is sized by the rise in usual moves and held for 3 days, so its gains test exactly the claimed give-back and its growth with size.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 30). It is put on the forecast scale by its own past 60 values (standardise 60/20). It is then clipped at 0, so only up days count, and negated. On an up day the forecast is negative, a short, and its size grows linearly with the rise in usual moves, capped at -20. On a down or flat day it is 0. It never goes long. Code keeps the book flat outside the falling agitated profile.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, in the short direction. A case is a short bet: the forecast falls by at least 2 points, read once per 3-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 3-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift over all windows, with its sign flipped. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together, before costs.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median absolute forecast. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the larger-half minus smaller-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2: prices rise further after up days, against the short.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming t with the larger half not above the smaller half is also inconclusive. At least 30 days with cases are required; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
