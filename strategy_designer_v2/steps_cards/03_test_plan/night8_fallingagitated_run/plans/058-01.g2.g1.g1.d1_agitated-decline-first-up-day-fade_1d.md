Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, the first up day after a down day -> kept: the profile is added by code, and a regime selects bars whose up-close streak is exactly 1 (a flat prior day, rare on daily bars, also counts).
- Comparison: that day's low-to-high range against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: `RangeInReturnStdComponent` with `vol_period` 30, which grades the short.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the wider the range -> kept: short for 1 bar, sized by the range measure.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the next-day move on exactly the idea's days, sized by the idea's own ratio.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the up-close streak length (`UpCloseStreakLengthComponent`, side "up"). On bars where it equals 1 (today closed up, yesterday did not), the bar is in `mean_reversion`; every other bar is flat. That includes the second and later up days of a run. Inside the regime, the forecast is the day's range as a fraction of the close, divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns (`RangeInReturnStdComponent`, `vol_period` 30). It is standardised over its past 60 values (`min_periods` 20) and negated. So it is always short on the idea's days, near -10 for a usual range and down to -20 for a range twice its usual size or more, and 0 elsewhere. The code adds the falling-agitated profile, so the book is also flat outside that profile.
