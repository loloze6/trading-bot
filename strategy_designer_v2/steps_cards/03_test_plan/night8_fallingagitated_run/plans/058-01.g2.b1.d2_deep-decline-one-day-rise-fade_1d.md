Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a one-day rise, sized by that rise and by the coin's 60-day fall -> adapted: the rise becomes a regime condition (rise above 0); inside it the forecast is graded by the rise size plus the fall depth, as a weighted sum (no product component exists); the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the one-day return in 30-day standard deviations.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 3 days -> kept: a short held for 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the short appears only on rise days inside the profile, and it grows with both the scaled rise and the 60-day fall, as the idea states.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where today's close is above yesterday's, measured by the one-day return in 30-day standard deviations. On every other bar it is 0. It is the equal-weight mean of two parts: - the scaled one-day rise, standardised, kept at 0 or above, and negated; - the 60-day return in percent, standardised and kept at 0 or below (the depth of the fall). The forecast is never positive. The short grows as the up day gets sharper against usual swing and as the 60-day decline gets deeper.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over the cases, with its t. A case is a short bet (a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points), read once per 3-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 3-day return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's mean 3-day return over all its windows, taken in the short direction. The t takes same-day cases together, and the move is measured before costs.
- Supporting check: The correlation between |forecast| at the case and the unusual move in the short direction is above 0.
- Confirm: Confirmed when the mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: Rejected when the mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning prices rose after the shorts.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t whose correlation is not above 0. It is also inconclusive with fewer than 30 distinct case days across all coins and windows.
