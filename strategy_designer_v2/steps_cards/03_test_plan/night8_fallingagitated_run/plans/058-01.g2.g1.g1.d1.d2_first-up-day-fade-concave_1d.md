Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, the first up day after a down day -> kept: the profile is added by code, and inside it a regime holds only on bars whose up-close streak is exactly 1.
- Comparison: that day's low-to-high range against the standard deviation of its past-30-day daily returns, the fall growing less than in proportion -> adapted: the range in 30-day return standard deviations sizes the short linearly, flattening at the standardise cap.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day -> kept: a short held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a reliable next-day fall after these days that grows with the range confirms the idea, and a reliable rise after them rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars inside the falling agitated profile where `UpCloseStreakLengthComponent` (side "up") reads 1, meaning today closed up and yesterday did not. On those bars it is minus the standardised `RangeInReturnStdComponent` (vol_period 30). That component is today's (high − low) / close divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. The value is set against its mean absolute size over the prior 60 bars, so a usual range gives −10 (100 % short). A range twice the usual size or more is capped at −20, so the position grows with the range and then stops growing. The forecast is always short or zero. It is 0 on every other bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The unusual next-day move in the short's direction on each case bar: −(close[t+1]/close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean daily return over all its windows). A case bar is one in the regime and the profile with a forecast below 0. Cases on the same day are summed into one value, and a t of the mean is taken across days.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual fall of cases with |forecast| above the cases' median, minus that of cases below it, is above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is below 0 with t ≤ −2, meaning these days are followed by a rise.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails, and so is a result with fewer than 100 case-days.
