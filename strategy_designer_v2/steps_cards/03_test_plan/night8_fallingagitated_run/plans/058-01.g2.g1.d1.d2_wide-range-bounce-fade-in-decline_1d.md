Forecast: Bars enter the active regime only on an up day (1-day return above 0) whose 3-day return, today's close against the close 3 days earlier, is below 0. The falling agitated profile is added by code. On all other bars the forecast is 0. Inside the regime, the forecast is minus the day's range in usual moves: (high − low) / close divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns. This value is standardised against its own past 60 values (about 10 on an average day, capped at 20), negated so the position is short, and passed through signed_power with exponent 2. That step leaves narrow bounces with a small short and gives wide, violent ones a large short. The forecast is never positive, and its size grows continuously with the range ratio.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, in the short direction. A case is a bar with a nonzero forecast. Its unusual move is minus (next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows). Its t is computed with the cases of one day pooled together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases whose |forecast| is above the median of the cases, minus that of the cases below it, is above 0.

- Inconclusive: A main t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with the check failing. At least 30 case-days are needed; with fewer, no verdict.
