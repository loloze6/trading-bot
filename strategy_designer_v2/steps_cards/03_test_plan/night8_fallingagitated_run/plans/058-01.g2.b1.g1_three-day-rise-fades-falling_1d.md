Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a coin's 3-day rise -> kept: the profile is added by code, and the 3-day return is clipped so the book holds only on rises.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the 3-day return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns. The extra 1/sqrt(3) factor is a constant and cancels in standardise.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 3 days, more after larger rises -> kept: the short is graded by the scaled rise and held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly the scaled 3-day rise inside the profile, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 3 and vol_period 30. This is the 3-day return in usual daily moves over the past 30 days. It is standardised (window 60, min_periods 20), negated, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is negative (short) after a 3-day rise and grows in size linearly with the rise against usual swing, about -10 for a typical rise, capped at -20. It is 0 after a flat or falling 3 days. It is never positive. The code keeps the book flat outside the falling agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: Cases are bets (forecast falls of at least 2 points below 0), read on a non-overlapping 3-bar grid per window. For each case, take the coin's 3-day return minus its normal 3-day drift over all windows, in the short direction. The measure is its mean, with a t that takes cases on the same day together. This is judged before costs and against holding the coin.
- Supporting check: Across cases, the least-squares slope of the short-direction unusual move on |forecast| at the case bar is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move in the short direction is above 0 with t >= 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move in the short direction has t <= -2, meaning the coin rose more than its drift after the rises.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a slope at or below 0. Fewer than 30 distinct case days is inconclusive whatever the t.
