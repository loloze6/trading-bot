Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 3-day rise in a falling, agitated market -> kept: the 3-day return, rise side only; the profile is added by code
- Comparison: the rise against the std of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the 3-day return divided by the 30-day daily-return std
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 15 days, giving back a larger share of the rise -> kept: a short graded by the scaled rise, held 15 bars
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the scaled 3-day rise itself, on the idea's 15-day horizon, inside its profile.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 3 and vol_period 30. This is the 3-day return divided by (the std of the last 30 daily returns x sqrt 3); the sqrt 3 is a constant factor. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), then clipped at 0 so only rises count, then negated. The forecast is 0 after a flat or falling 3 days and negative after a rise. The short grows linearly with the rise's size in usual daily moves, up to a cap of -20. The book is flat outside the falling-agitated profile, which code adds.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 15 bars
- Main measure: Use grid bars (positions 0, 15, 30, ... of each window) where the forecast is below 0. On these bars, take the OLS slope of the coin's 15-bar return minus its normal 15-bar drift (all windows) on the forecast. Report the slope's t and the number of cases.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, compute the share given back: minus the 15-bar return divided by the 3-day rise ending at the signal bar. Its mean in the top half of |forecast| minus its mean in the bottom half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2 on at least 30 cases (a larger short is followed by a larger fall beyond drift), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2 on at least 30 cases (larger scaled rises are followed by relatively higher prices).
- Inconclusive: The slope t is between -2 and 2, or the slope confirms but the share check fails, or there are fewer than 30 cases.
