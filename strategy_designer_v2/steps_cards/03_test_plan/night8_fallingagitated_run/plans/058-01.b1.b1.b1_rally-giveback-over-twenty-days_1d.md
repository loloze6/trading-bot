Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 3-day rise, in a falling, agitated market -> kept: the 3-day return, positive part only; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the 3-day return divided by the 30-day return std.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 20 days, more after larger scaled rises -> kept: a short sized by the scaled rise, held 20 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a short that grows with the scaled 3-day rise, inside the profile, either earns the predicted larger 20-day falls or does not.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 3 and vol_period 30. Its raw value is the 3-day return divided by the 30-day std of daily returns. The constant sqrt(3) in the component cancels under standardise. The value is standardised against its own past 60 values, clipped at 0 so only rises count, then negated. The forecast is 0 after a flat or falling 3 days. It is negative (short) after a rise, and its size grows linearly with how many usual swings the rise spans, capped at -20. It is never positive. Code keeps it flat outside the falling-agitated profile.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: Sample the non-overlapping 20-bar grid of each window, using cells with forecast below 0. Take each cell's 20-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 20-bar return over all windows (holding the coin), before costs. Regress it on abs(forecast) and read the slope's t.
- Supporting check: The mean of the same drift-adjusted 20-bar returns over those cases is below 0, with a t of -2 or less (cases of one day summed together).
- Confirm: The slope t is -2 or less, so larger scaled rises are followed by larger excess falls, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope t is +2 or more, or the mean drift-adjusted 20-bar return over the cases has a t of +2 or more (rises followed by further rises).
- Inconclusive: Every result between those, including slopes or means near 0. The test needs at least 30 non-overlapping cases, else it is inconclusive.
