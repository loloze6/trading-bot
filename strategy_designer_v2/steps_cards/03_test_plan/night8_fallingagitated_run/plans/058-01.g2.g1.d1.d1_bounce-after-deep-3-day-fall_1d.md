- Situation: an up day that comes right after a 3-day fall -> adapted: the 3-day fall is the return ending yesterday, measured against the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns; together with today's up close it is the regime condition, and the forecast inside it is graded by depth.

- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on the idea's bars, sized by the scaled depth, so its next-day results bear directly on the claim.
Forecast: The regime detector selects a bar only when two conditions hold. First, the day closed up: the 1-day return is above 0. Second, the 3-day return ending yesterday is negative, measured in units of the 30-day return standard deviation (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 3, vol_period 30, lag 1). On every other bar the book is flat. Code adds the falling-agitated profile on top. On selected bars the forecast is that same scaled 3-day return, standardised over 60 bars. It is negative there, so the book is short. The short grows in proportion to the depth of the fall, and a clip at max 0 keeps it from ever going long.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: For each case (a bar with forecast < 0), take the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, and flip the sign so a fall counts as a gain. Take the mean over all cases and its t, with cases on the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median absolute forecast. The deeper half must show a larger mean unusual next-day fall than the shallower half (deeper minus shallower above 0).
- Confirm: The main measure's t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's t is -2 or less, meaning the coin rises more than usual the day after these bounces.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a t between -2 and 2, or a main t of 2 or more with the check failing. A verdict needs at least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days.
