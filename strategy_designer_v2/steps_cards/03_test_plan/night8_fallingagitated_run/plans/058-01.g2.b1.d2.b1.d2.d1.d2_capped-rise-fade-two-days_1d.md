Forecast: The forecast uses the day's return divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns (`VolatilityScaledReturnComponent`, period 1, vol_period 30). A regime rule keeps only bars where this value is above 0 and at most 1. Every other bar is flat, and code adds the falling-agitated profile. Inside the regime, the value is standardised against its own past 60 values and negated. The forecast is therefore always short. It is near 0 for a tiny rise and grows in size with the rise, to about -12 at one usual swing.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, read on a 2-bar grid. A case is a short bet: the forecast rising at least 2 points on the short side. Each case's move is the coin's 2-bar return after the signal minus the coin's normal 2-bar drift over all windows, taken in the short direction. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The cases are split at the median of |forecast|. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ -2, so rises continue. Or the upper-minus-lower difference has a Welch t ≤ -2, so the give-back shrinks with the rise.
- Inconclusive: Anything else, including t between -2 and 2, or a positive main measure with a non-positive check. Fewer than 30 distinct days with cases is inconclusive.
