- Outcome: the price falls over the next 3 days -> kept: a short held for 3 daily bars.

Forecast: The indicator is VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 30. It is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. It is standardised against its past 60 values, then clipped at 0 from below, so only up days count, then negated. On a down or flat day the forecast is 0. On an up day it is negative (short), and its size grows linearly with the day's rise in usual moves, up to the cap of -20. The book holds nothing outside the falling agitated profile; the profile is added by code.

- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window and keep the grid bars with a forecast below 0. On those bars, regress the coin's 3-day forward return, minus the coin's mean 3-day return over all its windows, on abs(forecast). Report the slope's t, with the number of cases and the number of distinct days they fall on.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean excess 3-day return must be below 0 (the shorts gain against holding the coin).
- Confirm: The slope's t is -2 or less and the supporting check passes: larger rises are followed by larger falls.
- Reject: The slope's t is +2 or more (larger rises are followed by further rises), or the mean excess 3-day return over the cases is above 0 with a t of +2 or more.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive, and so is a slope t of -2 or less with a mean excess return at or above 0 but a t below +2. Fewer than 30 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, are also inconclusive.
