Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day rise, in a falling, agitated market -> kept: the day's return, kept only when positive; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the return divided by the 30-day std of daily returns.
- Outcome: the larger the scaled rise, the larger the fall over the next 5 days -> kept: a short sized by the scaled rise, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled rise, short-side, graded, inside the profile, over its 5-day horizon.
Forecast: VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 1, vol_period 30) gives the day's return in 30-day standard deviations of daily returns. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), clipped at 0 to keep up days only, then negated. The forecast is 0 on down or flat days. It is negative on up days, and the short grows linearly with the size of the rise in usual moves, capped at -20. It never goes above 0. The profile code keeps it flat outside falling, agitated markets.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases: each short bet's 5-bar coin return minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift (holding the coin), in the short direction. It is read once per 5-bar grid cell, and its t takes the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The cases are split at the median of |forecast| on the bet's bar. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2: after a scaled rise the coin rose more than its drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirm-strength main measure whose supporting check is not positive. Fewer than 30 distinct case days is inconclusive whatever the t.
