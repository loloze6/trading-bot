Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a coin's 3-day rise scaled by its 30-day daily-return std -> kept: the profile is added by code, and inside it the scaled 3-day rise is the signal, active on rises only
- Comparison: larger scaled rises against smaller ones -> adapted: graded, with a smooth dead zone so small rallies get near-zero size
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 5 days, more after larger rises -> kept: short, held 5 bars
- Verdict transfer: yes: a short sized by the scaled 3-day rise inside the profile, judged on 5-day moves against size, is the idea's claim in both directions.
Forecast: The signal is VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 3 and vol_period 30: the 3-day return divided by the 30-day std of daily returns (times sqrt 3, a constant). It is standardised over its past 60 values. It is clipped at 0 so that only rises count, then damped with signed_power (exponent 2, pivot 10) so small rallies give almost nothing, then negated. The forecast is 0 after a 3-day fall or a flat stretch. After a rise it is negative (short), and its size grows with the square of the scaled rise. It never goes long. Code adds the falling-agitated profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On each window's non-overlapping 5-bar grid, take the cases with forecast < 0. On those cases, fit the slope of the coin's 5-bar forward return minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift (all windows) against the forecast, before costs. The t is clustered by day.
- Supporting check: The mean 5-bar excess return over the same cases, in the short direction (minus the excess return), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 (a more negative forecast is followed by a lower excess return), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2 (larger scaled rallies are followed by relatively higher returns).
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope with t ≥ 2 whose mean short-direction excess return is not above 0. It is also inconclusive with fewer than 30 distinct days of cases.
