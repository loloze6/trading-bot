Forecast: The regime detector reads the day's close-to-close move (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 1). When that move is above 0 (an up day), the bar is `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. The market profile (falling agitated) is added by code. On up days the forecast is the day's range, (high − low) / close, divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (RangeInReturnStdComponent, vol_period 30). That value is standardised over its 60 past values (min_periods 20) and negated. The forecast is therefore always short on up days, about −10 on an up day of usual width, nearer 0 on narrow days, and up to −20 on very wide days. It is 0 on down days, on flat days and outside the profile.
