Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a coin's rise over the past 3 days, measured in usual 3-day swings -> kept (the 3-day return over the 30-day daily std x sqrt(3); the profile is added by code)
- Comparison: the rise against the usual 3-day swing (30-day std of daily returns scaled to 3 days) -> kept
- Outcome: the larger that rise, the larger the fall over the next 3 days -> kept (a short graded by the rise, held 3 bars)
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled 3-day rise, shorted only inside the named profile and held for its 3-day horizon.
Forecast: One component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 3 and vol_period 30. It computes the 3-day return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns times sqrt(3), so it reads the rise in usual 3-day swings. It is standardised over its own past 60 values (min 20), negated and clipped at max 0. The forecast is therefore 0 after a flat or falling 3 days. It is negative (short) after a 3-day rise, and grows continuously more negative as the rise gets larger in usual swings, down to the cap of -20. The falling-agitated profile is added by code, so the book is flat outside that profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bars on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window where the forecast is below 0. For each case, take the coin's 3-day return from that close minus the coin's normal 3-day drift (its mean over all windows), times -1 (the short's direction). Report the mean of this unusual move and its t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean unusual move of the larger-half cases minus that of the smaller-half cases must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the larger half's mean exceeds the smaller half's.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2, so 3-day rises in this profile continue.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t >= 2 whose size check fails is also inconclusive. So is a result from fewer than 30 cases on distinct days.
