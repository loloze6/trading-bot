- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, the first up day after a down day -> kept: a regime on an up-close streak of exactly 1 bar (a flat prior day also counts, which is negligible on daily bars), inside the profile.
- Comparison: the close's distance below the day's high against the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns -> kept: (close − high) / close / s30, graded.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the further the close sits below the high -> kept: a short over 1 bar, sized by that distance.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same day, the same graded distance and the same next-day fall.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless today closed up and yesterday did not (an up streak of exactly 1). On those days it is short, from `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` (period 1, source high, current bar included, base close, vol_period 30). That value is (close − high) / close / s30, which is never positive. It is standardised over its past 60 values and keeps its negative sign. It is 0 when the day closed at its high, and it grows more negative (a larger short) the further the close sits below the high in usual daily moves, capped at −20. The code limits the book to the falling agitated profile.
