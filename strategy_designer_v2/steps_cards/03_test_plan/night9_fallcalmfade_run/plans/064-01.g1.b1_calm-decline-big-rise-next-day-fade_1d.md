- Situation: a one-day rise, large against the 20-day daily swing, beyond about one usual daily move, in a falling calm market -> adapted: a regime holds only bars whose rise is at least 1 swing unit (20-day std); inside it the size is graded by that rise; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise in units of the 20-day daily swing (std of daily returns) -> kept
- Outcome: the rise is given back over the next day, more for larger rises -> kept: short for 1 bar, sized by the rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test fades exactly the large scaled up days for one day, graded by their size, and nothing else.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 20). When that value is 1.0 or more, the bar is `mean_reversion`; every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime, the same scaled return is standardised over its past 60 values and negated. The forecast is then short and grows with the rise: a rise of about 1 swing gives roughly -12, and 1.5 swings or more reach the -20 cap. The forecast is never positive. It is zero on ordinary days, on down days and on small up days. The falling-calm profile is added by code.

- Main measure: Cases are the bars where a short bet is opened (the forecast falls by at least 2 points). For each case, take the coin's next-day return minus its normal daily drift over all windows, in the short direction. Report the mean and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of their scaled rise (`last_history_value`). The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.

- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ -2, meaning prices rose further than their drift after the large scaled up days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2. A confirming t whose supporting check fails is also inconclusive. Fewer than 30 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, decide nothing.
