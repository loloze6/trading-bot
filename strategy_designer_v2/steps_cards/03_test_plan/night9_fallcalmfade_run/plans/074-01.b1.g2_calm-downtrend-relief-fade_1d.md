Fidelity:
- Situation: the first up day after a run of down days, in a falling calm market -> kept: a regime rule selects a bar that closes up after at least 2 down closes; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the run's fall against the coin's usual daily swing over the last 30 days -> kept: the run's loss divided by the std of the 30 daily returns before the up day.
- Outcome: the coin's close falls over the next day, more after deeper runs -> kept: a 1-bar short, sized by the scaled depth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly these up days, sized by the idea's own depth measure, over the idea's one-day horizon.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent`. A bar is active (mean_reversion) only when it closes up after a run of 2 or more down closes. Every other bar is flat, and the profile code also keeps the book flat outside falling calm. On active bars the forecast reads `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent`: the run's total fall divided by the std of the 30 daily returns before the up day. It is then standardised (window 60, min_periods 20) and negated. The forecast is therefore always negative or zero (short only), and its size grows with the run's depth in usual daily swings. The component is 0 on most bars, so its past mean is diluted. Typical and deep runs reach the -20 cap, and shallower runs get smaller shorts.
