Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, the first up day after a run of down days -> adapted: "after the run" made "while": the regime is the up-day bar that breaks the run, and the falling profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the run's return over its down days against the coin's usual daily swing over the last 30 days -> kept: the run's loss divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns.
- Outcome: the close falls over the next day, more than in proportion to the depth -> kept: the 1-bar short grows convexly (cubed) with the depth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on run-breaking up days, sized convexly by depth in usual swings, so its next-day result answers the idea both ways.
Forecast: The regime is the bar that closes up and ends a run of down closes. On that bar the forecast reads the run's total loss: `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent` with `base` "std", `window` 30 and `exclude_current` 1. That is the loss in standard deviations of the 30 daily returns before the up day. On every other bar the forecast is 0. The depth is standardised over its last 60 stored values. The target is 2.5 because about three bars in four store 0, so an ordinary event lands near 10 and is not stuck at the cap. The value is then cubed with pivot 20 (`signed_power` exponent 3), so an ordinary event gives about -2.5 and only the deepest reach -20. It is then negated (short) and clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is never positive. It is zero outside the event and outside the falling profile, and its size grows like the cube of the run's depth.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The coin's next-day return after each signal bar (forecast below 0), minus its normal daily drift (all windows), in the short direction. Averaged weighted by abs(forecast), with t taken over days, cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases by raw depth (`last_history_value`) into the deepest third and the other two thirds. The deepest third's mean short-side excess fall per unit of mean depth must exceed the rest's.
- Confirm: The weighted mean short-side excess fall is above 0 with t ≥ 2 over at least 30 case-days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The weighted mean short-side excess fall is below 0 with t ≤ -2 over at least 30 case-days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a failed supporting check, is inconclusive. Fewer than 30 case-days is also inconclusive (minimum 30 case-days).
