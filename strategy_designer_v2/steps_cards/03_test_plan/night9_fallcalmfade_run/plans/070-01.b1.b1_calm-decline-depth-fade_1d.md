Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling calm market, a day that closes up after an unbroken run of down days -> adapted: the profile is added by code; the up close that ends a run of at least two down closes is a regime, and inside it the position is graded by the run's total fall.
- Comparison: deeper total fall of the run vs shallower -> kept: the size of the short grows with the run's loss in percent.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day, more after deeper runs -> kept: the horizon is 1 bar on daily bars, short only.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the next-day move after the same up-close bars, sized by the same run loss, so a confirm confirms the idea and a reject rejects it.
Forecast: The regime is active only on a bar that closes up right after at least two consecutive lower closes. This is a rule on `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (count ≥ 2). On every other bar the book is flat. The profile vetoes, added by code, also keep it flat outside falling calm markets. Inside the regime, the component is `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent` (side "down", base "percent"). Its value is the percent the broken down run took off the price. It is standardised over its past 60 values, then negated, so the forecast is never positive: a short that grows with the depth of the run. The standardise target is 2.5 instead of 10, for this reason. The component is non-zero only on the roughly one bar in four that breaks a down run (coin-flip odds of a down close followed by an up close), so its past mean absolute value is about a quarter of a typical break. With target 2.5, an average break reads about -10, deeper ones up to the -20 cap, and shallow ones near 0. With the default target of 10, almost every break would sit at the cap, which would be on/off.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On case bars (the forecast falls by at least 2 points on the short side), take the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, sign flipped so that a fall is positive. Average it over the cases. Its t treats all cases on the same day as one.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean short-side unusual move of the deeper half minus that of the shallower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is positive with t ≥ 2, and the deeper-minus-shallower difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning the break days are followed by rises beyond drift. Or the deeper-minus-shallower difference is negative with Welch t ≤ -2, meaning deeper runs give back less.
- Inconclusive: Any result with -2 < t < 2 on the main measure, or a main confirm with a deeper-minus-shallower difference at or below 0 but with t above -2. At least 30 distinct case days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
