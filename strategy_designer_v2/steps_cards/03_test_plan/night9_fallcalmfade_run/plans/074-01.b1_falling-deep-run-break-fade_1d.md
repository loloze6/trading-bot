- Comparison: the run's fall in usual daily swings over the last 30 days -> kept: the run's loss divided by the 30-day std of daily returns, setting the size of the position.
- Outcome: the close falls over the next day, more after deeper runs -> kept: a short held for 1 bar, larger the deeper the run.

Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (max_bars 30). When it is 1 or more, the bar is the first up close after a down run, and the bar goes to `mean_reversion`. Every other bar goes to `unknown`, which is null and flat. The falling profile is added by code. Inside the regime, the forecast comes from `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent` (base "std", window 30). This is the run's total loss in 30-day daily standard deviations. It is standardised over its past 60 stored values and then negated. Its history is 0 on non-bounce days, about three bars in four, so the standardise target is set to 2.5 rather than 10. This puts a typical case near -10 instead of at the -20 cap. The forecast is never positive. It is near 0 after a shallow drift down and grows more negative with the depth of the run.
