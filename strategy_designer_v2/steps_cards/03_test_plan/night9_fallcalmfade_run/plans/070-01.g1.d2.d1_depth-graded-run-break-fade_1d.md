Fidelity:
- Situation: an up day that ends a run of down days -> kept: a regime rule on the up close that breaks a run of at least 2 down closes.
- Comparison: fall larger the more the price dropped over the run, not by its length -> kept: inside the regime the short is graded by the run's percent loss.
- Outcome: price falls over the next day -> kept: short, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the event, its grading by run depth and the next-day fall are all measured as stated.
Forecast: The forecast is non-zero only on a bar that closes up right after at least 2 consecutive down closes. This is the regime `mean_reversion`, chosen by a rule on `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (count >= 2). Every other bar is flat. On those event bars the forecast is short, sized by `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent` (`side` "down", `base` "percent", `max_bars` 30): the percent the price lost over the run that the up day ends. That loss is standardised over its own 60 past stored values (min 20), negated, and clipped at 0. A deeper run gives a larger short, up to the cap of 20, and the forecast is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On event bars (forecast < 0), the next-day return close[t+1]/close[t]-1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, taken in the short direction. Its mean and t, with cases on the same day summed per day.
- Supporting check: Split the event bars at the median of |forecast|. The deeper half's mean short-direction excess return minus the shallower half's must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is above 0 with t >= 2, and the deeper-minus-shallower difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure has t <= -2 (rises follow run breaks). Or the deeper-minus-shallower Welch t is <= -2 (deeper runs give back less).
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a main t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure with a difference at or below 0 but Welch t above -2. At least 100 event bars over at least 80 distinct days are required, else inconclusive.
