Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, the first up day after a run of consecutive down days -> kept: a regime rule fires only on the up close that breaks a down run; the code adds the "falling" profile.
- Comparison: the run's loss over its down days against the usual daily move of the last 30 days -> kept: the run loss in mean absolute daily moves over 30 days.
- Outcome: the close falls over the next day, faster than the run's depth grows, mostly after the deepest runs -> adapted: a one-day short sized convexly (squared) by depth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on run-break days in falling markets, sized convexly by run depth, and held one day.
Forecast: On every bar the regime detector reads the loss of the down run that today's up close ends, measured in usual daily moves (mean absolute daily return over the 30 days ending yesterday). This is `PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent`, `side` "down", `base` "usual_moves", `window` 30, `exclude_current` 1. When that loss is above 0, the bar is a run break and goes to `mean_reversion`. Every other bar goes to `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime the same reading is processed in four steps: - It is standardised against its past 60 values. The target is 2.5 because about one day in four is a break and the other days read 0. With that target, a break of average depth is near 10, capped at 20. - `signed_power` with exponent 2 and pivot 20 makes the size convex. An average break gives about 5 (allocation 0.5), a break twice as deep gives 20, and shallow breaks stay near 0. - `negate` turns it into a short. - A `clip` at a maximum of 0 keeps it short only. The forecast is 0 on every non-break bar and never positive. Its size grows with the square of the run's depth. The code adds the falling profile and holds each bet for 1 bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Per case (a short bet, the forecast falling by at least 2), the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, with the sign flipped to the short side. The measure is its mean and its t, with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual short gain of the cases in the top third by |forecast| exceeds that of the bottom third (top minus bottom above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual short gain is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual short gain is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check, is inconclusive. Fewer than 30 distinct case days is also inconclusive.
