Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, the first up day after a run of consecutive down days -> kept: the profile keeps the book flat outside falling markets, and a regime rule holds only on the up close that ends a run of at least 2 down closes.
- Comparison: the 5-day fall, against the usual daily swing over the last 30 days -> kept: the 5-day return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns.
- Outcome: the close falls over the next day, more after a deeper 5-day fall -> kept: a short position, graded by the depth of the fall and held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly the stated up days, sized by the stated depth, over the stated next day.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (`max_bars` 30, `form` "count"). On an up close that ends a run of 2 or more down closes, the bar is `mean_reversion`; on every other bar it is `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime the forecast comes from `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` (`period` 5, `vol_period` 30): the 5-day return in usual daily moves, negative after a fall. It is standardised over 60 bars, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore short only: 0 when the coin did not fall over the 5 days, and more short the deeper the fall in usual moves, capped at -20. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a bar with forecast below -2. For each case, take the coin's next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, signed for the short side. The measure is the mean of this unusual move, with its t taken with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean short-side unusual move of the top half minus that of the bottom half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean short-side unusual move is above 0 with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-side unusual move has a t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is a confirming t whose supporting check fails. The test also needs at least 50 cases on at least 50 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
