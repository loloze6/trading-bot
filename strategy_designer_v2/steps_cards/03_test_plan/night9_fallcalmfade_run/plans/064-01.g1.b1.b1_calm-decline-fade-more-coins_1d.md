Fidelity:
- Situation: a one-day rise, large against its 20-day daily swing, in a falling calm market -> adapted: the regime is a rise of at least one 20-day return std, the profile is added by code, and inside the regime the size is graded by the rise.
- Comparison: the rise in 20-day swing units, beyond about one usual daily move -> kept: the day's return over the std of the last 20 daily returns, with the regime threshold at 1.
- Outcome: the rise is given back over the next day, more for larger rises -> kept: a short held for 1 bar, sized by the scaled rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the same days, the same swing units, the same side and the same one-day horizon are tested.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 1, vol_period 20), which is the day's return divided by the std of the last 20 daily returns. The regime detector sends a bar to `trending` only when this value is at least 1.0, a rise of one usual daily move or more; every other bar is flat. In `trending` the same component is standardised (window 60, min_periods 20) and negated. The forecast is therefore negative (short) only on those large up days, zero on every other bar, and its size grows with the rise in swing units until the ±20 cap. The book holds nothing outside falling calm, which the code adds.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: For each case (a short bet of the forecast), take the coin's next-day return minus its normal drift (its mean daily return over all windows), in the short direction. Measure its mean and its t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at their median |forecast|. The mean short-direction unusual move of the top half minus that of the bottom half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when the main measure's mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning the large rises continue. It is also rejected when the main measure confirms but the top-minus-bottom difference has a Welch t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t of the main measure between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A main t ≥ 2 with a check difference at or below 0 but a Welch t above -2 is also inconclusive. Fewer than 50 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, is inconclusive whatever the result.
