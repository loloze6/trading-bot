Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, the first up day after a run of consecutive down days -> kept: a regime on the down-run count of at least 2 broken by the up close; the "falling" profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the run's fall and the 5-day fall, each against the usual daily swing over 30 days -> adapted: both are scaled by the 30-day std of daily returns and combined as a graded weighted mean, so the short grows with each.
- Outcome: the close falls over the next day -> kept: short held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly the run-ending up days in falling markets, sized by both falls, and judges the next day's move.
Forecast: The forecast is active only on a bar that closes up after at least 2 consecutive down closes; the code also requires the falling profile. On every other bar it is 0. On an active bar it is the equal-weight mean of two parts. The first part is the broken run's total fall in 30-day standard deviations of daily returns (`PriorDownRunLossOnUpCloseComponent`, base "std", window 30), standardised and negated. The second part is the 5-day return in 30-day daily standard deviations (`VolatilityScaledReturnComponent`, period 5, vol_period 30), standardised, with its rises clipped to 0. Both parts are 0 or negative, so the forecast is short only. It is more negative the deeper the run's fall and the deeper the 5-day fall. Each bet is held 1 bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars with forecast < 0, the next bar's return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, taken in the short direction (negated). Its mean and t, with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: The same short-direction excess return is larger on the cases in the top half of |forecast| than on those in the bottom half (difference top minus bottom > 0).
- Confirm: The mean short-direction excess return is > 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-direction excess return has t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with the check failing. Fewer than 30 independent case-days is inconclusive whatever the t.
