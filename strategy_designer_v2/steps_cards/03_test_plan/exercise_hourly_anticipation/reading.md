# The hourly anticipation

For one night, at the operator's request, every step 2 idea was also given an hourly test plan and, when testable, an hourly backtest, "in anticipation". That gave two plans per idea.

The rule now: **one idea, one plan.** The bar size comes from the brief, or is chosen with the idea, or is a default. It is decided before the test, not by running every idea twice. So the hourly plans and backtests were moved out of the production folders, and are kept here as a record:
- plans: `03_test_plan/exercise_hourly_anticipation/plans/`;
- backtests: `04_backtest/exercise_hourly_anticipation/backtests/`.

The backtests' `run.md` files point to the plans' old place (`03_test_plan/plans/`); the plans are now in this exercise folder. They are not inputs to the later steps.
