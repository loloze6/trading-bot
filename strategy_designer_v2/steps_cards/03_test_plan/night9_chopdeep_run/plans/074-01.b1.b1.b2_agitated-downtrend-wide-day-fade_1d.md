- Situation: an up day whose range is wide against its 20-day average range, in a falling agitated market -> adapted: the up day with a range above its 20-day mean is a regime, graded inside it by the width ratio; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: the mean high-low range of the last 20 bars, which includes the day itself.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day -> kept: a short held for 1 bar, sized by width.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets exactly on the next-day fall after wide up days, sized by width, so its result transfers both ways.
Forecast: The regime detector picks `mean_reversion` only on bars where the day's close is above the prior close and the day's high-low range is more than 1.0 times the mean bar range of the last 20 bars. Every other bar is flat, and so is every bar outside "falling agitated". Inside the regime, the width ratio (RangeCompressionRatio, short 1, long 20, mean_bar_range, width) is standardised over its past 60 values, so a typical ratio gives about 10. It is then negated, and averaged at equal weight with a standardised constant of +10. The forecast is (10 − x)/2, where x is the standardised width. It is near 0 for a day just above its usual width and grows more negative (short) as the day widens, down to about −5 at twice the usual size. Small positive values below the 2-point band do not open a position.
