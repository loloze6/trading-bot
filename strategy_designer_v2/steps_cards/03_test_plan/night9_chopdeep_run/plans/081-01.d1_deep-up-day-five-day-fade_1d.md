Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closes up while sitting below the coin's 20-day high, in usual daily swings -> kept: the up close is a regime and the depth sets the graded size.
- Comparison: deeper below the high versus shallower -> kept: the short grows with the depth in 20-day swing units.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next five days -> kept: short, held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same up days, the same depth unit and the same five-day fall.
Forecast: A position exists only on days that close up, where the 1-day return is above 0. That rule sends the bar to `mean_reversion`; every other bar is flat. On those days the forecast is the close's distance below its 20-day closing high, bar t included. The distance is divided by the high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, then standardised over 60 bars with min_periods 20. The raw value is never positive, so the forecast is short only. It is 0 on an up day that closes at the 20-day high. The short grows linearly with the depth in usual swings and is capped at -20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the short bets, read once per non-overlapping 5-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 5-day drift over all windows, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of this unusual move, with its t computed with each day's cases taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of the absolute forecast. The deeper half's mean unusual move minus the shallower half's must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning up days deep below the high rise more than the coin's drift over the next 5 days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t with a failed supporting check. So is fewer than 100 cases, or fewer than 50 distinct days.
