- Situation: in a choppy market, a day that closed up, with the close below the 20-day high -> adapted: the up day is a regime (one-day return above 0) and the code adds the choppy profile; inside it, the depth is graded.
- Comparison: depth in 20-day daily-return standard deviations, not against its own recent depths -> kept: the raw value is the distance from the 20-day high divided by the 20-day return std, and `standardise` only sets its scale.
- Outcome: a fall over the next 10 days that grows with depth -> kept: the short grows with depth and each bet is held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on up days in choppy markets, sized by the swings below the high, so its 10-day results confirm or reject the idea as stated.
Forecast: On every bar outside the choppy profile, the forecast is 0; step 3's code adds that profile. On bars that did not close up, the forecast is also 0. On up days it is short, sized by `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent`. The settings are `period` 20, `source` "high", `exclude_current` 0, `base` "level" and `vol_period` 20. That value is (close − 20-day highest high) / that high / std of the last 20 daily returns. It is 0 at the high and grows more negative with each further swing below it. The value is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20) and clipped at max 0. The forecast is therefore never positive. It is near 0 just under the high and reaches about −10 at a usual depth, down to −20 at about twice that depth. The size is linear in the number of swings below the high.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, in the short direction. A case is a short bet: a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-day return after the signal bar, minus the coin's mean 10-day return over all windows, with the sign flipped for the short. Its t clusters same-day cases together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast| at entry. The mean unusual move of the deeper half minus that of the shallower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move must be above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the deeper half must beat the shallower half.
- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ −2: after deep up-day closes in choppy markets, prices rise more than the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with the deeper half not beating the shallower half is also inconclusive. So is a test with fewer than 30 distinct days of cases.
