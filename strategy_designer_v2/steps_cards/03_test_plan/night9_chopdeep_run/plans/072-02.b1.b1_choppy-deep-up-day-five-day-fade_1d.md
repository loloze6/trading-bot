Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closed up, with the close far below the 20-day high in 20-day daily-return std units -> kept: the up day is a regime rule, and the depth is graded inside it.
- Comparison: deeper closes against shallower ones (near the high) -> kept: the short position grows with the depth.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next five days -> kept: short, held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config shorts only on up days, sized by the idea's own depth measure, over the idea's own horizon.
Forecast: On a day that closed up (1-day return above 0), the forecast is the close's distance below the 20-day high: (close − highest high of the last 20 days) / that high / the std of the last 20 daily returns. This value is never positive. It is standardised (window 60, min_periods 20), so it stays negative. It is near 0 when the close sits at the high and grows more negative, so more short, the deeper the close sits below the high. On down or flat days the forecast is 0 (flat). It never goes long. The choppy profile is added by code.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, on grid bars with a nonzero forecast. Take the coin's 5-day return minus its normal 5-day drift (its mean over all windows). Regress it on the forecast, pooled over all coins, and take the slope's t with cases clustered by day. A deeper (more negative) forecast should be followed by a lower return, so the slope should be positive.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual 5-day move over the cases, taken in the short direction, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope t is 2 or more, with at least 30 cases, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope t is −2 or less, meaning deeper up-day closes are followed by larger rises, with at least 30 cases.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between −2 and 2. A confirming slope whose supporting check fails is also inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 cases on the grid.
