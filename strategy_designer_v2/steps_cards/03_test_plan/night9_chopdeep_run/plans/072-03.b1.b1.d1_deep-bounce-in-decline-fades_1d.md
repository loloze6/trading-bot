- Situation: a choppy-market day that closed up while the 20-day return is still negative -> kept: both conditions are a regime rule, and the code adds the choppy profile.
- Comparison: the close's distance below the 20-day high, in units of the 20-day daily-return standard deviation -> kept: measured by a volatility-scaled distance from the high, and the position is graded by it.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 10 days, and falls more the deeper the close sits -> kept: a short held for 10 bars, larger when the close sits deeper.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own condition, its depth measure and its 10-day fall.
Forecast: The forecast is non-zero only on bars where both conditions hold: today's close is above yesterday's (1-bar return above 0), and the 20-bar return is below 0. The code also keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile. On those bars the forecast is the close's distance below its highest close of the last 20 bars, bar t included. That distance is divided by the close level and by the 20-bar standard deviation of daily returns, so it is negative or zero. It is then standardised over its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). The forecast is therefore 0 or negative (short only): 0 at the 20-day high, and a larger short the deeper the close sits in usual daily moves. On all other bars it is 0.
