Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, an up day with a range wide against the coin's 20-day average daily range -> kept: the profile code keeps the falling market; a regime requires the close above yesterday's and the day's range at least 1.5x the 20-day mean range (the convention for a wide-range bar), with the mean including the day itself.
- Comparison: the closer the close sits to the day's high, the larger the fall; per the brief, the wider the day, the larger the fall -> kept: the short is graded by close location in the range and by the range multiple.
- Outcome: price falls over the next day -> kept: short, horizon 1 daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a graded short held one day after exactly these days tests whether they fall next day, and fall more the nearer the close is to the high.
Forecast: The forecast is 0 except on bars where two conditions hold: the day closed above the prior close (1-day PriceEvolution > 0), and the day's high-low range is at least 1.5x its 20-day mean bar range (RangeCompressionRatio, short 1, long 20, base mean_bar_range, form width). On those bars the forecast is short. It is the negated equal-weight mean of two standardised measures (window 60, min_periods 20). The first is the range multiple, about +15 or more on these bars and growing with width. The second is the close location from the low, (close - low) / (high - low): about +10 at an average location and up to +20 at the high. So the forecast runs from about -8 (a barely wide day closing near its low) to -20 (a very wide day closing at its high). It is never positive.
