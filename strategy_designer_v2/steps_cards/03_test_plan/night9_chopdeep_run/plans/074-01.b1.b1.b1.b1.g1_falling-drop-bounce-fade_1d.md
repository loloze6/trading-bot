- Situation: in a falling market, a wide up day after a sharp fall over the previous 5 days -> adapted: falling is the profile (added by code); "up day", "range wider than its 20-day mean" and "prior 5-day fall of at least one usual 5-day move" are the regime; inside it the position is graded by the depth of that fall.
- Comparison: the deeper the previous 5-day fall, the larger the next day's fall -> kept: the short size grows with the 5-day fall (close[t-1] against close[t-6]), measured in usual moves.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day -> kept: a short held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on wide up days after a sharp 5-day fall, sized by that fall's depth, so a confirm or a reject of its next-day result is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where three things hold. The day closed up (1-day return above 0). Its high-low range is above the mean daily range of the last 20 days (range ratio above 1). The 5-day return ending the day before (close[t-1] against close[t-6]) is at most minus one usual 5-day move, the usual move being the 20-day return standard deviation times the square root of 5. On every other bar the forecast is 0, and the falling-market profile keeps the book flat outside falling markets. Inside that situation, the forecast is the lagged 5-day volatility-scaled return, standardised over its past 60 values and clipped at 0 from above. It is therefore always negative (short), and its size grows with the depth of the prior 5-day fall, up to the cap of -20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The unusual move on the cases, in the short direction. A case is a bar where the forecast turns negative by at least the band. The unusual move is the coin's return from that bar's close to the next close, minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, taken in the short direction. Its t is computed with the cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median forecast size. The mean unusual move of the larger-size half minus that of the smaller-size half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2 (the next day falls more than the coin's drift), on at least 30 case-days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2 (the next day rises more than the coin's drift), on at least 30 case-days.
- Inconclusive: Any of the following: - the t is between -2 and 2; - t ≥ 2 but the supporting check fails; - fewer than 30 case-days.
