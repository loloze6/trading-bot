Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closed up, in a choppy market -> kept: the up close is a regime rule (one-bar return above 0); code adds the choppy profile.
- Comparison: close below the 20-day high, in 20-day daily-return std units -> kept: `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` (20-bar high, 20-bar return std) sets the position size.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 10 days the deeper the close -> kept: the short is graded by depth and held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same up-day depth and the same 10-day fall the idea states, short only as the brief requires.
Forecast: On bars where the day closed above the prior close (inside the choppy profile), the forecast is the close's distance below the highest high of the last 20 bars (current bar included). That distance is divided by the high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, then standardised over its past 60 values. The forecast is never positive: it is 0 at the high and grows more negative (a larger short) the deeper the close sits, capped at -20. A clip at 0 keeps it short only. On down or flat days the forecast is 0, and each bet is still held for its 10 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual move over 10 bars after each case, in the short direction. A case is a short bet: a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return minus its normal 10-bar drift over all windows. Its t uses cases taken together per day.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast| at the case. The deeper half's mean unusual move (short direction) minus the shallower half's must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is positive with a t of 2 or more (the coin falls more than its drift), and the deeper-minus-shallower difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is negative with a t of -2 or less: the coin rises more than its drift after deep up-day closes.
- Inconclusive: Any main-measure t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t whose deeper-minus-shallower difference is 0 or below. A test with fewer than 50 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, is inconclusive whatever its t.
