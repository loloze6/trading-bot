Fidelity:
- Situation: close below its 20-day high, in 20-day daily-return std units, in a choppy market -> kept
- Comparison: deeper closes against shallower ones (the further, the larger) -> kept
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next five days, more the deeper the close -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own depth measure, short and growing with depth, held for its five days.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator: VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent with period 20, vol_period 20, source close, high including today (exclude_current 0) and base level. It measures how far today's close sits below the highest close of the last 20 days, divided by that high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The value is 0 at a new 20-day high and more negative the deeper the close sits below the high. It is standardised over its past 60 values, which keeps the sign (about -10 at a usual depth, down to -20 when twice as deep). It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so the book is short only. The short grows with the depth and is flat at a fresh high. The choppy profile is added by code.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, take the bars with a forecast below 0. On them, regress the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its normal 5-bar drift (all windows together) on the forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: On grid bars with forecast at or below -10 (deeper than usual), the mean drift-adjusted 5-bar return is below 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is +2 or more (more negative forecast, lower excess return) and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less (deeper closes followed by better excess returns).
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming slope whose check fails. At least 100 grid cases with a forecast below 0 are required, else the test is inconclusive.
