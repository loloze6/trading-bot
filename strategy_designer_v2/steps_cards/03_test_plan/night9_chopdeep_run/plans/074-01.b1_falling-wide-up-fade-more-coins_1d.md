Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, an up day whose range is wide against the 20-day average range -> adapted: the falling market comes from the profile, and the up day with a range above its 20-day average is a regime condition, graded inside by the range multiple.
- Comparison: the coin's average daily high-to-low range over the past 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the wider the range -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the short forecast exists only on falling-market wide up days, grows with the range multiple, and is held one day, which is exactly the claim.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars inside the falling profile, where the day closed up and the day's range is above its 20-day mean bar range. The 20-day mean includes the day itself. On those bars the forecast is short, sized by the day's range divided by the 20-day mean bar range (RangeCompressionRatioComponent, short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). That ratio is standardised over 60 bars (min_periods 20) and negated. A range at its usual size gives a forecast of about -10, and a wider range gives a larger short, up to -20. On every other bar the forecast is 0. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Over the cases (bars where the forecast turns short by at least the band), take the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, signed in the short direction. Compute its mean and t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: The same excess short-direction return is higher in the cases with the larger half of |forecast| than in the smaller half (Welch t of larger minus smaller >= 1).
- Confirm: The mean short-direction excess return is > 0 with t >= 2 over at least 100 cases, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-direction excess return is < 0 with t <= -2 over at least 100 cases.
- Inconclusive: The main t is between -2 and 2, or the main t is >= 2 but the supporting check fails. Fewer than 100 cases spread over fewer than 60 distinct days is also inconclusive.
