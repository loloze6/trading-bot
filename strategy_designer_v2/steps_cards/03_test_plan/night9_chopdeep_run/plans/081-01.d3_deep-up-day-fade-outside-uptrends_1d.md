Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closes up while sitting below the coin's 20-day high, the depth measured in usual daily swings over the last 20 days -> adapted: the up close is a regime rule (1-day return > 0); inside it, the depth is graded from the 20-day closing high in 20-day return standard deviations.
- Comparison: deeper up days against shallower ones, except when the coin has been rising over the last 60 days -> kept: the position grows with depth, and the regime excludes a 60-day return above +10 % (the profile convention for "rising").
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next day -> kept: a short held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets short on exactly the idea's up days outside rising trends, sized by depth, and judges the next day's fall against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast is non-zero only on bars whose 1-day return is above 0 and whose 60-day return is at most +10 %. On every other bar the forecast is 0. On those bars it is the distance of the close below the highest close of the last 20 days, bar t included. That distance is divided by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns and standardised over 60 bars (window 60, min_periods 20). It is never positive. It is 0 at the high and about -10 at the usual depth, and it grows short, up to -20, the deeper the close sits.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars where the forecast is below 0, take the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, in the short direction. Report its mean and its t, with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the active bars at the median of |forecast|. The deeper half's mean short-direction unusual return minus the shallower half's must be positive (Welch t > 0).
- Confirm: The main measure's t is 2 or more over at least 100 days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's t is -2 or less over at least 100 days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more is also inconclusive when the deeper half does not fade more than the shallower half. Fewer than 100 days with cases is inconclusive whatever the t.
