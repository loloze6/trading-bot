Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, an up day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's 20-day average daily range -> adapted: "wide" is a regime condition (close above the prior close, and the day's range at or above its 20-day mean bar range, the plain reading of "wide compared with average"); inside it the position is graded by the range multiple. The falling profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the day's range against the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: today's range over the mean bar range of the last 20 bars, whatever the close.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day -> kept: short for 1 bar, sized by the range multiple.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on wide up days, more so the wider they are, so its next-day result answers the idea in both directions.
Forecast: The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only on up days (the 1-bar return is above 0) whose range multiple is at least 1. The range multiple is today's high minus low over the mean bar range of the last 20 bars (`RangeCompressionRatioComponent`, short 1, long 20, `base` "mean_bar_range", `form` "width"). Every other bar is flat. Inside the regime the forecast is (10 − x) / 2. Here x is the range multiple standardised over its last 60 values (about 10 at an ordinary range, capped at 20). The 10 is a standardised constant offset. So the forecast is about 0 at an ordinary range and grows more negative as the day widens, down to −5 at twice the usual range. It is a short, sized by the width.
