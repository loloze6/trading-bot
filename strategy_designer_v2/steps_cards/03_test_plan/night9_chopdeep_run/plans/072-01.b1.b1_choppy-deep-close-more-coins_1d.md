Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the close's distance below its 20-day high, in 20-day daily-return standard deviations -> kept: the profile line holds the choppy market, and `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` measures the distance as stated.
- Comparison: deeper closes against shallower ones ("the further ... the larger") -> adapted: the depth is graded, so the short position grows with it.
- Outcome: the fall over the next five days, downward -> kept: the short is held for 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own depth measure, held over its five-day horizon inside its profile, so a confirm or a reject on it is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: One component. It takes the close minus the highest high of the last 20 daily bars (bar t included), divides that by the high, then divides by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The value is 0 at the high and grows more negative the deeper the close sits below it. It is standardised against its own last 60 values (min_periods 20), so a typical depth gives about -10, capped at -20. It is then clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is therefore never positive, as the brief's short-only side requires. It is 0 with the close at the 20-day high, and the short grows in proportion to the depth. Outside the choppy profile, code keeps the book flat.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, on bars with a nonzero forecast. Regress the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its normal 5-bar drift (its mean over all windows) on the forecast. Report the slope's t, with the cases of one day taken together, and the number of cases and days.
- Supporting check: Split the grid cases into thirds by |forecast|. The mean excess 5-bar return of the deepest third minus that of the shallowest third must be negative (Welch t).
- Confirm: The slope is positive (deeper short, larger fall) with t >= 2. The check must also pass: deepest minus shallowest third is negative with t <= -2.
- Reject: The slope is negative (deeper closes followed by relative rises) with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive, including a slope with |t| < 2 or a confirming slope whose tercile check fails. The test needs at least 100 grid cases on at least 60 distinct days; with fewer, it is inconclusive whatever the t.
