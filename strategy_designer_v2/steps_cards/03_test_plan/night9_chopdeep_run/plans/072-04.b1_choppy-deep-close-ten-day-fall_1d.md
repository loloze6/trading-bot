Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the close sits below its 20-day high, measured in 20-day daily-return standard deviations -> kept (the choppy profile is added by code; the distance is the graded strength)
- Comparison: the further below the high, the larger the fall -> kept (the position grows with the depth)
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next ten days -> kept (short, held 10 bars)
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the exact depth in usual moves inside the choppy profile and is short in proportion to it over ten days.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, the distance of the close below its 20-day closing high, divided by that high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The high includes the current bar, so the value is 0 at a new high and negative below it. It is standardised over its past 60 values (target 10). The forecast is therefore 0 at the high and grows more negative, so a larger short, as the close sits more usual moves below the high, capped at -20. It is never positive, so the book is short only, and flat at a new high. Outside the choppy profile the code keeps the book flat.
Config: 072-04.b1_choppy-deep-close-ten-day-fall_1d_config.json
Side: short only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code
Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The slope of a least-squares fit of the coin's 10-bar forward return, minus that coin's mean 10-bar return over all windows, on the forecast. It uses the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window and only bars with a nonzero forecast. The slope's t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean drift-removed 10-bar return of the grid cases with forecast at or below -10 (deeper than usual) is below that of the cases with forecast between -10 and 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t of 2 or more (a more negative forecast, a lower excess return), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t of -2 or less (deeper closes are followed by relatively higher returns).
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2, or a confirming slope with a failed check, is inconclusive. Fewer than 100 grid cases with a nonzero forecast across all coins and windows is also inconclusive.
