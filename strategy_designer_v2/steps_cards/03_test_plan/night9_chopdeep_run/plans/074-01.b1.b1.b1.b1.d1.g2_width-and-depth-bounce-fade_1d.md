Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, an up day -> adapted: the falling profile is added by code, and "up day" (close above the prior close) is a regime rule; nothing is held outside it.
- Comparison: the day's high-to-low range against the 20-day average daily range, and the 5-day return including that day -> kept: both are measured as stated and are graded inside the regime.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day and the deeper the 5-day loss -> kept: a graded short held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book shorts exactly the up days of falling markets, sized by both of the idea's quantities, for one day.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 1-day return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 1). A bar whose close is above the prior close goes to "trending"; every other bar goes to "unknown", which is flat. Inside "trending" the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two components: - the day's range over its 20-day mean bar range (RangeCompressionRatioComponent: short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"), standardised over 60 bars, then negated. It is always short, about -10 at a usual width, and larger the wider the day. - the 5-day return including that day (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 5), standardised over 60 bars and clipped at a maximum of 0. It adds to the short the further the coin sits below its level of 5 days ago, and adds nothing when the coin is up over the 5 days. The forecast is never positive and is zero off up days. Its size grows with both the width of the day and the depth of the 5-day loss.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Over the case bars (forecast below 0), the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, taken in the short direction (the unusual move). Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together, before costs.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of abs(forecast). The larger half's mean unusual move minus the smaller half's must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the larger-half minus smaller-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2, meaning up days in falling markets are followed by rises beyond the drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t whose size check fails. At least 200 cases on at least 100 distinct days are required; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
