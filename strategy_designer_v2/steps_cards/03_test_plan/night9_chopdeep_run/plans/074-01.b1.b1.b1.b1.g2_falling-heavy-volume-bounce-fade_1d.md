Forecast: The regime detector requires three things on a bar: the day closed up (1-day return above 0), the day's high-low range is above the mean bar range of the last 20 days, and the day's volume is above the mean of the prior 20 days' volume. On those bars the forecast is minus the standardised excess volume ratio. The ratio is the day's volume over the prior 20-day mean, minus 1, standardised over its past 60 values. The forecast is 0 at average volume and grows more negative the heavier the volume. It is never positive. On every other bar the book is flat. The falling-market profile is added by code.
