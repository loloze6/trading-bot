Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy calm market, where the coin rose over the past 7 days, measured in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over 30 days, with its close near its 30-day highest close -> kept
- Comparison: the rise against the usual 7-day swing of the past 30 days, and the close against the 30-day highest close -> kept
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 14 days, larger the larger the scaled rise and the nearer the high -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own product of scaled rise and nearness to the high, held for its 14-day horizon inside the profile.
Forecast: One component, `HighProximityScaledReturnComponent`. It takes the 7-day return (`return_period` 7) and divides it by the mean absolute overlapping 7-day return of the last 30 days (`window` 30). It multiplies that by the proximity weight 1/(1+D), with `power` 1, where D is the percent distance of the close below its highest close of the last 30 days (`period` 30). The proximity weight is 1 at a new 30-day high and shrinks as the close sits lower. The result is standardised over its past 60 values (`min_periods` 20). It is then clipped at 0, because the idea is long only and the brief says falls need not mirror. The forecast is positive and grows with both the scaled rise and the nearness to the month's top. It is zero after a 7-day fall or a flat week. It runs on every bar of the default regime; step 3's code adds the choppy-calm profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 14 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the non-overlapping grid of 14 bars in each window, keeping the grid bars with forecast > 0. On each, take the coin's 14-bar return from that close minus the coin's mean 14-bar return over all its windows (its drift). The measure is the forecast-weighted mean of that excess return, with a t-statistic in which the cases of one day are pooled.
- Supporting check: Among the same grid bars, the least-squares slope of excess 14-bar return on forecast is positive. The larger the signal, the larger the further rise.
- Confirm: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result counts as inconclusive. This covers a t between -2 and 2, a confirming mean whose slope is 0 or negative, and fewer than 30 grid bars with forecast > 0 (or fewer than 20 distinct days).
