Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a one-day rise after a 5-day fall -> adapted: the rise (today up) and the prior fall (close[t-1] below close[t-6]) form a regime; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: rise sized by the 30-day std of daily returns, and by the depth of the 5-day fall -> kept: both are measured in 30-day std units and graded inside the regime.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 3 days, more for larger readings -> kept: a short held 3 bars, sized by both readings.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on these bounce days, sized by the two strengths the idea names, and held for its 3-day horizon.
Forecast: Two detector measures set the regime. The first is today's return over the 30-day std of daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). The second is the 5-day return ending yesterday, in 30-day std units (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 5, lag 1, vol_period 30). The bar is in the mean_reversion regime when the first is above 0 and the second is below 0. Every other bar is flat, and so is every bar outside "falling agitated", which code adds. Inside the regime the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two parts. The first part is the standardised scaled rise, negated. The second part is the standardised prior fall, which is already negative. Both parts are clipped at 0 from above, so the forecast is never long. It grows more negative as the rise gets larger in usual moves and as the prior 5-day fall gets deeper. It is about -10 when both are of usual size and is capped at -20.

Profile: falling agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: For each case, take the coin's 3-bar return after the signal minus its normal 3-bar drift, in the short direction. A case is a short bet of at least 2 forecast points, read once per 3-bar grid cell. The measure is the mean of these values, with the t computed with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median absolute forecast. The mean unusual move of the larger cases minus that of the smaller cases (Welch's t) must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main mean is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the large-minus-small difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main mean is below 0 with t ≤ -2 (bounces are followed by rises), or the large-minus-small difference has a Welch t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any main t between -2 and 2, or a main t ≥ 2 whose difference is ≤ 0 with a t above -2. At least 50 cases falling on at least 30 distinct days are needed, else the test is inconclusive.
