Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy calm market, a coin's 7-day rise in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over 30 days -> kept
- Comparison: only while the coin's 30-day return is a rise -> adapted: a regime condition (30-day return above 0), with nothing held outside it
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 7 days, larger for a larger scaled rise -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes
Forecast: The profile "choppy calm" is added by code. Inside it, the regime detector reads the coin's 30-day return in percent (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30, sf 1). Bars where that return is above 0 go to `trending`. All other bars go to `unknown`, which is flat. In `trending`, the forecast is the 7-day return divided by the mean absolute overlapping 7-day return over the last 30 bars (`MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent`, period 7, window 30). It is standardised against its own past 60 values and clipped at 0. The forecast is 0 when the week fell. It is positive and grows in proportion to the week's scaled rise, capped at 20. It never goes short.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window, take the cases with forecast > 0. For each case, compute the coin's 7-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 7-bar return over all windows. Average these excess returns weighted by the forecast. Compute its t with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 7-bar return of the top third of cases by forecast, minus that of the bottom third (Welch t), is above 0.
- Confirm: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0 with t >= 2, over at least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days. The top-third minus bottom-third difference must also be above 0.
- Reject: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is below 0 with t <= -2, over at least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive. A confirming main measure with a top-minus-bottom difference at or below 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 cases or fewer than 30 distinct days.
