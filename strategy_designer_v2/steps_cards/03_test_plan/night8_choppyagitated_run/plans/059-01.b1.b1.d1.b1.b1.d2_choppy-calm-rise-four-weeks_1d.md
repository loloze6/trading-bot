Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's rise over the past 7 days, in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return over the mean absolute 7-day return of the last 30 bars, rises only. The choppy calm profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the larger the scaled rise, the larger the further rise -> adapted: graded, so the position grows with the scaled rise.
- Outcome: the price rises further over the next 28 days -> kept: a long position held for 28 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled weekly rise inside its profile, held for its own 28-day horizon.
Forecast: One component, MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent (period 7, window 30, base "mean_abs_return"). It is the 7-day return divided by the mean absolute overlapping 7-day return of the last 30 bars. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a usual-sized reading is about 10, capped at 20. It is then clipped at 0. The forecast is zero after a flat or falling week. It is positive after a rise and grows linearly with the scaled size of the rise. It is never negative. The code keeps it at zero outside the choppy calm profile.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 28 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 28-bar grid of each window and keep the cells where the forecast is above 0. For each cell, take the coin's 28-bar forward return minus its normal 28-bar drift (its mean 28-bar return over all its windows). Average these excess returns weighted by the forecast. Compute t with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the same cases at their median forecast. Take the mean excess return of the upper half minus that of the lower half (Welch t).
- Confirm: The weighted mean excess return is positive with t ≥ 2, and the upper-half minus lower-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The weighted mean excess return has t ≤ -2, or the upper-half minus lower-half difference has a Welch t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a main t between -2 and 2. The test needs at least 30 cases with a positive forecast, on at least 30 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
