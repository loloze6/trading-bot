Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's rise over the past 7 days, in a choppy calm market -> adapted: graded by the rise's size, 0 when there is no rise; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise in multiples of the coin's usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return over the mean absolute 7-day return of the last 30 days.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 7 days, larger for a larger scaled rise -> kept: long, held 7 bars, sized by the scaled rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's scaled weekly rise, held for its week, so a rising or a non-rising forward return graded by it decides the idea.
Forecast: One component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` (period 7, window 30, base "mean_abs_return"). It is the 7-day return divided by the mean absolute overlapping 7-day return of the last 30 days. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), then clipped at 0. The forecast is positive and grows linearly with the week's rise in usual 7-day swings. It is 0 after a flat or falling week, and the book never goes short. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy calm profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window, take bars with forecast > 0. Regress the coin's next-7-bar return, minus its normal 7-bar drift (the mean over all windows), on the forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean of the same excess 7-bar return over those cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more AND the mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, or the mean excess return is below 0 with t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a t between -2 and 2. Any result is inconclusive with fewer than 100 grid cases with forecast > 0, or fewer than 50 distinct days.
