Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling agitated market, a one-day rise after a fall over the last 5 days -> adapted: the rise and the prior 5-day fall are a regime condition, and the falling agitated profile is added by code; inside the regime the forecast is graded by the depth of the fall.
- Comparison: the depth of the 5-day fall against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the 5-day return ending the day before the rise, divided by the 30-day return standard deviation at that day.
- Outcome: a fall over the next day that grows with that depth -> kept: a short held 1 bar, sized by the depth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the regime holds exactly the idea's bars, and the short's size follows the scaled depth the idea names.
Forecast: Two conditions select the regime. The day must close up: `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 1 is above 0. The 5 days before it must have fallen: `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` (period 5, vol_period 30, lag 1) is below 0; this is the 5-day return ending at t-1 in units of the 30-day daily-return standard deviation. On those bars the forecast is that same scaled fall, standardised over 60 bars. It is always negative there, so the book is short, and the short is larger the deeper the slide in usual moves (capped at -20). A `clip` at max 0 keeps it short only. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). The code adds the falling agitated profile.

- Supporting check: Regress the next-day excess return on |forecast| across the case bars. The slope must be negative, so the giveback grows with the depth.

- Inconclusive: Anything else is inconclusive, including a t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure with a slope that is not negative. At least 100 cases on at least 60 distinct days are needed, else the result is inconclusive.
