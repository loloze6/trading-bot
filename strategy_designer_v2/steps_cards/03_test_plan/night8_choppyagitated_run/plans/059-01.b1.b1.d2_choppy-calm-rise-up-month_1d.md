Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy calm market, a rise over the past 7 days, only while the 30-day return is positive -> kept: the profile is added by code, and the 30-day return above 0 is a regime rule; inside it the 7-day rise is graded and is zero when there is no rise.
- Comparison: the rise against the coin's usual daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return is divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 14 days, larger for a larger scaled rise -> kept: a 14-bar horizon, with gains that should grow with the position.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures exactly the stated situation, scaling, condition and horizon, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the 30-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30, sf 1). When it is above 0 the bar is `trending`; otherwise the bar is `unknown`, which is flat. Code also keeps the book flat outside the choppy calm profile. In `trending`, the forecast comes from `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` (period 7, vol_period 30): the 7-day return divided by the 30-day daily return standard deviation times sqrt(7). It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a usual-size rise reads about +10, capped at 20. A clip at 0 keeps it long only. The forecast is 0 when the week did not rise, and it grows in proportion to the week's rise measured in usual daily swings.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 14 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a forecast rise of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 14-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 14-bar return from the signal bar minus the coin's normal 14-bar drift over all windows. The measure is the mean over cases, with its t taken with the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: On the 14-bar grid cells with forecast > 0, the slope of the drift-adjusted 14-bar forward return on the forecast is positive. This checks that the gain grows with the position.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ -2, or the slope has t ≤ -2: a giveback, or a further rise that shrinks as the scaled rise grows.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including t between -2 and 2, or a positive main measure with a slope at or below 0 that is not significant. The test also needs at least 30 cases falling on at least 30 distinct days; with fewer it is inconclusive.
