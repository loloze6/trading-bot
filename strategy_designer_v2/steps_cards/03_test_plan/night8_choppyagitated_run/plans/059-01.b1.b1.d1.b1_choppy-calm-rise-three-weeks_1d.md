Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy calm market, a coin's rise over the past 7 days, in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the choppy calm profile is added by code; the rise is the 7-bar return over the mean absolute overlapping 7-bar return of the last 30 bars, positive side only.
- Comparison: larger scaled rise against smaller scaled rise -> adapted: the position is graded by the scaled rise, so a larger rise gives a larger long.
- Outcome: further rise over the next 21 days, growing with the scaled rise -> kept: a long held 21 bars, judged against the coin's normal 21-bar drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled weekly rise inside its own profile, measured on its own 21-day horizon, so a confirm or a reject on it is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: One component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` (`period` 7, `window` 30, `base` "mean_abs_return"). It gives the 7-day return divided by the mean absolute 7-day return over the last 30 bars, which is the week's rise in usual 7-day swings. It is standardised over its past 60 values (`min_periods` 20), so a usual-sized move reads about 10, with a cap of 20. It is then clipped at 0, so the strategy is long only. The forecast is 0 after a flat or falling week. After a rising week it is positive, and it grows in proportion to the rise measured in usual swings. Outside the choppy calm profile the book is flat; the profile is added by code.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 21 bars
- Main measure: The grid is the non-overlapping bars at positions 0, 21, 42, … of each window. Only grid bars with a forecast above 0 are cases. On these cases, regress the coin's 21-bar forward return minus its normal 21-bar drift (holding the coin, mean over all its windows) on the forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with cases of the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean of the 21-bar forward return minus the normal drift is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the mean excess return of the cases is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, or the mean excess return of the cases has a t of -2 or less (a giveback).
- Inconclusive: Any other result, for example a slope t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope with a mean excess return of 0 or below. A verdict needs at least 30 cases spread over at least 20 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
