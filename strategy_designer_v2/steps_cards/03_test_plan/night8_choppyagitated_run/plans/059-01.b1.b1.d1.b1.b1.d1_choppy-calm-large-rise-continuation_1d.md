- Situation: a rise over the past 7 days, in a choppy, calm market -> kept: the 7-day return, rises only (clipped at 0); the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise in multiples of the usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return over the mean absolute 7-day return of the last 30 bars.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 14 days, larger for larger scaled rises; small scaled rises give back -> kept: long, graded by the scaled rise, held 14 bars, with small rises damped towards 0.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's scaled weekly rise inside its profile, so the 14-bar returns that follow it confirm or reject the idea directly.
Forecast: One component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` (period 7, window 30). It is the 7-day return divided by the mean absolute overlapping 7-day return of the last 30 bars, so a rise is counted in usual weekly swings. It is standardised against its own past 60 values. It is then clipped at 0, so the book is long only after a rise and flat after a fall. A signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10 damps small scaled rises smoothly towards 0 and amplifies large ones. The forecast is 0 when the week did not rise. It grows with the square of the scaled rise. It is held 14 bars. The choppy-calm profile is added by code, and the book is flat outside it.
