Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day rise, in a falling, agitated market -> kept: the rise is a regime (today's 1-day return above 0), and the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days, and the depth of the 5-day return before the rise -> kept: the rise is measured in 30-day usual moves, and the prior 5-day return (ending the day before) adds to the short size the deeper it is.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 1 day -> kept: the position is short for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on rise days, sized by the scaled rise and the prior fall, so a confirm or a reject of the next-day fall growing with that size is the idea's.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless today's close is above yesterday's. On a rise day it is the mean of two parts. The first is the day's return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, standardised over 60 bars and negated, so it is always at or below zero. The second is the 5-day return ending the day before the rise, divided by its 30-day usual move, standardised over 60 bars and clipped at a maximum of 0. It is negative only after a prior fall, and it is deeper the harder that fall. The forecast is never positive. The short grows with the rise in swing units and with the depth of the prior 5-day fall. Outside the falling agitated profile the book holds nothing.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On active bars (forecast below 0), the slope of the next 1-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, regressed on the short size (minus the forecast). The t uses standard errors with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: On active bars, the mean of the drift-adjusted next-day return is below 0.
- Confirm: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2 (larger shorts are followed by larger falls than the coin's drift), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is positive with t ≥ +2 (larger shorts are followed by higher returns than the coin's drift).
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and +2, or a confirming slope with a mean drift-adjusted return at or above 0, is inconclusive. The test needs at least 100 active bars falling on at least 50 distinct days; with fewer, it is inconclusive.
