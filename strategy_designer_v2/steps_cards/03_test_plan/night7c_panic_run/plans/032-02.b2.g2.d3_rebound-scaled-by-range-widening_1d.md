Fidelity:
- Situation: the past 30 days' daily high-low ranges are wide against the past 90 days' -> kept: a regime is active only while the 90-day mean bar range over the 30-day mean bar range is below 1.
- Comparison: a lower close within the day's range, and wider 30-day ranges against the 90-day ones -> kept: both are graded inside the regime, the forecast growing as the close nears the low and as the ratio widens.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 1 day -> kept: each bet is held 1 daily bar and judged against the coin's normal daily drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the same ranges, the same close location and the same 1-day rise that the idea names.
Forecast: Bars are traded only in the wide-range regime. That regime holds while `MeanBarRangeRatioComponent` (long 90, short 30), the 90-day mean daily range over the 30-day one, is below 1. All other bars are flat. Inside the regime, the forecast is (3 x loc − w) / 2: - loc is `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (`reference` "high"), standardised over 60 bars. It is near 0 when the close is at the day's high and about 20 when the close is at the low. - w is the same range ratio, standardised over 90 bars. It is smaller when the 30-day ranges are wider against the 90-day ones. So the long grows as the close sits lower in the day's range and as the month's ranges widen. A close at the very top of the day gives a small short, at most about −4, which is mostly below the 2-point trading band. The forecast is clipped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On regime bars, pooled over coins and windows, the t of the slope of the next-day return on the forecast. The next-day return is close[t+1]/close[t] − 1, minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Same-day cases are summed into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily timing gain before costs is above 0. The timing gain is the book's gain minus the part that comes from the market's own rise.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the timing gain is positive.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A t between −2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a timing gain of 0 or below. The test needs at least 100 days carrying regime cases; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
