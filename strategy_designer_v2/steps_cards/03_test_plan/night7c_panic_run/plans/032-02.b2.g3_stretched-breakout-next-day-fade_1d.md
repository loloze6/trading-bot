Fidelity:
- Situation: the close sits above the highest high of the prior 30 days -> kept: a regime holds only on bars where the close is above max(high[t-30..t-1]).
- Comparison: the further the close sits above that high -> kept: the percent distance of the close above the prior 30-day high grades the position.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 1 day -> kept: a short position, held 1 bar, larger the further the close is above the high.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own breakout, its own distance and its own 1-day fall, so its result carries to the idea.
Forecast: On every bar, DistanceFromHighComponent (period 30, source "high", exclude_current 1, form "percent") gives the close's percent distance from the highest high of the prior 30 bars. The regime detector reads the same measure. When it is above 0, the bar is in the breakout regime ("mean_reversion"). On every other bar the regime is null and the forecast is 0. Inside the breakout regime, the distance is standardised over its own past 60 values (min_periods 20) and negated. The forecast is then negative (short), and its size grows linearly with how far the close sits above the prior high, measured against the coin's usual distance from that high. The forecast is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bars
- Main measure: Over breakout bars (regime "mean_reversion"), regress the coin's next-bar return minus its mean daily return over all windows on the breakout size (-forecast). Group the cases by day and report the slope's t.
- Supporting check: Over the same bars, the mean unusual move in the short direction (the next-bar return minus the coin's normal drift, sign-flipped) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope has t ≤ -2, so a larger stretch is followed by a larger fall than usual, AND the supporting check's mean is above 0.
- Reject: The slope has t ≥ +2, so a larger stretch is followed by a larger rise than usual.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive, as is a confirming slope whose mean unusual move is not above 0. The test needs at least 30 distinct days with a breakout case across the coins; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
