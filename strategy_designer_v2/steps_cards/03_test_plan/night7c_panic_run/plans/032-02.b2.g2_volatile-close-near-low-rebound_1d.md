- Situation: the close sits low in the day's high-low range, on 1d bars -> adapted: graded by the close's location in the bar, from 0 at the high to 1 at the low.
- Comparison: daily ranges over the past 30 days wide against the past 90 days -> kept: a regime holds only when the 30-day mean bar range exceeds the 90-day mean (MeanBarRangeRatio below 1, the usual "above average" level).
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more the lower the close -> kept: the 1-bar return measured against the forecast's size.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the regime is the idea's wide-range condition, the forecast grows as the close nears the low, and the next-day rise is measured against that size.
Forecast: Each day the regime detector computes the 90-day mean bar range over the 30-day mean bar range. When that ratio is below 1, the last 30 days swung wider than the last 90 days, and the bar is in `mean_reversion`. On every other bar the regime is `unknown`, which is null, so the book is flat. Inside the regime the forecast is the close's location in the day's range, measured from the high: (high − close) / (high − low). It is standardised over its past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). The forecast is long-only. It is 0 when the close is at the day's high, near +10 for an average close location, and up to +20 when the close sits at the day's low. It grows continuously as the close nears the low.

- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a positive slope with t ≥ 2 whose top-minus-bottom-third difference is 0 or below. The test needs at least 300 regime bars falling on at least 150 distinct days, else it is inconclusive.
