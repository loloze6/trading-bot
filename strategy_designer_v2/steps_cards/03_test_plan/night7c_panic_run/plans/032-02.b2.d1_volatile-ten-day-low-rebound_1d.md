Fidelity:
- Situation: a 30-day fall, wide 30-day daily ranges against the past year, and a close below the prior 10-day low -> kept: the fall and wide ranges are a regime, and the forecast is zero unless the close is below the 10-day low.
- Comparison: the deeper the close below the prior 10-day low, in 30-day mean daily ranges, the larger the rise -> kept: the forecast is graded by that exact depth.
- Outcome: price rises over the next 1 day -> kept: a long position held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's situation, depth and 1-day rise as stated, so a confirm or reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The regime rule sets `mean_reversion` when two conditions hold. The 30-bar close-to-close return is below 0. The mean daily high-low range over 365 bars, divided by the mean over the last 30 bars, is below 1, so the recent ranges are wider than the past year's. On every other bar the regime is `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime the component is `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent`, set to `period` 10, `base_period` 30, `side` "below", `extreme` "low". It reads max(0, lowest low of bars t-10..t-1 minus close[t]) divided by the 30-day mean daily range. It is 0 when the close is at or above the prior 10-day low. It grows linearly as the close sinks further below that low. It is standardised over its own past 60 values, so a typical break gives about 10, capped at 20. The forecast is never negative (long only). It is zero outside a break, and larger for deeper breaks.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a bet: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, one per 1-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's return from that bar's close to the next close, minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. The measure is the mean of these unusual moves, with its t computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median `post_pipeline_value` of `depth_below_low`. The mean unusual move of the deeper half minus that of the shallower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 distinct case days, and the deeper-minus-shallower difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2, over at least 30 distinct case days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t ≥ 2 when the deeper half does not beat the shallower half. Any result on fewer than 30 distinct case days is inconclusive too.
