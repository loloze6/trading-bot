Forecast: The position exists only on bars where two conditions both hold. First, the mean daily range over the last 30 bars is at least the mean daily range over the last 365 bars (MeanBarRangeRatio 365/30 at or below 1). Second, the close is the lowest close of the last 30 bars (distance from the 30-bar closing low at or below 0). On those bars the forecast is long and sized by the 30-day fall. That fall is the 30-bar return (PriceEvolution, period 30), standardised over 60 bars (min 20), negated and clipped at 0. A fall of usual size gives about +10, a fall twice the usual size gives +20 (the cap), and no fall gives 0. The forecast is never negative. On every other bar it is 0, and each bet is held 5 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: A case is a bet: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift (its mean over all windows). The measure is the mean of these unusual moves, with its t, cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median entry forecast. The mean unusual move of the upper half must exceed that of the lower half (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, over at least 30 distinct case days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2, over at least 30 distinct case days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a positive t >= 2 when the supporting check fails, and so is any result from fewer than 30 distinct case days.
