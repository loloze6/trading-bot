Fidelity:
- Situation: a close low (or high) in that day's high-low range -> kept
- Comparison: that day's range against the average daily range of the previous 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: a rise over the next 1 day after a low close and a fall after a high close, larger on wider days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own close location times range multiple, negated, judged on the next day's return.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, `RangeMultipleWeightedCloseLocationComponent` with period 20. Its raw value is (2 x close − high − low) divided by the mean daily range of the 20 prior days. That equals the signed close location (−1 at the low, +1 at the high) times the day's range multiple. The value is standardised over its last 60 values (min_periods 20) and then negated. The forecast is positive (long) after a close in the lower half of the day's range and negative (short) after a close in the upper half. It is 0 at mid-range. Its size grows with how near the close is to an extreme and with how wide the day was against its 20-day average. A low close on a quiet day gives a small position; a low close on a wide day gives a large one. It is ungated, because the idea is a graded relation and not a condition. It is capped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On every recorded bar t, compute (forecast[t] / 10) x (close[t+1] / close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean daily return over all its windows), within each window. Take the t of the mean of these values, with the values of one day summed across coins so that each day counts once. This measures timing beyond simply holding the coin.
- Supporting check: Split bars into thirds by |forecast|. The mean of sign(forecast) x next-day excess return in the top third minus the same mean in the bottom third must be above 0 (the effect grows with range width).
- Confirm: The main measure's t is 2 or more and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's t is −2 or less (larger positions predict the next day's move against their side).
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with a top-minus-bottom difference of 0 or below is also inconclusive. A minimum of 1000 days with cases is needed, else the result is inconclusive.
