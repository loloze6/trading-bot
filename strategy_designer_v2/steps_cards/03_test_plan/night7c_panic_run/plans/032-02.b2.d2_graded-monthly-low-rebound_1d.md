Fidelity:
- Situation: a close below the lowest low of the prior 30 days, in a coin that fell over the past 30 days -> kept: a regime rule on the 30-day return and the depth below the prior 30-day low, with flat outside it.
- Comparison: depth in units of the 30-day mean daily range, and those ranges against the past year -> adapted: the two are made graded and averaged, so the position grows with each.
- Outcome: a rise over the next 1 day -> kept: a long held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures next-day returns on exactly these break bars, sized by depth and range width.
Forecast: The regime is "mean_reversion" only on bars where both of these hold: - the close is below the lowest low of the prior 30 bars: `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent` (period 30, base_period 30, side "below", extreme "low") is above 0; - the 30-bar return is below 0. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside the regime, the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two standardised parts: - the depth of the close under the prior 30-day low, in 30-day mean daily ranges; - the 30-day mean daily range over the 365-day mean daily range (`MeanBarRangeRatioComponent`, form "inverse"). Both parts are positive, so the forecast is long only. It grows with the depth of the break and with how wide the month's ranges are against the past year.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The mean next-1-day return over all bars with forecast > 0, minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Among those cases, the mean excess next-day return of cases above the median forecast minus that of cases below the median is > 0.
- Confirm: Both of these hold: - the mean excess return is > 0 with t >= 2, over at least 30 distinct days; - the supporting check is > 0.
- Reject: The mean excess return has t <= -2, over at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any of these is inconclusive: - t between -2 and 2; - t >= 2 but the supporting check fails; - fewer than 30 distinct days with cases.
