Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin down over the past 30 days closes below its prior 30-day lowest low -> kept: a regime where the 30-day return is below 0 and the depth below the prior 30-day low is above 0.
- Comparison: the depth in mean daily ranges of the past 30 days, and those ranges against the past year -> replaced: the depth is kept (`BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent`), but the range-width part exists only as its inverse (`MeanBarRangeRatioComponent`, 365-day over 30-day mean range).
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 1 day, more with depth and width -> kept: a long forecast held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: no: the forecast cannot grow with the width of the ranges while staying long inside the situation.
Reason: The only catalogue measure of the 30-day mean high-low range against the past year is `MeanBarRangeRatioComponent`, which grows as the ranges get quieter. Inside the situation's regime, its negation is always negative, and the guide forbids a constant offset to make it positive, so the forecast would turn short on shallow breaks in quiet months. No config step gives 1 / x, and the return-based volatility ratios measure a different quantity, not high-low ranges.
