Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin that fell over the past 30 days closes below its lowest low of the prior 10 days -> kept: both are regime conditions (30-day return below 0, close below the prior 10-day low).
- Comparison: the depth of the close below that low, in percent -> kept: the forecast grows with the percent distance below the prior 10-day low.
- Outcome: a rise over the next 1 day, larger with the depth -> kept: long for 1 bar, judged on next-day return against holding the coin.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test is long only in the stated situation and sized by the stated depth, so a confirm or reject on next-day returns is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is flat (0) unless two conditions hold at the bar's close. The 30-day return (PriceEvolutionOnPeriodComponent, 30) must be below 0. The close must sit below the lowest low of the prior 10 days (DistanceFromHighComponent, period 10, source "high", extreme "low", exclude_current 1, percent below 0). Inside that regime the forecast is long. Its size is the percent depth of the close under the prior 10-day low, standardised against its own past 60 values, then clipped to the break side and negated. A deeper break gives a larger long. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Each case is a bar with a long position. For each case, take next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. The measure is the mean of that excess, weighted by the position held (`postRebalance_current_allocation`). Its t counts the cases of one day together, before costs.
- Supporting check: The least-squares slope of the cases' next-day excess return on `debug_info.components.break_depth.post_pipeline_value` is above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean excess is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope is positive.
- Reject: The weighted mean excess is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 cases (distinct days) across all coins and windows.
