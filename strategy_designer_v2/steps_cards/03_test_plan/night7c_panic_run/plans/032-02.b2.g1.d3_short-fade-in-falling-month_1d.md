Fidelity:
- Situation: the day's close sits high in its high-low range while the 20-day return is negative -> adapted: the falling month is a regime (20-day return below 0); inside it, the close location is graded, with no threshold.
- Comparison: the day's range against the previous 20 days' average range -> kept: the day's range over the mean daily range of the last 20 days, a graded weight.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 1 day, more so the wider the range -> kept: a short held for 1 bar, larger as both measures grow.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether next-day falls in falling months grow with the close location and the range multiple, which is the idea's own claim.
Forecast: The forecast is set only on bars where the coin's 20-day close-to-close return is below 0. Those bars form the `trending` regime. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside the regime, two components enter with equal weight: - the close location, (close − low) / (high − low), which is 1 at the day's high; - the range multiple, the day's high−low range over the mean daily range of the last 20 days. Each component is standardised over its own past 60 values (about +10 at its usual level) and then negated. The forecast is the mean of the two, always short inside the regime. Its size grows as the close nears the high and as the day's range widens against its 20-day average. It is near −5 or smaller in size on a low close of a narrow day, and up to −20 on a close at the high of a very wide day.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The cases are the regime bars with a non-zero forecast. For each case, take the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, and multiply it by −1 (the short's side). The measure is the mean of that value weighted by |forecast|, with its t computed with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The |forecast|-weighted mean excess fall is above 0 with t ≥ 2.
- Reject: The |forecast|-weighted mean excess fall is below 0 with t ≤ −2 (the coin rises more than usual after these days).
- Inconclusive: Any result with t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a result from fewer than 100 case-days across all coins and windows.
