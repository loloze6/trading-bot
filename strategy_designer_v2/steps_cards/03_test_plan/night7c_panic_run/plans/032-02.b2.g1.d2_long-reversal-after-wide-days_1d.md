Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close sitting low within a day's range that is wide against the prior 20 days' average range -> kept: (high − close) / mean range of the previous 20 bars equals the close-location share times the range multiple, on every bar.
- Comparison: the day's range against the average daily range of the previous 20 days -> kept: base_exclude_current 1 gives bars t-20..t-1.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more for a lower close on a wider day, long only -> kept: 1-bar horizon, forecast clipped at 0 by construction.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the idea's graded product, so a rise that grows with it confirms the idea and a fall that grows with it rejects it.
Forecast: BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent with period 1, high_current 1, extreme "high" and side "below" makes the level X equal to the day's own high. It returns (high[t] − close[t]) / (mean of high − low over the 20 bars before t). That equals the share of the range below the close's position ((high − close)/(high − low)) times the range multiple ((high − low)/prior mean range). The value is standardised over its last 60 values. The forecast is 0 when the close is at the high. Otherwise it is positive and grows linearly as the close nears the low and as the day widens, about +10 at its usual size and capped at +20. It is never negative, so the book is long only, and no regime is used.

- Main measure: The next-bar return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, on bars in the top third of the forecast minus bars in the bottom third. Terciles are taken per coin over all its windows. The test is Welch's t, with the cases of one day pooled per group.
