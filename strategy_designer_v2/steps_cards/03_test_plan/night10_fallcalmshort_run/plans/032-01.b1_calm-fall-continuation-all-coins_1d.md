Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin in a falling, calm market, fallen over the past 30 days -> kept: the falling calm profile is added by code, and inside it the 30-day fall grades a short.
- Comparison: larger past falls against smaller ones -> adapted: graded, so the short grows with the size of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls further over the next day -> kept: the position is short, held 1 daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the short is sized by the 30-day fall only inside falling calm markets, so its gain against holding is the idea's claim.
Forecast: The forecast uses the 30-day price change in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30). It is standardised against its own past 60 values, which keeps its sign. It is then clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is negative (short) when the coin is below its close of 30 days ago, and it grows in size with the depth of that fall, up to -20. It is 0 when the coin is at or above that close. It is never long. Code adds the falling calm profile, so the forecast is 0 outside that profile.

Profile: falling calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The book's timing gain before costs, on bars in a position. Each bar's gain is the position x (next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows). Gains are summed per day across coins, and the measure is the t of their mean over days.
- Supporting check: Split the bars in a position at the median of their negative forecasts. The coin's next-day return minus its drift must be lower after the deeper-fall half than after the shallower half (deeper minus shallower below 0).
- Confirm: The timing gain has t >= 2 over at least 100 days in a position, and the deeper-half minus shallower-half next-day excess return is below 0.
- Reject: The timing gain has t <= -2 over at least 100 days in a position. In that case the shorts are followed by rebounds against the drift.
- Inconclusive: Either of these is inconclusive: - a timing-gain t between -2 and 2; - t >= 2 with the deeper half not lower than the shallower half. A test with fewer than 100 days in a position is also inconclusive.
