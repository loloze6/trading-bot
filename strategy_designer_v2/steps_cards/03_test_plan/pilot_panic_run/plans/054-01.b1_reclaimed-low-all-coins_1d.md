Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling agitated market, a day's low goes below the prior 30-day low but the day closes back above it -> kept: `ReclaimedPriorLowPierceDepthComponent`(30) is above 0 only on such days and selects the regime; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: a larger rise the deeper the low went below the 30-day low -> kept: the forecast grows with the depth of the pierce in percent of the prior low.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 5 days -> kept: a long held for 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this pierce-and-reclaim, its depth and the 5-day rise as stated.
Forecast: The component computes the percent depth of the day's low below the lowest low of the prior 30 days, on days that close back at or above that low, and 0 on all other days. The regime detector sends a bar to `mean_reversion` only when this depth is above 0; every other bar is flat, and the profile vetoes, added by code, keep the book flat outside falling agitated markets. Inside the regime, the forecast is the depth standardised against its past 365 values. Those values include the many zero days, so the target is set to 0.4, which puts a typical pierce near +10 and a pierce twice as deep near +20. The forecast is long only, zero without a reclaimed pierce, and grows linearly with the depth. Each bet is held for 5 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: For each case (a long bet: the forecast rising by at least 2 points, one per 5-bar grid cell), the coin's 5-day return from the close of the signal bar minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all its windows. Take the mean over cases, with a t computed from deviations summed per day.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of their forecast. The mean excess 5-day return of the deeper half minus that of the shallower half must be above 0 (Welch t).
- Confirm: The mean excess 5-day return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the deeper-half minus shallower-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean excess 5-day return is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any result with t between -2 and 2. A confirmed main measure whose depth check is not above 0 is also inconclusive. Fewer than 30 cases, or cases on fewer than 25 distinct days, gives an inconclusive result whatever the t.
