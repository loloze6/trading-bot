Forecast: The forecast is the short side of the reclaimed-pierce reading. - When it is active. The regime detector reads `ReclaimedPriorLowPierceDepthComponent` (period 30): (30-day prior low − today's low) / that low × 100, counted only when the close is at or above that low. A day with a value above 0 is the `mean_reversion` regime. Every other day is flat. The profile code also keeps the book flat outside the rising profile. - How it is sized. Inside the regime, the same component is standardised over its past 500 values (min_periods 120). The target is 0.25 rather than 10, because the component's history is mostly zeros. With target 10, every event would sit at the +20 cap, an on/off signal. Target 0.25 keeps the size graded with pierce depth. Note that 0.25 is my estimate of the share of days with a pierce, not a convention. - Its sign. The value is then negated. The forecast is 0 on non-pierce days and negative on pierce-and-reclaim days. The deeper the low went below the 30-day low, the larger the short, up to the -20 cap.
