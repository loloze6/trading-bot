- Situation: the day trades below its lowest low of the past 30 days, then closes back above it -> kept: the regime is active only when low[t] < the prior 30-day low < close[t].
- Comparison: the rise is larger the lower the close sits in the day's range -> kept: inside the regime, the forecast is graded by (high - close) / (high - low).

Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorLowReclaimInRangeComponent` (period 30, form "reclaim"). It is above 0 only on a day whose low went under the lowest low of the prior 30 days and whose close ended above that low. Those days go to `mean_reversion`; every other day is flat. In `mean_reversion`, the forecast is `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "high"), the close's distance from the day's high as a share of the day's range: 0 with the close at the high, 1 with the close at the low. It is standardised over the past 60 days, window 60 and min_periods 20, which gives about 10 at the usual close location and up to 20 for a close at the low. The forecast is therefore long and grows the lower the reclaim closes in its range. It is never negative, and it is zero outside the situation. The code adds the falling-agitated profile.
