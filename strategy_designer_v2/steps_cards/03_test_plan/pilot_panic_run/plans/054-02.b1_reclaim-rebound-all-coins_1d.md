Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, the day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above it. -> kept: the profile is added by code; a regime rule fires when `PriorLowReclaimInRangeComponent`(30) > 0, which means low[t] < L < close[t], with L the prior 30-day low.
- Comparison: the idea states none; the brief compares with ordinary days in the same market. -> kept: case days are measured against the other falling-agitated days, rebuilt from the closes.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 5 days, more as the close sits higher above the low against the day's range. -> kept: a long held 5 bars, sized by (close − L) / (high − low).
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the stated pierce-and-reclaim inside the stated profile, graded by the stated strength, and holds for the stated 5 days.
Forecast: The forecast is 0 except on reclaim days inside the profile. A reclaim day is a day whose low went under the lowest low of the prior 30 days and whose close ended back above that low. On those days the forecast is long and sized by `PriorLowReclaimInRangeComponent` (period 30, form "reclaim"). That reading is (close − prior 30-day low) / (day's high − day's low). It is near 0 when the close barely clears the low and nears 1 when the close sits at the day's high. Standardise divides it by its mean absolute value over the past 500 days, most of which are 0. The target is set to 0.25 (about 10 times the event rate of roughly 1 day in 40) so that a typical reclaim reads about +10 instead of sitting at the +20 cap. The forecast is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The 5-day forward return (close[t+5] / close[t] − 1, inside one window) after case bars, minus the mean 5-day forward return of the other falling-agitated bars. Case bars are bars where the forecast rises by 2 or more. The other bars are rebuilt from the closes: a 60-day return below −10 %, and a 30-day daily-return std above its past-year median. Both groups are sampled on the 5-bar grid of each window. The result is a Welch t, with cases of the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Across the case bars, the Spearman correlation between `post_pipeline_value` of `reclaim` and the 5-day forward return minus the coin's normal drift is above 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed only if both hold: - the difference is positive with t ≥ 2; - the supporting-check correlation is above 0.
- Reject: The difference is negative with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is a confirming t whose supporting check fails. Fewer than 30 cases, or cases on fewer than 20 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
