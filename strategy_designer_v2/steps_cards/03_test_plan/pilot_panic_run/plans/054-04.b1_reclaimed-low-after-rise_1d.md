Fidelity:
- Situation: a day trades below the past 30 days' lowest low and closes back above it -> kept: a regime rule on `ReclaimedPriorLowPierceDepthComponent` (period 30) above 0; the agitated profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the higher the coin's 60-day return before that day -> kept: the 60-day return in percent, ending at the reclaim day's close.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 10 days -> kept: a short position, held for 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on reclaim days, and it is larger the higher the prior 60-day rise, which is exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: The forecast exists only on a day whose low went under the lowest low of the prior 30 days and whose close came back to that low or above it. On other days the regime is null and the forecast is 0. On a reclaim day the forecast is minus the standardised 60-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 60, standardise 60/20, then negate), clipped at a maximum of 0. It is short and grows with the prior 60-day rise, up to -20. It is 0 when the coin fell over those 60 days, and it is never long.

Profile: agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, before costs, in the short direction. A case is a bet of the forecast (a fall of at least 2 points from 0), read once per 10-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's return over the next 10 bars, subtract the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows, and change the sign. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of their |forecast|. The mean unusual short move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual short move is above 0 with t ≥ 2 over at least 30 cases, and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual short move is below 0 with t ≤ -2 over at least 30 cases. This means the coin rose more than its drift after these reclaims.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a supporting check at 0 or below, or fewer than 30 cases.
