Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling market whose running 20-day fall is a real one, not a slight dip -> kept: the profile "falling" is added by code, and the regime holds only when the 20-day return is below -10 % (the usual size of a correction, since the brief gives no number).
- Comparison: volume over the past 30 days against the 30 days before, scaled by how deep the 20-day fall is -> kept: the log ratio of the two 30-day mean volumes, times the 20-day fall in percent.
- Outcome: a smaller fall or larger rise over the next 5 days with volume growth, and a larger fall with dry-up -> kept: a 5-day hold, long on growth and short on dry-up.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's product, signed by the volume change and graded by fall depth, held only in real falls, so its 5-day result answers the idea both ways.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 20-day return in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 20, scaling_factor 1). When it is below -10, the bar is in the regime. On every other bar the book is flat, and the code's falling-profile vetoes also keep it flat outside a falling market. Inside the regime, the forecast is VolumeChangeTimesFallDepthComponent (volume_period 30, fall_period 20): ln(mean volume of the last 30 days / mean volume of the 30 days before) x the 20-day fall in percent. It is standardised over its own past 60 values (min_periods 20). The forecast is positive (long) when volume grew and negative (short) when volume dried up. Its size grows with the size of the volume change and with the depth of the fall. It is 0 when the two volume means are equal. The cap is ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bars on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window where the forecast is not 0. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return from that close, minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all its windows, times the sign of the forecast. The measure is the mean of this signed excess return weighted by |forecast|. Its t is computed with cases on the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The weighted mean signed excess return is above 0 with t >= 2, over at least 30 independent days.
- Reject: The weighted mean signed excess return is below 0 with t <= -2, over at least 30 independent days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or fewer than 30 independent days with a case.
