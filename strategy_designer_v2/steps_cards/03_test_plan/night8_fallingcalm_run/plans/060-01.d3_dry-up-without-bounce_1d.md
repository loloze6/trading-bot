Forecast: The regime is "trending" when the 30-day mean volume is below the mean of the 30 days before it (log ratio < 0) and the 5-day return is negative. The first part is the log volume ratio (VolumeRatioComponent: period 30, short_period 30, exclude_current 30, form log). The second is the 5-day return (PriceEvolutionComponent: period 5). The short grows the more volume dried up and the deeper the 5-day fall. It goes to 0 once the 5-day return turns non-negative, which is the bounce.
