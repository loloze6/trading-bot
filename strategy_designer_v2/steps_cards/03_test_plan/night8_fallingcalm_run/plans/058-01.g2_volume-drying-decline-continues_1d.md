Forecast: The regime detector computes the 30-day percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30). Bars where it is below 0 go to `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. Code adds the falling calm profile vetoes on top. Inside the regime, one component gives the volume dry-up: `VolumeRatioComponent` with period 30, short_period 30, exclude_current 30 and form "log". That is ln(mean volume of bars t-29..t / mean volume of bars t-59..t-30). It is negative when volume shrank. It is standardised over 60 past values (min 20), then clipped at max 0. The forecast is therefore short only. It is 0 when volume held or grew, and it grows in size continuously as the volume shrink deepens, capped at -20.
