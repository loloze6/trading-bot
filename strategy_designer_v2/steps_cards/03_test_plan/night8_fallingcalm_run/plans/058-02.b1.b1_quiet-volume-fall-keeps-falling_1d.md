Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin is after a 30-day fall -> adapted: "after" made "while"; the 30-day return below 0 is a regime, and the book is flat otherwise.
- Comparison: 30-day volume against the coin's own past-year average volume -> kept: the mean of the last 30 volumes over the mean of the last 365, as a log ratio.
- Outcome: the lighter that volume, the more the price keeps falling over the next 5 days -> kept: a graded short, larger the lighter the volume, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own fall, volume ratio and 5-day horizon, so its verdict is the idea's.
Forecast: A regime rule keeps the book active only while the coin's 30-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30, sf 1) is below 0. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside that regime, one component sets the forecast: `VolumeRatioComponent` with period 365, short_period 30 and form "log", which is ln(mean of the last 30 volumes / mean of the last 365). It is standardised over its own past 365 values (min_periods 91), then clipped at max 0. The forecast is negative (short) when the 30-day volume is lighter than the coin's yearly average, and it grows in size, up to -20, the lighter the volume. It is 0 when the volume is at or above average, so a heavy-volume fall is not shorted. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the bars of a 5-bar grid, in each window, where the regime is "trending" (a 30-day fall). On those bars, regress the coin's 5-bar forward return, minus its normal drift (the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows), on the forecast. The slope's t is computed with the grid bars of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Take the grid bars with a forecast of -2 or lower. On those bars, the mean drift-adjusted 5-bar return in the short direction must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2, so a more negative forecast is followed by a lower drift-adjusted return. The supporting check must also pass.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2: lighter-volume falls are followed by less falling than heavier-volume ones.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a positive slope with t >= 2 when the supporting check fails. The test needs at least 100 grid bars in the fall regime with a forecast of -2 or lower, spread over at least 30 distinct days. With fewer, the result is inconclusive.
