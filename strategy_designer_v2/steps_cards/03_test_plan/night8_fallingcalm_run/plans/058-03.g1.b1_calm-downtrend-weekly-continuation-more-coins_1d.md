Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's last 5 days' move, in a falling, calm market -> kept: the 5-day return, graded by its size; step 3's code adds the profile.
- Comparison: the further it fell (or rose), the more it continues -> kept: the position grows with the size of the 5-day move.
- Outcome: the next 5 days move the same way as the last 5 -> kept: a 5-bar holding period in the direction of the past 5-day return.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the past 5-day move signed for continuation, held for 5 days, and it trades only inside the falling calm profile.
Forecast: One indicator, the coin's 5-bar percent return, `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 5. It is standardised against its own 60 past values (minimum 20), so its average size is about 10, and it is capped at ±20. The sign is kept: the forecast is positive after a 5-day rise (long) and negative after a 5-day fall (short). Its size grows linearly with the size of the move, and it is 0 only when the coin is flat over the 5 days. No regime of its own is used. Step 3's code adds the falling calm profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Profile: falling calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the bars on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window where the forecast is not 0. On those bars, take the least-squares slope of the coin's next 5-bar return, minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows, on forecast / 10. The t of the slope is computed with cases on the same day summed as one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2. The return beyond the coin's drift grows with the forecast, on its side.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2. The next 5 days move against the past 5 days, more strongly the larger the move.
- Inconclusive: A slope with t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is a result from fewer than 100 grid cases or fewer than 60 distinct days.
