Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling, calm market where the coin has fallen over its last 30 days -> adapted: the profile is added by code, and "fallen over 30 days" is a regime (30-day return below 0), held "while" it lasts.
- Comparison: volume over those 30 days against its usual volume -> kept: mean volume of the last 30 days over the mean of the past 365 days, as a log ratio.
- Outcome: the quieter that volume, the more the coin keeps falling over the next 5 days -> kept: a short that grows as the volume gets thinner, flat on heavy volume, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only in a calm, falling market after a 30-day fall, sized by how thin the volume was, so its 5-day result answers the idea directly.
Forecast: The forecast is zero on every bar unless the coin's 30-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30) is below 0. The profile code also keeps it flat outside a falling, calm market. On a qualifying bar the forecast comes from the log of mean volume over the last 30 bars divided by mean volume over the last 365 bars (`VolumeRatioComponent`, short_period 30, period 365, form log). This log ratio is negative when the recent volume is thinner than usual. It is standardised against its own past 365 values, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore a short that grows smoothly as the 30-day volume gets quieter than usual. It is 0 when volume is at or above usual, and it is never positive.

- Main measure: Take the in-regime bars (regime "trending", inside the profile) on a non-overlapping 5-bar grid per window. Regress each bar's 5-day return, minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all windows, on that bar's forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: On grid bars with forecast below 0, the mean drift-adjusted 5-day return is below 0, so quiet-volume falls continue against simply holding the coin.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 (a more negative forecast, meaning thinner volume, is followed by lower returns than holding), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning heavy-volume falls continue more than quiet ones.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2, or a confirming slope whose check fails. The test needs at least 100 in-regime grid bars, with at least 30 bars on each side (forecast below 0, and forecast 0), spread over at least 30 distinct days. Results before costs.
