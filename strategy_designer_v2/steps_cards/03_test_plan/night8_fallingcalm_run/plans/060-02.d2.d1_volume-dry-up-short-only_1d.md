Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling market while the 10-day return is still negative -> kept: the profile "falling" plus a regime rule that the 10-day return is below 0.
- Comparison: volume over the past 30 days against the 30 days before, the more shrunk the stronger -> kept: the log ratio of the two adjacent 30-day mean volumes, graded.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 5 days -> kept: a short held 5 bars, sized by the shrinkage.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the same situation, comparison and horizon are measured as stated, and only the short side the consequence names is traded.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 10-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 10, scaling factor 1). When it is below 0, the bar is `mean_reversion`; otherwise it is `unknown`, which is flat. Code adds the "falling" profile on top of this. Inside the regime, one component reads ln(mean volume of the last 30 days / mean volume of the 30 days before) (`VolumeRatioComponent`, period 30, short_period 30, exclude_current 30, form "log"). That value is standardised over its past 60 values and clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is negative (short) when volume has shrunk, and its size grows with the shrinkage, up to -20. It is 0 when volume has grown or held steady, so there are no longs. It is also 0 outside the regime and outside the profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move in the short direction, per case. A case is a short bet (a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points), read once per cell of the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window. For each case, take minus the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar, minus minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows. Report its mean, and its t with cases on the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The larger-shrinkage half must have a mean unusual short move above that of the smaller half (Welch difference, larger minus smaller, above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual short move is above 0 with t >= 2, over at least 30 cases, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual short move is below 0 with t <= -2, over at least 30 cases.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2. Also a confirming main measure whose supporting check fails, or fewer than 30 cases.
