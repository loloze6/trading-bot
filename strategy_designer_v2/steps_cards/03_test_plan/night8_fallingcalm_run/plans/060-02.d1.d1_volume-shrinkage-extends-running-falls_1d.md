Forecast: Bars whose 20-day return is at or above 0 are flat. The profile (falling) is also flat outside its bars, and code adds that profile. On the other bars, the forecast uses `VolumeRatioComponent` with these settings: mean volume of the last 30 days over the mean of the 30 days before (`period` 30, `exclude_current` 30, `short_period` 30), log form. This value is negative when volume has shrunk. It is standardised over its past 60 values and then clipped at 0 from above. So the forecast is negative (short) when volume shrank, and the short is larger the more it shrank. The forecast is 0 when volume grew or stayed even. It is never positive.

On those bars, regress each coin's next 5-bar return, minus its normal 5-bar drift, on the forecast. The forecast is 0 when volume grew and negative as volume shrank. The measure is the slope's t. - Supporting check: On grid cases with forecast < 0, the mean unusual move on the short side (minus the drift-adjusted 5-bar return) is above 0.

- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2: shrinkage is followed by a smaller fall than volume growth.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t strictly between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a positive slope with t ≥ 2 when the supporting mean is not above 0. The test needs at least 30 grid cases with forecast < 0 and at least 60 in-regime grid cases in total; with fewer it is inconclusive.
