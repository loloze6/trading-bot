Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 30-day fall, measured against its usual 30-day move, in a choppy agitated market -> kept: the 30-day return over the mean absolute 30-day return of the past year, falls only; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the deeper the fall, the stronger the continuation -> adapted: the short position is graded by the fall's depth in usual moves.
- Outcome: the price keeps falling over the next 5 days -> kept: short, held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall depth, held for its own horizon, inside its own profile.
Forecast: One component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 30 and `window` 365. It is the 30-day return divided by the mean absolute 30-day return over the past year, so it counts the fall in usual 30-day moves. The component is standardised over 395 past values, with `min_periods` 99, which keeps its sign. It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so only falls remain. The forecast is 0 after a 30-day rise or a flat month. It is negative (short) after a fall, and it grows continuously in size the deeper the fall is in usual moves, up to -20. It is never positive. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Profile: choppy agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Cases are grid bars (positions 0, 5, 10, … of each window) where the forecast is below 0. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar return from that bar's close, minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all windows, and flip its sign so that a fall counts positive (the short direction). Average these per-case values weighted by |forecast|. Compute its t with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: The mean short-direction excess return of the cases in the top third of |forecast| minus that of the bottom third is above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean short-direction excess return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The weighted mean short-direction excess return is below 0 with t ≤ -2: the deeper the falls, the more the coin rises instead.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure whose supporting check fails. Fewer than 50 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
