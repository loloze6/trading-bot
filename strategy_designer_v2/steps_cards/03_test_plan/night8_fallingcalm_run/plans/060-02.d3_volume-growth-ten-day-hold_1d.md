It is positive (long) when volume grew and negative (short) when it shrank. Its size grows with the size of the log ratio, capped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: On a grid of non-overlapping 10-bar samples in each window, take the cells whose forecast is not 0. For each cell, compute (forecast / 10) × (the coin's 10-bar return − the coin's mean 10-bar return over all windows). The measure is the mean of these values, with its t; cells on the same date across coins are summed into one case.
- Supporting check: The Spearman rank correlation, over the same cells, between the forecast and the coin's excess 10-bar return is above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's t is 2 or more, on at least 30 day-cases, and the rank correlation is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's t is -2 or less, on at least 30 day-cases.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected when the t lies between -2 and 2, when the t is 2 or more but the rank correlation is 0 or below, or when there are fewer than 30 day-cases.
