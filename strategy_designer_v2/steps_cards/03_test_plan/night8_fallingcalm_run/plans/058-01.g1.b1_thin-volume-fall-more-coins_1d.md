Fidelity:
- Situation: a deep 30-day fall on volume shrunk against the prior 30 days, in a falling calm market -> kept: the 30-day fall times the ratio of prior-30-day to last-30-day mean volume, inside the falling calm profile.
- Comparison: volume over the last 30 days against the 30 days before -> kept: the ratio of those two means scales the short.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 5 days -> kept: a short held 5 bars, graded by depth times volume shrinkage.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's depth-by-thinning product in its own profile, so its 5-day result speaks for the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses one ThinVolumeScaledReturnComponent with period 30, volume_period 30, base_near 31, base_far 60 and window 365. Its raw value is the 30-day return in usual 30-day moves (measured over the past year), times the mean volume of days t-60..t-31 over the mean volume of the last 30 days. The value is standardised against its own past 395 values. It is then clipped at 0 from above, so the book is never long. The forecast is negative (short) when the coin fell over 30 days. The short grows with the depth of the fall and with how much volume thinned. The same fall on swelling volume gives a smaller short (ratio below 1). The forecast is 0 when the 30-day return is not negative. The profile code keeps the book flat outside falling calm markets.
