Forecast: The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only when two things hold. The first is that ln(mean volume of the last 30 days / mean volume of the 30 days before) is above 0. The second is that the 10-day return is below 0. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside the regime the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two standardised parts (window 60, min_periods 20): - the 30-day log volume ratio, clipped at 0; - minus the 10-day percent return, clipped at 0. Both parts are positive inside the regime, so the forecast is long only. It is near 0 when the volume growth is small and the fall is shallow. It grows with each part and reaches about +10 when both are at their usual size, up to the cap of 20. The profile code adds the "falling" condition on top.
