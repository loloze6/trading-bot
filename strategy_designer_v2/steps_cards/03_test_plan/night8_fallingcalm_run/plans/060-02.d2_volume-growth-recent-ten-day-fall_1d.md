- Situation: a falling market where the coin's 10-day return is still negative -> kept: the profile "falling" is added by code, and a regime rule allows a position only while the 10-day return is below 0.
- Comparison: the volume of the past 30 days against the 30 days before -> kept: the log ratio of the two adjacent 30-day mean volumes, graded.
- Outcome: the more volume grew, the smaller the fall or the larger the rise over the next 5 days -> kept: the next 5-day return, judged against the coin's normal drift, should grow with the forecast.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own volume measure, active only in the idea's situation, and it is judged on the idea's 5-day horizon.
Forecast: The forecast exists only while the coin's 10-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 10) is below 0; the profile adds the falling-market condition. On those bars it is the log of mean volume over the last 30 days divided by mean volume over the 30 days before them (`VolumeRatioComponent`, period 30, short_period 30, exclude_current 30, form "log"), standardised over its past 60 values. It is positive (long) when volume grew and negative (short) when volume shrank. Its size grows with the size of the change, and it is 0 when volume is unchanged or the 10-day return is not negative.
