Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, 30-day volume shrunk against the 30 days before -> kept: the profile "falling" is added by code, and the shrinkage is the log of mean volume over the last 30 days divided by mean volume over the 30 days before.
- Comparison: the shrinkage among the deepest of the coin's past two months -> adapted: "among the deepest" is made graded by setting the shrinkage against its own past 60 days (standardise) and damping shallow readings (signed_power).
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 20 days -> kept: a short that grows with the shrinkage, held 20 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded shrinkage, inside its falling market, judged on its own 20-day fall.
Forecast: One component, `VolumeRatioComponent` with period 30, short_period 30, exclude_current 30 and form "log". It computes ln(mean volume of bars t-29..t / mean volume of bars t-59..t-30). It is negative when volume has shrunk and larger in size the deeper the shrinkage. `standardise` (window 60, min_periods 20) sets it against the mean size of its last 60 daily values, the coin's past two months. A shrinkage deeper than usual reads beyond -10. `signed_power` (exponent 2, pivot 10) damps ordinary shrinkage towards 0 and amplifies the deepest. `clip` with max 0 keeps only the short side. The forecast is 0 when volume grew or held, and negative and growing in size with the depth of the dry-up, most of all where it is among the deepest of the past two months. Code keeps the book flat outside the falling profile.
