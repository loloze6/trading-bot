Fidelity:
- Situation: a falling market, the 10-day return negative but the fall still moderate -> adapted: the profile and "10-day return below 0" are kept as conditions; "moderate" is made graded, so the position shrinks as the 10-day fall deepens.
- Comparison: volume over the past 30 days against the 30 days before -> kept: the log shrinkage of the two adjacent 30-day mean volumes, which grades the position.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 5 days -> kept: a short held 5 bars, sized by the shrinkage.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the dry-up-sized short return over 5 days, only while the 10-day return is negative in a falling market.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where the 10-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 10) is below 0. On all other bars it is 0, and the code-added profile keeps the book flat outside a falling market. On active bars the forecast comes from `VolumeDryUpTimesFallModerationComponent` (`volume_period` 30, `fall_period` 10). That component is max(0, -ln(mean volume over the last 30 days / mean volume over the 30 days before)), multiplied by 1/(1 + the 10-day fall in percent). It is standardised over its past 60 values and then negated. The result is never positive: 0 when volume has not shrunk, and more negative (a larger short) as the shrinkage grows and as the fall stays shallow. It is capped at -20.
