- Situation: deep 30-day fall on volume that shrank against the prior 30 days, in a falling calm market -> kept: the 30-day return times prior-30-day over last-30-day mean volume; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: volume over the last 30 days against the 30 days before -> kept: the ratio of the two 30-day mean volumes.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 5 days, more so the deeper the fall and the thinner the volume -> kept: a short graded by that product, held for 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own interaction of fall depth and volume shrink, so its result on the next 5 days confirms or rejects the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, ThinVolumeScaledReturnComponent, read on daily bars inside the falling calm profile. Its raw value is the 30-day return, in the coin's usual 30-day moves over the past 365 days, times the mean volume of days t-59..t-30 over the mean volume of the last 30 days. The value is standardised against its own past 395 values. It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so the book is short only. The forecast is 0 when the coin did not fall over 30 days. It is negative after a fall and grows in size with the depth of the fall. It grows further as volume thins (the volume factor is above 1) and shrinks as volume swells (the volume factor is below 1). It is capped at -20.

Criterion:

- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window and keep the cells with a nonzero forecast. On those cells, take the slope of the coin's next 5-bar return on the forecast. The return is taken minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows, before costs. The slope should be positive, since a more negative forecast should be followed by a lower return.
- Supporting check: On grid cells with a forecast of -2 or below, the mean of minus the excess 5-bar return (the gain of a short against holding) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check's mean is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: deeper, thinner-volume falls are followed by relatively higher returns.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a slope t of 2 or more with the check failing, is inconclusive. Fewer than 100 grid cells with a nonzero forecast is also inconclusive.
