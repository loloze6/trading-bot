Forecast: The forecast is active only while the close sits in the top quarter of its 20-bar closing range. That is the "at or near a new high" condition, set as a regime: the raw DonchianBreakout(20) value is at least 10, which means the position in the range is at least 0.75. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is −(P + R) / 2, built from two parts. P is DonchianBreakout(20), standardised over its past 60 values; it is positive and near its cap of +20 here, because the close is near the top of its range. R is RSIPullback(14), which is (50 − RSI) and positive when RSI is below 50, standardised the same way. A new high with strong RSI (RSI well above 50) gives R near −P, so the forecast is near 0 and there is no divergence. A new high with weak or middling RSI gives R near 0 or positive, so the forecast is short, down to about −20. The short grows smoothly as RSI weakens relative to where price sits in its range. A long appears only in the rare case where R is more negative than P.

Confirm: Pool all regime bars over the 3 coins and 6 windows, sampled every 12 bars so that no 12-bar return is counted twice. The 12-bar forward return of the close after strong-divergence bars (forecast ≤ −5) must average at least 0.3 percentage points below the return after no-divergence bars (new highs with forecast > −2), with t ≤ −2. As a supporting check, the config's before-cost return must beat holding the coin over the same regime bars in at least 4 of the 6 windows on pooled coins.
Reject: The difference in mean 12-bar forward return between strong-divergence and no-divergence bars is zero or positive, or its t is above −1, with the same non-overlapping sampling. In that case moves after divergences look no different from moves after new highs without divergence.
Inconclusive: A difference that is negative but has t between −2 and −1. A difference of at least 0.3 points that holds on only one coin. Fewer than 100 non-overlapping strong-divergence samples in the pool.
Deviation: "RSI makes a lower high than at the previous price high" becomes "RSI is weak relative to the close's position near the top of its 20-bar range". The catalogue cannot store the RSI value at the previous swing high, so the comparison is against the RSI's current level, not its prior peak. "A new high" becomes "the close is in the top quarter of its 20-bar range". "Price then falls back" is tested while the condition holds: the position closes as soon as the close leaves the top quarter, so a retracement that continues after that is seen only in the 12-bar forward-return measure, not in the portfolio. Only the bearish side, at highs, is tested; the mirror case at lows is not.
