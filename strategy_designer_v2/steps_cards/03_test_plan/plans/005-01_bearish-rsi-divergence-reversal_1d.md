Forecast: Price's place in its 20-day range is set against the strength of 14-day RSI momentum, and the position takes the side of momentum wherever the two disagree. Two daily indicators are used. (a) Where the close sits inside its 20-bar range of closes: DonchianBreakoutComponent, period 20, which reads +20 when the close is at a new 20-day high and -20 at a new 20-day low. (b) RSI(14) momentum: RSIPullbackComponent, period 14, negated, which reads (RSI - 50) x 0.4. Each is standardised against its own previous 60 values (target 10, cap 20). The forecast is the equal-weight mean of (b) minus (a): (RSIstd - RANGEstd) / 2. When price is at a 20-day high and RSI is just as strong, the two cancel and the forecast is near 0. When price is at the high but RSI is weaker than its usual size, the forecast goes negative (short). It grows more negative as the gap between price strength and RSI strength widens, down to -20 at most. The mirror case, price at a 20-day low with RSI not as weak, gives a positive (long) forecast. The forecast is zero only when the two readings agree, and its size follows the size of the disagreement.

Confirm: The test uses bars where price is near a new high: the recorded raw range_pos value is at or below -16, meaning the close is in the top 10% of its 20-day range. Each such cluster is counted once, at its first bar, with clusters at least 10 bars apart, and all coins and windows are pooled. On those bars, the mean 10-bar forward return of the coin, before costs, must be at least 2 percentage points lower when the forecast is ≤ -4 (divergent) than when it is > -4 (momentum confirms). This needs at least 15 independent divergent episodes, and the gap must have the same sign in at least 2 of the 3 coins. Supporting check: over all bars with a negative forecast, the coin's mean 10-bar forward return is below its unconditional mean 10-bar return over the same windows, so the short side beats simply holding.
Reject: Under the same counting, with at least 15 divergent episodes, the divergent group's mean 10-bar forward return is equal to or higher than the confirmed group's. Rejection also follows if the 2-point gap fails to appear in at least 2 of the 3 coins.
Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected if there are fewer than 15 independent divergent episodes. The same holds if the divergent group is lower by between 0 and 2 percentage points.
Deviation: The idea compares two price peaks with the two RSI readings at those peaks. The config cannot do that. Instead it measures continuously, on every bar, whether price's place in its 20-day range is stronger than RSI's level relative to its own recent size. So "RSI lower than at the previous high" becomes "RSI weaker than usual while price is at the top of its range". The forecast is also non-zero away from new highs, including mirror-image longs at new lows (which the brief covers but this idea does not). It is also non-zero in mid-range disagreements; the criterion restricts the judgement to near-high bars. The idea's "after the divergence" becomes "while the divergence lasts".
