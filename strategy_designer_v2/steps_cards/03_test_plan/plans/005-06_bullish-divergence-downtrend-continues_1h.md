Forecast: Run the RSI-divergence-low condition as a regime. A bar is a "new low" when the close sits in the bottom 5% of its 48-bar close range, meaning a raw DonchianBreakoutComponent(period 48, sf 20) reading of -18 or below. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). On a new-low bar the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two standardised components (window 60, min_periods 20): (a) the Donchian 48 range position, which reads about -16 to -20 at a new low; (b) RSIPullbackComponent(14), which is 50 - RSI and is large when RSI is deeply oversold. When price is at a new low and RSI is equally washed out (no divergence), (a) and (b) cancel and the forecast is near 0. When price is at a new low but RSI stays high (RSI has not made its low: the divergence), (b) shrinks and the forecast becomes short, down to about -10 when RSI is near 50 and below -10 if RSI is above 50. The short grows steadily with how far RSI sits above its oversold reading while price is at its low. A small positive value (about +2 at most) can appear when RSI is extremely oversold at a shallow new low.

Confirm: Use a horizon of 24 bars. Take new-low regime bars, sampled at most once per 24 bars so overlapping returns count once, and pool them over the 3 coins and 6 windows. The idea is confirmed if the forward 24-bar return, minus the coin's mean 24-bar return over the window, is lower after the strongest-divergence tercile (most negative forecast) than after the weakest-divergence tercile (forecast near 0). The gap must be at least 0.3 percentage points, with t ≥ 2. Supporting check, before costs: the strategy's in-regime gross return beats the drift-adjusted return of simply holding the coin on those bars.
Reject: The idea is rejected if the strongest-divergence tercile's drift-adjusted forward 24-bar return is equal to or higher than the weakest tercile's, with the difference not below zero at t ≤ -1. That is, new lows with divergence are followed by no more decline than new lows without it, or by a rebound.
Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected if the gap goes in the predicted direction but is smaller than 0.3 points or has 1 < |t| < 2, if fewer than 60 non-overlapping regime samples exist in total, or if the sign holds in fewer than 4 of the 6 windows.
Deviation: The config does not compare RSI's current low with its earlier low. It approximates "RSI makes a higher low" by RSI's level being high relative to price's position at the bottom of its 48-bar range. It also tests "while" price sits at a new low rather than "after" the divergence forms, and it gives a slight long on new lows where RSI is unusually oversold.
