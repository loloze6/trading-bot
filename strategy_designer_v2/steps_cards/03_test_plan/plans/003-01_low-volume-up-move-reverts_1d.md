Forecast: The forecast is computed on every daily bar, but it is non-zero only on a day whose volume is below its 20-day average volume, which is the low-volume condition. On such a day the forecast is a short position sized by that day's upward move. Take the 1-bar close-to-close return of the day, in percent. Standardise it against its last 60 values (min_periods 20), so a typical absolute daily move is worth 10. Negate it, then apply signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10, so small moves are damped towards 0 and sharp ones are amplified. Then clip at a maximum of 0, so only up-moves give a position. The result is a short that grows with the square of how unusually large the up day was, held to that day's close, then re-evaluated. The forecast is zero on down days and on any day whose volume is at or above its 20-day average. It never goes long.
Config: 003-01_low-volume-up-move-reverts_1d_config.json
Confirm: Pool the active bars (forecast < 0) from all 6 windows; each is held for 1 bar, so they do not overlap. The position-weighted mean next-bar return, sum(|position_t| × r_{t+1}) / sum|position_t|, must be below the coin's mean daily return over all bars of the same windows. The difference must have a t-stat ≤ -2, computed on at least 30 active bars. Supporting check: the strategy's before-cost profit on those bars is positive in at least 4 of the 6 windows.
Reject: The rejection is the same measure going the other way. The weighted next-bar return after low-volume up days is at or above the coin's all-bar mean daily return (difference ≥ 0). The short strategy's before-cost profit is also zero or negative in at least 3 of the 6 windows.
Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected if any of these hold: - the difference is negative but its t-stat is between -2 and 0; - fewer than 30 active bars occur; - the sign of the difference holds but the window count of the supporting check disagrees.
Deviation: The config differs from the idea as written in five ways: - "Unusually low volume" becomes a yes/no condition: the day's volume is below its 20-day average, which the average itself includes. How low the volume is does not size the position. - "Sharp" is graded by the size of the single daily move, damped by the squaring, rather than set by a threshold. - "Followed by price falling back" is tested only over the one day after the move. A slower reversion over several days is not captured. - The position is not compared with high-volume moves of similar size, which is the brief's contrast. - In engine terms, a "low-volume day" is a bar whose volume_filter output is 0; the veto sends that bar to the mean_reversion regime.
