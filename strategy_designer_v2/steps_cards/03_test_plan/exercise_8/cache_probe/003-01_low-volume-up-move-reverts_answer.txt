forecast: On each daily bar, the regime check asks whether today's volume is below its own 20-bar average volume. If it is not, the forecast is 0 (flat). If it is, the forecast is set from today's one-bar close-to-close percent move. That move is standardised against the mean absolute size of the 60 previous one-bar moves (target 10, cap 20), sign-flipped, and clipped at a maximum of 0. The result: a low-volume up day gives a short position that grows in proportion to the size of the rise, from 0 for a tiny rise to -20 (200% short) for a rise twice the usual daily move or more. A low-volume down day or an unchanged day gives 0. The position therefore holds a short only over the day after a thin-volume rise, and it closes as soon as the next bar does not meet the condition.

confirm: Horizon is 1 bar. The main measure is excess return: the coin's next-bar return on active bars (forecast < 0) minus the mean next-bar return over all bars, pooled over the 6 windows, before costs. Each bar's return is counted once, since 1-bar returns do not overlap. The idea is confirmed if this excess is negative with a t-statistic of -2 or lower. It is also confirmed if the same sign appears in the supporting check: the active bars with the largest third of position sizes have a more negative mean next-bar excess return than the smallest third.
reject: The idea is rejected if the pooled mean next-bar excess return on active bars is zero or positive (t ≥ 0). It is also rejected if the largest third of positions show a less negative excess than the smallest third, together with a t-statistic above -1.
inconclusive: The test is inconclusive if the excess is negative with a t-statistic between -2 and -1. It is also inconclusive if there are fewer than 30 active bars across all windows, or if the sign of the main measure and of the supporting check disagree.
deviation: "Unusually low volume" becomes "volume below its 20-day average", which is an on/off gate not graded by how low the volume is. "A sharp upward move" becomes the size of today's close-to-close rise, scaled against recent daily moves. "Followed by price falling back" is tested only over the next one day, not over a longer giving-back period. The brief's contrast with high-volume moves is not part of this idea and is not tested.
