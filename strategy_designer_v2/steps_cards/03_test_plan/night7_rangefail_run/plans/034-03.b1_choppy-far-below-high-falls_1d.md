Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the daily close sits below its 20-day high -> kept: the close's distance under the prior 20-day closing high, zero at or above it; code adds the choppy profile.
- Comparison: depth counted in the coin's own daily swings over the last 20 days -> kept: the distance divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 5 days, more the deeper the slip -> kept: a short whose size grows with the depth, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's depth-in-swings reading, short-only and graded, inside the profile the idea names.
Forecast: The forecast uses `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` with period 20 and vol_period 20. Its reading is (close − the highest close of the prior 20 days) / that high / the std of the last 20 daily returns, so it is the distance from the high counted in daily swings. The reading is standardised over its past 60 values, so a usual reading is about 10, capped at 20. A clip at max 0 keeps only the below-high side. The forecast is 0 when the close is at or above the prior 20-day high. Below the high it is negative (short) and grows in size the more swings the close stands below the high. It is never positive. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, take the bars with forecast < 0. On each, compute the coin's 5-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows. Measure the least-squares slope of that drift-adjusted return on the forecast. A positive slope means a deeper slip (a more negative forecast) brings a larger fall. Cases of the same day are summed for the t.
- Supporting check: Split the grid cases into thirds by |forecast|. The deepest third must have a negative mean drift-adjusted 5-bar return.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the deepest third's mean drift-adjusted return is below 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ −2: deeper slips are followed by relatively higher returns.
- Inconclusive: Every other result is inconclusive, including a slope with |t| < 2, or a confirming slope where the deepest third's mean is not below 0. The measure needs at least 100 grid cases on at least 50 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
