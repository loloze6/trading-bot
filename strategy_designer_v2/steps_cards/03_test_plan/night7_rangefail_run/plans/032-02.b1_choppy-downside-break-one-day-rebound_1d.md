Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the daily close is below the lowest low of the prior 20 days -> kept: a regime rule holds the book only while the close is under min(low[t-20..t-1]); the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the further the close sits below that low, the larger the rise -> kept: inside the regime, the forecast grows with the close's percent depth under the prior 20-day low.
- Outcome: price rises over the next day -> kept: long, held for 1 bar, so the position is gone before the following days.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys exactly the break and sizes it by its depth, so a next-day rebound that grows with depth confirms the idea, and a next-day fall rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast uses DistanceFromHighComponent with period 20, source "high", extreme "low", exclude_current 1 and form "percent". This is the close's percent distance from the lowest low of the prior 20 days, negative below that low. The regime detector sends a bar to mean_reversion only when this value is below 0, which is a close under the prior 20-day low. Every other bar is flat. Inside that regime, the same measure is standardised over its past 60 values, clipped at a maximum of 0 and negated. The forecast is therefore positive (long) and grows linearly with the depth of the close under the low. It starts near 0 at the edge of the low and is capped at 20. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a bar with forecast > 0. For each case, take the coin's next-day return (close[t+1]/close[t] - 1) minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. The measure is the mean of this unusual move over the cases, with its t, counting the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The least-squares slope of the next-day unusual move on the case's forecast must be positive: larger breaks, larger rebound.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is > 0 with t ≥ 2.0, and the supporting slope is > 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is < 0 with t ≤ -2.0.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2.0 and 2.0 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2.0 with a slope ≤ 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 cases, counted as distinct days, across all coins and windows.
