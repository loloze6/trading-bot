Fidelity:
- Situation: in a rising market, a daily close above its 20-day high, in 20-day daily swings -> kept: the profile is added by code, the close above the prior 20-day closing high is a regime, and the size is graded by the distance in usual moves.
- Comparison: further above the high against less far above -> kept: the position grows linearly with the distance.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 3 days -> kept: long only, held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures exactly whether rises over 3 days grow with the volatility-scaled distance above the 20-day high in rising markets.
Forecast: The forecast uses `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` with period 20 and vol_period 20. It is (close − highest close of the prior 20 days) / that high / std of the last 20 daily returns, so it measures the distance above the high in usual daily moves. The regime detector selects "trending" only when this distance is above 0, meaning the close stands above its prior 20-day high. On every other bar the book is flat, and the code's rising-profile vetoes add a further flat condition. Inside the regime, the same distance is standardised (window 60, min_periods 20) and clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore positive, starts near 0 just above the high and grows linearly the further the close stands above it. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bars on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window with forecast > 0. For each case, take the 3-bar forward return minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift (its mean over all windows). The measure is the forecast-weighted mean of these excess returns, with its t computed with same-day cases pooled.
- Supporting check: Among the cases, the least-squares slope of the 3-bar excess return on the forecast is positive.
- Confirm: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope on the forecast is positive.
- Reject: The forecast-weighted mean excess return is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 with a slope that is not positive. The test is also inconclusive with fewer than 30 cases on distinct days.
