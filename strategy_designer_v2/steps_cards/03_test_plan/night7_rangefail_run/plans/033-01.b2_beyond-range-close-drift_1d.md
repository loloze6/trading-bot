Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close's position against the high-to-low range of the 20 days before it -> replaced: no listed component reads the prior 20-day high/low position.
- Comparison: higher against lower positions in that range, beyond the top and below the bottom -> kept: graded across and past the range.
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next 5 days the higher the close sits -> kept: a 5-bar forward return, long and short.
- Verdict transfer: no: the catalogue's listed components cannot measure the position against the prior bars' highs and lows.
Reason: `DonchianBreakoutComponent`'s listed params are only `period` and `scaling_factor`. It reads closes with the current bar included, so it gives neither the high-to-low range of the 20 prior days nor a value past the range's edges. No weighted mean of the listed components can divide by the range's width. `DistanceFromHighComponent` and `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent` give distances from one edge only.
