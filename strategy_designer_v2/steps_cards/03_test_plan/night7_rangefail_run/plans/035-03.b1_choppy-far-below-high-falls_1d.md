Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close many daily swings below the coin's 20-day high, in a choppy market -> kept: a regime on the close's distance below the prior 20-day closing high, in 20-day swings, at 2 or more swings (convention); the choppy profile is added by code
- Comparison: the fall is larger the more swings below the high -> kept: the short position is sized by the depth in swings
- Outcome: price falls over the next 5 days -> kept: horizon 5 daily bars, short side
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts deep-below-high closes, sized by depth, in choppy markets only, and judges the next 5 days' fall against the coin's own drift.
Forecast: The forecast uses `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` with period 20 and vol_period 20. This is the close's distance from the highest close of the prior 20 days, counted in usual daily moves (the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns). The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only when that distance is -2 or lower, meaning the close sits 2 or more swings below the high. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside the regime the same distance is standardised over 60 bars (min 20). It is always negative there, so the forecast is a short. The short grows in size the more swings the close sits below the high, and is capped at -20. Code adds the choppy profile, so the book is flat outside a flat 60-day trend.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: A case is a short bet: the forecast falls by at least 2 points below 0, read once per non-overlapping 5-bar grid cell of each window. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift (all windows together), with the sign flipped to the short direction. Measure the mean of this excess fall and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean excess fall of cases with |forecast| above the cases' median must be greater than that of cases at or below the median (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean excess fall is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean excess fall is below 0 with t <= -2, meaning the coin rises more than its drift after deep-below-high closes.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t >= 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. So is a test with fewer than 30 independent case days across coins and windows.
