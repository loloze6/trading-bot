Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close above its 20-day high -> kept: a regime holds only bars whose close is above the prior 20 bars' highest high.
- Comparison: the further the close sits above that high, the larger the rise -> adapted: the forecast is graded by the percent distance above the prior 20-day high.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 5 days, with the rise completed within them -> kept: each bet is held 5 bars, and a position opens only on bars that make a fresh break.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the 5-day move after each break against the coin's drift, and checks that it grows with the break's distance.
Forecast: One indicator, `DistanceFromHighComponent` with `period` 20, `source` "high", `exclude_current` 1 and `form` "percent". It gives the close's percent distance from the highest high of the prior 20 bars. The regime detector reads the same quantity and sends a bar to `trending` only when it is above 0, meaning the close broke the 20-day high. Every other bar is flat. Inside `trending`, the signed distance is standardised against its own past 60 values, which are mostly negative and so set a wide scale. It is then clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore 0 at the old high and grows linearly with how far the close sits above it. For example, a break by the distance's usual size gives about +10, and the forecast is capped at +20. It is never negative. On later bars without a fresh break the forecast returns to 0. The 5-bar holding period keeps each bet for the 5 days after its break and then lets it go.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a long bet, one per 5-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's return from the case bar's close over 5 bars, inside the window, minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows. Report the mean, its day-clustered t, and the number of days.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of their forecast. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: Mean unusual move above 0 with t >= 2 on at least 30 case-days, and the larger-half minus smaller-half difference above 0.
- Reject: Mean unusual move with t <= -2 on at least 30 case-days, or the larger-half minus smaller-half difference with Welch t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or t >= 2 with the half difference at or below 0 but its Welch t above -2, or fewer than 30 case-days.
