Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close's place in its own 20-day high-to-low range -> kept: the close's position between the highest and lowest close of the last 20 days, graded from bottom to top.
- Comparison: the coin's following moves beyond its usual drift -> kept: forward returns are measured minus the coin's normal drift.
- Outcome: over the next 5 days, higher in the range rises more and lower falls more, graded through the range -> kept: 5-bar forward return against the signed, graded range position.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the range position itself, long in the upper half and short in the lower half, so its graded link to 5-day excess returns confirms or rejects the idea directly.
Forecast: The forecast is the position of the daily close within its last 20 daily closes (DonchianBreakoutComponent, period 20, closes only). It runs from -sf at the lowest close to +sf at the highest close and is 0 at the middle. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20). It is positive and grows toward +20 as the close nears the top of the range. It is negative and grows toward -20 as the close nears the bottom. It is ungated, so it is active on every bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The least-squares slope of each coin's 5-bar forward return, minus its normal drift (its mean 5-bar return over all windows), on the forecast at the signal bar. Cases are pooled over coins on each window's non-overlapping 5-bar grid. The t is computed with same-day cases summed into one.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 5-bar return of cases with forecast above +10, minus that of cases with forecast below -10, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2: closes high in the range are followed by relative falls and closes low in the range by relative rises.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with -2 < t < 2, or a positive slope with t >= 2 that fails the check. At least 500 grid cases on at least 100 distinct days are required; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
