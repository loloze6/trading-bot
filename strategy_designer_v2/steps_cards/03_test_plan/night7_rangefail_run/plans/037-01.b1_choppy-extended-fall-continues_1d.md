Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 5-day fall spans many of its own 20-day daily swings -> kept: the 5-day return divided by the 20-day std of daily returns, short side only, graded by size.
- Comparison: only while its 20-day return is also a fall, in a choppy market -> kept: a regime rule on the 20-day return below 0; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Outcome: the coin falls more over the next 5 days, more after larger falls -> kept: a short held 5 bars, sized by the fall in swing units.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the stated fall in swing units, only inside the stated 20-day fall, and shorts for 5 days.
Forecast: When the coin's 20-day return is below 0 (inside the choppy profile), the forecast is built from the 5-day return divided by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns (times √5, a constant that standardise cancels). This value is standardised over its last 60 values and clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is therefore 0 after a 5-day rise and negative (short) after a 5-day fall, larger in size the more swings the fall spans, capped at -20. When the 20-day return is 0 or above, the forecast is 0.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, with its t (cases of one day taken together). A case is a short bet, read once per 5-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows, taken in the short direction.
- Supporting check: Across cases, the slope of the short-direction unusual move on the size of the forecast at the case bar must be above 0, so that larger falls are followed by larger declines.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move has t ≥ 2 and the supporting slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ -2, meaning prices rise after these falls more than the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0. A verdict also needs at least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days.
