Fidelity:
- Situation: in a rising market, a coin's daily close sits below its 20-day high, in units of its 20-day daily-return std -> kept: `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent` (period 20, vol_period 20) gives the distance to the prior 20-day closing high in usual daily moves; the code adds the rising profile.
- Comparison: the closer to the high, the larger the rise -> kept: the forecast grows with nearness, centred on the coin's usual distance, signed and graded.
- Outcome: price rises more over the next 10 days -> kept: 10-day forward return, 10-bar horizon.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast's rank is the idea's nearness measure inside the rising profile, so a return slope on it confirms or rejects the idea itself.
Forecast: The forecast starts from the distance of the close from the prior 20-day closing high, in 20-day daily standard deviations. This distance is usually negative, 0 at the high and positive above it. It is standardised over 60 bars, so a usual distance reads about -10. It is averaged with a standardised constant of +10 at equal weight, giving (x + 10) / 2: - positive, and long, when the close is nearer the high than usual or above it, growing as it nears and passes the high (up to +15); - negative, and short, when the close is further below the high than usual (down to about -5); - about 0 at the usual distance. The book holds nothing outside the rising profile, which the code adds.
