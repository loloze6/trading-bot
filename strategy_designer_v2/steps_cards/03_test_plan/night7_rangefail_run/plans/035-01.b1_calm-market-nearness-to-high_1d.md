Fidelity:
- Situation: in a calm market, how many daily swings the daily close sits below its 20-day high -> kept: the close's distance from the prior 20-day high in 20-day daily-return standard deviations; the calm profile is added by code.
- Comparison: fewer swings below the high (or above it) against more swings below -> kept: graded, centred on the coin's usual distance, so nearer than usual is long and further than usual is short.
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next 5 days -> kept: the 5-bar forward return against the coin's normal drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast grows with nearness to the high in swing units, so a 5-bar return that rises with it confirms the idea and one that falls with it rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent with period 20 and vol_period 20. It measures (close − highest close of the prior 20 bars) / that high / the std of the last 20 daily returns. The result is mostly negative and positive above the prior high. It is standardised over 60 bars (min_periods 20), which gives about −10 at a usual distance. It is averaged with equal weight with the constant BuyAndHold, standardised to +10. The forecast is therefore (x + 10) / 2: - It is 0 when the close sits at its usual swing-distance below the high. - It is positive and grows as the close nears or breaks above the high, up to +15 at the cap. - It is negative and grows as the close sits further below the high than usual, down to −5. The calm profile keeps it flat outside calm bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope t of the coin's 5-bar forward return, minus its normal 5-bar drift, regressed on the forecast. It uses non-overlapping 5-bar grid bars in each window where `near_high` is recorded (in profile), pooled over coins, with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean excess 5-bar return of the top tercile of forecast minus that of the bottom tercile must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the top-minus-bottom tercile difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is t ≥ 2 with a tercile difference at or below 0. At least 200 grid cases on at least 100 distinct days are required; with fewer cases the result is inconclusive.
