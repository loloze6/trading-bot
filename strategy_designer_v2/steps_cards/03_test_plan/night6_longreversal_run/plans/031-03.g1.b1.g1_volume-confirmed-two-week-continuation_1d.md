Forecast: The regime detector reads mean volume over the last 14 days divided by mean volume over the last 30 days. - At or above 1 (trending regime): the forecast is the 14-day return, scaled by the mean absolute 14-day return over the past 365 days. It is then standardised, so it is long after a rise, short after a fall, and larger the bigger the move and the stronger the volume swell. - Below 1 (mean_reversion regime): the forecast is the same scaled 14-day return multiplied by the earlier 16 days' mean volume over the last 14 days' mean. It is standardised and then negated, so it is short after a rise, long after a fall, and larger the bigger the move and the deeper the dry-up. - Otherwise: the forecast is 0 only before the strategy is ready. - Size: it is continuous in the return and the volume ratio, capped at ±20.
