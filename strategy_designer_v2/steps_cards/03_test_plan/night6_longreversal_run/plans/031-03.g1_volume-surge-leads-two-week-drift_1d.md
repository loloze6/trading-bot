- Situation: the coin's 14-day mean daily volume above or below its mean, more or less so -> kept
- Comparison: against its own 30-day mean daily volume -> kept
- Outcome: price rises over the next 14 days when volume swelled, falls when it dried up, more with a larger gap -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded volume ratio, it is held for the idea's 14 days, and it is judged on the 14-day move.
Forecast: The forecast is one component, VolumeRatioComponent. It takes the mean volume of the last 14 daily bars (today included) and divides it by the mean volume of the last 30 bars (today included), then takes the natural log. It is 0 when the two means are equal. It is positive when recent volume is above the 30-day mean and negative when below. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), so its size grows with the gap and averages about 10 in absolute value, capped at ±20. No regime is used: the idea is a steady relation on every bar, long or short.
