- Situation: 14-day mean daily volume above the 30-day mean, while the close is above its 50-day mean close -> adapted: the 50-day condition is a regime, and inside it the position is graded by the volume swell (14-day mean / 30-day mean − 1).
- Comparison: the 14-day mean volume against the 30-day mean volume, and the close against its 50-day mean -> kept
- Outcome: price rises over the next 14 days, more with a larger swell -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own swell, long only while the close is above the 50-day mean, judged on the next 14 days' rise.
Forecast: A regime detector reads the close's percent distance from its 50-day SMA. When the distance is above 0 the bar is `trending`; every other bar is `unknown` and flat. In `trending`, the forecast comes from the volume ratio, which is the mean volume of the last 14 days over the mean volume of the last 30 days, both including today, centred so that 0 means equal. The ratio is standardised against its own past 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20), which keeps its sign. It is then clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore 0 when the 14-day volume is at or below the 30-day volume. It is positive and grows continuously with the swell, up to the cap of 20. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 14 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the bars of each window's non-overlapping 14-bar grid where the regime is `trending`. On each of these bars, take the coin's 14-bar forward return minus that coin's mean 14-bar return over all its windows. Regress this excess return on the bar's forecast (0 to 20). The measure is the t of that slope, with cases on the same day clustered together.
- Supporting check: Take the grid cases with forecast above 0. Their mean excess 14-bar return, in the idea's direction, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with the supporting check failing is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 100 grid cases with forecast above 0, or fewer than 300 `trending` grid cases in total.
