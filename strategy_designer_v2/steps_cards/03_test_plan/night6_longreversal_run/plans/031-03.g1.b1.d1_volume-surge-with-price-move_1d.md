Fidelity:
- Situation: 14-day mean volume above (below) its 30-day mean, while the 14-day return is up (down) -> kept: the two agreeing signs are the regimes, and the volume excess grades the position inside them.
- Comparison: the 14-day mean daily volume against the 30-day mean daily volume -> kept: VolumeRatioComponent with short_period 14 and period 30, centred on 1.
- Outcome: price rises (falls) over the next 14 days, more the larger the volume excess -> kept: the book holds 14 bars, long in the up case and short in the down case.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's graded volume excess, active only when the idea's sign agreement holds, so a confirm or reject of its forward moves is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads two raw values. The first is the 14-bar return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 14). The second is the 14-day over 30-day mean volume ratio minus 1 (VolumeRatioComponent, period 30, short_period 14, form "centred"). - When both are above 0, the bar is "trending". The forecast is that volume excess standardised (window 60, min_periods 20). It is positive and grows from 0 as the 14-day volume rises above its 30-day mean, so the book goes long. - When both are below 0, the bar is "mean_reversion". The same standardised value is negative, and grows in size as volume falls further below its 30-day mean, so the book goes short. - When the two disagree, the bar is "unknown" and the forecast is 0 (flat).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 14 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual move over the cases, with its day-clustered t. A case is a bet: a rise of the forecast on its side of at least 2 points, read once per 14-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 14-bar return from the signal bar, minus the coin's normal 14-bar drift over all windows, taken in the signal's direction.
- Supporting check: The cases with |forecast| at or above the median have a larger mean unusual move than the cases below the median (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t strictly between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails. The minimum is 30 cases falling on at least 20 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
