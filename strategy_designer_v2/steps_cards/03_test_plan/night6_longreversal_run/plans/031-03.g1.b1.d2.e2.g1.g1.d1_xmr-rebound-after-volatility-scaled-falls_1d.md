It is standardised over 210 bars (min_periods 52) and then negated, so a fall reads positive. It is clipped at 0, so the book is long only. On an up or flat day the forecast is 0. On a down day it is positive and grows linearly with the fall's depth in usual moves, and with how far the 30-day volatility stands above its six-month mean. It is capped at +20.
