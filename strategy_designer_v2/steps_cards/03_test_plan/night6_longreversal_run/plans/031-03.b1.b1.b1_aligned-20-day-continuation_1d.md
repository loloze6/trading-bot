Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 20-day return, larger is stronger, only while its 60-day return is on the same side -> kept: the 20-day and 60-day returns having the same sign is a regime, and inside it the position is graded by the 20-day return
- Comparison: a 60-day return on the same side versus a 60-day return against the 20-day move -> kept: when the signs disagree the regime is null, so the book is flat
- Outcome: over the next 10 days, price moves further in the 20-day move's direction -> kept: the horizon is 10 bars, long after a rise and short after a fall
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config holds exactly the idea's conditioned, graded continuation bet over its 10-day horizon, so a confirm or reject of it is a confirm or reject of the idea
Forecast: The regime detector computes the 20-day percent return and the 60-day percent return. - Bars where both returns are above 0, or both are below 0, are `trending`. All other bars are `unknown`, and `unknown` is flat. - In `trending`, the forecast is the 20-day return from PriceEvolutionComponent (period 20), standardised against its own past 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20). - The forecast is positive after a 20-day rise and negative after a 20-day fall. - Its size grows linearly with the size of the 20-day return relative to its usual size: about ±10 at a usual move, capped at ±20. - It is zero whenever the 60-day return points the other way, and near zero when the 20-day return is near zero.
Config: 031-03.b1.b1.b1_aligned-20-day-continuation_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars on a non-overlapping 10-bar grid in each window (positions 0, 10, 20, ...) where the forecast is not 0. For each case, take forecast/10 × (the coin's 10-bar forward return − the coin's mean 10-bar return over all its windows). The measure is the mean of this value across cases, with its t. Cases from the same day are summed into one piece of evidence before the t is computed.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean drift-removed forward return in the forecast's direction is larger for cases with |forecast| above the median than for cases below it (Welch difference above 0).
- Confirm: The main measure is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A main t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. At least 100 grid cases spread over at least 50 distinct days are required; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
