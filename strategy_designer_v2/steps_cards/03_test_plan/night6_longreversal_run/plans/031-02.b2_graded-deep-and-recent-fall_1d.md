Forecast: The forecast is zero unless the coin's 90-day return is at or below minus one usual 90-day move. The usual move is the mean absolute 90-day return over the last 365 days. Inside that fall, the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two parts. The first is the negated scaled 90-day return, standardised; it is positive and larger the deeper the fall. The second is the negated 30-day percent return, standardised and clipped at 0; it is positive and larger the further the last month sits below zero, and 0 when the last month rose. The forecast is always zero or positive (long only), and it grows with both falls.
