Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 20-day return, the larger the stronger -> kept
- Comparison: larger 20-day return against smaller, graded -> kept
- Outcome: price moves further in the same direction over the next 10 days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the 20-day return itself and is judged on the 10-day move after it, so a confirm or a reject carries straight to the idea.
Forecast: One component, the 20-bar percent return of the close (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 20). It is standardised against its own last 60 values (min_periods 20). There is no regime, so it is active on every bar. The forecast is positive after a 20-day rise and negative after a 20-day fall. It is zero when the 20-day return is zero. Its size grows linearly with the 20-day return relative to the coin's usual 20-day move, capped at ±20.
Config: 031-03.b1_short-horizon-continuation-after-moves_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window. Regress each case's 10-bar forward return, minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift (its mean over all windows), on the recorded forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with cases on the same day taken together (standard errors clustered by day).
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily return before costs, minus its gain from the market's own rise (the timing part), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the timing part of the book's mean daily return is positive.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less. Then larger 20-day moves are followed by moves back the other way.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a timing part at or below 0. At least 300 grid cases over at least 100 distinct days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
