- Situation: the coin's 90-day return stands below zero, in units of its usual 90-day move -> kept
- Comparison: against its own usual 90-day move, from its past volatility -> kept
- Outcome: the deeper the fall, the larger the rebound over the next 45 days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own vol-scaled 90-day fall, held for 45 days and judged on the 45-day rebound.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent`, with `period` 90 and `window` 365. It is the 90-day return divided by the mean absolute 90-day return over the past year, so it is counted in usual 90-day moves. It is standardised against its own past (window 455, min_periods 114). It is then negated and clipped at 0. The forecast is positive only when the 90-day return is negative, and it grows linearly with the depth of the fall in usual moves. It is 0 after a 90-day rise. It is never short.
