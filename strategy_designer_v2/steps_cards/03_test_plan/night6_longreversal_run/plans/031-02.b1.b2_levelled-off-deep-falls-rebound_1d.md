Fidelity:
- Situation: 90-day return far below the coin's own usual 90-day move -> kept: a regime rule puts the 90-day return, in mean absolute 90-day moves over 365 days, at -2 or lower; the -2 is a convention.
- Comparison: a smaller fall over the last 30 days against a larger one -> kept: the forecast grows with the 30-day return, in usual 30-day moves.
- Outcome: the price rebounds more over the next 30 days -> kept: 30-day forward return against the coin's drift, held 30 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures inside the deep-fall situation whether a smaller 30-day fall is followed by a larger 30-day rebound, which is the idea's own claim.
Forecast: The forecast exists only on bars where the coin's 90-day return is at least 2 mean absolute 90-day moves below zero, with the usual move measured over 365 days. On every other bar it is flat. Inside that situation the forecast is the mean of two parts at equal weight. The first is the 30-day return in mean absolute 30-day moves (365-day window), standardised against its own past. The second is a constant +10 offset. The forecast is therefore about 5 × (x + 1), where x is the 30-day return in usual 30-day moves. It is long, and larger, the smaller the last month's fall or the more it has levelled off or turned up. It is zero at a fall of about one usual 30-day move. It turns short when the coin is still dropping hard. It grows continuously with the 30-day return and is capped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 30 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars of the situation (regime mean_reversion) on a non-overlapping 30-bar grid per window. For each case, take the coin's 30-day forward return minus the coin's mean 30-day return over all windows. Split the cases at the median forecast of the situation's cases. The measure is the Welch t of the upper half minus the lower half.
- Supporting check: Situation cases with a forecast above 0 have a mean 30-day forward return above the coin's drift (the rebound exists).
- Confirm: Main-measure t ≥ +2 (the upper half rebounds more) and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: Main-measure t ≤ -2: coins whose last 30 days fell less rebound less than those still dropping.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive, as is t ≥ 2 with the supporting check failing. A test with fewer than 20 cases in either half, which is 40 situation cases in all, is inconclusive whatever its t.
