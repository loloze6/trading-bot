Fidelity:
- Situation: the 90-day return stands below the coin's own usual 90-day move -> adapted: a regime holds only while the 90-day return is below minus one usual 90-day move; inside it, the forecast grows with the depth of the fall.
- Comparison: the coin's own usual 90-day move, from its past volatility -> kept: the return is divided by the mean absolute 90-day return over the past 365 days.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 30 days more than its usual drift, the deepest falls the most -> kept: a long position held for 30 bars, judged against holding the coin.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this fall, against this yardstick, over this horizon, so a confirm or a reject on it carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses one measure, MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent (period 90, window 365): the 90-day return divided by the coin's mean absolute 90-day return over the past year. The regime detector sets the bar to mean_reversion only while this measure is at or below -1, a fall beyond one usual 90-day move. Every other bar is flat (forecast 0). Inside the regime, the same measure is negated, so a deeper fall reads larger and positive. It is then standardised against its own past values (window 455, min_periods 113) and passed through signed_power with exponent 2. This damps falls just beyond the usual and amplifies the deepest ones. The forecast is therefore long only: zero outside the deep-fall situation, and growing with the depth of the fall in usual moves.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 30 bars
- Main measure: The 30-day return after each case, minus the coin's mean 30-day return over all its windows. A case is a mean_reversion bar with a positive forecast on the non-overlapping 30-bar grid of each window. The t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Among those cases, the mean excess return of the half with the larger forecast minus that of the half with the smaller forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean excess 30-day return is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean excess 30-day return is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming t whose supporting check fails is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 independent case-days across coins and windows.
