Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin has risen over the last 14 days -> adapted: "after a rise" made "while the 14-day return is positive", kept as a regime condition.
- Comparison: the 14-day mean volume against its 30-day mean -> kept: the 14-bar mean volume over the 30-bar mean volume, graded from 0 above 1.
- Outcome: price rises over the next 14 days, more so with more volume -> kept: the 14-bar forward return against holding, graded by the volume swell.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this rise, this volume comparison and this horizon, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 14-bar price change (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 14). When it is above 0, the bar is `trending`; otherwise it is `unknown`, which is flat. In `trending`, one component measures the mean volume of the last 14 bars over the mean of the last 30 (`VolumeRatioComponent`, period 30, short_period 14, form "centred", which is ratio minus 1). It is standardised over 60 bars (min_periods 20), then clipped at 0. The forecast is 0 when the 14-day volume is at or below its 30-day mean. It grows continuously, long only, as the 14-day volume swells further above that mean, capped at 20. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 14 bars
- Main measure: Take the bars of the `trending` regime on a non-overlapping 14-bar grid in each window. On those bars, regress the coin's 14-bar forward return minus its mean 14-bar return over all windows (holding the coin) on the forecast. Report the slope's t, with cases of the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2. Larger volume swells behind a rise are then followed by larger 14-day excess rises.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2. Larger volume swells are then followed by smaller 14-day excess returns.
- Inconclusive: A slope with |t| < 2 is inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 100 grid cases with forecast above 0, or fewer than 200 grid cases in the regime.
