Fidelity:
- Situation: XMR's one-day return against its own 30-day daily volatility, the larger the stronger -> kept
- Comparison: a reversal larger after a fall than after a rise -> kept
- Outcome: price moves back the other way over the next day -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast fades each day's volatility-scaled move in proportion to its size and leans twice as hard after falls, so its next-day gains test both the reversal and its asymmetry.
Forecast: The forecast uses one quantity, z. z is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). - First component. z is standardised over its past 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20), then negated. It is short after a rise and long after a fall, and its size is proportional to the move. - Second component. The same z, standardised the same way, clipped at 0 from above so that only falls remain, then negated. It is long only after a fall, and its size grows with the fall. - Combination. The two have equal weights. After a fall the forecast is about the full standardised size, long. After a rise it is half of it, short. - Size. The forecast is near 0 on a quiet day and grows smoothly with the size of the move, up to the cap of ±20. - No gate. It is active on every bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On every bar, the excess gain is the position held after the bar's trade times (the next day's return minus XMR's mean daily return over all windows). The measure is the mean of this excess gain and its t, with returns kept inside each window.
- Supporting check: On bars where z < 0, regress the next-day return on z; do the same on bars where z > 0. The check passes if the fall-side reversal slope is larger in size than the rise-side slope, both being negative (reversal).
- Confirm: The mean excess gain has t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean excess gain has t ≤ -2: fading the large moves loses against the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: Either of these is inconclusive: - t between -2 and 2; - t ≥ 2 without the fall-side slope exceeding the rise-side slope. At least 500 bars with a non-zero position are required.
