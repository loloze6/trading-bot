Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a day closes below the middle of its range -> kept: the profile is added by code, and the forecast is 0 on days closing at or above the middle.
- Comparison: the close's distance below the middle, times the day's range against its 30-day swing -> kept: CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMovesComponent(30) computes exactly that product.
- Outcome: a rise over the next day, larger the further below the middle -> kept: long only, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own quantity, so a confirm or reject on next-day returns transfers to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is built from the close's distance from the day's mid-range, 2 x (close − (high+low)/2) / close, divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. That equals the close's signed position in the day's range times the day's range in 30-day swings. The value is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), negated so that a close below the middle reads positive, and clipped at 0 for long only. The forecast is 0 when the close is at or above the middle. Above that it grows linearly the deeper the close sits below the middle and the wider the day is against its usual swing. Step 3's code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Over the cases (a forecast rise of at least 2 points on the long side), measure the next-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Take the forecast-weighted mean, with its t computed with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Among bars with forecast > 0, the least-squares slope of the next-day excess return on the forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The main mean is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting slope is positive.
- Reject: The main mean is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: A t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0. Fewer than 200 cases, or cases on fewer than 100 distinct days, are also inconclusive whatever t is.
