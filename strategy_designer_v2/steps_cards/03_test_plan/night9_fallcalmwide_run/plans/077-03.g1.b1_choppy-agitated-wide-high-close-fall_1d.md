Fidelity:
- Situation: a day closing near its high, in a choppy agitated market -> kept: the close in the top quarter of the day's range is a regime condition, and the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the day's range against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns -> kept: RangeInReturnStdComponent with a 30-day window, graded.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day -> kept: a short held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the same situation, scaled by the same width, short for the same one day.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless the day closes in the top quarter of its range, meaning (close − low) / (high − low) is 0.75 or more. The 0.75 level is the usual "upper quarter" convention, since the brief gives no number. Inside that regime, the indicator is the day's range as a fraction of the close, divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (RangeInReturnStdComponent, vol_period 30). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20) and negated. The forecast is therefore always short there, about −10 for a day of usual width and growing toward −20 as the day widens against its usual swing. Outside the regime it is 0. The choppy-agitated profile is added by code as vetoes.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases in the short direction. A case is a short bet, read on the 1-bar grid. The unusual move is the next day's return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal daily drift over all its windows, with the sign flipped for the short. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean unusual move of the wider half minus that of the narrower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed only when both hold. The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2. The wider half's mean unusual move exceeds the narrower half's.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ −2, meaning the next day rises against the short beyond the drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2. Also a t of 2 or more where the wider half does not beat the narrower half. Any result with fewer than 50 cases, or with cases on fewer than 30 distinct days.
