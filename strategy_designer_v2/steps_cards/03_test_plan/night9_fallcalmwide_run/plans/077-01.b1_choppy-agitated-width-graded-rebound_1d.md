Forecast: The book is flat unless two conditions hold. First, the day's high-low range must be at least its 30-day mean bar range (`RangeCompressionRatioComponent`, short 1, long 30, mean_bar_range, width ≥ 1). Second, the close must sit in the lower half of the day's range (`CloseLocationInRangeComponent` "low" < 0.5). Inside that situation, the forecast is long. It is `RangeInReturnStdComponent` (vol_period 30), the day's range over the close divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns, standardised over 60 bars (min 20). It is always positive, about 10 for a usual width and up to 20 for twice the usual width. It grows continuously with how wide the day was against the 30-day swing. It never goes short, and it is zero outside the situation. The profile (choppy agitated) is added by code.

- Main measure: The cases are the bars with forecast > 0. For each case, take the coin's next-day return minus that coin's mean daily return over all windows. The measure is the mean of this excess return, with its t computed with cases of the same day summed into one deviation.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median forecast. The mean excess next-day return of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0, so that the rise grows with width.
- Confirm: The mean excess return is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes (upper half minus lower half > 0).
- Reject: The mean excess return is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a failed supporting check. At least 100 cases on at least 50 distinct days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
