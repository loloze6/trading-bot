Fidelity:
- Situation: in a rising calm market, a day with a high-to-low range wide against the 30-day swing of daily returns that closes near its low -> kept: a regime where range/close over the 30-day return std is at least 2 and the close sits in the bottom quarter of the range; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the nearer the close to the day's low, the larger the rise -> adapted: inside the regime the forecast is graded by the close's nearness to the low.
- Outcome: price rises over the next day -> kept: long, horizon 1 daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the same days are traded long and sized by nearness to the low, so a next-day rise confirms the idea and a next-day fall rejects it.
Forecast: The regime "mean_reversion" is active only on a day when both conditions hold: - the bar's range divided by the close, over the std of the last 30 daily returns (`RangeInReturnStdComponent`, 30), is at least 2; - the close's location from the high (`CloseLocationInRangeComponent`, "high") is at least 0.75, meaning the close sits in the bottom quarter of the day's range. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is built from the same close location: - it is standardised over 60 bars and then put through a graded dead zone (`signed_power` exponent 3, pivot 20); - a close at the edge of the bottom quarter gives about +8, and a close at the very low gives up to +20. The forecast is always long and grows with the nearness to the low.

- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: For each case (a rise of the forecast of at least the band), take the coin's next-day return minus its normal drift (its mean daily return over all windows), in the long direction. Report the mean of these unusual moves and its t, with the cases of one day pooled.

- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2 over at least 30 cases.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A positive mean with t ≥ 2 but a nearness difference at or below 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 cases.
