Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy, agitated market and a day closing near its high -> adapted: the profile is added by code; "near its high" is a regime (close in the top quarter of the day's range, the most common convention), graded inside it.
- Comparison: the day's range against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns -> kept: the range divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day, more the closer to the high and the wider the day -> kept: a one-day short, sized by closeness to the high times range in usual moves.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly these days for one day, graded as the idea states, so its result is the idea's.
Forecast: The regime detector reads where each day closes in its range (CloseLocationInRangeComponent, reference "high": 0 at the high, 1 at the low). A day with a value of 0.25 or less, meaning a close in the top quarter of its range, goes to the regime that holds the forecast. Every other day is flat, and so is every day outside the choppy agitated profile, which code adds. Inside the regime the forecast reads CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMovesComponent (vol_period 30). This is 2 x (close − mid-range) / close / the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns. It is positive above mid-range, and it grows as the close nears the high and as the day's range widens against the 30-day swing. The value is standardised over 60 bars, negated, and clipped at 0. The result is always a short in the regime, from 0 to −20, larger the closer the close is to the high and the wider the day. It is never long.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: For each case (a short bet on a bar), take the coin's next-day return minus its normal daily drift (its mean over all windows), in the short direction. Average over the cases, with a t computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at their median |forecast|. The mean next-day unusual move in the short direction of the larger half, minus that of the smaller half, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure is negative with t ≤ −2, meaning the next day rises against the short beyond the drift.
- Inconclusive: A t between −2 and 2. A confirmed main measure whose supporting difference is not above 0 is also inconclusive. Any result with fewer than 100 cases, or fewer than 60 distinct days, is inconclusive.
