Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a day closing below the middle of its range -> kept: the profile is added by code, and a negated, clipped close-off-mid reading is zero unless the close is below the middle.
- Comparison: the distance below the middle multiplied by the day's range against its 30-day swing -> kept: CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMovesComponent (vol_period 30) is exactly that product.
- Outcome: a rise over the next three days, larger the further below the middle and the wider the day -> kept: a long position held 3 bars, graded by that product.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own product, long only below the middle, inside the named profile, held for its three days.
Forecast: One component, CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMovesComponent with vol_period 30. It reads 2 x (close - mid-range) / close / s30, which is the close's signed position in the day's range times the day's range in usual 30-day one-bar moves. The value is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), then negated, so a close below the middle reads positive. It is then clipped at 0. The forecast is 0 when the day closes at or above its middle. Below the middle it is positive, and it grows continuously with how far below the middle the close sits and with how wide the day was against its 30-day swing, up to the cap of 20. It is never negative. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the forecast's long bets: a rise of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 3-bar grid cell of each window. For each case, take the coin's 3-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift over all windows. The measure is the mean of that excess return, with its t computed with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median forecast. The cases in the top half must have a higher mean excess 3-bar return than the cases in the bottom half (a difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean excess return is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean excess return is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming t with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 30 distinct days with cases; with fewer, the result is inconclusive whatever the t.
