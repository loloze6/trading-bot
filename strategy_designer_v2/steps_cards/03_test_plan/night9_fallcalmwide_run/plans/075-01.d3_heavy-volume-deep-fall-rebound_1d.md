Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closed lower, its volume against its 20-day average, its fall against its 30-day daily swing -> kept: the down-day fall in 30-day return standard deviations, times the day's volume over its 20-day mean, exactly 0 on up and flat days.
- Comparison: volume against its own 20-day average, and the fall against its own 30-day swing -> kept: both are inside the component (the 20-day volume mean includes the day itself, the common convention).
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next day, growing with both -> kept: a 1-bar horizon, long, sized by the product.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own product, graded and held only after down days, so its next-day gains decide the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolumeWeightedScaledReturnComponent with vol_period 30 and volume_period 20. Its raw value is (the day's return / the std of the last 30 daily returns) x (the day's volume / the mean of the last 20 volumes). The raw value is standardised over its past 60 values, negated so that a fall reads positive, and clipped at 0. On up and flat days the forecast is exactly 0, so the book holds nothing. On down days it is positive and grows linearly with the depth of the fall in usual moves and with the volume's multiple of its average. It reaches about +10 on a typical down-day reading and is capped at +20. It is never negative (long only).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars with forecast > 0, the forecast-weighted mean of the next-day excess return. The excess return is close[t+1]/close[t] - 1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Its t pools the cases of one day across coins (deviations summed per day).
- Supporting check: Among bars with forecast > 0, the mean next-day excess return of the top third by forecast, minus that of the bottom third, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes (top third minus bottom third above 0).
- Reject: The main measure is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Inconclusive covers a t between -2 and 2. It also covers a main measure with t >= 2 whose top-minus-bottom difference is 0 or below. The test needs at least 300 bars with forecast > 0, spread over at least 150 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
