- Situation: in a rising calm market, a day with a wide high-to-low range against the 30-day swing of daily returns, closing in the lower part of its range -> kept: the profile is added by code, and inside it a regime holds the wide-range and lower-half-close conditions.
- Comparison: the range against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns -> kept: RangeInReturnStdComponent with vol_period 30; "wide" is not given a number, so it takes the convention of at least 2 usual moves.
- Outcome: a rise over the next day, larger the nearer the close sits to the low -> kept: a long position graded by the close's nearness to the low, held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets exactly on the idea's situation, its sizing and its next-day horizon, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads two measures on each daily bar: - the day's range in usual moves: RangeInReturnStdComponent, vol_period 30, which is (high - low) / close divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns; - where the close sits: CloseLocationInRangeComponent, reference "high", which is 0 with the close at the high and 1 with the close at the low. A bar is in the situation when its range is at least 2 usual moves and its close is in the lower half of the range (close location at least 0.5). Inside the situation, the forecast is the close location standardised over its last 60 values (min_periods 20). This gives about +10 at mid-range, rising to about +20 as the close nears the low. The forecast is always long and grows the closer the close is to the low. On every other bar it is 0, and the profile code also keeps it at 0 outside rising calm markets.

- Main measure: On bars with forecast > 0, the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Least-squares slope of that next-day excess return on the bar's forecast (post_pipeline_value of low_close) across the cases. It must be positive with a t of 1 or more.
- Confirm: Mean next-day excess return above 0 with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting slope check passes.
- Reject: Mean next-day excess return below 0 with a t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure whose slope check fails. At least 30 distinct days with cases are needed, otherwise the result is inconclusive.
