Fidelity:
- Situation: a down day on volume heavier than the coin's 20-day average, the heavier the more -> adapted: the down day and the heavy volume are a regime; inside it the position is graded by how far volume exceeds its average.
- Comparison: the day's volume against its own average daily volume over the past 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next day, more after heavier volume -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on heavy-volume down days, sized by volume relative to its 20-day average, and is judged on the next day's return.
Forecast: The regime detector reads two measures. The first is the day's return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 1). The second is the day's volume over the mean of the 20 prior days (`VolumeRatioComponent`, period 20, exclude_current 1). A bar is active (`mean_reversion`) only when the day closed lower and that volume ratio is above 1. On every other bar the book is flat. On active bars the forecast is the centred volume ratio (ratio − 1), standardised over its last 60 values and clipped at 0. It is 0 at the edge of the situation, where volume equals its average. It grows linearly as volume rises further above its 20-day average, up to the cap of 20. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Take the case bars, where the forecast is above 0. On each, compute the next-day return close[t+1]/close[t] − 1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Regress this excess return on the bar's forecast and read the slope's t, with cases of the same day summed into one observation.
- Supporting check: The mean excess next-day return over all case bars is above 0. This is the unusual move in the long direction, before costs.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the mean excess next-day return over the cases is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a slope t of 2 or more while the mean excess return is 0 or below. At least 300 distinct case days are required across coins and windows; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
