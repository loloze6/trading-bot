- Situation: in a rising calm market, a day whose high-to-low range is wide against its 30-day swing of daily returns and that closes near its low -> kept: the profile is added by code; "wide" (range over close, divided by the 30-day std of daily returns, at least 2) and "near its low" (close in the bottom quarter of the day's range) form the regime, using the usual 2-std and bottom-quarter conventions; inside it the forecast grows with nearness to the low.
- Comparison: the nearer the close to the day's low, the larger the rise -> kept: the forecast grows with the close's location from the high (0.75 to 1).
- Outcome: price rises over the next three days -> kept: a long position held 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this situation and this horizon directly, so a confirm or a reject applies to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector computes two values on each daily bar. The first is `RangeInReturnStdComponent` (vol_period 30): the day's range as a fraction of the close, divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns. The second is `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "high"): 0 when the close is at the day's high, 1 when it is at the low. A bar is `mean_reversion` only when the range is at least 2 usual moves and the location is at least 0.75. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. The profile vetoes added by code keep the book flat outside rising calm. Inside the regime the forecast is the same close-location value, standardised over its last 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20). Its usual mean is about 0.5, so situation bars read about 15 to 20. Then `signed_power` (exponent 3, pivot 20) spreads that range to about 8.4 to 20. The forecast is always long (never below zero), zero outside the situation, and largest when the close is right at the low. Each bet is held 3 bars.
