- Situation: a day in a choppy, agitated market closing near an end of its range, scaled by its width against the 30-day swing -> adapted: "near an end" made graded by the close's distance from mid-range times the range in 30-day return stds, with a squared dead zone near the middle; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the day's range against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns -> kept
- Outcome: over the next day price moves back toward the other end (down after high closes, up after low closes), more for wider days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the fade of the close's off-middle distance in usual moves, held one day inside the profile, so its next-day gain decides the idea both ways.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMovesComponent with vol_period 30. Its raw value is 2 x (close − mid-range) / close / s, where s is the 30-day std of daily returns. It is positive when the day closes above the middle of its range and negative below. Its size grows with the distance from the middle and with the day's width against s. - It is standardised over 60 bars (min_periods 20), then negated (fade), then put through signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10. - Days closing near the middle give almost 0. Wide days closing near the high give a short position, and wide days closing near the low give a long one. The size grows smoothly with width and distance from the middle. - The forecast is ungated inside the config. Step 3's code adds the choppy-agitated profile, outside which the book is flat.

- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars with a non-zero forecast, the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Each case is weighted by forecast / 10 (sign and size), cases of one day are summed, and the measure is the t of the mean over days.
- Supporting check: The mean drift-adjusted next-day return in the forecast's direction is larger on the top third of |forecast| than on the bottom third (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The main t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes (top-third minus bottom-third difference above 0).

- Inconclusive: Any main t between −2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with the check failing. At least 100 cases on at least 50 distinct days are needed; fewer is inconclusive.
