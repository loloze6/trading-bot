- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a day closing near its low on a range wide against the 30-day swing -> adapted: the profile is added by code, and the day's lower-quarter close on a range of at least 2 30-day return standard deviations is a regime, graded inside it by width times distance below mid-range.

- Outcome: the price rises over the next day -> kept: long only, held 1 bar.

Forecast: Outside the choppy agitated profile (added by code) the forecast is zero. It is also zero unless both of these hold: the day's close sits in the lower fifth of its range (CloseLocationInRange "high" of at least 0.8), and its high-low range is at least 2 standard deviations of the last 30 daily returns (RangeInReturnStd(30) of at least 2). On those days the forecast comes from CloseOffMidRangeInUsualMoves(30), negated: the day's range in 30-day return standard deviations times how far the close sits below mid-range. It is standardised over its past 60 values (10 is a typical size, capped at 20) and clipped at 0, so it is long only. It grows with the day's width and with the close's nearness to the low.
