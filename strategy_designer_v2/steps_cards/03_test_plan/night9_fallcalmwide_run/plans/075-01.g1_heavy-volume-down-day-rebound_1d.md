Fidelity:
- Situation: a day the coin falls, with its volume heavy against its usual volume -> kept: a down day (close below the prior close) is a regime; inside it the position is graded by the volume ratio.
- Comparison: the day's volume against its own average daily volume over the past 20 days -> kept: volume[t] / mean(volume[t-20..t-1]).
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next day, the heavier the volume -> kept: the next 1-bar return, long, compared across sizes of the volume ratio.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds long only on down days, scaled by the 20-day volume ratio, and judges whether the next-day gain grows with that size.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 1-day return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 1). When that return is below 0, the bar is a down day and goes to `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. On a down day, the forecast is the day's volume over the mean volume of the prior 20 days (VolumeRatioComponent, period 20, exclude_current 1), standardised over its past 60 values. That value is always positive, so the book holds a long on every down day. The long is about +10 at a usual volume, smaller on a light-volume fall, and grows up to the +20 cap on a heavy one. The forecast is zero on up and flat days. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Use the down-day bars with a non-zero forecast. On those bars, take the coin's next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with the cases of one day pooled across coins.
- Supporting check: On the same bars, compare the mean excess next-day return of the top third of forecasts with that of the bottom third. Top minus bottom must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the top-minus-bottom difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, meaning heavier-volume down days are followed by weaker next days.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with a top-minus-bottom difference at or below 0 is also inconclusive. Fewer than 300 down-day cases on fewer than 150 distinct days is too few to judge.
