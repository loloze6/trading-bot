Fidelity:
- Situation: a day that closed lower, with its volume against the past 20 days' average -> kept: down day as a regime, volume ratio to the prior 20-day mean graded inside it
- Comparison: the day's volume relative to its own average daily volume over the past 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next two days, the heavier the volume -> kept: 2-bar hold, return graded against forecast size
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether the two-day return after down days grows with the volume ratio, as the idea states.
Forecast: A detector component reads the day's return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 1, sf 1). When that return is below 0 (the day closed lower), the bar is `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is null, so the book is flat there. Inside `mean_reversion`, the forecast is `VolumeRatioComponent` with `period` 20 and `exclude_current` 1. That is the day's volume divided by the mean volume of the prior 20 days. It is standardised over its own past 60 values. The forecast is always positive (long only): about +10 on a down day of usual volume, larger the heavier the volume (up to the cap of 20), and smaller the lighter it is. Code holds each bet for 2 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the down-day bars with forecast > 0 that fall on the non-overlapping 2-bar grid of each window. For each case, take the coin's 2-bar return from its close minus the coin's mean 2-bar return over all its windows (holding the coin). Regress that excess return on the case's forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with standard errors clustered by day.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 2-bar return of the top third of cases by forecast, minus that of the bottom third, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the top-minus-bottom-third difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a top-minus-bottom difference at or below 0. At least 300 cases on at least 150 distinct days are needed; fewer is inconclusive.
