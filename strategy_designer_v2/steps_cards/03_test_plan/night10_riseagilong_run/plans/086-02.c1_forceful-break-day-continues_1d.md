Fidelity:
- Situation: the first up day after a run of consecutive down days -> kept: a regime fires on an up close that breaks a run of at least 2 down closes, the most common meaning of a "run"; the code adds the rising agitated profile.
- Comparison: the up day's return against the standard deviation of the past 30 daily returns -> kept: the day's return in 30-day standard deviations sizes the forecast, larger for a stronger day.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 5 days, more after a larger scaled up day -> kept: each bet is held 5 bars and judged on the 5-day return against the coin's drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config buys exactly the idea's turning days, sizes each by its scaled return and holds for the idea's 5 days, so a confirm or a reject applies to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (count form, down side, at most 30 bars). A bar is in the idea's regime only when it closes up and breaks a run of 2 or more down closes. Every other bar is flat. On a turning bar, the forecast is `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` (period 1, vol_period 30): the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. This is standardised over its past 60 values (minimum 20) and clipped at 0, so the book is long only. The forecast is positive on turning days and grows linearly with how forceful the up day is, about 10 for a typical move and up to 20. It is 0 on all other bars. A weak turning day gives a forecast under 2 points, which makes no bet. Code holds each bet for 5 bars.

Side: long only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code
Profile: rising agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual move over the cases. A case is a bet, a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-day drift over all windows, before costs. Its t clusters cases by day, so cases on several coins the same day count as one.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases with a forecast above the cases' median, minus that of cases at or below it, must be above 0 (Welch t).
- Confirm: The main measure is positive with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting difference is positive.
- Reject: The main measure has a t of -2 or less, or the supporting difference (larger minus smaller turning days) has a Welch t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a main t between -2 and 2, or a positive main t with a difference at or below 0 whose t is above -2. At least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days are needed, otherwise the result is inconclusive.
