Fidelity:
- Situation: in a rising, agitated market, the first up day after a run of consecutive down days -> kept: a regime holds only on an up close that breaks a run of 2 or more down closes, inside the profile that code adds.
- Comparison: the up day's size against the coin's usual daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the up day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns grades the position.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 3 days, more after a larger up day -> kept: the bet is held for 3 bars, long only.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys exactly those break days, sized by the up day in usual swings, and judges the next 3 days, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (max_bars 30, count, side down). This is the length of the down run that today's up close breaks, and it is 0 on every other day. The `trending` regime holds only when that count is at least 2, which is a real run of down days. Every other bar is `unknown` and flat. Inside the regime the forecast is today's one-day return in usual moves: `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with period 1 and vol_period 30, giving the return over the std of the last 30 daily returns. That value is standardised over 60 bars (min_periods 20), so a typical day's move is about 10. It is then clipped at 0, so the book is long only. On a break day the forecast is positive and grows in proportion to how forceful the up day is. A timid up day gives a small position. On every bar outside the situation the forecast is 0. Code holds each bet for 3 bars and keeps the book flat outside the rising, agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a bet, meaning a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points on a break bar, read once per 3-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 3-bar return from that close minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift over all windows. The measure is the mean of these, with t taken on the cases of each day combined.
- Supporting check: Least-squares slope, across cases, of the unusual move on the case's forecast (`post_pipeline_value`), with its t. It must be positive with t ≥ 1, meaning a larger up day is followed by a larger rise.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting slope is positive with t ≥ 1.
- Reject: The mean unusual move has t ≤ -2, or the slope has t ≤ -2 (the rise shrinks as the up day grows).
- Inconclusive: Anything else: a mean t between -2 and 2, or a mean t ≥ 2 with a slope t below 1. The test needs at least 30 cases spread over at least 30 distinct days; with fewer, it is inconclusive.
