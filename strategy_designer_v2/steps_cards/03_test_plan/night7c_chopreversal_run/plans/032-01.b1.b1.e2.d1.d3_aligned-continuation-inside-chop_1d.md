Fidelity:
- Situation: the past 30 days went back and forth, with little net progress against the sum of daily moves -> kept: the raw 30-day efficiency ratio at or below 0.3 (the common chop level) is a regime, checked at each day's close.
- Comparison: the 30-day return points the same way as the 7-day move -> kept: a regime rule requires the 7-day and 30-day returns to have the same sign.
- Outcome: the next 7 days move the same way as the 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings, more for a larger scaled move -> kept: a graded continuation forecast held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config holds positions only in aligned chop, sized by the scaled 7-day move, so a confirm or a reject of the forecast's 7-day continuation is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is active only on bars that meet both conditions: - the raw 30-day Kaufman efficiency ratio is at or below 0.3; - the 7-day and 30-day returns have the same sign. On those bars the forecast is the 7-day return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns (times √7). It is standardised over its past 60 values. It is positive after an up week and negative after a down week, and it grows with the size of the scaled move. On every other bar the forecast is 0.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Cases are bars on each window's non-overlapping 7-bar grid where the forecast is non-zero. For each case, take the coin's 7-bar forward return minus its normal 7-bar drift (the mean over all windows). Regress it on the forecast (the slope).
- Supporting check: The mean of the same drift-adjusted 7-bar return, signed by the forecast's direction, is positive. Its t is computed with the cases clustered by day.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 cases.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ −2: larger scaled moves are followed by larger reversals.
- Inconclusive: The result is inconclusive in any of these cases: - the slope's t is between −2 and 2; - the slope confirms but the supporting check's t is below 2; - there are fewer than 30 cases.
