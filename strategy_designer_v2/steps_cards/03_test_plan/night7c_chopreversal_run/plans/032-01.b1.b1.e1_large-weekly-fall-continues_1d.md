Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin fell a large amount over the past 7 days -> adapted: the 7-day fall must be at least 2 usual moves (the most common convention, since the idea gives no number), set as a regime; inside it the position is graded by the fall's size, and "after the fall" becomes "while the fall stands".
- Comparison: the fall is counted in the coin's typical daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return is divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (times √7, a constant).
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 7 days, more after larger falls -> kept: a short position held 7 bars that grows with the scaled fall.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this scaled weekly fall and the coin's next week after it, short side, graded by the fall's size.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 7, vol_period 30). Its value is the 7-day return divided by (the 30-day daily return standard deviation × √7), so it counts the week's move in usual weekly moves. - The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` (used here only as a label) when this value is at or below -2. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). - Inside the regime, the same value is standardised against its own last 60 values with target 4. Its usual absolute size of about 0.8 maps to 4. A fall of 2 usual moves then gives about -10, and a fall of 4 or more gives about -20 (the cap). - The standardise target is lowered from 10 to 4 so that the regime's bars are not all pinned at the -20 cap. - The forecast is always short, and it grows with the depth of the scaled fall.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Each case is a short bet (a fall of the forecast of at least the band), read once per non-overlapping 7-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 7-bar return from the signal bar minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift (all windows together), on the short side. The measure is the mean of these unusual moves and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median |forecast|. The deeper half's mean short-side unusual move minus the shallower half's must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean short-side unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 cases, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-side unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2, over at least 30 cases: large scaled weekly falls are followed by rises beyond the drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t when the deeper half does not outperform the shallower half. Fewer than 30 cases is inconclusive whatever the result.
