Fidelity:
- Situation: past 30 days went back and forth with little net progress against the sum of daily moves -> adapted: a regime where the 30-day efficiency ratio is at most 0.3 (the common choppy convention), with smoothing 1 so the ratio is unsmoothed.
- Comparison: only while the 7-day move points the same way as the 30-day net move -> kept: the regime also requires the 7-day and 30-day returns to have the same sign.
- Outcome: a move the same way over the next 7 days, larger after a larger 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings -> kept: long or short with the 7-day return over 30-day volatility, held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the regime is the idea's condition and the forecast is its scaled move, so a confirm or a reject of this test is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless three things hold at the bar: the 30-bar efficiency ratio (unsmoothed) is at most 0.3, and the 7-day and 30-day returns are both up or both down. Inside that regime, the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the 30-day std of daily returns x sqrt(7)), standardised over its past 60 values. It is long after an up week and short after a down week, and it grows in proportion to the size of the scaled move. Because of the agreement condition, its sign is always the sign of the month's net move.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: On regime bars of the non-overlapping 7-bar grid, regress the coin's next 7-day return minus its normal 7-day drift (its mean over all windows) on the bar's forecast. The measure is the t of that slope.
- Supporting check: The mean of sign(forecast) x (next 7-day return minus drift) over the same cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope t is 2 or more, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The slope t is -2 or less (the following move goes against the scaled week, or shrinks as the week grows).
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a confirming slope with a non-positive supporting mean. Fewer than 50 grid cases in the regime across coins and windows is also inconclusive, whatever the t.
