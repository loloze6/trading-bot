Forecast: A bar qualifies only when two things hold. First, the unsmoothed 30-day efficiency ratio (net move over the sum of absolute daily moves) is at or below 0.3. Second, the 7-day and 30-day returns are both above zero or both below zero. Outside that situation the forecast is 0. Inside it, the forecast is the 7-day return in 30-day daily swings, return / (30-day std of daily returns × √7). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (about 10 on average), then passed through signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 20. The forecast is positive after an up-week and negative after a down-week, the same way as the month's net move. Its size grows with the cube of the scaled move: a typical scaled move (10) gives 2.5, and the largest (20) gives 20. Small moves stay near flat.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over cases. A case is a bet of the forecast, read once per non-overlapping 7-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 7-day return from the signal bar's close in the forecast's direction, minus the coin's normal 7-day drift over all windows. Take the mean over cases, with its t computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases into thirds by abs(forecast). The mean unusual move of the top third, minus that of the bottom third, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the top-minus-bottom-third difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected when the t lies between -2 and 2. The same holds when t ≥ 2 but the top-third difference is not above 0. It also holds with fewer than 30 cases, or with cases on fewer than 30 distinct days.
