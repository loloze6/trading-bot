Fidelity:
- Situation: 30-day daily swings large against the coin's own recent history, with steady 30-day net progress rather than chop -> kept: a regime is active when the 30-day volatility is at or above its 60-day average and the 30-day efficiency ratio is at least 0.3.
- Comparison: the 7-day rise judged against the coin's typical daily swing over 30 days -> kept: the 7-day return is divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: the 7-day rise is partly given back by a decline over the next 7 days, larger for a larger rise -> kept: the position is short, graded by the scaled rise, and held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets only on the stated situation, in the stated direction and with the stated grading, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The forecast is non-zero only when two conditions hold on the bar. - Agitation: the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns is at or above its 60-day mean (VolatilityRatioToAverage ≥ 1). - Steady progress: the raw 30-day Kaufman efficiency ratio is at least 0.3. Inside that regime, the forecast is built in three steps: - It starts from the 7-day return divided by (the 30-day daily-return standard deviation × √7). - That value is standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). - It is then negated and clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore short, and grows in size with the 7-day rise counted in usual daily swings. It is zero when the 7-day move is flat or down, and zero outside the regime. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The measure is the mean unusual move over the cases, in the short direction. - A case is a short bet: a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 7-bar grid cell of each window. - The unusual move of a case is minus the coin's 7-bar return, minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift (its mean over all windows). - The t-statistic sums the cases of one day into one deviation.
- Supporting check: Over the cases, the least-squares slope of the unusual move (short direction) on |forecast| is positive.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when both hold: - the mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 cases; - the slope in the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2, over at least 30 cases.
- Inconclusive: The result is inconclusive in any of these cases: - t lies between -2 and 2; - t ≥ 2 but the slope is not positive; - there are fewer than 30 cases.
