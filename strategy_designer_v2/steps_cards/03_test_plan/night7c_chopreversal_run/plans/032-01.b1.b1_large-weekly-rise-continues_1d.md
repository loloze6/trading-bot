Fidelity:
- Situation: a large 7-day rise compared with the 30-day daily swing -> kept: a regime rule (7-day return over 30-day daily std x sqrt(7) at least 2, the usual 2-sigma convention, about 5.3 daily swings); inside it the forecast is graded by that ratio.
- Comparison: the coin's typical daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the std of the last 30 daily returns (the sqrt(7) factor is a constant).
- Outcome: price keeps rising over the next 7 days, more so the larger the scaled rise -> kept: a long position held 7 bars, sized by the scaled rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test longs exactly the large scaled weekly rises, sized by their size, for 7 days, against holding the coin.
Forecast: The forecast uses one quantity, the 7-day return divided by (the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns x sqrt(7)). Off the situation, the forecast is 0. When this quantity is at least 2, the bar is in the active regime, and the forecast is long. Its size is that same quantity standardised against its own past 60 values (target 4, so a 2-sigma rise gives about +10). It grows with the rise up to the cap of +20. It is clipped at 0, so the forecast is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars with a long bet (forecast > 0), read once per cell of a non-overlapping 7-bar grid in each window. For each case, take the coin's 7-bar return from that close, minus the coin's mean 7-bar return over all windows. The measure is the mean of this unusual move and its t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the least-squares slope of the unusual move on the forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t >= 2 with a slope at or below 0. Fewer than 30 cases (or fewer than 20 distinct days) is also inconclusive, whatever the t.
