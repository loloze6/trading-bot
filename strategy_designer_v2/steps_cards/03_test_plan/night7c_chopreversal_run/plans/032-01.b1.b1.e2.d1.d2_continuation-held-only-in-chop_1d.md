Fidelity:
- Situation: past 30 days back and forth, little net progress against the sum of daily moves -> adapted: the 30-day Kaufman efficiency ratio (unsmoothed) at or below 0.3 is a regime, and it is checked at every daily close.
- Comparison: 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings -> kept: the 7-day return over the std of the 30 daily returns.
- Outcome: a same-way move over the next 7 days, larger after a larger scaled move -> adapted: graded long or short in the move's direction, held 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the chop condition, the scaling and the continuation are each measured as stated, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: On each daily close the detector computes the raw 30-day efficiency ratio. This is the absolute 30-day net move over the sum of the absolute daily moves, with smoothing span 1, so it is the raw value. When the ratio is at or below 0.3, Kaufman's usual chop level, the bar is "mean_reversion". On those bars the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the std of the 30 daily returns x √7), standardised over its past 60 values. It is positive after a scaled rise and negative after a scaled fall, and its size grows with the scaled move, up to ±20. On every other bar the forecast is 0, so the book is flat.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window, take the bars with a non-zero forecast. Regress the coin's 7-bar forward return minus its mean 7-bar return over all windows on the forecast (post_pipeline_value). Report the slope's t, with the cases pooled over all coins.
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily return before costs, minus its gain from the market's own rise (the timing part), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the timing part is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a positive slope whose timing part is at or below 0. At least 100 grid cases with a non-zero forecast are needed, else the result is inconclusive.
