Fidelity:
- Situation: a fall over the past 7 days while the past 30 days were choppy and agitated, with little net progress -> adapted: a regime that requires all three conditions: the 7-day return below 0, the 30-day efficiency ratio below 0.3, and the 30-day volatility percentile at or above 0.5. Inside the regime, the short is graded by how small the 30-day ratio of net progress to path is.
- Comparison: net progress relative to the path travelled over 30 days -> kept: the efficiency ratio over 30 bars (net move divided by the sum of absolute daily moves), unsmoothed.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 7 days, more so the less the net progress -> kept: a short held for 7 bars, larger as the ratio falls further below its usual level.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only in that situation and sizes the short by the idea's own strength, so a further fall confirms the idea and a rise rejects it.
Forecast: The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only when three conditions hold together: the 7-day close return is below 0, the 30-bar efficiency ratio is below 0.3 (Kaufman's usual chop level), and the percentile of the 30-day return volatility over the last 100 bars is at least 0.5 (agitated). On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is the mean of two parts. The first is the 30-bar efficiency ratio, standardised against its own past 60 values and capped at its usual size of +10. The second is a constant offset of -10. The forecast is therefore (min(ER_std, 10) - 10) / 2, which runs from 0 to -5. It is 0 when the ratio is at or above its usual level, and the short grows as the 30-day path makes less net progress. The forecast is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a short bet, read once per 7-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 7-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift over all windows, taken in the short's direction. The measure is the mean of these values, with its t computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Rank the cases by the size of their forecast. The mean unusual move of the cases above the median must exceed that of the cases at or below it (stronger minus weaker greater than 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive (a further fall below the drift) with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative (the coin rises relative to its drift after these cases) with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirm with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 cases or fewer than 30 distinct days.
