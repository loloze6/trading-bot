- Comparison: the 7-day move points the same way as the 30-day net move -> kept: a regime rule that requires both returns to have the same sign.
- Outcome: a move the same way over the next 7 days, larger after a larger 7-day move in 30-day daily swings -> kept: the forecast is that scaled 7-day move, held 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book holds only in the stated situation, follows the scaled weekly move with its sign and size, and is judged on the next 7 days.
Forecast: The regime detector checks every daily close. It needs the raw 30-day efficiency ratio below 0.3. It also needs the 7-day and 30-day returns to share the same sign, both up or both down. When all three hold, the bar is "chop". On any other bar the book is flat. Inside chop, the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns x sqrt 7), standardised over 60 bars. It is positive after a rise and negative after a fall, and grows in size with the scaled weekly move.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Sample each window on a 7-bar grid and keep the grid bars with a non-zero forecast. On each, take the coin's 7-bar forward return minus its normal 7-bar drift (its mean over all windows), signed by the forecast's sign. The measure is the mean of this unusual move and its t, with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: The cases in the top half of |forecast| have a mean unusual move above that of the bottom half (difference greater than 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2, meaning the move reverses instead of continuing.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, a confirm-level t without the supporting check passing, or fewer than 30 distinct case days.
