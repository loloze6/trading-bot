Fidelity:
- Situation: past 30 days back and forth, small net progress against the sum of daily moves -> kept: the 30-day Kaufman efficiency ratio, unsmoothed, at or below 0.3 (the common "choppy" convention), as a regime
- Comparison: the 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings -> kept: the 7-day return over the std of the last 30 daily returns (times sqrt 7, a constant)
- Outcome: a move the same way over the next 7 days, larger after a larger scaled move -> adapted: graded, the position grows with the scaled 7-day move and is held 7 bars
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own condition, its own scaled move and its own 7-day continuation, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: On each daily bar the 30-bar efficiency ratio is read: the absolute 30-day net move over the sum of the 30 absolute daily moves, with `smooth_period` 1 so it is the raw value. When it is at or below 0.3, the bar is choppy. On choppy bars only, the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the std of the last 30 daily returns x sqrt 7), standardised against its own last 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20). It is not negated: positive (long) after a rise, negative (short) after a fall. Its size grows linearly with the scaled 7-day move, up to the ±20 cap. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Use the bars at positions 0, 7, 14, ... of each window where regime is mean_reversion and the forecast is not 0. On those bars, take the least-squares slope of the coin's 7-bar forward return, minus that coin's mean 7-bar return over all its windows (holding the coin), on the bar's forecast. Report its t, before costs.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, on at least 100 cases. Larger scaled weekly moves are then followed by larger same-way moves beyond the coin's drift.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, on at least 100 cases. That is a reversal, the opposite of the idea.
- Inconclusive: The t lies between -2 and 2, or there are fewer than 100 non-overlapping cases on choppy bars.
