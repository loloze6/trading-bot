Forecast: The regime detector checks three things on each daily bar. The first is the raw 30-day Kaufman efficiency ratio: the net 30-day move over the sum of the 30 absolute daily moves, with smoothing span 1. The second is the 7-day percent return. The third is the 30-day percent return. A bar is "chop" when the ratio is below 0.3 and both returns are above 0, or both are below 0. Every other bar is flat. On chop bars the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns × √7). This is the week's move in 30-day daily swings. It is standardised against its own past 60 values. It is positive after a rising week, negative after a falling week, and its size grows with the scaled move, capped at ±20. It is held 1 bar at a time. It goes to 0 on the first day the chop or the agreement ends.
