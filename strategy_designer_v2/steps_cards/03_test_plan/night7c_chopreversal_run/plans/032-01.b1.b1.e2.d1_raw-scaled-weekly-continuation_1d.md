Fidelity:
- Situation: past 30 days went back and forth with little net progress against the sum of daily moves -> adapted: a 30-bar efficiency ratio (unsmoothed) of 0.3 or less, the common choppy convention, is a regime, and the book is flat outside it.
- Comparison: the 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings, at its own size -> kept: the 7-bar return over the 30-bar standard deviation of daily returns, scaled only, not centred or ranked.
- Outcome: a move the same way over the next 7 days, larger after a larger scaled move -> kept: continuation over 7 bars, with the position proportional to the scaled move.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the regime is the idea's choppy condition, and the graded position is its scaled 7-day move, so a continuation that grows with size confirms it and a significant opposite relation rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where the 30-bar efficiency ratio is at most 0.3. The efficiency ratio is the absolute net 30-day move divided by the sum of the absolute daily moves, taken raw (smooth_period 1). On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime, the forecast is the 7-bar return divided by (the 30-bar standard deviation of daily returns x sqrt 7). It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20) and capped at ±20. It is positive after a rise and negative after a fall, and it grows linearly with the size of the scaled move.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Cases are taken on a non-overlapping 7-bar grid per window, on bars in the mean_reversion regime with a non-zero forecast. For each case, compute the coin's 7-bar forward return minus its normal 7-bar drift (the mean over all windows). Regress that excess return on the forecast; the measure is the slope's t, with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move in the signal's direction over the cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2 (scaled moves followed by moves the other way).
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope whose supporting check fails. At least 30 grid cases, on at least 30 distinct days, are needed for either verdict.
