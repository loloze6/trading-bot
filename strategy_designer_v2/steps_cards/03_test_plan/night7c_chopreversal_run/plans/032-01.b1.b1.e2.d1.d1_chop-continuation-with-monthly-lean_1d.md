- Situation: past 30 days back and forth, little net progress against the sum of daily moves -> kept: a regime on the raw 30-day efficiency ratio below 0.3, the Kaufman choppiness convention.
- Comparison: 30-day return points the same way as the 7-day move -> kept: a regime rule that requires both returns to be above 0, or both below 0.
- Outcome: a same-way move over the next 7 days, larger after a larger 7-day move in 30-day daily swings -> adapted: the threshold is made graded, so the position grows with the scaled move, held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds positions only in choppy, aligned bars and sizes them by the scaled weekly move, which is exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: The regime is active only when three things hold: the raw 30-bar efficiency ratio is below 0.3; the 7-bar return in 30-bar daily volatility is above 0; and the 30-bar return is also above 0. The same applies with both returns below 0. On all other bars the forecast is 0. Inside the regime, the forecast is the 7-bar return divided by the 30-bar daily standard deviation times sqrt(7), standardised over its past 60 values (about 10 at a usual size, capped at 20). It is long after a rise and short after a fall, and its size grows with the scaled move.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, per the workflow's size definition. A case is a bet made in the regime, read once per 7-bar grid cell. The measure is the coin's return over the next 7 bars, minus its mean 7-bar drift over all windows, in the forecast's direction. It is reported with its day-clustered t.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases with |forecast| above the cases' median, minus that of cases at or below it, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the large-minus-small difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2, meaning the moves are given back.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails, and so is any result with fewer than 30 distinct case days across all coins and windows.
