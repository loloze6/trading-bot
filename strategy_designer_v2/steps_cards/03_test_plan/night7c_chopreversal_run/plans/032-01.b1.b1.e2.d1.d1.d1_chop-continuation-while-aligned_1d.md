Inside the regime, the forecast is the 7-day return divided by (the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns × √7), standardised over its past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). The forecast is positive after a 7-day rise and negative after a 7-day fall, so it follows the move. Its size grows with the move counted in usual 30-day daily swings. Outside the regime the forecast is 0.
