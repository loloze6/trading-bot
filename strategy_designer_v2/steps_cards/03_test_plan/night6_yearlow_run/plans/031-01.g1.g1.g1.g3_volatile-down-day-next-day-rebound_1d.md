Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's last 20 days were unusually volatile for it -> adapted: a regime that holds only while the 20-day return volatility ranks in the top fifth of its past year (convention level).
- Comparison: the one-day fall measured in its usual daily moves -> kept: the day's return divided by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns, falls only.
- Outcome: the larger the fall, the more it rises over the next day -> kept: a long position graded by the fall's size, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this graded relation only inside the volatile stretches, so a confirm or reject applies to the idea as stated.
Forecast: The regime detector ranks the coin's 20-day volatility of daily returns against its last 365 days, unsmoothed. When the rank is 0.80 or more, the regime is mean_reversion. Otherwise it is unknown, which is flat. In mean_reversion the forecast is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. That value is standardised over its past 60 values, negated, and clipped at 0. The forecast is 0 on up days. On down days it is positive and grows linearly with the size of the fall in usual moves: about +10 for a fall of the usual absolute size, capped at +20. It never goes below zero.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Use the bars with forecast > 0 (regime mean_reversion, down days). Regress the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows on the forecast, and take the slope's t. Each day is one case: cases on several coins on the same day have their residuals summed per day.
- Supporting check: On the top third of those bars by forecast, the mean next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope with t ≥ 2 while the top-third mean excess return is ≤ 0. The test needs at least 100 bars with forecast > 0, summed over the coins and windows; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
