Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 20-day gain at Friday's close (UTC), measured against its usual 20-day moves -> adapted: the 20-day return in mean absolute 20-day moves over the past 365 days, held only while the coming bar is Saturday.
- Comparison: the gain against the coin's own usual 20-day moves -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next day, Saturday, and the rise grows faster than the gain in the largest gains -> adapted: a graded convex long sized by the gain, held one daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast measures the scaled gain only at Friday's close and is judged on Saturday's return, so a confirm or a reject transfers to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads `WeekdayComponent`. When the coming bar is Saturday (5), the bar is `trending`; on every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). In the trending regime the forecast is built in four steps: - Start from `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` (`period` 20, `window` 365): the 20-day return in multiples of the coin's mean absolute 20-day return over the past year. - Standardise it over the 385 bars of its warm-up (min_periods 96). - Clip it at 0, so the book is long only on gains and flat on losses. - Apply `signed_power` with exponent 2 and pivot 10. A small gain is damped towards flat, and an unusually large gain is amplified, which gives a convex size. The forecast is positive and grows with the square of the scaled gain. It is 0 on Friday closes after a 20-day loss and on all non-Friday bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a Friday bar with a forecast above 0. For each case, compute the forecast x (Saturday's return close[t+1]/close[t]−1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows). Take the mean of these products, with a t computed with cases of the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Rank the cases by forecast within each coin. The mean excess Saturday return of the top fifth must exceed that of the other positive cases (difference top minus rest above 0).
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any result with t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 100 cases spread over at least 50 distinct Fridays; with fewer, the result is inconclusive whatever the t.
