Fidelity:
- Situation: the Friday close before a Saturday, with a positive Monday-close-to-Thursday-close return -> kept: a weekday regime selects only the bar whose next bar is Saturday, and the return is read from close[t-4] to close[t-1], clipped at 0.
- Comparison: the grading must not come from Friday's own move -> kept: the lag of 1 bar leaves Friday's bar out of the return.
- Outcome: the larger the Monday-to-Thursday gain, the larger Saturday's (UTC) rise; never short after a midweek fall -> kept: long only, held for the 1 Saturday bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the clipped Monday-to-Thursday gain, held only over Saturday, so its gains test the claim directly.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on the Friday bar, the one whose next bar is stamped Saturday (WeekdayComponent of the coming bar = 5). On all other days it is 0 (flat). - The measure is max(0, close[Thu] / close[Mon] − 1), from RiseTimesVolatilityCalmnessComponent with period 3 and lag 1. With vol_lookback 1 the volatility factor is exactly 1, so the value is the raw rising-side return. - It is standardised against its own last 60 values (min 20). - It is 0 after a midweek fall. It is positive and grows linearly with the midweek gain, about +10 for a gain of usual size and capped at 20. - It is never negative.

- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Over Friday bars with forecast > 0, the slope of Saturday's return in excess of the coin's mean daily return (all windows) on the forecast. Its t uses residuals summed per Saturday, so the same Saturday across coins counts as one case.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean of Saturday's excess return over those bars is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive, including a slope t between −2 and 2, or a confirming slope whose weighted mean is not above 0. At least 300 Friday cases with forecast > 0 on at least 100 distinct Saturdays are required, else inconclusive.
