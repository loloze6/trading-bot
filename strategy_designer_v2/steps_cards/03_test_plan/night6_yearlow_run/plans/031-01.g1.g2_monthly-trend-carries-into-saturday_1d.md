Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 20-day return at Friday's close (UTC) -> kept: a regime holds the book only on bars whose coming bar is Saturday; inside it, the forecast is graded by the 20-day return.
- Comparison: higher versus lower 20-day returns -> kept: the forecast's size and sign follow the 20-day return, so larger gains give larger longs.
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next day, Saturday -> kept: the position is held 1 daily bar, Friday's close to Saturday's close.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether Saturday's return grows with the Friday 20-day return, as the idea states.
Forecast: A regime detector reads `WeekdayComponent` for the weekday of the coming bar. When that bar is Saturday (value 5, rule between 4.5 and 5.5), the regime is `trending`; on every other bar the regime is `unknown`, which is null and flat. In the `trending` regime the forecast is `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 20, the 20-day percent return, standardised over its past 60 values with min_periods 20. The forecast is positive after a 20-day rise and negative after a 20-day fall. It grows in proportion to the size of the return against its usual size, up to the cap of ±20. It is zero on every bar that is not a Friday close.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Each case is a Friday-close bar where the regime is `trending`. For each case, take the forecast / 10 times the excess return: the next bar's return (close[t+1]/close[t] − 1) minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Sum these per case within each calendar day, then take the t of their mean across days. This tests whether gains grow with the signed size of the position, net of the coin's drift.
- Supporting check: Compare the Saturday excess return in the top third of the forecast with the bottom third (top minus bottom), using Welch's t. The check passes when the difference is above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's t is 2 or more and the top-minus-bottom difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is a t of 2 or more with a top-minus-bottom difference at or below 0. The test needs at least 200 distinct Friday days with cases (about 2,000 coin-cases) to give any verdict.
