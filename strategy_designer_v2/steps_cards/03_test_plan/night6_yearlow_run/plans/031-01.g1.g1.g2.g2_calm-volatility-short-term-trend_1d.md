Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's 20-day volatility is low relative to its usual level -> adapted: a regime where the 20-day return volatility is below its 60-day mean (ratio under 1); flat outside it.
- Comparison: the slope is steeper than when volatility is not low -> kept: the same slope is recomputed from closes on the other bars and compared.
- Outcome: the 14-day return grades the next 2-day move in its own direction -> kept: a signed position sized by the 14-day return, held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the calm-regime slope of the 2-day move on the 14-day return, and compares it with the non-calm slope.
Forecast: The forecast is active only when the 20-day volatility of daily returns is below its 60-day mean (VolatilityRatioToAverageComponent 20/60 under 1.0). In that state it is the 14-day percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 14), standardised over its past 60 values (min 20). It is positive after a rise and negative after a fall, and grows with the size of the run, capped at ±20. The forecast is 0 on every bar where volatility is at or above its usual level.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: On calm-regime bars of a non-overlapping 2-bar grid per window, take the least-squares slope of the coin's 2-day forward return, minus the coin's mean 2-day return over all windows, on the forecast. Report its t and the number of cases.
- Supporting check: Recompute the standardised 14-day return from closes on the non-calm grid bars and fit the same slope there. The calm slope minus the non-calm slope must be positive, with a t of at least 2.
- Confirm: The calm slope has t ≥ 2, the supporting check passes, and there are at least 300 calm cases.
- Reject: The calm slope has t ≤ -2, or the calm minus non-calm slope difference has t ≤ -2 (flatter in calm), with at least 300 calm cases.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive. That covers a calm slope with |t| < 2, and a positive calm slope whose difference from the non-calm slope has t between -2 and 2. Fewer than 300 calm grid cases, or fewer than 300 non-calm cases for the check, is also inconclusive.
