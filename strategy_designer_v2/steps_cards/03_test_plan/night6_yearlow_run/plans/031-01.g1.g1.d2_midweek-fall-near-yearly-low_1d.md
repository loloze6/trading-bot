Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's return from Monday's close to Thursday's close, read at Friday's close -> kept: the lagged 3-bar return, close[Thu]/close[Mon]-1, divided by the coin's recent daily volatility. That division only puts the return on a stable scale.
- Comparison: the grading must not come from Friday's own move, and a fall counts only in the lower part of the past-year range -> kept: the signal ends at Thursday. Short positions exist only while the close sits in the lower half of its 365-day range, which is the conventional lower part.
- Outcome: Saturday's (UTC) move, up after a midweek gain and down after a midweek fall near the yearly low, larger the larger the move -> kept: the position is opened at Friday's close and held for 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded bet on Saturday, long everywhere and short only in the lower half of the yearly range, so a confirm or reject of its Saturday gains is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only at a Friday's close, when the coming bar is Saturday. The weekday comes from `WeekdayComponent`, which reads 5 for a coming Saturday. The signal is the return from Monday's close to Thursday's close, in usual daily moves: `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with period 3, lag 1 and vol_period 20, put through `standardise` with window 60 and min_periods 20. The book then depends on where the close sits in its 365-day close range (`DonchianBreakoutComponent`, period 365): - Lower half of the range: the standardised signal is used signed. It is long in proportion to a midweek gain and short in proportion to a midweek fall. - Upper half of the range: the signal is clipped at 0. It is long in proportion to a midweek gain and flat after a fall. - Any other weekday: the forecast is 0. The size grows linearly with the midweek return, capped at ±20.
