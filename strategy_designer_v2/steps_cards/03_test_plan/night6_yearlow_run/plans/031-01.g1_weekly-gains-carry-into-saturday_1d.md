Fidelity:
- Situation: going into the weekend, a coin's return over the past 5 days -> adapted: the coming bar being Saturday (UTC) is a regime; inside it the 5-day return is graded, kept long-only.
- Comparison: the higher the 5-day return, the more it rises -> kept: the position grows with the 5-day gain.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next day, Saturday -> kept: 1-bar horizon from Friday's close to Saturday's close.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets only on Friday closes, sized by the weekly gain, and judges Saturday's return.
Forecast: On each daily close, a weekday rule checks whether the coming bar is Saturday (UTC). On those bars only, the forecast is the coin's 5-bar close-to-close return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 5). It is standardised against its last 60 values, with 20 needed before it starts, and clipped at 0. The forecast is positive and grows with the size of the weekly gain. It is 0 after a weekly loss or flat week, and 0 on every other weekday.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars in regime `trending` with forecast > 0, compute forecast/10 × (close[t+1]/close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean daily return over all windows). Take its mean, with a t in which the cases of one day are summed across coins.
- Supporting check: Compare Friday bars with forecast in the top third of positive forecasts against Friday bars with forecast 0. Their mean Saturday excess return, top minus zero, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The main measure is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. At least 300 distinct days with a positive forecast are needed, otherwise the result is inconclusive.
