Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 14-day return, in its usual daily moves, at Thursday's close (UTC) -> adapted: Thursday's close is a regime (the coming bar is Friday); inside it the forecast is graded by the volatility-scaled 14-day return.
- Comparison: higher 14-day returns against lower ones ("the higher..., the more") -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the following Friday and Saturday together, more the higher the return -> kept: each bet is held 2 daily bars from Thursday's close.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own measure, taken only at Thursday's close and held over Friday and Saturday, so a confirm or a reject transfers.
Forecast: The regime is active only on the daily bar whose close is Thursday's close, where the coming bar is stamped Friday (WeekdayComponent between 3.5 and 4.5); all other bars forecast 0. On that bar the forecast is the 14-day return divided by the coin's usual daily move. VolatilityScaledReturnComponent uses period 14 and vol_period 20, the convention, and its fixed sqrt(14) factor cancels under standardise. The value is standardised over its last 60 daily values (min 20) to about 10 on average and capped at ±20. It is positive after a 14-day rise, negative after a fall, 0 for no move, and grows in size with the move in usual moves. Each bet is held 2 bars, over Friday and Saturday.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: On each Thursday-close bar, take forecast/10 times the coin's 2-bar return (close[t+2]/close[t] − 1, within the window) minus the coin's mean 2-bar return over all its windows. The measure is the mean of this product, with its t computed with cases of one day summed together. This is the gain beyond holding, growing with the forecast's size.
- Supporting check: Among Thursday cases, the mean drift-removed 2-bar return of the top third of forecasts minus that of the bottom third is above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's t is ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's t is ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2, or a t ≥ 2 with the check failing. At least 200 Thursday cases with a nonzero forecast, on at least 100 distinct days, are needed; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
