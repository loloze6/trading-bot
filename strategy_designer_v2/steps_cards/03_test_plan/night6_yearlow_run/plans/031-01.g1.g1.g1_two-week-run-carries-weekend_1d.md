Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's 14-day return in usual daily moves, read at Friday's close (UTC) -> kept: a Friday-close regime (the coming bar is Saturday); inside it, the forecast is graded by the volatility-scaled 14-day return.
- Comparison: higher run against lower run ("the higher ... the more") -> kept: the position's size and sign follow the size and sign of the scaled return.
- Outcome: the coin rises more over Saturday and Sunday together -> kept: each bet is held 2 daily bars, from Friday's close to Sunday's close.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets only at Friday's close, sizes each bet by the 14-day run in usual daily moves, and holds it over exactly the two weekend days.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the weekday of the coming bar. On the bar whose next bar is Saturday (Friday's daily bar, which closes at Friday's close), the forecast is the 14-day simple return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The component also divides by sqrt(14), a constant that cancels out. This value is standardised by its own past 60 daily values (target 10, cap 20). The forecast is signed: long after a two-week rise, short after a two-week fall, and larger the more usual daily moves the run spans. It is near 0 when the run is flat. On every other weekday the regime is null and the forecast is 0. The 2-bar holding period then carries the Friday bet through Saturday and Sunday.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: Uses the Friday-close bars (regime "trending"), which fall 7 days apart and so do not overlap. On each, take forecast/10 x (close[t+2]/close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean 2-day return over all its windows). This is the position-weighted unusual weekend move. Take its mean and its t, with the cases of one Friday summed across coins into one piece of evidence. All of this is before costs.
- Supporting check: Split the Friday cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean unusual weekend move, taken in the forecast's direction, must be larger in the upper half than in the lower half (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean weighted unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, over at least 100 distinct Fridays, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean weighted unusual move is below 0 with t <= −2, over at least 100 distinct Fridays.
- Inconclusive: These results neither confirm nor reject: |t| < 2; t >= 2 with the size check not passing; fewer than 100 distinct Fridays with cases. About 400 are expected: 24 windows of about 17 Fridays each, across 6 coins.
