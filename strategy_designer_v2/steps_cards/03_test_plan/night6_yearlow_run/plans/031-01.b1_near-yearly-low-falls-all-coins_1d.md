Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close's nearness to its lowest close of the past 365 days -> kept
- Comparison: closer to the low against farther from it ("the closer, the more") -> kept
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 20 days, more the closer it was -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: a short sized by nearness to the 365-day closing low, judged on 20-day returns, confirms or rejects exactly this graded claim.
Forecast: The indicator is `DistanceFromHighComponent` with `extreme` "low", `source` "close", `period` 365, `exclude_current` 0 and `form` "proximity" (`power` 1). Its raw value is 1 / (1 + D), where D is the percent by which the close sits above its 365-day lowest close. It is 1 at the low and near 0 far above it. The value is standardised against its own past 365 values, then negated. The forecast is therefore never positive: a short that is near zero far from the yearly low and grows smoothly as the close nears the low, up to -20 at the cap. It is ungated, because the idea is a steady graded relation.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: On a non-overlapping 20-bar grid in each window, regress the coin's 20-bar forward return minus its mean 20-bar return over all windows on the bar's forecast. The result is the slope and its t, with the cases of the same date pooled.
- Supporting check: The cases in the top quintile of |forecast| have a mean excess 20-bar return below 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when both hold: - the slope is positive (a deeper short is followed by a lower excess return) with t ≥ 2; - the top-quintile mean excess return is negative.
- Reject: The slope is negative (a deeper short is followed by a higher excess return, i.e. a bounce near the low) with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Inconclusive covers: - a slope with |t| < 2, whatever its sign; - a confirming slope whose supporting check fails; - fewer than 100 grid cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct dates.
