Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close's closeness to its lowest close of the past 365 days, while the 60-day return is positive -> kept: the distance of the close above its 365-day closing low, graded, inside a regime that exists only while the 60-day return is above 0.
- Comparison: how close the coin has usually been to that low over the year -> kept: the distance is divided by its own mean over the past 365 days, then centred by a constant, so the forecast turns negative when the coin sits nearer the low than usual.
- Outcome: the coin falls more over the next 20 days the nearer it sits -> kept: a graded short, larger the nearer the coin is, held 20 bars and judged against holding the coin.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the idea's closeness, its comparison, its condition and its horizon as the idea states them.
Forecast: The forecast is 0 unless the coin's 60-day return (close against the close 60 bars ago) is above 0. While it is, the forecast has two parts. The first is the percent distance of today's close above the lowest close of the last 365 bars (today included). It is standardised over its past 365 values, so it reads about 10 when the coin sits at its usual distance from the yearly low, near 0 at the low, and up to 20 at twice the usual distance or more. The second is a constant -10. The two are averaged with equal weights, so the forecast is (x - 10) / 2. It is negative (short) when the close is nearer the yearly low than usual, down to -5 at the low itself. It is positive (long) when the close is farther than usual, up to +5. It is 0 at the usual distance.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: Cases are bars on a non-overlapping 20-bar grid in each window where the regime is trending and the forecast is not 0. For each case, take forecast / 10 x (the coin's 20-bar return - its mean 20-bar return over all windows). The measure is the mean of this product, with its t. Cases on the same day are summed into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 20-bar return (return minus the coin's mean) on cases with forecast below 0 is below 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t when the supporting check fails, is inconclusive. Fewer than 100 cases (or fewer than 60 distinct days) is also inconclusive.
