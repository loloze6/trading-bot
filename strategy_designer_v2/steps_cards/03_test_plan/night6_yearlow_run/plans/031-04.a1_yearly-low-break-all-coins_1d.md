Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close below the lowest close of the prior 365 days -> adapted: the break is a regime (close below the prior 365-day closing low), and inside it the position is graded by the close's percent depth under that low.
- Comparison: the further below the old low, the larger the rebound -> kept: the forecast grows with the percent depth below the prior 365-day low.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 10 days -> kept: long, held 10 daily bars, judged on the 10-bar return against holding the coin.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets long only on closes below the prior yearly closing low, sized by depth, over 10 days, so its result answers the idea directly.
Forecast: The forecast uses DistanceFromHighComponent with `period` 365, `source` close, `exclude_current` 1, `extreme` low and `form` percent. It is the close's percent distance from the lowest close of the prior 365 days, and it is negative when the close is below that low. The regime detector reads the same measure. When it is below 0, the bar is `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat, so the forecast is exactly 0 outside a break. Inside a break, the strategy standardises the distance against its own past 365 values (window 365, min_periods 91) and negates it. The forecast is then positive (long) and grows linearly with the depth below the old low, up to the cap of 20. It never goes below zero.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over the cases: each case's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift (holding the coin, all windows), long side. A case is a bet, read once per 10-bar grid cell. Its t is computed with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases with an above-median forecast (deeper breaks) minus that of cases with a below-median forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2 (breaks extend).
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure whose check fails, is inconclusive. The test needs at least 30 cases falling on at least 20 distinct days; fewer cases are inconclusive whatever the t.
