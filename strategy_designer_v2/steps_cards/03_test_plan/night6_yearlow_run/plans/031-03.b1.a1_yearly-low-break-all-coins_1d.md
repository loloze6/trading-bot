Fidelity:
- Situation: daily close below the lowest close of the prior 365 days -> kept
- Comparison: the coin's usual drift over 10 days -> kept
- Outcome: rises more than drift over the next 10 days, more the deeper the break -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on closes below the prior 365-day closing low, sized by the depth of the break, and judged on its 10-day move against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast reads the close's percent distance from the lowest close of bars t-365 to t-1 (DistanceFromHighComponent: period 365, source close, exclude_current 1, extreme low). The regime detector selects mean_reversion only when that distance is below 0, that is, when the close is under the old yearly low. Every other bar is flat. Inside the regime the distance is standardised over its last 365 values (min_periods 91), clipped at 0 and negated. The forecast is therefore positive and grows linearly with how far the close sits below the old low. It is near 0 just under the level, and it is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case: the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close minus its normal 10-bar drift (all windows together), before costs. A case is a long bet, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The measure is its mean, with a t-statistic that takes the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: Across cases, the slope of the unusual move on the signal bar's forecast is positive: deeper breaks are followed by larger excess rises.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, on at least 30 distinct days, and the supporting slope is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2, on at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a slope at or below 0, or fewer than 30 distinct case days over all coins and windows.
