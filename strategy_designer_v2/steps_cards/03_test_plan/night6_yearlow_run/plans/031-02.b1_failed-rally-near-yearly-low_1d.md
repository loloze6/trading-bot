- Situation: the 60-day return is positive -> adapted: "while" made a regime condition, with the 60-day return above 0; the book is flat otherwise.
- Comparison: how close the close is to its 365-day closing low, compared with how close it usually was over that year -> kept: the percent distance above the 365-day low, divided by its mean over the past 365 days.
- Outcome: the closer than usual, the more it falls over the next 20 days -> kept: the forecast is short when the close is nearer than usual and grows with the nearness, judged on the 20-day return.

Forecast: The regime is "trending" only while the 60-day close-to-close return is above 0; otherwise the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside the regime the forecast has two parts, averaged with equal weights: - D is the percent distance of the close above its lowest close of the last 365 days (bar t included). It is standardised over its past 365 values (min_periods 91), so its usual distance over the year reads 10. - A constant of -10. The forecast is (D_std - 10) / 2. It is negative (short) when the close is nearer its yearly low than usual. It reaches -5 at the low itself and grows more negative the nearer the close sits. It is positive, up to +5, when the close is farther from the low than usual. It is 0 at the usual distance.
