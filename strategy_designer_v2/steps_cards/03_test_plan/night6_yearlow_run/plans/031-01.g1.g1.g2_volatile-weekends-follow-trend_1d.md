Fidelity:
- Situation: Friday's close (UTC) while the coin's 20-day volatility is high against its usual level -> kept: a regime that needs the coming bar to be Saturday and the 20-day volatility over its 60-day mean to be above 1 (the usual-level convention); the book is flat outside it.
- Comparison: steeper grading than when volatility is not high -> kept: the criterion compares the slope on high-volatility Fridays with the slope on other Fridays, rebuilt from closes.
- Outcome: the move over Saturday and Sunday is in the 14-day return's direction and grows with it -> kept: the forecast is the 14-day return, held 2 daily bars (Friday close to Sunday close).
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config bets only on high-volatility Fridays, in proportion to the 14-day return, and the criterion tests the slope and how it compares with other Fridays.
Forecast: The regime is "trending" only on a Friday bar (WeekdayComponent says the coming bar is Saturday, between 4.5 and 5.5) when VolatilityRatioToAverageComponent (vol_period 20, vol_lookback 60) is above 1.0. On every other bar the forecast is 0. On those Fridays the forecast is the 14-day return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 14), standardised over its 60 past values (min_periods 20). It is long after a 14-day rise and short after a fall. Its size grows linearly with the size of the return, capped at ±20. Each bet is held 2 bars, through Saturday and Sunday.
Config: 031-01.g1.g1.g2_volatile-weekends-follow-trend_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The gain per case, on Friday bars in the "trending" regime with a nonzero forecast. Each case is (forecast/10) × (close[t+2]/close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean 2-bar return over all its windows). Cases on the same Friday across coins are summed into one value per day. The measure is the t of the mean over those days.
- Supporting check: The steeper slope. On high-volatility Fridays, regress the coin's excess weekend return on the 14-day return, standardised as in the config and recomputed from closes. Do the same on all other Fridays. The check is the high-volatility slope minus the other-Fridays slope, with its Welch t.
- Confirm: The main t is at least 2 over at least 100 distinct Fridays, and the slope difference is above 0.
- Reject: Either the main t is at most −2, or the slope difference has a t of at most −2 (no steeper grading when volatility is high). Either needs at least 100 distinct Fridays.
- Inconclusive: Any main t strictly between −2 and 2. Also a main t of 2 or more with a slope difference at or below 0 whose t is above −2. Also fewer than 100 distinct Fridays with cases.
