- Situation: the coin's 60-day return is positive -> adapted: "while" made a regime condition (60-day return above 0); the book is flat elsewhere.
- Comparison: the daily close's nearness to its 365-day low, against how near it has usually been over that year -> kept: 365-day low proximity divided by its own mean over the past 365 days, centred at that usual level.
- Outcome: the more unusually near the low, the more it falls over the next 40 days -> kept: short, graded by how much nearer than usual, held 40 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded relation inside its own condition, judged on 40-day moves against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast exists only while the 60-day close-to-close return is above 0, set by a regime rule on `PriceEvolutionOnPeriodComponent` (60). Inside that regime, x is the 365-day low proximity, 1/(1+D) with D the percent distance of the close above its lowest close of the past 365 days, standardised over the 365 values before it (window 365, min_periods 91). Under 10 is less near the low than usual; above 10 is nearer than usual. The forecast is the equal-weight mean of x negated and a standardised +10 offset, so it equals (10 − x)/2. It is 0 at the usual nearness, short when the close is nearer the low than usual (down to −5 at the cap), and mildly long when it is farther than usual. Its size grows with how unusual the nearness is. It is 0 whenever the 60-day return is 0 or below.

- Horizon: 40 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars of a non-overlapping 40-bar grid in each window where the forecast is not 0. For each case, take the forecast/10 times the coin's 40-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 40-bar return over all windows. The measure is the mean of these products and its t, with cases on the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: On those cases, the mean drift-removed 40-bar return when the forecast is −2 or lower must be below 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when the mean product is above 0 with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean product is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. Fewer than 30 grid cases on separate days is inconclusive whatever the t.
