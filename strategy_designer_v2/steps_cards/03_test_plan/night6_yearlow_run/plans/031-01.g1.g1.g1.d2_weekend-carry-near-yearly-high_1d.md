Fidelity:
- Situation: a Friday close (UTC) after a two-week rise -> adapted: a regime is active only on Friday bars with a positive 14-bar return; inside it the position grows with nearness to the yearly high.
- Comparison: Friday close against the highest price of the past year -> kept: proximity of the close to the 365-bar high of daily highs.
- Outcome: the coin rises over Saturday and Sunday together, more the nearer it is to the high -> kept: the 2-bar return from Friday's close, held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config holds a long only on post-rise Fridays, sized by nearness to the yearly high, over exactly the weekend.
Forecast: The forecast is non-zero only when two conditions hold. The coming bar must be Saturday, so the current bar is Friday (WeekdayComponent between 4.5 and 5.5). The 14-bar close return must be above 0 (a detector PriceEvolutionComponent with period 14). On those bars the forecast is DistanceFromHighComponent with period 365, source "high", form "proximity" and power 1, which gives 1/(1+D), D being the percent distance of the close below the 365-day high. This value is standardised over its past 365 values (min_periods 91). It is always positive, about +10 at a usual nearness and up to +20 when the close presses the high. It falls smoothly as the close sits further below the high. On every other bar the regime is null and the forecast is 0. The position is never short. It is held 2 bars, Saturday and Sunday.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: A case is each Friday bar in the trending regime, with return close[t+2]/close[t]-1 inside the same window. Its excess return is that return minus the coin's mean 2-bar return over all its windows on the non-overlapping 2-bar grid. The measure is the least-squares slope of the excess return on the case's forecast (post_pipeline_value), with its t. Deviations are summed per day, so the same Friday on several coins counts as one case.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean excess weekend return over all cases is above 0.
- Confirm: Slope t ≥ 2 and a forecast-weighted mean excess return above 0.
- Reject: Slope t ≤ -2: weekends after closes nearer the yearly high do worse than the coin's normal drift.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t ≥ 2 with a weighted mean excess return at or below 0. A verdict needs at least 100 cases on at least 50 distinct Fridays; below that the result is inconclusive.
