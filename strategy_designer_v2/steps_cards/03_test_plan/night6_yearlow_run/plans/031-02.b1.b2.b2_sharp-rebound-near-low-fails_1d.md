Fidelity:
- Situation: daily close nearer its 365-day closing low than its usual distance over that year -> kept: a regime from a veto; the 365-day distance from the closing low, standardised against its own past 365 values, below 10 (under its yearly mean).
- Comparison: the larger the 60-day return, the larger the fall -> adapted: inside the regime, the short is graded by the 60-day return; falling coins (return at or below 0) are held flat.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 20 days -> kept: each bet is held 20 bars and judged against holding the coin.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the same situation, grading and horizon are measured, and the slope of the 20-day return on the 60-day return inside the regime decides either way.
Forecast: The detector reads the close's percent distance above its lowest close of the past 365 bars, which is never negative. A veto standardises that distance over its past 365 values. If the result is below 10, the distance is below its yearly mean, and the bar is `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is set to null, so the book is flat there. In `mean_reversion` the forecast comes from the 60-bar price change (`PriceEvolutionComponent` 60). It is standardised over 61 values, then negated, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore 0 while the 60-day return is at or below zero. It turns short when that return is positive, and grows in size with the rebound, up to -20. It is never long.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: Use the `mean_reversion` bars on each window's non-overlapping 20-bar grid, all coins pooled. On those bars, regress the coin's 20-bar forward return, minus its all-window mean 20-bar return, on the 60-bar return recomputed from closes. The result is the slope and its t, with the cases of one day grouped together.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases (short bets, measured in the signal's direction, net of drift) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, and the supporting check is positive.
- Reject: The slope is positive with t ≥ +2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2, or a confirming slope whose supporting check fails. Either way at least 30 grid cases inside the regime, on at least 20 distinct days, are required; fewer is inconclusive.
