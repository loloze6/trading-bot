Fidelity:
- Situation: a 60-day rise while the close sits nearer its 365-day closing low than usual -> adapted: the nearness is a regime (the distance from the yearly low ranks at or below the median of its past values), and inside it the 60-day rise is graded.
- Comparison: the usual nearness to the yearly low over that year -> kept: the median of the coin's past distances from its yearly low.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 20 days, more after a bigger rise -> kept: a short sized by the rise, held 20 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: both the condition and the graded rally are measured as stated, so a short forecast that does or does not earn the 20-day fall settles the idea either way.
Forecast: The regime detector measures the percent distance of the close above its lowest close of the last 365 days. It ranks that distance within its stored history using `negate_percentile`. The idea is active only when the score is at least 0.5, which means the close is nearer the yearly low than its median distance. On all other bars the forecast is 0. Inside the regime, the forecast is the 60-day percent return, standardised over the 61 values before it and clipped at 0. It is then negated, so the forecast is 0 when the coin has not risen. It is negative and grows in size with the rally, about -10 for a usual-sized rise and down to -20 for double that. The forecast is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a short bet, read once per cell of each window's 20-bar grid. The measure is the coin's 20-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 20-day drift, taken in the short direction. Its t is computed with the cases of one day clustered together.
- Supporting check: On the same grid, among in-regime bars with a nonzero forecast, compare the 20-day return after the largest-third forecasts in absolute size with the return after the smallest third. The largest third must be lower: the difference, largest minus smallest, must be below 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive (the coin fell more than its drift) with a day-clustered t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative (the coin rose more than its drift after the shorts) with a day-clustered t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t whose supporting check fails. So is any result from fewer than 30 cases on distinct days across all coins and windows.
