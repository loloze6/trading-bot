- Situation: the daily close falls below the lowest close of the prior 365 days -> kept: a regime rule on the close's percent distance below the prior 365-day closing low (bar t excluded) being under 0.
- Comparison: rises more than its usual drift, more the further below the old low -> kept: the position grows with the depth below the old low, and the outcome is judged against the coin's normal 10-day drift.
- Outcome: the price rises over the next 10 days -> kept: long for 10 bars, judged on the 10-bar forward return.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds long only on closes below the prior yearly closing low, sized by the depth of the break, over the idea's 10 days, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The indicator is the percent distance of the close from the lowest close of the prior 365 daily bars, with bar t excluded. Its settings are `DistanceFromHighComponent` with `extreme` "low", `source` "close", `exclude_current` 1 and `period` 365. The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only when this distance is below 0, that is, when the close is under the old yearly low. On every other bar the forecast is exactly 0 (flat). Inside the regime the forecast is that distance, standardised against its own past 365 values (window 365, min_periods 91) and negated. It is therefore positive (long) and grows the deeper the close sits below the old low. It is never short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a bet, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows. The measure is its mean, with its t (cases of one day taken together).
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases whose forecast is above the median case forecast minus that of the cases at or below the median. This difference must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes (deeper breaks show a larger unusual move).
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t <= -2, meaning the coin falls more than its drift after a break of the yearly low.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more when the supporting check fails, and so is a count under 30 distinct case days pooled over all coins and windows.
