Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day move, the larger the stronger -> kept
- Comparison: against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps moving the same way over the next 5 days, up after up days, down after down days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the day's move in 30-day swing units, signed and graded, and each bet is held 5 days, so its 5-day follow-through is the idea's claim.
Forecast: One component computes the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (`VolatilityScaledReturnComponent`, period 1, vol_period 30). `standardise` puts it on the forecast scale: it is divided by the mean absolute value of its 60 previous readings and multiplied by 10, capped at ±20. The forecast is positive after an up day and negative after a down day. Its size grows linearly with the day's move in swing units. It is zero only after a flat day. There is no regime and no dead zone, because the idea is a graded relation on every day.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window (positions 0, 5, 10, ...). On it, take the least-squares slope of the coin's excess 5-bar return on the forecast at the grid bar. The excess return is close[t+5]/close[t]−1 minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows. Report the slope's t and the number of cases.
- Supporting check: Among grid cases in the top fifth of |forecast|, the mean of sign(forecast) × the excess 5-bar return must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is above 0 with t ≥ 2 over at least 300 cases, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is below 0 with t ≤ −2 over at least 300 cases, and the top-fifth mean is below 0.
- Inconclusive: Any of these is inconclusive: - a slope with |t| < 2; - a slope whose sign disagrees with the top-fifth check; - fewer than 300 grid cases.
