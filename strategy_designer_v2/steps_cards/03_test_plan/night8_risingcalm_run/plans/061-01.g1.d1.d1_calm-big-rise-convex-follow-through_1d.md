Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day rise of at least one usual day's swing (30-day std), in a choppy calm market -> adapted: the rise of at least 1 std is a regime rule, and "after the big day" is made "while it is the day"; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise against the standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days, more than in proportion to the rise -> kept: a convex graded forecast held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets only on rises of 1 std or more, sized convexly on the raw swing-unit rise, so its result is the idea's.
Forecast: The indicator is the day's return divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). The regime detector selects only bars where this value is at least 1.0. On every other bar the book is flat. On a selected bar, the same value is standardised over its 60 past values (min 20), clipped at 0 so the book is long only, and squared with signed_power (exponent 2, pivot 10). The forecast is positive and grows faster than the rise. It is never negative and is zero outside the regime and outside the choppy calm profile. Each bet is held 5 bars.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: For each case (a long bet of the forecast), on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid, the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all windows; the mean over cases, with its t taken with the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The least-squares slope of each case's excess 5-day return on its forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: Mean excess 5-day return above 0 with t >= 2 over at least 30 cases, and the slope above 0.
- Reject: Mean excess 5-day return below 0 with t <= -2 over at least 30 cases.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, a confirming t with a slope at or below 0, or fewer than 30 cases.
