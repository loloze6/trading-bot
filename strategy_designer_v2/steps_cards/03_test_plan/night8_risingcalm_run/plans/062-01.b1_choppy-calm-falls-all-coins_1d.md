Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy calm market, a 5-day return measured against the 30-day daily swing -> kept: the 5-day return over the 30-day one-bar return std, with the profile added by code.
- Comparison: the larger the swing-scaled fall, the larger the further fall -> kept: the short position grows with the size of the swing-scaled fall.
- Outcome: the price falls further over the next 5 days -> kept: short, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same scaled fall, in the same profile, against the same 5-day further fall.
Forecast: One component, `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 5 and `vol_period` 30. Its raw value is the 5-day return divided by (the 30-day std of daily returns x sqrt 5). The constant sqrt 5 cancels in the next step. `standardise` (60/20) puts it on its own past scale, about 10 for an average move. `clip` with `max` 0 then keeps only falls. The forecast is 0 after a flat or rising 5 days. It is negative (short) after a 5-day fall and grows in size with the fall in swing units, up to -20. Code adds the choppy calm profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, take the bars with forecast below 0. Regress the coin's next 5-bar return, minus its mean 5-bar return over all windows, on the forecast. Report the slope's t, the cases of one day pooled. If the idea is true the slope is positive: a more negative forecast is followed by a lower excess return.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases in the short direction (the coin's 5-bar return after the signal, minus its normal drift, sign-flipped) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes (mean unusual move in the short direction above 0).
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger scaled falls are followed by larger rebounds.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a mean unusual move at or below 0. Also inconclusive with fewer than 30 grid bars with forecast below 0, or with those bars on fewer than 20 distinct days.
