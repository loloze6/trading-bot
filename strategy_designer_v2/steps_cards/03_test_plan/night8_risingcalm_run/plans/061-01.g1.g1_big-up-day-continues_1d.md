Fidelity:
- Situation: a one-day rise, measured against the standard deviation of the past 30 days' daily returns -> adapted: graded, a larger rise in swing units gives a larger long position, with a graded dead zone on small days; zero on down days.
- Comparison: the day's rise against the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns -> kept
- Outcome: the price keeps rising over the next 5 days, more after larger days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the day's rise in 30-day swing units, held for 5 days, so the test confirms or rejects exactly this relation.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1, vol_period 30, lag 0 and vol_lookback 1. Its raw value is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. This value is standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a typical day's size is about 10. It is then clipped at 0, which makes it long only: the forecast is 0 on down days and flat days. Finally it goes through signed_power with exponent 2 and pivot 10. Small up days get a position close to 0, and large up days get a position that grows with the square of their size, up to the +20 cap. The forecast is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over 5 bars after each case. A case is a long-side rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 5-day return minus its normal 5-day drift (holding the coin). Its t is computed with same-day cases pooled.
- Supporting check: The cases are split at the median forecast. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails, or fewer than 100 cases.
