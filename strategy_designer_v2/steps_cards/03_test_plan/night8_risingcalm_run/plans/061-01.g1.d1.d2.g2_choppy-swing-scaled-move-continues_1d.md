Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 5-day move, in a choppy market -> adapted: graded by the 5-day return over 30-day daily-return std; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the move set against the std of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept
- Outcome: keeps moving the same way over the next 5 days, more for larger moves -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's scaled move, signed and graded, held 5 days inside the choppy profile only.
Forecast: One component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 5, vol_period 30). It is the 5-day return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. Its constant 1/sqrt(5) factor cancels under standardise. It is standardised over its 60 past values, so an average move gives a forecast of about 10, capped at ±20. The forecast is positive after a 5-day rise and negative after a 5-day fall, in the same direction as the move. Its size grows linearly with the move in swing units. It is near 0 when the coin barely moved. There is no regime of its own: the code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On each window's non-overlapping 5-bar grid, take the bars with a nonzero forecast. On those bars, regress the coin's next 5-bar return, minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows, on the forecast. Pool all coins and give the t of the slope, with cases of the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, compare the top third of |forecast| with the bottom third. The mean of sign(forecast) x the drift-adjusted 5-bar return must be larger in the top third (top minus bottom > 0).
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger moves are followed by reversals.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more with a failed supporting check. So is any result on fewer than 150 grid cases with a nonzero forecast.
