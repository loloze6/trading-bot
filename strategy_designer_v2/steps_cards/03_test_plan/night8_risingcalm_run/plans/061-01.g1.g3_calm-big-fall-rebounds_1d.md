- Situation: a coin's one-day fall, measured in its past-30-day daily-return standard deviations, in a calm market -> kept: the fall in 30-day swing units; the calm profile is added by code.
- Comparison: none -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises back over the next 10 days, more after a larger fall -> kept: long, graded by the fall's size, held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall-in-swing-units, long only on down days inside the calm profile, judged on the 10-day rise.
Forecast: Inside the calm profile (added by code), the forecast uses one indicator, `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 1 and `vol_period` 30. Its raw value is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. The forecast standardises that value over its past 60 values, negates it, and clips it at 0. It is 0 on up days and flat days. On a down day it is positive, and it grows linearly with the fall measured in 30-day swing units: a fall of the usual size gives about 10, and the forecast is capped at 20. It is never negative, so the book is long only.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The bars used are those on the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window where the forecast is above 0. On these bars, regress the coin's 10-bar forward return minus its mean 10-bar return over all windows (holding the coin) on the forecast, all coins pooled. Report the slope's t, with grid bars falling on the same day summed into one case.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean excess 10-bar return (forward return minus the coin's mean 10-bar return) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, or the mean excess return is below 0 with a t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any result in between, a t between -2 and 2, is inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 independent cases (days).
