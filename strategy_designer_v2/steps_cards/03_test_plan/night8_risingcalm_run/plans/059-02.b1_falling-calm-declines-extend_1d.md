Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, calm market, close below its 14-day highest close, in 30-day daily-swing units -> kept: the profile is added by code, and VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent computes (close - 14-day high close) / high / 30-day return std.
- Comparison: deeper declines against shallower ones -> kept: the short grows with the depth.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 5 days -> kept: a 5-bar short held 5 bars, judged against the coin's drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the same depth measure, profile and horizon decide both a confirm and a reject.
Forecast: The distance of today's close below the highest close of the last 14 daily closes (today included), divided by that high and by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), so the average depth reads about -10, capped at -20. The forecast is 0 at a new 14-day high and grows short as the close sinks deeper below the high in usual daily moves. It is never positive. Code keeps it flat outside the falling-calm profile.

Profile: falling calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Take bars with forecast below 0, on each window's non-overlapping 5-bar grid. On them, regress the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its normal 5-bar drift (all windows) on the forecast. Report the slope's t with cases of one day pooled. A positive slope means deeper short forecasts are followed by larger drift-adjusted falls.
- Supporting check: Compute the mean unusual move of these cases in the short direction (the 5-bar fall beyond the coin's drift). It must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the mean unusual move in the short direction is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, meaning deeper declines are followed by relatively larger rises.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a slope t of 2 or more with a mean unusual move at or below 0. The test needs at least 30 grid cases with forecast below 0, on at least 20 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
