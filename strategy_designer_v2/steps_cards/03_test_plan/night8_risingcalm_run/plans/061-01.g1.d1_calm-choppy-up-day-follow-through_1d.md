Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy, calm market, a coin's one-day rise against the std of its daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: the 1-day return over the 30-day std (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30), inside the choppy calm profile that code adds.
- Comparison: the larger the rise in swing units, the further the rise that follows -> adapted: the position is graded by the rise's size in swing units, never fixed.
- Outcome: keeps rising over the next 5 days; long only, falling days not shorted -> adapted: "after the day" becomes "while the day's reading holds", each bet held 5 bars; down days are clipped to 0.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own measure, sized by it, held for its 5-day horizon, and long only inside its profile.
Forecast: One component, the day's return divided by the std of the last 30 daily returns (today's included). It is standardised over its past 60 values and then clipped at 0. On an up day the forecast is positive and grows linearly with the rise in swing units, up to the cap of 20. On flat or down days it is 0, so the book never goes short. It runs on every bar; code keeps it flat outside the choppy calm profile.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the 5-bar forward return in excess of the coin's mean 5-bar return over all windows, regressed on the forecast. It uses non-overlapping 5-bar grid bars with forecast > 0, pooled over coins and windows, with its t.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases (forward 5-bar return minus the coin's normal drift) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the mean unusual move of the cases is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger rises are followed by smaller or more negative excess returns.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a confirming slope with a mean unusual move of 0 or below. Fewer than 50 grid cases with forecast > 0 is inconclusive whatever the t.
