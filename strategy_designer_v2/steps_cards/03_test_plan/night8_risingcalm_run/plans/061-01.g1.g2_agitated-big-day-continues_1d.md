Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day move against its 30-day daily-return standard deviation, in an agitated market -> kept: the agitated profile is added by code, and the day's move in 30-day swing units is the graded size.
- Comparison: the day's move against the standard deviation of the past 30 days' daily returns -> kept
- Outcome: the price keeps moving in that day's direction over the next 5 days, more so the larger the day -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the signed day move in swing units, inside the agitated profile only, held for 5 days.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 30. Its raw value is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns, so a value of +1 is an up day of one usual swing. It is standardised against its past 60 values (min_periods 20), which gives about ±10 on an average day, capped at ±20. The forecast is long after an up day and short after a down day, and 0 only on a flat day. Its size grows linearly with the day's size in swing units. Code holds it flat outside the agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope of a least-squares fit of the coin's 5-bar forward return, minus its mean 5-bar return over all windows, on the bar's forecast. It uses non-overlapping grid bars (0, 5, 10, ... in each window) where the forecast is not zero. All coins and windows are pooled, the cases of one day are summed together, and the slope's t is reported.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 on at least 100 cases.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2 on at least 100 cases.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or fewer than 100 non-overlapping cases with a non-zero forecast.
