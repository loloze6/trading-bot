Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy calm market, a one-day rise above the coin's 30-day daily-return std -> adapted: the profile is added by code, "exceeds one std" is a regime (rise ≥ 1 std), and the position inside it is graded by the size in swing units.
- Comparison: the day's rise against the past 30 days' std of daily returns -> kept: the rise is divided by the 30-bar std of one-bar returns.
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days, more the larger the excess -> kept: each bet is held 5 bars, long, and sized by the excess.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys only calm, choppy up days beyond one swing and sizes them by swing units, which is exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the day's return in usual moves: `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with period 1 and vol_period 30, which is today's return over the std of the last 30 daily returns. A bar enters the active regime only when this value is 1.0 or more, a rise of at least one usual swing. Every other bar is flat. The choppy calm profile vetoes are added by code. Inside the regime the forecast is the equal-weight mean of two parts: - the same scaled return, standardised (window 60, min_periods 20); - a standardised constant, negated to -10. The forecast is therefore about (z_std − 10)/2. It is near zero for a rise of about one usual swing, because the typical size of the scaled return makes z_std about 12 there. It grows with the number of swings the day covered, up to +5 where the standardised value reaches its cap of 20. The forecast is positive inside the regime and zero outside it.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual move over the cases. A case is a long bet, a forecast rise of at least 2 points, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows. The t of that mean is computed with each day's cases pooled into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of cases whose forecast is above the cases' median, minus that of cases at or below the median, is greater than 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with a t of −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a confirming t whose supporting check fails. Fewer than 30 cases, or fewer than 30 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
