Fidelity:
- Situation: a calm market and a one-day rise of more than one 30-day standard deviation of daily returns -> adapted: the calm profile is added by code; the day's rise in 30-day standard deviations is a regime condition (at least 1), graded by its size inside it.
- Comparison: larger rises against smaller rises, in units of the coin's usual daily swing -> kept: the position grows with the rise in standard deviations.
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 5 days -> adapted: "after the rise" becomes "while the rise is the bar's close", and the bet is held for 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: long bets on calm-market rises beyond one usual swing, sized by that rise and held 5 days, are what the idea claims.
Forecast: The detector computes the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). A bar is `trending` when that value is at least 1.0. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. Code adds the calm profile and keeps the book flat outside it. Inside `trending`, the same scaled return is standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20) and clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore positive and grows with the size of the rise in usual swings, up to the cap of 20. It is zero on every other bar and never negative.

Profile: calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case: the coin's 5-day return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 5-day drift over all windows, long direction. A case is a long bet, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Its mean and its t are computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The cases in the upper half of entry forecasts have a mean unusual move above the cases in the lower half (the difference upper minus lower is above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a failed supporting check, is inconclusive. Fewer than 30 distinct days with cases is also inconclusive, whatever the t.
