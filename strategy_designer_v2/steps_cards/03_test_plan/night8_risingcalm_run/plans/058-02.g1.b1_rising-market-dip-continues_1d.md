Fidelity:
- Situation: the close's distance below its highest close of the past 5 days, measured against the usual daily swing over the past 30 days, in a rising market -> kept: the profile "rising" is added by code, and the distance in usual moves is measured as stated.
- Comparison: the depth of the dip, relative to the 30-day usual daily swing -> kept: the 5-day closing high is divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 5 days, the deeper the dip -> kept: a short position graded by depth, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a graded short that grows with the scaled dip gains more as the dip deepens exactly when the idea holds, and gains nothing or loses when it fails.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, `VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent`, with these settings: period 5, vol_period 30, source close, bar t included, base level. It computes (close minus the highest of the last 5 closes) divided by that high, divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns. The value is 0 at a new 5-day closing high and more negative the deeper the dip, in usual daily moves. It is standardised over its past 60 values, so a usual dip reads about -10, capped at -20. It is never positive, so the book is short in proportion to the dip and flat at the high. The profile code keeps the book flat outside rising markets.
