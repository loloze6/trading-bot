Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the coin's close sits below its highest close of the past 20 days, the further the stronger -> kept
- Comparison: the close against its 20-day highest close -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 5 days, more so the larger the gap -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: a short sized by the gap below the 20-day closing high, held 5 days inside the choppy profile, pays only if larger gaps are followed by larger falls.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, `DistanceFromHighComponent` (period 20, close source, current bar included, percent form). It is the close's percent distance below its highest close of the last 20 days. It is 0 at a new high and negative below it. `standardise` (60/20) puts it on the forecast scale, about -10 at the usual gap and down to -20 at twice the usual gap. The forecast is therefore never positive. It is 0 at the 20-day high and grows more negative (a larger short) the further the close sits below the high. The choppy profile is added by code, which keeps the book flat outside it.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars on each window's 5-bar grid where the forecast is below 0. On each case, take the coin's 5-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all windows, and take it in the short direction. Weight it by |forecast| divided by the mean |forecast| of the cases. Then compute the t of the mean of these weighted excess short returns, with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The mean excess short return of the larger-gap half minus that of the smaller-gap half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean excess short return is above 0 with t >= 2, and the larger-gap half beats the smaller-gap half.
- Reject: The weighted mean excess short return is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected in these cases: - t is between -2 and 2; - t >= 2 but the larger-gap half does not beat the smaller-gap half; - there are fewer than 100 grid cases, or fewer than 60 distinct days with a case.
