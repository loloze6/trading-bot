Fidelity:
- Situation: a one-day rise, measured against the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, that closes at the highest close of the past 20 days, in a choppy calm market -> kept: the 20-day closing high is a regime condition, the rise in 30-day std units is graded inside it, and code adds the profile.
- Comparison: a rise of the same size that ends well below the 20-day high -> kept: the book holds nothing there, and the comparison is recomputed from prices in the supporting check.
- Outcome: the price keeps rising over the next 5 days, more after larger rises -> kept: the book is long, sized by the rise, and held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on 20-day-high closes and grows with the rise in swing units, so its 5-day results confirm or reject exactly the claim.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the close's percent distance from its highest close of the last 20 days, today included (DistanceFromHigh, period 20). The bar is active only when that distance is 0, which means today's close is the 20-day closing high. On every other bar the forecast is 0. On active bars the forecast is today's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturn, period 1, vol_period 30). It is standardised against its past 60 values and clipped at 0 from below, so it is long only. The position grows linearly with the rise in swing units: an ordinary-size rise gives about +10, and the cap is +20. The choppy calm profile is added by code.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Cases are the forecast's bets: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 5-bar grid cell of each window. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return from the case's close minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all windows. The measure is the least-squares slope of that excess return on the case's forecast, with t computed on day-clustered cases.
- Supporting check: Recompute from prices, inside the same profile, the rises whose 30-day-std size is in the same range as the cases but whose close is at least 5% below the 20-day closing high. Their mean 5-day excess return must be below that of the cases (cases minus far-below rises greater than 0).
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting difference is greater than 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2 is inconclusive. So is a slope with t ≥ 2 when the supporting difference is ≤ 0. At least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days are required; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
