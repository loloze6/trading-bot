Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin in a choppy, calm market has a one-day move -> kept: the profile is added by code, and the move is the day's signed return.
- Comparison: that day's move against the std of daily returns over the past 30 days -> kept: return / 30-day std of daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent).
- Outcome: the price keeps moving in that day's direction over the next 5 days, more for larger days -> kept: a signed forecast held for 5 bars and graded by the scaled move.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled move, signed and graded, held for its 5-day horizon inside its profile.
Forecast: One indicator, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 1, vol_period 30). It is bar t's close-to-close return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a usual-sized day reads about ±10, capped at ±20. The forecast is positive after an up day and negative after a down day, and its size grows linearly with the day's move in swing units. It is near 0 on a flat day. Code adds the choppy calm profile, so the book is flat outside it.
Config: 061-01.g1_choppy-calm-day-follow-through_1d_config.json
Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 5-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all windows, regressed on the forecast. It uses non-overlapping 5-bar grid bars of each window where the forecast is not 0, all coins pooled, with its t.
- Supporting check: On those grid bars with |forecast| ≥ 10, the mean of sign(forecast) × (5-bar forward return − coin's mean 5-bar return) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: A slope with |t| < 2, or a positive slope with t ≥ 2 while the check is not above 0. A test needs at least 100 grid cases with a non-zero forecast; with fewer it is inconclusive.
