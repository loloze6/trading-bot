Forecast: One component, `MovingAverageDistanceComponent` with period 20, SMA, base "usual_moves" and vol_period 20. It reads (close − SMA20) / SMA20 / std of the last 20 daily returns: negative below the average and larger in size the further below. It is standardised over 60 bars (min 20) against its own past mean size, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore 0 at or above the average and negative (short) below it, and it grows smoothly with the stretch, to −10 at a usual-sized distance and −20 at twice that. It is never long. The code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.
