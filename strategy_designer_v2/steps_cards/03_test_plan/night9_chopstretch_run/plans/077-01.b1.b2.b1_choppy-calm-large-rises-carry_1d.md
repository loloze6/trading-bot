- Situation: a coin's 14-day rise that is large, in a choppy calm market -> adapted: "large" is a regime, the 14-day return at or above 2 of its usual 14-day moves (convention), graded inside it; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise measured against its own daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 14-day return over (30-day std of daily returns x sqrt(14)).
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 7 days, more after larger swing-scaled rises -> kept: long, sized by the swing-scaled rise, held 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys only after large swing-scaled 14-day rises in choppy calm markets, sized by the rise, and judges the next 7 days' move.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 14, vol_period 30), which is the 14-day return in usual 14-day moves (the 30-day daily swing times sqrt(14)). The regime detector sends a bar to `trending` only when this value is at least 2.0. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is null, so the book is flat there. Inside the regime the same measure is standardised against its own past 60 values (target 5, so the threshold rise sits near mid-scale and does not saturate at once) and clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore positive, and it grows with the swing-scaled rise up to the cap of 20. It is never negative. It is zero outside the regime and outside the choppy calm profile, which code adds.

Measure the mean of this unusual move, with its t computed with cases of one day taken together.

- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when the mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2 and the supporting correlation is above 0.
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t with a correlation of 0 or below. So are fewer than 30 cases, or cases falling on fewer than 20 distinct days.
