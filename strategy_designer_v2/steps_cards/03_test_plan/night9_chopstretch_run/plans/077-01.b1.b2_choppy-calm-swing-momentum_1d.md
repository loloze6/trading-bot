Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin in a choppy, calm market showing a 14-day move up or down -> kept: the choppy-calm profile is added by code, and the 14-day return is measured on every bar inside it.
- Comparison: the move measured against the coin's own daily swing over the past 30 days -> kept: the 14-day return is divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: the price carries on the same way over the next 7 days, further for larger swing-scaled moves -> kept: a signed, graded position held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's swing-scaled 14-day move, both ways and sized by its size, inside the idea's profile, so the test's result is the idea's result.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 14 and `vol_period` 30. On each bar it computes the 14-day return divided by (the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns x sqrt(14)), so the move is counted in usual swings. `standardise` (window 60, min_periods 20) then puts this value on the scale of its own past values, about ±10 on average and capped at ±20. Next, `signed_power` (exponent 2, pivot 10) damps moves of ordinary size smoothly towards 0 and enlarges large ones, as the brief says ordinary moves carry nothing. The sign is kept throughout. The forecast is positive (long) after a rise and negative (short) after a fall, and it is near 0 for small moves. Its size grows with the square of the swing-scaled move. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy-calm profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 7-bar forward return, minus that coin's normal 7-bar drift over all its windows, regressed on the forecast. It uses non-overlapping 7-bar grid bars with a nonzero forecast, and cases on the same day are combined into one when computing t.
- Supporting check: Both sides must carry. On long cases the mean excess forward return must be above 0, and on short cases it must be below 0 (the move continues in the forecast's direction on each side).
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes on both the long and the short side.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger swing-scaled moves are followed by reversals.
- Inconclusive: The slope's t is between -2 and 2, or the slope's t is 2 or more but one side fails the check. A result also needs at least 100 grid cases with a nonzero forecast, with at least 30 long and 30 short; fewer cases is inconclusive.
