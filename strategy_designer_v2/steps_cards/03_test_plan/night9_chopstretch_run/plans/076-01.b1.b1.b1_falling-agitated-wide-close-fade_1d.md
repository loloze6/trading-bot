Forecast: Inside the regime, the forecast is set by CloseFromMidRangeInMeanRangesComponent (base_period 20). Its raw value is (close − (high+low)/2) / the mean high-low range of the last 20 days. That equals (2 × close location − 1)/2 × the day's range multiple, so it is the product of width and height above the middle. On those bars the forecast is short. It starts near 0 for a close just above the middle and grows with width × height, up to the cap of 20. On every other bar it is 0. The falling-agitated profile is added by code.
