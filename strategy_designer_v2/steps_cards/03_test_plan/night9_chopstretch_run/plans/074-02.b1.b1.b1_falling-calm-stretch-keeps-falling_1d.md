Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator: the distance of the close from its 20-day simple average, as a fraction of the average, divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. It is standardised against its own past 60 values, so a usual stretch reads about 10. It is then clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is therefore negative (short) only when the close is below its average, and the short grows with the depth of the stretch, up to the cap of 20. It is 0 at or above the average. The code keeps the book flat outside the falling calm profile.

- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual 5-bar move after each case, in the short direction. A case is a short bet, read once per 5-bar grid cell of each window. The unusual move is the coin's 5-bar return from the signal's close minus its normal 5-bar drift over all windows, sign-flipped. Its t takes the cases of one day together, before costs.
- Supporting check: The deeper half of the cases, by absolute forecast at the signal, has a larger mean unusual short move than the shallower half (difference deeper minus shallower above 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual short move is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual short move is below 0 with t <= -2 (the stretched coins rise more than their drift).
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A positive main measure with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive, and so is any result with fewer than 30 cases or fewer than 20 distinct days.
