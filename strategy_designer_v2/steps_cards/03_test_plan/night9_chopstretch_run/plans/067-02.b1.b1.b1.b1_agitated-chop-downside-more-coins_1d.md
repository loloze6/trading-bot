Forecast: The forecast uses MovingAverageDistanceComponent with period 20, average "sma", base "usual_moves" and vol_period 20. Its raw value is (close − SMA20) / SMA20 divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns: the stretch from the average in usual daily moves. It is standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore negative (short) when the close is below its 20-day average and grows in size the further below it sits. It is 0 at or above the average, so there is never a long position. No regime of its own is used: the choppy-agitated profile is added by code, and the book is flat outside it.
