- Situation: in a choppy calm market, a coin's 14-day return is large against its 30-day daily swing -> adapted: the profile is added by code; inside it, "large" is graded by the 14-day return in 30-day daily-swing units, with the strongest graded dead zone.
- Comparison: the move against the coin's own 30-day daily swing -> kept
- Outcome: the coin keeps moving the same way over the next 7 days, and the carry grows faster than the move -> kept: the horizon is 7 bars, and a cubic graded dead zone gives the faster growth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the swing-scaled 14-day move, signed with it and growing faster than it, held 7 days inside the choppy calm profile, so a confirm or reject of its carry is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 14 and vol_period 30: the 14-day return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns (times sqrt 14, a constant that cancels). It is standardised over its past 60 values, so a usual-size move reads about ±10, capped at ±20. Then signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 20 is applied. A usual move of 10 becomes 2.5 and a move of 5 becomes 0.3, so the position is almost zero. The largest moves (20) give 20. The forecast is positive after a large rise and negative after a large fall, and grows with the cube of the move's size. It is zero only for a flat move. The profile (choppy calm) is added by code and keeps the book flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars on each window's non-overlapping 7-bar grid where the forecast is non-zero. For each case, the signed unusual move is sign(forecast) × (the coin's 7-day return − the coin's mean 7-day return over all windows). The measure is the mean of these values weighted by |forecast|, with its t computed by taking the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The growth check uses the cases in the top third of |forecast|. Their mean signed unusual move must exceed that of the bottom third (Welch t ≥ 1).
- Confirm: The weighted mean signed unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the growth check passes.
- Reject: The weighted mean signed unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ −2, meaning the moves reverse.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming main measure with a failed growth check is also inconclusive. So is a test with fewer than 100 grid cases, or with cases on fewer than 60 distinct days.
