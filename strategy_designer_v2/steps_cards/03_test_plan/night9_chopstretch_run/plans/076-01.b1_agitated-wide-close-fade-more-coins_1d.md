That equals the close's location in the day's range (0 at the low, 1 at the high) times the range multiple. It is standardised over 60 bars (min 20) and negated, so the forecast is never above zero. Its size grows with both width and the close's nearness to the high, multiplicatively, up to the −20 cap. A close at the day's low gives zero.
