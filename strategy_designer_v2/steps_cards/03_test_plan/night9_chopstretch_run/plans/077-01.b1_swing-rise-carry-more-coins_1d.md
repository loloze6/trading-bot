Fidelity:
- Situation: a 14-day return that is large and positive against the coin's 30-day daily swing -> adapted: a regime keeps bars where the 14-day return is at least 2 usual 14-day moves (from the daily swing, by sqrt(14)); inside it the forecast grows with that scaled rise.
- Comparison: the larger the swing-scaled rise, the larger the further rise -> kept: the position grows with the scaled rise, and the check compares outcomes across position sizes.
- Outcome: the coin keeps rising over the next 7 days -> kept: the 7-day forward return from the signal, held 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds positions only after large swing-scaled rises in choppy calm markets and sizes them by that rise, so a carry or its absence shows directly.
Forecast: One indicator, the 14-day return divided by the 30-day standard deviation of daily returns times sqrt(14) (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 14, vol_period 30). The regime detector puts a bar in the active regime only when this value is at least 2.0. No number is given for "large", so this uses the usual convention of 2 standard deviations. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is the same value, standardised against its own past 60 values. The target is 5 so that a rise just past the threshold gives about 12 and the forecast reaches the 20 cap only near 3 usual moves. Without this, the forecast would sit at the cap on every active bar. It is clipped at 0, so it is long only and positive, and it grows with the size of the swing-scaled rise. The choppy calm profile is added by code, and the book is flat outside it.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case: the coin's 7-day return from the close of the signal bar, minus the coin's normal 7-day drift. A case is a forecast rise of at least 2 points, read once per 7-bar grid cell. The measure is its mean and its t, with cases on the same day taken together. This is before costs and against holding the coin.
- Supporting check: The size of the forecast at the signal against the unusual move. Cases at or above the median forecast must have a larger mean unusual move than cases below it.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with a t of 2 or more, over at least 30 days with cases, and the check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with a t of -2 or less, over at least 30 days with cases.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected if the t lies between -2 and 2, if the t is 2 or more but the larger-forecast cases do not show the larger mean unusual move, or if there are fewer than 30 days with cases.
