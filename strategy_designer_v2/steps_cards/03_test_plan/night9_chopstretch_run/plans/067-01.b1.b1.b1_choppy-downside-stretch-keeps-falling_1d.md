- Situation: in a choppy market, the close stands below its 20-day average, in units of its 20-day daily-return std -> kept: the choppy profile is added by code; the distance from the 20-day SMA is measured in usual 20-day moves.
- Comparison: the deeper the stretch below the average, the more it falls -> kept: the short is graded by the stretch's depth and is zero at or above the average.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 5 days -> kept: short, held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own stretch, on its own side and horizon, so a confirm or reject of the graded short is a confirm or reject of the idea.
Forecast: One indicator, `MovingAverageDistanceComponent` (period 20, SMA, base "usual_moves", vol_period 20). It reads (close − SMA20) / SMA20 divided by the std of the last 20 daily returns. It is standardised against its own last 60 values (min_periods 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. - The forecast is negative (short) when the close is below its 20-day average. - It grows in size continuously with the depth of the stretch, up to the −20 cap. - It is exactly 0 at or above the average, with no jump at the edge. - Inside the choppy profile only; the code keeps the book flat elsewhere.

- Main measure: The cases are the bars with forecast < 0, on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window.
