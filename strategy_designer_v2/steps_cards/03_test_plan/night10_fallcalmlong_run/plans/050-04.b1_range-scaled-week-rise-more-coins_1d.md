Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, calm market, a coin that has risen over the last 7 days -> kept: the profile is added by code; the 7-day rise is clipped at 0, so the book is flat when the coin has not risen.
- Comparison: the 7-day rise in units of the 30-day average daily high-low range -> kept: the 7-day return divided by the 30-day mean of (high - low) / close.
- Outcome: keeps rising over the next 3 days, more for a larger scaled rise -> kept: a long position graded by the scaled rise, held for 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures exactly the scaled 7-day rise, inside the named profile, against the 3-day move that follows.
Forecast: One component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` with `period` 7, `window` 30, `base` "mean_relative_range" and `form` "ratio". It gives the 7-day return divided by the mean daily (high - low) / close over the last 30 days. It is standardised against its own past 60 values (`min_periods` 20), so a usual-sized value is about 10, capped at 20. It is then clipped at 0. The forecast is long and grows linearly with the scaled 7-day rise. It is 0 whenever the coin has not risen over the 7 days. It is never short. The detector is ungated, and the falling-calm profile is added by code.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 3-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 3-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift over all its windows. The t of the mean is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the slope of the unusual move on the case bar's forecast (`post_pipeline_value`) is positive.
- Confirm: The main measure has a mean above 0 with t >= 2, and the slope check is above 0. Both are needed.
- Reject: The main measure has a mean below 0 with t <= -2, or the slope is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including a positive main measure with t under 2, or a confirmed main measure with a slope at or below 0 and t above -2. At least 100 cases falling on at least 50 distinct days are required; with fewer, the test is inconclusive.
