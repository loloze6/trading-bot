Fidelity:
- Situation: an up day on volume light against the coin's 20-day average, in a falling calm market -> kept: the regime holds a position only on an up close with volume below its prior 20-day mean; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the lighter that day's volume against the 20-day average, the larger the rise -> kept: inside the regime, the forecast grows as the volume ratio falls below 1.
- Outcome: a rise over the next 5 days -> adapted: "followed by" becomes a bet made on the up day and held for the 5-day horizon.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own light-volume up days, graded by lightness, over its own 5 days.
Forecast: The forecast is positive only on a day that closes above the prior close (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 1) with volume below the mean of the prior 20 days (`VolumeRatioComponent`, period 20, base excluding the day, ratio below 1). It is zero on every other day, and the code keeps it zero outside falling calm. On those days the forecast is the volume ratio minus 1, standardised over 60 days (min 20) and negated. It is clipped at 0, so it is long only. A ratio just under 1 gives a small long. The lighter the day's volume, the larger the long, up to the cap of 20. Each such day is a bet held for 5 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a bet, read once per 5-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 5-bar return from the signal close minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows. The t is computed with cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Compare the cases above the median forecast with those below it. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0, so that lighter volume is followed by a larger rise.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is negative with t ≤ -2. It is also a reject if the upper-minus-lower difference has Welch t ≤ -2, meaning lighter up days are followed by smaller rises.
- Inconclusive: Any result between these is inconclusive: a main t between -2 and 2, or a main t ≥ 2 with a check difference at or below 0. Fewer than 30 distinct days with cases is also inconclusive.
