Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, calm market, heavy volume on up days over the last 20 days -> replaced: no component weighs up-day volume against a separate base, only up-day share of the same window (`UpDayVolumeShareComponent`).
- Comparison: against usual daily volume over the last 60 days -> replaced: `VolumeRatioComponent` (20 over 60) can see that base, but not tied to up days, and the config cannot multiply it with the share.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 5 days (5 bars) -> kept.
- Verdict transfer: no: the share or a sum of share and ratio is a different quantity, and the idea's consequence explicitly rejects up-day share as the measure.
Reason: The idea needs the volume traded on up days over 20 days, measured in units of the 60-day mean daily volume. No component computes it. The only way to get it from the catalogue is to multiply `UpDayVolumeShareComponent` by `VolumeRatioComponent`, and the config can only take weighted means of components, not products.
