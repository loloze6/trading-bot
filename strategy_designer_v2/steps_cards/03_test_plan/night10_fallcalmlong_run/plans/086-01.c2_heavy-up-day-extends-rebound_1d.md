- Situation: in a falling calm market, an up close on volume heavy against the 20-day average, with a positive 5-day return before that day -> adapted: a regime on that day (up close, volume at least 1.5x its prior 20-day average by convention, positive return from t-6 to t-1); the profile is added by code; "after the day" becomes "on the day", and the 10-day hold carries it.
- Comparison: the heavier the volume against its 20-day average, the larger the rise -> adapted: the forecast is graded by the standardised 20-day volume ratio.
- Outcome: a rise over the next 10 days -> kept: a 10-bar holding period, long only, judged against the coin's normal drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys exactly the stated days, sizes them by the stated volume ratio and holds them for the stated 10 days.
Forecast: The regime "trending" is active only on a day that meets three conditions: - the day closes up (1-day return above 0); - its volume is at least 1.5 times the mean volume of the 20 prior days; - the 5-day return ending the day before is positive (its volatility-scaled reading is above 0). On every other bar the forecast is 0. On a qualifying day the forecast is that day's 20-day volume ratio, standardised over its past 60 values (the usual day is about +10). Because the threshold sits at 1.5x, the forecast starts around +15 and grows with the volume ratio up to the cap of 20. It is never negative, so the strategy is long only. Each bet is held for 10 bars.
