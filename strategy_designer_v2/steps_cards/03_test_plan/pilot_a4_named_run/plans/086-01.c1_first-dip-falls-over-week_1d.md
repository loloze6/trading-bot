Fidelity:
- Situation: in a rising, agitated market, a coin closes down after a streak of consecutive up days -> kept: the profile is added by code, and a regime rule fires on the down close that breaks an up run of 2 or more days ("a streak", plural).
- Comparison: the fall is larger the more up days the streak held -> adapted: the threshold is made graded, so the short grows with the up-run's day count.
- Outcome: it keeps falling over the next 7 days -> kept: a short held for 7 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the 7-day move after the same dip bars, sized by the same day count.
Forecast: The signal is `PriorDownRunOnUpCloseComponent` (side "up", form "count", max_bars 30). On a down close it gives the length of the up run that the close breaks. On every other bar it is 0. The regime detector selects only the bars where that count is 2 or more. All other bars are flat. Inside that regime the same count is standardised over a 60-bar window. The standardise target is 2, not 10, because the count is 0 on about three bars in four. Its past mean size is about 0.5, so target 10 would put every dip at the +20 cap and the signal would be on/off. With target 2, a 2-day streak gives about 8, a 3-day streak about 12, and 5 days or more reach the cap. The value is then negated. The forecast is never positive (short only). It is zero except on qualifying dip bars, and it is more negative the more up days the streak held. Each bet is held for 7 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: For each case (a short bet on a qualifying dip bar, read once per 7-bar grid cell), take the coin's 7-bar return from the signal's close minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of this unusual move and its t, with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Regress each case's short-direction unusual move on its |forecast| (the streak's standardised size). The slope must be above 0 with a t of 2 or more.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting slope is positive with a t of 2 or more.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with a t of -2 or less (the price rises more than usual after the dip). Or the slope on |forecast| is negative with a t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive, including means or slopes with |t| below 2. Fewer than 40 cases, or cases on fewer than 40 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
