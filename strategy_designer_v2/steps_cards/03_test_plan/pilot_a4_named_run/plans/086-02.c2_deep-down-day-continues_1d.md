Forecast: The forecast reads `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` (period 1, vol_period 30). This is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. On up days and flat days it is 0. On a down day it is negative (short), and its size grows linearly with the depth of the fall in usual daily moves: about -10 for a typical-size move, capped at -20. It is never positive, so the book is short only. The code keeps it flat outside rising agitated markets and holds each bet for 10 bars.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move, measured over the cases. A case is a short bet: the forecast falls by at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 10-bar return from the signal's close, minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of these, with a t that sums each day's cases into one.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of the absolute forecast. The deeper half's mean unusual move in the short direction, minus the shallower half's, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure has t ≥ +2 (the coin falls more than its normal drift after the signals) and the supporting difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure has t ≤ -2 (the coin rises more than its normal drift after the signals).
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and +2. A main t ≥ +2 whose supporting difference is not above 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 distinct case days.
