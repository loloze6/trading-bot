Forecast: The indicator is the 3-day return divided by the 30-day mean of (high − low) / close, with bar t included. The regime detector selects `trending` only when this scaled rise is at least 1.0, that is a 3-day rise of at least one usual daily range. On every other bar the book is flat, and code adds the rising-agitated profile on top. Inside the regime, the forecast is the same scaled rise standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20) and clipped at 0. It is therefore positive and grows with the size of the rally, so a larger scaled rally gives a larger long. It is never short.
