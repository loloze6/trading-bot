Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 30). It is zero on down days and flat days. It is long-only and has no regime of its own: the rising-agitated profile is added by code, and the book is flat outside that profile.
