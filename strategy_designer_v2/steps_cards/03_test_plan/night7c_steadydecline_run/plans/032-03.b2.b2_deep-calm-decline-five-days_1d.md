Fidelity:
- Situation: volume over the past 28 days below the coin's own usual volume, with a fall over those 28 days -> adapted: the low-volume condition is a regime (28-day mean volume below the mean of the 90 days before it), and inside it the size of the 28-day fall is graded.
- Comparison: a larger 28-day fall against a smaller one -> kept: the short position grows with the depth of the 28-day fall.
- Outcome: a larger further fall over the next 5 days -> kept: each bet is held 5 bars, and the 5-day return is judged in the short direction against the coin's drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether deeper thin-volume 28-day falls are followed by larger 5-day falls, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where the 28-day mean volume is below the mean volume of the 90 days before that stretch (VolumeRatioComponent below 1). On every other bar it is 0. Inside that regime, the 28-day price change (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 28) is standardised over its last 60 values (min 20) and clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore negative (short) only after a 28-day fall, larger the deeper the fall, and 0 after a 28-day rise. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, take the cases where the forecast is below 0. For each case, compute the unusual move in the short direction: minus (close[t+5]/close[t] − 1 − the coin's mean 5-bar return over all windows). Weight it by |forecast|/10, sum the cases of one day together, and take the t of the mean across days.
- Supporting check: Over the same cases, the least-squares slope of the short-direction unusual move on |forecast| is above 0 (a deeper fall is followed by a larger further fall).
- Confirm: Main-measure t ≥ 2 and a positive slope, with at least 30 distinct case days.
- Reject: Main-measure t ≤ −2, with at least 30 distinct case days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2. Also a t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0, or fewer than 30 distinct case days.
