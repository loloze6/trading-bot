Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin has fallen over the past 28 days -> kept: a regime rule, active only while the 28-bar return is below 0.
- Comparison: volume over those 28 days against the coin's own usual volume -> kept: the 28-bar mean volume over the mean of the 90 bars before it, as a log ratio.
- Outcome: the lower that volume, the larger the further fall over the next 5 days -> kept: the short grows as volume thins, and the position is held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: within 28-day declines the forecast is the relative volume itself, so the slope of 5-day returns on it is the idea's relation.
Forecast: Only while the 28-day return is negative (regime "trending"); flat otherwise. Inside that regime, the forecast is ln(mean volume of the last 28 days / mean volume of the 90 days before them), standardised against its own past 118 values. It is negative (short) when the decline's volume is below usual, growing in size as volume gets thinner. It is positive when volume is above usual, near 0 at usual volume, and capped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bars in regime "trending" with a nonzero forecast, taken on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window. For each case, compute the 5-day forward return minus the coin's normal 5-day drift (its mean over all windows). Regress that excess return on the recorded forecast and report the t of the slope, with cases of the same day grouped (deviations summed per day).
- Supporting check: Cases with forecast < 0 (thin-volume declines) have a mean excess 5-day return below 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 (thinner volume, which gives a more negative forecast, is followed by a larger excess fall), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: A slope with |t| < 2, or a confirming slope whose supporting check fails. A verdict needs at least 150 cases spread over at least 100 distinct days; fewer is inconclusive.
