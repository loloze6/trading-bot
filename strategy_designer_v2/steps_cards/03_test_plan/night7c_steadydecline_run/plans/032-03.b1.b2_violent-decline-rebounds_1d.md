Fidelity:
- Situation: coin fell over the past 28 days, with daily high-low ranges wider than its usual range -> kept: a regime holds only when the 28-day return is below 0 and the 28-day mean bar range exceeds the coin's 365-day mean bar range (the convention for "usual").
- Comparison: the larger the 28-day fall, the larger the rise -> kept: inside the regime the long forecast is graded by the size of the 28-day fall.
- Outcome: price rises over the next 5 days -> kept: horizon 5 daily bars, long side.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures exactly whether deeper wide-range 28-day falls are followed by larger 5-day rises, and holds nothing outside that situation.
Forecast: Two regime conditions are measured on each bar. The first is the 28-day percent return, which must be below 0. The second is the ratio of the 365-day mean high-low range to the 28-day mean high-low range, which must be below 1, so the recent bars are wider than usual. When both hold, the bar is in the "mean_reversion" regime. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime the forecast is the 28-day percent return, standardised over its past 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20) and then negated. It is therefore positive, a long position, and grows with the depth of the fall: an average fall gives about +10 and the deepest falls give up to +20. It never goes below zero inside the regime, because the 28-day return there is always negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, take the in-regime bars (forecast > 0). For each, compute the coin's 5-day return minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all its windows. Regress this excess return on the forecast, pooled over coins, and read the slope's t.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 5-day return over those same in-regime grid bars is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes (mean excess return > 0).
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less. Deeper wide-range falls are then followed by smaller rises or larger further falls.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A slope t of 2 or more is also inconclusive when the mean excess return is 0 or below. The test is inconclusive in any case with fewer than 100 in-regime grid cases pooled over coins and windows.
