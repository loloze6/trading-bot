- Situation: close above its close 28 days ago, close near its 28-day highest close, on a day whose range is wide against its last 28 days' ranges -> adapted: uptrend and wide range are regime conditions (28-day return > 0, today's range above its 28-day mean range); "near the high" is graded by proximity to the 28-day highest close.
- Comparison: today's high-low range against the ranges of the last 28 days; the close against the 28-day highest close -> kept
- Outcome: a rise over the next 5 days, larger the nearer the close is to the high -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds only on uptrend, wide-range days, and it sizes the long by nearness to the high, so its result over 5 days answers the idea as stated.
Forecast: Two conditions select the regime. The first is that the 28-day price change, `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 28, is above 0. The second is that today's high-low range over the mean bar range of the last 28 bars, `RangeCompressionRatioComponent` with short 1, long 28, base "mean_bar_range" and form "width", is at least 1. On those bars the forecast is long only. It comes from `DistanceFromHighComponent` (period 28, closes, form "proximity", power 1), which equals 1/(1+D), where D is the percent distance below the 28-day highest close. That value is standardised (60/20), then put through `signed_power` with exponent 2. A close at the high gives a large long. A close a few percent below the high gives a small long, damped toward 0. The forecast is never negative. On every other bar it is 0.
