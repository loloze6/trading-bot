Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin closing at its highest close of the last 28 days -> kept: a regime that is active only on bars where the close equals the 28-bar closing high (the consequence's level).
- Comparison: daily-return volatility over the last 20 days against the last 60 days -> kept: the ratio of the 20-day to the 60-day return standard deviation, so the quieter the coin, the larger the long.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 5 days, the quieter the coin -> kept: the 5-day forward return, judged by how it grows with the forecast.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same situation, comparison and horizon, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: Off the 28-day closing high the forecast is 0. On a bar whose close equals the highest close of the last 28 bars, the regime is "trending". There the forecast is built from RecentToPriorVolatilityRatioComponent (vol_period 20, base_period 60, base_lag 0, form "centred"). That component gives s20/s60 − 1. The forecast standardises it over 61 bars and negates it. It is positive and grows as the 20-day volatility falls below the 60-day one, so the more compressed the coin, the larger the long. It is negative when recent volatility has expanded, about 0 when the two are equal, and capped at ±20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are regime "trending" bars on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its normal 5-bar drift (its mean 5-bar return over all windows). Regress that excess return on the bar's forecast and read the t of the slope.
- Supporting check: Among the same cases, the mean excess 5-bar return of the top third of forecasts minus that of the bottom third is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the top-third minus bottom-third difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is −2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t of 2 or more when the top-minus-bottom difference is not above 0. Fewer than 100 grid cases across all coins and windows is inconclusive whatever the result.
