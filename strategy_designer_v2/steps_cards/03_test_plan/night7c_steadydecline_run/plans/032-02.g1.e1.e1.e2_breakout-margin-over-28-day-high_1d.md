Fidelity:
- Situation: a close above the highest close of the previous 28 days -> kept: a regime fires only when the close exceeds the 28-day prior closing high (bar t excluded).
- Comparison: how far the close exceeds that prior high -> kept: the percent margin over the prior high grades the long.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 5 days for a larger margin -> kept: each bet is held 5 daily bars, long only.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this breakout margin against this 5-day rise, only on fresh 28-day closing highs.
Forecast: One indicator, the distance of the close from the highest close of the prior 28 days (`DistanceFromHighComponent`, period 28, source close, prior bars only, percent). The detector selects `trending` only on bars where this distance is above 0, which is a fresh 28-day closing high. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is null, so the book is flat there. On breakout bars the forecast is the margin in percent, standardised against the indicator's own last 60 values. Those values are mostly distances below the high, so a breakout as large as the usual recent distance reads about +10. A `clip` at 0 keeps the forecast long only. The forecast is 0 at a margin of 0 and grows linearly with the margin, up to +20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are breakout bars (regime `trending`, forecast > 0) on a non-overlapping 5-bar grid in each window. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return minus its normal 5-day drift (its mean over all windows). Regress this excess return on the forecast (`post_pipeline_value` of `margin`) and read the slope's t, with cases of the same day summed into one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean excess 5-day return over the cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less: larger breakout margins are followed by smaller excess 5-day returns.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope with a weighted mean excess return at or below 0. Fewer than 100 breakout cases (or fewer than 60 distinct days) across the coins is inconclusive whatever the t.
