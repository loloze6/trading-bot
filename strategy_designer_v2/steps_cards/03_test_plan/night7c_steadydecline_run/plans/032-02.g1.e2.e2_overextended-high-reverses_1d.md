Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin closes at its 28-day highest close -> kept: a regime condition, distance from the 28-day closing high (bar t included) at or above 0.
- Comparison: the 28-day rise against the coin's own usual 28-day move -> kept: the 28-day return divided by the mean absolute 28-day return over the past 365 days, graded.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next 5 days -> kept: a short position, larger as the scaled rise grows, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets short only at 28-day closing highs, sized by the scaled run-up over 5 days, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: On bars where the close equals its 28-day highest close, the forecast is the 28-day return divided by the mean absolute 28-day return of the past 365 days (MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent, period 28, window 365). This value is standardised against its own past (window 393, min_periods 98) and negated. A clip at max 0 keeps only the short side. The forecast is therefore negative and grows in size with the run-up's size in usual 28-day moves. It is 0 if the 28-day return is not positive, and 0 (flat) on every bar that does not close at its 28-day high.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the trending-regime bars on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window. On each, take the coin's next-5-bar return minus its mean 5-bar return over all windows (the drift of holding it). Regress it on the case's strength, abs(post_pipeline_value of runup). The measure is the slope's t.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 5-bar return over those cases, taken in the short direction, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope has t ≤ -2 (a larger scaled run-up is followed by a larger fall beyond drift) and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope has t ≥ +2 (a larger run-up is followed by a smaller fall or a larger rise).
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2, or a slope t ≤ -2 when the supporting check fails. At least 30 independent cases across coins and windows are needed, else the result is inconclusive.
