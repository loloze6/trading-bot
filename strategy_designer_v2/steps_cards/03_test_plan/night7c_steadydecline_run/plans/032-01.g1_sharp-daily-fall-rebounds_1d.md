Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily fall that is large against its own typical daily move over the past 20 days -> adapted: a regime rule fires when the day's return is -2 or less in 20-day standard deviations, the most common convention for "large"; inside it the position grows with the fall's size.
- Comparison: the coin's typical daily move over the past 20 days -> kept: the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 to 2 days, more after a larger fall -> kept: long for 2 bars, sized by the fall in usual moves.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same fall, the same yardstick and the same 2-day rebound, so its verdict is the idea's.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 20). On bars where that value is -2 or lower, the forecast is the same scaled return, standardised, then negated. The standardise target is 4, so the forecast stays graded across the range of large falls: a 2-usual-move fall gives about +10 and a 4-usual-move fall reaches the +20 cap. The default target of 10 would put every case near the cap. The forecast is always positive (long) in that regime and larger the deeper the fall. On every other bar it is 0 (flat).

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bets: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 2-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's return over the next 2 bars minus its mean 2-bar return over all windows (holding the coin). Report the mean of these excess returns and its t, with cases on the same day taken together, before costs.
- Supporting check: Across cases, the slope of the excess 2-bar return on the case's forecast (its fall size in usual moves) is positive, with t of 2 or more.
- Confirm: The mean excess return is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting slope is positive with t ≥ 2.
- Reject: The mean excess return is negative with t ≤ -2, or the slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including t between -2 and 2 on either measure, or fewer than 30 cases (case-days) in total.
