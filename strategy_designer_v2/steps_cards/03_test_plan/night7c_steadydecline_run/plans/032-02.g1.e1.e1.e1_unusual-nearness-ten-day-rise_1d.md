Fidelity:
- Situation: how close the coin closes to its highest close of the last 28 days -> kept: `DistanceFromHighComponent` (28 closes, bar t included), in percent.
- Comparison: against how close it has usually closed over the past 60 days -> kept: `standardise` over the 60 prior values, centred by a +10 offset, so the forecast is positive when the coin sits nearer the high than its usual distance.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 10 days, whatever the 28-day return -> kept: no return condition; the 10-bar hold runs from the 1d close.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded closeness against its 60-day norm, and the test measures 10-day returns that grow with it.
Forecast: The forecast has two parts with equal weight. - The first is the percent distance of the close below its 28-day highest close. It is always 0 or negative. It is standardised by the mean absolute distance of the 60 prior days, so it is about -10 at the usual distance, 0 at the high and down to -20 when the close is twice as far as usual. - The second is the constant +10. The forecast is their mean. It reaches +5 at a new 28-day closing high and is positive whenever the close sits nearer the high than usual. It is 0 at the usual distance. It is negative, down to -5, when the close is farther than usual. Its size grows with how unusually close or far the close sits. There is no regime condition.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: On the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window, take each cell's forecast / 10 x (the coin's 10-bar forward return minus its mean 10-bar return over all windows). Measure the mean of this timing gain and its t, with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 10-bar return of grid cells with forecast >= +2, minus that of cells with forecast <= -2, is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean timing gain is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean timing gain is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t >= 2 with the supporting check failing. At least 300 grid cases on at least 150 distinct days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
