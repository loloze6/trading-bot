Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin's volume over the past 28 days is below its own usual volume -> kept: a regime holds when the 28-day mean volume is below the mean of the 90 days before it (conventional base); flat elsewhere.
- Comparison: the larger the fall over those 28 days -> adapted: graded, so the short grows with the 28-day fall, and it is zero when the coin did not fall.
- Outcome: a larger further fall over the next 10 days, downward -> kept: short, held 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures, only on thin-volume bars, whether a deeper 28-day fall comes before a larger 10-day fall, net of the coin's drift.
Forecast: The forecast exists only on bars where the mean volume of the last 28 days is below the mean volume of the 90 days before them (ratio < 1). Outside those bars it is 0. Inside them, the 28-day price change is standardised over its past 60 values (about 10 for a usual size) and clipped at 0 from above. A coin that rose over 28 days has a forecast of 0. A coin that fell has a negative forecast, a short, which grows in size with the depth of the fall, up to -20. The forecast is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: Take bars on a non-overlapping 10-bar grid in each window, where the regime is mean_reversion and the forecast is below 0. On those bars, regress the coin's 10-bar forward return minus its normal 10-bar drift (its mean over all windows) on the forecast. Report the slope's t, with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Over the same cases, the mean drift-adjusted 10-bar return in the short direction (-(return - drift)) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2: a more negative forecast is followed by a lower drift-adjusted return. The supporting mean is also above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t <= -2: deeper thin-volume falls are followed by relatively higher returns.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A positive slope whose supporting mean is not above 0 is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 30 independent cases (grid cells on distinct days), else it is inconclusive.
