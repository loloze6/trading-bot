Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin closes at or near its highest close of the last 28 days -> kept: regime condition, close equals its 28-day closing high (the consequence's "a close at the 28-day high"), flat elsewhere.
- Comparison: among those closes, a smaller 28-day return versus a larger one -> kept: inside the regime the long size falls as the 28-day return grows.
- Outcome: the price rises more over the next 5 days after a smaller 28-day return -> kept: long held 5 bars, size graded by how small the run-up was.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether 5-day gains after 28-day highs grow as the prior 28-day return shrinks, which is exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless today's close is the highest close of the last 28 daily bars, read from DistanceFromHigh with period 28, which is 0 exactly at that high. On those bars, the 28-day price change (PriceEvolution, period 28) is standardised against its past 60 values, giving x, at most +20. The forecast is 2 × (+10 constant) − 1 × x, so 20 − x. It is near +20 when the new high comes from a flat or slightly negative 28-day base. It shrinks toward 0 as the run-up gets steeper, and is 0 at the steepest. It never goes negative, so a steep climb is trimmed, never shorted.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Cases are the bars in regime "trending" on each window's non-overlapping 5-bar grid. For each case, take the coin's 5-day return minus its mean 5-day return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the case's forecast and read the t of the slope.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean of the cases' excess 5-day return is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope t is 2 or more and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope t is −2 or less, meaning larger positions are followed by smaller excess rises.
- Inconclusive: Anything else is inconclusive, including a t between −2 and 2, or a confirming slope with a weighted mean at or below 0. At least 100 cases are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
