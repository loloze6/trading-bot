Fidelity:
- Situation: volume over the past 28 days below the coin's own usual volume -> kept: a regime where the 28-day mean volume is below the mean of the prior 365 days, with nothing held outside it.
- Comparison: inside that situation, a larger 28-day fall against a smaller one -> adapted: graded, so the short grows with the depth of the 28-day fall.
- Outcome: a larger further fall over the next 10 days -> kept: a short held 10 bars, sized by the fall.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets only in thin-volume stretches, grades the short by the fall, and judges the next 10 days.
Forecast: On bars where the 28-day mean volume is below the mean volume of the 365 days before that stretch, the forecast is the 28-day percent price change, standardised over 60 bars and clipped at 0 from above. It is short only, and the short grows linearly with the depth of the fall. It is 0 when the coin did not fall over 28 days. On bars where the 28-day volume is at or above usual, the forecast is 0 (flat). It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The cases are bars on the 10-bar non-overlapping grid of each window that are in the thin-volume regime with a forecast below 0. The measure is the slope of the coin's 10-bar forward return, minus the coin's mean 10-bar return over all windows, on the forecast. A positive slope means a deeper fall is followed by a lower return; its t uses cases grouped by day.
- Supporting check: Over the same cases, the mean excess 10-bar return in the short's direction (minus the coin's excess return) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting mean is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming slope whose supporting mean is 0 or below. Fewer than 30 cases, or fewer than 20 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
