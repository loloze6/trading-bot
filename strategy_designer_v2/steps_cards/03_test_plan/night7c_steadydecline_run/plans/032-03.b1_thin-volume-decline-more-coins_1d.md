Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin has fallen over the past 28 days -> kept: a regime rule on the 28-bar return below 0; every other bar is flat.
- Comparison: the 28-day volume against the coin's own usual volume, lower meaning stronger -> adapted: the log ratio of mean volume over the last 28 bars to the 90 bars before them, graded so that thinner volume gives a larger short.
- Outcome: a larger further fall over the next 5 days, the thinner the volume -> kept: the book holds short for 5 bars, and its size grows as the volume gets thinner.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is short only inside 28-day declines and grows as volume falls below usual, so a short return that scales with that size confirms the idea, and a reversed relation rejects it.
Forecast: Only on bars where the close is below its close 28 bars earlier (the regime); flat everywhere else. Inside the regime, the forecast is ln(mean volume of the last 28 bars / mean volume of the 90 bars before them), standardised over its own past 120 values (target 10, cap 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. It is negative (short) when the recent volume is below the coin's usual volume, and the short grows the thinner the volume is. It is 0 when the volume is at or above usual. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: On in-regime bars of the non-overlapping 5-bar grid, the slope of the coin's 5-bar return minus its normal drift (its mean 5-bar return over all windows) on the forecast. Cases on the same day are taken together for the t. The idea predicts a positive slope: a more negative forecast should come with a lower excess return.
- Supporting check: On grid cases with forecast ≤ -2, the mean 5-bar return minus drift is below 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, the supporting check holds, and there are at least 100 in-regime cases on at least 60 distinct days.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, with the same minimum number of cases.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2. Also any confirm-level slope whose supporting check fails, or fewer than 100 cases or 60 days.
