Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily fall that is large against the coin's typical daily move over the past 20 days -> adapted: kept as a regime (1-day return ≤ -2 usual moves, the convention for "large"), graded inside by the fall's size.
- Comparison: the fall in units of the 20-day typical daily move -> kept: the 1-day return divided by the std of the last 20 daily returns.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 to 2 days, more after larger falls -> kept: a long held 2 bars, sized by the fall in usual moves.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the stated fall-to-rebound link at the stated horizon, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast uses VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 1 and vol_period 20: the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. When that value is -2 or lower, the detector selects mean_reversion. On every other bar the forecast is 0. Inside the regime, the same measure is standardised over 60 bars with target 4, cap 20, and negated, so it is always positive. The default target of 10 would put every case at the cap: a 2-usual-move fall is about 25 before the cap. With target 4, a fall at the threshold gives a long of about 10, a fall of about 4 usual moves or more gives the cap of 20, and the size grows linearly in between. The forecast is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a bet of the forecast (a rise of at least 2 points), read once per 2-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 2-bar return from the close of the case bar minus the coin's normal 2-bar drift over all its windows. The measure is the mean of these, with its t, cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Among the cases, the slope of the unusual move on the case's forecast (post_pipeline_value of `fall`) is positive: larger falls are followed by larger rebounds.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the slope check passes (slope > 0).
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0. Fewer than 30 distinct case days is inconclusive whatever the t.
