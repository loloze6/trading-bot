Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin whose close is above its close of 28 days ago -> kept: a regime rule on the 28-bar return above 0, flat on every other bar.
- Comparison: closes near the 28-day closing high against closes pulled further back, in raw percent -> kept: graded by the proximity 1/(1+D) to the 28-bar closing high, D in raw percent.
- Outcome: the closer to the high, the larger the rise over the next 5 to 10 days -> kept: long position that grows with proximity, held for 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test sizes longs in uptrending coins by their raw percent distance below the 28-day closing high and judges the following 5-day rise against that size.
Forecast: The forecast exists only on bars where today's close is above the close of 28 bars ago (regime "trending"). It is zero on all other bars. On those bars it is long and equals the proximity to the 28-bar closing high, (1/(1+D)), where D is today's percent distance below that high (bar t included, power 1). This value is standardised over its 60 previous values. A close at the high gives the largest long, about 10 to 20. A close a few percent below gives a much smaller long, and a deep pullback gives nearly zero. The forecast is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The sample is grid bars (positions 0, 5, 10, … of each window) with forecast > 0. On each, take the coin's 5-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows. Measure the t of the least-squares slope of this excess return on the forecast.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean of the same excess 5-bar returns over those cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is at least 2 and the forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and 2, or a t of 2 or more with a forecast-weighted mean excess of 0 or less. Fewer than 100 grid cases with forecast > 0 across all coins and windows is also inconclusive.
