Does a divergence between price and RSI warn of a coming reversal in BTC, ETH and BNB? A divergence is when price makes a new high or low but RSI does not. This is not the question of whether RSI extremes predict moves. It asks whether fading momentum behind a new price extreme carries information.

Why it could predict a reversal: a new high reached with weaker momentum may mean fewer buyers are still arriving. Late trend followers have already bought, so the push is running out of fuel. When the remaining holders take profit, there is little demand left to absorb it. The same logic applies to new lows and sellers.

Why it could predict nothing, or continuation: RSI only measures recent closes, so a divergence may just reflect a slower, steadier climb rather than real exhaustion. In strong trends, momentum often cools while price keeps grinding on as slow money keeps entering. Divergences are also widely watched, so any edge may already be priced away. I know of no firm reason to prefer one answer.

The reversal view is wrong if price moves after divergences look no different from price moves after new extremes without divergence.
