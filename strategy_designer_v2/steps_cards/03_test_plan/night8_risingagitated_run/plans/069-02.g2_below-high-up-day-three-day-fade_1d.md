Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin closes up on the day -> kept: a regime rule on the 1-day return above 0; the book is flat on other bars.
- Comparison: the further the close sits below its 20-day high, in 20-day daily swings, the larger the fall -> kept: graded by (close − 20-day high) / high / std of 20 daily returns.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next three days -> adapted: "after an up day" becomes "while on an up day", short held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the up-day condition, the swing-scaled depth below the high and the 3-day fall are each measured as stated.
Forecast: On up days only, the forecast is short. The regime detector passes a bar when its 1-day return is above 0; all other bars are flat. Inside that regime the forecast uses one quantity. It is the close's distance below the highest high of the last 20 bars, today's bar included. That distance is taken as a fraction of the high and divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The quantity is never positive: it is 0 when the close sits at the 20-day high and grows more negative the deeper the close sits below it. `standardise` (60/20) puts it on the forecast scale, about −10 at its usual depth and capped at −20. A clip at 0 keeps the forecast short only. So the short grows with the depth below the high.

Side: short only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the short bets: a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 3-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 3-bar return from the signal's close and subtract the coin's normal 3-bar drift (its mean over all windows). Count it in the short direction. The measure is the mean of these unusual moves, with its t where cases on the same day are summed into one.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast| and take Welch's t of the unusual move, deeper half minus shallower half. The check passes when that difference is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move in the short direction is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 100 cases, and the deeper half beats the shallower half.
- Reject: The mean unusual move in the short direction is negative with t ≤ −2, over at least 100 cases.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected in three cases: - the t lies between −2 and 2; - the main measure confirms but the deeper half does not beat the shallower half; - there are fewer than 100 cases.
