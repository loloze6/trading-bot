Forecast: The forecast is computed only on wide days, where the day's range (high minus low) divided by the mean range of the last 20 days, today included, is 1 or more (RangeCompressionRatioComponent, short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). On every other day it is zero. On a wide day it reads where the close sits in that day's own range: DonchianBreakoutComponent with period 1 and source "high_low" gives -sf at the low, 0 at the middle and +sf at the high. This value is standardised over its last 60 values (min_periods 20), negated, and clipped at a maximum of 0. The result is zero when the close is at or below the midpoint. It is negative, a short, when the close is above the midpoint, and it grows steadily toward about -20 as the close nears the day's high. It is never positive (short only). Outside the falling agitated profile, code keeps the book flat.
