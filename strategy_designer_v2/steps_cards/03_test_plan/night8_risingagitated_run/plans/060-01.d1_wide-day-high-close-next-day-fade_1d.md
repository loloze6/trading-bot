Forecast: The regime detector computes `RangeInReturnStdComponent` (vol_period 20): the day's high-low range, as a share of the close, over the std of the last 20 daily returns. When it is below 2, a veto sends the bar to a flat regime, so the book holds nothing on ordinary days. On wide days, the forecast is `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "low"), standardised (60/20) and then negated. It is about 0 for a close at the day's low and grows more negative as the close nears the day's high, to about -10 on an average reading and at most -20. It is never positive, so the book is short only. Outside the falling agitated profile, code keeps the book flat.

- Reject: The mean unusual move in the short's direction is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a failed supporting check. The test also needs at least 30 days with cases; with fewer, no verdict is given.
