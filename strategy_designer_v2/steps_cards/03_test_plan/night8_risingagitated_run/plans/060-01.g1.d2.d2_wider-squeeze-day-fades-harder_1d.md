Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a coin closes above the middle of its day's high-low range -> kept: the code adds the profile, and close location above 0.5 is a regime rule; the book is flat on every other bar.
- Comparison: the day's range against its average range of the last 20 days -> kept: the day's range divided by the mean daily range over 20 days.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next day, and falls more the wider the range -> kept: a short held 1 bar, sized by the range ratio.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the situation, the sizing and the one-day horizon are all measured as the idea states them.
Forecast: On days whose close is above the middle of the day's range (close location from the low above 0.5), the forecast is a short sized by the day's range over its 20-day mean daily range. That ratio comes from `RangeCompressionRatioComponent` (short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). It is standardised over its last 60 values (min_periods 20) and then negated. A day with an average range gives about -10; a day twice as wide gives about -20 (the cap); a narrow day gives a smaller short. The forecast is never positive. On every other bar, and outside the falling agitated profile, it is 0.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On each bar with a negative forecast (a case), the coin's next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, signed in the short direction. The measure is the mean of these unusual moves and its t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of the absolute forecast. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half is above 0 (wider days fall more).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2 (high closes are followed by rises beyond the coin's drift).
- Inconclusive: The t lies between -2 and 2, or t >= 2 while the check's difference is 0 or below. At least 100 cases on at least 60 distinct days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
