- Situation: in a rising calm market, the close stands above its 20-day average -> adapted: the profile is the regime, and the distance above the average is graded, zero at or below the average.
- Comparison: the 20-day average price -> kept
- Outcome: the further above, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own distance inside its own profile, held for its own 5 days, so a slope of the 5-day move on that distance confirms or rejects it directly.
Forecast: The forecast uses the percent distance of the close above its 20-day simple moving average, including today's close. It is standardised against its own last 60 values, so a typical distance reads about 10, capped at 20. It is then clipped at 0, so the forecast is zero whenever the close is at or below the average. It is positive and grows linearly with the distance above the average. It is never negative. The code adds the rising-calm profile and keeps the book flat outside it.
