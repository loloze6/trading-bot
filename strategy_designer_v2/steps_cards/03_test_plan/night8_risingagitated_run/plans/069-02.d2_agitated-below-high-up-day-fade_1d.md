Fidelity:
- Situation: in an agitated market, an up-day close sitting below its 20-day high, measured in 20-day daily swings -> kept: the up close is a regime rule, the depth comes from VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHigh, and the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: deeper up-day closes against shallower ones -> adapted: the depth is made graded, so a deeper close gives a larger short.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next day, more the deeper the close -> kept: horizon 1 bar, short.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own depth, held for its own next-day horizon, on up days inside the agitated profile.
Forecast: A regime rule makes the forecast exist only on up-day closes (the 1-bar return is above 0). On all other bars it is flat, and outside the agitated profile code keeps it flat. Inside the regime the forecast is the close's distance below the highest high of the last 20 days, bar t included. The distance is divided by that high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, then standardised over its last 60 values (min 20). The raw value is never positive, so the forecast is short only. It is 0 at the 20-day high, about -10 at a usual depth, and shorts more, up to -20, the deeper the close sits.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The cases are the bars with forecast < 0. On them, regress the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows on the forecast's size (abs forecast). The t of the slope is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean next-day excess return over those cases, taken in the short direction (minus the excess return), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is negative with t <= -2 (larger shorts are followed by larger falls than the drift), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is positive with t >= +2 (deeper up-day closes are followed by stronger next-day moves).
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2, or a confirming slope whose supporting check fails. The test needs at least 100 distinct case days across coins and windows; with fewer it is inconclusive.
