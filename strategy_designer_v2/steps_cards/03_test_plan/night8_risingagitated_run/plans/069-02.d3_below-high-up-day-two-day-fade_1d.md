- Situation: a coin closes up on the day, below its 20-day high -> adapted: "up day" is a regime condition (1-day return > 0); depth below the 20-day high in 20-day swings is graded.
- Comparison: none -> kept
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next two days, more the deeper the close -> kept: a short graded by depth, held 2 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's condition, its graded depth and its two-day fall, with nothing replaced.
Forecast: The forecast exists only on bars that close above the prior close; it is 0 on every other bar. On an up day it measures the close's distance below the highest high of the last 20 days, bar t included. The distance is counted in usual daily moves, which is the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The raw value is never positive. It is standardised against its own past 60 values, so a typical depth reads about -10, and clipped at 0 from above. The forecast is therefore short only. It is 0 when the up day closes at its 20-day high, and grows more negative the deeper the close sits below that high, up to -20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bets (a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points), read once per 2-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 2-bar return from the signal close, minus the coin's normal 2-bar drift over all windows, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of these values, with its t computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the slope of that short-direction excess return on the case's absolute forecast is above 0. This means deeper closes give larger falls.
- Confirm: The mean short-direction excess 2-bar return is above 0 with t >= 2, and the slope check is positive.
- Reject: The mean short-direction excess 2-bar return is below 0 with t <= -2. That means up days closing deep below the 20-day high are followed by rises beyond the drift.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected in either of these cases: - t lies between -2 and 2; - t >= 2 but the slope is not positive. At least 100 cases on at least 60 distinct days are required, else the result is inconclusive.
