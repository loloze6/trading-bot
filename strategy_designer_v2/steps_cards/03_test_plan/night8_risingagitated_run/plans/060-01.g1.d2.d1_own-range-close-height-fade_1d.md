Forecast: The forecast is zero unless two things hold on the bar. First, the day's high-low range divided by the mean bar range of the last 20 bars (today included) is at least 1. Second, the close lies above the middle of the day's range. On those bars the forecast reads the close's position within the day's own range: 2 x (close - low) / (high - low) - 1, which is 0 at mid-range and 1 at the high. That value is standardised against its own past 60 values and then negated, so it is short. A signed_power with exponent 2 shrinks closes just above the middle towards 0 and enlarges closes near the high. A clip at max 0 keeps the forecast short only. The short therefore grows smoothly from 0 at mid-range to its largest size at the day's high. Code adds the falling-agitated profile, so the book is flat outside that profile.
