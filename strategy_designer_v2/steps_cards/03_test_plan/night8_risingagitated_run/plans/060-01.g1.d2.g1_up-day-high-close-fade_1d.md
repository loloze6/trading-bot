- Situation: falling agitated coin, up close (close above prior close), close in upper part of the day's range -> kept: the profile is added by code, and "up close and close location above 0.5" is a regime rule (upper part = upper half, by convention).
- Comparison: next days after high closes against next days after mid-range closes -> adapted: graded by close location, so the short is larger the higher the close sits, from the half-range edge up.
- Outcome: falls over the next day, more the higher the close -> kept: short held 1 bar, sized by close location.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book shorts exactly the up-day high closes in the profile for one day, graded by close location, so its next-day result confirms or rejects the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only inside the falling agitated profile, which code adds. It is computed only on bars where the 1-day return is above 0 and the close location (close − low)/(high − low) is above 0.5. On all other bars it is 0. On those bars it equals minus the close location, standardised against its own past 60 bars (60/20), clipped at most to 0. Since the past mean location is about 0.5, the forecast is about −10 at a mid-high close and about −20 at a close on the high. It is never positive, and the short grows as the close nears the day's high.

- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2. Also a positive main result whose supporting check fails. Also fewer than 30 cases, or fewer than 20 distinct days of cases.
