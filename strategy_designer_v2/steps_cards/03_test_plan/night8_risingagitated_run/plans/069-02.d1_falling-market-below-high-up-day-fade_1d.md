Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling market, a coin closes up on the day -> kept: the falling profile is added by code, and "close above the prior close" is a regime rule; the book is flat on every other bar.
- Comparison: how far the close sits below its 20-day high, in units of its 20-day daily swing -> kept: the close's distance under the 20-day high, divided by the std of the 20 daily returns.
- Outcome: a larger fall over the next day, the deeper the close -> kept: a short position graded by that depth, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures this situation, this depth and this one-day fall, with nothing replaced.
Forecast: The forecast is active only on up-close days. On those days it is the distance of the close below its 20-day high, in usual daily moves: (close − highest high of the last 20 days, today included) / that high / the std of the last 20 daily returns. This reading is never positive. It is standardised against its own last 60 values, so the average up-day reads about −10 (a 100 % short). A close twice as deep as usual reads about −20, and a close at the high reads 0. A clip at 0 keeps the book short only. On down or flat days the forecast is 0. Outside a falling 60-day trend it is also 0 (the profile added by code).

Side: short only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On every bar with a nonzero forecast, take the coin's next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Sign it in the short direction, weight it by |forecast| and average it. The t uses cases of the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Among those cases, the rank correlation between |forecast| and the short-direction excess next-day return is above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean short-direction excess return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the rank correlation is above 0.
- Reject: The weighted mean short-direction excess return is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2. A t ≥ 2 with a rank correlation of 0 or below is also inconclusive. At least 100 case-days are needed across coins and windows; below that the result is inconclusive.
