Fidelity:
- Situation: a close in the upper half of the day's high-low range, in a falling agitated market -> kept: the upper-half close is a regime rule (close location above 0.5), and code adds the profile.
- Comparison: the day's range against its ranges of the last 20 days -> kept: the forecast grows with today's range over the 20-day mean bar range.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next day, and the fall is larger the wider the day -> kept: a short held for 1 bar, sized by that range multiple.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on upper-half closes inside the profile, sized by the idea's own width measure, so its next-day result stands for the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads where the close sits in the day's range, (close − low) / (high − low). On a bar where that value is above 0.5, the bar goes to regime `mean_reversion`. Every other bar goes to `unknown`, which is flat. Code adds the falling-agitated profile, so the book is also flat outside that profile. Inside the regime, the forecast comes from one component: today's high-low range divided by the mean bar range of the last 20 days, bar t included (`RangeCompressionRatioComponent`, short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). This ratio is about 1 on an ordinary day and larger on a wide day. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20) and then negated. The forecast is therefore always short or zero. It is about −10 on a day of usual width and grows towards −20 as the day widens against its last 20 days. It is 0 outside the upper-half-close situation and outside the profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: A case is a bar with forecast below 0. For each case, take the coin's next-day return close[t+1] / close[t] − 1 and subtract the coin's mean daily return over all its windows (holding the coin). Read the result in the short direction, so a fall counts as a gain. Measure the mean of this value over the cases and its t. Cases on the same day are summed into one, so the t counts days. This is judged before costs.
- Supporting check: The same excess fall is computed for the cases with |forecast| above its median and for those below it. The above-median mean minus the below-median mean must be greater than 0.
- Confirm: The idea is confirmed when the mean excess next-day fall is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes (wider days fall more).
- Reject: The idea is rejected when the mean excess next-day fall has t ≤ −2: the coin rises more than its drift after upper-half closes.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the wide-day cases fall no more than the narrow-day cases. A verdict needs at least 100 cases spread over at least 50 distinct days.
