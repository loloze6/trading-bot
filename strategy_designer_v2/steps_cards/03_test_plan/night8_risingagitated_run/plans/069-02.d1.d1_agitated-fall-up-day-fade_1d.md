Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin closes up on the day, in a falling agitated market -> kept: the up close is a regime rule (close above the prior close), and code adds the profile.
- Comparison: the further the close sits below its 20-day high, in units of its 20-day daily swing -> kept: the short is graded by that distance, measured in usual 20-day one-bar moves.
- Outcome: the larger its fall over the next day -> kept: a one-bar short whose size grows with the depth.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on up days in the profile, sized by the idea's own depth, so a deeper-is-larger fall confirms the idea and its absence rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is zero on every bar that does not close above the prior close; code also keeps it at zero outside the falling agitated profile. On up days, the forecast is the close's distance below the highest high of the last 20 days, bar t included, divided by that high and by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns (VolatilityScaledDistanceFromHighComponent: period 20, vol_period 20, source "high", exclude_current 0, base "level"). This distance is never positive, so the forecast is a short. It is standardised over its own past 60 values (min 20): about -10 at the usual depth, smaller near the high, and down to -20 for the deepest closes. It is never above zero.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: Over the cases (bars with forecast below 0, each bar once), take the depth d = -forecast and the excess return e = the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Measure the least-squares slope of e on d, with its t; the cases of one day are taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean of e over the cases, read in the short's direction (-e), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope of e on d has t of -2 or less (deeper closes are followed by larger falls than usual), and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope of e on d has t of +2 or more (deeper closes are followed by smaller falls, or by rises).
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and +2, or a confirming slope whose supporting check fails. The test also needs at least 100 cases on at least 60 distinct days, else it is inconclusive.
