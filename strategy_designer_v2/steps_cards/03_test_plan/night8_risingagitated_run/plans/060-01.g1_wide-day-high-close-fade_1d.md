Fidelity:
- Situation: a close near the top of a day whose range is wide against the last 20 days' ranges -> adapted: both are regime conditions (close in the top quarter of the day's range, day's range at least 1.5 times the mean of the 20 days ending today), and inside them the size is graded by the range multiple.
- Comparison: the day's range against its ranges of the last 20 days -> kept: the day's range over the mean bar range of the 20 days ending today.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day, more the wider the range -> kept: a 1-bar short, larger as the range multiple grows.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts exactly the strong closes on wide days, sized by width, and judges the next day's move.
Forecast: Two detector measures gate the regime. One is the close location in the day's range, from 0 at the low to 1 at the high. The other is the day's high-low range over the mean bar range of the last 20 days (today included). A bar is in `mean_reversion` when the close location is at least 0.75 and the range multiple is at least 1.5. Every other bar is flat, and the falling agitated profile is added by code. Inside the regime the forecast is the range multiple, standardised against its own last 60 values, then negated. It is always negative (short only), about -15 at the 1.5 edge and more negative as the day widens, capped at -20. It is zero outside the situation.
