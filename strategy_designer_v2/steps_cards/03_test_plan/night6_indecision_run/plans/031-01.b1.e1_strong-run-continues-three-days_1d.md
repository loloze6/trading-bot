Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 5-day rise, measured in its usual daily moves over 20 days -> adapted: graded by that ratio; no rise gives 0.
- Comparison: the rise against the usual daily move over the past 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: the larger the ratio, the more the coin rises over the next 3 days, beyond its drift -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's ratio itself, held 3 days, and judged against the coin's own drift.
Forecast: VolatilityScaledReturnComponent with period 5 and vol_period 20 gives the 5-day return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns, times √5. Standardise (window 60, min_periods 20) puts it on the forecast scale. A clip at min 0 keeps only rises. The forecast is 0 after a flat or falling 5 days and positive after a rise. It grows in proportion to the rise counted in usual daily moves, up to the cap of 20. There is no regime: the idea is a steady relation, not a condition.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: Use a non-overlapping 3-bar grid in each window, pooled over coins and windows. On it, take the least-squares slope of the excess 3-bar return on the forecast at the grid bar. The excess return is close[t+3]/close[t] − 1 minus the coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows. Report the slope's t.
- Supporting check: Look at the grid bars where the forecast is above 0. On them, the mean excess 3-bar return weighted by the forecast must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is ≥ 2 and the forecast-weighted mean excess return on positive-forecast bars is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a weighted mean excess return ≤ 0 is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 200 grid bars with a forecast above 0; with fewer, it is inconclusive.
