- Situation: a day that falls far more than the coin usually falls in a day and closes near its low -> adapted: "after X" made "while X". A regime holds only on bars whose 1-day return is at or below -2 of its usual 20-day moves and that close in the lowest quarter of the day's range.
- Comparison: the larger the swing of daily returns over the past 20 days, the larger the rise -> kept: inside the regime, the forecast is graded by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day -> kept: long, held for 1 daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds only on deep, low-closing down days, sizes by 20-day volatility, and measures the next day's rise against holding.
Forecast: The forecast is zero on every bar except deep low-closing down days. A regime holds on a bar when both of these are true: - the day's return divided by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 20) is -2 or below; - the close sits in the lowest quarter of the day's range (CloseLocationInRangeComponent, reference "low", 0.25 or below). On those bars the forecast is long. It comes from the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns (VolatilityFromStdDevComponent, vol_period 21), standardised against its own past 60 values. It is about +10 when volatility is at its recent average and grows toward +20 as volatility rises above it. It is always positive and continuous in volatility. Each bet is held 1 bar.
