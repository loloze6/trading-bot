Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin has risen over the past 5 days (any rise) -> kept: the component is exactly 0 unless the 5-bar return is positive, so no position exists outside the situation.
- Comparison: that day's high-low range against its average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: the bar's range over the mean range of the last 20 bars.
- Outcome: the coin rises more over the next 3 days, the larger the rise times the range multiple -> kept: a long position sized by that product, held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own product, so a graded rise-after-signal confirms it and a graded fall rejects it.
Forecast: The forecast is RiseTimesRangeMultipleComponent with rise_period 5 and range_period 20. It equals max(0, the 5-day rise in percent) times the day's high-low range over its 20-day mean range. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), so its typical size is about 10, with a cap of 20. It is 0 when the past 5 days did not rise. It is positive, and grows with both the rise and the range multiple, when they did. It is never negative (long only by construction). No regime gate is used: the component itself is 0 outside the situation and continuous at its edge, so a gate on "5-day return > 0" would change nothing.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the cases with a forecast above 0, read on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window. For each case, take the coin's 3-bar return from the case's close minus the coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows (holding the coin). The measure is the slope of that excess return on the forecast, with its t computed with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: The mean excess 3-bar return of the cases in the top third of positive forecasts is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with |t| < 2, or a confirming slope without a passing check. At least 300 non-overlapping cases with a forecast above 0 are needed, on at least 150 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
