Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's 20-day return relative to its daily volatility over those 20 days -> kept
- Comparison: the larger the scaled return, the larger the later rise; weak or negative means out -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 10 days, more so the larger the scaled return -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled 20-day return, held long for its 10-day horizon, so its result is the idea's.
Forecast: One indicator, `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with period 20 and vol_period 20. It is the 20-day return divided by (the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns × √20). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20) and clipped at 0. The forecast is positive and grows with the scaled 20-day rise. It is near zero for a small rise, zero for a flat or negative 20-day return, and never short. There is no regime: it is active on every bar.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 10-bar forward return minus its normal 10-bar drift, regressed on the forecast. It uses non-overlapping 10-bar grid bars in each window, pooled over coins and windows, and is judged before costs.
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily return minus its gain from the market's own rise (timing) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope has t ≥ 2 and the timing gain is above 0.
- Reject: The slope has t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2, or a confirming slope whose timing gain is not above 0. The test needs at least 100 grid bars with a forecast above 0, else it is inconclusive.
