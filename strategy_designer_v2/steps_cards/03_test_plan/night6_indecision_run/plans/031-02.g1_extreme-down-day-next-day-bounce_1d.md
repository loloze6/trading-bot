Fidelity:
- Situation: a day on which the coin falls far more than it usually does in a day -> adapted: a regime gate of a one-day fall of at least 2 usual moves (20-day return std, the common convention); inside it the forecast is graded by fall depth times close-near-low.
- Comparison: the fall's rank among the coin's own past daily returns, and the close's nearness to the day's low -> kept: depth counted in the coin's usual daily moves, times (high − close) / (high − low).
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more for deeper, lower-closing falls -> kept: long held 1 bar, sized by that strength.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the gate is the idea's deep down day, the size follows its two stated strengths, and the 1-day long is its outcome, so a confirm or reject carries to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector checks one measure: the day's return divided by the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns (`VolatilityScaledReturnComponent`, period 1, vol_period 20). When it is −2 or lower, the bar is `mean_reversion`; on every other bar the book is flat. Inside that regime the forecast comes from `FallCloseLocationComponent` (vol_period 20). Its value is the day's fall in usual moves times how near the close sits to the day's low (0 at the high, 1 at the low). It is standardised against its own past 60 values with target 2, because the component is 0 on every up day, so its past mean size is far below a deep-fall reading. It then goes through `signed_power` with exponent 3 (pivot 10), the strongest graded dead zone. A shallow qualifying fall that closed mid-range gets a small long. A deep fall that closed at its low gets a large long, up to the +20 cap. The forecast is never negative, and it is zero outside the situation.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: For each bar with a forecast above 0, take the coin's next-day return (close[t+1]/close[t] − 1) minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Measure the mean of this excess and its t, with cases on the same day summed into one deviation.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean of the same excess is at least the unweighted mean. This shows that deeper falls with lower closes are followed by larger rises.
- Confirm: The mean excess next-day return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean excess next-day return is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails, or fewer than 100 independent case-days across all coins and windows.
