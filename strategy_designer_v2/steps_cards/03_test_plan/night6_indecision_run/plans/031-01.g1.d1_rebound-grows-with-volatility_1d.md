Fidelity:
- Situation: a day whose high-low range is wide against its 20-day average range and whose close lands near the day's low -> kept: a regime rule that fires when the day's range is at least 1.5 times its 20-day mean bar range and the close sits in the lowest quarter of the day's range (both levels by convention).
- Comparison: the 20-day daily volatility against its own usual level, with the rise growing faster than that volatility -> kept: the 20-day return volatility over its 60-day mean, standardised and squared through signed_power exponent 2.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more so when volatility is high -> adapted: "after X" becomes "while X", so the position is taken at the signal day's close and held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only on wide low-close days, sized convexly by relative volatility, so its next-day gains test both the rebound and its growth with volatility.
Forecast: On bars where the day's range is at least 1.5 times its 20-day mean bar range and the close is in the bottom 25% of the day's range, the forecast is long. Its size is the 20-day return volatility over its 60-day mean, standardised over 80 bars (about +10 at usual volatility), then squared around 10 with signed_power exponent 2. At usual volatility the forecast is about 10. At 1.5 times usual it reaches the +20 cap. At half the usual level it is about 2.5. On all other bars the forecast is 0. It never goes below zero.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On signal bars (regime mean_reversion with forecast > 0), the next-day return close[t+1]/close[t]-1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows. Its mean and t are taken with the cases of one day pooled, before costs.
- Supporting check: The same excess next-day return for cases with forecast above the median of all cases, minus that for cases below it (Welch t). It must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure's mean excess return is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure's mean excess return is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure with a high-minus-low difference at or below 0. At least 30 cases (signal days) are needed in total, else the result is inconclusive.
