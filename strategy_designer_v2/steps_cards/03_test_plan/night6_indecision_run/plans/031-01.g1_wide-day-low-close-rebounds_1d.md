Fidelity:
- Situation: a day whose high-low range is wide against its 20-day average range and whose close lands near the day's low -> adapted: "after X" becomes "while X". A regime holds the situation: the range is at least 1.5x its 20-day mean and the close is in the bottom quarter of the bar. Inside it, the position is graded by range multiple x closeness to the low.
- Comparison: the day's range against its average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more for wider ranges and lower closes -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the regime is the idea's situation and the size is its strength, so a rise (or not) on those days confirms (or rejects) the idea itself.
Forecast: The regime detector reads two measures: - `RangeCompressionRatioComponent` (short 1, long 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"): today's range over its 20-day mean bar range. - `CloseLocationInRangeComponent` (reference "low"): where the close sits in today's bar. A day is `mean_reversion` when the range ratio is at least 1.5 and the close location is at most 0.25. Every other day is `unknown`, which is null and flat. In `mean_reversion`, one component sets the size: `BreakoutInMeanRangesComponent` (period 1, base_period 20, side "below", high_current 1, base_exclude_current 1). - Its value is (high − close) / the mean range of the prior 20 days, which equals the range multiple x closeness to the low. - It is standardised over 60 past values (min 20). - It is never negative, so the book is long only. It is larger the wider the day and the nearer the close to the low, and zero outside the situation.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars with forecast > 0, the next 1-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, averaged over cases. Its t is computed with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Across those cases, the least-squares slope of the excess next-day return on the forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean excess next-day return is positive with t ≥ 2, and the slope is positive.
- Reject: The mean excess next-day return is negative with t ≤ −2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with a slope ≤ 0 is also inconclusive. Fewer than 30 distinct case days is inconclusive whatever the result.
