- Situation: a day with a wide range against its 20-day average range, closing near its low, while 20-day volatility is high against its usual level -> adapted: the three conditions form a regime ("only"), and the forecast inside it is graded by range multiple x closeness to the low.
- Comparison: the day's range against its 20-day average range, and 20-day volatility against its usual level -> kept: range multiple of 1.5 or more (convention), close in the lowest quarter of the bar (convention), 20-day volatility at or above its 60-day mean.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 1 day, more so the wider the range and the lower the close -> kept: a 1-bar hold, judged against the coin's normal daily drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own day shape, its volatility condition and its 1-day rise, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector selects `mean_reversion` only on a day that meets all three conditions. The day's range must be at least 1.5 times its 20-day mean bar range (MeanBarRangeRatio with long 20 and short 1, at or below 0.667). The close must be in the lowest quarter of the day's range (CloseLocationInRange "low", at or below 0.25). The 20-day return volatility must be at or above its 60-day mean (VolatilityRatioToAverage 20/60, at or above 1.0). Every other day is flat. On a selected day the forecast is long. It equals (high − close) divided by the mean daily range of the prior 20 days (BreakoutInMeanRanges with period 1, base 20, side "below", high_current 1, base_exclude_current 1). That value is the range multiple times the close's distance from the high, so it grows the wider the day and the nearer the close sits to the low. It is standardised over 60 bars (min 20) and is never negative.

- Main measure: On days in the `mean_reversion` regime with forecast > 0 (the cases), take the coin's next-day return close[t+1]/close[t] − 1 minus that coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Report the mean of this unusual move and its t, with cases from the same day taken together.

- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ −2.
