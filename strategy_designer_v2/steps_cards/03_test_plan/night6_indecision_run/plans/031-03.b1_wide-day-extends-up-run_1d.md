Fidelity:
- Situation: a day whose range is wide against its 20-day average daily range -> adapted: a regime that holds only when today's range is at least 1.5 times the 20-day mean bar range (1.5 is the usual convention, since the idea gives no level); nothing is held on other days.
- Comparison: the larger the rise over the preceding 5 days -> adapted: graded, so the position grows with the 5-bar rise; falls give no position because the idea speaks only of rises.
- Outcome: a larger further rise over the next 3 days -> kept: long, held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: this measures whether, on wide days, larger 5-day rises are followed by larger 3-day rises.
Forecast: A regime detector computes today's high-low range divided by the mean high-low range of the last 20 bars, today included. The regime is active only when that ratio is 1.5 or more; on every other day the forecast is 0. On active days the forecast is the 5-bar percent change of the close, standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20) and clipped at 0. It is therefore positive and grows with the size of the preceding 5-day rise, and it is 0 after a flat or falling 5 days. It never goes short.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: Over the cases (bets on wide-range days, each read once per 3-bar grid cell), the forecast-weighted mean of the unusual move: the 3-bar return minus the coin's normal 3-bar drift. Its t is computed with cases on the same day pooled, and is judged before costs.
- Supporting check: The least-squares slope of the per-case unusual move on the case's forecast is above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the slope is above 0.
- Reject: The weighted mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with a slope at or below 0. At least 30 distinct case days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
