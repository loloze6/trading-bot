- Situation: after a 5-day rise, a day closing near its open with range at least its 20-day average range -> adapted: a regime that needs three things. The 5-day return is above 0. The body is at most 10% of the day's range, the usual doji convention, since the idea gives no number. The day's range over the 20-day mean range, that day included, is at least 1. "After X" becomes "while X".
- Comparison: the larger the 5-day rise, the larger the further rise -> adapted: inside the situation, the long position is graded by the standardised 5-day rise.
- Outcome: a further rise over the next 10 days -> kept: each bet is held 10 bars and measured on the 10-day return after the signal day.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the idea's own day, its own grading and its own 10-day rise, so a confirm or reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: Each daily bar is checked against three rules: - the 5-day return is above 0 (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 5); - the absolute close-minus-open is at most 0.1 of the day's high-low range (BarBodySizeComponent, relative to range); - the day's range divided by the mean daily range of the last 20 days, that day included, is at least 1 (RangeCompressionRatioComponent, short 1, long 20, base mean_bar_range, form width). When all three hold, the bar is "trending". The forecast is then the 5-day rise standardised over its past 60 values, clipped at 0. It is long, and larger the stronger the rise, about +10 for a usual 5-day move and capped at +20. On every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Each bet is held 10 bars.

- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move over 10 bars after each case, in the long direction. A case is a bet on the 10-bar grid. The unusual move is the coin's 10-day return from the signal day's close minus its normal 10-day drift. Its mean and t are taken with the cases of one day pooled.

- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
