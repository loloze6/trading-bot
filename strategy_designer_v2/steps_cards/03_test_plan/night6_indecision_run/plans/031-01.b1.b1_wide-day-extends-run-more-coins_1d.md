Forecast: The regime detector computes the 5-bar price change and marks a bar `trending` when that change is above 0. Every other bar is `unknown`, which is flat. Inside the regime, the forecast is a long whose size follows one ratio: bar t's high-low range divided by the mean daily high-low range of the last 20 bars, bar t included. That ratio is standardised over 60 bars, minimum 20 past values. The forecast is always above zero in the regime: about +10 for a usual-width day, near +5 for a narrow day, and up to +20 for a day twice as wide as usual or more.
