Fidelity:
- Situation: a day on which the coin falls far more than it usually does -> kept: a regime veto fires when the day's return ranks in the bottom 5 % of the coin's own past daily returns (the convention for "far more than usual").
- Comparison: the fall's rank among the coin's own past daily returns, and the close's nearness to the day's low -> adapted: the rank sets the gate; inside it, size grows with the fall in usual daily moves times the close's nearness to the low, which orders the same falls the same way.
- Outcome: it rises over the next 1 day -> kept: long at the day's close, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is long only after such days, sized by depth and close-near-low, so its next-day excess return judges the idea directly.
Forecast: On every bar, the detector ranks the day's 1-bar percent return within its stored history. When that rank is in the bottom 5 % (negate_percentile of at least 0.95), the bar is `mean_reversion`. On all other bars the forecast is 0. In `mean_reversion` the forecast is `FallCloseLocationComponent` with `vol_period` 60. Its value is the day's fall in usual daily moves times (high − close) / (high − low), so it is 0 on up days. It is standardised over its past 60 values, with target 2 because most of its past values are near 0. A `signed_power` with exponent 3 and pivot 20 then acts as a graded dead zone, and a clip at 0 keeps it long only. The result is long only and 0 outside the deep-fall days. On those days it grows smoothly with the depth of the fall and with how near the close sits to the low.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars with forecast > 0, one per coin-day. For each case, take the next-day return close[t+1]/close[t] − 1 minus the coin's mean daily return over all its windows. Measure the mean of this excess and its t, with cases on the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: A least-squares slope of the per-case excess next-day return on the case's forecast is positive, meaning larger positions are followed by larger rises.
- Confirm: The mean excess next-day return is > 0 with t ≥ 2 over at least 30 distinct days, and the supporting slope is > 0.
- Reject: The mean excess next-day return has t ≤ −2 over at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between −2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirming t with a slope ≤ 0, and so is any result with fewer than 30 distinct case days.
