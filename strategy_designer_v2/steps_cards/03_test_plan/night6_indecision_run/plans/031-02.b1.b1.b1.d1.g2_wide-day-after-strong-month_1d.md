- Situation: a day whose high-low range is wide against its average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: a regime holds only on days whose range is at least 1.5 times the 20-day mean bar range, the usual wide-range-bar level since the idea gives no number.
- Comparison: the larger the 20-day rise before that day, the larger the rise, and no long when the coin has been falling -> kept: inside the regime the forecast grows with the 20-day rise up to that day's close and is 0 when that return is not positive.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 3 days -> adapted: "after the wide day" becomes "on the wide day's close", and the bet is held 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test longs only after wide days, sized by the prior 20-day rise, over the idea's 3-day horizon, so its result is the idea's result.
Forecast: On each daily bar the regime detector computes the day's high-low range divided by the mean high-low range of the last 20 days, today included. When that ratio is 1.5 or more, the regime is "trending"; on every other day the forecast is 0. On a wide day, the forecast is the coin's 20-day percent return (close[t] / close[t-20]), standardised against its own past 60 values (about 10 for an average-sized move). It is clipped at 0, so it is long only and 0 when the coin fell over the 20 days. The larger the 20-day rise, the larger the long, up to the cap of 20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual 3-bar move after the cases. A case is a bet: a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 3-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's return from the case's close over 3 bars, minus its normal 3-bar drift from holding the coin over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median forecast. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is t ≥ 2 when the upper half does not beat the lower half. The test needs at least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days; fewer cases is inconclusive.
