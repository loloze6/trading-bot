- Situation: a coin has fallen over the past 5 days, then a day with a wide range against its 20-day average range closes near its high -> kept: a regime condition on three parts. The fall is close[t-1] below close[t-6], before the day. The range is at least 1.5x the 20-day mean bar range, which is the usual wide-range-bar convention. The close is in the top quarter of the day's range, which is the usual convention.
- Comparison: the day's range against its average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: the day's high minus low over the mean bar range of the last 20 bars; the day itself is in that mean.
- Outcome: a further fall over the next 3 days, larger the wider the day's range -> kept: the short position is graded by the range multiple and held for 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on the stated days, sized by the stated width, for the stated 3 days.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless three things hold on the bar. The close one bar ago is below the close six bars ago, so the prior 5 days fell. The bar's range is at least 1.5 times the mean bar range of the last 20 bars. The close sits in the top quarter of the bar's range. On such bars the forecast is negative: the bar's range multiple (high minus low over the 20-bar mean bar range), standardised over its past 60 values, then negated. It is about -15 at a 1.5x day and grows in size as the day widens, up to the -20 cap at about 2x. It is never positive.
