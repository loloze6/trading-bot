- Situation: a day inside a coin's 5-day rise, with its high-low range wide against its 20-day average daily range -> adapted: the 5-day rise is a regime (5-day return above 0); inside it, the range ratio is graded, with the strongest dead zone so the widest days dominate.
- Comparison: the day's range against its average daily range over the past 20 days, and the strength of the 5-day rise -> kept: today's range over the 20-day mean bar range, combined with the 5-day return, both graded.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 3 days -> kept: long only, held 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same days, the same range ratio, the same rise and the same 3-day rise, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The forecast is zero unless the coin's 5-day return is above 0; that rule is the regime. Inside the rise it is long. Two components set its size, and both have the strongest graded dead zone (signed_power, exponent 3, pivot 20): - Range ratio (weight 2): today's high-low range divided by the mean daily range of the last 20 bars, from RangeCompressionRatioComponent (short 1, long 20, form "width", base "mean_bar_range"), standardised over 60 bars. An ordinary day reads about 10 after standardise and about 2.5 after the dead zone. A day twice its usual width reaches 20. - Rise strength (weight 1): the 5-day return, from PriceEvolutionComponent with period 5, standardised over 60 bars and clipped at 0. The forecast is the weighted mean of the two. It is near 0 on ordinary days and grows steeply with the day's width. It grows further with the strength of the run. It is never negative.

- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The 3-day forward return minus the coin's normal 3-day drift, on in-rise bars of the non-overlapping 3-bar grid whose forecast is in the top fifth of in-rise forecasts. It is measured against holding the coin, with the t computed with same-day cases taken together.

- Reject: The main-measure mean is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
