Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin has risen over the past 6 days -> adapted: "after" becomes "while": the regime is on while the 6-day close-to-close return is above 0, and the book is flat otherwise.
- Comparison: the day's volume against its average daily volume over the past 20 days -> kept: volume[t] / mean(volume[t-20..t-1]) - 1, graded above 1 and zero at or below it.
- Outcome: a larger further rise over the next 3 days, long -> kept: each bet is held for 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test asks whether, during 6-day rises, the 3-day excess return grows with the volume ratio and is zero-or-less at light volume, which is exactly the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the 6-bar percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 6). When it is above 0, the bar is `trending`; on every other bar the forecast is 0 (flat). Inside `trending`, the forecast comes from one component: today's volume over the mean volume of the prior 20 days, minus 1 (VolumeRatioComponent, period 20, exclude_current 1, form "centred"). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (about 10 at a typical size) and clipped at 0. The forecast is therefore long only. It is 0 when today's volume is at or below its 20-day average, and positive above it, growing linearly with how far volume exceeds the average, capped at 20. It is never negative.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: Use the bars of the `trending` regime (6-day rise) that fall on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window. On these bars, regress the coin's 3-bar forward return minus that coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows (the excess over holding) on the recorded forecast, forecast-0 bars included. Read the slope's t, with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Among the same grid bars, the mean excess 3-bar return of cases with forecast > 0 (heavy volume) minus that of cases with forecast = 0 (light volume) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting difference is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2: more volume during a 6-day rise is followed by a smaller 3-day excess return.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with -2 < t < 2 is inconclusive. So is a slope with t ≥ 2 whose supporting difference is not above 0. A test needs at least 200 regime grid cases, of which at least 60 have forecast > 0; with fewer cases, no verdict.
