Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin has risen over the past 5 days, and that day's range is wide against its 20-day average range -> kept: a regime is active only while the 5-bar return is above 0; inside it, the 5-day rise in percent is multiplied by the day's range over its 20-bar mean range, so "after" becomes "while" on the same bar.
- Comparison: the larger the rise and the wider the range, the larger the gain; a wide day after a weak rise gives less -> kept: the forecast grows with both the rise and the range multiple, and is 0 when there is no rise.
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 3 days -> kept: the long is held for 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config measures the stated product only on rising runs and is judged on the 3-day move against holding the coin.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where the 5-day return is above 0, the "trending" regime; on every other bar it is 0 (flat). On those bars it uses `RiseTimesRangeMultipleComponent` with rise_period 5 and range_period 20. That component is the 5-day rise in percent times the day's high-low range over its mean range across the last 20 days, today included. The value is standardised against its own past 60 values (window 60, min_periods 20), so a typical reading is about +10 and the cap is +20. The forecast is never negative. It is near 0 for a tiny rise or a narrow day. It grows continuously as the rise gets larger and the day gets wider.
Config: 031-01.b1.b2_wide-day-extends-strong-run_1d_config.json
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual 3-bar move over the cases. A case is a rise of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 3-bar grid cell inside each window. Its unusual move is close[t+3]/close[t]-1 minus the coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows (non-overlapping stretches), long side, before costs. The t is computed with cases on the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median forecast. The mean unusual move of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0, because a larger rise times a wider range should give a larger move.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the upper-half minus lower-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result is inconclusive. This includes a t between -2 and 2, and a positive t ≥ 2 whose upper-minus-lower difference is 0 or below. At least 30 distinct case days are needed; with fewer the result is inconclusive.
