Fidelity:
- Situation: a day the coin falls sharply against its 20-day daily volatility -> adapted: it is a regime condition, the day's return of 2 or more 20-day standard deviations down (the most common convention for "sharply").
- Comparison: how near the close lands to the day's low -> adapted: graded, the share of the day's range between the high and the close, damped strongly when the close is well off the low.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next day -> kept: long, held 1 daily bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test buys only sharp down days, sized by how near the close is to the low, and judges the next day's rise against that size.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the day's return in units of the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 20). Bars at -2.0 or below go to the regime `mean_reversion`; every other bar is flat (forecast 0). Inside that regime one component sets the size. It is CloseLocationInRangeComponent with reference "high", which reads (high - close) / (high - low): 0 when the close is at the day's high and 1 when it is at the low. It is standardised over its 60 past values, which puts a mid-range close near 10 and a close at the low near the cap of 20. It is then damped by signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 20, the strongest graded dead zone the guide allows. A close at the low gives about 20 (allocation 2.0). A close at mid-range gives about 2.5. A close that has bounced well off the low gives near 0. The forecast is never negative, so the book is long or flat. It is graded continuously in the close location.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The cases are the bars in the regime `mean_reversion` (sharp down days). On each case, take the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows. Regress that excess return on the bar's forecast (`post_pipeline_value` of `near_low`). Report the t of the slope, taking the cases of one day together, with the number of days.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual next-day move of the cases with a forecast of 2 or more, measured in the long direction against the coin's normal drift, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more and the supporting check's mean unusual move is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, meaning closes nearer the low are followed by smaller next-day rises.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with a mean unusual move at or below 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 50 sharp-down-day cases across all coins and windows.
