Fidelity:
- Situation: the coin has risen over the past 5 days -> kept: a regime rule on the 5-day close-to-close return above 0; the book is flat outside it.
- Comparison: that day's high-low range against its average daily range over the past 20 days -> kept: today's range over the mean bar range of the 20 bars ending today, which grades the long.
- Outcome: the wider the range, the more the coin rises over the next 3 days -> kept: a long sized by the range ratio, held 3 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures whether, within 5-day up-runs, 3-day returns above drift grow with the day's range ratio, which is the idea's claim.
Forecast: The regime is "trending" when the 5-bar price change is above 0; otherwise the regime is "unknown" and the book is flat. In a trending bar, the forecast is today's high-low range divided by the mean high-low range of the last 20 bars, today included (`RangeCompressionRatioComponent`, short 1, long 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). This ratio is standardised against its own past 60 values. The forecast is always positive in the regime, about +10 on a day of average range and up to +20 on a very wide day. It shrinks toward 0 as the day narrows, so it is long only and grows with the width of the range.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The sample is the non-overlapping grid of bars at positions 0, 3, 6, … of each window, keeping the bars in the trending regime. On each, take the coin's 3-bar forward return minus its mean 3-bar return over all its windows. Regress that excess return on the bar's forecast. The measure is the slope's t, with cases of the same day taken together.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope of the excess 3-bar return on the forecast is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 200 independent cases.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, over at least 200 independent cases.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, as is any result from fewer than 200 independent in-regime grid cases.
