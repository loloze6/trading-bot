Fidelity:
- Situation: a day whose range is wide compared with its 20-day average range -> adapted: a regime on the day's range over its 20-day mean bar range at or above 1.5, the most common convention since the idea gives no number.
- Comparison: the larger the coin's rise over the past 20 days -> adapted: graded, with the long position growing with the 20-day rise and flat after a 20-day fall, as the consequence says ("refuse that trade").
- Outcome: a larger further rise over the next 3 days -> kept: each bet is held 3 bars, and the 3-day return is measured against the size of the position.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book holds only on wide days and is sized by the 20-day rise, so a slope of 3-day excess return on position size tests exactly the claimed relation.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the day's range divided by the mean bar range of the last 20 days, bar t included (RangeCompressionRatioComponent, short_period 1, long_period 20, base "mean_bar_range", form "width"). - When this ratio is at or above 1.5, the bar is a wide day, labelled `trending`. Every other bar is `unknown` and the book is flat there. - On wide days the forecast is the 20-day percent rise (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 20). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20), so an average-sized 20-day move gives about 10, capped at 20. - It is then clipped at 0. It is positive and grows linearly with the size of the 20-day rise. It is 0 after a 20-day fall, and 0 on every day that is not wide.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The cases are wide-day bars (regime `trending`) with forecast > 0, sampled on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window. For each case, take the coin's 3-bar forward return minus the coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows. Regress that excess return on the forecast; the measure is the slope's t, with cases on the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean excess 3-bar return over the same cases is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is ≥ 2 and the forecast-weighted mean excess return is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is ≤ -2, meaning larger prior rises are followed by smaller excess 3-day returns on wide days.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a t ≥ 2 while the weighted mean is ≤ 0. A run with fewer than 100 cases (or fewer than 60 distinct days) is also inconclusive.
