- Situation: a coin's one-day move against its usual daily swing over the last 20 days -> kept: the 1-day return over the 20-day std of daily returns, graded by its size.
- Comparison: the move's size against the usual 20-day swing -> kept
- Outcome: the next day's price moves against that move, more for sharper moves -> kept: the forecast is the negated scaled move, held 1 bar.

Forecast: One component, VolatilityScaledReturnComponent (period 1, vol_period 20): today's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. Standardise it over its 60 past values (min 20), then negate it. The forecast is negative (short) after an up day and positive (long) after a down day. Its size grows linearly with the move in usual swings, up to the cap of 20. It is zero only on a flat day. It applies on every bar. - Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On bars with a non-zero forecast, the slope of the coin's next-day return minus its mean daily return over all windows, regressed on the forecast (post_pipeline_value). Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: On the bars in the top fifth of |forecast|, the mean next-day excess return taken in the forecast's direction must be above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t >= 2 and the supporting check passes.
