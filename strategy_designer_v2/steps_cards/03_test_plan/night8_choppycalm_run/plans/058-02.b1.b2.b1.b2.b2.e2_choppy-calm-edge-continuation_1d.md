- Situation: in a choppy calm market, where the close sits in its 20-day high-low range -> kept: the 20-day high-low range position is graded, inside the profile that code adds.
- Comparison: nearer the top against nearer the bottom (the nearer, the larger) -> kept: the forecast grows continuously from the bottom of the range to the top.
- Outcome: a rise over the next 5 days near the top, a fall near the bottom -> kept: a 5-bar horizon, long near the top and short near the bottom.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same range position, in the same direction, over the same 5 days, inside the same profile.
Forecast: One indicator, `DonchianBreakoutComponent`. Its settings are period 20, source high_low, form position and exclude_current 0. It gives where today's close sits in the 20-day high-low range, from -20 at the low to +20 at the high, and 0 at the middle. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20) and is not negated, so the trade goes toward the edge. The forecast is positive above the middle of the range and negative below it. It grows linearly as the close nears either edge and is 0 at the middle. Code keeps it flat outside the choppy calm profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, on bars with a non-zero forecast. Regress each case's 5-day forward return, minus the coin's mean 5-day return over all windows, on the forecast at that bar. Report the slope's t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The book's timing gain is above 0. This is its mean daily return before costs minus its gain from the market's own rise.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2 on at least 100 cases, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2 on at least 100 cases. That would mean edges revert, against the idea.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2, or a positive slope whose supporting check fails, is inconclusive. Fewer than 100 grid cases is also inconclusive.
