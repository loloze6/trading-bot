Fidelity:
- Situation: a calm market, the coin swinging less than usual for it -> kept: the profile `calm`, which step 3's code adds as the regime.
- Comparison: the higher the close sits within its 20-day high-low range -> kept: the close's graded position in its 20-day high-low range.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 5 days -> kept: the 5-bar forward return, judged against the coin's normal drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the same quantity, in the same profile, over the same horizon.
Forecast: The forecast is the close's position in its 20-day high-low range, including today's bar. It comes from DonchianBreakoutComponent with period 20, source high_low and form position. That value is -sf at the range low, 0 at the midpoint and +sf at the range high. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), so its typical size is about 10, capped at ±20. The forecast is positive above the range's middle and negative below it, zero at the middle. It grows continuously as the close nears the top or the bottom of the range. Outside the calm profile the code keeps the book flat.

Profile: calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: Use the in-profile bars on a non-overlapping 5-bar grid in each window, with all coins pooled. On each such bar, compute the coin's 5-bar forward return minus its mean 5-bar return over all its windows. Regress that excess return on the recorded forecast and read the slope's t, taking the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The book's mean daily return before costs, minus its gain from the market's own rise (the timing part), is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is +2 or more and the timing part is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any slope t between -2 and +2 is inconclusive. So is a t of +2 or more with a timing part of 0 or below. The minimum is 200 non-overlapping in-profile cases on at least 100 distinct days; below that the test is inconclusive.
