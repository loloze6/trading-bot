Fidelity:
- Situation: a one-day rise against the usual daily swing of the last 20 days, in a falling market -> kept: the 1-bar return over the 20-day std of daily returns counts rises only; the falling profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the rise is judged against the usual daily swing over the last 20 days -> kept
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next day, more after a larger rise -> kept: a short over 1 bar, sized by the scaled rise.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled rise, shorted for its own one-day horizon, so a confirm or a reject carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 1, vol_period 20). It is standardised over its past 60 values, clipped at 0 so that only up days count, and negated. The forecast is 0 on down or flat days. On up days it is negative (short), and its size grows linearly with the rise in usual moves, down to -20. Code adds the falling-market profile, so outside it the book is flat.

Profile: falling (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: On every bar with a forecast below 0, take the next-day return minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows, in the short direction. Average it weighted by abs(forecast). Its t is computed with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: The same unusual move, comparing cases above the median abs(forecast) with cases at or below it (Welch's t). The difference, larger-forecast group minus smaller-forecast group, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean unusual move is positive with t >= 2, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The weighted mean unusual move is negative with t <= -2: the coin rises after sharp up days more than its drift.
- Inconclusive: A main t between -2 and 2, or a main t >= 2 with the supporting difference at or below 0. Any result with fewer than 30 distinct days carrying cases is also inconclusive.
