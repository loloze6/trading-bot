Forecast: The forecast uses one component, the Donchian range position over 20 daily bars. It reads the 20-day high and low including today, takes where the close sits between them, and maps that from -20 at the low to +20 at the high, 0 at the middle. The value is standardised against its own last 60 values (min_periods 20). The forecast is positive and grows as the close nears the 20-day high, which means long. It is negative and grows in size as the close nears the 20-day low, which means short. It is near zero at mid-range. It is graded continuously and capped at ±20. Code adds the rising calm profile, so the book is flat outside it.
