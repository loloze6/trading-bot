Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's close sits higher within its 20-day high-low range, in a choppy market -> kept: DonchianBreakoutComponent at 20 bars on highs and lows; code adds the choppy profile.
- Comparison: higher range positions against lower ones -> kept: the forecast grows with the position in the range, and is negative below the middle.
- Outcome: a larger rise over the next 5 days -> kept: the 5-bar forward return is set against the forecast.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the graded range position in a choppy market is the idea as stated, so the test's verdict is the idea's verdict.
Forecast: The forecast is where the close sits in its 20-day high-low range (bar t included). It is -1 at the range's low, 0 at the middle and +1 at its high. It is standardised against its own past 60 values, so its average size is about 10 and it is capped at ±20. It is positive (long) above the middle of the range and negative (short) below it. Its size grows linearly the nearer the close is to the top or the bottom. It is zero only at the exact middle. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 5-bar forward return on the forecast. The return is taken minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows. Bars are the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, with nonzero forecast (inside the profile), pooled over coins. The t of the slope uses standard errors clustered by day.
- Supporting check: On the same grid, the mean excess 5-bar return of the top third of forecasts minus that of the bottom third is above zero.
- Confirm: Slope t ≥ 2 and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: Slope t ≤ -2, meaning higher range positions are followed by smaller rises.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or t ≥ 2 with the top-minus-bottom difference at or below zero. Fewer than 100 grid cases inside the profile, or cases on fewer than 60 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
