Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's close position within its 20-day high-low range, in a choppy, calm market -> kept: the choppy calm profile is added by code, and the range position over 20 daily bars uses highs and lows.
- Comparison: the higher the close sits in the range, the larger the rise -> kept: the forecast is graded by range position, from the bottom of the range (short) to the top (long).
- Outcome: the coin rises over the next 5 days, more the higher the close -> kept: the horizon is 5 bars on daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a forward return that grows with range position inside choppy calm confirms the idea, and a reliably reversed relation rejects it.
Forecast: One component, the Donchian range position over the last 20 daily bars, with bar t included and highs and lows as the range. Its raw value is (2 x (close − 20-day low) / (20-day high − 20-day low) − 1) x 20, which runs from −20 at the range's low to +20 at its high and is 0 at the middle. It is standardised against its past 60 values (min_periods 20). The forecast is positive and grows as the close nears the top of the range, is negative and grows in size as the close nears the bottom, and is near 0 at the middle. It is active on every bar; step 3's code keeps the book flat outside the choppy calm profile.

Profile: choppy calm (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's 5-bar forward return on the forecast, on the non-overlapping 5-bar grid of each window, over bars with a nonzero forecast. The forward return is measured minus the coin's mean 5-bar return over all its windows, so that holding the coin is not credited. The slope's t is taken with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: none
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2: the excess 5-day return grows with the close's height in its 20-day range.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ −2: closes higher in the range are followed by smaller excess 5-day returns, which is a reversion and not a continuation.
- Inconclusive: A slope with t between −2 and 2. Fewer than 100 grid cases with a nonzero forecast, spread over at least 50 distinct days, is also inconclusive whatever the t.
