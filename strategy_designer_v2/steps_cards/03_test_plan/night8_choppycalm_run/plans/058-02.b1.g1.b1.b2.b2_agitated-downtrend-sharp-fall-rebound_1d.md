Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's one-day fall, in a falling, agitated market -> kept: the day's return scaled by usual swing, fall side only; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the fall against the usual daily swing over the last 20 days -> kept: the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns.
- Outcome: the larger the fall, the larger the rise over the next day -> kept: a long position graded by the fall's size, held 1 bar.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own scaled fall, sized as the idea says, judged on the next day's return.
Forecast: The indicator is `VolatilityScaledReturnComponent` with period 1 and vol_period 20. It is the day's return divided by the standard deviation of the last 20 daily returns. The forecast negates this value and clips it at 0, so it counts the day's fall in usual daily swings. It is then standardised against its own past 60 values (min_periods 20, average absolute value about 10, capped at 20). The forecast is positive after a down day and grows linearly with how many usual swings the fall measured. It is 0 on up and flat days, and never negative. It runs on every bar; the profile code keeps it flat outside falling agitated markets.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 1 bar
- Main measure: The slope of the coin's next-day return (close[t+1]/close[t] - 1, minus the coin's mean daily return over all windows) on the forecast, over bars with a positive forecast. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The forecast-weighted mean of the next-day return in excess of the coin's mean daily return, on bars with a positive forecast, is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: A slope t strictly between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a slope t of 2 or more with a failed check. Fewer than 100 bars with a positive forecast, or cases falling on fewer than 50 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
