Fidelity:
- Situation: in a falling, agitated market, a close near the top of the day's high-low range -> kept: a regime selects only bars whose close sits in the upper half of the day's range (the most common convention, close-location value above 0); the profile is added by code
- Comparison: the nearer the close sits to the day's high, the larger the fall -> kept: inside the regime the short grows linearly from 0 at mid-range to its largest at the day's high
- Outcome: the price falls over the next day -> kept: a short held for 1 daily bar
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on upper-range closes inside the profile, graded by nearness to the high, and holds each bet for the next day.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the close's location in bar t's range, (close − low) / (high − low). A bar with a location of 0.5 or more goes to `mean_reversion`; every other bar goes to `unknown`, which is flat. Code adds the falling-agitated profile vetoes. Inside the regime the signal is the day's range position from `DonchianBreakoutComponent` (period 1, `source` "high_low"), which is (2 × location − 1) × sf. It is 0 at mid-range and +sf at the day's high. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), negated, and clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore 0 or negative, so the book is short only. It is 0 at the regime's edge, so the position does not jump there, and the short grows in step with how near the close is to the high (about −20 at the high, the cap). It is zero outside the upper half and outside the profile.

Its t treats the cases of one day as one piece of evidence.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median |forecast|. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half (Welch) must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is positive with t ≥ 2, over at least 30 distinct days, and the supporting check's difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure is negative with t ≤ −2, over at least 30 distinct days.
