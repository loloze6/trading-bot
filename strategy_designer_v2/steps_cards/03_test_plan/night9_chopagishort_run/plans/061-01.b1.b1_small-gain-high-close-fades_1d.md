Fidelity:
- Situation: the daily close sits near the top of that day's high-low range, in a choppy, agitated market -> kept: a regime rule puts the close's location in the day's range at 0.8 or more (the usual "top fifth" convention); the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the smaller the day's gain, the larger the fall -> adapted: graded by the day's gain in usual daily moves, centred at one usual move, and zero on strong up days, as the consequence says.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next five days -> kept: a short held for 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts only near-high closes in the profile, sizes them by how little the day gained, and judges the 5-day fall.
Forecast: The regime detector reads where the close sits in the day's range (`CloseLocationInRangeComponent`, reference "low"). Bars where it is 0.8 or more go to `mean_reversion`; every other bar is flat. Inside that regime the forecast comes from one component, `MeanAbsScaledReturnComponent` (period 1, window 30, form "centred"). It is the day's close-to-close return divided by the mean absolute daily move of the last 30 days, minus 1. It is then standardised (window 60, min_periods 20) and clipped at a maximum of 0. The result: - On a day whose gain is one usual move or more, the forecast is 0, so a strong up day is never shorted. - Below that, the forecast is negative and continuous, so the position is short only. - The short grows as the gain shrinks: a small gain gives a moderate short, and a down day that still closes near its high gives a larger one, up to -20.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case: a case is a short bet (the forecast falling by at least 2 points), read once per 5-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift, in the short direction. Report its mean and its t, with the cases of one day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of the signal day's raw gain (`last_history_value`). The mean unusual move of the smaller-gain half minus that of the larger-gain half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive (the coin fell more than its drift), with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative (the coin rose more than its drift), with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails, and so is any result from fewer than 30 cases or cases falling on fewer than 30 distinct days.
