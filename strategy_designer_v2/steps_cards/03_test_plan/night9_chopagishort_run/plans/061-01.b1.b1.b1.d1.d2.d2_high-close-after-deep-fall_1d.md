- Situation: the daily close sits near the top of the day's high-low range after a 5-day fall -> adapted: a regime holds only on bars whose close is in the top fifth of the day's range (the 0.8 close-location convention) while the 5-bar return is below zero ("after" made "while").
- Comparison: the deeper the 5-day fall, the larger the fall that follows -> kept: inside the regime, the short is sized by the depth of the 5-bar fall.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next five days -> kept: each short is held 5 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on high-close bars after a 5-day fall, sized by the fall's depth and held 5 days, so its 5-day result answers the idea in both directions.
Forecast: The regime detector reads two values on every bar. The first is the close's location in the day's range, (close - low) / (high - low). The second is the 5-bar return. A bar is in the situation, the mean_reversion regime, when the close location is at least 0.8 and the 5-bar return is below 0. On every other bar the forecast is 0 and the book is flat. Inside the situation, the forecast is the 5-bar return, standardised against its own past 60 values, so it is about -10 for a usual 5-day fall and up to -20 for a fall twice as deep or more. It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so the book is short only. The forecast is always negative or zero, and its size grows continuously with the depth of the fall.
