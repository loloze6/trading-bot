- Situation: a choppy, agitated market where the daily close sits in the upper part of the day's high-low range after a negative 5-day return -> kept: the profile is added by code; "upper part" (close location above the midpoint, 0.5, by convention) and "5-day return below 0" are regime rules, so the book is flat outside them.
- Comparison: the deeper the 5-day fall, the larger the next fall -> adapted: the short position grows with the depth of the 5-day fall.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next five days -> kept: short, each bet held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the book is short only on the idea's bars, sized by the fall's depth, and is judged on the 5-day move after each bet.
Forecast: The forecast is zero except on bars inside the choppy agitated profile where two conditions both hold. First, the close sits above the middle of the day's range: (close − low) / (high − low) is above 0.5. Second, the 5-day return close[t]/close[t-5] − 1 is negative. On those bars the forecast is the 5-day return in percent, standardised against its own past 60 values (target 10, cap 20). Because the return is negative there, the forecast is negative, a short. Its size grows linearly with the depth of the 5-day fall, so a shallow fall gives a small short. A clip at 0 keeps it short-only.

- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, measured in the short direction against the coin's normal 5-day drift. - Inconclusive: Either of these is inconclusive: - a t between −2 and 2; - a t ≥ 2 while the larger-size half does not beat the smaller-size half. At least 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
