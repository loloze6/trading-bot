- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a daily close near the top of the day's high-low range -> adapted: the profile is added by code; "near the top" becomes a regime on close location of 0.8 or more (the usual IBS convention), and the position is graded by nearness inside it.
- Comparison: none -> kept
- Outcome: price falls over the next two days, more when the close is nearer the high -> kept: a short held 2 bars, sized by the close location.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test measures the fall after these closes inside the stated profile, and its size by nearness, as the idea states them.
Forecast: The regime is active only when the day's close location, (close - low) / (high - low), is at least 0.8, and only inside the choppy-agitated profile, which code adds. Every other bar is flat. Inside the regime, the forecast is the close location (`CloseLocationInRangeComponent`, reference "low") standardised over its past 60 bars and then negated. It is always zero or negative, so the strategy is short only. It runs from about -16 at a close location of 0.8 to -20 at a close on the high, so the short grows as the close nears the high.

Side: short only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code
Profile: choppy agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 2 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, taken in the short direction. A case is a short bet (a forecast drop of at least 2 points) read on a non-overlapping 2-bar grid. Its value is the coin's 2-bar return minus the coin's normal 2-bar drift, sign flipped. The measure is the mean over cases, with its t, counting the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at their median close location. The mean unusual fall of the upper half minus that of the lower half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual fall is above 0 with t >= 2, and the upper-half minus lower-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual fall is below 0 with t <= -2, meaning prices rise after high closes.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirming main measure whose supporting check fails is also inconclusive. The test needs at least 30 cases falling on at least 30 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
