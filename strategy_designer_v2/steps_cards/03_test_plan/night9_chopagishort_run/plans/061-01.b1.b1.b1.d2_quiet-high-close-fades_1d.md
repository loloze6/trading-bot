- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a close near the top of the day's high-low range on a day with a small gain -> adapted: "near the top" becomes a regime condition, close in the top 20 % of the day's range (the internal-bar-strength convention); "small gain" becomes an up day with a gain under one usual daily move, with the position graded by how small the gain is; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the day's gain against the coin's usual daily swing -> kept: the day's return divided by the mean absolute daily return over 20 days.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next five days, and the smaller the gain, the larger the fall -> kept: a short held 5 bars, larger as the gain nears 0.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts only high-close, small-gain up days in the profile, with size growing as the gain shrinks, and judges the 5-day fall.
Forecast: The forecast trades only on bars inside the profile (added by code) where three conditions hold: - the close sits at or above 80 % of the day's high-low range; - the day closed up; - the day's gain is less than one usual daily move (the mean absolute daily return over the last 20 days). On those bars the forecast reads the day's gain in usual moves minus 1, standardised over its past 60 values (20 at least), and clipped at 0 so that it is never positive. - At a gain near 0 the short is largest, about -10 (100 % short). - The short shrinks linearly as the gain grows toward one usual move, where it is 0. - A larger up day reads 0 (no fade). On every other bar the regime is null and the forecast is 0. The book is short only.
