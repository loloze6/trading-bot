- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a day closing in the upper part of its high-low range with a small gain -> adapted: the close location (at or above mid-range) and the small up move (above 0 and at most one usual daily move) form a regime; the brief's profile is added by code; "after the day" becomes "while that day".
- Comparison: the day's gain against its usual daily swing -> kept: the day's return in 20-day standard deviations of daily returns.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next ten days, more the higher the close sits in the range -> kept: a short graded by the close's position above mid-range, held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config shorts exactly these days, sized by the close's height in the range, over the idea's ten days.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on days that close at or above the middle of their own high-low range and gain more than 0 but at most one usual daily move (the day's return over the 20-day standard deviation of daily returns). On those days the forecast is short. Its size is the close's position in the day's range, 2 x (close − low) / (high − low) − 1. That position is 0 at mid-range and largest at the high. It is standardised over its past 60 values, negated, and clipped at 0, so it never goes long. On every other day the forecast is 0. The choppy agitated profile is added by code.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a short bet: a rise of the forecast on the short side of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return after the signal minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift, taken in the short direction. Its t clusters the cases by day, so this is judged before costs and against holding the coin.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median absolute forecast. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A positive t ≥ 2 with a failed supporting check is also inconclusive. So are fewer than 30 cases, or cases falling on fewer than 30 distinct days.
