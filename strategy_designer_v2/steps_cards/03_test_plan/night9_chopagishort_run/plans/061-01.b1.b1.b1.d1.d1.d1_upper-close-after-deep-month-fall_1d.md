A deep fall, which the idea leaves unnumbered, is taken as ≤ -10 % by convention.
- Comparison: the deeper the 20-day fall, the larger the fall -> kept: inside the regime the short is graded by the size of the 20-day fall.

Forecast: The forecast is zero on every bar outside one situation. That situation holds only when all five of these are true: - the 60-day return is between -10 % and +10 %; - the 30-day volatility percentile over the past 365 days is above 0.5; - the close sits above the middle of the day's high-low range; - the 5-day return is below 0; - the 20-day return is -10 % or lower. Inside that situation the forecast is short. It is the 20-day return in percent, standardised over its last 60 values (target 10, cap 20). It is clipped at 0 so it can never be long. A deeper 20-day fall gives a larger short.
