Forecast: The regime detector reads the 20-day return in percent, `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 20 and sf 1. When that return is -10 % or lower, the bar goes to the active regime; on every other bar the book is flat. Inside the regime the forecast is the same 20-day return, standardised against its own past 60 values (min 20) and clipped at 0 from above. It is therefore always short, and the short grows with the depth of the fall, up to the cap of -20. The choppy profile is added by code, so outside 60-day returns of -10 % to +10 % the book is flat.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The mean unusual move of the cases, short side positive. A case is a short bet read on each coin's 5-bar grid. Its unusual move is minus (the 5-bar return from the case bar minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift over all windows). The t of that mean is computed with each day's cases taken together.
- Supporting check: Among the cases, split at the median position size (larger shorts first). The mean unusual move of the larger-short half minus that of the smaller-short half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The main measure has a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check is above 0.
- Reject: The main measure has a t of -2 or less, meaning the prices rose more than their drift after deep 20-day falls.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A confirm-level t with the supporting check at or below 0 is also inconclusive. So is any result with fewer than 30 distinct days of cases.
