Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy, agitated market, a daily close in the upper part of its high-low range after a deep 5-day fall -> kept: the profile is added by code, and a detector rule requires a close location of at least 0.5 and a 5-day return of -1 usual move or less (the convention for "deep").
- Comparison: the deeper the 5-day fall, the larger the fall that follows -> kept: inside the situation the short is graded by the depth of the 5-day fall in usual moves.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next three days -> kept: short, horizon 3 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test holds shorts only in the idea's situation, sized by its own strength, over its own horizon.
Forecast: Two detector components set the situation. The first is the close's location in the day's range (CloseLocationInRangeComponent, reference "low"). The second is the 5-day return in usual moves (VolatilityScaledReturnComponent, period 5, vol_period 20, the most common volatility window on daily bars). A bar is in the situation only when the location is at least 0.5 and the 5-day return is -1 usual move or lower. Every other bar is flat. Inside the situation the forecast is the same 5-day scaled return, standardised over its past 60 values. It is always negative there, so the book is always short, and the short grows in size the deeper the fall. A clip at 0 keeps the book short-only.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: The coin's return over the next 3 bars, minus its normal 3-bar drift, read in the short direction. It is taken from the close of each case bar, a case being a short bet (the forecast falls by at least 2 points), on the non-overlapping 3-bar grid of each window. The t is computed with cases on the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: The same short-direction excess return is larger for the cases with |forecast| above the median than for those below it (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The mean short-direction excess return is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-direction excess return is below 0 with t ≤ -2: the price rises after these closes.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure whose supporting check fails. Fewer than 30 cases on distinct days is inconclusive whatever the t.
