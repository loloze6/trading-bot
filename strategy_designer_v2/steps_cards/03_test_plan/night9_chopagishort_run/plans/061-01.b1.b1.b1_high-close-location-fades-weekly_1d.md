Fidelity:
- Situation: a daily close near the top of the day's high-low range, in a choppy, agitated market -> kept: the close location (close - low) / (high - low) of at least 0.8 is the regime, using the usual internal-bar-strength level; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the nearer the close is to the day's high, the larger the fall -> kept: inside the regime, the short grows with the close location.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next five days -> kept: a short, held 5 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config shorts exactly those closes, sized by nearness to the high, for five days, so its result is the idea's.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the close location of the day's bar: (close - low) / (high - low), from 0 at the low to 1 at the high. When it is 0.8 or more, the bar is in `mean_reversion`; on every other bar the book is flat. Code also keeps it flat outside the choppy, agitated profile. Inside the regime, the forecast is the same close location, standardised against its past 60 daily values and then negated. It is always short, never positive. Its size runs from about -16 at a close location of 0.8 to -20 for a close at the day's high, so the nearer the close is to the high, the larger the short.

Side: short only (the brief): each component's forecast capped at 0 by step 3's code

Criterion:
- Horizon: 5 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bets (each rise of the short forecast of at least the band), read once per 5-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 5-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 5-bar drift, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of this unusual move, and its t with the cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: Split the cases by close location, read from `high_close.last_history_value`. The mean short-side unusual move of the cases at 0.9 or more, minus that of the cases from 0.8 to below 0.9, must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean short-side unusual move is above 0 with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean short-side unusual move is below 0 with a t of -2 or less: prices rose after these closes.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with a failed check. The test needs at least 30 cases falling on at least 30 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive whatever the t.
