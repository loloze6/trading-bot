Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy market -> kept: a regime is active only while the 30-day return is below 0, and code adds the choppy profile.
- Comparison: deepest slides carry the continuation more than in proportion, shallow ones little -> adapted: the short is sized by depth with the strongest graded dead zone (cubic), not by a cutoff.
- Outcome: a further fall over the next 10 days -> kept: a short held for 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts only falling coins, sized convexly by depth, so a pass or a fail speaks to the idea itself.
Forecast: The regime detector computes the 30-day percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30, scaling factor 1). When that return is below 0 (a monthly fall), the bar is "trending"; every other bar is flat. Inside the regime, the same 30-day return is the forecast source: - It is standardised over its past 60 values, which puts the depth on the scale of the coin's usual 30-day move. A usual-size move reads about -10, capped at -20. - signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 10 then makes it sharply convex. A slide of half the usual size gives about -1.25, so the position is near zero and below the trade band. A usual slide gives -10, and a slide of 1.5 times usual gives about -34, which the forecast clip holds at -20. - clip with max 0 keeps the forecast short-only. The forecast is negative only during a monthly fall, zero otherwise, and grows in size with the cube of the fall's depth relative to the coin's usual monthly move.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move in the short direction, measured over 10 bars after cases in the top third of position size. A case is a bet bar, read on the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window. The unusual move is minus the coin's 10-bar return after the case, minus its normal drift (that is, measured against simply holding the coin). Its t takes the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: Convexity: the mean unusual move of the top-third cases is larger than that of the bottom-third cases (top minus bottom > 0).
- Confirm: Both must hold: - the top-third unusual move is positive with t >= 2, on at least 30 cases over at least 30 distinct days; - the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The top-third unusual move is negative with t <= -2 (prices rise against drift after the deepest slides), on at least 30 cases over at least 30 days.
- Inconclusive: Any of these results is inconclusive: - a t between -2 and 2; - a confirming main measure whose convexity check fails; - fewer than 30 top-third cases, or fewer than 30 distinct days.
