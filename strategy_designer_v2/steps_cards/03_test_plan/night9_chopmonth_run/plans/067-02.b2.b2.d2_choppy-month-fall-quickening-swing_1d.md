Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, a plain 30-day fall below zero, larger means stronger -> adapted: the choppy profile is added by code; the 30-day return is clipped at zero and graded, so a deeper fall gives a larger short.
- Comparison: only while the 10-day swing is larger than the 30-day swing -> kept: a regime rule that holds while the 10-day standard deviation of daily returns is above the 30-day one, and nothing is held outside it.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 10 days -> kept: a short held for 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is short only, in proportion to the 30-day fall, only in choppy bars where recent swings are quickening, and it is judged on the next 10 days.
Forecast: The forecast is computed only on bars where the 10-day standard deviation of daily returns is above the 30-day one (from RecentToPriorVolatilityRatioComponent with vol_period 10, base_period 30, base_lag 0; rule: ratio > 1). It is zero on every other bar. Inside that condition, the 30-day return in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30) is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20) and clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore 0 after a flat or rising month and negative after a fall. The short grows linearly with the depth of the fall against the coin's usual 30-day move size, up to -20. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window and take the grid bars with forecast < 0. For each case, the score is (-forecast / 10) × -(close[t+10]/close[t] - 1 - the coin's mean 10-bar return over all windows). This is the position-weighted excess fall below holding the coin, before costs. Report its mean and its t, with cases of the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of |forecast|. The larger half must have a higher mean excess fall (close[t+10]/close[t] - 1 minus the coin's mean 10-bar return, sign reversed) than the smaller half.
- Confirm: The mean score is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean score is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is t ≥ 2 when the supporting check fails. At least 30 distinct case days are needed; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
