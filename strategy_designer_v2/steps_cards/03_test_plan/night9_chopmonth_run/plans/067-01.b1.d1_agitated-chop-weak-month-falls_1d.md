- Situation: a coin's fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy, agitated market -> kept: the 30-day return, short only when it is negative; code adds the profile.
- Comparison: a larger fall against a smaller one -> adapted: graded, so the short position grows with the depth of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 7 days, more after larger falls -> kept: the position is held 7 bars and judged on the 7-day forward return.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall, graded, short only, inside the idea's profile, so the test's result carries over to the idea.
Forecast: The 30-day return in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30), standardised against its own past 60 values (min 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is negative (short) when the coin is below its close of 30 days ago, and the short grows in proportion to the depth of that fall, up to -20. It is zero when the coin is at or above its close of 30 days ago. Code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window, on grid bars with a negative forecast. Regress each case's 7-bar forward return, minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift, on the forecast. The idea predicts a positive slope: deeper shorts are followed by deeper falls. Report the slope's t.
- Supporting check: The mean unusual move of the cases in the short direction (the workflow's size measure, before costs and net of the coin's drift) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope's t is 2 or more, and the mean unusual move in the short direction is above 0.
- Reject: The slope's t is -2 or less, meaning deeper falls are followed by weaker falls or by rises relative to drift.
- Inconclusive: A slope t between -2 and 2, or a confirming slope with a mean unusual move at or below 0. Also inconclusive if there are fewer than 30 non-overlapping cases on at least 20 distinct days.
