- Situation: a choppy market and a plain percentage fall over the past 30 days -> adapted: the choppy market (60-day return from -10 % to +10 %) is a regime; inside it, the 30-day percent fall is graded steeply.
- Comparison: larger slides against smaller ones -> kept: the position grows with the cube of the standardised fall, so the deepest slides carry most of the size.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 10 days -> kept: short, held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test shorts in proportion to the raw 30-day fall, only in a flat two-month market, over 10 days, which is the idea as stated.
Forecast: The regime is "chop" when the coin's 60-day percent return is between -10 and +10; every other bar is flat. Inside the regime, the forecast uses the coin's 30-day percent return. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), clipped at 0 from above so that only falls count, and passed through signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 10. The forecast is 0 after a 30-day rise. It is negative (short) after a fall and grows with the cube of the fall's size against its usual size: a usual fall gives about -10, half that gives about -1.25, a double one reaches the -20 cap. It never goes positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: Use the bets (forecast falls of at least 2 points on the short side), read once per 10-bar grid cell of each window. For each bet, take the coin's 10-bar return from the bet's close minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows, multiplied by -1 (the short direction). The measure is the mean of that value, with its t computed with cases of the same day summed together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases into thirds by the size of the forecast at the bet. The mean signed unusual move of the largest-forecast third minus that of the smallest third must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean signed unusual move is positive with t >= 2 and the supporting check passes (largest third minus smallest third > 0).
- Reject: The mean signed unusual move is negative with t <= -2: the coins rebound after deep slides in choppy markets.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or t >= 2 with the largest-third difference at or below 0. The minimum is 30 distinct days with cases; below that the result is inconclusive.
