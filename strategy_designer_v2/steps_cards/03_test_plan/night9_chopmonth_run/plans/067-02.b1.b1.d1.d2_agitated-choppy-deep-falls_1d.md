Forecast: The forecast uses PriceEvolutionComponent with period 30 and scaling_factor 1, the 30-day percent return. Then signed_power with exponent 3 and pivot 10 is applied. The forecast is never positive. It is near zero for shallow falls and grows cubically more negative as the month's fall deepens: a fall of one usual size gives -10, and half that gives -1.25. The book holds nothing outside the choppy agitated profile, which code adds.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 21 bars
- Main measure: The cases are grid bars of a non-overlapping 21-bar grid per window with forecast at -10 or below (the deepest falls). For each case, take the coin's 21-bar return from that close minus the coin's mean 21-bar return over all windows (holding the coin), counted in the short direction. The measure is the mean over cases, with its t taken over days (cases on the same day summed into one piece of evidence).
- Supporting check: On the same grid, the mean short-direction excess move of the deep cases (forecast -10 or below) is larger than that of the shallow cases (forecast below 0 and above -10).
- Confirm: The deep cases' mean short-direction excess move is above 0 with t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The deep cases' mean short-direction excess move is below 0 with t of -2 or less: the coin rises more than its drift after deep falls.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t whose supporting check fails, is inconclusive. Fewer than 20 deep cases is also inconclusive whatever the t.
