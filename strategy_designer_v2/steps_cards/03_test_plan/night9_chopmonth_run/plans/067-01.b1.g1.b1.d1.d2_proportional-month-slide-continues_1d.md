Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy (flat two-month) market, a coin's plain percentage fall over the past 30 days -> kept: choppy is a regime where the 60-day return is between -10 % and +10 % (the profile convention); inside it, the 30-day percent fall grades the short.
- Comparison: larger fall versus smaller fall, in linear proportion rather than steeply -> kept: the short is linear in the fall's depth, with no power transform.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 10 days -> kept: short positions held 10 bars, judged on the 10-day return against the coin's drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a linear short on the 30-day fall, taken only in the choppy regime and held 10 days, is exactly the idea's claim.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the 60-day percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 60, scaling factor 1). - Where the 60-day return is between -10 and +10, the bar is in the choppy regime. Elsewhere the book is flat. - Inside the choppy regime, the forecast is the 30-day percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30), standardised over its own past 60 values (min_periods 20) and clipped at a maximum of 0. - The forecast is 0 after a 30-day rise or a flat month. After a fall it is negative (short). - The short grows linearly with the depth of the fall: an average-sized fall gives about -10, and the cap is -20. - There is no dead-zone power, so moderate and deep slides are both sized in proportion.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars of the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window that are in the chop regime with forecast < 0. For each case, take forecast/10 x (the coin's 10-bar return - the coin's mean 10-bar return over all its windows). Measure the mean of this over the cases and its t, with cases on the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median |forecast|. The mean short gain over drift (-(10-bar return - drift)) is above 0 in both the moderate half and the deep half.
- Confirm: The main mean is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes (both halves positive).
- Reject: The main mean is below 0 with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: A t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with one half not positive. At least 30 independent case-days are required; fewer is inconclusive.
