Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy market, with the coin's close under its close of 30 days earlier -> adapted: choppy is a regime (60-day return from -10 % to +10 %); the 30-day fall below zero is graded, and the position is 0 when the 30-day return is at or above zero
- Comparison: the plain 30-day return against zero, not against a usual month -> kept
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 7 days, and falls more after larger 30-day falls -> kept
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config shorts only choppy-market bars with a 30-day fall, sized by that fall and held 7 days, so its result reads straight onto the idea.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the coin's 60-day return in percent (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 60, scaling factor 1). Bars with that return from -10 to +10 are the choppy regime; every other bar is flat. Inside the choppy regime, the forecast is the coin's 30-day return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30), standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20). It is then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is negative (short) only when the close sits below its close of 30 days earlier, and the short grows in proportion to the depth of that fall. The forecast is 0 when the 30-day return is at or above zero, and 0 outside a choppy market. It is never positive.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window, on cells with forecast < 0. For each cell, take -(forecast / 10) x (the coin's 7-bar return minus its normal 7-bar drift over all windows); this is the short's gain against holding the coin. Measure the mean of these values and its t, with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cells at the median |forecast|. The mean short-direction excess move of the deeper-fall half must exceed that of the shallower half (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The main mean is above 0 with t >= 2 on at least 30 case-days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main mean is below 0 with t <= -2 on at least 30 case-days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming t with the check failing, or fewer than 30 case-days.
