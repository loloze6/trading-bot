Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the close sits below its close of 30 days earlier, as a plain percent -> kept: "choppy" is a regime, the 60-day return between -10 % and +10 % (the brief's profile convention), and inside it the 30-day percent fall, with nothing held when the close is above its 30-day-earlier close.
- Comparison: the decline grows faster than the depth, so deep falls carry nearly all of it and shallow falls almost none -> kept: the short is a cubic power of the standardised depth.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 7 days -> kept: 7 daily bars, short side.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the config shorts in proportion to the cube of the plain 30-day fall, only in choppy markets, and holds each bet 7 days, which is the idea as stated.
Forecast: A regime rule reads the coin's 60-day return in percent (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 60, `scaling_factor` 1). When that return is between -10 and +10, the bar is choppy and the "chop" regime is active. On every other bar the book is flat. Inside the regime, the forecast starts from the plain 30-day percent change of the close (`PriceEvolutionOnPeriodComponent`, `comparison_period` 30). It is standardised against its own past 60 values (`min_periods` 20), and its positive side is clipped to 0, so a coin above its close of 30 days ago gets no position. The result then goes through `signed_power` with exponent 3 and pivot 10. The forecast is therefore 0 or negative (short), and its size grows with the cube of the fall's depth. A typical fall (about -10 standardised) gives -10, a fall half as deep gives -1.25, and the deepest falls reach the -20 cap. Shallow falls carry almost nothing.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars on each window's non-overlapping 7-bar grid where the forecast is below 0. Take the third of these cases with the largest absolute forecast. For each one, compute the drift-adjusted short return: minus (close[t+7]/close[t] - 1 - the coin's mean 7-bar return over all its windows). The measure is the mean of this return, with its t, clustering the cases of one day together.
- Supporting check: The same mean for the largest third of cases minus the same mean for the smallest third of negative-forecast cases. This difference must be above 0 (convexity).
- Confirm: The main mean has t >= 2 and the supporting difference is above 0.
- Reject: The main mean has t <= -2, meaning the deepest falls are followed by rises beyond the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: Any main t between -2 and 2. It is also inconclusive if the main t is 2 or more but the supporting difference is 0 or below. A decision needs at least 30 cases in the largest third, falling on at least 20 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
