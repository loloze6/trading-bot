- Situation: a coin's percentage fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy agitated market -> kept: the 30-day return, short side only; the profile is added by code.
- Comparison: larger falls against smaller falls -> kept: the short grows with the size of the fall.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 10 days -> kept: short, held 10 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes
Forecast: The 30-day percent return (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30), standardised over its past 60 values and clipped at a maximum of 0. It is zero after a 30-day rise or a flat month. After a fall it is negative (short), and the short grows linearly with the depth of the fall against the coin's usual 30-day move size, capped at -20. It never goes long. Code adds the choppy agitated profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 10-bar grid of each window and keep the grid bars with forecast < 0. For each such bar, the signed excess return is (-forecast / 10) x -(close[t+10]/close[t] - 1 - the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all its windows). It is positive when the fall continues. The measure is the mean of this value and its t, with cases of the same day summed into one.
- Supporting check: The mean signed excess return per case, unweighted, is larger in the half of cases with the largest |forecast| than in the half with the smallest (difference above 0).
- Confirm: The main measure's mean is above 0 with t >= 2 on at least 30 distinct days, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's mean is below 0 with t <= -2 on at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2. Also inconclusive: t >= 2 with the supporting check failing, or fewer than 30 distinct case days.
