Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the coin's close is under its close of 30 days earlier, and the gap is larger -> kept: the 30-day return, standardised and clipped at 0, so the book is flat at or above zero and the short grows with the fall; the choppy condition is the brief's profile, which code adds.
- Comparison: the depth of the fall, from zero ("the larger... the more") -> kept: the size of the forecast is the size of the fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 3 days, more after a deeper fall -> kept: a short held 3 bars, sized by the fall.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own graded short inside the choppy profile, so a confirm or a reject of the size-weighted 3-day outcome is a confirm or a reject of the idea.
Forecast: One component, the plain 30-bar return in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30). It is standardised over the 60 values before the bar, then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is 0 when the coin is at or above its close of 30 days earlier. It is negative (short) when the coin is below that close, and grows in size continuously with the depth of the 30-day fall against its usual size, up to -20. It is never positive. The code keeps it flat outside the choppy profile.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 3 bars
- Main measure: On each window's non-overlapping 3-bar grid, the bars with forecast below 0. For each such bar, the excess 3-bar return is the coin's return over the next 3 bars minus that coin's mean 3-bar return over all its windows. The measure is the mean of (-forecast / 10) x the negated excess return: the size-weighted short gain beyond holding the coin. Its t is computed with the cases of one day summed together.
- Supporting check: Among the same cases, the mean excess 3-bar return of the deepest third by forecast minus that of the shallowest third must be below 0.
- Confirm: The main measure is above 0 with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure is below 0 with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a confirming main measure whose supporting check fails. The test also needs at least 100 grid cases on at least 30 distinct days; fewer cases is inconclusive.
