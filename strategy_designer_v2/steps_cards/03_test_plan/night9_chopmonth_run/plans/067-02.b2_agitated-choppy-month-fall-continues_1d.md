- Situation: choppy agitated market, close under its close of 30 days earlier -> kept: the 30-day return clipped at zero, so there is no position at or above zero; the code adds the profile.
- Comparison: the larger the 30-day fall -> adapted: graded, so the short grows with the depth of the fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls more over the next 7 days -> kept: short, 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is exactly the graded 30-day fall inside the profile, held for 7 days.
Forecast: The forecast uses one component, `PriceEvolutionComponent` with period 30 and scaling_factor 1, which gives the 30-day return in percent. It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20), so a typical move reads about 10. It is then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is 0 whenever the close is at or above its close 30 days earlier, and negative (short) below it. It is continuous from 0 and grows in size with the depth of the fall, capped at -20. It is never positive. Step 3's code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window and take the grid bars with a forecast below 0. On those bars, take the coin's 7-bar return minus its normal 7-bar drift (its mean 7-bar return over all windows). Measure the least-squares slope of that excess return on the forecast. A positive slope means deeper falls are followed by larger excess falls. Its t uses errors clustered by day.
- Supporting check: Over the same cases, the mean unusual move in the short direction (minus the excess return) is above 0.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, and the mean unusual move in the short direction is above 0.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: The result is inconclusive in two cases. One is a slope t between -2 and 2. The other is a slope t ≥ 2 while the mean unusual move is at or below 0. At least 30 grid cases with a forecast below 0 are needed, on at least 20 distinct days; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
