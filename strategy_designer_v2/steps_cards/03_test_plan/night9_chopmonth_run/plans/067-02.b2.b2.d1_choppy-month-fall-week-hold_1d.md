Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's plain 30-day fall below zero, in a choppy market -> kept: the 30-day return is clipped at 0, so there is no position unless the return is below zero; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: the 30-day-old close, as zero, with larger falls compared to smaller ones -> kept: the short is sized by the depth of the 30-day fall, graded from 0.
- Outcome: the coin falls more over the next 7 days, even after it climbs back over its 30-day-old close -> kept: each short bet is held 7 bars after it is taken.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall, short only, and each bet is held for the idea's week.
Forecast: One component, the 30-day return of the close in percent (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30). It is standardised against its last 60 values (min_periods 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is therefore 0 whenever the close is at or above its close of 30 days earlier. It is negative (short) when the close is below that level, and the short grows with the depth of the fall, up to -20. It is never positive. Code adds the choppy profile and keeps the book flat outside it. The 7-day holding period keeps each short bet open after the coin recovers its month-old close.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the bars of each window's non-overlapping 7-bar grid where the forecast is below 0. For each case, take the coin's 7-bar return from that close and subtract the coin's normal 7-bar drift (its mean over all windows). Turn the sign to the short direction, so a fall counts as positive. Average the cases, weighting each by |forecast|. Compute the t with the cases of the same day summed into one deviation.
- Supporting check: Take the bars where the book is held short while the forecast is 0, that is, after the close has climbed back over its 30-day-old close. On those bars, the mean gain before costs (position x the next bar's return) must be at least 0.
- Confirm: The weighted mean unusual move in the short direction is above 0 with t >= 2, over at least 30 distinct days. In addition, the supporting check holds over at least 20 such held-recovered bars.
- Reject: The weighted mean unusual move in the short direction is below 0 with t <= -2, over at least 30 distinct days.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a confirm-strength main result whose supporting check fails or has fewer than 20 bars. So is any result with fewer than 30 distinct case days.
