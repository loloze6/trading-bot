Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, taken in the short direction. A case is a bet, a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per 10-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: Split the cases at the median of the forecast's absolute size. The mean unusual move of the larger half minus that of the smaller half must be above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move in the short direction is positive with t ≥ 2, and the larger-half minus smaller-half difference is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move in the short direction has t ≤ -2, meaning the coins rise more than their drift after the signal.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t ≥ 2 with the supporting check failing is also inconclusive. So is a test with fewer than 30 cases on at least 30 distinct days.
