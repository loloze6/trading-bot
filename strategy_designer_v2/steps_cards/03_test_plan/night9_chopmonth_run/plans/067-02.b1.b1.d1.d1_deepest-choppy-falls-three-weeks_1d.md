Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy market, with the close below its close of 30 days earlier as a plain percent -> adapted: the choppy market is a regime (60-day return between -10 % and +10 %, the profile convention), and the depth of the 30-day fall is graded inside it.
- Comparison: deeper falls against shallow ones, with the decline growing faster than the depth and only the deepest falls traded -> adapted: "only the deepest" becomes a cubic graded dead zone (`signed_power` exponent 3), so shallow falls give almost no position.
- Outcome: the price falls over the next 21 days -> kept: short, held 21 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a short sized by the convex depth of the 30-day fall, only in choppy markets and held 21 days, earns exactly when the idea holds and loses when it fails.
Forecast: The regime detector reads the coin's 60-day percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 60, sf 1). A bar from -10 to +10 is choppy and goes to `mean_reversion`. Every other bar is flat. Inside the choppy regime, the 30-day percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30) is standardised over 60 bars (min 20). It is then clipped at a maximum of 0, so a 30-day rise gives a zero position. It is then cubed by `signed_power` (exponent 3, pivot 10). The forecast is negative (short) only when the close is below its close of 30 days earlier. A shallow fall gives a position near 0: a standardised fall of 5 gives -1.25. A deep fall grows fast: 10 gives -10, 15 gives -33.75, clipped at -20. The forecast is never positive.

- Main measure: The cases are bars on the non-overlapping 21-bar grid of each window where the forecast is below 0. This is judged before costs.
- Supporting check: The same short-direction excess return is larger in the deepest fifth than in the other negative-forecast cases (deepest minus rest, Welch t of 1 or more), which shows the convexity.
- Confirm: The deepest fifth's mean short-direction excess return is above 0 with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The deepest fifth's mean short-direction excess return has a t of -2 or less: the deepest falls rise more than the coin's drift.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2, or a confirming main measure with a failed check. The test needs at least 30 distinct days among the deepest-fifth cases; with fewer, the result is inconclusive.
