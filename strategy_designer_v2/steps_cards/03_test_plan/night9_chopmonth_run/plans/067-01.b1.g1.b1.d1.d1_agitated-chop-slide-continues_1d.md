Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's plain percentage fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy agitated market -> kept: the 30-day percent return, falls only; the code adds the choppy agitated profile.
- Comparison: the larger the fall, the more it keeps falling -> kept: the short position grows with the depth of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the price keeps falling over the next 10 days -> kept: each short bet is held 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own fall, sized by depth, inside its own profile, judged on the next 10 days.
Forecast: The forecast is the coin's 30-bar percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30). It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0, so only falls count. It is 0 when the coin rose over the 30 days. Below 0 it is a short that grows in size with the depth of the fall, about -10 for a fall of usual size, capped at -20. The code keeps the book flat outside the choppy agitated profile.

Profile: choppy agitated (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case, before costs. A case is a short bet (the forecast falls by at least 2 points), read once per 10-bar grid cell. The move is the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift, in the short direction. Its t is computed with the cases of one day taken together.
- Supporting check: The cases are split at the median of |forecast| at the signal bar. The deeper-fall half must have a larger mean unusual move than the shallower half (deeper minus shallower > 0).
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive (the price falls more than its drift) with t >= 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative (the price rises more than its drift) with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2. A t >= 2 whose supporting check fails is also inconclusive. So is any result from fewer than 30 cases on fewer than 20 distinct days.
