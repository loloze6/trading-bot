Fidelity:
- Situation: a choppy market, and a coin's fall over the past 30 days -> adapted: choppy is a regime (60-day return from -10 % to +10 %, the profile convention), and inside it the 30-day fall is graded, zero after a 30-day rise.
- Comparison: a larger 30-day fall against a smaller one -> kept: the short grows with the size of the fall.
- Outcome: the coin keeps falling over the next 20 days -> kept: the short is held for 20 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the test bets short, in choppy bars only, in proportion to the month's fall, and judges the next 20 days' move against the coin's drift.
Forecast: The detector reads the coin's 60-day return in percent. Bars where it lies between -10 and +10 are choppy and go to the active regime; all other bars are flat. Inside that regime the forecast comes from the 30-day return, standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20) and clipped at a maximum of 0. It is zero after a 30-day rise. After a fall it is negative (short), and its size grows with the fall's size against the coin's usual 30-day move, up to -20. It never goes long.

Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move per case. A case is a short bet: the forecast falls by at least 2 points. Cases are read once per 20-bar grid cell. The unusual move is the coin's 20-bar return minus its normal 20-bar drift, signed in the short direction. The t is computed with cases of the same day pooled.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean unusual move of the large cases (forecast at or below -10) minus that of the smaller cases (forecast above -10) is above 0.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive with a t of 2 or more, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative with a t of -2 or less.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. A t of 2 or more with the supporting check failing is also inconclusive, as is any result from fewer than 30 independent case-days.
