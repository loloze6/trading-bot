Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy market -> adapted: the 30-day fall is graded (deeper fall, larger short); the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: larger falls against smaller falls -> kept: the position size grows with the depth of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the price keeps falling over the next 10 days -> kept: a short held for 10 daily bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast is the idea's own 30-day fall, short and graded, judged on the next 10 days' move inside the choppy profile.
Forecast: The 30-day percent return of the close (PriceEvolutionComponent, period 30), standardised against its own past 60 values (average absolute value about 10). It is clipped at a maximum of 0, so only falls count. The forecast is negative (short) when the coin is below its close of 30 days ago, and its size grows with the depth of that fall up to the cap of -20. It is zero after a 30-day rise or a flat month. It is never positive. The choppy profile, added by code, keeps the book flat outside choppy markets.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 10 bars
- Main measure: The cases are the short bets: a drop of the forecast of at least 2 points, read once per non-overlapping 10-bar grid cell. For each case, take the coin's 10-bar return from the signal bar's close, minus the coin's normal 10-bar drift over all windows, in the short direction. The measure is the mean of this unusual move, with its t computed with cases clustered by day.
- Supporting check: Across the cases, the correlation of the forecast's absolute size with the unusual move in the short direction is positive.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move in the short direction is positive with t >= 2, and the size correlation is above 0.
- Reject: The mean unusual move in the short direction is negative with t <= -2.
- Inconclusive: The idea is neither confirmed nor rejected when t lies between -2 and 2, or when t >= 2 but the size correlation is 0 or below. The test also needs cases on at least 30 distinct days; with fewer it is inconclusive.
