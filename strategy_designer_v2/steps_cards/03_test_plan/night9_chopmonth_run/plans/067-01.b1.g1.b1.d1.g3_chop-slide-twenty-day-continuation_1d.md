Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's percentage fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy market -> kept: the 30-bar return, shorts only when it is negative; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: larger falls against smaller ones ("the larger... the more") -> adapted: graded, so the short grows with the depth of the fall.
- Outcome: the price keeps falling over the next 20 days -> kept: the short is held 20 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: a graded short on the 30-day fall, held 20 days inside the choppy profile, is the idea as stated.
Forecast: One indicator, `PriceEvolutionComponent` with a period of 30 bars, which is the 30-day percent return. It is standardised over 60 past values (min_periods 20) and then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is zero when the coin rose or was flat over the 30 days. It is negative (short) when the coin fell, and the short grows in proportion to the depth of the fall, up to the -20 cap. It is never positive. The code keeps the book flat outside the choppy profile.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 20 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases, before costs. A case is a short bet: the forecast falls at least 2 points on the short side. It is read once per 20-bar grid cell of each window. Each case's unusual move is the coin's 20-bar return minus the coin's normal 20-bar drift (holding the coin), counted in the short's direction. The t of its mean is computed with cases taken together per day.
- Supporting check: Gains grow with depth. Split the cases into thirds by |forecast| at the bet. The mean unusual move of the deepest third must be above that of the shallowest third.
- Confirm: The mean unusual move is positive (the coin falls more than its drift) with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The mean unusual move is negative (the coin rises more than its drift after the slide) with t ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive, and so is a confirming t whose depth check fails. The result is also inconclusive with fewer than 30 cases or fewer than 30 distinct days.
