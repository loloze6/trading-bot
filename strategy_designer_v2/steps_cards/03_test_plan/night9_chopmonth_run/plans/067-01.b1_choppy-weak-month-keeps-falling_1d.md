Fidelity:
- Situation: a coin's fall over the past 30 days, in a choppy market -> kept: the 30-bar return, negative side only; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: larger falls against smaller ones -> kept: the short position grows with the size of the 30-day fall.
- Outcome: the coin falls more over the next 7 days -> kept: a short held 7 bars, judged against the coin's normal drift.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the forecast shorts in proportion to the 30-day fall, inside the choppy profile, over 7 days, exactly as the idea states.
Forecast: One indicator, the 30-bar percent return (`PriceEvolutionComponent`, period 30). It is standardised over its past 60 values (min 20), then clipped at a maximum of 0. The forecast is zero when the coin rose over the past 30 days. It is negative (short) when the coin fell, and its size grows linearly with the depth of the fall, up to -20. It never goes long. Code adds the choppy profile, so the book is flat outside it.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: The unusual move of the cases. A case is a short bet (a fall of the forecast of at least 2 points), read once per 7-bar grid cell. Its unusual move is the coin's 7-bar return from the signal bar minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift over all windows, taken in the short direction. The measure is its mean with its day-clustered t.
- Supporting check: On the non-overlapping 7-bar grid bars with forecast < 0, the slope of the coin's 7-bar excess return (minus normal drift) on the forecast is positive, with t ≥ 2 (deeper falls are followed by lower returns).
- Confirm: The main measure's mean unusual move is above 0 with t ≥ 2, and the supporting check passes.
- Reject: The main measure's mean unusual move is below 0 with t ≤ -2 (the coin rebounds after choppy-market falls), or the slope's t is ≤ -2.
- Inconclusive: Any other result, including t between -2 and 2, or a main measure that confirms while the slope check fails. Fewer than 100 cases, or cases on fewer than 30 distinct days, is also inconclusive.
