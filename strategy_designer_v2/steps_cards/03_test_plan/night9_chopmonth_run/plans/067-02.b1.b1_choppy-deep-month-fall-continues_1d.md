Fidelity:
- Situation: in a choppy market, the close sits below its close of 30 days earlier, measured as a plain percent -> kept: the 30-bar percent return, kept only where it is negative; the choppy profile is added by code.
- Comparison: deeper monthly falls against shallower ones -> adapted: the short is graded by the depth of the fall, so a deeper fall gives a larger short.
- Outcome: the coin falls over the next 7 days, more after deeper falls -> kept: 7-bar forward return, short side, held for 7 bars.
- Verdict transfer: yes: the graded short is exactly the idea's depth-to-decline relation inside the choppy profile.
Forecast: The forecast uses one indicator, PriceEvolutionComponent with period 30 (the 30-day percent change of the close). It is standardised over its past 60 values (min_periods 20) and then clipped at a maximum of 0. When the coin's close is above its close of 30 days earlier, the forecast is 0 (flat). When the close is below it, the forecast is negative (short). The short grows in proportion to the plain percent depth of the fall. The scale factor is the mean absolute 30-day move over the last 60 bars, which changes slowly. The cap is -20. The code adds the choppy profile, so the book holds nothing outside a choppy market.

Profile: choppy (the idea): no position outside it, by step 3's code (common/market_profile.md)
Criterion:
- Horizon: 7 bars
- Main measure: Use the non-overlapping 7-bar grid of each window and keep the grid bars where the forecast is below 0. On those bars, regress the coin's 7-bar forward return, minus the coin's normal 7-bar drift, on the forecast. The measure is the slope of that regression and its t, with cases on the same day pooled into one.
- Supporting check: On the same cases, the mean short gain is above 0. The short gain is minus the drift-adjusted 7-bar return.
- Confirm: The slope is positive with t ≥ 2, meaning deeper falls are followed by larger excess declines. The supporting check must also pass. At least 100 cases on at least 60 distinct days are required.
- Reject: The slope is negative with t ≤ -2, meaning deeper falls are followed by larger excess rises.
- Inconclusive: Any slope with t between -2 and 2 is inconclusive. So is a positive slope with t ≥ 2 when the mean short gain is not above 0. Fewer than 100 cases, or cases on fewer than 60 days, is also inconclusive.
