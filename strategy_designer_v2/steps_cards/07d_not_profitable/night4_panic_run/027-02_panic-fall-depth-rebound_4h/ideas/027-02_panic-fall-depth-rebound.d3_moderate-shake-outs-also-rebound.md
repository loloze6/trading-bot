Idea: Among 24-hour stretches where a coin's range is well above its 30-day average, the deeper the coin's fall over that stretch, the higher its return over the next 14 days.
Consequence: A trader can apply the liquidity premium to moderate shake-outs as well as extreme panics, holding a position more often for the same edge per trade.
Why:
- F5: The size of the panic does not add to the rebound. By tercile of the range ratio, the mildest stretches (3.0–3.16 times the usual range) returned +15.2 % while held, against +6.2 % and +6.6 % for the stronger ones. The deepest tercile of the 24-hour fall returned +4.4 %, against +17.2 % and +15.1 %, in line with the flat slope of the verdict (t 0.49).
- F6: The cost of trading is not the cause. The total move per case, +5.71 %, is 29.6 times the 0.19 % trade cost. The book's mean daily return is low because the book holds a position on only 16 % of coin-days (0.26 % a day in position), with 0.021 trades a day.
