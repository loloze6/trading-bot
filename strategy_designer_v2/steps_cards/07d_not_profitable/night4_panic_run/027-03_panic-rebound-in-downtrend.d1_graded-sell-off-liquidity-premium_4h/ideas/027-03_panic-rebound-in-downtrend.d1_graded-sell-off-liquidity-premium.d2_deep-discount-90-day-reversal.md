Idea: The further a coin's price stands below its 90-day average, the more it earns above its usual drift over the next 14 days.
Consequence: A trader would hold a coin in proportion to its discount to its 90-day average, and not hold it near that average, where 14-day returns fell short of drift.
Why:
- F3: The book's losses sit where price is near its 90-day mean, and its worst days come with heavier positions in coins above that mean.
- F4: Coins far below their 90-day mean earn an excess over 14 days; coins near it do worse than drift.
