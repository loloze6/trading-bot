Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is positive and that has just had a one-day rise of several usual daily moves, the larger that rise in usual daily moves, the larger its further rise over the next day.
Consequence: A trader should not fade sharp rises in rising coins and may lean with them for a day.
Why:
- F4: The bounce fade belongs to falling markets. In coins with a positive 90-day return, rises above 1 usual move are followed by excess next-day falls of -0.09 % to -0.35 %, so those coins keep rising, against +0.5 % to +3.9 % in falling coins.
