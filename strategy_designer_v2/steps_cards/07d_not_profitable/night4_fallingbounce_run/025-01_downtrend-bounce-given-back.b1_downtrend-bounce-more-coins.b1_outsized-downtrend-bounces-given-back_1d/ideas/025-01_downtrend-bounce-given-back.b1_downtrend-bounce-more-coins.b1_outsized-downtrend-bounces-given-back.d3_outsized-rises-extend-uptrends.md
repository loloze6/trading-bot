Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is positive, a one-day rise large relative to its usual daily move (its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns) is followed by a further rise over the next 5 days, growing with the size of the rise in usual daily moves.
Consequence: A trader would buy outsized up days in rising coins rather than fade them, scaled by the rise's size in usual moves.
Why:
- F7: In coins with a non-negative 90-day return, large rises are followed by further rises. Rises of 1.5–2 usual moves give -2.4 % unusual in the short direction (t -2.33, n 161), and rises of 2–3 usual moves give -9.9 % (t -2.36, n 100).
