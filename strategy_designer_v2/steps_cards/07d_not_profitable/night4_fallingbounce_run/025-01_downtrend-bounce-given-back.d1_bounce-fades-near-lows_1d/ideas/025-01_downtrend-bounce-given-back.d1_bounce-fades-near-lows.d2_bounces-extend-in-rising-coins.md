Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is positive, a one-day rise that is large relative to its usual daily move (its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns) is followed by a further rise over the next 5 days, the larger the rise counted in usual daily moves the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A trader would buy outsized up days in coins that are rising over the past three months, sizing up with the size of the day's move, rather than fading them.
