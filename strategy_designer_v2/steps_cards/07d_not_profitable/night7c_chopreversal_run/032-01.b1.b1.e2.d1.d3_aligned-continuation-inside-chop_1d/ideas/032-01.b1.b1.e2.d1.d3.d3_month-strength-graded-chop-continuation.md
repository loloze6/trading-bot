Idea: When a coin's past 30 days have gone back and forth with little net progress compared with the sum of its daily moves, its 7-day move counted in 30-day daily swings tends to be followed by a move the same way over the next 7 days, only while its 30-day return points the same way and the past 30 days are still going back and forth at each day's close. The larger the 30-day return counted in 30-day daily swings, the larger the following move.
Consequence: A trader should weight the choppy-market weekly continuation by how large the aligned month's move is against its swings, putting less on cases where the month barely leans the same way.
Why:
- F6: The signal is graded by the 30-day return counted in 30-day daily swings. Aligned-chop cases in its lowest, middle and highest terciles gain +2.4 % (t 1.95), +3.4 % (t 2.90) and +5.6 % (t 3.56), 114–115 cases each. The condition alone does not earn: bars outside aligned chop with a top-quartile scaled 30-day move give −1.0 % (t −0.87, 203 cases).
- F8: The book's gain grows with the forecast's size. Held bars in the lowest quartile of |forecast| earn +0.048 % a bar, against +0.16 %, +0.37 % and +0.26 % in the next three quartiles (about 925 bars each). The weakest quarter of positions adds exposure to falls with almost no gain.
