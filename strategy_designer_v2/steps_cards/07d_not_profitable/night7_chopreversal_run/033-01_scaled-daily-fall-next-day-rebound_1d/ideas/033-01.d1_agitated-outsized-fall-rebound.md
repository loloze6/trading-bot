Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A trader buys outsized one-day falls only while the coin is swinging more than usual for it, and stays out of the calm-market falls that carry the book's drawdowns without a reliable rebound.
Why:
- F1: The effect lives in agitated markets only. Here "agitated" means the coin's 30-day swing is above its median over folds B and C, which stands in for the past-year median. On signal days in agitated markets, the next-day move beyond drift is +0.57 % (t 3.75, 1795 cases; t 2.06 with each day's cases taken together, 655 days). On signal days in calm markets it is +0.07 % (t 0.71, 2475 cases; t 0.39 by day, 785 days).
- F2: In agitated markets the rebound grows with the fall, as the idea says. Falls beyond 2 usual moves are followed by +4.29 % (t 4.78, 68 cases), falls of 1 to 2 by +1.21 % (t 3.29, 350), and falls of 0 to 1 by +0.11 % to +0.30 % (t 0.44 and 1.35). In calm markets there is no grading: +0.25 %, +0.24 %, +0.20 % and −0.07 %, all with |t| < 1.
- F3: Agitation is not an effect on its own. On agitated bars without the signal, the next day is −0.42 % against drift (t −3.17, 1831 cases), against +0.57 % on agitated bars with the signal. So the condition only selects where the forced-selling rebound is large, which fits the theory: a fall that is outsized in a swinging, leveraged market is the one that triggers liquidations.
- F4: The failing Calmar comes from the calm half of the book. In an approximate rebuild (cost of 0.095 % per side on each position change), the full book has a yearly return of +27.3 %, a deepest fall of −27.6 % and a Calmar of 0.99. Keeping positions only in agitated markets gives +20.2 %, a deepest fall of −10.9 % and a Calmar of 1.86 (Sharpe 1.39 against 1.00), while holding on 20 % of coin-days instead of 48 %.
- F5: The recorded book's deepest fall is −27.7 %. It sits in w9, where the book lost 16.8 % while the coins fell 31.0 %. In calm markets the signal lost in 6 of 12 windows, for example w7 at −0.68 % (t −2.37) and w12 at −0.72 % (t −2.25). In agitated markets it was negative in only 3 of 12 windows, and none of those had |t| > 1.5.
