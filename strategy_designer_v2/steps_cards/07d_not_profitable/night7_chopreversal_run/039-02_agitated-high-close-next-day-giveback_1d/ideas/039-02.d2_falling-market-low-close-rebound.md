Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the lower a coin closes within its day's high-low range, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: After a fall of the past two months with larger swings than usual, a close pinned at the day's low is a graded reason to buy for a day, since the forced selling reverts.
Why:
- F2: The effect is graded and lopsided toward low closes. In agitated markets, the next day's move above the coin's drift falls from +0.84 % (t 2.6) and +0.87 % (t 2.7) in the two lowest close-location deciles to between -0.31 % and -0.75 % in the upper five deciles. Long trades on low closes earned +0.110 % per bar, against +0.035 % for shorts on high closes.
- F6: The low-close rebound lives in a falling agitated market. Agitated low closes with a 60-day return below -10 % gain +3.00 % next day (t 5.1, n 139). The falling trend alone, without a low close, gives -0.47 % (t -2.3, n 599), so the trend is not the effect. In a rising market low closes lose 0.66 % (n 98).
