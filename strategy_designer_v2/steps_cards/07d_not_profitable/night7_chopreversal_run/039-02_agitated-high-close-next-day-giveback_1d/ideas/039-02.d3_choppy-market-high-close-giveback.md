Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the higher a coin closes within its day's high-low range, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: A trader shorts agitated high closes only while the coin has gone nowhere over two months, and so avoids the rising markets where high closes keep climbing.
Why:
- F7: The short side loses in rising markets. High closes in agitated markets are followed by -0.91 % (t -3.5, n 474) when the 60-day trend is not rising, and by +0.40 % (n 212) when it is rising. In a choppy agitated market high closes give -0.90 % (n 483) and low closes +0.63 % (n 302).
- F8: The book's falls come from the short-heavy, low-edge later windows.
