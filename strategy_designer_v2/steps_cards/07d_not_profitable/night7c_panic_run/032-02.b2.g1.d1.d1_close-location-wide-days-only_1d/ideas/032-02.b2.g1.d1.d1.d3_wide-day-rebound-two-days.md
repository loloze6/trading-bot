Idea: The lower a coin's daily close sits within that day's high-low range, on a day whose range is wider than the coin's average daily range over the previous 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 2 days, the rise growing with how much wider the day was.
Consequence: A trader would hold the rebound after a wide low-closing day for two days instead of one, so that each trade captures a larger move against its fixed cost.
Why:
- F5: The long effect is graded and lasts two days.
- F1: The idea's effect sits almost entirely on its long side. On wide days a low close was followed by +0.88 % excess next-day return (t 5.12, 1245 cases), against +0.20 % (t 1.29, 1405 cases) after a high close. The book's long days earned 49.5 bp a day and its short days −0.49 bp, so the short half adds turnover, fees and variance but no gain.
