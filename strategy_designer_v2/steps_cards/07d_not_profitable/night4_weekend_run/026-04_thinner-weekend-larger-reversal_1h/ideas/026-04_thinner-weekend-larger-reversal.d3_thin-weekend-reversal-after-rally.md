Idea: The lower a coin's weekend volume is compared with its volume over the preceding five weekdays, the larger the share of its Saturday-Sunday move that is reversed over Monday and Tuesday, the more so the more the coin has risen over the preceding 30 days.
Consequence: A trader would bet hardest against thin weekend moves in a coin that has rallied for a month.
