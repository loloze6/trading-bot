Idea: The higher a coin's 14-day rise, measured in its usual daily moves at Thursday's close (UTC), the more it rises over the following Friday and Saturday together, the effect showing after 14-day rises only.
Consequence: A trader would buy at Thursday's close after a two-week rise, sized up with the rise, and would refuse to short Thursday's two-week falls, which removes the losses behind the book's deepest falls.
Why:
- F1: The book's failure on Calmar (0.46) comes entirely from its short side: alone, the long side has a Calmar of 1.82 (Sharpe 1.42, yearly +22.4 %, deepest fall −12.3 %), while the short side alone has a Sharpe of −0.50, a yearly return of −6.5 % and a deepest fall of −47.0 %.
- F2: The book's deepest fall (−26.3 % over 376 days, across 4 windows, ending in 2025) is mostly short losses: the short positions summed −0.205 of daily return against −0.053 for the long ones.
- F3: Short positions lost in most years: their summed daily returns were −0.187 in 2019, −0.114 in 2023, −0.121 in 2024 and −0.197 in 2025; the long side was positive in every year except 2022 (−0.008) and 2025 (−0.056).
- F4: In the Thursday cases, the effect exists only after a 14-day rise. Long cases have a mean drift-removed 2-day return of +0.99 % (t 3.23, n 1230). Short cases have −0.04 % in their direction (t −0.13, n 1235), so the short bets earn nothing to pay their 0.19 % cost.
- F5: On the long side the move grows with the forecast. The bottom third of long forecasts gains +0.20 % (t 0.57), the middle third +0.49 % (t 1.31) and the top third +2.12 % (t 3.77, n 444). On the short side no third earns: the bottom third +0.25 %, the middle −0.28 % and the top −0.09 %, with every t under 0.65.
- F6: Keeping only the rising side is not an effect of its own. On non-Thursday bars of the 2-day grid, a positive scaled 14-day return is followed by +0.16 % (t 0.67, 658 days), against +1.21 % (t 2.63, 107 days) on Thursday closes. In the top quintile of the scaled 14-day return, Thursday closes gain +2.13 % (t 2.25, 60 days) and other days +0.13 % (t 0.35).
- F7: The long-only version of the book holds up when windows are left out. Its lowest leave-one-out Calmar is 1.46 and its lowest Sharpe 1.27. It is in a position on 15.1 % of coin-days, with a mean daily return of 0.39 % on those days, about twice the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
