Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days while its volume stays near its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price recovers part of that fall over the next two to six hours, only while the coin's 24-hour return before that hour and its 7-day return are both rises; the larger the prior 24-hour rise and the larger the 7-day rise, the larger the recovery.
Consequence: A trader buys quiet-volume dips only inside a rising day within a rising week, and sizes by the strength of both rises, so that the trades left carry a move several times their cost.
Why:
- F1: The move per case is too small to pay for the book's turnover. A case's 6-hour total move is +0.09 % (unusual +0.06 %, t 2.27, 5857 cases), against 0.11 % per buy and sell. Over the record the book's gross gain was 1.47 on BTC and 2.89 on ETH, against fees of 2.58 and 2.11, from 6872 and 6659 rebalances and a summed turnover of 5054 and 3841. The book loses on fees, mostly on BTC.
- F2: The recovery exists only when the coin's 7-day return is a rise, and it grows with the 24-hour rise. With a 7-day rise, the unusual 6-hour move climbs from +0.05 % (t 1.0, n 968) through +0.12 % (t 2.39, n 1152) to +0.20 % (t 3.21, n 1243) across the low, middle and high thirds of the 24-hour rise. With a 7-day fall, cases lose −0.10 % (t −1.73, n 866) and −0.23 % (t −2.78, n 682) in the low and middle thirds, and the high third is near zero.
- F3: Grading cases by the 24-hour rise times the positive 7-day rise orders the move monotonically. Across its quintiles the unusual 6-hour move runs −0.13 % (t −2.41), −0.09 %, +0.05 %, +0.10 % and +0.25 % (t 3.76, n 1101). The top quintile's total move is +0.28 %, 2.6 times the 0.11 % cost; the full set's total is 0.9 times.
- F6: The effect is short and uneven over time. The unusual move is +0.04 % at 3 hours (t 2.40) and +0.06 % at 6 hours (t 2.27), then −0.01 % at 12 hours and −0.05 % at 24 hours. The unusual 6-hour move is about −0.10 % in both 2022 and 2025, both years of falling markets, and +0.07 % to +0.14 % in the other years. The book's mean daily return is −0.01 % over its 24 windows.
