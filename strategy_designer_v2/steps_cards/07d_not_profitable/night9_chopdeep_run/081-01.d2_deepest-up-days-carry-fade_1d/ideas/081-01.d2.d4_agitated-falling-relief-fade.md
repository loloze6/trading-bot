Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a day that closes up while sitting deeper below the coin's 20-day high, measured in the coin's own usual daily swing over the last 20 days, is followed by a larger fall over the next day, the fall growing with the depth.
Consequence: A trader shorts up days hardest when the coin has been falling and swinging more than usual, where the fade per case is several times the cost of a trade.
