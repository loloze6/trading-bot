Idea: The more evenly a coin's 24-hour fall is spread across many small hourly steps rather than a few large hours, the more the coin rises over the next 2 days.
Consequence: A trader would favour buying coins after grinding daily falls over coins after jump-made falls of the same size, and hold for days.
