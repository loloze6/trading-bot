Idea: The deeper a coin's 3-day fall, the more the coin falls over the next 3 days in excess of its usual return.
Consequence: A trader would take a short sized by the depth of the 3-day fall, or refuse a long after a deep 3-day fall.
Why:
- F7: Large 3-day falls are followed by further falls: on non-trending grid bars, the deepest-fall tercile has a next-72-bar excess return of -0.0083 (t -3.51, n 892), against +0.0049 (t 1.51) for the mildest tercile.
