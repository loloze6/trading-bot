Idea: The larger XMR's one-day fall measured in its 30-day daily volatility, the larger its rise over the next day, with the rebound growing faster than the fall, so that falls of more than a usual day carry most of it.
Consequence: A trader would buy XMR mainly after falls well beyond a usual day and leave small down days alone, since those earn less than their trading cost.
Why:
- F2: Over all bars the short side carries little and the long side carries the gain: mean gain per bar is +0.070 % on 1523 short bars against +0.311 % on 1361 long bars.
