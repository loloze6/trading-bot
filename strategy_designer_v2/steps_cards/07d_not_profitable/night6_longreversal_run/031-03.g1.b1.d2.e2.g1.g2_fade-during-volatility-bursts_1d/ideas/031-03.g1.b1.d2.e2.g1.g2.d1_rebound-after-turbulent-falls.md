Idea: The larger XMR's one-day fall against its 30-day daily volatility, the more it rises back over the next day, and this rebound grows with how high its volatility over the past 5 days stands against its 30-day volatility.
Consequence: A trader would buy XMR after sharp falls in turbulent stretches, sized by the fall and the turbulence, and would not short its sharp rises, which is the side that lost through the book's long falls.
Why:
