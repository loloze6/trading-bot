Idea: The more a coin's mean daily volume over the last 14 days has risen above its mean daily volume over the last 30 days, the more its price rises over the next 14 days, while its own 14-day return is up. The more that volume has fallen below its 30-day mean, the more its price falls over the next 14 days, while its 14-day return is down.
Consequence: A trader would lean with the volume signal only when the coin's two-week price move already points the same way, standing aside when they disagree, holding about two weeks.
Why:
- F1: The book's falls are what fail it: unchanged and before fees, the book has Sharpe 0.66, a deepest fall of −52.6 % and Calmar 0.30, against holding's Sharpe 0.62, so it barely beats holding.
