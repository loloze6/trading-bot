Idea: The larger a coin's 30-day return, the larger its rise over the next 3 days, the more so the closer the coin's close stands to its highest close of the past 60 days and the larger its 3-day return.
Consequence: A trader leans long hardest when monthly strength near the 2-month high is freshly confirmed by the last few days, and lightly when the coin is drifting down from that high.
Why:
- F3: The book's gain sits in longs whose close is near its 60-day closing high. Longs in the nearest quintile summed 9.13 of the 13.31 total long gain (mean +0.37 % per bar). Longs in the other four quintiles earned between +0.007 % and +0.039 % per bar.
- F5: Inside near-high longs, the gain grows with the coin's 3-day return: from +0.047 % per bar in the lowest quartile (t 0.52) to +0.203 %, +0.438 % (t 2.78) and +0.618 % (t 2.88) in the highest, about 703 bars each.
- F7: Among longs, near-high bars pay at every level of monthly return above the lowest: +0.389 % (t 3.37) in the mid 30-day-return tercile and +0.301 % (t 2.67) in the high one. Far-from-high longs earn +0.013 % to +0.019 % even with a high monthly return.
- F1: The book fails on its falls, not its gain: its yearly return is 22.0 % against a deepest fall of 46.4 % (Calmar 0.47). That fall built up over 887 days across windows w1 to w8, while the coins fell 56.6 %; the other falls are 27.0 % (w12–w14, coins −58.8 %) and 18.5 % (w21–w22).
