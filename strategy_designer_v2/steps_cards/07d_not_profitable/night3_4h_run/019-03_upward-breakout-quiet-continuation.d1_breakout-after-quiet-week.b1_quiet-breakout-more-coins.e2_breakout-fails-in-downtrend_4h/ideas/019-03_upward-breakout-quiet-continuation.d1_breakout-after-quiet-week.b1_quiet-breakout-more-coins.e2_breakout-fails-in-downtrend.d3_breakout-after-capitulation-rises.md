Idea: When a coin's four-hour bar closes above the high of its preceding two days after a 90-day fall, the coin rises over the next 12 days, and the deeper that 90-day fall, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader should take upward breakouts after a deep three-month collapse as the start of a rebound, not as a failed rally.
Why:
- F4: Breakouts after a very deep 90-day fall reverse up: with a 90-day return below -43 %, the 12-day total in the short direction is -14.1 % (t -6.94, 133 entries), against +2.3 % to +4.2 % in the other quartiles (Spearman of 90-day return with the move +0.26). Most of these cases sit in w13 (48 cases, -32.6 %) and w12/w14/w18.
