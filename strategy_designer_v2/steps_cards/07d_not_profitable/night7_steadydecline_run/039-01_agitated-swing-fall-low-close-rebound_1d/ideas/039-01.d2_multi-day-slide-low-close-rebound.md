Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a one-day fall against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, the nearer that day's close sits to its low, and the larger the coin's fall over the last 3 days, the larger the rise over the next day; the rise grows with the three together.
Consequence: A trader sizes the rebound trade up when the low-closing large fall ends a multi-day slide, where the forced selling has been heaviest, and down when it comes after a flat or rising stretch.
