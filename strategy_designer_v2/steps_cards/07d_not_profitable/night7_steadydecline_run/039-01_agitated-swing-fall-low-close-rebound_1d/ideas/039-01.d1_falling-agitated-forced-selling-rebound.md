Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a one-day fall against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, and the nearer that day's close sits to its low within the day's range, the larger the rise over the next day; the rise grows with the two together.
Consequence: A trader buys forced-selling days only while the coin's 60-day trend is down and its swings are high, and refuses the same days in rising or choppy markets, where they gave back the book's deepest fall.
