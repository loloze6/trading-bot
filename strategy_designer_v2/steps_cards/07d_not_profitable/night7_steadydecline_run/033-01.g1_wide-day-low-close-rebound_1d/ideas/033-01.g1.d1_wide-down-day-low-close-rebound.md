Idea: The wider a coin's day is against its usual daily range, and the nearer its close sits to that day's low on a day that ends down, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A trader would buy only the close of wide down days that end near their low, and stay out on narrow days and on near-high closes, where the move does not cover a trade's cost.
Why:
- F1: The book loses its gain by trading every day. It was in a position on 99 % of the coins' days with an average size of only 0.11, and the gain before costs per coin-day was +0.070 % on the long side and +0.010 % on the short side against an estimated fee drag of about 0.015 %. That leaves a mean daily return on days in a position of 0.009 % and a Calmar of 0.15, with a deepest fall of -10.1 % over 89 days in w17.
- F2: The effect lives in the strong cases on the long side. Bars in the top fifth of |forecast| (forecast at or above 4.1) on the long side returned +0.96 % the next day (t 2.79 with cases of one day together, 891 cases on 344 days), which is +0.77 % after the 0.19 % trade cost. The top twentieth returned +1.40 % (t 3.02, 208 cases on 117 days).
- F3: The short side, the mirror case of a near-high close, does not carry the effect. In the top fifth, short bets returned +0.30 % (t 1.02, 847 cases), below the 0.19 % cost once slippage is counted. Over two days they lost (-0.45 % signed excess) while the long side kept +0.72 %. In a rising 60-day trend the short side lost -0.29 % (t -1.46, 836 cases).
- F4: The forecast's grading is lopsided. The top decile (mean forecast 6.1) was followed by +0.85 % excess (t 4.22) and the ninth decile by +0.68 % (t 3.43). The bottom decile was only -0.39 % (t -1.91), and the middle short deciles were also negative (-0.52 %, t -2.92), so being short on mild forecasts bets on noise.
- F5: The rebound needs a wide day and is concentrated on down days. Closes in the lower half of the range were followed by +0.89 % (t 2.56, 879 cases) in the widest quartile of day width against the 20-day mean range, and by +0.71 % (t 2.48) in the third quartile. They were followed by -0.19 % and -0.22 % in the two narrowest quartiles. In the widest quartile, a down day closing in its lower half was followed by +1.07 % (t 2.86, 769 cases), against -0.39 % after an up day.
