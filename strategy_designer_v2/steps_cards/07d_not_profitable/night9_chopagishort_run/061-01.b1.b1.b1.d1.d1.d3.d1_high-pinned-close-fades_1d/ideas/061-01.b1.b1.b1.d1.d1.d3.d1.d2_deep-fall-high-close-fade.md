Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, when a coin's daily close sits in the upper part of that day's high-low range after its 5-day return has been negative, its price falls over the next five days, and the deeper that 5-day fall and the nearer the close to the day's high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A trader sizes the short up when a close pinned near the high follows a deep 5-day fall, and keeps it small after a shallow one, where the book's losses and its worst trade sat.
Why:
- F3: The size grows with the depth of the 5-day fall. On all signal bars, the unusual 5-day move in the short direction is 7.97 % (t 2.55), 7.31 % (t 3.33), 3.80 % (t 2.73) and 1.55 % (t 0.92), from the deepest 5-day fall quartile to the shallowest (30 bars each). A deep fall alone is not the effect: in-profile and out-of-profile grid cells without the signal and a 5-day fall below -5 % earn only +0.22 % (t 0.36, 477 cells).
- F4: The losses sit in the shallow cases. The 88 trades entered after a 5-day fall deeper than 5 % earn +2.41 % net (42 % winners, fixed 5-day hold +3.39 %). The 55 trades entered after a shallower fall lose -2.13 % (24 % winners), hold the worst trade (-62.8 %, ZEC in w11) and are negative in 6 of their 10 windows.
- F6: The book's gain rests on few windows, and its falls are in w11 and w12. w16 alone returns +15.4 % (13.0 bp a day), while w11 returns -2.65 % (trade mean -5.51 %) and w12 returns -1.22 % (trade mean -4.84 %). The deepest fall, -6.06 %, is in w12, against a yearly return of +3.2 %.
- F8: The grading by nearness to the high shows in the trades. Trades in the lowest, middle and highest third of entry forecast size earn -0.39 %, +0.65 % and +1.75 % net (49, 46 and 48 trades).
- F5: The profile carries the effect. The same situation (a negative 5-day return and a close in the upper half of the range) outside the choppy agitated profile has an unusual move of -0.26 % (t -0.37, 317 grid cells), against +12.1 % (t 3.35, 22 cells) inside it.
