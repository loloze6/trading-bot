Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, when a coin's daily close sits in the upper part of that day's high-low range after its 5-day return has been negative, its price falls over the next ten days, and the nearer that close is to the day's high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A trader holds the short on these high closes for ten days rather than five.
Why:
- F3: Neither added condition is an effect on its own; the close's place in the range is. In the profile:
