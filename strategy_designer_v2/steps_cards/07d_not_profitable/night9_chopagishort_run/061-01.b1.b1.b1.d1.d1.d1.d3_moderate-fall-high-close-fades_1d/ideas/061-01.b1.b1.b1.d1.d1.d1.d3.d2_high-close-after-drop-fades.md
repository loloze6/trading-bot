Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, when a coin's daily close sits in the upper part of that day's high-low range, its price falls over the next five days, and the deeper its 5-day fall before that close and the nearer the close is to the high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A trader shorts high closes in a choppy, agitated market by the depth of the coin's 5-day fall, which gives more cases than the tested 20-day band.
Why:
- F1: The tested situation is too narrow to trade: on the 5-day grid of folds B and C it gives only 5 cases on 5 days, so the book made 32 trades at 0.004 trades a day and was in a position on 1 % of coin-days.
