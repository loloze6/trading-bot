Your line: the book is not profitable enough. Its profitability check failed.

Objective: find why the book does not pay, angle by angle, and what idea would make it pay.

Look at, angle by angle:
- the forecast: the book's gain before fees; whether a larger forecast earns more;
- the trades: how many, how long they are held, their gain before fees against their fees;
- the position and the regime: where the losses come from (regimes, periods, the long or the short side, the market
  rising or falling, calm or volatile).

Propose: ideas about the market that would remove the cause you found, stated as what the forecast should capture
(for example: a signal that trades too often may hold its information in its slow part; losses in one market state
may mean the effect holds only in another; one losing side may mean the effect is one-sided). Each from a finding.
