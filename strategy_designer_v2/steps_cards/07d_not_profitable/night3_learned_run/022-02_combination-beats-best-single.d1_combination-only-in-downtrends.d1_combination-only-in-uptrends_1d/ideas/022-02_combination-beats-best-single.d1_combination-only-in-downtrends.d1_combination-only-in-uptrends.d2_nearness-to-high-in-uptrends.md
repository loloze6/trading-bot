Idea: While a coin's 90-day return is positive, the closer its close is to its 90-day closing high, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Inside a 90-day rise, a coin near its high is a reason to be long, and one far below it a reason to stay out.
Why:
- F3: The relearned weights give two components the opposite sign to what they predict in a rise.
- F6: In a 90-day rise, coins farthest from their 90-day high were followed by −1.22 % (t −1.74). Coins in the upper three fifths of closeness were followed by +1.02 % to +1.21 %.
