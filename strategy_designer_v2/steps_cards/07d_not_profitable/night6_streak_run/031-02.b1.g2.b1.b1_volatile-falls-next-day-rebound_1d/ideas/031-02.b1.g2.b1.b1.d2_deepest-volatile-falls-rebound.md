Idea: The larger a coin's percentage fall on its latest day, the more it rises over the next 1 day, larger the higher its 60-day volatility, with the rebound concentrated in the deepest falls, so that the next-day rise grows faster than in proportion to the day's fall.
Consequence: A trader should buy only the deepest falls of a volatile period, where the rebound covers the cost of the trade, rather than every down day.

- F1: The book failed only on Calmar (0.43 against 0.75), and its deepest fall was one stretch in w16 (2020): 19 days, the book down 40.1 % while holding the coins fell 55.6 %. In that window the book's position averaged 0.26, and its coins' 60-day return averaged −17 %.
