Idea: While a coin stands far below its 30-day high, the larger its 24-hour rise in multiples of its 30-day average absolute daily return, the larger its fall over the next 1 day.
Consequence: A sharp bounce in a coin deep below its recent high is a reason to short it, or to refuse to buy it, for the following day.
Why:
- F5: The rebound is concentrated in coins standing 30 % or more below their 30-day high.
- F6: In coins 30 % or more below their 30-day high, a 24-hour rise of more than 1 times the 30-day average daily move is followed by a fall the next day: strength -0.26, t -3.5, 279 cases, unusual move -1.3 %.
