Idea: A coin whose 7-day volatility is large against its 30-day volatility while its 7-day return is among its largest rises over the next 7 days by more than its usual 7-day drift, and the larger that volatility ratio, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A week that breaks out on expanding volatility is a reason to be long for the following week; a strong week on fading volatility is a reason to refuse the trade.
Why:
- F6: The idea is real only in its strong part, but the pooled measure drowns it.
- F8: Among active cells, a week whose volatility is high against its 30-day volatility is followed by a larger unusual move: +3.11 % (t 2.42, 171 cases) in the top quarter of the 7-day to 30-day volatility ratio (1.12 to 1.94). The other quarters range from −0.62 % to +0.65 %.
