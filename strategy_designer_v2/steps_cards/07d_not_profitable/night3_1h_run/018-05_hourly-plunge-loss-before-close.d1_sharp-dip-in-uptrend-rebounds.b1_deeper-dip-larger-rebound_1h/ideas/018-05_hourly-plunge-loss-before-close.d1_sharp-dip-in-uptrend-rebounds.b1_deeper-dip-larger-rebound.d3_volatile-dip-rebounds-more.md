Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return falls by several times its 30-day average absolute daily return while its 30-day return is positive, the coin rises over the next 3 days more than its normal drift, and the higher its 7-day volatility relative to its 60-day volatility, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Size dip-buys in a rising month up when the past week has been unusually volatile, and down when the market has been quiet, where the dips do not rebound.
Why:
- F5: Dips in a quiet market pay less than dips in a volatile one.
- F6: Size is not the problem. The total move is +0.96 % per case, 5.0 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade, and the unusual move is 2.9 times that cost. The failure comes from deep falls in collapsing markets, not from costs.
