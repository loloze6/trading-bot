Idea: When a coin falls sharply over one day on volume well below its own 30-day average daily volume, it keeps falling over the next 5 days. The larger the fall against its 20-day volatility, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A trader would treat a large fall on thin volume as a reason to be short or to stay out over the next days, not as a dip to buy.
Why:
- F6: Sharp falls on thin volume keep falling rather than reverting. The largest third of thin-volume down days shows an excess 5-day return of -0.0159 (t -2.43, n 174), against +0.0053 (t 1.03, n 318) for the largest falls on heavy volume.
