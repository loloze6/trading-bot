Idea: In an agitated market, after a down day, a coin rises over the next day, more so the larger that day's fall in units of its 30-day daily-return standard deviation.
Consequence: Trade the scaled-fall rebound when the coin swings more than usual for it, and stand aside from it when the coin is calm.
Why:
- F4: The rebound lives where the coin is swinging more than usual. I measured agitation as the coin's 30-day swing against its 90-day swing.
