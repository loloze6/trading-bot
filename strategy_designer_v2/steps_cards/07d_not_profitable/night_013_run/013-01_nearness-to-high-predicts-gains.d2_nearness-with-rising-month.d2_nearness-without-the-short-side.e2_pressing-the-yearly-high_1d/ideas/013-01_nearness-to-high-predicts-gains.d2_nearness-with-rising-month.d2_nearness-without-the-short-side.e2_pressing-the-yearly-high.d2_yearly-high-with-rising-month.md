Idea: The nearer a coin's daily close sits to its highest close of the past 52 weeks, the higher its return over the next 7 days, with the effect concentrated in the closes closest to that high, and the larger the higher the coin's own 28-day return.
Consequence: A trader should hold a coin pressing its yearly high larger when it got there with a strong month, and smaller when its month was weak.
