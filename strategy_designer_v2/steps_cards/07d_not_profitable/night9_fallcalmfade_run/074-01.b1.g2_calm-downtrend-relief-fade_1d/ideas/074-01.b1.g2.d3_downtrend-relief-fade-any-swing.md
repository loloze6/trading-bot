Idea: In a falling market, the first up day after a run of down days is followed by a fall in the coin's close over the next day, and the deeper the run's fall against the coin's usual daily swing over the last 30 days, the larger that next-day fall.
Consequence: A trader shorts the first relief day after a slide in any downtrend, agitated as well as calm, which gives the book more cases of the same effect.
Why:
- F6: The effect is not limited to calm markets, as the brief said. In falling markets, run-ending up days that the falling-calm book did not trade (outside the calm profile) gave a next-day short-signed move of +1.34 % (t 2.79, 89 cases). The traded ones gave +1.10 % (t 3.01, 164 cases). All falling-market bars that were not run-ending up days gave -0.07 % (t -0.52).
- F2: The grading the idea predicts is steep, and it lives in its top quarter. By depth quarter, the next-day short-signed move is -0.33 %, +0.44 %, -0.15 % and +2.95 % (t 5.96, 65 cases). By the 5-day return before the signal, the most fallen quarter gives +3.15 % (t 4.63) and the other three quarters give between -0.48 % and +0.16 %.
