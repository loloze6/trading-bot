Idea: In a falling, calm market, the first up day after a run of down days is followed by a fall in the coin's close over the next day, and that fall grows ever more steeply as the run's fall deepens against the coin's usual daily swing over the last 30 days, so that almost all of it comes from the slides that are deepest against that swing.
Consequence: A trader shorts relief days in proportion to how deep the slide was against the coin's usual swing, putting almost all the risk on the deepest slides, which carry the effect, and little on shallow ones, which only add losses.
Why:
- F1: The book's gain comes almost entirely from the deepest runs, and the shallower runs add risk but no gain. Among the signal cases on folds B and C, runs whose fall exceeds 2.85 of the coin's 30-day daily swing gave a short-signed next-day move of +3.17 % (t 5.68, 56 cases). The other 161 cases gave -0.03 % (t -0.09). Weighted by the position held, the deep cases sum to +1.82 and the shallow ones to +0.05. Yet the shallow cases hold the worst single day (-15.3 % against -4.3 % for the deep ones). So the sizing, which caps typical and deep runs alike at -20, spreads the book's risk over cases that earn nothing. That fails Sharpe minus holding's (-0.10) and Calmar (0.23).
- F2: The grading the idea predicts is steep, and it lives in its top quarter. By depth quarter, the next-day short-signed move is -0.33 %, +0.44 %, -0.15 % and +2.95 % (t 5.96, 65 cases). By the 5-day return before the signal, the most fallen quarter gives +3.15 % (t 4.63) and the other three quarters give between -0.48 % and +0.16 %.
- F3: The depth on its own is not the effect; the up day that ends the run is. In falling markets, a deep run (depth over 2.85) still falling on the bar gives a next-day short-signed move of -1.66 % (t -2.67, 109 bars), a bounce. The same depth ended by an up day gives +3.74 % (t 7.26, 62 bars). So the deeper case belongs to the idea's own theory: more trapped holders sell at the first exit. It is not a separate effect.
- F5: The book's deepest fall is a 216-day stretch from w7 to w9 that reaches -14.3 %. Over it the shallow cases lost: in w9, 38 shallow cases gave -0.85 % next day, while 7 deep cases gave +5.29 % (t 4.20). In w12, w13 and w17, shallow cases lost -0.94 %, -2.56 % and -0.84 % a day. In those three windows the deep cases were positive, or a single case.
