Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day move against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further it keeps moving in that day's direction over the next 5 days.
Consequence: When the coin has been swinging less than usual, a trader follows a large day in its direction for five days, sized by the day's size in swing units.
Why:
- F1: The tested situation shows no continuation at the plan's 5-day horizon. On the plan's grid in folds B and C, agitated cases give a 5-day slope of +0.0001 (day-clustered t 0.01, 591 cases), against -0.0006 (t -1.49) on all folds.
- F6: The continuation the brief describes shows where the brief places it, in calm markets at 5 days.
