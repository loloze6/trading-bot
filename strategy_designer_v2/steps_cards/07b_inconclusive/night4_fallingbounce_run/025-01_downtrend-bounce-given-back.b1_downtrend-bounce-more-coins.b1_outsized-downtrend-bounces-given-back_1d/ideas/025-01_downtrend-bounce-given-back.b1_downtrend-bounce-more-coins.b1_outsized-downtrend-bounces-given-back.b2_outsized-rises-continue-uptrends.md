Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is positive or zero, a one-day rise that is large relative to its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns is followed by a further rise over the next 5 days. The larger the rise in usual daily moves, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A trader would follow, not fade, outsized up days in a rising coin, sized by how many usual daily moves the day covers.
Why:
- F5: The same large rises in coins with a non-negative 90-day return were followed by further rises, the opposite of falling coins, which the brief names as its test. Rises above 2 usual moves were followed by an unusual rise of 8.69 % (t −2.63 in the short direction, n 131). In the event study, rises of 2 to 3 usual moves were followed by a 3.83 % rise (t −3.31, n 431).
