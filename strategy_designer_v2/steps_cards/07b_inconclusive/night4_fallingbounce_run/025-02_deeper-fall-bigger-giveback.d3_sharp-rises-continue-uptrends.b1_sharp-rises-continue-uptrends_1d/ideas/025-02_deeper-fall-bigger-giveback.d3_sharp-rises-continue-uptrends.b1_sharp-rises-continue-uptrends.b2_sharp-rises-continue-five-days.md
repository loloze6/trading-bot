Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is positive, the larger the day's rise measured against the standard deviation of its last 20 daily returns, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader taking the continuation after a sharp rise in a rising coin can expect most of it within about five days.
Why:
- F4: Measured directly on prices in coins with a positive 90-day return, the 10-day unusual move grows with the day's rise in 20-day volatility units, taking the first rise per 10-day cell. Rises of 0–1 moves gained +0.39 % (t 0.66, 792), 1–2 moves +0.98 % (t 1.13, 500), 2–3 moves +3.97 % (t 2.69, 184) and above 3 moves +4.87 % (t 1.35, 59).
- F5: Rises above 2 moves in rising coins are followed by rises at every horizon from 3 days on, and the effect is clearest at 5 days.
