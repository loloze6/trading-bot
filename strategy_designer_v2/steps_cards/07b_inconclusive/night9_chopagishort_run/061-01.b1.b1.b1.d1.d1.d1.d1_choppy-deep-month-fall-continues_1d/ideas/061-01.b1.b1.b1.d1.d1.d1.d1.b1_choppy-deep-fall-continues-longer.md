Idea: In a choppy market, a coin whose 20-day return is a deep fall falls further over the next ten days, and the deeper that 20-day fall is, the larger the further fall, the deepest falls of more than a fifth of its price included.
Consequence: A trader shorts coins that have dropped hard over 20 days while their two-month price has gone nowhere, with the largest shorts on the deepest falls rather than on the calmest coins, and holds them about ten days.
Why:
- F1: The test's result is on the predicted side. The short's unusual move over 5 days is +1.24 % per case, with a t of 2.25 over 432 days and 676 cases. It failed only on the supporting check (larger half minus smaller half −0.92 %), and that check measured position size, not the depth of the fall. On the folds B and C grid, position size follows the coin's 30-day swing (Spearman rho −0.71, p 4e-8), barely follows the 20-day fall's depth (rho 0.16, p 0.29, with the wrong sign) and does not predict the move (rho 0.007, p 0.96).
- F2: Measured on the fall's depth itself, which is what the idea grades by, the effect is graded as predicted. In choppy markets on the 5-day grid (folds B and C, 45 cases), the deeper half of 20-day falls (mean −24.4 %) gave an unusual move of +11.6 % (t 3.37, strength 0.91). The shallower half (mean −13.5 %) gave +4.7 % (t 2.76, strength 0.25).
- F3: The grading does not stop at a fifth of the price, so the idea's cap removes its strongest cases. By 20-day return bins in choppy markets:
- F4: The move keeps building past 5 days. Over 10 days the deep half's unusual move is +14.8 % against +11.6 % over 5 days, and the shallow half's is +7.6 % against +4.7 %. Over 2 days the shallow half shows almost nothing (+0.3 %).
- F5: The choppy profile carries the effect. The same deep 20-day falls in falling markets gave only +0.64 % over 5 days (t 0.89, 297 cases). Inside choppy markets the effect is present in every 30-day swing tercile: +7.2 %, +11.2 % and +6.5 %, with t of 2.26, 3.02 and 1.78.
