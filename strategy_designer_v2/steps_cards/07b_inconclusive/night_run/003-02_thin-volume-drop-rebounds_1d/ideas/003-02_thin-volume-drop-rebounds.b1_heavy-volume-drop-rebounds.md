Idea: When a coin falls sharply over one day on volume well above its own 30-day average daily volume, it wins back part of that fall over the next 3 days; the larger the fall and the heavier its volume against that average, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A sharp drop on heavy volume marks capitulation selling that is absorbed within days, so it is a reason to buy the drop rather than sell it.
Why:
- F3: The rebound the brief gives to thin-volume falls appears instead after heavy-volume falls, over about 3 days.
- F4: This heavy-volume rebound is strongest in the heaviest-volume falls.
- F5: The heavy-volume 3-day rebound is uneven across years and coins, though positive in most.
