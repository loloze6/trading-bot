Idea: The nearer a coin's close sits to its 90-day high, the higher its return over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A trader should prefer holding a coin over the coming 10 days the closer its close sits to its 90-day high.
Why:
- F2: Nearness to the 90-day high carries the effect; the 20-day return adds little. On its own, nearness has a slope t of 2.31 over 5 days (3,419 cases) and 2.58 over 10 days (1,691 cases), while the 20-day return has t 1.40 and 1.07. The two are correlated at 0.62, so adding the weaker term dilutes the stronger one.
- F4: The long side carries the slope (t 1.88 on 1,649 positive-forecast cases); the short side shows none (t -0.53 on 1,770 cases). The effect fades by 20 days (t 0.40 on 827 cases), so the move shows over 5 to 10 days.
