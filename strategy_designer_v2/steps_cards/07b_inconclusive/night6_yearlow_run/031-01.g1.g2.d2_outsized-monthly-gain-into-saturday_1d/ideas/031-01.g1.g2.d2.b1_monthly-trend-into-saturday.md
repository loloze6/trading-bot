Idea: The higher a coin's return over the past 20 days at Friday's close (UTC), measured against the coin's usual 20-day moves, from its largest 20-day losses up to its largest 20-day gains, the more it rises over the next day, Saturday.
Consequence: A trader would lean long into Saturday in proportion to how strong the coin's 20-day move has been, smallest after its deepest 20-day falls and largest after its biggest gains, without loading the extreme gains more than in proportion.
Why:
- F1: The tested idea is on its predicted side but not graded the way it claims. The main measure is +0.0775 with a day-clustered t of 2.58, over 1155 cases on 314 Fridays. Yet the Spearman correlation between forecast and excess Saturday return among the positive cases is −0.003, and with the raw scaled gain it is −0.003.
- F2: The supporting check failed because the convex top fifth does not stand out coin by coin. Its top-minus-rest excess return is −0.10 % overall. The top fifth beats the rest in BTC (+0.68 % vs −0.01 %), LTC (+1.24 % vs +0.85 %) and XMR (+0.67 % vs +0.36 %). It falls short in ETH (+0.37 % vs +0.53 %), XRP (−0.08 % vs +0.98 %) and ZEC (+0.03 % vs +1.06 %).
- F3: The pooled top forecast quintile's +1.41 % mean (t 3.14, 232 cases, plain t) rests on a few large Saturdays. Its median is only +0.06 %, its 2 %-winsorised mean is +1.04 %, and its standard deviation is 6.8 %, against about 3.1 % in the other quintiles, with a largest case of +39.8 %.
- F4: What carries the effect is whether the coin gained over 20 days, not how far past zero it gained. Fridays after a 20-day gain are followed by +0.64 % excess on Saturday (t 4.64, 964 cases; day-clustered t 2.90 on 257 days). Fridays after a 20-day loss are followed by −0.08 % (t −0.70, 1035 cases), and the forecast's positive quintiles 0 to 3 give +0.39 %, +0.44 %, +0.08 % and +0.59 %.
- F5: Across all Fridays, the signed 20-day return is graded roughly in a straight line, not convexly. Its per-coin quintiles, from the largest losses (−23.8 % mean) to the largest gains (+36.2 % mean), are followed by excess Saturday returns of −0.50 % (t −1.99), +0.22 %, +0.16 %, +0.54 % (t 3.54) and +0.91 % (t 3.28), with about 400 cases each.
- F6: The effect spreads over coins and years and is not only a weekday drift. After a 20-day gain, the Saturday excess return is positive in all six coins (t 0.88 for BTC to 3.24 for LTC) and in 6 of 8 years (2018 −0.33 %, 2023 −0.16 %). Saturday after any Friday averages +0.30 % (t 3.71, 2397 cases), half of what follows a 20-day gain.
