Idea: When a coin closes below its lowest low of the prior 30 days while its daily swings are wider than over the past months, the further the close sits below that low, the more the coin rises over the next 1 day.
Consequence: A trader would refuse to press a short on a deep new monthly low during an agitated fall and would favour a next-day rebound buy on the deepest breaks.
Why:
- F5: On the brief's own situation, a close below the prior 30-day low, a deeper break was followed by a larger next-day rise (slope +0.54 per unit of depth, t 9.16, 180 cases), and the rebound was concentrated where 20-day volatility ran above 1.2 times its 120-day level (+4.81 % next day, t 2.54, 28 cases, against +0.01 %, t 0.02, 152 cases elsewhere).
