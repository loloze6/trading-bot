Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the larger a coin's percentage fall over the past 30 days, the more it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: When a flat two-month market is swinging more than usual, a trader should short a coin in proportion to the depth of its plain monthly fall, for about a week, rather than buy it as a range bottom.
Why:
- F2: The test's forecast measured something other than the idea's plain fall. The forecast is the 30-day return standardised over its past 60 values and clipped at 0, so it is negative whenever the month is weaker than the coin's recent months, even when the coin did not fall. It correlates only 0.64 (Spearman) with the plain 30-day return. On the 5-day grid it picks 63 cases on 40 days against 39 cases with a plain negative 30-day return.
- F3: Measured on the plain 30-day fall itself, the effect shows clearly over 5 days and is graded. On 39 cases over 25 days, the signed excess 5-day return is +0.098 (clustered t 2.63). Deep minus shallow is +0.060. By tercile of the fall it is +0.130 for the deepest, +0.108 for the middle and +0.057 for the shallowest. Every year is positive: 2018 +0.161 (3 cases), 2019 +0.058 (12), 2020 +0.228 (9), 2021 +0.041 (15).
- F4: The plain fall is at its sharpest over 3 to 5 days and fades by 10 to 20 days. Using raw forward returns after a plain 30-day fall on the 10-day grid (33 cases), the mean is -0.051 at 3 days (t -2.19), -0.065 at 5 days (t -2.21), -0.037 at 10 days (t -1.17) and +0.003 at 20 days (t 0.07). The plan's 10-day horizon therefore sits past most of the move.
- F1: The test's result is on the predicted side but nowhere near the bar, and the grading runs the wrong way. The main measure is +0.0039 with t 0.21 on 70 days and 136 cases. The size is +1.36 % per case (t 1.51, 227 days, strength 0.055). The largest-|forecast| half minus the smallest half is -0.021. In folds B and C at 10 days, the big-forecast half minus the small half is -0.046 and the deep-fall half minus the shallow-fall half is -0.070 (33 cases, 20 days).
