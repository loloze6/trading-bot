Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 5 days, only while its 60-day return is positive, the larger that 5-day rise and the wider that day's high-low range compared with its average daily range over the past 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A wide day ending a strong 5-day rise inside a 60-day uptrend is a reason to hold the long for about 10 days rather than 3, where the move is larger than a trade's cost.
Why:
- F2: The grading the idea claims is present, mostly in the strongest cases.
- F3: The test measured the idea at a shorter horizon than where it shows. Over all cases the mean unusual move grows with the horizon:
- F6: Most of the grading comes from the size of the 5-day rise.
