Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 5 days, only while its 60-day return is positive, the larger that 5-day rise and the wider that day's high-low range compared with its average daily range over the past 20 days, the more the coin rises over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A wide day ending a strong 5-day rise inside a 60-day uptrend is a reason to join the run for 3 days, while the same day in a falling 60-day market is no reason to.
Why:
- F1: The tested result is on the side the idea predicts but far short of the bar: the mean unusual 3-day move is +0.20 % (t 0.68, 1,484 cases on 825 days), a strength of 0.020 of a usual 7.96 % move, which is 1.1 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
- F2: The grading the idea claims is present, mostly in the strongest cases.
- F5: Among the six coins, ZEC carries most of the 3-day effect.
