Idea: In a coin whose 30-day net move has been large relative to its 30-day high-low range and whose close sits below the middle of that range, the larger its fall over the past 10 days, the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trending coin that has slid deep below the middle of its month's range is a reversion long to take, or a short to refuse, rather than a breakdown to chase.
Why:
- F7: The clear graded effect is in trending coins below the middle of their 30-day range, and it is reversion.
- F5: The horizon does not rescue the tested direction.
