Idea: In a calm market, the more of its 20-day daily swings a coin's 5-day rise spans, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A large rise in a quiet coin is a reason to hold a long position for about two weeks rather than one.
Why:
- F1: The tested slope is on the predicted side but short of the bar (slope t 1.12 over 1779 grid cases on 387 dates, all folds), and its mean excess is slightly negative (-0.16 %), so its size is tiny: unusual move +0.17 % per case (t 0.47), 0.012 of a usual move, 0.8 times the 0.22 % cost of a trade. The test already used every coin and fold, so no more data is available.
- F5: The rise-only signal is uneven over time and horizons. Its calm-market mean excess is negative in 2018 (-2.4 %) and 2019 (-0.6 %) and positive in 2020 (+3.6 %, t 2.13) and 2021 (+2.5 %). Its unclustered slope t grows from 3.41 at 5 days to 4.62 at 10 days for calm rises.
