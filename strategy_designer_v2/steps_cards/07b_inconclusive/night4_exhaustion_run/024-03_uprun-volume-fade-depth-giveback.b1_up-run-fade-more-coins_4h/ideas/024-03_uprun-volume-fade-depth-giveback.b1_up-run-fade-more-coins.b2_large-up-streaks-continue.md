Idea: After an unbroken streak of rising four-hour periods, the coin keeps rising over the next three days. The larger the streak's gain relative to the coin's typical four-hour move over the past 30 days, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A trader would refuse to short the largest up-streaks and read their size as a reason for continuation, not exhaustion.
Why:
- F2: Run size is graded opposite to the idea. In the case regression the run-gain coefficient on the unflipped drift-adjusted return is +0.0026 (t 5.1). The smallest run-gain quintile gives back +0.75 % (t 4.6). The largest gains another 0.65 % instead (short-direction mean -0.65 %, t -2.73, about 2,430 cases each). So the largest runs continue rather than fade.
