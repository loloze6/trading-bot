Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days, the larger the share of that period's volume traded in its 3 busiest hours, the more of the fall the coin recovers over the next 1 day.
Consequence: Down moves made with a burst of volume are forced selling that overshoots, so a trader can lean long against them for a day.
Why:
- F5: The mirror effect after 3-day falls is clearer. Falls with the most concentrated volume did better than evenly traded falls by +0.0028 vs -0.0027 over the next 24 hours (t 2.20, 2,732 samples), and by +0.0010 vs -0.0085 over 144 hours (t 1.65). This fits the brief's claim that jump-made moves partly reverse.
