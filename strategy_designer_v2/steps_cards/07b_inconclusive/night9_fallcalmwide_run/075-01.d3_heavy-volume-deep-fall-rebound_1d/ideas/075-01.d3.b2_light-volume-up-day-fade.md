Idea: The lighter a coin's volume on a day that closed higher, relative to its own average daily volume over the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: A trader sells, or holds less, after up days on thin volume, more so the thinner the volume.
Why:
- F7: The brief's light-volume side shows on up days in folds B and C, graded by volume. Up days in the lightest volume tercile are followed by −0.59 % next-day excess return (t −2.86, 660 days), the middle tercile by −0.35 % (t −1.74), and the heaviest by −0.13 % (t −0.49).
