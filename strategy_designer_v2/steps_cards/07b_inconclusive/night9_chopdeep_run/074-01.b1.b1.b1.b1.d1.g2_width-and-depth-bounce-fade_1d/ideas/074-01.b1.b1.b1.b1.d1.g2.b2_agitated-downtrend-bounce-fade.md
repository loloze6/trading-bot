Idea: In a falling, agitated market, an up day is followed by a fall over the next day, larger the wider its high-to-low range against the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days and the more the coin is still down over its 5-day return including that day.
Consequence: A trader fades the up days of a downtrend for one day only while the coin is swinging more than usual for it, sized by the day's width and the depth of its 5-day loss.
Why:
- F6: Where the agitation can be read (about 730 cases), the fade lives in agitated markets. Agitated: +1.00 %, t 1.97, 361 cases on 110 days. Calm: −0.52 %, t −1.07, 369 cases.
