Idea: In a falling market, an up day is followed by a fall over the next day, larger the wider its high-to-low range against the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days and the more the coin is still down over its 5-day return including that day, only while the coin is still down over its 5-day return including that day.
Consequence: In a downtrend, a trader shorts for one day only those up days that leave the coin below its level of 5 days ago, sized up by how deep that loss is and how wide the day was, and leaves bounces that have already recovered the week alone.

- F4: Width helps less clearly than depth.
- F5: The effect is spent in one day, as the brief says. The unusual move is +0.46 % (t 2.05) over 1 day, +0.30 % (t 0.95) over 2 days and +0.24 % (t 0.68) over 3 days. It is also specific to up days: on falling-market down days the same short loses −0.32 % (t −1.38).
- F8: Over all cases the size is too small to trade. The total move per case is +0.17 %, below the 0.22 % cost of a trade. The deepest-loss fifth (+1.98 %) and the strongest-forecast fifth (+1.61 %) are each several times that cost.
