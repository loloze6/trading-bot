Idea: In a rising market, when a coin's day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above that low, it falls over the next 10 days. The fall is larger the higher the coin's 60-day return.
Consequence: In a rising market, a trader should not buy a reclaimed monthly-low pierce as a bounce, and the stronger the prior rally, the more such a day warns of a further fall.
Why:
- F5: Outside the falling, agitated profile, the same reclaimed pierce is followed by a fall, not a rebound. Over the 135 cases the 5-day excess return is −4.1 % (t −2.07, 82 days) and the 10-day one −6.1 % (t −3.22, hit 32 %). The fall grows with the coin's 60-day trend over 10 days: falling-calm cases −6.8 % (t −2.52), choppy −11.5 % (t −2.67), rising −12.7 % (t −3.62, 14 cases, hit 7 %).
