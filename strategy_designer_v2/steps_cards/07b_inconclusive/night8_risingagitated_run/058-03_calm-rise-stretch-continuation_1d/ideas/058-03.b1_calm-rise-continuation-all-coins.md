Idea: In a rising, calm market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average price, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In a calm rising market the distance above the average is built by steady, information-led buying, which can keep going, so the run continues.
Why:
- F1: Step 5's main measure is on the side the idea predicts but short of the bar: a slope t of +1.49 (day-clustered) on 428 grid cases over 188 days, with an unusual move of +1.01 % per case over 5 days (strength 0.093, t 1.61 on 338 days). The test used only the 6 default coins, so it lacks the cases needed to separate an effect of this size from 0.
- F2: In folds B and C, the cases with forecast > 0 rise beyond drift over 5 days on average (mean excess +1.90 %, t 2.49, 244 cases). The grading with the forecast is weaker (slope t +1.07). Most of the gain sits in the top forecast quartile (+4.14 % excess, mean forecast 19.8), against +1.07 % to +1.28 % in the three lower quartiles.
- F3: The effect is mixed across coins and years, and that spread drowns the slope. Across coins the 5-day slope t runs from -2.54 (LTC) to +1.92 (ETH), positive in 4 of 6. Across years it is +2.61 in 2020, -0.97 in 2019 and -1.13 in 2021.
