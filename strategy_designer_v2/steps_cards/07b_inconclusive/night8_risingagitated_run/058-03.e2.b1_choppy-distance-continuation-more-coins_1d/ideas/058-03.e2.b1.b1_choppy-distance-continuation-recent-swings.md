Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close stands below its 20-day average price, the more it falls over the next 10 days, and the further it stands above, the more it rises. This holds only while the coin's daily swings over the last 5 days are larger than over its last 30 days, and more so the larger that recent swing.
Consequence: In a trendless two-month market, a trader follows the coin's distance from its 20-day average only when its last days have swung harder than usual, and refuses the trade when the coin has been quiet.
