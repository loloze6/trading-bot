Idea: The more a coin has fallen over the 24 hours up to the 20:00 UTC close, the more it rises over the next 2 days, only while that fall was made of many small hourly steps rather than a few large hours.
Consequence: An evening long after a deep fall is worth holding for two days when the fall was a grind, and not when one or two violent hours made it.
Why:
- F3: The gain sits in the deepest falls only.
- F6: The rebound follows falls made as a grind, the opposite of the brief's jump story.
