Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the larger a coin's rise over the past 7 days in multiples of its usual 7-day swing over the past 30 days, and the closer its close sits to its highest close of the past 30 days, the larger its further rise over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A trader taking the range-break continuation should expect it to pay out within about a week of the coin reaching its month's top, not over two weeks.
Why:
- F2: In folds B and C, inside the choppy-calm profile, the whole 14-day gain sits with coins whose close is within 2.5 % of their 30-day highest close. Those cases earn +14.6 % excess (median +11.4 %, 61 % positive, t 1.20, 18 cases on 9 days). Cases further from the high earn -1.1 % (t -0.30, 48 cases). Rises that end below the high earn +0.3 % (20 cases), so the scaled rise alone carries nothing.
