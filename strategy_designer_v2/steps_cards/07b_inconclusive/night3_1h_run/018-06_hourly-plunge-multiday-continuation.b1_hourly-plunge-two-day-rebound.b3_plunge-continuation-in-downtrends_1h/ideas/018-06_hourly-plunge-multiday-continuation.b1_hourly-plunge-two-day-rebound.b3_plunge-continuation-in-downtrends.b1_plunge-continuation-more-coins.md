Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return, read at an hourly close, falls by a large multiple of its 30-day average absolute daily return while its 30-day return is negative, the price falls further over the next 4 days by more than the coin's usual 4-day drift, and the deeper the 30-day fall, the larger the further decline.
Consequence: A sharp hourly plunge in a coin that has fallen over the past month is a reason not to buy the dip, and a reason to short it for four days.
Why:
- F1: The effect lies wholly in 2018. In 2018 the mean unusual 96-hour short move is +2.13 % (t 3.24, 376 cases), and five of six coins show it there (BTC t 2.38, ETH 2.47, XMR 2.04, LTC 1.33, ZEC 1.50; XRP -0.09). Without 2018 it is -0.05 % (t -0.24, 1,898 cases), and from 2019 to 2025 every year lies between -0.54 % and +0.93 % with |t| below 1.9.
- F4: The effect is not spread across coins. Only 52 % of coin-windows have a positive mean. BTC (+0.67 %, t 1.84) and ETH (+0.74 %, t 1.57) lead, while LTC, XMR and XRP are at zero (|t| below 0.1).
