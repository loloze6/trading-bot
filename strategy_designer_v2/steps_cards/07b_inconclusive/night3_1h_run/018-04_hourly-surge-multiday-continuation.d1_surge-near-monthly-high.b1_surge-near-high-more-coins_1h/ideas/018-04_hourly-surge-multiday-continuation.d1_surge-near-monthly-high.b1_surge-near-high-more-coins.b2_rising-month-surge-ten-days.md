Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return, read at an hourly close, first reaches several times its 30-day average absolute daily return on the upside while its 30-day return is positive, the price keeps rising over the next 10 days by more than the coin's usual 10-day return: the larger the surge, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader catching such a surge would hold it for about ten days rather than five, since the move keeps growing after five days.
Why:
- F2: The effect splits cleanly on the sign of the coin's own 30-day return at the signal hour (3,669 cases have one).
- F4: Inside the positive 30-day return cases, the move is still graded by the forecast. The upper half of the forecast averages +1.87 % (t 3.01) against +0.95 % (t 2.30) for the lower half, 846 cases each.
- F6: The move outlasts the plan's 5-day horizon, in line with earlier runs' F9.
