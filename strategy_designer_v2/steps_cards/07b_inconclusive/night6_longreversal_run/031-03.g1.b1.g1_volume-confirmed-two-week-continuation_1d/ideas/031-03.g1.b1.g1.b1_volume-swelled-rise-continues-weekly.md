Idea: The more a coin's mean daily volume over the last 14 days has risen above its 30-day mean while its 14-day return points up, the further its price keeps rising over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A trader would buy a coin whose two-week rise comes with swelling volume, more so the bigger the swell, and hold about a week, rather than fading moves or following falls.
Why:
- F1: The effect sits on one side only: rising volume behind a 14-day rise. In the top fifth of the 14-day/30-day volume ratio (mean ratio 1.39), cases whose 14-day return was up continued beyond the coin's drift by +1.42 % over 3 days (t 2.47, 947 cases, 337 days), +1.78 % over 5 days (t 1.99, 560 cases), +3.55 % over 7 days (t 2.32, 375 cases, 131 days) and +7.71 % over 14 days (t 1.93, 168 cases, 56 days). The same swell behind a 14-day fall gave +0.00 % over 3 days (t 0.01), −0.05 % over 7 days (t −0.03) and −3.63 % over 14 days (t −1.25, 88 cases).
- F2: The fade half of the test adds nothing and dilutes the result. The mean_reversion regime's cases average +0.34 % (t 0.41, 1088 cases) against +1.52 % (t 1.25, 960 cases) in the trending regime. Within it, the long side (fading a fall) is −0.76 % (t −0.55, 619 cases). In the lowest volume fifth (mean ratio 0.67), the 14-day return's sign leans neither way over 14 days (+0.06 %, t 0.05).
- F3: The plan's 14-day horizon is longer than where the effect shows. The test's main measure has a t between 1.00 and 1.35 at every horizon from 7 to 42 days. But the continuation of the 14-day return on rising volume (ratio at or above 1) has a t of 2.04 over 7 days (+1.10 %, 1876 cases), against 1.14 over 14 days. For a rise on rising volume it is +2.04 % over 7 days (t 2.09, 833 cases, 197 days), while a fall on rising volume gives +0.10 % (t 0.10).
- F4: The effect grows with the volume swell. The 7-day continuation by volume-ratio fifth is +0.57 %, +0.88 %, +0.56 %, +0.29 % and +2.15 % (t 2.10 in the top fifth). Within volume above its 30-day mean, by quarter it is +0.82 %, +0.41 %, +1.77 % and +1.74 %.
- F5: The rising-volume continuation over 7 days is positive in every year from 2018 to 2025 (+0.19 % to +2.74 %), with no single year above t 1.19. It is a small, steady effect that needs all years pooled, not one period's luck.
- F7: Size against cost: the 7-day move in the strongest cases (+3.55 %, F1) is about 16 times the 0.22 % a trade cost, and the tested idea's whole-sample unusual move (+0.71 %) is 3.2 times it. A narrower idea on the up side keeps a margin well above cost.
