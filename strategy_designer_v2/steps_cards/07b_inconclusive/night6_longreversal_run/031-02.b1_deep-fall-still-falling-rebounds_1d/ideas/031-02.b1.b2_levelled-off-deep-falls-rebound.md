Idea: Among coins whose 90-day return stands far below their own usual 90-day move, the smaller the coin's fall over its last 30 days, the more its price rebounds over the next 30 days.
Consequence: A trader prefers deep two-to-three-month falls whose last month has levelled off, and is wary of those still dropping hard over the past 30 days.
Why:
- F4: The "still falling over 30 days" condition works against the idea. Among cases with a known 30-day return (97), the closer that return is to zero, the larger the rebound: Spearman 0.25, p 0.015. Cases still falling hardest over 30 days give −6.2 % (t −1.14; −5.8 % clipped). Cases with the smallest 30-day fall give +20.6 % (t 1.78; +8.5 % clipped, t 1.81).
- F1: The tested mean unusual 30-day move of +4.35 % (t 1.19, 140 cases on 97 days) rests on a few outliers. The median case is −1.7 % and only 47 % of cases rise more than usual. The five largest cases, three of them XRP (+330 %, +157 %, +127 %), carry 120 % of the sum. Without those five the mean is −0.9 %, and clipped at the 5th and 95th percentiles it is +1.1 % with t 0.51.
- F5: The result is also mixed over time. The yearly means are −8.9 % in 2022 (36 cases, t −1.96) and −10.2 % in 2025 (26 cases, t −2.52). Against them are +13.9 % in 2023 (t 2.77) and +21.5 % in 2024 (t 1.51). By coin, DOGE (+12.3 %), XMR (+11.7 %) and XRP (+44.5 %) are positive, while BTC, ETH, LTC, ZEC, ADA, SOL and AVAX are negative.
