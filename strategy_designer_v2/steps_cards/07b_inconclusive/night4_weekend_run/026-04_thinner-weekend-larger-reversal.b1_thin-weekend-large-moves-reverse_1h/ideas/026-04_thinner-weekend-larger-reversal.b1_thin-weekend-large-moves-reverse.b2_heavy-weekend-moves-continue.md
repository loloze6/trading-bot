Idea: A coin's Saturday-Sunday move continues in its own direction over the next 48 hours (Monday and Tuesday), only while its weekend volume is higher than its mean volume over the preceding five weekdays; the heavier the weekend volume against the weekdays, the further the move continues.
Consequence: A weekend move made on heavier volume than the week's is a reason to follow it into Tuesday and to refuse any trade that leans against it.
Why:
- F3: At the other end, weekends heavier than the weekdays continue rather than reverse. The heaviest octile has a 48-hour strength of -0.195 (t -2.71, 293 cases) and an unusual move of -1.42 % against the reversal. The heavy-weekend group as a whole shows -0.151 (t -2.38, 439 cases), and the pattern holds at 72 hours (-0.206, t -3.05).
- F1: The test measured the reversal on every weekend with volume below the weekdays', which is 81 % of weekends (0.813 against 0.402 for the Wednesday control). The middle of that set does not reverse: across the weekend-volume octiles o3 to o7 the 48-hour strength runs from -0.012 to -0.118 (t -0.20 to -1.53). That dilution explains the test's zero result: slope t 0.86 and a mean unusual move of -0.00077 over 1,948 cases.
