Idea: A coin whose 3-day return is large against its usual daily range (the average of its daily high-to-low range over the past 30 days) keeps rising over the next 7 days, and the larger that range-scaled 3-day rise, the larger the further gain.
Consequence: A rally that is unusual against the coin's usual daily range draws late and forced buyers, so it continues over the following week.
Why:
- F1: Step 5's main measure points the way the idea predicts but falls short of the bar. The slope of the next-7-day excess return on the forecast is +0.00071 with t 1.43, over 1278 positive-forecast cells on 338 days. The 7-day size is null: the unusual move is -0.15 % per case (t -0.33, 875 days, 1882 cases), -0.012 of a usual move.
- F7: Across coins the 7-day positive-forecast excess is positive on BTC, XMR and XRP (+1.5 %, +1.6 %, +2.9 %, t 1.0 to 1.2) and about zero or negative on ETH, LTC and ZEC. The test used only six of the 19 coins available.
