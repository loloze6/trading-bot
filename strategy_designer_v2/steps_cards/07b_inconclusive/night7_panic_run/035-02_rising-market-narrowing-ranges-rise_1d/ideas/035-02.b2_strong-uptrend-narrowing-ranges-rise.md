Idea: In a rising market, a coin rises over the next 3 days, more so the lower its average daily high-to-low range over the last 14 days compared with its average daily range over the 90 days before them, and more so the larger its 60-day return.
Consequence: A trader should favour quieting coins only in the strongest uptrends, where calm ranges mark the orderly accumulation the theory describes, and refuse them in weak ones.
Why:
