013-01_nearness-to-high-predicts-gains: The nearer a coin closes to its 52-week high, the larger its return over the next 30 days.
013-02_follow-through-stronger-near-high: Past 30-day returns persist more strongly into the next 30 days the nearer a coin closes to its 52-week high.
013-03_breakout-above-high-continues: The further a coin's close breaks above its prior 52-week high, the larger its gain over the next 30 days.
013-04_heavy-volume-below-high-breakout: Heavier volume in the 10 days before a 52-week-high breakout precedes a larger 30-day gain after it.
013-05_longer-stall-bigger-breakout: The longer a coin stalled just under its 52-week high before breaking it, the larger its 30-day gain afterwards.
013-06_breakout-volume-drives-continuation: Higher breakout-day volume on a close above the 52-week high precedes a larger gain over the next 30 days.
