---
brief: 013_anchoring-to-recent-high
---
Idea: A coin's past 30-day return carries on into the next 30 days more strongly the closer the coin closes to its 52-week high. The same past return carries on less the further the coin sits below that high.
Consequence: A coin far below its high has no anchoring brake, so its moves carry less follow-through than those of a coin near its high.
