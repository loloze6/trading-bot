Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, when a coin's daily close sits near the top of that day's high-low range, its price falls over the next two days, and the nearer the close is to the day's high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: Late, urgent buying that pins the close at the high is absorbed by liquidity providers, and with no trend followers or patient buyers to carry it, the push is given back within two days.
