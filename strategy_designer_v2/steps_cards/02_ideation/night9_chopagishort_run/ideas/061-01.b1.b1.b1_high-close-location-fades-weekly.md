Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, when a coin's daily close sits near the top of that day's high-low range, its price falls over the next five days, and the nearer the close is to the day's high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: In a range-bound, agitated market, a trader can short a daily close pinned at its high and hold it for about a week, sizing the short by how close the close is to the high rather than by how little the day gained.
Why:
- F1: Step 5's result is on the predicted side (mean unusual 5-day move +2.51 %, t 3.44, 140 cases on 122 days), and only the gain grading fails. The supporting check is -0.0047. In folds B–C, among in-profile closes at or above 0.8 of the range, the day's gain in usual moves does not rank the 5-day fall: Spearman rho -0.01 (p 0.97). By gain bin the mean unusual 5-day move is +8.8 % for 0 to 0.5 usual moves (6 cases), +10.2 % for 0.5 to 1 (6) and +5.6 % for 1 to 2 (3). No such close fell on a down day, so the forecast's grading only ever separated small gains from moderate ones.
- F2: The brief's own grading does rank the fall: the nearer the close to the high, the larger the 5-day fall. Over all in-profile days, close location against the unusual 5-day move gives Spearman rho +0.215 (p 0.047). By close location the mean unusual 5-day move is +4.6 % at 0.6–0.8 (13 cases), +7.2 % at 0.8–0.9 (11 cases, t 1.63) and +7.1 % at 0.9–1.0 (10 cases, 7 days, t 2.70). Closes at or above 0.8 average +7.2 % (21 cases, 16 days, t 2.80), against +1.9 % below 0.8 (65 cases, t 0.74).
- F3: The fall shows over five days or more, not over one or two. In the 0.9–1.0 close-location bin the unusual move is 0.0 % over 1 day (t 0.00) and -2.6 % over 2 days (t -1.00). It is +7.1 % over 5 days (t 2.70) and +17.1 % over 10 days (7 cases, t 3.74). Over 5 and 10 days the low-close bins are flat or negative (-11.3 % at 0–0.2 over 10 days).
- F4: Over 5 days the near-high fall has the same sign in every year of folds B–C: +5.5 % in 2019 (4 cases), +12.0 % in 2020 (5 cases) and +5.6 % in 2021 (11 cases). Over 2 days it is mixed (2019 +3.1 %, 2020 -0.1 %, 2021 +0.9 %).
