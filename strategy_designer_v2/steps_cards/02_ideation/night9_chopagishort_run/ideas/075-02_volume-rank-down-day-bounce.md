Idea: After a daily close below the prior close, the coin rises over the next day, and the rise is larger the higher that day's volume ranks among its own daily volumes of the previous 30 days.
Consequence: The discount paid by forced sellers on a heavy-volume down day is repaid quickly, mostly in the first day after the forced flow stops.
