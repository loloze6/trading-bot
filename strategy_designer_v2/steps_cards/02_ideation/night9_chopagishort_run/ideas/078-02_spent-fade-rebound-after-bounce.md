Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a close near the top of the day's range after a losing seven-day stretch has been followed by a two-day fall, the larger that two-day give-back, the larger the rise over the next five days.
Consequence: Selling into the bounce is the last forced flow of the deleveraging wave, so once it is spent the coin rebounds by more than it gave back.
