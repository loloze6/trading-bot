Idea: When a coin closes a day near the top of its range, the deeper its five-day fall before that day, measured against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next two days.
Consequence: A deeper fall leaves more trapped holders and more shorts to cover, so the relief push is larger and the selling waiting above it is heavier, and the giveback grows.
