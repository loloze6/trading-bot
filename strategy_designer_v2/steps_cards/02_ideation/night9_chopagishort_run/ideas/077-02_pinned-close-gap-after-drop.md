Idea: After a seven-day fall that is deep against the coin's own usual swings, a smaller gap between the daily close and the day's high, measured against the coin's average daily range over the past 30 days, is followed by a larger fall over the next three days.
Consequence: The effect lives in closes pinned at the very top, so the nearness to the high, scaled by the coin's normal range, grades the later fall.
