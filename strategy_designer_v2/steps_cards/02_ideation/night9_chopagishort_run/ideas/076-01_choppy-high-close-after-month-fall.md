Idea: In a choppy market, after a daily close near the top of the day's range, the deeper the coin's fall over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next two days.
Consequence: After a few-week fall, a high close meets underwater range-buyers selling into strength, so it is followed by days of decline that grow with the size of the prior fall.
