Idea: After a daily close below the prior close, the coin rises over the next two days, and the rise is larger the heavier that day's volume was against its own average daily volume over the previous 20 days.
Consequence: Heavy volume on a down day marks forced selling sold at a discount, which is repaid as the price recovers once the forced flow stops.
