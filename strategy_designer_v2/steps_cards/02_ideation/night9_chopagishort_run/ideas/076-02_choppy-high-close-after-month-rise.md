Idea: In a choppy market, after a daily close near the top of the day's range, the larger the coin's rise over the past 30 days, the larger its further rise over the next two days.
Consequence: After a few-week rise, a high close shows buyers pressing the top of the range while caught range-faders cover late, so it is followed by days of further rise that grow with the size of the prior rise.
