Idea: In a coin that has fallen over the past five days, the nearer a day's close sits to that day's high, the larger the fall over the next two days.
Consequence: After a multi-day fall, a high close is temporary relief buying that trapped holders sell into, so the strength is given back, and more so the higher the close.
