Idea: In a falling, agitated market, after a losing seven-day stretch, a daily close nearer the top of the day's range is followed by a larger net rise from that close over the next seven days.
Consequence: The fade is short-lived and the rebound after it exceeds it, so a week later the coin is higher than at the bounce close.
