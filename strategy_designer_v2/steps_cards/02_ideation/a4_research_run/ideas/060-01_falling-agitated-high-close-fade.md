Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin that closes near the top of its day's high-low range falls over the next day, and the nearer the close sits to the day's high, the larger that next-day fall.
Consequence: The late-day buying in a falling, agitated market is short-covering and bargain hunting that is soon used up, so a strong close gives way to the selling still behind the downtrend.
