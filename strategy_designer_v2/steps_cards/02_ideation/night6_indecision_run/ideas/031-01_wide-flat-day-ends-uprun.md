Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 5 days, a day whose range is wide compared with its average daily range over the past 20 days, but whose close lands near its open, is followed by a fall over the next 5 days. The wider that day's range relative to its 20-day average range, and the closer its close sits to its open, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A wide-range day that closes flat after an up-run shows buying absorbed and reversed, so the run ends and price moves back down.
