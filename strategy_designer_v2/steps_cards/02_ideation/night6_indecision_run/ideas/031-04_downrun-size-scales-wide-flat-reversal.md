Idea: On a day whose range is wide compared with its 20-day average daily range and whose close lands near its open, the larger the coin's fall over the preceding 5 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: The failed push matters because a run came before it; a bigger down-run that is absorbed leaves more trapped sellers and more exhausted fuel, so the move back is larger.
