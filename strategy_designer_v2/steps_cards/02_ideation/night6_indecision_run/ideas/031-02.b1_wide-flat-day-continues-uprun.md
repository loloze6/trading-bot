Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 5 days, a day whose range is wide compared with its average daily range over the past 20 days, but whose close lands near its open, is followed by a further rise over the next 10 days. The larger the coin's 5-day rise before that day, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A wide, flat day at the top of an up-run is a pause that absorbs supply without stopping buyers, so a trader should stay long or add, not fade it.
Why:
- F4: The mirror situation shows a clear effect, but in the opposite direction to the brief. After a 5-day rise, a wide-range flat day is followed by a further rise, not a reversal. The unusual move is +1.93 % over 5 days (t 2.29, 197 cases on 173 days) and +4.12 % over 10 days (t 2.74, 188 cases on 166 days).
- F5: The continuation after a rise needs the wide range. Narrow flat days after a rise give -0.34 % over 5 days (t -0.62, 472 cases) and -0.22 % over 10 days (t -0.27). All days after a rise give -0.05 % (t -0.14, 2,329 cases).
- F6: The continuation is graded by the size of the prior 5-day rise, not by the range ratio. By rise tercile the 5-day unusual move is -0.19 % (t -0.22), +2.30 % (t 1.79) and +3.53 % (t 1.91). The correlation with the 5-day rise is 0.10, against -0.04 for the range ratio and -0.09 for flatness. Range-ratio terciles give +1.26 %, +2.56 % and +1.95 %.
- F7: The continuation is broad but strongest in two coins. All six coins are positive: ETH +6.20 % (t 3.07), XRP +4.28 % (t 1.63), ZEC +1.47 %, BTC +0.66 %, XMR +0.30 % and LTC +0.21 %. Six of eight years are positive, with 2019 at -1.51 % and 2022 at -0.50 %.
- F8: Neither version can pay at this cost unless the move is larger. A trade costs 0.19 %, while the tested idea's total move per case is +0.07 %. The rise-side continuation's unusual move of about 1.9 % over 5 days and 4.1 % over 10 days is many times that cost.
