bar: 1d

Idea: When a coin has been choppy over the past 30 days, with large daily swings in both directions and little net progress, a 7-day fall tends to be partly recovered with a rise over the next 7 days. The larger that 7-day fall is relative to the coin's typical daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: In agitated trendless markets a large weekly down-move is noise from forced and hurried selling, so price drifts back up as that flow dies.
