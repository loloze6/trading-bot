Idea: For a coin's 7-day move scaled by its typical daily swing over the past 30 days, the share of that move given back over the next 7 days grows as the past 30 days become more agitated and trendless. More agitated and trendless means larger daily swings in both directions relative to the net 30-day move.
Consequence: Agitation raises both the share of rushed flow and the discount patient traders demand, so reversal is strongest when the market is most agitated and trendless.
