Idea: When a coin has been choppy over the past 30 days, with large daily swings in both directions and little net progress, a 7-day rise tends to be partly given back with a decline over the next 7 days. The larger that 7-day rise is relative to the coin's typical daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger the decline.
Consequence: In agitated trendless markets a large weekly up-move is noise pushed by rushed flow, so price drifts back down as that flow dies.
