030-01_absorption-close-high-rises: After a heavy-volume, flat day, the nearer the close to the high, the larger the 5-day rise, more so with heavier volume. [bar: 1d]
030-02_absorption-close-low-falls: After a heavy-volume, flat day, the nearer the close to the low, the larger the 5-day fall, more so with heavier volume. [bar: 1d]
030-03_heavy-flat-high-close-reverses: After a heavy-volume, flat day, the nearer the close to the high, the larger the 5-day fall, as the last buying push unwinds. [bar: 1d]
030-04_heavy-flat-low-close-reverses: After a heavy-volume, flat day, the nearer the close to the low, the larger the 5-day rise, as the last selling push unwinds. [bar: 1d]
