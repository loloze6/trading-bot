Idea: The heavier a coin's volume on a day relative to its own prior 20-day average, the more the coin rises over the next day, the lower the day's close sits within its high-low range. The rise grows faster than the volume surge, so it is concentrated on the days of the largest surges with the lowest closes.
Consequence: A trader buys only the days whose volume surge is several times usual and whose close sits near the low, sizes by the square of the surge, and is out of the coin on ordinary-volume days, which removes the exposure that made the falls.
Why:
- F1: The book failed on its falls, not on its gain. Its deepest fall is -48.1 %, over 310 days in w1-w3 (holding the coins lost -66.4 % then), against a yearly return of +24.2 %. Its daily returns correlate 0.82 with holding the coins, because a position is open on 97.9 % of coin-bars (the forecast is above 0 on 99.1 % of them).
- F2: The gain sits in the top fifth of the forecast, which earns +0.44 % next-day excess return per bar (n 3474), and partly in the fourth fifth (+0.01 %). The bottom three fifths earn -0.23 %, -0.09 % and -0.14 %, yet still hold allocations of 0.07, 0.20 and 0.36. The book is long the coin on weak-signal days that lose against its average.
- F3: During the deepest fall (w1-w3) the book still held a mean allocation of 0.38 with the forecast above 0 on 99.3 % of bars, while the coin's next-day return averaged -0.18 % (+0.16 % in the other windows). The fall is the coin's own decline, carried by a book that never steps out.
- F4: Weak-signal bars lose most when the coin's 20-day return is negative (excess -0.30 %, t -2.66, n 4354). Signal bars in the same downtrend still earn excess +0.22 % (t 3.39, n 2959), about as much as in uptrends (+0.24 %). Being out on weak-signal days, rather than following the trend, is what removes the losing exposure.
- F5: The effect is much stronger in its strong case. With volume above 2.5 times its prior 20-day mean and the close in the lower third of the range, the next-day excess is +2.16 % (n 199). With volume 1.5-2.5 times its mean and the close in the lower third, it is +0.73 % (n 580). With volume at or below its mean it is -0.07 % to -0.12 %. Heavy volume with a close near the high reverses the sign (-0.53 %, n 285). The move grows faster than the surge.
- F8: Against the 0.19 % cost of a trade, the top fifth's total move (+0.56 %) is about 3 times the cost and the strong case's (+2.16 % excess) about 11 times. The idea's average case is only 1.3 times the cost. Trading only the strong part of the signal pays for its trades.
