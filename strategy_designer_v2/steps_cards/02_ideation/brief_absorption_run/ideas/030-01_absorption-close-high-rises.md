Idea: On a day when a coin's volume is far above its 20-day average but its close is little changed from its open, the closer the close sits to the day's high, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days. The effect is larger the heavier that day's volume is.
Consequence: Yes, continuation: a close near the high after heavy flat-result volume shows buyers absorbed the selling and will defend their position, so price moves up.
