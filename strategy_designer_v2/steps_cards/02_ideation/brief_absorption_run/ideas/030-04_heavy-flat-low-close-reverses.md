Idea: On a day when a coin's volume is far above its 20-day average but its close is little changed from its open, the closer the close sits to the day's low, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days. The effect is larger the heavier that day's volume is.
Consequence: Reversal: a close near the low is a large seller finishing, and liquidity providers who bought from them unwind, so price drifts back up.
