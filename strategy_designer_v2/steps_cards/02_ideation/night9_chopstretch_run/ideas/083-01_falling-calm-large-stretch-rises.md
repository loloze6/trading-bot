Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose close sits far above its 20-day average, measured in its own 30-day daily swing, keeps rising over the next 10 days. The rise grows faster than the stretch: modest stretches are followed by little, the largest by most.
Consequence: A large push above the average in an orderly decline marks new demand and an early turn, which slow trend followers and allocators keep feeding.
