Idea: In an agitated market, a day whose high-low range is wide against the coin's average range over the past 20 days and which closes near its high is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the wider the day's range and the closer its close sits to its high, the two multiplying, so a high close on an ordinary-width day is followed by little fall.
Consequence: Late forced or reflexive buying on a wide agitated day is spent by the close and gives back over the next day, more for wider days that close higher in their range.
