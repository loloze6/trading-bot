Idea: A coin whose 20-day return is a fall that is large against its daily swing over those 20 days keeps falling over the next 5 days, and the deeper that fall in units of its swing, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A few-week fall that is deep for the coin's usual swing leaves selling still to come and buyers holding back, so the decline carries on in proportion to its depth.
