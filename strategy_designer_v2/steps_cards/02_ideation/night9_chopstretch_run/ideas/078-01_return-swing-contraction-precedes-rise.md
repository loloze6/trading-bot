Idea: A coin whose standard deviation of daily returns over the last 20 days has shrunk against its standard deviation over the last 120 days rises over the next 10 days, and the deeper that contraction, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Holders who size positions by recent swing add exposure gradually as swings contract, which gives a one-sided buying flow that grows with the contraction.
