Idea: A coin whose close has dropped far below its highest close of the past 20 days, measured against its daily swing over those 20 days, keeps falling over the next 5 days, and the deeper that drop in units of its swing, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: Holders who bought during the past weeks are under water by an amount that is large for the coin's swing, and their staggered selling keeps the decline going in proportion to its depth.
