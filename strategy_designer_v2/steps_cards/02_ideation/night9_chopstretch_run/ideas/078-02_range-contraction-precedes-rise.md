Idea: A coin whose average daily high-to-low range over the last 20 days has shrunk against its average daily range over the last 120 days rises over the next 10 days, and the deeper that contraction, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Risk-sized holders whose measures of realised moves loosen as daily ranges narrow carry more of the coin and buy it steadily, more so the more the ranges have narrowed.
