Idea: In a rising market, a coin whose daily close stands far above its 20-day average, measured in its own usual daily swing, keeps rising over the next 10 days, and the further above the close stands, the larger that rise.
Consequence: The carry is not limited to trendless markets: it also shows in rising markets, for stretches above the average.
