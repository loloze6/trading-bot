Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose 14-day rise is large against its own daily swing keeps rising over the next 7 days. The rise grows faster than the push: modest rises carry little, the largest carry most.
Consequence: A large push in a calm decline is the early part of a turn, not a bounce to be sold, so it carries on while followers buy back in steps.
