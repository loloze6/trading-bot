Idea: In a falling market, a coin whose daily close stands far below its 20-day average, measured in its own usual daily swing, keeps falling over the next 10 days, and the further below the close stands, the larger that fall.
Consequence: The carry is not limited to trendless markets: it also shows in falling markets, for stretches below the average.
