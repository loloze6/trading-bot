Idea: In an agitated market, the further a coin's close sits below its 20-day average, measured in units of the standard deviation of its daily returns over the same 20 days, the more it rises over the next 10 days.
Consequence: Forced selling when swings widen pushes price below where patient buyers would set it, and the discount is repaid over days, more after deeper stretches.
