Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average, measured in units of its 20-day standard deviation of daily returns, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days.
Consequence: A large upward stretch from the recent average, judged against the coin's own swing, is followed by more rise, more so the larger the stretch.
