Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the smaller the gap between a coin's close and its highest close of the past 30 days, measured in units of its 30-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: Holders who sized for normal swings reach breakeven near the month's high and cut risk, so nearness counted against the coin's current swing marks how close that selling is.
