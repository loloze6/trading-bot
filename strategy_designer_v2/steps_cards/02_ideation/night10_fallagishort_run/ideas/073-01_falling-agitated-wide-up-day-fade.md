Idea: In a falling, agitated market, after a day that closes above the day before, the wider that day's high-low range is against the coin's average high-low range over the past 20 days, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: The forced, uninformed flow on a wide up day overshoots and is repaid the next day, and a wider day means more flow to repay.
