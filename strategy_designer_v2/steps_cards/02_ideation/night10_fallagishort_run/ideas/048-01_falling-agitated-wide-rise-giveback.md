Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a day's rise against the coin's 30-day daily swing, multiplied by how wide that day's high-to-low range is against its 30-day usual range, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: Up days in a falling, agitated market are short-lived squeezes, and the share of the gain later given back grows with the rise's size and the day's range together.
