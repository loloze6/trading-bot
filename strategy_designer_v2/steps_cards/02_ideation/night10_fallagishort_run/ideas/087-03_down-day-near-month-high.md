Idea: In a falling, agitated market, on days that close below their open, the nearer the close sits to the coin's highest close of the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: The breakeven supply depends on where the price stands against the month's best close, not on the last day's move, so it appears after down days too.
