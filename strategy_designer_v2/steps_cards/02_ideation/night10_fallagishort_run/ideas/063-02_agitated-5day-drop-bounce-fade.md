Idea: In a falling, agitated market, on a coin's first up day after a multi-day slide, the larger the fall of its 5-day return up to the day before the bounce against its 30-day swing of daily returns, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: The bounce rests on flow that will not return and on supply from holders trapped through the slide, so a deeper recent fall gives a larger fade the day after the bounce.
