Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin whose 14-day return is a rise that is large against the standard deviation of its daily returns over those 14 days falls over the next 7 days, and the larger that swing-scaled rise, the larger the fall.
Consequence: In a falling, agitated market, a two-week recovery runs into holders selling near their entry and uses up the short-covering and bargain buying, so the larger the swing-scaled rise, the more of it is given back over the following week.
