Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin's up day that comes while its 3-day return is still negative is followed by a fall over the next two days, and the wider that day's high-to-low range against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger the two-day fall.
Consequence: A trader holds the fade of a wide bounce in an agitated decline for two days rather than one, capturing the give-back that continues after the first day.
Why:
- F2: Inside the book, the gain comes only from the widest bounces. The top range quartile of the cases earns 0.943 of the 1.197 book gain. Its cases fall by 2.72 % beyond drift the next day (t 3.43, 53 cases), against −0.76 % and −0.25 % for the two narrowest quartiles. The Spearman correlation of range ratio with the next-day unusual move is 0.26 (p 0.0001).
- F6: The give-back lasts one to two days, then fades.
