Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the smaller the gap between a coin's close and its highest close of the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: As price nears the month's best close, more underwater holders reach breakeven and sell, and this supply pushes the price down once the short-lived buying is spent.
