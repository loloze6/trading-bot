Idea: In a falling, agitated market, on a coin's first up day after a run of consecutive down days, the larger the run's total fall against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: The deeper the slide before the first bounce, measured in the coin's own swings, the more trapped holders sell into it and the more short profit-taking runs out, so the larger the next-day giveback.
