Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the first up day after a down day is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the wider that day's low-to-high range against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, and the smaller the share of that range its rise from the previous close covers.
Consequence: A trader would fade first up days whose gain held only part of their range, and leave alone those that closed near the top of a wide range, as these are where squeezes keep running.
