Idea: In a falling, agitated market, after a day that closes above the day before, the larger that day's high-low range is in units of the coin's daily swing over the past 20 days (the standard deviation of its daily returns), the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: A wider up day, measured against how much the coin has been swinging, means a larger overshoot from forced buying, and so a larger fall the next day.
