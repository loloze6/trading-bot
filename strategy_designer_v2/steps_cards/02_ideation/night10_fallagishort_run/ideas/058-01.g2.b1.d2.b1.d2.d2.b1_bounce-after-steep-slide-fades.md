Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a one-day rise of up to about one usual swing that follows a steep fall over the previous four days is given back over the next day, only after the steepest four-day falls, and more the deeper that four-day fall against the coin's 30-day swing.
Consequence: A trader would short a modest up day only when it interrupts a sharp several-day slide, for one day, sizing up with the slide's depth, and would leave alone the bounces after shallow slides.
