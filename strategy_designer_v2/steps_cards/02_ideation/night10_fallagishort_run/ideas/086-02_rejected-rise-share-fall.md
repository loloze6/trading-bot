Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger the share of a day's rise above its open that is given back by its close, the larger the coin's fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: The information is the rejected intraday rise, not where the day closed: the more of the relief rally sellers absorb, the more supply remains for the following days.
