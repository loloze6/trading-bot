Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the longer a day's upper shadow (its high above the higher of its open and close) against the coin's usual daily range over the past 30 days, the larger the coin's fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A rise sold back by resting supply from underwater holders is followed by further falls, larger as the rejected rise is longer.
