---
brief: 003_thin-volume-moves-revert
---
Idea: Among a coin's sharp down bars of similar size, the share of the drop kept over the following bars grows steadily as the bar's volume, relative to the coin's usual volume, goes from low to high.
Consequence: How well a move holds depends on who is trading it: the more participants behind it, the more it holds, and the fewer, the more it reverts.
