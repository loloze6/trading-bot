003-01_low-volume-rise-reverts: Sharp single-bar rises on unusually low volume are partly given back over the following bars.
003-02_low-volume-drop-reverts: Sharp single-bar drops on unusually low volume are partly recovered over the following bars.
003-03_high-volume-rise-continues: Sharp single-bar rises on unusually high volume are followed by further rises over the following bars.
003-04_high-volume-drop-continues: Sharp single-bar drops on unusually high volume are followed by further declines over the following bars.
003-05_rise-holding-grows-with-volume: For similar-size sharp rises, the higher the relative volume, the more of the rise is kept afterward.
003-06_drop-holding-grows-with-volume: For similar-size sharp drops, the higher the relative volume, the more of the drop is kept afterward.
003-07_quiet-hours-moves-revert: Sharp moves made in quiet hours or weekends are partly reversed when busier, higher-volume hours return.
