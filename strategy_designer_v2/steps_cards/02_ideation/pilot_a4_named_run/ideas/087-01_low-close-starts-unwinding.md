Idea: In a rising, agitated market, a day that closes near the low of its own high-low range is followed by a decline over the next three days, and the nearer the close sits to the day's low, the larger that decline.
Consequence: A close at the day's low leaves the latest levered longs underwater, and their stops and margin calls fire in waves over the following days, so the decline grows as the close nears the low.
