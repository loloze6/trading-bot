---
brief: 005_rsi-divergence-predicts-reversal
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: When a coin's close makes a new 30-day low while its 14-day RSI is above its reading at the previous low in that window, the price rebounds over the next 10 days, and the further the RSI stays above its earlier trough, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: Yes, a bullish divergence predicts a turn up: a decline that keeps going with smaller steps has used up its urgent sellers and liquidations, so part of it gives back.
