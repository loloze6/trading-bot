---
brief: 005_rsi-divergence-predicts-reversal
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: When a coin's close makes a new 30-day high while its 14-day RSI is below its reading at the previous high in that window, the price falls back over the next 10 days, and the further the RSI falls short of its earlier peak, the larger the drop.
Consequence: Yes, a bearish divergence predicts a turn down: a rally that keeps going with smaller steps has used up its urgent buyers, so part of it gives back.
