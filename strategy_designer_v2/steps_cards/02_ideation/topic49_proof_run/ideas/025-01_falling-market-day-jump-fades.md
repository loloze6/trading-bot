Idea: In a falling market, the larger a coin's one-day rise measured in its usual daily moves (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days), the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A sharp bounce in a long fall meets holders under water selling into it and returning short sellers, so it is given back and the fall resumes.
