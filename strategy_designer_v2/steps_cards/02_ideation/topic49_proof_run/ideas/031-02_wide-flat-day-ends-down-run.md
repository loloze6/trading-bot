Idea: After a coin has fallen over the last 5 days, a day that closes near its open but has an unusually wide high-low range against its 30-day average range is followed by a rise over the next 5 days. The wider that day's range, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A wide-range, flat-close day ends a down run because buyers absorb the sellers' push, so the coin then moves back up.
