Idea: After a coin has risen over the last 5 days, a day whose high pushes above the run's earlier highs but whose close falls back to near its open is followed by a fall over the next 5 days. The farther that high reached above the prior 5-day high, measured against the coin's 30-day average range, the larger the fall.
Consequence: Buyers who chased at the failed push's high are left with losing positions, and their exits add to the move back down.
