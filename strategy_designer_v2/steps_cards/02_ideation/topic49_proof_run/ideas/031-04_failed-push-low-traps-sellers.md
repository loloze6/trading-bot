Idea: After a coin has fallen over the last 5 days, a day whose low pushes below the run's earlier lows but whose close rises back to near its open is followed by a rise over the next 5 days. The farther that low reached below the prior 5-day low, measured against the coin's 30-day average range, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Sellers who chased at the failed push's low are left with losing positions, and their exits add to the move back up.
