Idea: In a choppy market, the farther a coin's close sits above the middle of its 30-day high-low range, measured as a share of that range's width, the more it falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A coin far above the middle of a flat month falls back toward it, as range traders sell near the top of the agreed band of value.
