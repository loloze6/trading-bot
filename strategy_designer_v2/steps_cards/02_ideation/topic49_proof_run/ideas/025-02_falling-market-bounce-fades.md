Idea: In a falling market, the larger a coin's 3-day rise measured in its usual daily moves (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days), the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A bounce built over a few days in a long fall is sold into by trapped holders and returning shorts, so it is given back and the fall resumes.
