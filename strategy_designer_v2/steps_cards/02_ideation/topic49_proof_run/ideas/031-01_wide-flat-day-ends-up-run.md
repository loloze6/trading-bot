brief: 031_wide-flat-day-ends-run
bar: 1d

Idea: After a coin has risen over the last 5 days, a day that closes near its open but has an unusually wide high-low range against its 30-day average range is followed by a fall over the next 5 days. The wider that day's range, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A wide-range, flat-close day ends an up run because sellers absorb the buyers' push, so the coin then moves back down.
