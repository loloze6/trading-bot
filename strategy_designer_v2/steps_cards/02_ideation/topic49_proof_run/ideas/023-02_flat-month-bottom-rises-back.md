brief: 023_range-reversion-when-no-trend
bar: 1d
profile: choppy
---
Idea: In a choppy market, the farther a coin's close sits below the middle of its 30-day high-low range, measured as a share of that range's width, the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A coin far below the middle of a flat month rises back toward it, as range traders buy near the bottom of the agreed band of value.
