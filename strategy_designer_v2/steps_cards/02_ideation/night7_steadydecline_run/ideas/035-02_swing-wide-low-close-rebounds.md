Idea: The larger a day's high-to-low range is as a multiple of the standard deviation of the coin's daily returns over the past 20 days, and the nearer its close sits to the day's low, the larger the coin's rise over the next day.
Consequence: The day's width against the coin's own recent swing measures how far forced selling exceeded normal liquidity, and so how large the next-day rebound is.
