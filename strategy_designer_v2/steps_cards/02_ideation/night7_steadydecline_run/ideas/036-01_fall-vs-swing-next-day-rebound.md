Idea: The further a coin's one-day close-to-close fall exceeds the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A fall far outside the coin's usual daily swing contains forced and frightened selling that is partly given back the next day, more so the larger the fall against that swing.
