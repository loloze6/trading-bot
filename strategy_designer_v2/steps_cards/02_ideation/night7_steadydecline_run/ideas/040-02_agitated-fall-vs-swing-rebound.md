Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A fall that is large for the coin's current swing is more likely to be a forced overshoot from liquidations, stops and volatility-sized cuts, and it reverts the next day.
