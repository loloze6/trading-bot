Idea: The larger a coin's one-day fall and the further that day's high-to-low range stretches beyond the size of the closing loss itself, taken together, the larger the rise over the next day.
Consequence: a fall whose intraday travel goes well beyond its closing loss marks a forced sweep of the book, which gives back part of the fall the next day
