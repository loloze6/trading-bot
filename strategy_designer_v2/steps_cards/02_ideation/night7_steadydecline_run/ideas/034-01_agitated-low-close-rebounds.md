Idea: In an agitated market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the day's low within its high-low range, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: In agitated stretches, forced late-day selling pushes the close toward the day's low and overshoots, and the price moves back the next day once that flow is spent.
