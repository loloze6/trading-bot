Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a one-day rise against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, the larger the fall over the next day.
Consequence: A sharp up-day, pushed by shorts covering in a squeeze, overshoots the thin book and is partly given back the next day.
