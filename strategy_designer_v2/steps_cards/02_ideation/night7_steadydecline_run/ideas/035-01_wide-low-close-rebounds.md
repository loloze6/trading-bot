Idea: The wider a day's high-to-low range is against the coin's average daily range over the past 14 days, and the nearer its close sits to the day's low, the larger the coin's rise over the next day.
Consequence: A wide day closing near its low marks forced selling beyond normal liquidity, and the price recovers the concession the next day, more so the larger the overshoot.
