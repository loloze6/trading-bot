Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: In an agitated decline, a fall that is unusually large for the coin's current swing is more likely a forced sweep that overshoots, so the next-day rebound grows with it.
