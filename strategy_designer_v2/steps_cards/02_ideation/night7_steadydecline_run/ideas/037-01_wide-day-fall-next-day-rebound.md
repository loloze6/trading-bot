Idea: The larger a coin's one-day fall and the wider that day's high-to-low range against its own average daily range over the past 14 days, taken together, the larger the rise over the next day.
Consequence: a large fall rebounds the next day when the day's range was unusually wide against recent ranges, and the rebound grows with that width
