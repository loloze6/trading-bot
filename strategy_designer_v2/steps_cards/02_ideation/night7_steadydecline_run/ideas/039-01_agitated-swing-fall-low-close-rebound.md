Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a one-day fall against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, and the nearer that day's close sits to its low within the day's range, the larger the rise over the next day. The rise grows with the two together.
Consequence: Forced selling, seen as a fall far beyond the usual swing that is still pressing at the close, overshoots and is recovered the next day in agitated markets.
