Idea: The nearer a coin's daily close sits to that day's low, measured as its position within the day's high-low range, the larger the rise in its price over the next day.
Consequence: A close pinned near the day's low reflects forced late selling below fair value, which absorbing buyers are paid for as the price drifts back the next day.
