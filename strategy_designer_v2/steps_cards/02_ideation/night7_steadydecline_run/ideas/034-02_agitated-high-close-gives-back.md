Idea: In an agitated market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the day's high within its high-low range, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: In agitated stretches, forced late-day buying pushes the close toward the day's high and overshoots, and the price gives it back the next day once that flow is spent.
