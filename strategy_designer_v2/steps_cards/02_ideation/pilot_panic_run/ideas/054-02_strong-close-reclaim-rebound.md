Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin's day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above that low, it rises over the next 5 days, and the rise is larger the higher the close sits above that low, measured against the day's range.
Consequence: Rebound: a stronger reclaim shows that buyers took all of the supply set off below the low and leaves the breakdown sellers further under water.
