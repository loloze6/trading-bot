Idea: On a day whose high goes above the coin's highest high of the previous 30 days but which closes back below that 30-day high, the coin rises over the next 14 days, and the further the day's high went above the 30-day high, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A failed pierce of the monthly high is followed by a rise, larger for deeper pierces, because a one-day block of overhead supply is spent and late buyers and covering shorts follow.
