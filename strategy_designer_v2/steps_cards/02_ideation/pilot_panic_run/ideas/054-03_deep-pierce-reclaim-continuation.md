Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin's day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above that low, it falls over the next 5 days, and the fall is larger the deeper the day's low went below the 30-day low.
Consequence: Continuation: the pierce is noise in wide ranges, holders under water sell into the reclaim, and a deeper probe means the next break cascades further.
