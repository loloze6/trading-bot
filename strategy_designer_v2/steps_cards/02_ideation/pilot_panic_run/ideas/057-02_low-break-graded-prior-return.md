Idea: A day that closes below the coin's lowest price of the past 30 days is followed by a fall over the next 5 days, and the higher the coin's 60-day return before that close, the larger the fall.
Consequence: The later decline after a monthly-low break grows with the prior two-month return, large after a rise and small after a long fall, because weak and levered holders are already gone after a fall.
