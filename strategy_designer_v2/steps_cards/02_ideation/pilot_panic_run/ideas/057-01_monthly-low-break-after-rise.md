Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes below the coin's lowest price of the past 30 days is followed by a fall over the next 5 days, and the larger the coin's 60-day rise before that close, the larger the fall.
Consequence: After a two-month rise, a close below the monthly low releases stop, margin and late profit-taking sales spread over the following days, more the bigger the prior rise.
