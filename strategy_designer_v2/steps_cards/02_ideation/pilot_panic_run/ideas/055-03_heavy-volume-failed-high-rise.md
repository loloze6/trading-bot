Idea: On a day whose high goes above the coin's highest high of the previous 30 days but which closes back below that 30-day high, the coin rises over the next 14 days, and the higher that day's volume against the coin's average daily volume of the previous 30 days, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A larger block of supply absorbed on the failed-break day leaves thinner overhead supply and more shorts to cover, so the following rise is larger.
