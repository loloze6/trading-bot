Idea: On a day whose high goes above the coin's highest high of the previous 30 days but which closes back below that 30-day high, the coin rises over the next 14 days, and the larger the fall from the day's high to its close, the larger the rise.
Consequence: The more supply sellers dumped at the old high that day, the more of it is spent and the more shorts must buy back, so the larger the following rise.
