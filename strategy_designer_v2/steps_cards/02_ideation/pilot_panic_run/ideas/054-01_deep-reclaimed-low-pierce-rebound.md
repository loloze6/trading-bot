Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin's day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above that low, it rises over the next 5 days, and the rise is larger the deeper the day's low went below the 30-day low.
Consequence: Rebound: a deeper failed pierce triggered and absorbed more stop and breakdown selling, and trapped more short-sellers who then cover.
