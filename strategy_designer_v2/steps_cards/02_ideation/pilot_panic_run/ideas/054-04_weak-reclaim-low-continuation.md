Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin's day trades below its lowest price of the past 30 days but closes back above that low, it falls over the next 5 days, and the fall is larger the closer the close sits to that low, measured against the day's range.
Consequence: Continuation: a weak reclaim only delays the break, as holders under water sell into the small recovery and the next attempt goes through.
