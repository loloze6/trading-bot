brief: 058_falling-agitated-multiday-rallies-fail
profile: falling agitated
---
Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's 3-day rise is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger the fall in its price over the next 5 days, taking back a larger share of that rise.
Consequence: Multi-day rises in falling, agitated markets are mostly forced short covering, so they are partly given back once covering ends, more so the larger the rise against the coin's usual swing.
