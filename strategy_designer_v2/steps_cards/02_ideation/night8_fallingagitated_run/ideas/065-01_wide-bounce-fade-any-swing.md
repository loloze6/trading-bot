Idea: In any market, on an up day that leaves the coin still down over its last 3 days, the wider that day's high-to-low range is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: A hurried relief bounce inside a short fall overshoots and gives back more the wider its range, in every trend, not only in falling markets.
