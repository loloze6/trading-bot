Idea: In any market, on an up day that leaves the coin still down over its last 3 days, the wider that day's high-to-low range is against its average daily high-to-low range over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: The same fade of a hurried bounce inside a short fall, with the day's width read against the coin's usual daily range, in every trend.
