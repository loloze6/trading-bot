Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a one-day rise that is large against the coin's own 30-day daily swing is followed by a further rise over the next 7 days, and the larger the rise in units of that swing, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: In a quiet, range-bound coin, a large one-day rise is informed buying rather than forced flow, so others absorb it slowly and the price keeps rising for about a week.
