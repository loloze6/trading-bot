Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a one-day rise that is large against the coin's own 30-day daily swing is followed by a rise from 2 to 7 days after it, and the larger the rise in units of that swing, the larger that later rise.
Consequence: Range sellers first lean against the rise and then step aside, while dormant holders and trend followers act only after more than a day, so the further rise builds in the days after the first.
