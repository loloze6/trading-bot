Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise measured in units of its daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its rise from 1 to 5 days after that day, leaving out the first day after it.
Consequence: Buyers who spread their orders over days, and traders who add risk to a quiet coin after the signal, arrive after the first day, so the further rise builds up in the days that follow.
