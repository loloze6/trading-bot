Idea: In a falling market, on a day a coin closes up while its 3-day return is still negative, the wider that day's high-to-low range is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: In a decline, a wide-range up day draws out underwater holders' supply once short-lived buying is spent, so price falls back, and more so the wider the bounce.
