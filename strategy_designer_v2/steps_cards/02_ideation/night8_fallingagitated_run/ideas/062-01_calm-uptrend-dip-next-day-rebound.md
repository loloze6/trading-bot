Idea: In a rising, calm market, the larger a coin's one-day fall measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: In a rising, calm market, a large swing-scaled one-day fall is an overshoot by a hurried seller, stop sweep or liquidation, and patient buyers repay it within a day.
