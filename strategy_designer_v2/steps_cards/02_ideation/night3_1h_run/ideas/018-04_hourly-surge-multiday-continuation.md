Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return, read at an hourly close, first reaches several times its 30-day average absolute daily return on the upside, the price keeps rising over the next 5 days by more than the coin's usual 5-day return. The larger the 24-hour return in those multiples, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Flow from daily traders after the close pushes a large up move further over the days after the hour it first holds.
