brief: 018_daily-condition-caught-on-the-hour
bar: 1h

Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return, read at an hourly close, first reaches several times its 30-day average absolute daily return on the upside, the price keeps rising from that hour to the same day's daily close. The larger the 24-hour return in those multiples, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Part of a large up move is made after the hour it first holds and before the daily close, so acting at that hour earns more than acting at the close.
