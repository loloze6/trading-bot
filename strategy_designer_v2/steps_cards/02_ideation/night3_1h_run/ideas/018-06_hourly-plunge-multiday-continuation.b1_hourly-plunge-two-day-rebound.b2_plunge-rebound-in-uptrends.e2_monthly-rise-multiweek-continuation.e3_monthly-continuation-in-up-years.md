Idea: In a year when a coin's own 1-year return is positive, a coin whose 30-day return is positive rises over the next 7 days by more than its usual 7-day drift, more as its 30-day return is larger.
Consequence: A month-long rise is a reason to go long for a week mainly while the coin's year is rising, and a reason to refuse that trade while its year is falling.
