Idea: A coin whose 7-day return is among its largest rises over the next 7 days by more than its usual 7-day drift, and the larger its 7-day return, the larger that rise.
Consequence: A strong week is a reason to stay long in the coin for the following week, in proportion to the strength of that week's rise.
Why:
- F6: A coin's 7-day return ranks the next move about as well as its 30-day return. The top 7-day-return quintile is followed by a 3-day excess of +0.66 % (strength 0.072) and a 7-day excess of +0.96 % (strength 0.058). The middle quintiles are negative at both horizons (-0.42 % to -1.00 % over 7 days).
