bar: 1h

Idea: When a coin's 24-hour return, read at an hourly close, first reaches several times its 30-day average absolute daily return on the downside, the price keeps falling over the next 5 days by more than the coin's usual 5-day move. The larger the 24-hour drop in those multiples, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: Flow from daily traders after the close pushes a large down move further over the days after the hour it first holds.
