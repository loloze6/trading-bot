Idea: When a coin's hourly close first rises above its highest price of the prior 20 days, the price keeps rising over the next 5 days by more than the coin's usual 5-day return. The further that close sits above the 20-day high, in multiples of its 30-day average absolute daily return, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Flow from daily traders after the close pushes a breakout further over the days after the hour it first holds.
