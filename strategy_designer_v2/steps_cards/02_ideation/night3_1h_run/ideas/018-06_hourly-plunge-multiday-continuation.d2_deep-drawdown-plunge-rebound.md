Idea: While a coin stands far below its 30-day high, the larger its 24-hour drop in multiples of its 30-day average absolute daily return, the larger its rise over the next 3 days. The deeper the coin stands below that high, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader buying plunges should do so mainly in coins already deep below their recent high, where the rebound is largest and covers the cost many times.
Why:
- F4: Over the next day a large 24-hour drop is followed by a rebound, and the rebound grows with the drop.
- F5: The rebound is concentrated in coins standing 30 % or more below their 30-day high.
