Idea: When a coin's hourly close first rises above its highest price of the prior 20 days, the price keeps rising from that hourly close to the same day's daily close. The further that close sits above the 20-day high, measured in multiples of the coin's average absolute daily return over the past 30 days, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Part of a daily breakout is made after the hour it first holds and before the daily close, so acting at that hour earns more than acting at the close.
