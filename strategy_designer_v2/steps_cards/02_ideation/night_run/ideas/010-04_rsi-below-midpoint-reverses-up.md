---
brief: 010_rsi-midline-regime-direction
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: The larger the share of the past 14 days that a coin's 14-day RSI has spent below its midpoint, the larger the coin's rise over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A long stretch below the midpoint means the crowd watching the indicator has already sold, and market makers absorbing that one-sided flow push price back up.
