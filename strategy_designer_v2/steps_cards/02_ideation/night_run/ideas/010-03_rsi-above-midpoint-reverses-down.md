---
brief: 010_rsi-midline-regime-direction
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: The larger the share of the past 14 days that a coin's 14-day RSI has spent above its midpoint, the larger the coin's fall over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A long stretch above the midpoint means the crowd watching the indicator has already bought, and market makers absorbing that one-sided flow push price back down.
