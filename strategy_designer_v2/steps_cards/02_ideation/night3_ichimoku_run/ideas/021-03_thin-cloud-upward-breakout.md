Idea: After a daily close that crosses up from below a coin's Ichimoku cloud to above it, the coin rises over the next 26 days, and the thinner the cloud at the cross, measured as its width relative to price, the larger the rise.
Consequence: An upward close through a thin cloud is followed by a larger move than one through a thick cloud, because a thin cloud means weak equilibrium and little resistance.
