Idea: When a coin closes above its Ichimoku cloud and the cloud is rising, its return over the next 26 days is positive, and larger the faster the cloud has risen over the past 26 days. The cloud is the band between the average of the 9-day and 26-day high-low midpoints and the 52-day high-low midpoint, plotted 26 days ahead.
Consequence: A coin trading above a rising cloud tends to keep rising.
