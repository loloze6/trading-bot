Idea: After a daily close that crosses down from above a coin's Ichimoku cloud to below it, the coin falls over the next 26 days, and the thinner the cloud at the cross, measured as its width relative to price, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A downward close through a thin cloud is followed by a larger move than one through a thick cloud, because a thin cloud offers little support.
