021-02_below-falling-cloud-continues-down: Closing below a falling Ichimoku cloud, a coin's 26-day forward return is more negative the faster the cloud fell over 26 days. [bar: 1d]

021-04_thin-cloud-downward-breakdown: After a close down through the Ichimoku cloud, the 26-day fall is larger the thinner the cloud relative to price. [bar: 1d]
