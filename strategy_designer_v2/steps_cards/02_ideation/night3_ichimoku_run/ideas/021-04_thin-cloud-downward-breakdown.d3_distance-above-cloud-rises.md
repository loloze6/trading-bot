Idea: A coin whose daily close stands above its Ichimoku cloud rises over the next 26 days, and the further the close stands above the cloud's middle as a share of the price, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trader would be long the coin over the following weeks when it trades well above its cloud, and more so the wider that gap.
Why:
- F6: On a 26-day grid over all bars, the side of the cloud predicts the next 26 days. Above the cloud the unusual move is +12.5 % (t 2.57, 59 cases) and the total move +19.5 %. Inside the cloud it is -12.9 % (t -2.96, 25 cases). Below the cloud it is -7.0 % (t -1.78, 59 cases).
- F7: The effect grows with the close's distance from the cloud's middle, but not past it on the downside. More than 11 % above the middle, the unusual move is +10.7 % (t 2.40, 36 cases) and the total +17.8 %. Between 17 % and 1 % below the middle, it is -13.7 % (t -4.16, 36 cases) and the total -6.2 %. More than 17 % below, it is only -4.2 % (t -0.72).
- F9: Above the cloud, a thick cloud is followed by the larger rise, the opposite of the brief's grading. With a width above 5.6 % of the price, the unusual move is +26.5 % (t 2.28, 20 cases); with a width below 2.4 %, it is +5.6 %.
- F10: The size is far from the cost. A round trip costs 0.19 %, while the moves the cloud separates are 6 % to 18 % of total move per 26 days (F6, F7). The cost does not cause the failure; the direction of the bet does.
