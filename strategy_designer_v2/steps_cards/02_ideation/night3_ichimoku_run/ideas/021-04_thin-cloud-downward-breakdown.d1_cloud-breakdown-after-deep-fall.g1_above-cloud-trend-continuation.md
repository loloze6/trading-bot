Idea: A coin closing above its Ichimoku cloud rises more than its normal drift over the next 26 days, and the larger its 26-day return at that close, the larger that rise.
Consequence: A trader would hold long a coin trading above its cloud in an established climb, and would refuse to bet on a fresh rise that has not yet carried the coin above its cloud.
Why:
- F1: A coin closing above its Ichimoku cloud (9/26/52, displaced 26 days) went on to an unusual 26-day move of +15.1 %, with t 3.05 over 59 non-overlapping cases. Its strength is 0.51 of the usual 26-day move, about ten times the 0.05 typical of real ideas. Its total move of +19.5 % is about 100 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade. All 6 coins were positive (BTC +4.4 % to XRP +35.0 %), but the gain sits in 2019 to 2021 and 2024 (+14 % to +41 %), while 2022, 2023 and 2025 were flat (-1.5 % to +0.6 %). The tables looked at about 40 angles, so a t of 3 is only moderately safe.
- F2: Among bars whose 26-day return is positive, those above the cloud were followed by a 26-day unusual move 18.2 % higher than those not above it (Welch t 2.53, 50 vs 27 cases). The cloud adds something beyond a plain 26-day trend, at least on the up side.
- F3: Inside the above-cloud cases, distance above the cloud does not grade the move. The slope on distance scaled by volatility is -0.054 (t -0.86). The terciles run +17.7 %, +16.1 %, +11.6 %, and a rising cloud gave a smaller move (+10.5 %, 41 cases) than a non-rising one (+25.7 %, 18 cases).
- F4: The 5-day return predicts the 26-day unusual move in the same direction. The correlation is 0.137 (t 2.86, 429 cases), positive in 6 of 6 coins, and rose from 0.002 at 5 days to 0.061 at 10 days. The 26-day return does the same: correlation 0.117 (t 2.44, 5 of 6 coins), with 26-day unusual moves by its quartile rising steadily from -2.3 % to +0.2 %, +2.2 % and +5.1 %. As a sign bet it is mostly a long-side effect (+3.1 % after rises, +0.7 % after falls), with strength 0.054.
