Idea: A coin trading above its Ichimoku cloud rises over the next 26 days, and the thicker the cloud as a share of the price, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A trend that holds above a thick cloud is a reason to stay long for weeks, the opposite of reading a thin cloud as the stronger signal.
Why:
- F2: The cloud's thinness carries nothing. The step-5 check gives a thin-minus-thick Welch t of -0.36 over 198 cases, with an unusual move of -0.19 % (strength -0.020), so neither the main measure nor the grading the forecast is sized by is in the data.
- F6: On a 26-day grid over all bars, the side of the cloud predicts the next 26 days. Above the cloud the unusual move is +12.5 % (t 2.57, 59 cases) and the total move +19.5 %. Inside the cloud it is -12.9 % (t -2.96, 25 cases). Below the cloud it is -7.0 % (t -1.78, 59 cases).
- F9: Above the cloud, a thick cloud is followed by the larger rise, the opposite of the brief's grading. With a width above 5.6 % of the price, the unusual move is +26.5 % (t 2.28, 20 cases); with a width below 2.4 %, it is +5.6 %.
