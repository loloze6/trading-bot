Idea: A coin's Saturday-Sunday move made on higher volume than its mean over the preceding five weekdays continues in the same direction over the next 48 hours when it extends the coin's own move over those five weekdays. The continuation is the larger the larger the weekend move, the heavier the weekend volume against the weekdays, and the larger the preceding five-day move in the same direction.
Consequence: Follow a heavy-volume weekend move into Monday and Tuesday only when it carries on the week's direction; one that turns against the week is a reason to stay out.
Why:
- F4: The continuation exists only when the heavy weekend move extends the coin's move of the five weekdays before it.
- F1: The Calmar fails (0.67 against 0.75) because of one late fall: the deepest fall of the book is −12.5 %, running 304 days from w22 to w24. Those three windows each lose (window sums −4.3 %, −3.9 %, −3.2 %). Their 51 weekend cases move −1.65 % in the bet's direction over 48h, against +1.18 % for the 344 cases of w1–w21, and 2025 alone loses −1.43 % per case.
- F2: The losses are concentrated in large bets on large weekend moves.
