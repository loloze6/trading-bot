031-01_extreme-rise-partly-reverses: The larger a coin's 90-day rise relative to its usual volatility, the larger its decline over the next 30 days. [bar: 1d]
031-02_extreme-fall-partly-rebounds: The larger a coin's 90-day fall relative to its usual volatility, the larger its rebound over the next 30 days. [bar: 1d]
031-03_fading-volume-after-rise-reverses: After an extreme 90-day rise, a larger 14-day volume drop versus the run's volume precedes a larger 30-day decline. [bar: 1d]
031-04_fading-volume-after-fall-rebounds: After an extreme 90-day fall, a larger 14-day volume drop versus the fall's volume precedes a larger 30-day rebound. [bar: 1d]
