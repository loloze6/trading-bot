Idea: After an unusually large 90-day fall, the more a coin's volume over the last 14 days has dropped below its average daily volume during the fall, the more its price rebounds over the next 30 days.
Consequence: Once forced selling and capitulation in an extreme fall are exhausted, the selling pressure fades and price drifts back up.
