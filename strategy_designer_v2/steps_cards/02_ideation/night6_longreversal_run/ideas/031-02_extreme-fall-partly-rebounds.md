Idea: The further a coin's 90-day return stands below its own usual 90-day move, measured against its own past volatility, the more its price rebounds over the next 30 days.
Consequence: An unusually large two-to-three-month fall was partly overreaction from capitulation and deleveraging, and it partly unwinds over the following weeks.
