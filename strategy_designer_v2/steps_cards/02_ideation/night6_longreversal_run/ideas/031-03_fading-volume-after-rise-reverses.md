Idea: After an unusually large 90-day rise, the more a coin's volume over the last 14 days has dropped below its average daily volume during the run, the more its price falls over the next 30 days.
Consequence: Once an extreme rise stops drawing new participants, the flow that held it fades and price drifts back.
