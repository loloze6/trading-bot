Idea: For a coin with a strong 3-day run down, the return over the 2 hours after each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is negative, and the stronger the 3-day fall, the larger that post-settlement decline.
Consequence: Shorts who closed to avoid funding reopen after the payment, so the timing-driven bounce reverses.
