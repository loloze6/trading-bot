Idea: For a coin with a strong 3-day run up, the return over the 2 hours before each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is negative, and the stronger the 3-day rise, the larger that pre-settlement decline.
Consequence: Before settlement, crowded longs close to avoid paying funding and basis traders adjust, so spot leans down against an uptrend.
