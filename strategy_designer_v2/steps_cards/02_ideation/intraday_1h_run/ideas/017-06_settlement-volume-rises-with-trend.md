Idea: A coin's volume in the hours around each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is raised compared with its own volume in other hours, and the larger the absolute 3-day return, the larger that volume rise.
Consequence: Crowded-side holders and basis traders close and reopen around the settlement clock time, adding volume that grows with how crowded one side is.
