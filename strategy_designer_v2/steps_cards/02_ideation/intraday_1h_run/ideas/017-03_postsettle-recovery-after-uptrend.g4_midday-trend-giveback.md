Idea: Over the 3 hours from 11:00 UTC, a coin moves against its own 3-day return, and the larger that 3-day return, the larger the move against it.
Consequence: Expect trending coins to give back part of their run during the European midday hours, and avoid adding to the trend then.
Why:
- F5: Later in the day the trend-signed returns flip. For the bars stamped 10:00, 12:00 and 13:00 UTC they are negative (-2.8, -3.3 and -3.6 bp, t -3.7, -3.5 and -3.8). For the bar stamped 22:00 UTC (the hour into 00:00) the trend-signed return is +4.0 bp (t 4.9).
