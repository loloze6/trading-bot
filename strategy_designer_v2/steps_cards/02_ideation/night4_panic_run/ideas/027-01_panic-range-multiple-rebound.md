Idea: When a coin falls over a 24-hour stretch and its high-low range over that stretch is a large multiple of its average 24-hour range over the past 30 days, it earns more than its usual return over the next 14 days, and the larger that range multiple, the higher the return.
Consequence: Forced selling in a panic leaves a discount that is earned back as calm buyers return, so more intense panics are followed by larger excess returns.
