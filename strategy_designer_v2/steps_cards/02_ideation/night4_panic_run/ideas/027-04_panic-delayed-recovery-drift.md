Idea: After a 24-hour panic stretch, the coin's return from one day after the stretch ends to 14 days after it is higher than usual, and the more extreme the panic, the higher this later return.
Consequence: Once the forced selling stops, the price drifts back up over days as calmer buyers come back, rather than only bouncing at once.
