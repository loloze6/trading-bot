Idea: The higher a coin's daily close sits within its own 20-day high-to-low range, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days. A close at the top of the range is followed by the largest rise, and a close at the bottom by the largest fall.
Consequence: A close's place in its recent range marks buying or selling by trend followers and chasers that has not yet finished, so the coin drifts the way of that place, graded through the whole range.
