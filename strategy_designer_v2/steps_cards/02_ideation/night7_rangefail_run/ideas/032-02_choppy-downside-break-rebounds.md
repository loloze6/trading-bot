Idea: In a choppy market, when a coin's daily close is below the lowest low of its prior 20 days, its price rises over the next 5 days, and the further the close sits below that low, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Downward breaks of the recent range in a choppy market fail and give back more of the break the further they overshoot, because stops and chasing sell orders are spent.
