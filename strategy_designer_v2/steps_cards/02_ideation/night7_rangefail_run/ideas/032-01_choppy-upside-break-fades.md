Idea: In a choppy market, when a coin's daily close is above the highest high of its prior 20 days, its price falls over the next 5 days, and the further the close sits above that high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: Upward breaks of the recent range in a choppy market fail and give back more of the break the further they overshoot, because stops and chasing buy orders are spent.
