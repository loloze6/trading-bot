Idea: In a rising market, a daily close above the prior 20-day high rises over the next 5 days, more the more of its own daily swings over the last 20 days the close stands above that high.
Consequence: In a rising market, a close above the recent high draws trend followers, breakout buyers, late buyers and short covering over days, so the rise grows with the distance above the high.
