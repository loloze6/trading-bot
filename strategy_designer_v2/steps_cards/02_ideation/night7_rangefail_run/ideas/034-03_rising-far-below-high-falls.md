Idea: In a rising market, a coin whose daily close has slipped below its 20-day high falls over the next 5 days, more the more of its own daily swings over the last 20 days it stands below that high.
Consequence: In a rising market, a coin far below its high has a failed rally: holders near the top sell into recovery and chasing money goes elsewhere, so it drifts lower.
