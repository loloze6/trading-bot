Idea: The more of its own daily swings (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 20 days) a coin's one-day fall spans, the more the coin rises over the next day.
Consequence: A one-day fall that is large in the coin's own swing units is driven by urgent sellers who push the price below value, so the next day pays the discount back, more the larger the fall.
