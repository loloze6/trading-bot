Idea: In a rising market, the closer a coin's daily close sits below its 20-day high, measured in units of its own daily swing over the last 20 days (the standard deviation of its daily returns), the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In a rising market, the rise over the following days grows steadily as the close nears the recent high, because chasing money hunts for visibly winning coins.
