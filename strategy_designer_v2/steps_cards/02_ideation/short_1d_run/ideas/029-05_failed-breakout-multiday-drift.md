Idea: After a close back below the old 20-day high within 3 days of a breakout above it, the coin's decline continues across the following days, and the deeper the re-entry close sits below the old high, the larger the fall from day 2 to day 10 after the failure.
Consequence: The removal of dip buyers and the addition of forced sellers make the downward drift last for days rather than hours.
