Idea: When a coin's daily close moves above its prior 20-day high and then, within the next 3 days, closes back below that old high, the coin falls over the next 5 days, and the deeper that close sits below the old high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A failed breakout is followed by further decline because trapped breakout buyers' stops and margin calls turn into forced selling.
