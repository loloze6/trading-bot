Idea: After a daily close above the prior 20-day high is followed within 3 days by a close back below that high, the coin falls over the next 5 days, and the higher the breakout day's volume was against its 20-day average volume, the larger the fall.
Consequence: The more breakout buyers and leveraged longs the break drew in, the more trapped holders there are to sell when it fails.
