Idea: When a coin closes above its prior 20-day high, reaches further above it within the next 3 days, and then closes back below the old high, the coin falls over the next 5 days, and the larger the peak excursion above the old high was, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A bigger push above the watched high draws in more late buyers at worse prices, whose losses and forced exits drive more selling once the break fails.
