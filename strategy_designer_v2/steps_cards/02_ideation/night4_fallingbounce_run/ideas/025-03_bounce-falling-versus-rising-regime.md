Idea: In one coin, the 5-day return after a one-day rise of several usual daily moves is lower when its 90-day return is negative than when its 90-day return is positive, and the gap widens as the rise gets larger.
Consequence: A sharp rise meets more supply in a falling market than in a rising one, so it is followed by weaker moves than the same rise in a rising market.
