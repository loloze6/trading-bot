Idea: In a coin whose 90-day return is negative and that has just had a one-day rise of several usual daily moves, the deeper its 90-day fall, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: The longer and deeper the fall, the more holders are under water and waiting to sell into a bounce, so the more of the bounce is given back.
