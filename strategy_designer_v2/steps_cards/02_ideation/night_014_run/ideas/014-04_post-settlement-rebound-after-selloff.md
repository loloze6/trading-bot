---
brief: 014_funding-settlement-hour-pressure
---
Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 3 days and then fallen in the 2 hours before a funding settlement time, its return over the 2 hours after settlement is positive. The larger the pre-settlement fall, the larger that post-settlement gain.
Consequence: Once the funding payment is made, selling pressure from longs lifts and price partly drifts back up.
