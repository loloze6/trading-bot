---
brief: 014_funding-settlement-hour-pressure
---
Idea: A coin's trading volume in the two hours either side of each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) runs higher than its volume in the other hours of the same day. The excess is larger the larger the absolute size of the coin's 3-day return going into that settlement.
Consequence: Leveraged traders and basis traders close positions at the same scheduled moment, so trading clusters around settlement, more so when the move behind the crowding is stronger.
