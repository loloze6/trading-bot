---
brief: 014_funding-settlement-hour-pressure
---
Idea: After a coin has fallen over the past 3 days and then risen in the 2 hours before a funding settlement time, its return over the 2 hours after settlement is negative. The larger the pre-settlement rise, the larger that post-settlement decline.
Consequence: Once the funding payment is made, short-covering pressure lifts and price partly drifts back down: the mirror case.
