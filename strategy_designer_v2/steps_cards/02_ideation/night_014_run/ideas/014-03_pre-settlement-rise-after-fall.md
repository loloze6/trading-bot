brief: 014_funding-settlement-hour-pressure
---
Idea: After a coin has fallen over the past 3 days, its return over the 2 hours before each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is positive. The larger the 3-day fall, the larger that pre-settlement gain.
Consequence: Crowded shorts facing negative funding cover before settlement, which pushes spot up: the mirror of the positive-funding case.
