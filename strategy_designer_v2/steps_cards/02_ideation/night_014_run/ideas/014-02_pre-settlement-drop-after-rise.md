---
brief: 014_funding-settlement-hour-pressure
---
Idea: After a coin has risen over the past 3 days, its return over the 2 hours before each funding settlement time (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC) is negative. The larger the 3-day rise, the larger that pre-settlement decline.
Consequence: Crowded longs facing positive funding, and basis traders unwinding, sell into settlement, and the selling spills into spot.
