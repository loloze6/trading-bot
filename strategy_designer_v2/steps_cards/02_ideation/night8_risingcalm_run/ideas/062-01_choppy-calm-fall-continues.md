Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a coin whose 5-day return is more negative, measured against its 30-day daily swing, falls further over the next 5 days. The larger the swing-scaled 5-day fall, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: Volatility-sized holders cut exposure in pieces after a fall that is large for the coin's quiet swing, and with no trend crowd or range extreme to absorb it, the selling carries on.
