Idea: In an agitated market, a one-day fall that is larger against the coin's 30-day swing, and on volume that is heavier against its 20-day average volume, is followed by a larger rise over the next day, the two compounding so that a large fall on heavy volume brings the largest rebound.
Consequence: Forced, price-insensitive selling on heavy volume leaves a discount that is repaid once it ends, wider when absorbers' risk capacity is stretched.
