profile: rising calm
---
Idea: In a rising, calm market, the further a coin's 3-day return has fallen below zero, measured against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In a steady, low-swing uptrend, waiting buyers absorb a few-day pullback, and a deeper pullback relative to the coin's usual swing draws a larger recovery.
