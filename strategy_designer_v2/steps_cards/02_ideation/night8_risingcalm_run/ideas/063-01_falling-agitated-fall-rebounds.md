Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the deeper a coin's 5-day fall measured in its own 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next 10 days.
Consequence: Forced selling in a falling, agitated market pushes the price below what unforced holders accept, and the overshoot is regained gradually as absorbing capital returns.
