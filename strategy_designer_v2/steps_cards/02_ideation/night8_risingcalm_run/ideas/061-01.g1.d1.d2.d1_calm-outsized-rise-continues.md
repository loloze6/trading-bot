Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the further a coin's one-day rise exceeds the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the further it keeps rising over the next 5 days. Rises within the coin's usual daily swing carry no follow-through; the move grows with how many usual swings the day covered.
Consequence: A trader buys only calm-market up days that are large against the coin's own swing and sizes up with the excess, refusing the ordinary up days that made this book lose.
Why:
- F1: Inside the choppy-calm profile, the 5-day move after an up day is not graded the way the forecast assumes. On the 5-day grid, the excess return is -0.88 % after rises of 0 to 0.5 swing units (n 43), -2.67 % after 0.5 to 1 unit (n 24, t -1.64), +8.72 % after 1 to 1.5 units (n 11) and +3.72 % above 1.5 units (n 11, t 1.98). Rises above one 30-day swing earn +6.22 % in excess (total +6.92 %, n 22, t 2.17, strength 0.50). Rises below one swing lose. This is why the idea's size per case is -0.43 % (t -1.15, 651 cases) and its total move, +0.19 %, only equals the 0.19 % cost of a trade.
- F2: The book trades mostly the losing small rises. The forecast is each component standardised over its past 60 values and clipped at 0, so any ordinary up day gets a position. The median entry day was a rise of only 0.76 swing units, and just 32 % of the 98 entries rose more than one swing. On the grid, the bottom half of positive forecasts loses -1.80 % in excess over 5 days (n 48, t -1.50) and -5.36 % over 10 days. The top half gains +2.80 % (t 1.61) and +5.18 % over 10 days (t 1.92). The book's position-day mean is therefore 0.030 % a day against the 0.05 % required, and its Sharpe is 0.40 below holding.
- F4: The book's falls come from these frequent weak trades, not from one bad window. The deepest fall of the chained daily book is -15.2 %, with its trough in w10. Falls of -14.0 % in w14, -14.4 % in w15 and -12.1 % in w9 show the same bleed spread across windows. Against a yearly return of +1.3 %, this gives a Calmar of 0.04. Large in-profile rises paid on every coin: their mean 5-day excess was +3.4 % to +19.1 % on all six coins (BTC +5.7 % n 4, XMR +3.4 % n 5, XRP +19.1 % n 3). XMR's trades still lost -1.46 % net on average, so its losses come from the small-rise entries.
