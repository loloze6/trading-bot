Idea: In any market, the further a coin's close falls below its highest close of the last 5 days, measured in units of its own 30-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A pullback that is deep for the coin's usual swing pushes more peak-anchored holders past their drawdown limits, and their selling arrives in turn over the following days.
