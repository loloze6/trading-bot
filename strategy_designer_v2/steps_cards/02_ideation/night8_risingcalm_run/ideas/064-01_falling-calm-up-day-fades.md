Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise measured in units of its own 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, the larger the fall in its close over the next 3 days.
Consequence: In a calm decline, a large up day is a single burst of buying that patient sellers and liquidity providers lean against, so price gives it back in proportion to its size.
