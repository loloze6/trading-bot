Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the deeper a coin's close sits below its highest close of the past 14 days, measured against its 30-day daily swing, the larger the rise that follows over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Distance below the two-week high reverses the same way: the deeper the coin sits below it, the more it recovers.
