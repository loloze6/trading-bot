Idea: In any market, the further a coin's close rises above its highest close of the previous 5 days, measured in units of its own 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A coin at a fresh few-day high has forced no peak-anchored holder out and leaves none with a drawdown to cut, so it keeps rising.
