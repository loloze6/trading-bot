profile: rising calm

Idea: In a rising, calm market, the further a coin's close sits below its highest close of the past 5 days, measured against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Waiting buyers anchor on the recent level, so the further price drops below that recent high, the more of them buy and the larger the climb back toward it.
