Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the further a coin's close sits below its highest close of the past 10 days, measured in its own 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next 10 days.
Consequence: Waves of forced selling over several days leave the price below its unforced value, and the gap is partly regained over the following week or two.
