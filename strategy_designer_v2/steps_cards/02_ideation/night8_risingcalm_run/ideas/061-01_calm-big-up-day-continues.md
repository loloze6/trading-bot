Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In calm markets, a day large against the coin's usual swing starts a move that continues in its own direction, upward after an up day, and more so the larger the day.
