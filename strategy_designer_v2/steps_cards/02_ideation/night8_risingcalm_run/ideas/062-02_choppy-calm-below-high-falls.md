Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a coin whose close sits further below its highest close of the past 5 days, measured against its 30-day daily swing, falls further over the next 5 days. The deeper the swing-scaled drop below that high, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: The same gradual, forced cutting of exposure, seen as a drop from the recent high rather than as a 5-day return.
