Idea: When a coin's price rises from Saturday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC, it falls over the following two days (Monday and Tuesday). The larger the weekend gain, the larger the fall.
Consequence: An upward move made in thin weekend trading is partly reversed once full liquidity returns early in the week.
