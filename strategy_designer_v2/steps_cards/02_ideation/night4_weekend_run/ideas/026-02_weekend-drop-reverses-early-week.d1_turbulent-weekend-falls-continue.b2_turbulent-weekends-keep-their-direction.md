Idea: When a coin's hourly moves over the weekend (Saturday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC) were more volatile than over its previous week, the coin keeps moving in the direction of its weekend return over the next 2 days. The continuation is larger the higher the weekend's volatility compared with the previous week.
Consequence: A trader should refuse to fade a weekend move made on turbulent trading, and may follow its direction over Monday and Tuesday, more so the more turbulent the weekend was.
