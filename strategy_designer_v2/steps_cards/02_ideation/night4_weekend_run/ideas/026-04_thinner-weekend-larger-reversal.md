Idea: The lower a coin's weekend volume is compared with its volume over the preceding five weekdays, the larger the share of its Saturday-Sunday move that is reversed over Monday and Tuesday.
Consequence: The thinner the weekend liquidity, the further a modest order pushes the price, and the more of the move is undone when the desks return.
