Idea: When a coin's price falls from Saturday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC, it rises over the following two days (Monday and Tuesday). The larger the weekend loss, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A downward move made in thin weekend trading is partly reversed once full liquidity returns early in the week.
