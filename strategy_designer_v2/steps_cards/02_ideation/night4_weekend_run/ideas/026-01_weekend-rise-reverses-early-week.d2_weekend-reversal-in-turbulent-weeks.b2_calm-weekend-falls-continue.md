Idea: When a coin's price falls from Saturday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC while the coin's 30-day hourly volatility is low against its own last 6 months, it keeps falling over the next two days (Monday and Tuesday). The larger the weekend fall, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: In calm weeks a trader would follow a weekend fall into Monday and Tuesday rather than fade it.
Why:
- F7: In calm weeks a weekend fall carries on early in the week. When the recorded regime is low volatility, weekend falls keep falling, with a reversal of -1.01 % (t -3.74, n 601) over the next 48 hours. Weekend rises keep rising, with a reversal of -0.41 % (t -1.55, n 742).
