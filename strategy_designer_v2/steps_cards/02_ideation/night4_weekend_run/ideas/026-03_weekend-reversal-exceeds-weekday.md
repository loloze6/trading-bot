Idea: A coin's 48-hour return over Saturday and Sunday is followed by a larger opposite move over the next two days than a 48-hour return of the same size that ends on any other weekday.
Consequence: The reversal comes from weekend thinness, so weekend moves are reversed more than same-size weekday moves.
