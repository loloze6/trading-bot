Idea: A coin whose 3-day return is large against its usual daily range (the average of its daily high-to-low range over the past 30 days) keeps rising over the next 7 days, and the larger that range-scaled 3-day rise, the larger the further gain.
Consequence: A rally that is unusual against the coin's usual daily range draws late and forced buyers, so it continues over the following week.
