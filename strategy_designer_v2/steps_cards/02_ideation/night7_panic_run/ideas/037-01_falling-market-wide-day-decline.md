Idea: In a falling market, a day whose high-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns is followed by a decline over the next 3 days. The wider that day's range in units of this swing, the larger the decline.
Consequence: Delayed volatility-driven deleveraging after a stress day pushes price down in a falling market with few patient buyers.
