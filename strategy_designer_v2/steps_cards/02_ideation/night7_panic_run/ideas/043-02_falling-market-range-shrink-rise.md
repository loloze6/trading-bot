Idea: In a falling market, a coin rises over the next 3 days, and the rise is larger the narrower the day's high-low range is against its average daily high-low range over the last 30 days.
Consequence: A day that barely moves in a downtrend marks selling exhaustion, so a short rise follows, larger the quieter the day.
