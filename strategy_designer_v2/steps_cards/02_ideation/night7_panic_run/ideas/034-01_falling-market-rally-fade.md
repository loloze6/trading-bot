Idea: In a falling market, a coin's up day is followed by a decline over the next 3 days, and the decline is larger the larger that day's rise is in units of the coin's 30-day daily swing.
Consequence: A sharp rally inside a downtrend is mostly forced, unsupported buying, so it is given back, and the more so the larger it is against the coin's usual swing.
