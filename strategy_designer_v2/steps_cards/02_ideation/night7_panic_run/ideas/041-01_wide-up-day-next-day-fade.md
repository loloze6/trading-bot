Idea: After an up-closing day, a coin falls over the next day, more so the wider that day's high-to-low range in units of its 30-day daily-return swing.
Consequence: A wide-range up day is an overshoot from urgent buying that is partly undone the next day, and a wider range means a larger give-back.
