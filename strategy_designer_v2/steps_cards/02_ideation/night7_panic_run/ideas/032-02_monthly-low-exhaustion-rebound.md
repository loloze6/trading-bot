Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin closes below the lowest close of its previous 30 days, it rebounds over the next 3 days, and the further the close sits below that prior 30-day low, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A fresh monthly low in a falling, agitated coin marks the end of forced selling, and price snaps back once that pressure stops.
