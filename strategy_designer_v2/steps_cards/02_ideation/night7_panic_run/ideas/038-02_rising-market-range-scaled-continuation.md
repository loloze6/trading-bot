Idea: In a rising market, a coin keeps rising over the next 3 days, more so the larger its 3-day return measured in units of its average daily high-low range over the last 30 days.
Consequence: In an uptrend, a rise that is unusual for the coin's own usual daily range stands out to traders whose buying is spread over the next days, so the price keeps going.
