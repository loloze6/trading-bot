Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin that has fallen over the last 3 days keeps declining over the next 3 days, more so the larger that 3-day fall in units of its 30-day average daily range.
Consequence: In calm downtrends, large scaled falls are orderly exits spread over days with little patient demand, so the fall continues in proportion to its size.
