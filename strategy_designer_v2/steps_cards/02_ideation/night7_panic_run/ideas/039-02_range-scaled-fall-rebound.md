Idea: After a down day, a coin rises over the next day, more so the larger that day's fall measured in units of its 30-day average daily high-to-low range.
Consequence: An unusually large one-day fall for the coin's usual swing is partly made up the next day, and the larger the fall, the larger the rebound.
