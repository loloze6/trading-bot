Idea: In a rising market, a coin keeps rising over the next 3 days, more so the larger its 3-day return measured in units of the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days.
Consequence: In an uptrend, a rise that is large for the coin's usual swing draws chasers, trend followers and buy-backs spread over the following days, while sellers are scarce, so it continues.
