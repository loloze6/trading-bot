Idea: In a rising market, a coin rises over the next 3 days, more so the lower its average daily high-to-low range over the last 14 days compared with its average daily range over the 90 days before them.
Consequence: Quieting swings, seen in narrowing daily ranges, let risk-sized holders carry more of the coin and mark orderly accumulation, so the uptrend's next rise is larger.
