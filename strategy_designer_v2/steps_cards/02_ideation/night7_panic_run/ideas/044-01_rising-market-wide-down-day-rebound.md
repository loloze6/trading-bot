Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes down rises over the next 3 days, and the rise is larger the wider that day's high-low range is in units of the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
Consequence: In an uptrend, a wide down-closing day is a forced-selling shakeout that buyers absorb, so price recovers the overshoot, and more so the deeper the cascade.
