Idea: In a rising market, after a Thursday or a Friday close, a coin rises over the next day, more so the larger its 60-day return.
Consequence: Late-week flow, thinned of market makers and led by retail buyers chasing the trend, lifts Friday and Saturday more the steeper the two-month rise.
