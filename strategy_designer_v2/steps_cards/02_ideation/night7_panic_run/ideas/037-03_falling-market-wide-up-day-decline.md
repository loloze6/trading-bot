Idea: In a falling market, a day that closes up but has a high-low range wide against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns is followed by a decline over the next 3 days. The wider that day's range in units of this swing, the larger the decline.
Consequence: The decline answers the range, not the close: even wide days that close up are followed by delayed forced selling in a falling market.
