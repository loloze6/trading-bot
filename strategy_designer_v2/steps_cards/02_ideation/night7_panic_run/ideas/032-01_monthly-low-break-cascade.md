Idea: In a falling, agitated market, when a coin closes below the lowest close of its previous 30 days, it keeps falling over the next 3 days, and the further the close sits below that prior 30-day low, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A fresh monthly low in a falling, agitated coin triggers stops and margin calls that feed a self-reinforcing selling cascade.
