Idea: In a falling market, a day whose high-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day average daily high-low range is followed by a decline over the next 3 days. The wider that day's range relative to this average, the larger the decline.
Consequence: The jump in range, seen against the coin's usual daily range, triggers delayed risk cuts that push price down in a falling market.
