Idea: In a falling market, after a down day, a coin rises over the next day, and the larger that day's fall in units of its 30-day daily-return swing, the larger the next day's rise.
Consequence: An outsized fall in a downtrend is an overshoot driven by forced selling into thin books, and it is partly repaid the next day.
