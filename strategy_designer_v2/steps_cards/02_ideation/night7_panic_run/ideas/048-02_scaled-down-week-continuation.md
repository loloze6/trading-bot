Idea: After a week in which a coin fell, it keeps falling over the next 3 days, more so the larger its 7-day fall in units of its 30-day standard deviation of daily returns.
Consequence: A down week that is unusual against the coin's own recent swing draws trend-following selling and triggers stops on the long side, so the fall continues over the following days.
