Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a down day is followed by a rise over the next 3 days, and the larger the day's fall in units of its 30-day daily-return swing, the larger that rise.
Consequence: In this profile, an unusually large fall against the coin's usual swing is partly undone, and more so the more unusual it was.
