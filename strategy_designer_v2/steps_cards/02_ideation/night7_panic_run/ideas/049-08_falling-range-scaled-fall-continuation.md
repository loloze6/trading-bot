Idea: In a falling market, after a five-day fall, a coin falls further over the next 3 days, more so the larger its 5-day fall in units of its 30-day average daily range.
Consequence: Outside rising trends, a five-day fall that is large for the coin's own swing, measured by its daily ranges, keeps going as slow participants sell late.
