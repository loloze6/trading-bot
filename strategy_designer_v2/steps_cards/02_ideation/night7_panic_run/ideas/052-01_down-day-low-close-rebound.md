Idea: After a down day, a coin rises over the next day, more so the nearer that day's close sits to its low, as a share of the day's high-low range.
Consequence: A down day's rebound is still owed when it closes near its low, because no buyers absorbed the forced selling before the close, so the next day pays it out.
