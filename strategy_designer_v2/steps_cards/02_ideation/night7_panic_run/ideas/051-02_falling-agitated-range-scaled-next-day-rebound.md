Idea: In a falling, agitated market, after a down day a coin rises over the next day, and the rise is larger the larger that day's fall is in units of the coin's 30-day average daily high-low range.
Consequence: Forced selling in a falling, agitated market overshoots in proportion to its size against the coin's usual daily range, and the overshoot is paid back mostly the next day.
