Idea: In a falling market, after an up day, a coin falls over the next day, and the larger that day's rise in units of its 30-day average daily range, the larger the next day's fall.
Consequence: An outsized rise in a downtrend, measured against the usual daily range, is an overshoot that is partly given back the next day.
