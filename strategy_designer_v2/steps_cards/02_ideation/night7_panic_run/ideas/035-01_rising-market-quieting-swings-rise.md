Idea: In a rising market, a coin rises over the next 3 days, more so the lower the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 14 days compared with the standard deviation over the 90 days before them.
Consequence: Risk-sized holders add to a quieting coin in an uptrend, and a calm advance shows orderly accumulation, so quieting swings bring a larger coming rise.
