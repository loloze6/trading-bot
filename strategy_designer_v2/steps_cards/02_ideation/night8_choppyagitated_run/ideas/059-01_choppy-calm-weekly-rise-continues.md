Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the larger a coin's rise over the past 7 days against its usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger its further rise over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A week's rise that is large for a quiet, trendless coin draws trapped range sellers and late buyers, so the rise continues and grows with its scaled size.
