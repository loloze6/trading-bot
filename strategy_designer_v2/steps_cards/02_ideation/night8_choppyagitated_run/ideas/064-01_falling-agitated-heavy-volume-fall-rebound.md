Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a down day on heavier volume than the coin's own 20-day average volume is followed by a larger rise the next day, and the heavier that day's volume against the average, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Heavy-volume falls in a falling, agitated market are forced selling sold at a discount, and the discount is repaid within the next day.
