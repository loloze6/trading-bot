Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin's one-day rise that is larger against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days is followed by a larger fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A sharp up day in an agitated decline is a forced short squeeze that overshoots, and the overshoot is given back once covering ends, more so the larger the squeeze.
