brief: 058_choppy-agitated-weekly-continuation
profile: choppy agitated
---
Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, a coin whose 7-day return is a rise rises further over the next 7 days, and the larger that 7-day rise is against the coin's usual daily swing over the past 30 days, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: An upward week-long push in a trendless, high-swing market forces range-faders and leveraged shorts out in stages while absorbers stand back, so the rise carries on.
