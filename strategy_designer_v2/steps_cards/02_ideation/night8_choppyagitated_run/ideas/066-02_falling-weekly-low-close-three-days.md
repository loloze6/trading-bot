Idea: In a falling market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the lowest close of its last 7 days, within the range between its lowest and highest closes of those 7 days, the larger its fall over the next 3 days.
Consequence: The selling set off by a fresh short-term low starts in the first days after it, because the holders it pushes past their limit sell before demand returns.
