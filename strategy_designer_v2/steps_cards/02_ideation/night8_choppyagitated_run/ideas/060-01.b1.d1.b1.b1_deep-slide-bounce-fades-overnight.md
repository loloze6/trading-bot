Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin's one-day rise that comes after a fall over its last 5 days is followed by a fall over the next day that grows with the depth of that 5-day fall, measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days.
Consequence: A trader in an agitated decline should short the bounce harder the deeper the slide it interrupts, and hold it about one day, rather than size the short by how large the bounce itself is.
Why:
- F1: The test's main measure is on the predicted side but indistinguishable from zero (slope -0.00036, day-clustered t -0.65, 1598 bars on 535 days), while the level of the giveback is real: the shorts earn +0.65 % against holding per case (t 2.04, 421 days), and in folds B and C +1.04 % the next day (t 1.92, 430 bars); the effect is a flat level, not a graded one.
- F2: The giveback does not grow with the size of the rise, and fails in the largest rises: by quartile of the scaled rise the next-day short excess is +1.36 %, +1.11 %, +2.17 % and then -0.59 % (rise about 1.48 sd, 95 cases); by bin, rises of 0 to 0.75 sd give +1.15 % to +1.98 % and rises of 1.0 to 1.5 sd give -1.19 % (38 cases); the Spearman correlation of the rise with the next-day excess is -0.01; the rise slope has a t of -1.05 (rank form t -0.64), on the wrong side of the idea's grading.
- F3: The depth of the prior 5-day fall carries what grading there is, on the predicted side: the deeper half of the 5-day falls gives +1.68 % next day (t 1.70, 189 cases on 86 days) against +0.35 % for the shallower half (t 0.64, 188 cases on 100 days); by tercile the gives are +1.42 %, +1.34 % and +0.54 %; the deepest bin (below -2 usual moves) gives +4.62 % (21 cases); the depth slope is t 0.93.
- F4: The depth effect holds in every year shown: the deeper half gives +1.88 % in 2018, +1.16 % in 2019, +2.54 % in 2020 and +1.17 % in 2021 (69, 23, 35 and 62 cases).
- F5: Mixing the two grades blurs the forecast: the cell with a large rise after a shallow fall gives -0.80 % (74 cases), while a large rise after a deep fall gives +1.86 % (114 cases); the strongest forecasts (quartile mean -14.8) give only +0.42 % against +1.46 % to +1.51 % for the middle quartiles; ranked on the forecast, the slope's t is -0.02.
- F6: The test's one-day horizon is where the effect is largest: the short excess decays from +1.04 % (t 1.92) at 1 day to +0.79 % (t 1.05) at 2 days and +0.57 % (t 0.73) at 3 days; the deep half's one-day give is 7.6 times the 0.22 % cost of a trade.
- F7: No more data is available on this idea's bar: the test already used all 19 coins on daily bars over the four folds, so the answer is the idea amended to where the effect shows, the depth of the prior fall, without the rise's size, which grades the wrong way.
