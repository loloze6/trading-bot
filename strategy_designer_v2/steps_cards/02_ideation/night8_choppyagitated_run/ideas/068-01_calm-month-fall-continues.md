Idea: In a calm market, a larger fall in a coin's 30-day return is followed by a larger fall over the next 7 days.
Consequence: An orderly monthly fall with quiet swings is distribution by sellers who still hold inventory and meets no capitulation buying, so the deeper the month's fall, the deeper the following week's fall.
