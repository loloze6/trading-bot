Idea: In a choppy, calm market, a coin's one-day rise scaled by its 30-day daily swing is followed by a fall the next day, and the larger the scaled rise, the larger that fall.
Consequence: The giveback starts at once, because the urgency behind the jump fades and the liquidity providers who were lifted unload their inventory into the next session.
