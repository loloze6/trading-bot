---
brief: 006_range-compression-precedes-expansion
---
Idea: The longer a coin has stayed inside a tight band, measured by how many of the past 60 days its closes have stayed within a range that is narrow against its own 100-day range, the larger its absolute price change over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A longer quiet period lets more stops and leverage build up at the band's edges, so the eventual move is larger.
