---
brief: 006_range-compression-precedes-expansion
---
Idea: The more compressed a coin's 20-day range is against its 100-day range, the further its realised volatility over the next 7 days rises above its own 100-day average volatility, not just back up to that average.
Consequence: What follows compression is an overshoot of volatility, not only a return of ordinary volatility to its average.
