---
brief: 006_range-compression-precedes-expansion
---
Idea: When a coin closes below the low of a tight 20-day band, the narrower that band was against the coin's 100-day range, the further price keeps falling beyond the band's bottom over the next 3 days.
Consequence: A downward break trips clustered stops and liquidations into thin books, so the move runs past the band's edge.
