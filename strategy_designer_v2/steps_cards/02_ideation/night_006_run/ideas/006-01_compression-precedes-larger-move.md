---
brief: 006_range-compression-precedes-expansion
---
Idea: The narrower a coin's average daily high-low range over the past 20 days, compared with its own range over the past 100 days, and the more its 20-day volume has fallen against its 100-day volume, the larger its absolute price change over the next 5 days.
Consequence: After a quiet, compressed stretch, the coin makes a larger move than usual, in either direction.
