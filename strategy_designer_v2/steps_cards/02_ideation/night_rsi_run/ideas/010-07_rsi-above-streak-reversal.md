---
brief: 010_rsi-midline-regime-direction
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: The longer a coin's 14-day RSI has stayed above 50 without a break, measured in days up to the current close, the lower the coin's return over the next 14 days.
Consequence: The longer buyers have crowded in, the more positioning is exhausted, so the move reverses downward.
