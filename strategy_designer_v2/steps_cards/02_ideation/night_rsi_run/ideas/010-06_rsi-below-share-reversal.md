brief: 010_rsi-midline-regime-direction
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
---
Idea: The larger the share of the past 30 days that a coin's 14-day RSI has spent below 50, the higher the coin's return over the next 14 days.
Consequence: Crowded selling on a widely watched signal, together with market makers absorbing one-sided flow, makes the move reverse after sellers have dominated.
