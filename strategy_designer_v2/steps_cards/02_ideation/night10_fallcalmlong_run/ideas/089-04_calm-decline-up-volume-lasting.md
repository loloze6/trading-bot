Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger the share of a coin's last 20 days' volume traded on its up days, the more the coin rises over the next 10 days, with the rise building over those days rather than fading.
Consequence: The market adjusts slowly to the buyer's footprint because the trend still looks down on price alone, so the rise builds over the following days.
