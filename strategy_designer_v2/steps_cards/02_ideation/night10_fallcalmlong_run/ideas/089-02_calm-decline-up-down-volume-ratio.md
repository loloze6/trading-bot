Idea: In a falling, calm market, the higher a coin's average up-day volume over the last 20 days compared with its average down-day volume over the same days, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Up days carry the accumulating buyer's volume while down days grow lighter, so heavier up days compared with down days come before a rise.
