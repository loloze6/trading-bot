Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's 5-day return divided by the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days predicts its rise over the next 5 days: the larger this scaled push upward, the larger the rise that follows, and the weaker or more negative it is, the smaller the rise.
Consequence: A multi-day rise that is large for the coin's usual swing shows a buyer still working through steady supply, so the rise carries on for a few more days.
