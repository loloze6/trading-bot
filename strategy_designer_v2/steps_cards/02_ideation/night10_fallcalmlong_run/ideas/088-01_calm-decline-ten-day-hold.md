Idea: In a falling, calm market, the higher a coin's 10-day return, the larger its rise over the next 5 days, and a coin whose 10-day return is still negative rises least.
Consequence: When the slow sellers of a calm decline run out, the coin stops falling and waiting buyers step in gradually, so a held-up coin rises over the following days.
