Idea: In a falling, calm market, the more a coin's average down-day volume over the last 10 days has fallen below its average down-day volume over the 20 days before, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: The remaining sellers thin out, so down days grow lighter before the buyer's continued demand lifts the price.
