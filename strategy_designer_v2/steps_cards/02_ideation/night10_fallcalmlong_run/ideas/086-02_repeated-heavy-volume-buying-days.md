Idea: In a falling, calm market, the more of a coin's last 3 days that closed up on volume heavy against its 20-day average volume, and the heavier those volumes, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A large buyer works the order over several sessions, so heavy-volume buying that repeats across days shows demand that is still present and that late followers join.
