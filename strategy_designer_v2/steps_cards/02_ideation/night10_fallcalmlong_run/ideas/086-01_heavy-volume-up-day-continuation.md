Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day that closes up on volume heavy against the coin's 20-day average volume is followed by a rise over the next 5 days. The heavier that day's volume relative to its 20-day average, the larger the 5-day rise.
Consequence: A heavy-volume up day in a calm decline marks a deliberate buyer absorbing unforced supply, so the price keeps rising afterwards, more so the heavier the volume.
