Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger the share of up days among a coin's last 10 days, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A coin whose daily slide has stopped, with up days replacing down days, has used up its unhurried sellers, and buyers then lift it gradually.
