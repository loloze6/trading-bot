Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose 30-day return is positive keeps rising over the next 10 days, and the larger that 30-day rise is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the same 30 days, the larger the rise that follows.
Consequence: Monthly reviewers add to a coin whose month stood out as a real rise, and they add in pieces, so in a calm downtrend the repricing upward is still unfinished at the month's end and carries on.
