Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger the share of a coin's last 20 days' volume traded on its up days, the more the coin rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Volume leaning toward up days over recent weeks shows a buyer absorbing supply, and the price rises once the sellers' supply is spent.
