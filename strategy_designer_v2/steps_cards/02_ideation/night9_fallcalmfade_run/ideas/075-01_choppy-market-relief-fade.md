Idea: In a choppy market, the first up day after a run of down days is followed by a fall in the coin's price over the next five days. The deeper the run's total fall, measured in units of the coin's standard deviation of daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger that fall.
Consequence: In a rangebound market, holders caught underwater by a deep slide sell into the first bounce, and no trend followers absorb that supply, so the bounce is sold back over the following days, more after deeper slides.
