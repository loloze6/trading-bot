Idea: After a coin has closed up several days in a row, the first day that closes down is followed by a rise the next day, and the rise is larger the further the run of up days carried the price, measured in the coin's usual daily moves (its daily-return swing over the past 30 days).
Consequence: The first down day after an up run is profit taking absorbed by slower buyers, so the price lifts the next day, more after stronger runs.
