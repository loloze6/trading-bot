Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against its own daily swing over the past 20 days is followed by a fall over the next three days. The larger the rise in those swing units, the larger that fall.
Consequence: In a calm decline a sudden large up day is one burst of buying with no second wave behind it, so patient sellers resume, liquidity providers lean against it, and the price drifts back by more the bigger the burst.
