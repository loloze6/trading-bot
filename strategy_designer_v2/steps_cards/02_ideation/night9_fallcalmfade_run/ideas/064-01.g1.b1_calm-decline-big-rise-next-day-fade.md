Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against its 20-day daily swing is given back over the next day, and the larger the rise in those swing units beyond about one usual daily move, the larger the fall that follows.
Consequence: A trader in a calm decline should fade only the up days that are large against recent swings, for about a day, and leave the ordinary up days alone.
Why:
- F1: The test's main measure is essentially zero, not short of the bar: its t is 0.07 over 268 independent days (534 cases), and the size is an unusual move of -0.02 % per 3-day case (t -0.05 over 483 days, strength 0.001). The test already used all four folds, so more data would not settle it.
- F2: The fade is confined to the large rises. In the falling-calm profile, up days above one 20-day swing unit (z>1) were followed over the next day by a fall beyond drift of 1.11 % (t 2.25, 169 cases on 94 days). The ordinary rises (0<z≤1) were followed by 0.18 % (t 0.60, 587 cases on 290 days).
- F3: The same split holds over the plan's 3-day horizon, but less surely. Rises above one swing unit faded by 2.09 % (t 1.44, 27 days), while the ordinary rises gave -0.15 % (t -0.18, 104 days). The bin from 0.5 to 1 unit was negative (-0.48 %, t -0.36). The smallest and most common rises therefore dilute the plan's linear, no-dead-zone forecast toward zero.
- F4: The large-rise fade leans the predicted way on five of six coins over 3 days: ETH +2.92 %, LTC +3.51 %, XMR +0.80 %, XRP +1.83 % and ZEC +2.21 %. BTC is the exception at -2.42 % on 3 cases.
- F7: The fade is shorter than the plan's horizon. All up days in the profile gave 0.39 % over 1 day (t 1.44), 0.29 % over 3 days (t 0.38) and -0.41 % over 5 days (t -0.32). Large rises gave 1.11 % over 1 day (t 2.25) against 1.03 % over 2 days (t 1.54).
- F8: The idea's size cannot pay this backtest's costs. The 3-day total move per case is -0.41 %, which is -2.1 times the 0.19 % cost of a trade. Only a large-rise version, with about 1.1 % over the next day per case (F2), would clear that cost.
