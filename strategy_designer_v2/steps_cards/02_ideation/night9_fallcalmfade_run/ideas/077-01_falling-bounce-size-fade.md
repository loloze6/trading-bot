Idea: In a falling market, an up day that comes straight after a down day is followed by a fall the next day, and the larger that up day's rise in units of the coin's 30-day daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns), the larger the next day's fall.
Consequence: A relief bounce after a down day in a falling coin draws selling from trapped holders the next day, and a stronger bounce brings more of them to their exit price.
