Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day that closes up after an unbroken run of down days is followed by a fall over the next day, and the longer that run of down days, the larger the fall.
Consequence: Trapped holders sell into the first relief day after a quiet decline, and few buyers chase it, so the bounce is given back, more after longer runs.
