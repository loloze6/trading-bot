Idea: In any market, when an up day ends a run of down days, the coin falls over the next three days, and the fall is larger the deeper the run's total decline was, measured in the coin's 30-day daily swing.
Consequence: The selling that follows the first relief is set by how many holders the slide trapped at a loss, which grows with the slide's depth in swing units.
