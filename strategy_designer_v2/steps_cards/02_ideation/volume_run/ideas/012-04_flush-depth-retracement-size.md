brief: 012_liquidation-flush-partial-rebound
---
Idea: Among bars that fall sharply on a volume burst with a long lower wick, the larger the bar's drop from its open to its low, the larger the price rise over the next 3 days, as price drifts back toward where it traded before the cascade.
Consequence: Price after the flush is pulled back toward its pre-cascade level, so a deeper cascade leaves more to retrace.
