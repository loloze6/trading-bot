---
brief: 003_volume-separates-reversal-from-continuation
---
Idea: When a coin falls sharply over one day on volume well below its own 30-day average daily volume, it wins back part of that fall over the next 5 days. The larger the fall and the thinner its volume against that average, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A sharp drop on thin volume comes from a forced or impatient seller hitting a thin book, so liquidity providers push the price back up afterwards.
