---
brief: 013_anchoring-to-recent-high
---
Idea: When a coin closes above its prior 52-week high, the higher its volume on the breakout day relative to its own 90-day average, the larger its return over the next 30 days.
Consequence: Follow-through after the break is driven by late buyers who waited for confirmation, so it comes with heavy volume rather than unusually low volume.
