---
brief: 003_volume-separates-reversal-from-continuation
---
Idea: When a coin rises sharply over one day on volume well below its own 30-day average daily volume, it gives back part of that rise over the next 5 days. The larger the rise and the thinner its volume against that average, the larger the giveback.
Consequence: A sharp rise on thin volume comes from one impatient buyer hitting a thin book, so patient traders push the price back down afterwards.
