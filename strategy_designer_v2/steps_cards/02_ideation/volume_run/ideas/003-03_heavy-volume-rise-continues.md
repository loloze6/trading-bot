---
brief: 003_volume-separates-reversal-from-continuation
---
Idea: When a coin rises sharply over one day on volume well above its own 30-day average daily volume, it keeps rising over the next 5 days. The larger the rise and the heavier its volume against that average, the larger the further gain.
Consequence: A sharp rise on heavy volume reflects new information or a demand shift that is still spreading, so the move continues as slower traders arrive.
