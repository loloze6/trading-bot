Idea: After an hour in which the coin rises far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days on volume at or below its 30-day average, the price falls back over the next four hours, and the larger that hour's rise relative to its typical 30-day hourly move, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A large hourly rise on thin volume is a reason to refuse a long, or to lean against it, for the next few hours.
Why:
- F4: Large rising hours on thin volume (below 1.5 times the 30-day average) do the opposite and fade, as the brief says. Their unusual move is -0.12 % over 2 hours (t -2.16, 677 cases), -0.15 % over 4 hours (t -2.08) and -0.15 % over 6 hours (t -1.65). The fade is strongest in BTC (-0.23 % over 6 hours, t -2.05).
