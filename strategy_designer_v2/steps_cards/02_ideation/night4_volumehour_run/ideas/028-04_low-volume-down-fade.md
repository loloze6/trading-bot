Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days while its volume stays near its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price recovers part of that fall over the next two to six hours. The larger that hour's fall, the larger the recovery.
Consequence: A large down-move without a volume surge is noise from thin liquidity, which fades.
