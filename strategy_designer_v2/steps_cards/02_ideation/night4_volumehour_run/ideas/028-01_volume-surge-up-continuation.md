Idea: After an hour in which the coin rises far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days, on volume several times its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price keeps rising over the next two to six hours. The larger that hour's volume is relative to its usual volume, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A large up-move on surging volume reflects real information or a large buyer, and the rest of the market follows over the next hours.
