Idea: After an hour in which the coin rises far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days on volume several times its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price keeps rising over the next 24 hours. The heavier that hour's volume against its 30-day average and the larger its rise against its typical hourly move, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A trader would buy after a sharp high-volume hourly rise and refuse to buy into a sharp high-volume hourly drop, since only the rise is followed by a one-day move several times the cost of a trade.
Why:
- F4: It is the heavy-volume rises that continue, as the brief said. An up hour of at least 3 standard deviations on volume over 3 times its 30-day mean is followed by a 24-hour excess return of +0.55 % (t 2.88, 518 cases). The same rise is followed by +0.26 % (t 0.76, 201) on volume 1.5 to 3 times the mean, and +0.10 % (t 0.20, 42) on ordinary volume. The continuation grows with the volume.
- F5: Up hours with a product above 5 are followed by a 24-hour total move of +0.40 % (t 2.89, 1158 cases), with a strength of 0.058. That is 3.6 times the 0.11 % cost of a trade. The excess return is positive in 7 of 8 years, the exception being 2018 (-0.60 %), and in both coins (ETH +0.41 % t 1.82, BTC +0.11 % t 0.64). It rises by size from +0.03 % (product 2 to 5) to +0.24 %, +0.21 % and +0.36 % (above 20).
- F1: The tested result is on the wrong side and depends on the year: the mean excess 24-hour return after the bets is -0.09 % per case (t -1.56, 4990 cases). The recorded forecast's 24-hour cases split by year: 2018 -0.49 % (t -2.37, 690), 2022 -0.40 % (t -2.66, 694) and 2020 +0.43 % (t 2.49, 696). Their mean over all years is near zero (BTC -0.04 %, ETH -0.06 %).
- F2: Over 24 hours the grading runs against the idea. The larger the heavy-volume fall (the hour's fall in 30-day standard deviations times its volume ratio), the lower the following excess return: -0.42 % for a product below -20 (t -1.08, 159 cases), -0.35 % for -20 to -10 (t -1.21, 308), and -0.02 % for both -10 to -5 and -5 to -2 (697 and 2249 cases).
