Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days, on volume several times its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price keeps falling over the next two to six hours. The larger that hour's volume is relative to its usual volume, the larger the further fall.
Consequence: A large down-move on surging volume reflects real information or a large seller, and the rest of the market follows over the next hours.
