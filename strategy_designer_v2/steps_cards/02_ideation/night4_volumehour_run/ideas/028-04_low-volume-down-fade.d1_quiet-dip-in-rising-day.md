Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days while its volume stays near its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price recovers part of that fall over the next two to six hours, only while the coin's 24-hour return before that hour is a rise; the larger that prior 24-hour rise, the larger the recovery.
Consequence: A sharp quiet-volume dip inside a rising day is a pullback worth buying, and a dip that is not inside a rising day is a reason to stay out.
Why:
- F1: The move per case is smaller than the cost of a trade, so the book loses before any other effect. The total move is +0.07 % per case over 6 hours against a cost of 0.11 % per trade. - F2: On BTC the book does no timing at all. The gain from the market's own rise (0.031 % a day) equals almost all of its gross gain (0.032 % a day). On ETH, timing adds only 0.034 % a day of its 0.062 %.
