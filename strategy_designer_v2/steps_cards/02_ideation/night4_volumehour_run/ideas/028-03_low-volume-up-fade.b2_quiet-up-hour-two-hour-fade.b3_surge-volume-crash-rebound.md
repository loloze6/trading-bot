Idea: After an hour in which the coin falls far more than its typical hourly move over the past 30 days on volume several times its average hourly volume over the past 30 days, the price rebounds over the next two hours. The larger that hour's fall, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A trader would refuse to chase a high-volume hourly crash with a short for the next two hours, since it more often bounces than extends.
Why:
- F6: The opposite case is clear: large falls on surging volume rebound. Hours falling more than 3 times the usual move on volume above 3 times its 30-day mean are followed by a rise of 24.3 bp beyond drift over 2 hours (t −3.01 in the short-signed measure, 838 cases) and 24.9 bp over 4 hours (t −1.56, 432). This is against the brief's continuation, and on the coin's own bars.
