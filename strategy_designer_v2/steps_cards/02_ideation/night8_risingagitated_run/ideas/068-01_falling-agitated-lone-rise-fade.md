Idea: In a falling, agitated market, on a day that closes up, the more of the five days before it closed down, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: A rise that breaks a string of falls meets supply from trapped holders and waiting sellers, so the more falls just before it, the more of it is given back the next day.
