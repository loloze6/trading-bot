Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin closes below its 20-day high, measured in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next three days.
Consequence: The selling comes from many separate holders reaching their own limits, so it is spread over the following sessions and the deep close is followed by a fall that lasts several days.
