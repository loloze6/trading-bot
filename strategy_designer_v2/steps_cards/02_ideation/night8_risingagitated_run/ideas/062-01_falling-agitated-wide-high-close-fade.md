Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the higher a coin's close sits within the day's range and the wider that day's range is against its average daily range over the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day, with the two compounding as a product.
Consequence: Forced buying that leaves a wide day closing near its high is repaid the next day, more so the more of it there was and the later it pressed.
