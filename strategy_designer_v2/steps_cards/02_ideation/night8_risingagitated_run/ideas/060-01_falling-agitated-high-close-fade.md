Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to the top of that day's high-low range, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: In a downtrend with forced two-sided swings, late-day buying is short covering and bargain hunting that runs out, so the next day the remaining supply pushes price down.
