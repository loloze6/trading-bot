Idea: When a coin closes up on the day, the lower that close sits within its high-low range of the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: Up days that close near the bottom of the recent range are given back the next day, more so the deeper the close sits, as trapped holders above sell into the rise.
