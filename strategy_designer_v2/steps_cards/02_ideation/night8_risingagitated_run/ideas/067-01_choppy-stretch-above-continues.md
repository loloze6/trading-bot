Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average, measured in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next 10 days.
Consequence: In a trendless market, a large swing-scaled stretch above the recent average carries real demand that range-anchored traders reprice only in steps, so the rise continues.
