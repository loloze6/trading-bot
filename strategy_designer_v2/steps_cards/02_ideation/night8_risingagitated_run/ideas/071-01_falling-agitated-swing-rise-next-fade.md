Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day rise in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: Larger swing-scaled up days in agitated falling markets carry more forced short covering past the accepted price, so more of the rise is given back the very next day.
