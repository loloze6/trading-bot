Idea: The larger a coin's 20-day rise measured in units of its own daily swing over those 20 days (the 20-day return divided by the standard deviation of its daily returns over the same days), the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A few-week rise that is large against the coin's own noise reflects a real shift in demand that late, volatility-scaled and risk-sized buyers keep feeding, so it keeps going up.
