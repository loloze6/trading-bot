brief: 058_rising-agitated-overextension-gives-back
profile: rising agitated
---
Idea: In a rising, agitated market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average price, the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: The late part of a run in a rising, agitated market is carried by fragile levered chasers, forced short covering and late momentum buyers, so it is given back as their buying runs out and they sell.
