Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close stands below its 20-day average price, the more it falls over the next 10 days; the further it stands above, the more it rises.
Consequence: In a market with no trend over two months, a trader follows the coin's distance from its 20-day average instead of fading it, including on the short side when the coin is far below.
Why:
- F6: The strongest graded relation between the signed distance to the 20-day average and the move that follows is in choppy markets, a profile the book never trades.
