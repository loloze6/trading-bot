---
brief: 058_rising-agitated-overextension-gives-back
profile: rising agitated
---
Idea: In a rising, agitated market, the further a coin's close stands above its 20-day average, measured in units of its daily swing over the last 20 days, the more the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: The stretch above the average that fragile, levered buyers build is given back, and a stretch that is large for the coin's current swing marks the most leverage at risk on a pullback.
