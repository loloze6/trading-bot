Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin closes below its 20-day high, measured in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next day, on up days and down days alike.
Consequence: Holders who bought near the top of a sideways range sell once they are deeply underwater, so a deep close below the range high is followed by further falling the next day.
