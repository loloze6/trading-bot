Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to that day's high within the day's high-low range, the larger its fall over the next two days.
Consequence: Late, urgent buying that pins the close at the day's high is absorbed by liquidity providers and given back within a day or two, because no trend followers add to it and no patient buyers settle in.
