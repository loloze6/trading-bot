Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day fall measured in units of its own 20-day daily swing, the larger its fall over the next 7 days.
Consequence: A calm-market down day that stands far out from the coin's quiet noise carries information or patient selling, which spreads slowly and keeps pushing the price down over the following week.
