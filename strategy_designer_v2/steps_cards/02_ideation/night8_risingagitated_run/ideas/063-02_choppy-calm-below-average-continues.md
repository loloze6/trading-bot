Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the farther a coin's close stands below its 20-day average price, measured in units of its own 20-day standard deviation of daily returns, the larger its fall over the next 10 days.
Consequence: Below the average, a calm sideways market keeps drifting downward rather than reverting, and the drift grows with the swing-scaled distance.
