Idea: In a falling, agitated market, on a day that closes up, the longer the unbroken run of down days that ends just before it, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: The longer the run of falls before the first bounce, the more trapped holders are waiting and the thinner the buying behind it, so the larger the giveback.
