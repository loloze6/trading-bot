Idea: In a coin that has fallen over the past 30 days and whose daily high-low ranges over that month are wide compared with its own past year, a close below its lowest low of the prior 30 days is followed by a rise over the next 5 days. The further that close sits below the prior 30-day low, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Rebound. By the time a volatile decline breaks to a new monthly low, the forced sellers are used up, so buyers and short-covering lift price.
