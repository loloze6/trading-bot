Idea: In a coin whose daily high-low ranges over the past 30 days are wide compared with its own past year, a close at a new 30-day low is followed by a rise over the next 5 days. The larger the coin's 30-day fall into that low, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Rebound. The longer and deeper the volatile fall before the new monthly low, the more of the forced-seller pool is already liquidated.
