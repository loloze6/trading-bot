brief: 032_monthly-low-in-volatile-decline
bar: 1d
---
Idea: In a coin that has fallen over the past 30 days and whose daily high-low ranges over that month are wide compared with its own past year, a close below its lowest low of the prior 30 days is followed by a further fall over the next 5 days. The further that close sits below the prior 30-day low, the larger the fall.
Consequence: More falling. A new monthly low in an agitated decline sets off stops and margin exits that feed on each other.
