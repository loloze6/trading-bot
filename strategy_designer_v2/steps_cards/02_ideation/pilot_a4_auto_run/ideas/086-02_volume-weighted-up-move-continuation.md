Idea: In a falling, calm market, the size of a day's up move multiplied by that day's volume relative to the coin's 20-day average volume predicts a rise over the next 5 days. The larger that product, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A buyer who takes the whole day's supply and more shows as a strong up day on heavy volume, and the strength of that absorption grades how much unfinished demand lifts the price over the next days.
