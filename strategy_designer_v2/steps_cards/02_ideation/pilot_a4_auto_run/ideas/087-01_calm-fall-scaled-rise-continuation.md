Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose 3-day return is a rise, measured against its usual daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days), keeps rising over the next 5 days, and the larger that swing-scaled rise, the larger the 5-day gain that follows.
Consequence: A rise that is sharp for the coin in an orderly decline is the visible start of patient accumulation, so it carries further the stronger it is.
