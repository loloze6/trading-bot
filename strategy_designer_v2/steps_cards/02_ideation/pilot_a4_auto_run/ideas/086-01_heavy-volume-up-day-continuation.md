Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day that closes up on volume heavy against the coin's average daily volume over the past 20 days is followed by a further rise over the next 5 days. The heavier that day's volume relative to its 20-day average, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A heavy-volume up day in a calm decline shows a deliberate buyer whose demand is not finished, so the price keeps rising over the following days, more the heavier the volume.
