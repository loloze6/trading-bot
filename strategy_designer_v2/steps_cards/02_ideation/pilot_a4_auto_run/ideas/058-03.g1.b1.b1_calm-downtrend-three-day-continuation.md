Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's last 5 days carry on over the next 3 days: the further it has fallen over its last 5 days, the more it falls over the next 3 days, and the further it has risen over those 5 days, the more it rises.
Consequence: In a calm downtrend, a trader would follow the coin's past week for a few days only, mainly shorting the sharpest recent drops, rather than holding the bet for a full week.
