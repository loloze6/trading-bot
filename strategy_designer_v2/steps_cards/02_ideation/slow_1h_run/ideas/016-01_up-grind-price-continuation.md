Idea: When a coin has risen over the past 3 days, the larger the share of that rise made by many small hourly gains in the same direction, rather than by its few biggest hours, the more the coin keeps rising over the next 3 days.
Consequence: Up moves built as a grind keep going, because a patient buyer's split order is not yet finished.
