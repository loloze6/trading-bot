bar: 1h
---
Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 3 days, the larger the share of that fall that came from its few biggest hourly losses, the more of the fall the coin wins back over the next 3 days.
Consequence: Down moves made by a few violent hours partly reverse once the forced selling (stops, liquidations) is spent.
