Idea: In a falling market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has grown against the 30 days before, the smaller its fall, or the larger its rise, over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Growing volume in a fall means someone is taking the other side, through capitulation or new buyers, which breaks the slow one-sided drift and stalls the decline.
