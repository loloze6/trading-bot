Idea: In a falling market, the lower a coin's 30-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns) stands against its own swings over the past year, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A quiet decline is orderly selling with no forced exits and no bargain hunters, so the calmer the swing, the more supply is still to come and the further price keeps falling.
