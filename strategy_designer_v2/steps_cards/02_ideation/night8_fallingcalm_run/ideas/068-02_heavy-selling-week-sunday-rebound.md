Idea: In a falling market, the heavier a coin's volume over the 7 days up to Saturday's close, measured against its 30-day volume, during a weekly fall, the larger its rise on Sunday, the next day.
Consequence: Heavier weekday volume in a fall means more forced selling went through the books, so more discount is left to be repaid on Sunday.
