brief: 058_quiet-volume-calm-decline-continues
profile: falling calm
---
Idea: In a falling, calm market, among coins that have fallen over their last 30 days, the heavier the volume over those 30 days compared with the coin's own average volume over the past year, the less the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A deep fall on heavy volume has already cleared much of the selling supply into patient buyers, so it carries on less than the same fall on quiet volume.
