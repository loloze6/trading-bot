Idea: A coin's one-day fall, measured against its own usual daily move over the past 30 days, is followed by a rise over the next day when the fall comes on volume heavy against the coin's own 30-day volume. The rise is larger the deeper the fall and the heavier the volume, and largest when both are extreme together.
Consequence: Forced selling, seen as a deep fall on heavy volume, pushes price below value, and the discount is repaid the next day.
