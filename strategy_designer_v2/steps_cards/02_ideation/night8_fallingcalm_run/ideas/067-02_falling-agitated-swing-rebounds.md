Idea: In a falling market, the higher a coin's 30-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns) stands against its own swings over the past year, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: An agitated decline has already forced out its weakest holders and overshot, so the squeezes and rebounds that follow grow with the agitation and turn the fall into a rise.
