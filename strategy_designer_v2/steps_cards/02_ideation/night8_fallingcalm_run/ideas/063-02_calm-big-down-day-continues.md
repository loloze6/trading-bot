Idea: In a calm market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against its usual daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days), the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In calm markets a large swing-scaled down day is new information or deliberate selling, absorbed slowly, so the price keeps drifting down.
