Idea: In a falling, calm market, the more a coin has fallen over its last 30 days, the more it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A calm downtrend carries on, and a deeper past-weeks fall is followed by more falling, because the holders still selling a little at a time have not finished.
Why:
- F1: The tested effect is on the predicted side but small and uncertain: step 5's weighted mean signed 5-day excess return is +0.29 % with a day-pooled t of 0.37 (475 cases, 201 days), and the unusual move is +0.74 % per case (t 1.16, 321 days, strength 0.067). On folds B and C the in-position short excess stays positive at every horizon but never reaches t 2 (1 day +0.24 %, t 1.16, 1464 cases; 5 days +0.69 %, t 0.66, 277 cases; 20 days +5.3 %, t 1.18, 72 cases). The likely problem is too few cases, not the wrong horizon.
- F5: The effect is uneven across periods and coins, which also blurs the pooled test. In the profile, by year: 2018 +1.27 % (133 cases), 2019 +2.02 % (101), 2020 -6.91 % (17), 2021 -2.45 % (26). By window, w16 to w18 are negative (-11.7 %, -6.0 %, -4.1 %, 19 cases together). By coin, ETH is +2.96 % and XRP -1.93 %.
