Idea: In a rising market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the larger its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: An uptrend whose volume dries up has no sellers left to stop it, so thinner volume comes before a stronger continuation.
