Idea: In a rising market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has grown against the 30 days before, the weaker its return over the next 10 days: a large surge in volume is followed by a smaller rise, or by a fall.
Consequence: In a rising coin, swelling monthly volume marks a crowd of late buyers whose recent, levered positions unwind while early holders keep selling into strength, so the trend stalls or gives back over the following days.
