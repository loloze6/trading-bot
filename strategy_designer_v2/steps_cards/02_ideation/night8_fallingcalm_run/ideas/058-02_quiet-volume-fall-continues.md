Idea: In a falling, calm market, the deeper a coin's 30-day fall and the lighter its volume over those 30 days compared with its own average volume over the past year, the more it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A deep fall made on quiet volume shows most disillusioned holders have not yet sold, so their remaining supply keeps pushing the price down.
