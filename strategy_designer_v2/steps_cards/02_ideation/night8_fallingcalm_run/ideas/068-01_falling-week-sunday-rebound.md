Idea: In a falling market, the deeper a coin's fall over the 7 days up to Saturday's close, the larger its rise on Sunday, the next day.
Consequence: The week's forced weekday selling leaves Saturday's close at a discount, which is partly repaid on thin Sunday books, more so the deeper the week's fall.
