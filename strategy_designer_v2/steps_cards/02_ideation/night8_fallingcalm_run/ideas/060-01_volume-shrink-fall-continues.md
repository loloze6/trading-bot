Idea: In a falling market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A downtrend whose volume dries up has lost its buyers, so the thin, unabsorbed selling keeps pushing the price down, and pushes it further the more volume has shrunk.
