Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day rise against its usual daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days), the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In agitated markets large up days are more often forced flow, such as short liquidations and stop cascades, and partly reverse.
