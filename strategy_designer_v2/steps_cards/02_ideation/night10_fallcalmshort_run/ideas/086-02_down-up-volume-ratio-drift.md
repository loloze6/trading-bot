Idea: In a falling, calm market, the more a coin's average volume per down day exceeds its average volume per up day over the last 20 days, the further the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Heavy down days and thin up days show sellers pressing on weakness and holding back on rallies, so their remaining supply keeps pushing price lower.
