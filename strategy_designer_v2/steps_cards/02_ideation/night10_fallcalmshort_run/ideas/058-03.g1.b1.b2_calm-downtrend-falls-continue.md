Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's fall over its last 5 days carries on: the further it has fallen over its last 5 days, the more it keeps falling over the next 3 days.
Consequence: In a calm downtrend, a trader would short a coin after a sharp week's drop, more heavily the deeper the drop, and would not buy its rebounds.
