Idea: In a falling, calm market, the more a coin's trading volume over the past 30 days has shrunk against the 30 days before, the further it falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A trader looks for short continuation trades in quiet downtrends where volume is drying up, a profile and side where the research holds almost no strategy.
Why:
- F3: The book earns mainly on its short side, when volume shrinks. Active short cases have a 5-day excess return of -2.4 % (t -1.91, 192 cases on 82 days); active long cases have +2.3 % (t 1.32, 87 cases on 50 days). The lowest forecast quintile alone (forecast -20 to -14.5) gives -5.3 % (t -3.44, 56 cases).
- F4: The signal is stronger when the coin is calmer than its own recent norm (30-day swing at or below its window median so far): the signed excess return is +4.0 % (t 2.48, 125 cases on 53 days) in calm bars, against +2.1 % (t 1.61, 48 cases on 31 days) in agitated bars. In calm bars the short side carries the gain: -4.5 % over 36 cases.
- F2: On the book's active grid bars, the signal's signed 5-day excess return rises steadily with the depth of the 20-day fall: +0.6 % (t 0.54, 70 cases) at 0 to -5 %, +2.8 % (t 2.16, 47) at -5 to -10 %, +3.3 % (t 2.99, 53) at -10 to -15 %, +5.7 % (t 3.90, 66) at -15 to -21 %. Taken together, a 20-day fall of -10 % or deeper gives +4.6 % per case (t 3.40 with each day's cases together, 119 cases on 57 days), against +1.5 % (t 1.21, 117 cases on 64 days) for a shallower fall. It is positive in every year of 2018 to 2021 and on all six coins, from +2.5 % on BTC to +8.5 % on LTC.
