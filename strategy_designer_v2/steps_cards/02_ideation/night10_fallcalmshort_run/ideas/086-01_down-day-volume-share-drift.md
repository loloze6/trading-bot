Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger the share of a coin's last 10 days of volume that traded on its down days, the further the coin falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Lopsided volume, heavy on down days, marks unforced sellers who are not finished, so price keeps drifting lower while it lasts.
