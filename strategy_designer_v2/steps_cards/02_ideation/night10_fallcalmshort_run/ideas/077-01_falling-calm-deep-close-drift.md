Idea: In a falling, calm market, the further a coin's close sits below its highest high of the past 20 days, measured in its standard deviation of daily returns over the past 20 days, the larger its fall over the next three days.
Consequence: Holders who bought near the recent peak cross their own loss lines as the close sinks below it, and in a calm decline they sell in pieces over the following days.
