Idea: In a falling market, an up day whose high-to-low range is wide compared with the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days is followed by a fall over the next day, and the wider that range is against the 20-day average, the larger the next day's fall.
Consequence: A wide up day in a downtrend is a relief bounce driven by short covering and chasing, and it is given back the next day in proportion to how hard it pushed.
