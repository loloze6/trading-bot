Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, an up day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days is followed by a fall over the next day, and the wider that day's range against its 20-day average, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A wide up day in a trendless, swollen-swing market is forced buying that range sellers unwind the next day, with a giveback that grows with the overshoot.
