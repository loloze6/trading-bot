Idea: In a falling market, an up day is followed by a larger next-day fall the closer that up day's close sits to the coin's highest close of the past 30 days.
Consequence: Early in a shallow decline, up days meet nearby supply from holders back near breakeven, so the next-day fade is largest when the fall so far is smallest.
