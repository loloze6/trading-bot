Idea: In a choppy market, the nearer a coin's daily close sits to that day's high, as a share of the day's range, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: Late urgent buying that pins the close at the high meets range-bound sellers and no trend buyers, so price gives back more the tighter the close is to the high.
