Idea: In any market, a day that closes up while sitting deeper below the coin's 20-day high, measured in the coin's own usual daily swing over the last 20 days, is followed by a larger fall over the next day.
Consequence: Holders under water since the recent high sell into an up day's relief, and the deeper the loss in swing units, the more supply waits above the price to meet the next session once the up day's buying is spent.
