Idea: In a choppy market, the faster a coin's swing has been rising, as its swing over the last 10 days against its swing over the last 30 days, the larger its fall over the next five days.
Consequence: Forced trimming happens as a trendless coin starts swinging more than usual, so a fresh rise in swing brings selling that pushes price down until positions match the new swing.
