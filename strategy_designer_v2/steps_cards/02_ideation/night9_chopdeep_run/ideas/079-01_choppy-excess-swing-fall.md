Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's swing over the last 30 days (the standard deviation of its daily returns) runs above its own usual swing over the past year, the larger its fall over the next five days.
Consequence: Holders who size positions to the swing they see must sell more the more a trendless coin's swing exceeds its usual level, and no trend crowd absorbs that selling, so price falls for some days.
