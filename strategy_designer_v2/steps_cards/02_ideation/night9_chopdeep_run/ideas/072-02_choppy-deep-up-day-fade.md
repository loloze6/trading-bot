Idea: In a choppy market, on a day that closed up, the further the close sits below the coin's 20-day high, in units of its 20-day daily-return standard deviation, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: An up day far below the range top is a bounce that underwater holders use to sell, so the pressure continues the next day.
