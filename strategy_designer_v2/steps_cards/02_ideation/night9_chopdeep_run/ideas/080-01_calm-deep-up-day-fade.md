Idea: In a calm market, an up day whose close sits further below the coin's 20-day high, measured in its usual daily swings over the last 20 days, is followed by a larger fall over the next day.
Consequence: Holders who bought near the recent peak sell into a rise, and their selling lands the next day, more so the deeper the coin sits below that peak.
