Idea: In a choppy market, the further a coin's close sits below its 20-day high, measured in units of the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 20 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: A deep close below the recent range's top pushes range buyers underwater and fires their stops, so the breakdown continues the next day.
