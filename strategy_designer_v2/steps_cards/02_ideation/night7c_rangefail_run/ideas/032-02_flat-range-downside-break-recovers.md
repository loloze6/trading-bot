Idea: In a coin whose price has gone nowhere over the past 60 days, a daily close below the prior 60-day low is followed by a rise back toward the range over the next 10 days. The further that close sits below the old low, the larger the share of the overshoot recovered.
Consequence: A downside close beyond a trendless coin's range comes from a limited pool of stops and breakout sellers that range traders fade, and bigger overshoots leave a bigger imbalance to unwind.
