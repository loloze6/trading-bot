Idea: In a coin whose price has gone nowhere over the past 60 days, a daily close above the prior 60-day high is followed by a fall back toward the range over the next 10 days. The further that close sits above the old high, the larger the share of the overshoot given back.
Consequence: An upside close beyond a trendless coin's range comes from a limited pool of stops and breakout buyers that range traders fade, and bigger overshoots leave a bigger imbalance to unwind.
