Idea: When a daily close falls below the lowest close of the prior 365 days, the coin rises over the next 10 days. The further the close is below that old low, the larger the rebound.
Consequence: A break below the yearly low is a stop run that clears out the last weak holders, leaving no sellers, so price climbs back above the old low.
