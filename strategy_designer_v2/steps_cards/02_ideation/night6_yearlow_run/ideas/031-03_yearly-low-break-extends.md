Idea: When a daily close falls below the lowest close of the prior 365 days, the coin keeps falling over the next 10 days. The further the close is below that old low, the larger the further decline.
Consequence: A break of the yearly low fires clustered stops, ends holders' hope of getting out near cost and draws trend-following shorts, so the break starts a new leg down.
