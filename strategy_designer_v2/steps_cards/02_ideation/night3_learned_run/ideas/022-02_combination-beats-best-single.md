Idea: A coin's return over the next 5 days lines up more closely with its relearned combined score than with the score of whichever single signal has predicted best. The single signals are its 28-day return, last 1-day return, closeness to its 90-day high and 30-day volatility.
Consequence: Because the effects' errors are partly unrelated, the combination forecasts better than the best single effect.
