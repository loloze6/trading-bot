Idea: On days when a coin's combined score is far from its usual value, the coin's move over the next 5 days is larger than the move that follows equally rare readings of any single signal. The further out the combined score, the larger that move.
Consequence: The combination's coming move is larger relative to trading cost than that of any single effect.
