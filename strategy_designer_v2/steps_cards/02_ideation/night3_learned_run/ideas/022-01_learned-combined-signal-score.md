Idea: When a coin's four signals are combined into one score, the coin's return over the next 5 days is higher the higher that score is. The four signals are its 28-day return, minus its last 1-day return, its closeness to its 90-day high, and minus its 30-day volatility. Each signal is weighted by how well it predicted that coin's own 5-day returns over the past 365 days, and the weights are relearned each day.
Consequence: A combination of weak effects, weighted by past predictive power, forecasts coming returns.
