Idea: A coin that rose over the weekend (its Saturday-to-Sunday return) while its 20-day return is negative falls over the next 3 days, and the fall is larger the larger the weekend rise and the deeper the 20-day fall.
Consequence: Sell or short weekend rallies in coins that are in a 20-day downtrend, and refuse to buy them at Sunday's close.
Why:
- F4: Weekend rises in a downtrend reverse down. When Sunday rose while the 20-day return was negative, the unusual 3-day move was −1.39 % (t −6.0). When the whole Saturday-to-Sunday move was a rise with a negative 20-day return, it was −0.79 % (t −3.5, n 1009). The same rise-in-downtrend gave −1.02 % on Mondays (t −3.8) and −1.20 % on Wednesdays (t −4.2).
- F7: Caveat on all the t-statistics above: they pool 19 coins on the same days, and those coins move together. The effective number of independent cases is well below the stated n, so each t is overstated, probably by a factor of about two. F1 to F5 are the patterns that stand out across the many cells examined.
