Idea: Among coins that have fallen over the past 28 days, the lower the volume over that period compared with the coin's own usual volume, the larger the further fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: A decline without a volume flush leaves the overhang of willing sellers in place, so it continues more than a decline with heavy volume.
