brief: 032_calm-decline-keeps-falling
bar: 1d
---
Idea: Among coins that have fallen over the past 28 days, the smaller the daily high-low ranges over that period compared with the coin's own usual ranges, the larger the further fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Calm declines continue more than violent ones, because a violent fall releases forced selling and a calm fall never does.
