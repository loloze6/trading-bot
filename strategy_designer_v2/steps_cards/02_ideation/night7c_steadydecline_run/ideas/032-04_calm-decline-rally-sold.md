brief: 032_calm-decline-keeps-falling
bar: 1d
---
Idea: When a coin in a calm 28-day decline closes a day up, it falls over the next 5 days, and the deeper the 28-day fall, the larger that drop.
Consequence: Holders sell into every pause or small rise in a calm decline, and underwater supply sits just above the price, so small rallies fail.
