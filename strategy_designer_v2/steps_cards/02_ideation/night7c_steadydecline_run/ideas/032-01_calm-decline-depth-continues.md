Idea: When a coin has fallen over the past 28 days while its daily high-low ranges and daily volume stayed near their own usual levels, it keeps falling over the next 5 days. The larger its 28-day fall, the larger that further fall.
Consequence: Calm declines continue, and a deeper calm fall over past weeks is followed by a larger further fall over the next days.
