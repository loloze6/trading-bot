Idea: When a coin closes below a price where it turned up several times in the past 90 days, it tends to keep falling over the next 5 days, and the more times the level held before, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A close through a repeatedly held level exhausts the defending orders and triggers stops in the break's direction, accelerating the move, more so the more often it held.
