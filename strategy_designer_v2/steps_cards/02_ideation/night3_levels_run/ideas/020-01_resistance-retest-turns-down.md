Idea: When a coin's price rises back to a price where it turned down several times in the past 90 days, it tends to fall over the next 5 days, and the more times it turned down there before, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A level that repeatedly reversed price acts as a barrier from below, because limit orders defend it, more strongly the more often it held.
