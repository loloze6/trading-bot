Idea: When a coin's price falls back to a price where it turned up several times in the past 90 days, it tends to rise over the next 5 days, and the more times it turned up there before, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A level that repeatedly reversed price acts as a barrier from above, because limit orders defend it, more strongly the more often it held.
