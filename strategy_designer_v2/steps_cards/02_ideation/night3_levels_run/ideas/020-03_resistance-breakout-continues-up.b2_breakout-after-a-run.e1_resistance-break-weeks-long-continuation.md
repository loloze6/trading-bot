Idea: When a coin closes above a price where it turned down several times in the past 90 days, it tends to keep rising over the next 20 days. The more times the level held and the further the close above it, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A close through a repeatedly held 90-day level is a reason to stay long for weeks rather than days, without requiring a strong prior month.
