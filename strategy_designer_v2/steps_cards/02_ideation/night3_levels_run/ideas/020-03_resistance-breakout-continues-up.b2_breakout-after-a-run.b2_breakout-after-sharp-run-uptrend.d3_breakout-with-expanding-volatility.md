Idea: When a coin closes above a price where it turned down several times in the past 90 days, it tends to keep rising over the next 5 days, only while its 60-day return is positive, and the larger its 10-day volatility relative to its 60-day volatility, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A break that comes with widening daily swings is a reason to buy; a break in a quiet, contracting market is not.
Why:
- F6: The 10-day run-up the idea grades by does not grade the trades: mean net gain per trade by run-up quartile is 2.2 %, 3.1 %, 2.0 % and 3.8 % (452 trades each).
- F7: Calm, contracting volatility carries no gain. Held bars where the coin's 10-day volatility is below 0.65 of its 60-day volatility earn +4.6 bps (t 0.54, 666 bars). Above 1.27 of it they earn +53.6 bps (t 2.55).
