brief: 023_range-reversion-when-no-trend
bar: 1d
---
Idea: In a coin whose 30-day net move has been small relative to its 30-day high-low range, the further its close sits below the middle of that range, the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: In a flat market a push toward the bottom of the band lacks follow-through and gets bought back toward the middle.
