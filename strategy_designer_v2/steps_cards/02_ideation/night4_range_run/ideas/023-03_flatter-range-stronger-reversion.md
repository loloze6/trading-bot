brief: 023_range-reversion-when-no-trend
bar: 1d
---
Idea: For the same distance of a coin's close from the middle of its 30-day range, the smaller its 30-day net move is relative to that range, the larger its move back toward the middle over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Reversion to the band's middle works only when there is no trend, so it is stronger the flatter the past weeks have been.
