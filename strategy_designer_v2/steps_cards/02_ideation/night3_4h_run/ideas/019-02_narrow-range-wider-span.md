bar: 4h
---
Idea: The narrower a coin's average four-hour high-low range over the past two days compared with its average four-hour range over the past 30 days, the wider the span between its highest high and lowest low over the next three days, measured against its price.
Consequence: After a stretch of unusually narrow ranges, a coin tends to make a large move, seen as the price span it covers rather than its net return.
