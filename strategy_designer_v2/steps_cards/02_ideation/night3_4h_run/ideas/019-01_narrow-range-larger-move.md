bar: 4h
---
Idea: The narrower a coin's average four-hour high-low range over the past two days compared with its average four-hour range over the past 30 days, the larger the absolute size of its return over the next three days.
Consequence: After a stretch of unusually narrow ranges, a coin tends to make a large move.
