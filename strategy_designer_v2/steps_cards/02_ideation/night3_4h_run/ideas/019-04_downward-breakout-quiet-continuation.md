bar: 4h

Idea: When a four-hour bar closes below the lowest low of a coin's preceding two-day narrow stretch, the coin falls over the next three days, and the narrower that stretch was compared with the coin's 30-day average four-hour range, the larger the fall.
Consequence: The first decisive four-hour bar downward out of a narrow range points the way price continues over the next one to three days.
