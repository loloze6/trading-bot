Idea: In a rising, agitated market, a day that closes up with a high-to-low range wide against the coin's average daily high-to-low range over the last 30 days is followed by a further rise over the next 3 days, and the larger that ratio, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A day wider than the coin's usual daily ranges shows a sweep through offers and stops whose late-arriving buyers lift the price over the following days.
