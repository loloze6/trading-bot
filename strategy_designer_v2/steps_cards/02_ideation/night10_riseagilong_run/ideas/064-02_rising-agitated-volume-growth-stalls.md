Idea: In a rising, agitated market, the more a coin's total volume over the last 30 days has grown against the 30 days before, the smaller its rise over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Growing volume in a rising, swinging market marks late, leveraged buyers whose buying is spent, so the rise that follows is smaller.
