Idea: In a rising, agitated market, a day that closes up with a high-to-low range wide against the coin's 30-day daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns) is followed by a further rise over the next 3 days, and the wider that scaled range, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Forceful buying on a wide up day draws covering shorts, late trend followers and swing-sized holders in stages over the following days, so the rise continues.
