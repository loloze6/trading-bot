Idea: In a rising, agitated market, on the first up day after a run of consecutive down days, the coin rises over the next 5 days, and the deeper the run's total fall, measured against the standard deviation of the coin's daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger that rise.
Consequence: A deeper down run, relative to the coin's usual swing, means more forced selling cleared and more buyers held back, so the recovery after the first up day is larger.
