Idea: In a choppy market, a coin whose one-day return is a large rise compared with its usual daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days) keeps rising over the next 5 days. The larger that one-day rise is against the swing, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A jump far beyond the usual swing in a trendless coin pushes through the supply that caps the range, so sellers who were wrong buy back late and slow spot buyers arrive late, and the rise continues.
