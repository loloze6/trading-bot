Idea: In an agitated market, a daily close near the top of that day's high-to-low range is followed by a rise from the end of the next day to the end of the seventh day, larger the nearer the close sits to the day's high.
Consequence: The first day pays back the late urgent buying, and the rest of the week then follows the buyers who won the day.
