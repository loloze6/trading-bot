Idea: In a choppy market, the larger a coin's plain 30-day fall below zero (its close under its close of 30 days earlier), the more it falls over the next 7 days, a fall that keeps going on days when the coin has already climbed back over that 30-day-old close.
Consequence: A short taken on a deep month-long fall in a trendless market should be held for about a week, not closed the moment the coin recovers its month-old price.
Why:
- F1: The book loses at the exit, not in the signal: of its 690 trades, all short, the 429 that lasted 3.5 days or less lost 2.00 % gross each, while the 145 lasting 3.5 to 6 days gained +0.80 %, the 104 lasting 6 to 15 days +0.82 % and the 11 longer ones +1.52 %. 71 % of trades closed when the forecast fell back to 0, so a short is cut exactly when the coin climbs back over its close of 30 days earlier. That gives an overall mean of -0.93 % gross and -1.03 % net per trade, with 39 % winners.
- F2: The fall the signal predicts is larger over 5 to 10 days than over 3. On the non-overlapping grid in folds B, C, the short's total gain per case is +0.32 % over 3 days (350 cases, t 0.47 with one day's cases together), only 1.5 times the 0.22 % cost of a trade. Over 5 days it is +1.62 % (204 cases, 105 days), 7 times the cost, with an unusual move of +2.84 % (t 2.19). Over 10 days the unusual move is +3.36 % (106 cases, t 1.33).
- F3: The effect is graded at every horizon. The deepest third of falls gives an unusual short gain of +2.15 % over 3 days against +0.31 % for the shallowest third (t 2.45 and 0.53, unclustered). Over 5 days it is +3.62 % against +2.00 %, and over 7 days the deepest third gives +5.46 % (53 cases, t 1.87).
- F4: The short fights the coins' normal rise. Over all bars in these folds a short earns -0.78 % per 3 days, -1.34 % per 5 and -2.65 % per 10 (t about -4). Choppy bars with no signal give a short -0.82 % per 3 days, so a small 3-day unusual move (+1.02 %) leaves almost no total gain. This is why the book's Sharpe trails holding by 0.46 and it earns 0.020 % a day in position against the 0.05 % needed.
- F7: The result leans on one window, and the effect belongs to the choppy market. w16 has 8 cases over 5 days with a +25.9 % unusual gain, and the book's best daily mean, +0.108 %, is in w16, so one window carries it; still, the 5-day signal is positive in 8 of 12 windows and in every year from 2018 to 2021. Outside the choppy profile, a 30-day fall in a falling market gives a 5-day short only -0.16 % total and +0.98 % unusual (421 cases, t 0.73), well below the +2.84 % inside choppy markets.
