Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, a coin that closes near the top of its day's high-low range falls over the next day, and the closer the close sits to the day's high, the larger the fall.
Consequence: Late pushes that pin a close near the day's high are urgent flow that range traders fade, so price drifts back down the next day.
