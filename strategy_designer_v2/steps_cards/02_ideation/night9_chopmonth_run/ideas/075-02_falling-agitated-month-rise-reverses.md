Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin whose 30-day return is positive, scaled by its 30-day swing, falls over the next 10 days. The larger the month's rise in swing units, the larger the fall.
Consequence: A month's rise in a falling, agitated market is a chain of short squeezes and covering that overshoots, and it is partly given back once that flow is spent, more for larger rises.
