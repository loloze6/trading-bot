Idea: In a choppy market, a coin that closes above its highest close of the past 30 days rises further over the next 5 days. The further that close sits above the old high, measured in units of its 30-day daily swing, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A move that clears the top of the range where range traders and holders sold leaves those sellers and shorts wrong, so they buy back over several days and push the coin higher.
