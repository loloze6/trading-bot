Idea: In an agitated market, a daily close near the top of that day's high-to-low range is followed by a rise over the next 7 days, larger the nearer the close sits to the day's high.
Consequence: A close at the high shows buyers absorbed the day's forced selling, so short covering and slower holders who read the daily candle lift the coin over the following week.
