Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin whose 30-day return is negative keeps falling over the next 10 days, and the larger that 30-day fall is against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the same 30 days, the larger the fall that follows.
Consequence: Monthly reviewers cut a coin whose month stood out as a real fall, and they cut in pieces, with no flush and few dip-buyers in a calm downtrend, so the repricing downward is still unfinished and carries on.
