Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a coin whose 30-day return is negative, scaled by its 30-day swing, rises over the next 10 days. The larger the month's fall in swing units, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A month's fall in a falling, agitated market overshoots under forced selling and is partly given back once that selling is spent, more for larger falls.
