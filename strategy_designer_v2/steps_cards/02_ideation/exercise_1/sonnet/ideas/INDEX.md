001-02_low-extreme-direction-shift: After a low extreme reading from a one-sided fall, the next move's direction differs from the one after neutral readings, as rebound or continuation. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]

003-01_low-volume-move-partial-reversal: Sharp moves on unusually low volume are partly retraced afterward.
003-02_high-volume-move-holds-continues: Sharp moves on unusually high volume hold or extend in the same direction afterward.
003-03_volume-separates-reversal-from-continuation: For equal-sized sharp moves, later drift runs against the move when volume was low and with it when volume was high.
003-04_thin-hours-low-volume-reversal: Low-volume sharp moves during quiet hours or weekends give back more than similar low-volume moves in active periods.
004-01_btc-move-leads-altcoin-follow: After a sharp bitcoin move, altcoins drift in the same direction over the next bars, beyond what their own past returns explain.
004-02_thin-coins-lag-more: Following a sharp bitcoin move, low-volume altcoins catch up by more, and later, than high-volume altcoins.
004-03_wide-range-coins-lag-more: After a sharp bitcoin move, altcoins with wider bar ranges catch up more in following bars than narrow-range altcoins.
004-04_btc-leads-more-than-follows: Bitcoin's past returns forecast altcoin returns more than altcoin past returns forecast bitcoin's, so the lead runs mainly from bitcoin outward.
004-05_altcoin-gap-to-btc-closes: After a sharp bitcoin move, altcoins that have not yet moved as much as bitcoin implies close that gap over the next bars.
004-06_altcoin-response-delayed-after-btc: Altcoins react only partly to a sharp bitcoin move in the same bar, with more of the response arriving in later bars.
005-01_bearish-divergence-direction-shift: After a new price high with weaker RSI, the next move's direction differs from that after a new high with confirming RSI. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]
005-02_bullish-divergence-direction-shift: After a new price low with stronger RSI, the next move's direction differs from that after a new low with confirming RSI. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]
005-03_divergence-move-size-shift: After divergences, the size of the next price move differs from the size after new extremes where RSI confirms. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]
005-04_divergence-in-strong-trend-contrast: Divergences inside strong trends are followed by different price moves than divergences in weak or sideways markets. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]
005-05_repeated-divergence-outcome-shift: Repeated divergences over successive price extremes are followed by different moves than a single divergence. [instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB]
