bar: 4h

Idea: After an unbroken streak of falling four-hour periods whose loss is large relative to the coin's typical four-hour move over the past 30 days, the coin rises over the next three days. The more the volume declined from the start of the streak to its end, the larger the rise.
Consequence: For down-runs of the same size, the give-back grows the more the volume behind the run has faded, because only fragile late sellers remain.
