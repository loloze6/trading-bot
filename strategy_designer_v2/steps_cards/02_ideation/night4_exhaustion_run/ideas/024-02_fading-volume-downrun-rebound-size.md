Idea: When a coin has just fallen in an unbroken streak of four-hour periods, and the volume across the streak has been declining, it rises over the next three days. The larger the streak's loss relative to the coin's typical four-hour move over the past 30 days, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A down-run far larger than usual, made with fading volume, is partly given back over the next one to three days.
