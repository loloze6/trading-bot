Idea: When a coin trades a four-hour volume far above its 30-day average, it rises over the next three days. The higher the volume against that average, the larger the rise.
Consequence: A volume surge is a reason to lean long over the next three days rather than fade it.
Why:
- F7: The top quintile of bar volume relative to its 30-day mean was followed over 3 days by +1.12 % above drift (strength 0.123, t 3.31, n 1032). The lowest two quintiles were followed by about −0.48 % (t −1.96 and −1.77). This grouping did not separate up bars from down bars.
