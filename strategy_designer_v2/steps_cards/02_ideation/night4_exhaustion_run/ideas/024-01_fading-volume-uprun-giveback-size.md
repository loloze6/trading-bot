Idea: When a coin has just risen in an unbroken streak of four-hour periods, and the volume across the streak has been declining, it falls over the next three days. The larger the streak's gain relative to the coin's typical four-hour move over the past 30 days, the larger the fall.
Consequence: An up-run far larger than usual, made with fading volume, is partly given back over the next one to three days.
