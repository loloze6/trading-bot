Idea: In a rising, calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days is followed by a further rise over the next 7 days. The larger the day's rise in those swing units, the larger the rise that follows.
Consequence: When the coin has been rising for weeks, a large calm up day confirms a move that late buyers are already chasing, so the follow-through holds there.
