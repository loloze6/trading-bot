Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a one-day fall that is large against the coin's 60-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 60 days) is followed by a rise the next day, and the larger the fall against that swing, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Forced selling overshoots beyond what the coin's book normally absorbs, so the next-day rebound is graded by the fall measured against the longer-run swing.
