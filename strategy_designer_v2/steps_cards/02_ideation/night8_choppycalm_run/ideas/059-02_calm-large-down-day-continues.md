Idea: In a calm market, a coin's one-day fall that is large against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days is followed by a further fall over the next 7 days. The larger the day's fall in those swing units, the larger the fall that follows.
Consequence: In calm markets, a large down day is deliberate, slowly spreading selling that later sellers keep joining, so it carries on downward.
