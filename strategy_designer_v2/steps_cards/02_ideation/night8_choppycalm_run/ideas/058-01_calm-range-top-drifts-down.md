profile: choppy calm
---
Idea: In a choppy, calm market, the nearer a coin's close sits to the top of its 20-day high-low range, the larger its fall over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Closes near the top of the recent range drift back down toward the middle, and the pull grows nearer the edge.
