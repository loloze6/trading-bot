Idea: In a calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against its own 30-day daily swing is followed by further rise over the next 7 days, and the larger the rise in those swing units, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: In quiet markets a big up day draws buyers who arrive over the following days, so the surge carries on, more so the larger it stands out.
