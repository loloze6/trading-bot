Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the deeper a coin's fall over the four days before the latest day, the larger its rise over the next day, even when the latest day's own fall is small.
Consequence: The slide's depth, not the size of the last day alone, sets the rebound, so a small down day ending a deep slide still rebounds.
