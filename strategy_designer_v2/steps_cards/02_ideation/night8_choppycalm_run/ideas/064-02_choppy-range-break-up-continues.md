Idea: In a choppy market, a one-day rise that closes above the coin's highest close of the prior 20 days is followed by a further rise over the next 7 days. The further that close is above the prior 20-day high, measured against the 30-day daily swing, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: A one-day jump that pushes a flat coin through the supply capping its range leaves range sellers and shorts wrong, and their buying back over several days carries it higher.
