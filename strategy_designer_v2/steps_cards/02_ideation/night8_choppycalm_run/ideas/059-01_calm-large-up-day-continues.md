Idea: In a calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days is followed by a further rise over the next 7 days. The larger the day's rise in those swing units, the larger the rise that follows.
Consequence: In calm markets, a large up day is deliberate, slowly spreading buying that later buyers keep joining, so it carries on upward.
