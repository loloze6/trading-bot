Idea: In a choppy market, a one-day rise that is large against the coin's daily swing over the past 30 days is followed by a further rise over the next 7 days. The larger the rise against that swing, the larger the further rise.
Consequence: In a trendless coin, a jump far beyond the usual swing breaks through range-top supply, so trapped sellers and late spot buyers keep lifting it for days.
