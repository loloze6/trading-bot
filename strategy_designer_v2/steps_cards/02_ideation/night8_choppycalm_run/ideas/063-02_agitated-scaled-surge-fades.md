Idea: In an agitated market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against its own 30-day daily swing is followed by a fall over the next 7 days, and the larger the rise in those swing units, the larger the fall.
Consequence: In agitated markets big up days are squeezes and two-sided forced flow with no news to spread, so they fade rather than continue.
