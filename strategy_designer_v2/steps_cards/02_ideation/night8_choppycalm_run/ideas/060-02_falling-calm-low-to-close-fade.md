Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day whose close stands far above its low against the coin's 30-day daily swing is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the further the close stands above the low against that swing.
Consequence: The size of the intraday push that set the close is the overshoot that patient overhead sellers take back the next day.
