Idea: In a falling, calm market, a coin's one-day rise that is large against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days is followed by a fall over the next day, and the larger the scaled rise, the larger that next-day fall.
Consequence: Underwater holders sell into a sharp up day bought by thin, short-lived demand, so the rise is partly given back the next day, more so the larger it was.
