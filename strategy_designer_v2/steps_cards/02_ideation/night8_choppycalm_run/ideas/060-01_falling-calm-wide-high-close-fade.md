Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day daily swing and whose close sits near the day's high is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the wider the range is against that swing and the nearer the close sits to the high.
Consequence: Urgent buying that pins a wide day's close at its high meets patient overhead sellers and is given back the next day, more so the larger the overshoot.
