Idea: In a falling, agitated market, a one-day fall that is large against the coin's swing over the past year (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 365 days) is followed by a rise the next day, and the larger the fall against that swing, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Dip-buyers size their absorbing capacity on the coin's long experience, so a fall far beyond the past year's normal swing earns a larger discount that is repaid the next day.
