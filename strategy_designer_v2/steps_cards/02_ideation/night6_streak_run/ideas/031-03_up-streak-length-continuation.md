Idea: The more days in a row a coin has closed higher, measured against the lengths of its own up-close streaks over the past 365 days, the more it keeps rising over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Continuation from slow information and herding: each extra higher close draws in late trend followers and forces shorts to cover, so a longer up streak brings more buying still to come.
