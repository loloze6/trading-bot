Idea: The more days in a row a coin has closed lower, measured against the lengths of its own down-close streaks over the past 365 days, the more it keeps falling over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Continuation from slow information and herding: each extra lower close draws in late sellers and forces longs out, so a longer down streak brings more selling still to come.
