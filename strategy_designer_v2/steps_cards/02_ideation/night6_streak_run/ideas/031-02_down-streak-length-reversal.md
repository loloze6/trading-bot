Idea: The more days in a row a coin has closed lower, measured against the lengths of its own down-close streaks over the past 365 days, the more it rises over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Reversal from crowding: a longer down streak means more one-sided selling and stretched short positioning, so the price recovers more once that flow runs out.
