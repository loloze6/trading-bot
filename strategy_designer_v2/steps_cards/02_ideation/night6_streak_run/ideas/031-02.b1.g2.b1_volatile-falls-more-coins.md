Idea: The larger a coin's percentage fall on its latest day, the more it rises over the next 2 days, and this rebound is larger the higher the coin's volatility over the past 60 days.
Consequence: A trader buying the dip should expect the largest short rebounds after big falls in turbulent periods and little after falls in calm ones, which makes the bet worth its cost mainly when volatility is high.
Why:
- F1: The tested measure lands on the predicted side but short of the bar: slope t 1.47 on 8,650 grid points over 1,509 days; the unusual move is only +0.09 % per case (t 0.53, 1,681 days), a strength of 0.014, while one trade costs 0.19 %.
