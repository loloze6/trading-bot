031-01_up-streak-length-reversal: The longer a coin's run of higher daily closes, relative to its usual streaks over the past year, the larger its decline over the next 5 days. [bar: 1d]
031-02_down-streak-length-reversal: The longer a coin's run of lower daily closes, relative to its usual streaks over the past year, the larger its rebound over the next 5 days. [bar: 1d]
031-03_up-streak-length-continuation: The longer a coin's run of higher daily closes, relative to its usual streaks over the past year, the larger its further rise over the next 5 days. [bar: 1d]
031-04_down-streak-length-continuation: The longer a coin's run of lower daily closes, relative to its usual streaks over the past year, the larger its further fall over the next 5 days. [bar: 1d]
