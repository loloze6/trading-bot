Idea: The more days in a row a coin has closed higher, measured against the lengths of its own up-close streaks over the past 365 days, the more it falls over the next 5 days.
Consequence: Reversal from crowding: a longer up streak means more one-sided buying and stretched long positioning, so the price gives back more once that flow runs out.
