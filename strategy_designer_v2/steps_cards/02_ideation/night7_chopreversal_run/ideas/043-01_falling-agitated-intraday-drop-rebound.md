Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the larger a coin's drop from the day's open to its close, in percent, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: Within-day selling by forced sellers in a nervous, thinly bid book is absorbed at a discount that grows with the day's flow and is paid back as a rebound the following day.
