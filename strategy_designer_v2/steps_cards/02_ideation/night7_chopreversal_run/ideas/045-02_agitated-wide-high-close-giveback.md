Idea: In an agitated market, the higher a coin closes within its day's high-low range, and the wider that range is against its usual daily range over the past 30 days, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: Forced buying, such as short stop-outs and risk-chasing, pins the close near the high, and the premium it leaves is given back the next day, more so after a wide day.
