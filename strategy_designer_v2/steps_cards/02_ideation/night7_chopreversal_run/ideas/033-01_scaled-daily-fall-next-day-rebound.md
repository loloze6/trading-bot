Idea: The larger a coin's one-day fall measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: Unusually large one-day falls for the coin are forced selling below fair value, which the patient buyers who absorbed it are paid for as the price recovers the next day.
