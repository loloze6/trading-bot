Idea: In a falling, agitated market, the lower a coin's close sits within that day's high-low range, the larger its rise over the next day, with a close at the very bottom of the range followed by the strongest rise.
Consequence: Forced selling that presses into the day's close in a fallen, swinging market is absorbed only at a discount, and that discount is repaid by a rise the next day.
