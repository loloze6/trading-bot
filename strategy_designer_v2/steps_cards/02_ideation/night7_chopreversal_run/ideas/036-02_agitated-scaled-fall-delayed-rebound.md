Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against its 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the second and third days after the fall, leaving out the day right after it.
Consequence: The rise after a large fall is not finished the next day, because held-back capital returns in steps as volatility settles and risk limits reset.
