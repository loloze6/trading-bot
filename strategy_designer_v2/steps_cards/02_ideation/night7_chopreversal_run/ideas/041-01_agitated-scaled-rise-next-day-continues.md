Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day rise measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A sharp rise in an agitated market comes from new information or chosen buying, not forced flow, so it holds or keeps going the next day instead of being given back.
