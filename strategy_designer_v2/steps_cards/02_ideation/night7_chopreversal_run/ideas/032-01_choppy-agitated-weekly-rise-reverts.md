Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the larger a coin's 7-day rise, the larger its fall over the next 7 days, giving back part of that rise.
Consequence: In trendless, high-swing conditions, a past-week rise comes from liquidity-demanding flow that few traders follow, so range traders fade it and the price drifts back.
