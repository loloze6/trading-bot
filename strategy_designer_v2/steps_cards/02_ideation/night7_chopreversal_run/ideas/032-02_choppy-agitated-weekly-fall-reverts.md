Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the larger a coin's 7-day fall, the larger its rise over the next 7 days, recovering part of that fall.
Consequence: In trendless, high-swing conditions, a past-week fall comes from forced selling and stops with no lasting information, so liquidity providers buy it and the price drifts back up.
