Idea: In an agitated market, after a day the coin closes lower, the next day's rise grows with the product of two things: the day's fall against the coin's 30-day standard deviation of daily returns, and the day's high-to-low range against its average daily range over the past 30 days. A large fall on a wide day is followed by the largest rise over the next day.
Consequence: Forced selling that crosses a thin book leaves a discount, larger the deeper and wider the push, which is repaid the next day.
