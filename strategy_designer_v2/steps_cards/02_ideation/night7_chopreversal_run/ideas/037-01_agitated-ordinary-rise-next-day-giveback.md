Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day rise relative to its 30-day daily swing, while staying within that usual swing, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: In agitated markets, ordinary up days are a temporary push from flow that carries no news, which is partly given back the next day in proportion to the rise's size.
