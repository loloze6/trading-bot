Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall measured against its 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: When a coin swings more than usual, a sharp fall is partly forced selling, and buyers who took it at a discount hand that discount back the next day once the selling stops.
