Idea: The lower a coin closes in its day's high-low range, and the wider that day's range is against the coin's usual daily range over the past 30 days, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A close pinned near the low of a day far wider than usual marks forced selling absorbed at a concession, which is repaid by a rise the next day.
