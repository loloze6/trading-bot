Idea: In a calm market, the larger a one-day rise is against the coin's 30-day daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns), the larger the coin's rise on the second day after it, from the close one day after the rise to the close two days after.
Consequence: Late buyers, who act on confirmed daily closes or notice an outlier move late, arrive after a day of short-term selling and push the price up on the second day.
