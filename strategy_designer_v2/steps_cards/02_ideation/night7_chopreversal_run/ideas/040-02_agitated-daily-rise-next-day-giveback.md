Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day rise, measured as its percent change from the previous close, the larger its fall over the next day.
Consequence: The day's net rise is partly reversed the next day, in proportion to its size, because stretched liquidity providers absorb urgent buying only at a premium.
