Idea: In an agitated market, after a day the coin closes lower, the wider that day's high-to-low range against its average daily range over the past 30 days, the larger the coin's rise over the next day.
Consequence: A wide range on a falling day marks forced flow through thin quotes, whose discount is given back the next day, unlike an orderly narrow-range repricing.
