Idea: In an agitated market, the larger a coin's one-day fall against its 30-day daily swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days), the larger its rise over the next three days.
Consequence: Forced selling in agitated markets is absorbed slowly, so the recovery after a large scaled fall builds over several days and grows with the fall.
