Idea: In a rising, calm market, a day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns and that closes near its low is followed by a rise over the next day. The wider the day against that swing and the nearer the close to the low, the larger the rise.
Consequence: Hurried selling that pushes a wide day to close at its low in an orderly uptrend is an overshoot that waiting buyers repay the next day, more so the wider the day.
