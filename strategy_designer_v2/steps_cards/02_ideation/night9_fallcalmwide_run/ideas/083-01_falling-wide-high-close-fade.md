Idea: In a falling market, a day that closes near its high is followed by a fall over the next day, and the fall is larger the wider that day's high-to-low range is against the coin's average daily range over the past 20 days.
Consequence: In falling markets, the short-lived buying that pushes a wide day to a close at its high meets waiting supply from trapped holders, so price gives back part of the gain the next day, more the further the burst stretched.
