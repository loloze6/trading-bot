Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, the next day's move runs against the side of the range where the day closed. It is larger the further the close sits from the middle of the range, multiplied by the day's range against its 30-day swing.
Consequence: The discount paid to absorb forced flow grows with the day's width and the close's distance from the range's middle, on either side, and is repaid the next day.
