Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes in the lower half of its own range is followed by a rise over the next day, and that rise is larger the nearer the close sits to the day's low.
Consequence: Weak closes in an uptrend are a short-term discount that waiting dip buyers repay next day, and a close nearer the low leaves a larger discount to repay.
