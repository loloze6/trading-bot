Idea: On a down day, the heavier the coin's volume relative to its own 20-day average daily volume, the larger its rebound over the next day.
Consequence: In crypto, heavy volume is most often forced selling such as liquidations, stop cascades and margin calls, so heavy-volume falls are the ones whose absorbers are repaid next day.
