Idea: In a falling, calm market, the distance a day's close sits above that day's low, measured against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns, is followed by a fall over the next day that grows with that distance.
Consequence: The size of the push pinned into a day's close, seen as its climb from the day's low against usual swing, is the overshoot that patient sellers take back the next day.
