Idea: In a falling, calm market, the larger a coin's one-day rise measured against the standard deviation of its daily returns over the past 30 days, the larger the coin's fall over the next day.
Consequence: Underwater holders sell into a sharp up day bought by short-lived demand, so a rise is partly given back the next day, and more so the sharper the rise.
