Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes in the lower half of its own range is followed by a rise over the next day, and that rise is larger the higher the coin's 60-day return.
Consequence: A stronger recent rise means more buyers wait below the price and the crowd trusts the trend more, so the weak-close rebound is larger.
