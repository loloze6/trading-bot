Idea: After a day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day swing and whose close sits near the day's low, the coin rises over the next day, and the rise is larger the further that day's volume sits above its 20-day average.
Consequence: Forced, urgent selling absorbed at a discount on heavy volume is repaid the next session, so the next-day rebound after a wide low close grows with the day's volume.
