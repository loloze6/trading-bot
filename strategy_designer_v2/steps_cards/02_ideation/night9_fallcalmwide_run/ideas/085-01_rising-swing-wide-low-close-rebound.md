Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes in the lower half of its range is followed by a rise over the next day, and the rise is larger the wider that day's range is against the coin's 30-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns).
Consequence: Forced selling in a rising coin leaves a discount that grows with the day's width against its normal swing, and waiting buyers repay it the next day.
