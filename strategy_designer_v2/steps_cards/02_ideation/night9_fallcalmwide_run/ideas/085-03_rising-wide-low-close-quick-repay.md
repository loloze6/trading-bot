Idea: In a rising market, after a day closing in the lower half of its range, the rise over the next day is larger than the rise over the two days after it, and the gap grows with that day's range against its 30-day swing.
Consequence: Waiting buyers in a rising coin step in as soon as the forced flow stops, so the discount is repaid within a day rather than slowly over several days.
