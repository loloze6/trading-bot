Idea: After a day on which the coin closes higher, the next day it falls. The fall is larger the smaller that day's rise is against the standard deviation of the coin's daily returns over the past 30 days.
Consequence: A timid up day shows that standing supply absorbed the buying, and that supply presses the price down the next day, more the more timid the rise.
