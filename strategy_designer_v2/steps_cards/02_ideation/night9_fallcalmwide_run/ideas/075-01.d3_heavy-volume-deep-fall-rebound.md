Idea: The heavier a coin's volume on a day that closed lower, relative to its own average daily volume over the past 20 days, and the deeper that day's fall against its 30-day daily swing, the larger its rise over the next day.
Consequence: A trader takes the largest long positions after deep falls on the heaviest volume, where the forced selling being absorbed is greatest.
Why:
- F3: On down days alone, the next-day rise is graded by volume.
- F5: A large fall alone is only part of the effect.
