Idea: In a choppy, agitated market, a wide day that closes near its high is followed by a fall over the next day, larger the wider the day's range against its 30-day swing and the nearer the close sits to the high.
Consequence: Forced buying pushes the close to the high of a wide range without information, and the overshoot is given back next day as price returns toward the middle.
