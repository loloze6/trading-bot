Idea: In a rising market, a day's three-day forward rise grows with the width of its range against the coin's 20-day average range times how near the close sits to the day's low.
Consequence: The more forced selling (wider day) and the more it was still pressing at the close (close at the low), the larger the discount the trend buyers repay over the following days.
