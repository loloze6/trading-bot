Idea: In a rising market, after a day that closes near the bottom of its range, the rise over the next three days exceeds the rise over the next day alone, and the gap is larger the wider that day's range is against the coin's 20-day average range.
Consequence: The buyers who repay the sweep decide on the close and buy over the following sessions, so the repayment spreads over days rather than coming in one bounce.
