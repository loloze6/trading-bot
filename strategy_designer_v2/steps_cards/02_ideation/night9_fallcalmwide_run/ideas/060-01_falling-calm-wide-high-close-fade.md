Idea: In a falling, calm market, a day whose high-to-low range is wide against the coin's 30-day swing of daily returns and that closes near its high is followed by a fall over the next day. The fall is larger the wider that range and the closer the close sits to the day's high.
Consequence: An urgent buying burst that pins a wide day's close at its high, against steady overhead supply, is given back the next day, more so the wider and higher-closing the day.
