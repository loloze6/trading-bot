Idea: In a rising market, a day that closes in the lower half of its own range is followed by a rise over the next day, larger the more the coin's 60-day return and the close's nearness to the day's low are both high together.
Consequence: The rebound combines both drivers: the number of waiting buyers, set by the trend's strength, and the size of the discount owed, set by how near the low the day closed.
