Idea: In a falling market, the day after a coin closes higher brings a fall over the next day, and that fall is larger the lower the coin's own 60-day return, whatever the size, close or volume of the up day.
Consequence: Trapped holders sell into any rise, so the day after an up day weakens with the depth of the coin's two-month decline.
