Idea: In a falling market, an up day is followed by a fall over the next day, and the fall grows as the up day's rise gets smaller against the coin's 30-day swing (the standard deviation of its daily returns over the last 30 days).
Consequence: In a coin that has fallen for weeks, a timid bounce shows a bid too weak to clear the underwater holders selling into it, so the decline resumes the next day.
