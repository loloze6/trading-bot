brief: 001_rsi-extremes-predict-next-move
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
bar: 1h
---
Idea: After the relative strength index on a coin's hourly bars reaches a low extreme following a one-sided run down, the coin's price rises over the following hours, recovering part of the drop, more than it does after neutral readings.
Consequence: Reversal answer: a downward run that pushes the index to an extreme reflects forced liquidations or panicked sellers overshooting, and price recovers part of the move once that pressure is spent.
