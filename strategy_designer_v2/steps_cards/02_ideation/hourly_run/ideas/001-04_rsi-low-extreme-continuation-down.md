Idea: After the relative strength index on a coin's hourly bars reaches a low extreme following a one-sided run down, the coin's price keeps falling over the following hours, more than it does after neutral readings.
Consequence: Continuation answer: a downward run reflects slowly spreading news, trend-following funds cutting exposure and leveraged traders chasing, so weak readings come before further declines.
