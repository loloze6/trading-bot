Idea: After the relative strength index on a coin's hourly bars reaches a high extreme following a one-sided run up, the coin's price keeps rising over the following hours, more than it does after neutral readings.
Consequence: Continuation answer: an upward run reflects slowly spreading news, trend-following funds adding exposure and leveraged traders chasing, so strong readings come before further gains.
