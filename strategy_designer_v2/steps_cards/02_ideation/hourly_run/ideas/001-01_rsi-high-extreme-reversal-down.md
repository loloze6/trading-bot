brief: 001_rsi-extremes-predict-next-move
instruments: BTC, ETH, BNB
bar: 1h
---
Idea: After the relative strength index on a coin's hourly bars reaches a high extreme following a one-sided run up, the coin's price falls over the following hours, giving back part of the run, more than it does after neutral readings.
Consequence: Reversal answer: an upward run that pushes the index to an extreme reflects crowding or overshooting buyers, and price gives back part of the move once that pressure is spent.
