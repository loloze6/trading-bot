---
brief: 015_directional-volatility-asymmetry-question
---
Idea: The larger a coin's 1-day rise, the larger the absolute size of its daily close-to-close returns over the next 7 days, and this increase is larger than the one that follows a 1-day fall of the same size.
Consequence: Rallies raise later volatility more than equal falls, through leveraged chasing, short squeezes and the jump in attention after up moves.
