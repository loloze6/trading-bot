Does the relative strength index, which compares the size of recent gains with recent losses, help predict how BTC, ETH and BNB move next? The answer could go either way.

It could predict reversal. A one-sided run that pushes the index to an extreme may reflect crowding, forced liquidations or emotional traders who overshoot. Once that pressure is spent, price would give back part of the move, which pays the traders who supplied liquidity against the crowd.

It could predict continuation. In crypto, a one-sided run may instead reflect news that spreads slowly, trend-following funds adding exposure and leveraged traders chasing. Strong readings would then come before further moves in the same direction.

It could predict nothing. The index is only a transform of past returns. If these large, liquid coins absorb information quickly, it should carry nothing beyond what past returns already show.

These reasons are mine, not from a source.

The question has a clear answer only if behaviour after extreme readings differs from behaviour after neutral readings. If later price moves look the same in direction and size after both, for all three coins, the index has no predictive value.
