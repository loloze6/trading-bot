When price makes a new high while RSI makes a lower high, or a new low while RSI makes a higher low, does this divergence predict that price will turn?

**Why it could predict a turn (my reasoning, not from a source):** The divergence means the new extreme was reached with smaller, less frequent moves. That suggests fewer participants are still pushing the trend. Once the last trend-chasers are in, no one is left to extend it, and price falls back.

**Why it could fail:** In crypto, strong trends often slow down without reversing. Momentum can fade in a range or a pause, and the trend can then resume.

**What would show it wrong:** After divergences, price does not turn against the prior trend more often or further than it does after comparable new highs or lows without divergence.
