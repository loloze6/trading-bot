Price moves made over the weekend partly reverse once the working week begins. The reason is mine, not from a source. On weekends many market makers, trading desks and arbitrageurs who rely on traditional banking and staff hours trade less, so order books are thinner. Flow of a given size then moves price further than it would on a weekday, and part of that move is price impact rather than new information. When weekday participants return, they supply liquidity and trade against the stretched price, pulling it back. The theory is wrong if weekend moves persist into the week as much as weekday moves of similar size do. It is also wrong if weekend bars do not show lower volume.
